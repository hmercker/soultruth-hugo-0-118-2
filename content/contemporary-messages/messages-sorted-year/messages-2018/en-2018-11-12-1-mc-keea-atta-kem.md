# Praying for Divine Love is Simple, Profound and All Changing

November 12<sup>th</sup>, 2018

Online Prayer Circles, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Keea Atta Kem and I wish to invite our new friends and welcome them into this beautiful Circle of Light, into this home where prayers for Divine Love have been happening for a very, very long time. The condition in this home is one of light and one of love. My dear friends, each of you have your relationship with your Creator. Each of you feel a sense of understanding to some degree of the one who has created you. And in this time, as you sit opening up your souls, opening your heart to the blessing of Divine Love, inviting the inflowing of this most beautiful and high energy into your souls, a great sense of well-being comes over you.

I wish to let you know that in this very simple practice comes the highest gift, the Essence of the Creator. As you receive this Essence, this Love, there is a transformation of your soul that begins to occur. Those things that keep you outside of love, in your actions and words and thoughts, will be healed by the energy of this love, receiving it into your souls. And my dear friends, you will find with enough of this love received into your soul that great harmony will come to you.

It is not necessary to struggle by the way of the mind into being what you feel is a good person. For when this love is activated in your soul, it will be by your nature that you wish to be loving in your words, in your thoughts and by your actions. It may seem something very, very simple that you are doing to open up your heart, and to long, to yearn for this energy to flow into you. Beloveds, it truly is that simple. But please do not confuse something that is simple cannot be profound, cannot be all-changing. For this simple practise will change you in ways far greater than any other practise that you currently engage in. I encourage you to sit daily, as often as you can and open up yourself to this great blessing of love.

There will come to you a great peace and a great balance in your life that you could not have expected. Many blessings come to you and you have the ability to give and to share with others as this love graces your soul. My friends there is a great, beautiful light that is here and a condition that is of love.  So I leave you at this time for you to sit with an open heart allowing your mind to rest and to fall deep into your soul uniting and receiving this beautiful blessing of love.

It is wonderful to see you here together in prayer and I thank you for hearing my words. May each of you be touched by this blessing of love as you sit together this evening. I am Keea-Atta-Kem and my love is with each of you. God bless you.
