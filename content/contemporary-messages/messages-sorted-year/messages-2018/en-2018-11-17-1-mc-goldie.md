# Great Love and Great Joy

November 17<sup>th</sup>, 2018

Online Prayer Circles, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Maureen Cardoso

I just can’t stay away. There is just so much joy here at this moment. I must come and let you know that I am with you.  I could do it in silence but I just wish to let you know I am here. I think you feel me. Oh yes, and I am bringing great, great love and great joy. This is going to be a wonderful time together. Please enjoy, my friends. Enjoy the experience of your love expanding and growing in God’s Light.

Ok, that is all. Enjoy your prayer. God bless you. Goldie loves you.
