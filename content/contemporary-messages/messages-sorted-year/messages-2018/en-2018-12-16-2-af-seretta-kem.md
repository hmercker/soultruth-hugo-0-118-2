# Earth Changes - What Comes Will Test Your Mettle and Reveal to Yourself the Power of Your Own Souls

December 16<sup>th</sup>, 2018

Online Prayer Circles, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

I am Seretta Kem. I welcome you all back into this fold, this time of prayer and your desire to serve God in these various ways, to express your love and the beauty of your souls. The coming year will be filled with service. There will be many opportunities for each one of you to step forward and be a channel of Love for many others. It is a year that will bring great difficulties to your world, unforeseen reactions, conditions that will challenge every soul upon this planet. So, beloved souls, it is for you to step forward in strength and Love, to be prepared to follow your guidance, to walk in the Light, to be closely aligned with God so that you may be used to bring comfort, healing, Truth, and Love to your brothers and sisters.

As conditions escalate in your world, there will be great fear and confusion. The naysayers will no longer be able to deny that there are conditions and problems in the weather, in the earth and in the oceans that are not reflective of God’s creation but rather the response to the conditions that humanity besets the world with. The world will respond with greater storms, fires, droughts, shakings of the Earth, eruptions, difficulties that will wreak havoc upon mankind. It is a time that you must be close to God, that you must follow your guidance and prepare yourselves for the coming Earth changes and conditions. In time, as we have said, the Earth will right itself. There will be harmony, but the time for transition is now. You will come to know how the Earth responds to the attack upon its well-being and how God responds to the apathy and darkness, greed, and ignorance of mankind towards this beautiful creation of life. If adjustments and corrections are not made voluntarily and in the way of creating harmony consciously, then the responses come that will insist upon bringing these corrections and eventual equilibrium to the world.

So, we have asked and cajoled you to awaken, beloved souls, to awaken in your soul, to be in alignment with God, to receive ever greater quantities of the great gift of His Love, that your gifts and abilities may come forth in their expression and ripeness of Love and Light. We will be close to you, my beloved, beloved friends, very close, for we have promised our help, assistance, protection and guidance in the coming times. We will stand by our dedication of love towards all those who seek Love and seek Light and wish to serve their brothers and sisters. You will need one another. You will need to reach out to many. You will be guided. Much guidance will be given to relay to those who are willing to listen. There will be surprising manifestations and outcomes. Most of all, God will be close and you will benefit from His Touch and Protection. Stay close to God in your heart. Be fervent in your prayers. Walk in the Light with each moment, each breath. Love one another and support one another.

Though these times may be difficult, you will emerge from the other side of the tumult with joy and a clear understanding and recognition of what must be in your world so that future generations may indeed experience a life that is fulfilling, both materially and spiritually, to be close to God, and to be in harmony with one another, to know Love, to express Love. These things must be in your world for this world to be in harmony and balance. So, humanity must awaken and know their true selves and know from whence they have come and where they are going, to recalibrate their values, their desires, and that the wisdom of their souls may emerge and come forth into their consciousness. This we have encouraged you to do for many years. You are indeed coming ever closer to this awakening. You must continue upon this journey as the coming times will be intense. Intense in your spiritual experiences and communion with God and in coping with the conditions that are changing all around you. You need to be strong, forthright, with integrity, honesty, and a desire to express love and harmony in your lives, to be with God, beloved souls, as earnestly and fervently as you can and to not allow the changing conditions of this world to distract you from that vital pursuit. There is a great strength within each one of you, beloved souls. What comes will test your mettle and reveal to yourself the power of your own souls as God awakens you and sends you forth in the world to calm the troubled waters, to bring comfort and strength and peace wherever you go.

Though these may be challenging times, my friends, they are also wonderful times, opportunities to express your gifts, to be God’s beautiful channels of Love, to walk in the world as lights, and to be guided with each step. You will walk with deep faith and great strength and a deep knowing of your relationship with God and a deep compassion in your relationship with your brothers and sisters. Savour these times, beloveds, these times in which all is well and safe and full of love, and nurture yourselves, strengthen your souls as never before with a keen eye on what is happening in the world and what is possible as times unfold and change comes to pass as we have talked about for so many years. These times are now.

May God bless you deeply within your souls, beloved friends, and awaken you, awaken you so that you may have the strength and wisdom and insight and gifts to help usher in the coming changes and do so with joy in your hearts, with a deep knowing of the blessings that these changes are. This will come to you. God will infill you with this knowing, understanding, and strengthen you with His Love so that you may walk in the world, not with fear, but compassion and deep insight into Truth. These things are coming, beloveds. And I will be close. Many will be close. You will come to know us. Some of you will come to see us as if we are in the flesh and there will be a great cooperation and collaboration between us to help bring the world back from the brink.

May God bless you, beloveds, always. May your souls be eager for Love, eager and open so that God may indeed be close and you may walk in Light. Beloved, beloved friends, God bless you and I love you. God bless you.
