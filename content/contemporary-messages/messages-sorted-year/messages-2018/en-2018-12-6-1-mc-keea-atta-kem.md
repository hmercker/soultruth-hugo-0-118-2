# Change is the One Constant in Life’s Experience

December 6<sup>th</sup>, 2018

FUSC, Burnaby, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

Beloved souls I am Keea, Keea Atta Kem and I have been asked to come and share a message with you on the topic of change.   For each of you here in this circle and each beloved soul upon your earth and your earth are moving through great, great change at this time.  There is not one soul that is not being affected by the many changes that are occurring.  For change is the one constant in life’s experience.  You see in your day, your day turns into night, your days turn into weeks, your weeks into a month, months turn into a year and so on.  Things are always moving and changing.  You see it in your seasons spring becomes summer, summer becomes autumn, autumn becomes winter.

Beloveds the greatest change and effort that you can make in your life is the change of your soul.  For when your soul is created this already is change and there comes a time when you as a soul incarnate into the womb of your maternal parent, this again is change.  Then there is a time of gestation where great change is occurring, the development of the embryo into an infant, preparation happening for the time of birth.  Upon your birth this is another change and your experience then begins here upon your earth in your earth life.  Moving from this infant to toddler to child to adolescent to adult to senior to when you once again will transition into spirit.  All of this is change.

The importance of opening your soul and receiving this Essence of the Creator’s Love causing great change will be one that you can continue for all eternity.  When a soul is kissed by the love of God, there is a bond that begins to manifest between your soul and the Soul of your Creator.  A deep love a sense of belonging where your soul’s desire will continue to bring you back into this union to receive more of this Love, to be in prayer constant prayer, constant yearning for this Essence.  This Essence, my beloveds causes change.  The most vital change that you can choose to be involved in for it does require your personal effort.  God will never interfere with your own free will.God is always waiting for you to open and be present receiving this Essence. How does this Essence change your soul?  In dynamic ways that will strengthen you, bring you peace; bring great joy, stability, wisdom, faith, trust, truth, a deep inner knowing and a continuous progression to atonement with your Creator.

At this time in your life there are conditions that inhibit your soul from expressing love and expressing light.  So when you receive this Love into your soul, your very foundation is changed opening up and widening this space of your soul to receive this Love.  These conditions that once held you back from loving ways will be healed and lifted off and then this deep love will reside. It is then, there from this loving place this love that resides with your soul, you have greater ability to choose love.  Your essence becomes love.  This is a forever change, when this love resides in your soul it will never leave, it will only build and expand eternally.  Yet, it always requires each individual person to make their effort to be in union with the One who has created them and yearn to receive this Love and to be changed by this Love.  It is an eternal process and as you continue to return to this time with your Creator building upon this Love you will notice how the characteristics that you may have carried that were unloving have left you and are replaced by characteristics of love.  It enhances your personality; it brings to you a desire to connect with your brothers and sisters, a desire to be in service.  It will shift and change you from the very inside out.

So beloveds, when you are faced with change in your life, whether that be on a level of soul or in your material world do not resist.  Accept the change that is occurring for nothing is constant.  In your material world, it is often seen that your material things will bring you security and this is an illusion for you may lose these things.  One who has set their securities on materialism may find themselves very lost, scrambling, filled with anxiety and fear about the change that is happening.  Yet, a person who has developed a deep bond with God will have the stability to move through this change with grace and have the strength to stand before the change and allow, to understand that their experience here on this earth world is for a short time of their soul’s existence.  Your soul moves through many transformations right from its creation through your material existence and continuing on in spirit.  For beloveds, you are soul, you are not your body you are not your mind, you are soul.  When you have a deep understanding of that you are open to the grace of God guiding your life, the Soul of God guiding you as a soul moving through time and space learning, developing, helping and sharing.

Change is a gift beloveds. Change is the cycle of creation for nothing remains the same.  You are changed by sitting in circle praying for the Love of God.  When you leave this time together you are changed by this experience.  Embrace change for it is a gracious gift by your beloved Creator allowing the creative expressions to come into form.

I thank you for your time for your attention, for your love.  I embrace each of you, so happy to be with you and share this teaching on change.  God bless each of you, my beloveds.  I am Keea Atta Kem, I love you.  God bless you.
