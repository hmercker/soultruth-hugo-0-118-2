# Divine Rays Accelerate Earth Changes

November 30<sup>th</sup>, 2024

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. It is Orion. I wish to continue speaking about the effects and the intensities of the changes that are happening in your world. There is a great intensity of light and what you call energies coming down upon this world of yours from Divine Source. That intensity will have its effects upon humanity, whether they are aware of its presence or not.

Those of you who are sensitive will feel the intensity and feel somewhat disoriented or *"off your game,"* as you would say, because of it. These rays are designed to bring change and to uplift the consciousness of humanity. They are also intended to heal this planet of yours, to help for the realignment. Even though the overt expression of it seems to be chaos and conditions that are unpleasant, storms that are violent. Etc. Yet, with every adjustment, there comes some change, something that disrupts. Indeed, the disruptions continue to happen on your earthly plane.

It is similar in your consciousness that you may be feeling somewhat disrupted in the stream and flow of your daily condition and mindful exercise. That steadiness that you have grown to rely upon, which is a reflection of your mind, is becoming disrupted by the conditions and blessings that are entering into your bodies, your minds, your souls and your spirits. All parts of you are being touched by these blessings and they will continue to have their effects upon you, my friends.

This is all part of God's plan, the Divine intentions to bring this planet back to harmony. No one can escape from the rawness of these energies. Like being in direct sunlight, those rays can be intense. Often, you seek shade or you cover yourself to ameliorate the intensity and so it is with the rays of Divine blessings upon this world of yours. When you feel the intensity of it, often you wish to dampen it down so that you may feel more comfortable. This is understandable. Indeed, you are responsible for your response to it. We do not judge you for seeking ways of making it less intense and more comfortable for yourself in your daily life.

Yet, these rays will have their effects. You will feel them on all levels of your being. In this, changes come, changes of attitude, changes of action, changes of intention and changes of perception. You come into a willingness to merge into these conditions. As a result, different perceptions, feelings and senses within the body will respond accordingly. Because you are each unique individuals, you will feel these things differently and respond differently, but you will, indeed, feel and sense that there is a change and a disruption to a degree.

Many of you will feel restless and desire peace. Our hope is that you will go to prayer in those conditions and ascertain peace from your prayers. You will begin to see things differently. What was important to you in the past may change and shift. Some of those things will become irrelevant and unimportant. Other, more subtle perspectives may come to the fore. You will find that these things will become important.

The shifting of consciousness is a powerful motivator for humanity. Indeed, all of humanity is challenged by the dark conditions, the disruptions and unfortunate attitudes that have been created by humanity over millennia of time. These things are now becoming difficult, intense and challenging to many. Their response may be contrary to that which is of light. There may be a reaction, anger, some sense of judgment and pointing fingers at others. Yet, these things are happening within each individual upon this world of yours. This uncomfortable condition and situation will lead to their expressions that are reactions rather than an attempt to adjust and to be in harmony with what must come and what is coming.

Our hope is that you will respond and react in harmony, utilising the laws that you know and coming to that place of acceptance, allowing the shifts to take place within your consciousness. Hopefully, you will see them as a gift, a blessing from God, which they are. As you continue to grow in Love, as your soul continues upon its journey of consciousness, of shifting and seeing things from a harmonious perspective, it is important that you integrate your spirit perceptions and your material perceptions, which are also being influenced by these rays of blessings from God. Bring them into your soul. Integrate them into your soul consciousness. Allow your soul to accept, to embrace and, with wisdom, see the source and the purpose of these changes and shifts that are happening within.

Be brave, beloved souls, for with the sensitivities that you have cultivated, with the understanding that comes from the soul, you will feel these things more intensely and they will have their effects more directly upon you. Allow this to be so and see this as a need to be in greater harmony with God's creation, with life. For what comes from God, these rays, these beautiful blessings, is life itself asserting itself in a more overt way, coming to each living thing upon your planet, including yourselves and having their effects upon it.

Thus, you cannot escape these intense rays. The only approach is to accept and to go through the process, the changes and the shifting that must come. This is somewhat different from your soul's shifting and growing, but they are complementary, these two elements. They will help you to integrate all parts of yourself. They will help in your soul's perceptions of things that are of God and are of God's creation.

In time, there will be much of a sense of acceptance and expansive love for your brothers and sisters and for life. A gentleness will befall you within your mind and you will have much compassion. You will see from different eyes, know from that different place, that deeper place and be truly in harmony with the Laws of Love.

These things are given by God's great compassion for the world. These things are added to the world, for the world is in danger of human destruction and intervention. God's intervention, which is far greater than that of humanity, must come into being and be expressed. So, you are feeling this intervention and blessing. Continue to be aware of it and to accept and have gratitude for what is being given. For it is Divine mercy upon your world that is bringing the changes and accomplishing the shift that must take place in order for the world to be safe, to be in harmony, to be at peace, all within the world, including yourselves.

May you be blessed, beloved and beautiful children of God, with the great Love that is the Essence of God's Soul. May it be conveyed in great quantities into your soul, that this may augment the blessings that I have spoken of and integrate readily what is being given. May you be at peace, my beloved friends. Know that I am close. I am Orion and my love for you is great. Blessed and beautiful friends, my love for you is great. I have great compassion for the changes and the conditions that you must deal with at this time. Have great faith in what God intends for each one of you and be at peace, my friends. Be at peace. God bless you.
