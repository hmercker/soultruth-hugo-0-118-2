# Wherever God’s Will manifests there will be Harmony and joyous Awakening and wondrous Change

September 16<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

Your teacher Augustine here.

There will be no missteps, my children. You are well prepared. And you will know what to say. You will be cognizant of what is required and you will walk in faith. And your efforts, my children, will cause a change that will ripple forth in your world.

Continue to prepare yourselves. Continue to pray, to listen, to answer the questions, to reassure the children who clamour and want to know that they are welcome; and they are truly welcome. And you shall be loving, and generous and kind, with the Love in your soul burn in warmth and generosity and kindness. Put aside your needs. Think of their needs. Put aside your expectations and be a channel of God’s will, and what is meant to be shall be.

The world needs you, my children. The world needs what you have. The world needs to be loved. And the barriers will fall, the suspicions will evaporate, the fears will sink into the earth. And there will be a joyous connection, a loving awakening, a wondrous (...?) opening. And you will be a part of this. And you will find joy in this. And you will glean understanding, a deeper understanding of all these words we’ve spoken over the years and encouragement that we have given, and guidance. You will come to understand that we have worked together to these ends; and this, my children, is just the beginning of many such efforts and events and gatherings and work.

The doors open, the mighty doors open, and we will walk through hand in hand, stepping in joy and surety and love. God bless you, precious souls. Be channels of love and allow all that, that may stand in the way of this, fall away. You are ready to do this. The only obstruction that may be in your way is that of your own making, that of your own choosing. Allow the Father to work through you, and His Love to envelope you, and the angels to uphold you, and all will be well. You will see. Wherever God’s Will manifests there will be harmony and joyous awakening and wondrous change.

God bless you, my children. Your teacher Augustine will be with you. I am always with you, and I love you deeply. I love you deeply. God bless you. God bless you.
