# The Chain of Love

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Solomon received by Al Fike

You seek earnestly the truth and light and goodness and service and righteousness, the light of Love. God bless all of you, precious souls who gather in this light, in this intention to be instruments of truth, and love and change in the world. I say to you, my children, you will be these channels you aspire to and you are these channels you aspire to and you are doing the work that is in alignment with the Father’s will.

And our instrument has recently spoken of a vision he had many years ago about the bridges that span the water, that invite each soul to cross. And I tell you that the waters are your fears and your resistance to what your souls already know, that you are meant to be a channel of God’s Love in this world. You are imbued with purpose and there is great understanding within you, my children, of this purpose and these truths.

And yes, you are invited to cross the bridge to release your fears and your resistance and that which is within your mind that rationalizes your inability to move forward. I tell you, my children, you are crossing this bridge at this time as you gather together, as you make your plans, as you strengthen the bonds of love between you, as you harmonize your intentions and desires, as you clear away the smog of error, the veils of fear and mistrust.

As these things fall away from you, my children, you cross this bridge and you come to that place of clarity, of joy, of love, of purity of intention and loving, loving expression of your beautiful gifts, of your beautiful beings, each unique as the Father has created you, expressed in light and purity and love. And in this expression and this purity a great impact is made in the world, as souls are touched by your expression of God’s Love.

Oh, gracious, beautiful children, accept that you are in the flow of the Father’s Love, that God’s Will works through you, as we in spirit are in the flow of this Love, intention and will, so are you, my children. It is part of the chain of Love. And as you grow to accept this so the opportunities to express this will grow and multiply, and the depths of this expression, in fact the impact of this expression of love, will become greater and greater. And with every breath you will bring light. With every movement, you will bring change. With every word, you will bring truth.

The power of God’s Love is great and vast and beyond your ability to recognize but this will come as God’s Love opens your soul perceptions, your understanding from deep within. And you walk in faith, knowing this will come. And you walk in trust, acknowledging that you require ministrations of the Heavenly Father and love. And you walk in innocence, not truly understanding the power of the impact of your lives in this world and you are humble, and you are beautiful. Acknowledge this, my children, your beauty and the power of God’s Love within you and acknowledge that God has charged you with great purpose and will guide you upon the road to fulfilling this purpose in the world.

And there will be no accident but great providence and guidance and what is meant to be shall be. And the distractions will fall away, and the clarity will come with each step as you take your steps in faith, in love, in joy.

God bless you, my dear beloved children. You are charged with a great task. And you are given many blessings, and your needs will be met, and do you not see how God has guided you thus far? And the way is open, the path is clear, the bridges lead to freedom and grace and peace and joy. God bless you. I am Solomon. I love you. I have come a great distance to be with you. For I see a great light here and a pure and loving intent and I wish to support you and love you and be with you in your efforts to bring peace to this world, healing to this planet and joy to the children, wisdom to all. Bless you.
