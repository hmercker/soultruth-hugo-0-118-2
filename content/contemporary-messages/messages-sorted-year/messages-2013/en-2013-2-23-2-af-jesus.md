# Pray to be guided

February 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come my children. I come to be with you, with my brothers and sisters who long for the Father’s Love, who walk with me in this Love, who desire to do the work of love in this world. Walk with me, my children, and I shall walk with you as you desire to bring light and change and peace into the world, and truth. Pray to be guided, my children, pray for the Father’s Love to be a mighty river in this world, to bring the changes that are necessary, to bring the healing that is so deeply needed. And walk with me my children and you shall be guided, shown the way. Be at peace. And now that you are loved and surrounded by the angels and given great opportunity to teach [...?], to bring [...?] and light and do my work. I love you beautiful souls. God bless you. God’s glorious blessing of love for you my children, beautiful souls. Drink deep. God bless you. I am Jesus.
