# Pray earnestly, pray often, pray always with every Breath

September 16<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Isaiah received by Al Fike

A blessing of love to each one here who have gathered together in prayer, earnestly seeking the Love, seeking the guidance and the reassurance, reaching for the light, open to the Love.

And you are about to embark upon your journeys. And you walk in faith. And some of you put aside your fears and have accepted the invitation to gather in that place far off, to pray together with these souls who long for the Love, who long for the fellowship, who are curious and who are seeking. And you, my children, will ride on a wave of love. And you will walk in the light of God’s presence, surrounded by the angels, guided each step as you’ve answered the call, my children, you have answered the call. And you will see the unfolding of the Father’s Will as you are willing to follow this Will and this Love.

And there will be deeper understanding, my children, and deeper love, and deeper connection, one with the other, your soul with God’s soul. There will be a unification and your eyes will open. And each act of faith, each step you take, every moment you are open and willing and earnest and true in your efforts to be with God, to be a channel of God’s Love, to be in the flow of God’s Love, your faith and your understanding and your awareness and the love within will grow and deepen and flourish.

For as you release the conditions of this world within, as you allow the Father to work through you, so these old ways and old habits and old thoughts will fall away and turn to dust, and you will be redeemed in love. You will know great joy and freedom from the burdens that are so common in this world. And you will walk as examples, as a testimony to the power of God’s Love in this world. And others will turn to you and ask, *"How have you come to this place of joy and loving acknowledgment of the Creator?"* And through this open door you will give the truth. You will be a channel of love touching that soul, bringing the answer.

Continue to pray, my children. Pray earnestly, pray often, pray always with every breath, for the Father’s Love will fill your souls and bring you to that place of bright and beautiful light within, and the changes will happen all around you, my children. As you walk through your lives you will have this impact on many souls. There will be great healing, great comfort. The longing will be triggered within each soul as God works through you to reach the lost souls, so many, many lost souls, my children. So many need what you have, and long for what you have, and shall find the answers through your ministrations and your willingness to walk through the dark places in this world and bring love, and bring comfort, and bring peace.

Continue to walk in this way on this path. Continue to have your faith and your innocence and your humility. Continue to listen to your guidance and to be joyous, joyous in the light of God’s Love, joyous in the acknowledgment of the gift that is your life, the beauty that is your creation, the glory that is God’s Love within you working its wondrous miracles within. Be joyous, my children, and know as you journey you are on a mission and you are empowered by God’s beautiful Love and Will, and all will be well, all will be well. I love you, my children. I am Isaiah and I love you. And I am with you. I am with you often in your prayers. God bless you, beloved souls.
