# Continue to walk the Path Divine

March 18<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

Oh, precious souls of the Father’s Love. God bless you. God bless you for coming together in love and a desire to bring healing and peace and love and light into our troubled world, into this world. And yes, as you come together in this way, the Father touches you and brings comfort and healing and love as you open yourselves to this touch.

There is a change within you, my children, a great change from deep within that shall alter every part of you as this Love grows within your souls, as the beautiful wisdom of the soul, and the perceptions of the soul, and the discernment of the soul, the many faculties within that for so many lie dormant, come alive with this Love and emerge within your consciousness, shall bring greater vision and understanding and love and compassion and love for yourselves and love for your fellow man.

So much is brought with this Love, this blessing, and each one of you feels this change within, knows that you cannot turn back, that you are required, that your soul desires to step forward in this light, to glean the awareness, the truth, a greater understanding as you receive God’s Essence within you.

Continue in your efforts, my children, continue to walk the Path Divine, to pursue the truth, to beseech the Father for His great Love, that it may find an inflowing into you, my children, that never ends, as this transformation continues, as you become a clear and bright channel of His Love in the world. These truths are simple, yet they are so very powerful. And what is required of you, my children, is the faith that this blessing and transformation shall be yours, and to be joyed at this wondrous, miraculous gift, and to seek out the many expressions of this Love in your lives, in your world, within you and about you.

Be at peace, my children, be in the Love. I am John and I love you. I love you. Beautiful souls of the Father’s Love. Continue. Pray. Long. Allow yourselves to be in the flow of this Love. God bless you.
