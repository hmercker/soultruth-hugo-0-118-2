# When you walk in Light others respond; some with Love and some with Anger

June 1<sup>st</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

[Dear Heavenly Father?] *"...we ask for a blessing for each one of these precious souls who gather in this place of sanctuary, that they may be blessed with Your Love, a bounteous inflowing within, and that with this blessing of Love may come an opening of their awareness, a falling away of that which keeps them from You, dear Father, and a powerful communion with You in gracious loving peace; that their eyes might be open, that they may know Your presence fully, that they may see the glory of Your creation, of Your being, of Your Love; that they may walk in this light of awareness; that You may place within them, dear Father, a burning desire to receive Your Love with every breath. And as they walk in Your light, may they be clear channels of Your Love. Every step that they take, every breath that they make, may they be a channel of Your Love to bring harmony into this world, to bring healing into this world, to bring knowledge of truth into this world. Guide them, dear Father, upon the path. Help them to see the path clearly; and those doubts and fears that are a part of this human condition, may they fall away with the inflowing of this bounteous Love, that they may know peace and surety, great faith and understanding; that they may walk in strength; that they may know the simple truth that they are loved, that they are beautiful, that they are claimed by You and children of You. Father, guide them along, that they may know with certainty that they are loved."*

The angels surround you, my children. The Love pours into your souls. The healing is abundant and clearing away those conditions that you bring as you walk through this world. May you know peace, my children, the peace that passes all understanding, and may your eyes open to the truth and the power and the glory of the Father’s Love, which shall bring you to that place of clarity and strength, peace and surety.

Yes, there is nothing to fear, and though others may throw slings and arrows upon you, the light within withstands the assault, the wisdom within will give you the strength, the clarity from the soul will show you the way through, and compassion will rise up from within for those who assault you. For with this negative onslaught there is fear and a desire for power and control. Many are so terribly unaware of these conditions and lash out without knowing from where this comes. Their souls are in pain and struggle to find what you have found.

Yes, these are the conditions of this world, my children. Such pain, such great misunderstandings. And when you walk in light others respond; some with love and some with anger. And for you, my children, you must walk with a knowing that you are with the Heavenly Father, that you desire to follow His Will. And in your prayers and your desires and your soul longings you shall follow in accordance to the Love in your soul.

Follow this, my children, follow this path of Love. Have compassion. Be strong. Do not doubt. There will always be attempts to extinguish the light. It was ever thus, my children. This is why you are required to pray for this Love and protection and strength, knowledge and wisdom. For as you grow in the Love, that which you feared, that which hurts at this time will be as nothing, will fall from you without causing pain or disruption. The Love will give the strength. The Love will give this insight. The Love will give you compassion.

Blessed children, such beautiful lights in this world, walk with us. Walk within the Will of God and all shall be harmonious and filled with loving exaltation and deep and powerful perception and growing love and compassion for your fellow man. There is nothing to fear. A wonderful door opens for each one of you. May you have the insight, the perception, to walk through this door in faith and love and be a powerful channel of the Father’s Love, an agent of change in this world, a carrier of truth, bringing healing and comfort to many.

Beautiful souls, I am Andrew. I love you. I love you dearly. And this instrument is not to doubt his perceptions or to falter in fear. He reaches a new place of understanding and from that place there are ripples of reaction and response. And there will be deeper perceptions and stronger reactions, but he follows his purpose and is true to his soul’s desire. There is always need for greater understanding and the use of wisdom in all things. He will learn. There will be harmony and strength and humility.

Bless you, my children. Bless you for your longings, your desire for the highest, your integrity and your strength, your love and your willingness. Bless you. I love you, as do so many.
