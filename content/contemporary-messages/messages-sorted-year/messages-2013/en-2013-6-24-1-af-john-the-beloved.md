# This is Part of God’s Will

June 24<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

God bless you. I ask you to prepare yourselves, my children, for that journey to that English land. Now is the time for preparation, to bring yourself to a place of light and harmony, in the Love, for most of you here will be there. And it is important for you to be in this place. There will be far reaching effects in that part of the world from your presence, in bringing that which you have here, there, to make this channel possible for light and love to affect the many souls who live in this part of your planet.

This is part of God’s Will, my children, and yes, great effort has been made to open the door for this. And it is important to be a channel in the world, to change conditions, to affect many souls, to bring light and healing with you. This is a wonderful purpose, an opportunity for each one of you to collectively affect a mighty change, a change through this planet to build the network of light, to heal many, and to bring harmony.

Do not question yourselves as you are drawn to this purpose. And for some of you there will not be a clear awareness of the effect you will make. This is not necessary. What is important is your presence and your prayers, and your intention to gather in love and harmony, to support one another in your effort to be a channel of love, and to be a channel of God’s Will in the world. Yes, this does require effort upon your part to accomplish this. And we ask you to pray fervently for the Love. Be in prayer to ask God to help you to be in that place of love and light and clarity and peace so when you journey to this place you will clear away any conditions that may inhibit the flow of this Love.

There is much to accomplish there, there is much to give, and many hunger (for what you have?), many hunger for the Love. Be true to your souls’ desires, be humble, be in peace. I am John and I love you and I will be with you on your journey as will many, many celestial spirits who will accompany you on this journey and in this preparation. And there will be many more journeys to come and opportunities available to bring this light to the world.

Blessed are those who are willing, who desire to be channels of love and agents of change in this world, for there is great need, great need and much work to do. Bless you, my children. I love you. I will always be with you. And the Master will always uphold you, for you do his work. Bless you.
