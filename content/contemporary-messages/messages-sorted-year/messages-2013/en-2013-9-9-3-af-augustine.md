# Walk in Innocence, Simplicity and Faith

September 9<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

Bless you, my dear ones, I am your teacher Augustine. I see you are in steps of preparation for your travels, and I wish to encourage you to redouble your efforts of prayer, and of preparing yourselves in your minds, in your bodies, and of course, in your souls, in prayer for the Love. And the place you travel to is in great need of what you have to give, my children, the knowledge of the Love, the expression of the Love, to bring understanding and wisdom and healing and compassion. And you make a beachhead, my children, in this place and as you do so, you open a door that shall open many doors, that shall draw you back again and again into these places, these countries, these cultures.

Yes, one little step shall bring these steps in the future, opportunities to be a channel of love and teaching, wisdom. This is in the flow of God’s plan, my children, and you go willingly and with love and joy, and I am pleased that you accept this invitation, that you made these plans, you made these sacrifices, that you do so in love and faith and a desire to be a channel of God’s Love in the world. And there are many ways to be a channel of God’s Love, in the humble service to the needy souls; this is a powerful channel of love, my children, and the teaching of truth, in the administration of healing, and in walking this world as an example of love. Souls are touched in many ways, and you each have many gifts that the Heavenly Father can utilize to touch many souls.

Continue, my children, in your prayers, your efforts, your faith, in your travels your desire to be a channel of love and God will guide you and carry you and provide for you as you walk the path of love. As you walk in innocence, simplicity and faith. I am very, very pleased at your efforts and I am so very pleased to see all your shiny faces here tonight, eager for the Love, eager to serve, to express God’s Love in such diverse ways. Such beauty, such power to bring change, comfort and healing to this world and love to dissolve the barriers, to eradicate the error, the pain may fall away and the ignorance, and in their place love burning within souls, (peace?) emerging into the consciousness of each child, joy and peace. Blessings to you, my children, your teacher, Augustine, loves you dearly and I am with you often to encourage, to love. Thank you for your efforts and we will take this journey together and you will be supported and surrounded and enveloped in Love. God bless you, dear ones.
