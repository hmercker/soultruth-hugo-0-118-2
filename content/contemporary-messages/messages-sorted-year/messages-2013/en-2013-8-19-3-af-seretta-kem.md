# To be in good physical and spiritual Condition, helps to bring Blessings

August 19<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

God bless you, I am Seretta Kem. I have come to give you instructions for your journey. I would ask all of you who are planning to travel to make an effort for this month to monitor your diet, to cleanse your physical bodies, to strengthen your souls. There are reasons for this requirement as we prepare you for the manifestations that will take place at this place.

If you are in good physical condition, this will facilitate what we have planned. I would ask you not to indulge yourselves in foods that you already know are not beneficial to your body. There is a great temptation in your culture to overeat and over-indulge in that which is not beneficial and we ask you to be mindful and to eat those foods that are beneficial, fresh and full of life. And of course, your spirit diet of receiving the Divine Love is most important, most important. Re-double your efforts, my children, in your prayers. To be in good physical and spiritual condition will assist us from our side of life in these efforts to bring blessings to the souls who are coming to receive, who are open and eager.

And it begins in earnest. Yes, there is much to do and responsibilities to carry and efforts to make in preparation. And for those of you, who find this difficult, ask for the help you require, ask that God remove the temptations within your minds and help you to be strong in your choices, and clear in your intentions and loving in your efforts. As we work with each one of you here (and there are many layers to this work and many dynamics that you are not aware of), and we do prepare you and we make great effort at this, and we ask you to do your part to purify yourselves in many ways and to carry the Love abundantly.

Blessings, my precious children. We walk with you always and use the many opportunities that are available to you as you walk through your daily lives for the benefit of others. Blessings to many, love and light to the world. Bless you. Seretta Kem loves you and I am honoured to work with you and all your efforts, all your loving efforts, gives me great joy, as it does many. You will walk in light, and we all will walk with you into great light, greater love, wondrous works. My dear daughter, Helge, great love and healing goes to those you minister to in your homeland, and to the other daughter there in that place. And great healing and love goes to all of your loved ones here tonight, many that are connected to this group benefit from your prayers. Continue in your efforts, my children. Continue to love. Be at peace. God bless you.
