# Know that you have a Gift

October 26<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

Beautiful souls, I love you. Each of you continue upon this path and know that you have a gift. The Divine Love flows from each of you, precious souls, each of you. And this is deeply needed in your world. I am Jesus and I uphold you, my precious ones, I uphold you.
