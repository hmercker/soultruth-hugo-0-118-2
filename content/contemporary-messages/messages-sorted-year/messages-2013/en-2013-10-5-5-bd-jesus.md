# You will find your Path

October 5<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

….precious in your world, each of you is deeply needed and you will find your path, and it will take you in your own peaceful way, you will do this my precious ones. Each of you has a deep and wonderful connection with the Holy, Holy, beloved Creator. You must allow this. The love you carry within each of you, beloved souls, beloved souls, it will be important to (allow?) a deepening for love to flow in your world. Precious and beloveds, yes, I am Jesus, and I love you deeply. Each of you, I love you deeply.
