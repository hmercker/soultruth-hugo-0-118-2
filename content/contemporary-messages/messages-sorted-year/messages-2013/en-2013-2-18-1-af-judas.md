# This is a firm Foundation

February 18<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Judas received by Al Fike

...God bless you. Yes, it was I who dictated this message many years ago. I am Judas and I have come to be with you this evening, be part of your prayers as you beseech the Father for His love. And my prayer for you is that your souls open to a great inflowing of this Divine Essence, this beautiful blessing from the Father, from His great soul to your beautiful souls, this beautiful love.

It is the gift, the highest of blessings from our Heavenly Father. And yes, this message that I delivered is still pertinent this day, as so many are attached and put such great effort into the material world. To think that if a portion of this effort were put towards their spiritual progression, their understanding of the soul, and the relationship of the soul to the Creator, how this world would change, my children. And as your scientists dissect and delve into the intricacies of creation and are amazed by its beauty and its balance, and so you, my children, are delving into the intricacies of the soul, its relationship with God, the wonderment of His love, and how it transforms the soul and brings about a different and greater consciousness.

Indeed, you are the vanguards, my children, you are the vanguards. Continue in your efforts, in your focus, in your prayers, in your effort to understand, to have this great knowledge of truth, of love, of your relationship with God. And continue to write and to talk, to create your networks, for this little group, this core of light will spread many truths in the world as you are bonded in this love, as you walk upon this divine path, as you make your efforts to understand your footfalls and the progress that you make within.

So much to know, my children, so much to experience, so much to share. And you shall, and you will, give, for there is a great hunger and a thirst, a great desire for this within. And yes, many are distracted by the material, by their concerns and their worries, by the accumulation of such material wealth that they cannot see beyond this. But I tell you none of this is permanent. It is transient. It shall fall away. It shall crumble to dust. And I ask you, my children, *"What then?"* For those who have relied so heavily on this security of the material, what then?

There is much work for you to do, my children. Such a foundation you are building in this world, and this is a firm foundation, a wondrous work. And you will continue to build and to add to your knowledge, and your love, and this great light. And we in spirit will make our efforts to add to this. And God will bless this: every brick in this foundation will be blessed, every step you take will be blessed, every [drop?] of awareness is progression within your souls.

Continue to pray for the love, continue in this work, continue to love and support one another as you have and will continue. For without the cohesiveness, the bonds of love, the gifts that you share, your road will be much more difficult. Such a great light is created by your continued and mutual efforts together. Do not doubt this. Carry on in your love, carry on in your efforts. [...?]

And I wish to reassure this instrument that the new awarenesses that he has received in his relationship with God are real. His doubts, because he does not feel the familiarity in this, are unsubstantiated. This is real and this is an opening of a new relationship with the Creator. He must carry on with faith that God will gift him with the guidance and knowledge that he is meant to receive in this way. It is all part of the [?] of God’s love and care for him. And yes, all souls have the potential to communicate with God in a personal way, to have this inner voice speak. This is possible for all, for those who are willing to listen, for those who have faith that God can communicate in this way. It is possible. It is a gift.

Oh, my dear precious souls, continue to pray for the love, to be in the love, to delve into the wondrous mysteries of the love. So much for you to know, so many, many aspects of your relationship with your Heavenly Father that are still a mystery, that still await discovery, your awareness, your soul understanding. Make your efforts [...?] every day. Long for the Father’s love with every breath and wondrous gifts shall be given. A loving road, a beautiful journey. God bless you. I am Judas. And I love you. Such a blessing to be with you. I love you. God bless you.
