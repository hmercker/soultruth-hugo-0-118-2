# Open your Eyes and be loving in this World

March 9<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Yes, my children, each of you struggles with the conditions of the world, and within you. You must acknowledge your own strength, that you do, despite these terrible conditions and distractions and burdens, that you still come, as you do, to receive the love, to long for the light, and desire to be a channel of love in the world. This takes great strength, my children, and we in spirit acknowledge your strength, and your efforts, and your beauty.

This is not easy. It would be easy to be complacent and to blind yourselves from the disharmonies that you see all around you. But no, with the love within your souls you are compelled to open your eyes, to walk with integrity, to be loving in the world. And this brings us joy. And God blesses you with a wonderful light because of your desire deep within to be a channel of love. And this shall intensify, my children, this desire, and this channel [...?] beautiful manifestations that shall come through each one of you as you desire to be a channel of this light. And they will be ? , wonderful manifestations of love [...?] and healing, and peace.

And through your prayers and your efforts ? and we in spirit shall guide you [...?] every breath. You are never alone. Although you feel burdened at times and feel alone at times this is not the case for God is always with you, my children. His love always graces your presence. And we are always with you, to guide and protect you. Do not doubt this. Such beautiful souls. It gives us great joy to see such bright lights in the world.
