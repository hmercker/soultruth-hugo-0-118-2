# Be an Example for others

March 18<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

God bless you, I am Mary. If you could but see the light that you have drawn to your midst, through your prayers and your longings, the beautiful light that is here, you would be joyed at this wondrous blessing.

And yes, many, many from our side of life have been drawn to this light and we are able to use the beautiful spiritual power that is generated here to bring healing and comfort to many, to bring influence and love to the leaders in the world, and to fight darkness and despair in your world. This is a great work, my children. Continue in these efforts to come together in this way, in your prayers together; you create a great strength of love and light, of healing and peace. The world so desperately needs this light, this healing, this peace, this love.

Continue my precious, precious children, to work together, to pray together, to support one another, to be in harmony, to be an example for others, to love one another. And the light shall become ever stronger, more beautiful, more powerful, more influential in your world and bring about the changes that are needed, and the healing that is required, and the comfort that is so desperately desired. Continue in these efforts.

And we will be with you, as these are our efforts as well. We work together for this healing and change and love. I love you. I love you dearly. I continue to support you and be with you as you grow in the Love, as you continue to strive to be a clear and beautiful channel of the Father’s Will and the Father’s Love. Bless you, bless you my dear beloved souls. You are loved. You are beautiful. You are needed. You are strong and wise and loving. Bless you. I love you. I am Mary. And I bid you goodnight.
