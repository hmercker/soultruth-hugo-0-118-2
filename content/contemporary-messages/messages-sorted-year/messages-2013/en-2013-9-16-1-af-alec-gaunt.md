# As one steps forwards, you all step forward

September 16<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Alec Gaunt received by Al Fike

I want you to realize that a great effort has been made for you to do this work, to make these travels, and I am so very pleased that you have all followed your guidance and made these bold steps, because I know this is not easy. And you have made a great effort here and a willingness to walk in faith and love. And it truly does warm my heart to see how you have brought forward the efforts made many years ago by myself and others to lay the foundation, to bring these truths to the world, and you have made possible an opening, a great light and truth and love in this world. And do you see how the chain continues? How all our efforts bring further light and healing and love into this world?

And there are many plans afoot and much to do. And it is wise that you pace yourselves and not get ahead of yourselves but go one step at a time as the angels guide you along the path and your work manifests in beautiful and simple and powerful ways.

I had trouble in my day to temper my enthusiasm for this work. And unfortunately, it brought for me an early end to my life. And I see for many of you, you have tempered your expectations and walked a wiser path, and I applaud you for these efforts and this wisdom, and I am happy that you all continue in your communications with one another to raise your understanding and your acknowledgment of the work ahead.

And yes, truly God’s Love and hand is upon all of this work, and it will make a great change in the world. And if you could but see all the angels, all the spirits, all the light, all the love that envelopes you and cares for you and guides you and carries you, you would truly, truly understand the power of God’s Love and God’s will. And someday you will see this, someday you will acknowledge how great and glorious is God in this way.

I will be with you on your journeys, and yes, surprises await you, my children, my friends, and I am so very happy you have made these steps. And I wish to acknowledge this instrument, whom I have mentored for many years. Although we have not met in the flesh, we have met many times in spirit and had many conversations, and I have had conversations with each one of you many times when we worked together. And I’m happy at his development and his willingness to step forward as he has. And there will be more openings of awareness and gifts for him and for you, for all of you. For as one steps forwards, you all step forward. As each one steps forward, you all step forward. We work together in love. We are a team. And this team is strong and very gifted.

God bless you, my beloved friends. Know that I am with you and I do love you so very dearly. Blessings to you. Blessings to my family. Love to you all. God bless you. Alec loves you.
