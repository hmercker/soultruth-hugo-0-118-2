# Be at Peace, know you walk in Light and are loved

August 19<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, precious souls in the Father’s Love. I come to be with you. I am Jesus and I love you, love you dearly, each precious soul here, filled with light and love, channels of the Father’s Love, precious to God and precious to me as you make your efforts to be close to God and receive His Love within your souls. I come to pray with you to receive this great blessing and to acknowledge your efforts to be in this light, to pursue the truth and to be a channel of love in the world.

There is no higher calling, my children, no greater task and you are beautiful in this path and calling and efforts that you make to be a channel of love. Let your fears and trepidations fall away from you, my children. Know that I lead you on the path and I am with you always as you pray for the Love, as you seek the guidance, as you take your steps each day. And in this way, and in this flow of love, you do my work and you do God’s work in this world; the simple tasks, the loving presence, the comforting words, healing touch, the words spoken in truth, and acknowledgment of each soul you encounter, you do my work on the Divine Path and you carry the flame of light through the world.

Yes, there is much to do my children, many works to accomplish, many lost souls to save, much love to give. And as you pray earnestly for the Love, as you beseech the Heavenly Father to show you the way, you will see your path lit and the guidance given and the Love bestowed upon you. Do not doubt, my children, know that I am with you and are many, many angels of love surrounding all of you and urging you forward in this great task, this loving flow, this beautiful work. Be at peace, know you walk in light and are loved, so deeply loved and deeply needed in this world. Be at peace. I love you. God bless you. God loves you. God bless you.
