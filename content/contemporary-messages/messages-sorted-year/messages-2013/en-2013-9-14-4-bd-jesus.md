# Blessings

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

Each of you have a deep purpose. Accept this, change… I am Jesus. And you will have many angels to clear the Divine path. For each of you in your life will bring peace and love into this world, beloved souls, precious souls. You have been given a blessing of the Divine Love.
