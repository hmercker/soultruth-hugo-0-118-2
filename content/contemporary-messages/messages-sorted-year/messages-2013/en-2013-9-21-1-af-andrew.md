# To walk this Path, is to walk in true Life

September 21<sup>st</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

God bless you with a great inflowing of the Divine Essence into your souls; that your eyes may be opened and your awareness flourish, and the light of this Love. You are all given the gift of life, my children, to live in this world, to honour this gift in a loving, beautiful way.

And with the Divine Love you are given another life added to this gift of life: the life of God’s Essence living within your souls, bringing you deeper awarenesses, greater vision, a loving flow into your lives, and into this world. And as this Love accumulates within, so your souls emerge from their slumbers and express themselves in this world in a way that is meant for them to be: alive and dynamic, an expression of love and creativity and wisdom and joy.

God’s Love brings you life, my children, it brings renewed life, great joy, and peace. And this is why we ask again and again to pray for this Love with all your hearts, with all your souls, with all your desire; for it will open the door to greater life, greater vitality, and a vision of God’s Creation that is clear, an understanding of this true reality. Yes, the pathway to walk, to walk in light, to walk in peace, to walk guided and supported by the angels; to be a channel of love in this world, to bring what that brings - the changes, the harmony.

To walk this path, my children, is to walk in true life, in true honouring of this life that is a gift. It is a gift, and each soul is precious and beautiful, and each soul here is precious and beautiful. And soon you will come to truly love yourselves and understand how beautiful you are, how lovely is the gift of your life.

Blessings to you, my children, blessings and healing, and peace and love. For you are truly loved and cared for and guided. God bless you. I am Andrew and I love you. And I will journey with you as you cross the oceans, and you reach out to your brothers and sisters there. I will be with you, and many will be with you in this wondrous journey, in this beautiful task performed in accordance to the Will of God. Blessings to you. Love to you. Andrew loves you.
