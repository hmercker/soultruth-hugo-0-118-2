# Pray more intensely and more often for this Love

February 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God’s presence is with you, my children. Can you not feel the warmth of His love bathing you, enveloping you, flowing into your souls? Yes, you come together with this desire to be comforted and healed, to know peace and a greater understanding of that which your soul desires to know, the truth within. Your longings reach out to the Father. And in your prayers, there is great desire and depth of longing, and in the sincerity and in the power of this comes the blessing of the Father’s love flowing into you, flooding your life, your being, with this wonderful gift, this essence of God.

And as you beseech the Father with greater intensity, with greater longing, so the flow of love intensifies and the response more complete. And as this love flows into your souls, my children, so that which is within you that is not in harmony with this love shall be forced out, shall be expunged from you, so that you may know a great joy and inner peace that is unshakeable and deep and abiding, a powerful force within for love to flow and for you to know yourselves that place within you that is uniquely you, created by God, a beautiful, beautiful soul.

So, I urge you, my dear children, to pray more intensely and more often for this love, to long for this deep change within and it shall be so. And you will know a strength and an awareness. It shall be your guiding light. It shall lead you along the path divine and as you walk upon this path so you shall be a channel of love and light, healing, [?], and change in this world.

And yes, love is the answer to all of your questions. Love is the answer to all of your dilemmas. And love shall heal the pain within. And that place where you feel alone shall be filled with the presence of God. And you and the Creator shall have an unwavering and intense connection and knowing that shall last for all eternity, my children. There will be no doubts, there will be no pain, there will be no dilemmas. Only a surety, a knowing, and a peace.

This is God’s gift to you, my children. God desires for each soul upon this world, in all worlds, to know this gift, to feel this place within God’s universe - this place of peace and comfort, joy and understanding. It is yours for the asking and indeed it is your birthright to have this within you - this light to lead you forward, to bring healing into this world, my children, to bring love, to bring wisdom. This is your gift. And the Father beseeches you to walk forward in this light, to give yourselves over to this love, to be an instrument of peace and a channel of love.

God bless you, dear beloved souls. The love flows into you, the love that can be a constant [?] within for you, blesses you now. Recognize this love, long for this love. Be attuned to its beautiful flow and all the [...?] shall come to know this. This gift, this glorious gift shall be yours. Bless you my children, dear sweet souls. I love you. I am Augustine, your teacher. I love you.
