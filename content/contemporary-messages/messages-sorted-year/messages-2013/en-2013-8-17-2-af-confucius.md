# Be joyous, be sure, be courageous, and be humble

August 17<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Such beautiful souls! Many of you work tirelessly in the light and bring comfort and love to so many in this world. God is with you in your efforts, my children. The light flows through you and many of you are not aware of this wondrous flow of light and love, yet you persist, and you carry on with your efforts, and the light does flow, and there is comfort and change and love and harmony.

It matters not that you are aware of this, my children, it merely matters that you reach to the Heavenly Father and continue this connection, this loving flow within and through you. And all will be well, if you walk your path, as you live your lives. And many come to you for comfort. Many see your strengths and are drawn to your love. And this is what’s being channelled, God’s Love is, my children. Each connection, each touch to each dear lost soul, is a touch from God. And as you maintain this flow of love and light, God touches you and touches them, and the ripples of light flow through the many circles of your lives and bring healing and change and love.

Do not doubt this, my children, do not doubt yourselves, but know that you walk in light and you are beautiful and are loved so very deeply. And the channel of love shall intensify, and the light and the comfort will go out to many of the souls and in this, their souls are touched and the bell toll is rung for each one, that the desire for the Love manifest within them. And the journey begins for the lost soul into light, for the hungering soul into truth. Where there is deprivation there will be healing and abundance.

God works through you, my children. And we watch over you and guide you and protect you and love you. And God watches over all of us. And this simple, simple truth will change the world, my children, and bring peace. Step into the light. Be joyous, be sure, be courageous, and be humble. Confucius loves you and will always be with you, my children, my beautiful family. God bless you. Blessings to you.
