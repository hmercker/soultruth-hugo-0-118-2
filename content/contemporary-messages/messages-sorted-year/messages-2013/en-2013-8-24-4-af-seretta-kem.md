# So much hinges upon your Efforts

August 24<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

...I am very pleased to see that you have acted upon the guidance that I have given you, and you take this seriously. I would encourage all of you to make efforts to cleanse your bodies, to strengthen your bodies. For as these transitions take place and this work begins in earnest, you are required to be strong within your physical body, for we will be asking much of you, in travel, in efforts around the world. And this is not an easy task. For your minds to be clear and your souls to be bright with the Love we await your preparations and we support you in your preparations and we encourage you to continue as you have, in your prayers, in your diets, in your thoughts.

So much hinges upon your efforts, your preparations, and your willingness to be in that place of harmony and strength and clarity and light. Be assured that when you ask for help, help will be given; when you require healing, healing will be given. When you pray for the Love, it will pour upon you in abundance. A great effort is being made for each one here and those not present that you will walk in light as strongly and powerfully as is possible in this world.

And yes, it has taken, for some here, many years to be at this place of readiness. But those years have not been wasted, my children. You have gained much wisdom and strength and understanding. And you have great compassion, for you all have struggled to find the Love, to live in the Love, to be the Love. And these struggles and these experiences in your life have strengthened you and brought you wisdom and love and understanding, and this is required in your ministrations in this world. You are required to be equipped with this strength and love.

And your gifts are opening, my children, and they shall open more rapidly now as we work and bring about the conditions internally and as a group to open these awarenesses and these gifts within you, for these are required as well for this work. And I see within each one a willingness and a dedication to step forward and gift your life to God in this way. And God smiles upon you, my children, and brings to you many, many, many blessings. You will walk in a mighty flow of love and healing and truth.

God bless you for your efforts, your dedication, your humility, your love. Seretta Kem loves you and I see great possibilities here amongst you, and those who are with you in your spiritual family. A great power to make change in this world, and a great ability to work with the changes that are coming, to save many souls, to rescue them from their darkness. You will not fail. And you have, in this time in your life, saved many souls. And you will continue. God bless you. God bless you. I love you.
