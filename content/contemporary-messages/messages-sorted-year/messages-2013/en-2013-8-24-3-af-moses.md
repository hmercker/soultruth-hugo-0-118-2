# Live in Love, Compassion, Sensitivity, Awareness

August 24<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Moses received by Al Fike

God bless you. God bless you with God’s great, mighty Divine Love. May your souls open wide to receive this great blessing, my children, for it will redeem you and fill you with joy and insight and understanding of God’s presence and being.

You have had a conversation upon the rock, my children, about the coming changes in the world. And I tell you that too long the world has been burdened by the sins and errors of mankind, which have abused this beautiful planet and brought imbalance and inharmony. And this imbalance and inharmony have removed many of God’s creations upon this planet. And the planet suffers. And yes, there will be many changes to come, as the forces within this Earth shake off these burdens, this imbalance. For in God’s creation balance must be obtained. And yes, the pendulum has swung very far in one direction and must return to the middle.

And within this correction there will be cataclysms and chaos and great difficulties for all of you who live upon this planet. And I tell you, my children, to be close to the Creator, to be in the Love, to receive the Love, to listen to your guidance; for this will not only benefit you in these times to come, but many as you become a channel of wisdom and strength and love and guidance for many.

The world is full of change, my children; nothing ever stays in stasis. There is always a movement. And you, my children, must learn to adapt and have faith that this momentum forward will bring peace and harmony back to this world, to your lives, to the many, many souls upon this world. And this is required, and this is blessed by God. And there will be peace, there will be peace, and the completion of this transition.

And this is another reason for you to make your travels to this far off land. And we will give guidance regarding these transitions, and we will reassure the children in this part of your world, and they will be compelled to listen to God, to listen to truth, to listen to their hearts, their souls. And this is what is required of all of you, my children, to listen to those inner promptings, that knowing, so that you may, your footfalls, may be in harmony with the laws of creation.

So much for you to know, my children, so much wisdom and knowledge is required so that you may walk in harmony in this world, and a channel of God’s Love in this world, and to give forth the truths. And that is what is required of the children of this world: to understand that they may no longer live their lives with impunity; that there is a harmony and a rhythm and a purpose to this life; that they may open their ears and their eyes and see that their lives lived in blindness and ignorance and self-centeredness cannot continue; that they are meant to live in love, compassion, sensitivity, awareness - of themselves, of their brothers and sisters; and to acknowledge the beauty of creation, not to ignore this wondrous creation which is this world.

And for those who persist to live in this ignorance and blindness there will be great suffering, my children, for they will not come to this awareness that will lead them through the chaos and the cataclysms, and the collapse of the many structures made by man.

And for those of you willing to understand that which is made by God and sanctioned by the Creator, you will find your way, you will be safe, you will be guided, and you will guide many as you walk forward. And you will not fear but will be joyed by the changes. Surprising in their nature, glorious in their manifestations, beautiful as God’s Love pours, and healing pours upon this planet and washes away the inharmonious creations of man.

I give you this gift, my children, to reassure, not to bring fear. I want you to know that God will protect and guide you always, as will we in spirit bring you what you require; your needs will be met. There are indeed adjustments required. And for some these will be more painful than for others; but nonetheless, one will support the other - there will be love amongst you, and reassurance, and the path will be lit. You are all beautiful children of God’s Love. Beautiful.

God bless you. I am Moses. I have come to be with you in your prayers, in your efforts to reach God, to be in harmony with God. Walk in the light, my children. Do not falter. Pray earnestly for God’s Love to fill your souls and fill your consciousness with His Will and your purpose, each one, unique and beautiful. God bless you. Moses loves you.
