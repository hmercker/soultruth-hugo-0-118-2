# Put away your Fears and your Biases

October 4<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

It is Faith here. My elder brother Jesus has spoken to you. And those of you who know me, listen to these words, for he speaks the truth. Put away your fears and your biases, and that which you think you hold true to yourselves and be as children listening to this message of love, and you will find redemption and freedom in that; you will find light and joy. This simple truth is powerful - the greatest blessing that can be given to any soul.

Follow Jesus and his light. He leads you into redemption and love. There is nothing to fear and everything to gain, to allow this Love to come into your souls. Its magnificent healing power will bring to you everything you need; all that is required is yours with this gift. God bless you and thank you for coming. I am so happy to be here amongst you and I want to tell you there are many, many angels surrounding us, a great light here. There has been a great light here for many years, and this light is enhanced by your (?) and your being here in loving acceptance and communion.

Thank you, my friends, for being willing to step into this light and grow in this Love. It will clear the way to great, great understanding and wisdom, and freedom from all your worries and cares, your pains and your burdens. Be at peace and know that God above cares for you deeply and will lead you into light, light everlasting, loving joy, redemptive grace.

God bless you. I am Faith and I love you. And I love all of you who struggle for the light, who long for truth. It will come. It will come in drops of rain and mighty rivers, in beams of sunlight and rushes of ecstasy, and it will come for all eternity. God bless you and good night.
