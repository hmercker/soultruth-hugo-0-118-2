# Continue in your Efforts to seek Truth, to truly understand your true Nature

September 21<sup>st</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

You have struggled greatly, my son. A great battle of the physical. But within these struggles you have wiped away many, many errors and misunderstandings and grievances within, and you have come to know the hand of God upon you, my son, for the healing has been bestowed upon you and will continue to be bestowed upon you, my dear, beloved child.

Continue in your efforts to seek truth, to truly understand your true nature, to seek peace and clarity and love. Your dear brother has much to impart to you, my son. Listen to his words. They are simple but true, and he loves you dearly, as do all those who surround you at this time love you dearly, and this love carries you and gives you healing and comfort. And my daughter, you have performed a great service to your brother. You have carried him steadfastly through this ordeal, and you are blessed with your love and giving, so God blesses you as you care so deeply for those you love.

And your dear mother is blessed as well. And her sister is with her often and brings great comfort and love to her. And she will know peace when she comes to our side of life. She will know peace, my daughter; be reassured, though she fears this step it will be in harmony and love and light and joy. There is nothing to fear. Only a step in life from one place to another, one room to another. That is all it is, my children.

So many in this world fear the passing into spirit for they do not understand what this is. And I tell you the transition is not that great as the mortal coil is pulled away from the spirit, there is a great freedom and release from many burdens that this Earth plane and these conditions carry. And your dear mother will know this freedom and will be joyed as her loved ones surround her and show her the way to her new life. And this life upon this world is fleeting and short, but it is powerful and dynamic. As you seek to grow in your spiritual natures, in your souls, much can be accomplished here, my children. So much that is difficult while on the other side of life if there is ignorance. Here you may learn much and grow greatly within your souls and this will determine your path for a great long while.

And if you choose the Divine Path, my children, this path will go for all eternity, as you find your at-onement with God, for His Love; the power of this Love brings a powerful change within and the ability to become at one with God. And I tell you, each of you here has received this Love, and I encourage you to acknowledge this and to pursue this and to pray for more of this Love, ever more, for you will never receive enough. This will be an eternal pursuit, a magnificent journey into light, at-onement, glorious, glorious Love.

And I tell you that God’s angels will uplift you and lead the way on this path, and I shall be there with you, my children, I am Confucius. And I give you my blessing. And I shall be there in the gathering with you and your efforts and your love. My dear daughter____, your mother and your sister are enwrapped by the Father’s care and healing pours upon them. You are not to worry, my daughter. Continue in your prayers for their healing. All of you, my children, continue in your prayers for the healing for your loved ones, the healing for yourselves, and the opening of your awarenesses of God’s glorious Love and presence within your lives.

There is so much for you to know, to be aware, to understand, and to acknowledge. Does not God open many doors for you, my children? Is there not great care given, and Love bestowed, and peace? It is there for the asking; it is there as you step forward in faith and beseech the Father to carry you in the arms of love and light. And yes, you will be carried; you are carried. I love you so dearly, my children. Such precious souls. Pearls beyond price. Confucius loves you. I am always with you.
