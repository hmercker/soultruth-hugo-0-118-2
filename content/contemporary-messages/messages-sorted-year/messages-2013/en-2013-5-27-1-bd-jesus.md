# You are deeply needed in this World

May 27<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

You are precious and you are deeply needed in this world to bring the Love to the hungry from our Holy Father. It is so deeply needed, and you my beloveds are channels of this Love and you must give this gift to all the souls you touch and you will help to bring peace and Love and health and happiness to all life in your world. There are many challenges that face you and each of you has a gift of the Divine Love to use to heal this trouble. Know this, my beloveds, feel the Love that flows and touches each of you and know this, precious ones. I am Jesus. I will always uphold you.
