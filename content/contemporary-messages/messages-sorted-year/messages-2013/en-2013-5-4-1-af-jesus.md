# Each of you has a Part to play

May 4<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my dear beloved children, workers for light, channels of love, guardians of truth. I am Jesus. And I come to be with you and to encourage you in your efforts, your efforts to bring the gathering together in the place far away; to encourage you not to have fears or doubts, to not place any obstruction in the way of this effort, for there is a mighty effort afoot to bring this gathering together, to establish this great channel of light in that part of the world that is so in need of this blessing; that the refreshing and healing and cooling waters of the Father’s Love may flow in this place for these souls who are eager and are thirsty and are in need of this wonderful connection and family.

Each of you plays a part, my children, each of you has a part to play. Do not doubt this. For some of you do doubt, and are fearful, and place the thoughts within your minds of that which must be. And there is no need for this, my children, for as you follow the Will of God, as the Heavenly Father carries you upon the flow of Love in the world, what is required is faith and acceptance and a joyfulness that you have laid down that part of you that resists and fears and doubts and allowed the Will of God to flow through you. There is no need for anxiety. There is no need to worry about the material. What is required is your acceptance, your love, your inclusiveness, and your creativity and your gifts.

You each have so very much to contribute to this great effort, and for the many efforts to come, my children, for there are many to come. So the door is open for each one. What is required is for you to cross the threshold, to walk in the light joyfully, accepting the gift of these opportunities laid before you, and acknowledging that each one here is a channel of God’s Love. And each one here has wonderful gifts to contribute, that God’s Love may be expressed in these very many ways that are inherent in each beautiful soul around this circle.

And I will be there, my children, with you. There will be a great healing and new awarenesses will open, and error will fall, and unity will build amongst you. A strength and a surety and an awareness that you are all claimed by the Father, and all are committed to do this work that you have come into the world to do. There is no need to resist, there is just your ability to let go of that which causes you to resist, to walk in the light with faith and peace and joy; to think of the other’s needs before yourself. To be a clear and beautiful channel of the Father’s Love in this world, the Love that shall heal and demolish the barriers, that shall expunge the darkness and bring a simple truth to the consciousness of mankind.

This is your blessing and your gift and your calling, to be a channel in this way. And we in spirit will provide all that is necessary for you to be a channel in this way. Your fears and your resistance will inhibit this; your gracious acknowledgement that the Will of God will allow this. And the choice is always yours: to be willing or not, to be joyful or not, to love or not. It is your choice. And we are joyed when you make the choice for light, when you acknowledge the Will of God, when you step upon the path that is meant for you, each one unique, beautiful, but converging into a unity and a singleness of purpose that shall carry you far in the world and save many souls from their darkened, painful existence to bring a liberation from darkness to light as God uses you as His channel in this world.

I am so very pleased at your efforts and how you overcome your barriers and resolve your doubts and bring harmony amongst you and create a beautiful, beautiful construct of how this channel will be in the world. We are all working to build this channel together. We are all contributing in our own ways. And yes, we need you and your efforts and your abilities to bring this channel of light in a more tangible way into the world. And yes, you need us to help you clear away the barriers to this. And we all need our Heavenly Father, to pour His Love, his living waters upon all of these efforts, to bring the change and the healing and the light.

And God knows you, my children, knows your needs, sees and anticipates your needs before you know them. You are cared for. You are nurtured. You are loved. You are provided for. And this shall be till the end of your days and beyond. Bless you, my children, bless you for acknowledging the Love of God, the grace of God, the care of God, the power of God’s wondrous healing flow of Love. It shall carry you forward, and you are going forward in great paces, in great leaps. And you shall continue to do so. And there is nothing that can hold you back once you are committed to this flow and acknowledge this flow and allow this flow.

Blessings to you, my children, my beautiful brothers and sisters in love, my disciples. Blessings to you. And we shall all move upon the path in the flow of God’s Love together. Nothing to fear. Great joy to gain. Peace be with you, my children, the peace that passes all understanding, as the Father is with you, with us all, the acknowledgement of your light and your beautiful beings. God bless you. I am Jesus. God bless you.
