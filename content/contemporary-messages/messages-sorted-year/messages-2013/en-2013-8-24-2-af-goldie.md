# Many Opportunities for Joy and Service and Love

August 24<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Al Fike

It’s Goldie! I love you. I love you all. And you should be happy! Because God has favoured you so. Your lives are such blessings, filled with so many, many opportunities for joy and service and love! I love you. Goldie loves you. And I want you to know not to be so serious all the time! It’s fun to laugh, you know.
