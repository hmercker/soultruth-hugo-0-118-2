# It’s time to dance the dance of joy

April 20<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Al Fike

It’s time to let go of your worries, my children, all these burdens that you carry. I am Goldie and I love you. It’s time to dance the dance of joy, of life, the gift that God has given you, my children, to let go of all these worries, and these burdens; so much is from your fear, and of your own making. Be at peace. Know that God is looking after all of you. You are cared for and nurtured and protected. There is nothing to worry about! Be at peace. Be joyful. And God’s Love does flood your souls - oh to the very depths. So much is given to you, my children, so very much. Be at peace. Know you are loved. There are no dilemmas that God cannot solve and there is no barrier that your faith in God cannot overcome. Be at peace. Bless you, my dear friends. I am Goldie and I love you.
