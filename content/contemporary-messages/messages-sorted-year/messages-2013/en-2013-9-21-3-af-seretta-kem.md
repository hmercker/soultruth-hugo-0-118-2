# Think Thoughts that are positive and godly and loving

September 21<sup>st</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

Seretta Kem here. The coming days before you set off for your journey are very important, my children, for your preparation. These days are crucial, that you continue in your prayers, that you continue to think thoughts that are positive and godly and loving; that you continue in your diets, that you put your thoughts forward towards the coming events, and those who are to participate with you in this gathering.

I ask you to send love to each soul, though you may not know them, you know them by name and name them within your prayers, and ask that the cloak of protection be put around them, that an angel be present with them at all times, that they come together with you and you come together with them in a joyous light and acknowledgment of the gift that is this gathering. And we will continue to prepare you. And you will feel the sensations of this preparation, and you will know the Love pouring in and you will be supported with your thoughts and your prayers.

Continue and do not lose sight of these goals and these efforts and this preparation. It is very important that you continue and not to be distracted; to be focussed and prayerful. And be joyed, for God will touch each of you deeply there as you pray together and acknowledge your brothers and sisters from far off places. And in this gathering, you will carry a part of one another away with you, and there will be a bond of love that will be enduring, will endure for all eternity, my children. God bless you.

And my son I have been present in your healings, with this instrument and the other instrument. For prayers have been said for you, my son, and we in the Celestial Heavens have been asked to minister to you, my son, and we have done so, and you have received great healing. And indeed now you have a choice, to continue on a path of light, to nourish your body in ways that will keep you in strength and health, to nourish your soul in ways that will allow you to release all conditions of the negative, and to acknowledge that life is a beautiful, beautiful gift; and you will carry this gift for many years to come, my son, as you walk this path of light.

Open your eyes and see the truth. Open your soul and acknowledge God’s Love. Release your fears and know you are loved - truly loved - and all will be well. Blessings to all of you, dear souls. And yes, I ask you all to open your eyes to the truth, open your souls to the Love, walk the path of light. And the journey will be magnificent, and filled with many, many surprising and beautiful opportunities of service and love and joy. Bless you. I am Seretta Kem and I love you.
