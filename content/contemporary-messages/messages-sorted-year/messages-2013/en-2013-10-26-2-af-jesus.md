# Do not place Restrictions with your Mind upon what is possible

October 26<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, precious children, I have come to be with you. It is Jesus and I love you.

Do you not see that you have done my work, my children, in that far off place? You have brought the light of God’s Love to a place starving for this gift, yearning for change and truth and redemption within. And you have brought with pure hearts and willing souls your message, the message of truth, the flow of Love to that place over the seas, and the healing.

And yes, for some you have seen the disparaging conditions, the confusion, and the deep need with the dear souls who come to gather and drink the waters, the healing waters of God’s Love. And this, my children, you will encounter time and time again as you venture out into this world and you bring this message of truth to the hungering children. And God in His wisdom has set you upon the course to bring this truth to the hungering souls and we travel with you, surrounding you, surrounding them in this healing act of Love.

Yes, my children, there are many levels of perception and understanding and need and desire. And as you venture forth as a channel of God’s Love to the many places of this world where God will lead you, you will find this. You will find these challenges and these challenges will be met in prayer, in your desire to be a pure channel of Love and in letting go of your judgments and your fears to walk in faith and strength and to be a harbinger of peace and truth.

Yes, we ask much of you, my children, so much, yet you are supported and loved, nurtured and taught, given what is required to do this work. To do my work, to do God’s Will and you are ready for these efforts to unfold in the harmony that will be God’s Will, to flow in the river that is God’s Love. The unfoldment of this plan, this work will be beautiful and will change this world, my children. It will. It must. It has every possibility to do so. If you are willing to work in collaboration with God’s Will and our efforts and your efforts. In this is a great power to change, to bring healing and love and truth.

And yes, you embark upon this wondrous journey, this unfoldment of healing Love in the world and those events we have spoken of to you many years back are now unfolding. The time is now. Do not place restrictions with your mind upon what is possible and what will unfold in this light. Be willing, be loving and accepting. And you will not be in jeopardy. The negative forces will not turn against you. The light will flow to dissipate the darkness. Oh, beloved children, do you see what’s laid before you? What gifts are given? What opportunities are placed in your path?

Be joyed, be joyed that you have been given so much, so freely. And as you ask to be used to channel the Love, more will be given, much more and great journeys taken, and many souls touched in deep ways. And it matters not that their minds do not comprehend the depth of this touch, the simplicity of this truth, the wonderment that is the miracle of God’s touch of Love within the soul, it matters that this touch has been given, it matters that the soul was stirred.

And the understanding will come, my children. As you make your efforts to teach, as you teach these simple truths, again and again, for they must be heard many times to be accepted. But I tell you the touch, the experience of the Love, will never be forgotten and will plant a seed of great change within, and this is your purpose, this is what you have come into this world to do, my children. Yes, you are the gardeners, you till the soil, you plant the seeds and God will provide the warmth of His Love, the waters of His Love and you will till the soils and bring beauty into this world.

Oh, my beloveds, my beloveds walk the path of Love and we will guide you and show you the way. There is nothing to fear and great wisdom is within each one of you and with this wisdom and your faith and your love you will know the next steps as you are guided along the path.

God bless you, we love you so and we are so happy with the efforts you have made. The great efforts in love, the sacrifices you have done in your love of God and your love for your fellow man. God bless you. I love you. I am Jesus. I love you so, precious to me, precious to this world, my beautiful souls.
