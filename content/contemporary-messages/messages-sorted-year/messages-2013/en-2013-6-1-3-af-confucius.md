# In Humility comes Harmony and Grace and Peace

June 1<sup>st</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children, it is Confucius. Welcome to this gathering of light and love.

When you declare a truth in the world, my children, there will be a response, and often that response is not what you wish from another, but it is nonetheless a response and reaction that indicates that there has been an effect upon that soul, and this is good, my children. For without a response, this indicates a lack of understanding or attempt to understand a truth.

So, I urge you, my children, to speak truth in the world, but do not expect that all will respond in loving ways to your truth, your understanding of truth. This is not the way of the world. For a truth will make a disturbance within the soul; it elicits the soul to open to a greater understanding, and this, for many, is painful and difficult, causes ripples of discomfort; and this cannot be avoided, for to clear away the barriers and the encrustations of error within requires some power, the power of love, the power of truth.

And for many it is human to resist this effort and to respond often in a negative way. For to shift from error to truth is provocative and difficult. Yet, my children, have you not prayed for the Love to enter your souls? Have you not prayed to be a channel of God’s Love in the world? And in that there is truth. This comes with the Love and there will be responses from others. And you must be strong, my children, steadfast in what you know to be true; and it is good to acknowledge with humility that none of you here have all the truth, for none of us in spirit, in the Celestial Heavens, have all of the truth, have the great understanding of God’s creation in all its entirety.

Yes, you begin your journey, so your understanding of truth is somewhat limited, but it is powerful nonetheless in this world, for very little truth exists in the consciousness of mankind, and very great error does exist. And indeed it is your challenge to bring truth, to eradicate error, to have integrity, to carry the truth that you know wherever you go, and to live this truth as fully as you are able, and to be a channel of love wherever possible.

Yes, there are great challenges in this world, my children, great challenges, and tests and trials and tribulations. For as you overcome these barriers to truth and the barriers within yourselves and the fears of rejection and the hurts and pains that you have carried many years within, it becomes, at times, a great struggle. But God is always with you, my children, as are we in spirit, to uphold you, to show you the way; and yes, you may bend but you will not break. You will be given challenges, to be sure, but these challenges are opportunities for your growth, and the growth of others, to strengthen you.

As the steel is tempered in the fire, so shall you be tempered in the fire of those who would reject and cause obstruction to your efforts. But I tell you, my children, you will pass through these fires, you will pass the tests before you, you will come to a greater understanding of truth and be a clearer channel of love in the world as you accept these challenges in faith and are compassionate to those who would resist your efforts.

Have humility, my children, for in humility comes harmony and grace and peace, yet peace that’s fast with the truth. Do not compromise. Do not enter a negative condition without an attempt to purify this condition. Do not entertain darkness. Walk the path of light. Choose light. Choose truth. Choose love wherever you go, whatever situation you may encounter, for there are choices with every breath you take, every thought that you make. There are choices.

And with these steps we are with you, in love and support, guidance and protection. And God’s hand is upon you, my children. Do you not see this? Do you not feel this? Do you not know this from deep within?

Blessings to you. Blessings to all whom you love. Blessings to this world that longs for truth yet is fearful of its effects. Blessings, my children. Confucius loves you, loves you dearly. And takes great joy in your struggles (laughs). For as you struggle you move forward. And you are worthy of the challenge, and strong. Blessings to you.
