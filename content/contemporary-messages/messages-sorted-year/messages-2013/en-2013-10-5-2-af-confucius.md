# Seek the Love of God with every Breath, and you will know the Glory of Flight

October 5<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Confucius received by Al Fike

My children, it is Confucius. Yes, I reside in the Celestial Heavens, my children. And I love you and I bring my love and healing to you, my children. And yes, my little birds, you flock together, as do birds of a feather flock together (laughs). And you all have beautiful souls, longing for the Father’s Love; and yes, that Love pours into your souls in great abundance, my little birds, so that you may fly higher, closer to God, and know the joy of flight and Love, Love Divine, filling you and making you ever lighter and more beautiful, that your feathers may shine in rainbow colours and your souls may glisten in loving light.

Seek the Love of God, my children, seek the Love of God with every breath, and you will know the glory of flight, you will know the Heavens. Be joyed at what God has created, and you will know the beauty that God has created that is your soul, your being, unique and beautiful. So much of yourselves you do not know but shall know. In your flying, in your pursuit of truth, you will know, from deep within, the wonderment of God’s creation, the glory that is God. Long for this Love. Feel that longing, my children. Bring that longing forth, and your wings shall spread and you shall take flight in heavens of light.

Yes, this old spirit from China honours you for your efforts and your loving, loving tributes to God. I love you, and I am with you in your prayers, in your desire for Love. God bless you. Confucius loves you.
