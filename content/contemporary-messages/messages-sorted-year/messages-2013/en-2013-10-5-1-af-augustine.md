# Love will be the Currency of Mankind

October 5<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, dear sweet souls. It is Augustine. I was Bishop of Hippo while on Earth. And I am so very glad to see all of you gathered here in this sanctuary, in this house of light. And I want you to know that you are creating a great light here in your prayers in conjunction with our prayers and God’s loving attention upon all of us; there is a light being created in this place, at these times. And the purpose of this light is to bring change into this world. The purpose of this light is for the power of God’s Love to manifest in this world in ways that will have great benefit to mankind in the near future.

For your world, and all of your brothers and sisters upon this world, are undergoing great stresses and difficulties and disharmonies, and there is a need for healing on many levels for this planet and for all who dwell upon her. Mankind for too long has taken what he wants and not honoured the creation of God, not given in love back in a way that would create balance and harmony.

There is so much that you need to know, my children, and mankind operates from a level of knowledge that is inadequate to deal with the disharmonies that he is creating. And this is a dangerous situation - destructive, bringing great harm to many creatures and creations and elements of this beautiful Earth. And so, I ask you, my children, to keep in your thoughtful awareness what may be done to heal your home, this creation, this blessing of God; to direct your prayers to the Heavenly Father to bring healing and knowledge and love to your brothers and sisters, and to this place.

And as you pray together for God’s Love in this sanctuary, you are creating conditions that will help to heal this planet and bring greater awareness and love to mankind. There are many things that can manifest through your prayers and your loving harmony together in this place that will assist and circumvent destructive ways. There is great ignorance, my friends, great ignorance to overcome, and blindness, and an inability to love.

Yes, the work is great, the challenges so very difficult. But God’s Love is the most powerful energy in the universe, my children. It will overcome all barriers, heal all error, bring all balance. And you are praying for this Love to enter your souls, and in this you become agents of change in the world. Your very presence in the world will bring change. And I say it is not so much what you speak, or what you think, it is in who you are and the Love that you carry and the Love that flows through you from the Heavenly Father that will make the greatest impact, the most beautiful touch to those who require the awakening of their souls and their consciousness, their awareness; to bring love into their lives in every way, for this is what is required to make the changes necessary to bring love in every way to this world. Can you do this, my children? Can you pray to be an instrument of God’s Will, a channel of God’s Love? To lay down your fears and your prejudices? To allow God to work through you?

And I tell you, my friends, this will bring great change, you will be guided, you will touch many souls, and in turn, they will touch many souls, and this flame of truth and love and healing and light will spread one to the other to the other. And it is for you to start the flame, for God to bless you with this flame, and for you to touch another soul. It starts with your soul, my children, your beautiful souls who desire to be close to God, in harmony with these laws of love, and an instrument of peace. Can you do this, my children? Can you follow this path in willing commitment, in loving and honouring of God’s will and honouring the blessing that is your life?

Yes, the challenges are great, but the power of love is beyond your understanding; but you will understand, stepping along the Divine Path. Go ever closer to God, ever closer to the Divine Will, and your eyes will open, and your understanding will magnify, and your perceptions will grow from the soul, and that part of you within, that eagerly awaits your acceptance of its existence, its life, your soul, that part will emerge into your consciousness in beautiful ways, in piercing awareness, in loving action; and you will know, my children, you will know the steps, you will understand the purpose that has been imbued within each soul here and every soul in creation has been given a gift of a purpose, a part to play in the beautiful flow of creation. And that purpose will bring fulfillment and joy, and God will acknowledge your efforts to bring light into this world. And you will know great peace. You will know great peace.

Within this room, within your souls, lay the potential to change the world, my children, if you are willing, if you desire this, if you pray for the Love to change your world and unleash the magnificent gifts and abilities that lie within you, my children. For what you are aware of is but a speck of what is possible within. Great understanding will come. Deep insights and love will manifest through you. And you will affect, in wondrous ways, changes in this world that are required to bring harmony and balance and healing.

Together we build the light to envelop the planet, a network of light that will bring change and a higher level of consciousness for all. So, I present to you, my children, the invitation to walk upon this path and listen, listen with the ears of your souls, the desire of your souls, the love within your souls, and this will come to pass, and you will help to bring the salvation of mankind to fruition.

Beautiful souls, listen to my words, understand their meaning, pray for the changes within and without, pray for the Love to bring grace into your life, understanding to your awareness, love to your hearts. And we will all walk together to a bright future, to a place where love is more important than the material. Love will be the currency of mankind. Love will permeate everything. And there will be peace, great peace, so that your children and their children and their children will live a life of joy, fulfilling and beautiful. Is this not worth your efforts, to bring these gifts to these unborn children and those born? Is this not worth your prayers? Is this not worth your commitment to lay your will aside and accept the will of God? I tell you the rewards will be beyond your imagining, and the gifts given will flow and envelop this world.

I have placed my calling before you, my call to act in love, and I desire your response and prayerful beseeching of the Father for Love. Ask with every breath, with every breath, that God’s Love flows into your souls and brings to you what you require to fulfill your purpose, to bring this Love to all mankind. God bless you, precious souls, souls filled with the promise of new life, healing, and wisdom. God bless you. I am Augustine and I love you.
