# All you need do is ask, and receive

August 17<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children. Yes, you come from near and far, and you live your lives in diverse ways, and each has their perspective, and their vision, and their understanding of truth. And in this you are all beautiful, my children, in this you all long for the light, in this your souls long for the Love, the Divine Essence of the Heavenly Father, to fill you. And though there may be differences, there is a unity within the soul of each one here, as you all pray and open to the Father for His blessings, His Love, His peace, His grace. And many of you are great channels of love in the world and agents of change, and you do your work diligently. And the Heavenly Father is aware of your efforts, the love that you bring to this world, the changes that you make with each beautiful soul that you encounter, and this is beautiful, my children.

You are beautiful, each one here; a channel of love. You walk in light. You struggle for light and harmony and love. And I tell you what is in your mind, and the perspective of your intellect, does not affect greatly your souls; for the soul has its own consciousness, my children, its own desires, its own path. And in that you have been drawn to this family, this circle, to be replenished, to acknowledge the desires of your souls, to walk in this light of Love, to be together for these moments of replenishment of Love.

Strive to acknowledge this consciousness of the soul, my children; as you receive more of the Divine Essence within, this awareness, and this consciousness will flourish, and you will know from deep within that you are loved, and you will see from different eyes the beauty that is you in your creation, unique and glorious, beautiful and powerful, lovely and loving.

Pray that the Father will use you as a channel of His Love in the world. Strive to know the will of God, and you will be used to make great change, beautiful healing, harmony; and you will also confront that which is not in harmony with God’s creation, that which is not loving, that which is made by man but not sanctioned by God. These confrontations are necessary to make change, to bring harmony; and you will know within what is right. You will see from your inner eyes the right path. And God will guide you and bring you to a place of deep wisdom and understanding and love.

Bless you, my children, beautiful souls, beautiful souls. And some of you carry the burdens of grief, the burdens that come from living this life. And I tell you the Love will lift your burdens and heal you and bring you comfort. Pray for this Love to enter your souls, my children. Pray, and pray, and long for this, for this is the way out of these dilemmas, these pains, this darkness, this burden. You will be led. You will be nurtured. You will be cared for by the hand of God. All you need do is ask, and receive, and allow the Father to work through you as a clear channel, a mighty channel, His love into the world.

Yes, there are great changes coming, and each one of you has a purpose in this transition, in the course that must take place in the history of mankind. And again, you will be shown the way and given the answers, and the burdens will be lifted, and the wisdom will be given, and the guidance will flow freely to you, each one, if you but listen, if you but desire, if you but receive this Love. And many of you will travel through the world and ignite the changes and bring the wisdom, the truth. Be willing, my children, be eager.

God bless you. I am Augustine. I love you. This beautiful family of love sits in light, and God’s Love pours upon you. Drink deep, my children, drink deep. God Bless you.
