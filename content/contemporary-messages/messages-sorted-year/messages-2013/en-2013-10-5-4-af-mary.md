# Be charitable, be kind, be loving

October 5<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Mary received by Al Fike

Mary loves you, my family of love. God is with you, my children, God is with you. As you walk upon this Earth and you feel love, and nurture your fellow man, God is with you. As you carry the Love with you, God is with you. As you seek those opportunities to be a channel of love in this world, God is with you. As you smile to your neighbour and embrace your child, as you feed the hungry, as you give unceasingly to others, God is with you. Be a channel of love, my children, for as you give love in this way, God is with you, and God shall put within you His Love in ever increasing abundance, and your life will be filled with love, your life will be within the river of love.

Give unceasingly, give willingly. Give, my children. For the world hungers so, in so many ways; in so many ways you may give, in so many ways there is a need, and in this humble giving you touch the souls of others, you give a message of love and hope to others. Be charitable, be kind, be loving. Walk with truth and integrity and honesty. Walk in harmony with the laws of love and your life will be harmonious and beautiful. Do not resist, do not recoil in fear, in self-deprecation, but walk in love and joy, knowing that you are loved.

There are many opportunities in each of your lives, my children, to be a channel of love, to feed the hungering souls. God will guide you and show you and clear the path for you to give in this way. And as you give, you will make change in this world and bring light and healing into this world that hungers and thirsts for this. And this will bring you joy for all eternity, my children. As you serve in this way, so your soul will rejoice and know the touch of God, deeply and fully.

I am Mary, and I watch over you, my children. I have great love for each and every soul in this room. Beautiful lights who hunger and are fed in love, the bread of God. Continue in your prayers and be with God and walk with God and know God. Bless you, precious souls. Bless you in your courage, in your efforts to reach for light, to reject the darkness, to walk the path of love. Bless you. God bless you.
