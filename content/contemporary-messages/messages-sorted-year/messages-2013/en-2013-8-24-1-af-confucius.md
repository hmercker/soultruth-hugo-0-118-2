# Know the Will of God

August 24<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Do not fret, my children. Be reassured that you walk in the shadow of God’s Love, that you are carried by the angels.

And yes, these are serious times in the world, so burdened by the will of mankind. Your brothers and sisters ignore the Will of God to such a great degree, and your purpose, my children, we beseech you, to know the Will of God, and to express this Will in every breath as you walk upon this planet. Be strong, be with the Love, know the Love, that it may flow through you in great abundance and infuse every aspect of your life and your being. And in this all dilemmas that you may face in the coming years will be resolved, and truth will manifest. Truth will manifest with the Love.

Continue in your efforts, my children. Continue to speak to one another, to share your thoughts and your burdens, your joys and your experiences, as you walk through your life that is blessed by God, a gift from God. And you will know great joy and release from your pain, great wisdom and accumulation of knowledge that shall come through your relationship with God and your knowing of your own soul.

You are on the threshold, my children, of great work, great work. And the angels rejoice. And God smiles upon you. And the Master leads you. Blessings to you, my children. Confucius loves you, and is always present. And yes, when you had your conversation upon the hill I was there. And the animal was a gift to you, my daughter. For you must know, when you ask God with a pure heart your prayers will be answered, and the world responds in harmony. Beautiful souls. Redouble your efforts in prayer. Continue in your support of one another. And know that God and His angels are ever-present, ever-present. God bless you.
