# Blessed are you in your Fellowship

April 6<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Bless you on your journey back to your home. In the time to come there will be an opening of faculties of the soul, gifts revealed and flowering within you. And at times it will require adjustment and there will be some confusion. But know that we are with you always, my child, and God has placed His gifts within you for a purpose, to be used for the benefit of your brothers and sisters. And know that your brothers and sisters that surround you now will lend support in these transitioning times within you, that you may rely on their communications, and their love, and their insights into your growth and expansion of your soul.

Much awaits you, my daughter, much awaits. And much awaits all of you, my children, as you receive the Love, as you walk this path and you allow that which God has placed within you to flourish and emerge to your consciousness as the Love brings life to that which is within you.

This is a wondrous journey of discovery, surprising and beautiful and glorious. So much awaits all of you as you step upon the path, as you allow that part of you that is afraid to be healed with the Love, and you trust, you trust in your Father’s guidance, as you reach out to God and He guides you along the path, so will come the insights, the perceptions, the wisdom of the soul.

Blessed are you in your fellowship, your support and love of one another, that you may share your unique gifts and perceptions and understandings and experiences to strengthen your steps and give you greater understanding and faith and love. Precious children of the Father’s Love, I love you, I am with you often. I am Jesus. And I am your brother. Your days will be filled with wonderment. You will come to know God intimately, powerfully, and this relationship will open the truth to each one. Walk the path with me and I will light the way, and I will uphold you and never leave. Blessed children, blessed brothers, blessed sisters, I love you. Peace.
