# Will you bathe with me in God’s Love?

October 5<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you. We are now in an ocean of God’s Love, my children.

Will you bathe with me? Shall we swim within this ocean of Love? For too long mankind has walked in the desert and has been parched and lost, fearful of what direction to walk, unable to see the path to water, the living waters of God’s Love.

Will you bathe with me, and drink of these waters? Allow this water of life to flow through every part of your being, to bring light and lightness to your beings, my children. Too long has mankind been in the dark, thirsting and seeking, in ways that do not satisfy the soul.

Will you bathe with me in these waters and find what you seek, to be enveloped in this warmth, this fluid of Love, and all your cares and worries, my children, will fall away, washed away by these waters. And you will find respite there, great relief, great joy.

Will you bathe with me in God’s Love? For this mighty river of Love washes over us, my children, sweeping us away from those places of loneliness and weariness, those places of anxiety and fear, into a place of redemption and respite and peace.

Come bathe with me, my children, in the living waters of God’s Love, and you will know, you will know within you, that this is where you are meant to be, as God gathers you to the place of respite and love.

Come bathe with me and we will know this joy together; for all eternity, this joy shall be had by all who are willing to swim in the ocean of Love. God loves you, my children. Do you really know this? Can you accept this? Can you bring this within yourselves and be embraced by this sure knowing that you are loved and cherished by God, and in this, you shall love and cherish yourself, you shall love and cherish your brothers and sisters, and a deep knowing of the truth of this Love will carry you through all that assails you, through the world that is of a cold heart, this Love shall carry you and bring you the strength that you require and the acceptance and the compassion and the understanding.

It shall allow you to walk over those difficult passages and feel joy because you are loved, loved beyond measure, loved as if an ocean loved and redeemed. Lay your worries and cares at the feet of the Father. Allow God to wash away these cares, and feel the joy, and the peace that passes all understanding shall be yours. Ask and you shall be given. Ask and the doors will open. Walk the path of Love. Be the Love. Drink deep the Love.

God bless you. I am your brother and your servant Jesus, and I love you. I love you so much. And I am joyed that you are here together in love. God bless you.
