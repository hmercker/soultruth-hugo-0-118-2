# Be together in gracious Joy, in loving Commitment

October 4<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come. Bless you, my children, it is Jesus. I come to be with my brothers and sisters who long for the truth of the Father’s Love in their lives, who have struggled and parted the barriers to receiving this truth, and following this path of Love. And for many of you the struggle for truth has been great. For many of you there have been obstacles deep within and you have longed for the Father’s Love and pushed aside these things that would hold you from the Father. For the desire within your souls is great, and as you reach for God, and God reaches for you, there has been this blessing, this benediction of Love given to your souls.

And in this struggle, I have prayed for you; in this way, we rejoice for you. In your efforts comes great light and the possibility to be a channel of love in this world that so desperately needs this love and light, that so desperately needs souls who follow the will of God and bring truth and wisdom, healing and light, comfort for the many who suffer, who walk this world in blindness and ignorance, who are alone, who suffer greatly.

This is my work, my children, to bring comfort and love to the lost souls. And I beseech you to follow this path and follow my guidance and give to the lost souls, to be a channel of love in this world, to dissipate the darkness, to bring peace. As you walk, my children, you will have great opportunities to be a channel of love. We will guide you. We will take you to those places of need, of darkness, and we will guide you and show you the way. So much work is needed. Work of light, of love, of peace.

Walk with me, my children. Walk with me. And I will show you God’s Love and light lights our way. The path is clear. It is beautiful, it is bright. Long for the Divine Love to enter your souls in great abundance, and all will be added unto you, my children, all will be added unto you. Do you not feel the joy of God’s Love, of God’s presence with us? It is a gift to you, a gift to all souls who desire this Love. Be joyed, my children, and know that you are truly loved. Be joyed and love yourselves truly.

God bless you, and I love you. I love all my brothers and sisters who long for God’s Love, and I love all who are in the darkness, yet within them there is that desire. Look towards the flame. Bring the flame within you. Be in this perfect grace. Walk this loving path and be at peace. And know that the angels surround you and guide you and uphold you. God bless you, my dear sweet souls, my love is always with you, and God’s Love surrounds you. Breathe it in, take it into yourselves, my children, the redeeming Love. It is yours, a gift from God, in love. Blessings to you. Thank you for gathering and listening to my call, for my family of love to be together in gracious joy, in loving commitment, in eager eagerness to know the truth that is Love. I love you.
