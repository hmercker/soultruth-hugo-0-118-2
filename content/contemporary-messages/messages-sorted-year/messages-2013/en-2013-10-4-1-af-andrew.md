# Know you walk in Light

October 4<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Andrew received by Al Fike

You do not walk alone, my children, but you walk in Godʼs care, and His hand is upon each one of you. As you seek the Father’s Love within, so you invite the Heavenly Father to carry you into light, to guide you along your life’s path, over hills and into valleys and over hills and into valleys, from light to dark to light. You are carried through your life’s path by God. And His Love flows within you as you beseech the Father for this gift. And you are welcomed into these loving arms, embraced and fed and nurtured, and given the wisdom, the blessings which bring understanding and vision, which draw to you a wondrous opening, a gracious understanding of God’s creation.

Be at peace, my children, and know you walk in light. You are children of light, and that longing within your souls will bring to you all that you desire, will bring truth and love and understanding and peace. God bless you, dear sweet souls. I am Andrew and I love you and I welcome you into this circle where we will pray together and we will receive greater insight, bounteous love, fellowship, brotherhood and sisterhood in light and love. Blessings to you, love to you, dear souls. Drink deep the Father’s Love. God bless you.
