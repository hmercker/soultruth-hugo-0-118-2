# Know that God loves you so very much

July 13<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Al Fike

Goldie loves you. Such precious and beautiful souls here tonight and I love you. Know that God loves you so very much and you are precious to Him as you are precious to us. Beautiful children, be uplifted in the Love and know you are beautiful. Everyone, beautiful souls, loving souls. God bless you. Goldie loves you.
