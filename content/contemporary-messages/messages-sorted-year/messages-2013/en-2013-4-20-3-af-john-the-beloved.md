# The Understanding will grow and the Error will fall

April 20<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

Beautiful lights, children of love, God bless you. As you gather together the very light within you, of your souls, invokes the presence of the angels, of God’s Love flowing, of great light around you, of God’s presence in your midst. For the desire is within you, each one here, to be close to God, to be open to the flow of His Love, to seek the guidance and the understanding, and to pull away the veils of misunderstanding and error so that you may all know the truth clearly, that you may all grasp and bring within you great truth, great understanding, great awareness of your bond and connection with the Creator who longs within His Soul to be with you, to pour His Love upon you, to bring you into the presence of this beautiful flow of love and light, healing and peace.

As you struggle and emerge from the conditions of this world, into this place, this holy place of communion, so the understanding will grow and the error will fall and the truth will emerge within each one of you. As your eyes open, as your soul expands with the Love, as you come to see truth that can only be seen through the eyes of the soul, the awareness of the soul, the love deep within, bringing this truth, drawing it to you as you open to this wondrous blessing, this wondrous light, this wondrous presence.

God bless you, dear children. Blessings to each one, beautiful souls, wondrous lights. Blessings to your loved ones. Blessings to all who you have prayed for. Blessings into this world through your prayers, and your efforts, and your longing, and your beautiful lights. Make your efforts, my children, to be in this light, to breathe in this light, to walk in this light wherever you go. May your lives be a prayer and may you be great and mighty channels of the Father’s Love in this world, bringing comfort and healing and peace and love to many, for so many long for this, and do not know how to receive, do not know as you know, this pure and beautiful truth of God’s Love. Live this Love, children, be this Love.

Blessings to you. I am John. I love you. And I am with you often, my children, as you struggle for the light, as you release the barriers to the light, as you pray and beseech the Heavenly Father to be in this ecstatic joy, this love, this light; and so I pray with you, and I pray for you, and I add my love. For truly I love each one, each light here, precious, beautiful, brimming with such great possibilities and gifts. Walk along the path, my children. Walk with me, walk with the angels, walk with the Master Jesus. As you strain for the truth, as you struggle for the light, as you pray for the Love, walk along, and we will walk with you, every step. You will be supported and guided and enveloped. Bless you, my children, I am John. Blessings to each one.
