# You carry the Message of Love

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

So, I have seen you have made your plans, my children. It is good you construct the (…) wings that you fly upon, and you feel comforted by these wings, and I tell you my children, do not rest fully upon these wings for there will be changes as you go on your journey and as you share your love amongst the children who eagerly await your efforts and ministrations. After all, is it not the Love that you carry and wish to give? And you will, my children, in great abundance, for you carry the message of Love, and this message is simple and profound and powerful in its effects upon the children.

Many have made a great study of these words of Love but do not fully understand the essence that is God’s Love and seek eagerly for more understanding, for greater depth of understanding and experience. And, my children, you have come together for many years in this pursuit, and you do have the understanding that they long for and you will impart this to the children, you will give what they require. And they will come away from this gathering inspired and longing evermore greatly for this Love. And yes, many doors will be opened, and much Love will flood through these openings and bring a greater depth of understanding to the children.

Now that you know how to fly, I suggest you leave the nest. And spread your wings and fly for the glory of God in beautiful patterns, in loving ways, in generous giving, gracious ways of light in the world. Little chicks who’ve left the nest, the Father will guide you and you will fly to far off lands, places you have never been or imagined to be. You will fly and you will see the beauty of this Earth, the diversity of your brothers and sisters, the many cultures, expressions that have been created (an awareness), and in this you will bring the Love, to bring unity amongst all mankind. For the Love will knit together all the fabrics, all the threads, all the understandings will come together in a beautiful, beautiful tapestry of creation and understanding and love and truth and beauty. God will weave you altogether to bring peace to the world. The great weaver….

And you will carry the golden threads of Love and offer these threads to the many hungering souls and they will see the beauty of weaving, the wonderment of God’s Love, the necessity to join these fabrics together and honour one another and understand the power of Love, the glory of Love, the beauty of Love. Yes, you will find (what you are looking for?), upon God’s breath you will fly away and bring the message of Love. God bless you. Confucius loves you so dearly, I am so very happy that you have found your wings. Now soar into the sky and praise God with your light. God bless you.
