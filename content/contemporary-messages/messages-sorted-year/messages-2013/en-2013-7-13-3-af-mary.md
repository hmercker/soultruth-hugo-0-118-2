# The Power of Divine Love is greater than any Blessing

July 13<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

Blessings to you, my dear children, and may God’s Love find a deep and abiding place within you, in your souls, that you may come to rely on the strength and beauty of this Love within you. When there is hardship and when you feel burdened by the conditions of this world may this Love bring you comfort and strength and wisdom. And may it give you vision so that you may see beyond these intransigent conditions and situations which arise within your lives, my children. That you may see beyond the fleeting and see the road ahead, the road which is paved with love, the road which shall lead to at-onement to God, the road that leads you to many opportunities and situations where you may be a channel of love in the world.

As God’s Love accumulates within, pray for this blessing so the road will widen and the opportunities will increase and the burdens will fall and the worries and that which is within you that pulls you into darkness will be healed and dissipate and you will know great love within and you know love for yourselves and for all those around you for all your brothers and sisters. Do not doubt this, my children, but walk in faith – faith that you will be carried, you will be guided, you will grow in this Love and bring many into this awareness.

And many souls will choose this through your efforts, through your example, through your love and the channel of love that you are in the world. Some of you doubt this, many of you do not see yourselves in this way, but I say it is so. You walk in this Love and this light and as you pray for more of this Love so it will be so and you shall see the truth in this, the power of this, the beauty of this, that which you are, beautiful souls of the Father’s creation.

Do not allow your minds to supersede this awareness, allow the soul awareness to emerge from within that knowing place to come to your consciousness and pray that God will bring you to that place of knowing, of surety, of peace, of love. The power of Divine Love is greater than any blessing, my children. It shall transform you for all eternity. Remember this, acknowledge this, pray for this. Be at peace, dear precious souls. Be at peace. I am Mary. I love you.
