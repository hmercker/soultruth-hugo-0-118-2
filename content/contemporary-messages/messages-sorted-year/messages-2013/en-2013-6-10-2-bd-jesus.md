# Deep Healing

June 10<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

You are blessed with the power of the Creator’s deep Love and you, my beloveds, are like Angels in your world, your troubled world. I am Jesus and I shall always uphold you, my precious ones.

Beloveds, each of you have received a deep blessing of Love and light and joy and peace which you will bring into your world. Each of you will carry this gift and every life you touch will feel the power of this healing, blessing. My beloveds, the world needs you deeply. Continue upon this path and Angels will uphold you. You are beloveds, beloveds. I am Jesus and I shall always, always uphold you.

A deeper understanding of the power of the Divine Love, my beloveds, and this will be a deep gift that you will know and will flow this out to all the lives you touch. And this will bring healing and peace, precious love and understanding. Yes, it will guide you. My beloveds, I am Jesus and you are channels of the deep Love Divine. Let this flow to all the lives you touch and this will grow and deepen. My precious and beloved souls, you are receiving a deep healing, it is as if your place upon this path of Divine Love and where you are that….. your life will continue. Each of you, my beloveds, have received the gift of the Divine Love. Each of you. This is the blessing, and you will let it flow into the world (and finish?) Let this flow. You have a deep purpose. And each of you will bring this Love Divine to many, to many souls. That is so deeply needed in this world. Each of you is a channel of Love Divine, precious, beloved and deeply needed. I am Jesus. And you are beautiful, beloved souls with a deep purpose that grows and expands my precious ones.

You have a deep path to continue upon to bring the Love Divine to all the lives you touch. You have this deep purpose and you will bring change to many souls who will be placed upon the Divine path. This is a deep gift to the world and to you. And each of you, my beloveds, the power is deepening within you, the power to bring the peace and love and understanding to all the lives you touch. Beloveds, this will bring peace, joy and love into your troubled world. Blessed ones, I am Jesus and I will always uphold you.
