# Do not resist the Desires of your Souls

September 9<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Anonymous received by Al Fike

Sweet souls… you have nothing to fear, my children, nothing to fear, for your souls will lead you into the Light as the Father pours His Love within. (Pray ?) your doubts and fears, that part, the many parts within you, which resist the Love, will fall away, and in time your mind will accept the truth that your souls recognize, as the Father pours these truths and Love within and bring you to a place of deep understanding, of loving connection of the flow of love to (hungering?) souls in your world.

Yes, you are all upon the path which leads to true and deep understanding of your soul’s purpose and desires. And that which is within your mind that compels you to go in directions different than your soul knows you must go, will fall away, will be absorbed by the Love and you will know peace and harmony and wisdom and you will know the Will of God and walk the Will of God. Express the love in profound and powerful ways that will bring change, joy and comfort to this world. Do not doubt, do not fear, do not resist the desires of your souls, but accept in joyous acknowledgement that God’s Love burns within and will bring you to a place of harmony, purpose, a powerful flow of love. I love you, dear ones. I will be with you, I am with you in love. Be my precious family of love. God bless you and good night.
