# See beyond the superficial Creations of Mankind

June 24<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children. I am your teacher, Augustine.

The world needs you, my children. The world needs the light that you bring, the love that you bring, for below the surface there is great turmoil and anguish and suffering in humanity. For so many have strayed from the light and walk a path of confusion and darkness and ignorance. They are unable to see, to hear, to speak in a way that is meant for them to see, to hear, to speak. They do not understand the power of God’s Love, the beauty of their creation. They do not see the choices that must be made and the opportunities that are available to every soul to seek the light, to be true to their creation, to be true to their selves, their true selves, their souls.

And you, my children, have found to a degree this clarity, this truth, this ability to see beyond the superficial creations of mankind to a deeper place of understanding and appreciation of your own soul’s possibilities of being with God in harmony and love, and to walk within that flow to be a channel of love in the world. Each one here has received the Love within their souls and it burns bright. And for some of you it is difficult to acknowledge this, but you all are in this light, and the time will come when you will see clearly and powerfully.

And you all have a unique journey, a path to take and a purpose to fulfill in this world and this door of awareness and understanding and acknowledging and walking, this path, this purpose is opening for each one of you, my children. And hence you are drawn to this place to seek the answers you need to understand more clearly the Will of the Heavenly Father, to see within yourselves this light, to know deep and abiding peace within and to release all that within you that inhibits you from these awarenesses, this acknowledgement, this love.

You are coming to this place of clarity, my children. You are stepping upon a new threshold, and your eyes will be opened wide, you shall hear the voice, the guidance, the quiet, small voice of the Father within you, guiding you upon the path that’s unique and meant for you. These things will be yours, my children, these blessings are yours. And it is for you to choose, it is for you to listen, it is for you to open your eyes and to see and to walk in faith and trust and humility and grace and love.

My daughter, ____, we shall be with you as you journey and give to the children and do your work there. Indeed, my daughter, you stand as a beautiful example of one willing to follow their path, expressing their true purpose, of being a channel of Love. And we will be with you as you journey. You will be surrounded by the angels and guided along, for you are willing to do the Will of God, you are willing to be a channel of Love, you are willing to do so without fear, without doubt. And your brave steps are a wonderful example to many.

And for each of you, my children, (there?) will be these steps moving forward in the world, for great opportunities await each one of you. And it is but a moment of awareness to bring you to that place of deep understanding as the Love carries you forward, as God’s hand is upon you, (in the way?). When you are willing to step upon your path, to take your place, to express that deep and blessed purpose for each one, the doors will open, the path will be clear and you will trust in God’s guiding hand, willing, and open and strong.

Precious souls, pray for the Love to enter your souls in great abundance, to clear away the obstructions, to assuage the doubt, to bring the strength, to open your eyes, to give you the gift of hearing the Will of God and knowing the Will of God and knowing your true selves, the beautiful souls, you. God bless you. May the light always be around you, my children, and may the Love always flow through you. And may all those who seek the comfort and the Love and the truth, be touched by God’s hand in this world, in this world so deeply needing the truth, the truth and the veils of false error, the misguided conditions of humanity, may they fall away and truth be revealed and peace and harmony, a blessing for mankind.

Bless you, my children, bless you. Be strong. Pray for the Love. Pray for the Love with every breath and we will pray with you and be with you. God bless you. I am Augustine and I love you.
