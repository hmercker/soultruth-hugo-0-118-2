# Encouragement

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come. I am Jesus. I come to acknowledge your efforts and your love and to encourage you on your journey. I will be with you every step and many, many from the Heavens will accompany you on your efforts across the waters, across the Earth, and there will be a great opening of light and love.

As you sit in your circle and pray together with the beautiful souls and trust in what your souls know, my children. Trust in the Will of the Heavenly Father, trust in the guides who guide you. Have faith that the unfolding of this wondrous plan of the salvation of mankind will be as it is meant to be. And you will find your place within this plan that is meant to be, your purpose, your souls joyous in this fulfillment.

And this work you do will give you joy for all eternity, my children. Joy for all eternity. There is no greater path. The work you do will have effects you cannot possibly imagine nor will you see completely within your lifetime, but you will see as your existence carries on into the spirit world. You will see the effects of your lives, you will understand our words, you will see the purpose that is the unfolding will of God.

God bless you, beloved souls, my brothers and sisters who carry the truth of Love in the world, who carry the word, the word of truth, and embody the message that I gave so many years ago and continue to give. Yes, my children, you will know great joy and fulfillment, peace and love, unimaginable love. Continue in these efforts and I will continue to guide you and be with you, as will many. As long as your thoughts (??? recording was scratchy...) upon you, light your way. The angels are singing your praises, my children. The glory of God rests upon you as a mantle of light. God bless you, sweet, sweet souls. You are truly loved.
