# Angels surround you in great Numbers, more than has ever been in this World

October 6<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

The river of Love flows into you, and great gushes of living light, of loving essence of the Creator. Open wide and your souls will know the true Love, the true understanding of redemption, for you will be claimed by God and carried by God, and you will give yourselves to God, and know His Will in this way.

Do not doubt the words of Jesus, for he comes in truth always, and has shared a deep love with you, and an invitation. Carry this with you, and contemplate this, and make your choice, for this choice is profound, will bring great change to your lives, will open many doors.

To do the work of God, to heal the hungering hearts of humanity, to show the way to peace - now is the time, my children, for this effort. The world is in deep need, and without the efforts of those who are strong and in love, the consequences are too great to contemplate. There is much to be done at this time, and you must, my children, you must take seriously this invitation. You are all capable of making great contributions to this world through the development of your souls with love. And I say to you pray and pray for this Love. Beseech the Father with every breath, and the change will take place within and give you what you require to do this work.

I am John the Beloved, and I love you, gracious children of love. The angels surround you in great, great numbers, more than has ever been in this world. And they come to reinforce this message of love, of work, of understanding, that there is great need in this world, and your souls know that they may be of service to God in beautiful ways, in healing ways, in loving ways.

Be at peace, my children, and know that God is always with you in His Love, in His care, and you will be shown the way, each step given as a gift, of guidance and love, of knowing. Do not feel anxious but know that in the flow of God’s Love all will be harmonious and unfold in ways that will be harmonious for you, my children, each one unique on your path, beautiful in your souls, gifted in many unique and glorious ways. Your purpose will be fulfilled.

God bless you, my beautiful children. Acknowledge that each of you has struggled to come to this place, and in this struggle, you have been strengthened. In your choices you have chosen rightly and righteously with truth and love. And this gives you a wondrous gift. God bless you, beautiful souls, God bless you.
