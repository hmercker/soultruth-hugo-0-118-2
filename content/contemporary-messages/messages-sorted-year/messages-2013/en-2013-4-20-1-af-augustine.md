# Come to know yourselves

April 20<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

...grows in your midst, my children It is Augustine, your teacher. And yes, you will draw many, and there will be many gatherings of souls desiring with the Love. Follow your guidance, my children, seek - seek the light, and lay yourselves down before the Heavenly Father, and beseech your loving God to know His Will, that you may walk in the light more fully and be a channel of His Love in great abundance. Humility, my children. Come to know yourselves, truly know. Come to know God and all the beautiful manifestations of the Creator. And love creation with all your hearts and all your souls and all your mind. Love creation. And the love shall flow. Peace for you, my children, and the loving touch of God, who is ever with you, as are we. Peace, love, joy, understanding, truth, blessings to you. God bless you.
