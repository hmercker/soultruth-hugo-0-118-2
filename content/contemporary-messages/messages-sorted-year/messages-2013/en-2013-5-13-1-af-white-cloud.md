# So many in this World turn away from Truth

May 13<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

White Cloud received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am White Cloud and I have come to acknowledge my dear sister and charge, ___, whom I have been with since her birth and have protected and guided and been close and watched over this dear, beautiful soul. Indeed, she has pulled a heavy load in her life. She has struggled and overcome many obstacles. It is a testimony to the strength of her soul, of her being, and she has become a powerful and beautiful channel of the Father’s Love and manifested much healing from the Creator to many lost souls, has brought comfort and peace and truth and understanding to many in her life, a life well lived, a path well trod, a channel clear and beautiful like the running brook.

And she has been a channel of the clear waters, the living waters, into this world, and has impacted this world in such a way there will be ripples for many generations. She is unable to see this, she has deep humility, but it is so. And when her time comes to be with us in spirit, she will have great awakenings and an understanding.

Blessings to her, as she struggles with her physical pain and she takes her last steps upon this world. May these steps be many and fruitful as she struggles to overcome these physical burdens. Yet, the Creator does pour His Healing upon her, bringing her relief, uplifting her, showing her the tasks that are still to be accomplished. And I bring blessings and greetings to all of you, all of her loved ones, I come to acknowledge and embrace.

Blessings to you, my brothers and sisters. Many bright, shining faces I see, many beautiful souls shining with the Love. Continue with your prayers and your efforts. Continue to follow the Will of God in your daily lives. Continue to walk in the light, be a channel of the Father’s Love.

So much is required, so much healing to be given to this world, to make the peace, to bring the harmony, it is required. For so many in your world willingly close their eyes and their ears, and they turn away from truth because they cannot bear to acknowledge the disharmony that is within and without. They hide away in their own self-illusions and it is for you, my children, to break that illusion, to bring truth, to confront error and to love, to love in ways that make change, to walk in ways that are an example of change, and light and truth.

And these decisions that are made with every breath have an impact in the world, my children. And you have the choice to walk in this light, to be an example of truth. The path has been forged for you; the way has been given, for many of you have been instructed for a number of years on the truth, the path, and the way. Continue to listen, to acknowledge, to choose, to love and we will be with you guiding you, helping you as you walk upon your path. Acknowledge the beauty and the purpose that the Creator has put within you.

Blessings to you, my dear brothers and sisters, beautiful faces of love. Blessings to my dear daughter. May she know great peace and light and love. May the light go out to all of your loved ones, into the world. May it have profound effect on the lost souls, the tired souls, the lost souls, the dark souls. I am with you in your efforts. I love you, all of you, blessings to you. I am White Cloud. God bless you.
