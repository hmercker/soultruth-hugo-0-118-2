# It will be a joyous Journey

October 6<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Mary received by Al Fike

I am Mary, the mother of Jesus. My son is very earnest in his invitation, for his love for you is great, and his prayers for you are constant. And I ask you to listen with your souls what he has asked of you, and hear the calling from that place deep within, and God will speak to you and affirm to you what is meant to be in this plan for the salvation of mankind.

There will be many surprises and joyful gifts and loving connections with your brothers and sisters. It will be a joyous journey, and I will not say that all will be easy, for it will not, for this world does not make such things easy, but your struggles and your effort, your strength and your choices will bring great rewards, rewards that will bring joy to your souls for all eternity, my children, for you have been issued a call, a gift, and this gift is great, and where these gifts are given, these blessings are bestowed, much is expected.

And the angels rejoice as the call is given and God opens the mighty door. I love you. You are my family, everyone precious to me, everyone beautiful in the eyes of God, in the eyes of the angels. Lay down your worries and cares and accept this beautiful Love in great abundance, and be joyed that you know that God loves you, will always love you, and that your love for God increases with every day. I am Mary and I love you. I love you so much. You are my children.
