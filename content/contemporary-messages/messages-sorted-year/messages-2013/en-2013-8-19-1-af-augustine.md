# The Winds of Change bring many Blessings and Disruptions

August 19<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

Your teacher, Augustine, wishes to speak to you. God bless you my children, my students.

And I wish to instruct you upon your travels to your gathering. I want you to know that all the barriers will fall for those who are reluctant to join you to this place and yes there will be a great gathering, a surprising gathering of many diverse souls. And you, my children, will bring the light and the truth. And I encourage you to pray more earnestly for the Love to enter your souls and to ask to be guided and shown your role and purpose in this great event.

There is much for you to prepare for, my children. And those who remain here shall pray for you on your journey. And I would ask you to ask those who are not going to be present to pray for you in unity and unison as you embark upon these efforts. For it will require all of your prayers, all of your good will to bring about the opening, to push back the darkness, to bring clarity and peace and love to this place.

And those who will be present will drink deep of this Love. Though there may be some confusion and difficulties amongst a few, the great majority will have clarity and blessings in abundance. And you are not to be overly concerned with those who are resistant but to be clear and strong in your presence and your love, an open channel of love.

Each of you are being prepared. Each of you here has a role, whether you be there in the flesh or not, you have a role to play, and I ask you to take this seriously and earnestly and be in the grace of God’s Love. May you pour your loving intentions upon this gathering and focus your thoughts and your good wishes and your love. These efforts that you make will bring great change in the world and more efforts will be made in other locations upon the planet. You will all be a part of these efforts and this outpouring of love and light.

The winds of change bring many blessings and disruptions and you, my children, will bring comfort and peace and truth through this, and you will ride a wave of light and love. We are so joyed at your efforts and your strength and your love. Many of you have passed many tests and trials and grown greatly over these many years and now you are ready to share these truths in ways that others will accept and absorb and understand.

The doors will open and the Love will flow mightily and bring the changes within each soul touched and the ripples of light and love. You have been called upon my children, and the Master Jesus leads us on in this great work. And I say you cannot fail. You will succeed for great armies of angels uphold you and surround you and bring you what is required to succeed, and your souls compel you into the light, into the truth, beyond the impediments into a place of strength and clarity and wisdom and truth.

Let the Love flow and the light bathe all you touch and wherever you go, wherever you are in the world, it will be of love. Know this, my children, and we are so joyed at what you have accomplished within yourselves, in the glory of God’s Love. Your gifts are opening and there are many more gifts to come, many openings to come. The momentum builds and God’s Healing Love floods the world.

Bless you, my children, courageous, beautiful lights, bless you and may you know the peace that passes all understanding and may you give your will to God’s Will. Be guided by the flow of Love within you until it covers the world. Allow the healing, the truth; the world hungers for this, the world awaits this. You have been called. Step forward, my children. You fly the banner. God bless you. I love you and I will accompany you on this great journey.
