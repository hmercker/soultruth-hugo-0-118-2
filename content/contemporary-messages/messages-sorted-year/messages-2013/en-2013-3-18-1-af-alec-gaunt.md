# Continue to grow in the Love, continue to make your Connection with the Creator

March 18<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Alec Gaunt received by Al Fike

Alec here. How pleased I am to be with you and to see how these groups have grown, and to see the growth within each one of you as you receive more of the Father’s Love and how you have learned your lessons and passed your tests and struggled for the light. And I am very, very happy to see the efforts that have been made, and the strength that I see within each one of you here, and the light that glows from each one.

Yes, there is much work to do my dear friends. And I am so very, very happy that you have taken up this cause that I had and was so very passionate about when I was on Earth and my life was cut short. There is indeed so much work to do, and you will be guided and know that the angels are with all of you, and you will know the steps to take and you will meet many, many others whose souls long for this Love, who will contribute to this effort. And together, together you will be a powerful agent of change in the world, my friends.

My dear son Iain, continue in your efforts and your struggle for light. I am with you often, and I see you have made some good choices, and I want to encourage you to think about the choices that are available to you each day, and to continue to pray for the Love, this is the key, this will bring you forward and bring the healing that you need within. I love you, my dear son Iain. Continue. Be strong. Think of me when you need to be strong and I will be with you and add my strength and my love to you. And there are many helping you, as there are with each one of you here, many angels helping and assisting and guiding and protecting and showing you the way to truth and helping you to understand the many complicated aspects of this life which you need to move through and contend with and understand.

Yes, there is no turning back now. Only forward as your souls grow and expand in this wonderful Love, as you continue to pursue the truth, as we from our side of life continue to guide you and show you the truth and help you to understand yourselves, and this world, and the world of God’s creation, and the world of your own soul. So much to know and to learn. Every day is an opportunity to learn and to grow. Every day is a test - of the choices made, of the love given, of the awarenesses brought forth within.

I love you, all of you. I am so very happy to be able to speak tonight and to acknowledge that you have continued with this chain of light in the world. That you are forging a great and powerful link that shall affect and bring light and healing and love to many in this world. As you grow, you bring this. As you choose to be in the flow of God’s Love, you bring change into the world. And this change, the momentum will build and the opportunities will be many for you to be a channel of love. And each of you carries many gifts, great potential.

Continue to grow in the Love, continue to make your efforts and your connection with the Creator. Continue to be open as you are, to receiving insight and understanding and love, and yes we will all work together, my friends, for this great cause, this wonderful journey, this beautiful undertaking. To know the will of God is to have a wonderful and powerful gift. And in time this knowledge will be clear to all of you, what God wills for you for this world. It will come. It is coming. And for some it is here in your awareness. God bless you. I am your brother and I am your friend Alec, and I love you. Thank you. God bless you.
