# The Power of Prayer of a Group of Souls

August 17<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

It is Mary. Prayers have been answered because you have prayed. Love has been given because you have desired this. Light has been brought into this world because you have gathered. If you could but see the power of each soul present here, and the gathering of each soul in unity and love has brought a great light into this world, has made possible many manifestations of healing and comfort, bringing harmony and peace, influencing the leaders of the world, influencing the outcomes of many situations that could otherwise be negative; they have been transformed into something of light and harmony.

Yes, the power of a group of souls gathered together in prayer, in love, in desire, can change the conditions in this world and bring about many glorious blessings. If you allow yourselves to be a channel of love, if you strive for the light, as you walk ever forward on your path, much will be accomplished.

And my children there is much to do; great works. Continue in your efforts. Continue to gather as we call you together, continue to be in this light, a family of love, a circle of truth and grace and beauty. And the Master stands in your midst, blessing you, bringing you what you require, a gift to each one.

God bless you, lovely, lovely children. I love you, I am Mary, I love you dearly. I am with you, with you in your prayers and with you in your efforts, with you as you love, with you as you struggle. God bless you. Good night.
