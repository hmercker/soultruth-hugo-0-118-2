# As you test your Gifts and your Abilities, you will gain more Confidence

April 29<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

Bless you children, as you come together to receive the Father’s Love, this great blessing of Love, as you come together in your fellowship, and your efforts to be channels of Love in the world. Your life upon this material plane is very short, my children, and many of you are well past the middle of this journey, and we beseech you, my children, to make good of the time that you have, to prepare yourselves for the next leg of the journey.

Oh yes, this time is short, yet there is much that you must do within this period upon this earth, great work to come. As your souls are leavened by the Love, you are now ready to be clear and powerful channels in the world, my children. There has been great preparation and learning and growing within the Love. Your souls are filled with this beautiful light and you have learned many lessons, my children, and encountered many obstacles and tests along the path while you have been in this world and this has allowed you to grow strong and wise.

And many of you do not see this. You are troubled and you withdraw from this invitation to step forward. You do not feel worthy, yet you are worthy. And we ask you, my children, to pray for more of this Love. The small barriers will fall, your awareness of your purpose and your calling by the Heavenly Father will come to your consciousness and the fear will fall away, the Love will flow and heal all those places that still persist. And you will be guided by the angels, surrounded, upheld, protected, nurtured as God places His hand upon you. Great works await you, my children.

There is nothing to fear, only to have joy as you walk through this threshold, this last bit on the path you are meant to walk, the places you are meant to be and the many, many souls that shall be saved through your efforts, your love, your work. Many of you have the gift of teaching, some the gift of sight, some have great soul wisdom. There are many gifts amongst you, my children. Together you create a beautiful light, a wondrous resource, an ability to give great healing love to many.

And as you test your gifts and your abilities, you will gain more confidence and understanding of what is meant for each one to do with these gifts. Draw together, my children, in wisdom and harmony, in everlasting love and all will be well as you walk these steps upon the path of life, they will be meaningful steps, powerful steps.

Bless you, my children, beautiful sweet children. You are loved souls. We care for you with tenderness, with love and with joy. It is our joy to do so; it is our expression of love for our Heavenly Father to nurture our brothers and sisters, to bring the light, to open the gateways for Love to flow. Bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine.
