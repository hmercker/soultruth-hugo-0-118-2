# Do not fear, but Love

May 4<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Joseph received by Al Fike

My son A___: I am Joseph, the father of Jesus, and I am your guide. And I beseech you to lay down your resistance and your fears, for you are cradled in the hand of God, and you shall be guided forward.

You ask for a message: your message is given. Do not fear, but love. Do not resist but allow the Will of God to move through you, for you have many gifts to give, my dear son, many gifts to give, and many more to discover. And if you travel to this place of the gathering, a door will be opened for you, a door of awareness, an understanding of this. It is your fear to step through this door that gives you the resistance. And I ask you to lay down your fears and your doubts, for your soul longs for this, my son, your soul cries out for this, my son. And God will answer your cries and your desire. Place your fears aside. Allow yourself to acknowledge who you truly are and the beauty that is you. For when you do this, that which impedes you will fall away. Surrender. And gain yourself a true understanding.

Bless you, my son, I am with you often, as are many angels, as you toil through the day and you minister to those who come to your place of work. There is healing and love provided along with this material nourishment. And you and your partner are a channel of love, beautiful to behold. Do not turn away, but step forward. You are blessed. Each are blessed and gifted in ways that are unique and complementary to one another.

Bless you, my sons, bless you, and the light that you walk in and upon, and the path that is yours to follow. Do not hesitate, but be strong and resolute, loving and generous in your efforts. Be a channel of love. God will clear the path and bring the harmony that you desire. There is nothing to fear. Blessings to you and to all here, beautiful children of the light and love. Walk together. Love one another and support one another. This is all that God asks of you. Blessed children I love you. I am Joseph. God bless you.
