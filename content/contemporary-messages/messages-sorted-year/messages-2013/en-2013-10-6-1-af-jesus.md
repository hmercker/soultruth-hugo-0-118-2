# The Truth is without Words; It is a knowing within

October 6<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

My Heavenly Father, I ask you to pour Your Love upon these dear, sweet souls, my brothers and sisters who walk upon this world and seek the light and seek you in loving communion. Gracious God, my loving Father, these are Your children, and they are beautiful in your sight, and they carry light in this world. May Your peace be a mantel upon these, Thy children. Blessed Father, keep them safe in this world. May Your Love carry them through all the conditions of this world, the many trials and tribulations, and the beauty that is Your creation.

Those who walk with me walk in the path of love. You are my disciples. Each and every soul who desires to walk in the truth of God’s Love is a disciple of mine, and I walk with you. With every step I am with you, and my love for you is deep, and God’s love for you is infinite. And I will carry you; I will carry you. And the angels will be at your side, each one who wishes to be in the flow of God’s Love will be loved and protected and guided and shown the way to truth in all its beauty and simplicity.

The road, my children, is straight, and upon that road the Love pours upon you and keeps you upon this road of loving understanding, allows you to see your direction, lights your way and shows you the beauty that is you, your souls, and brings to you the Holy connection with your soul and God’s soul. Trust in this, my children. Trust in this great truth and all else will come to you as you make your steps in faith and love. The truth is without words. It is a knowing within. And in this way, you may acknowledge all souls who desire light and love. No matter their mental understandings; do not fear this or judge this but love every soul. Let your judgements fall away, my children, and in this way, you will love all humanity in the way that God intends, in the way that will bring peace and joy to this world, for there is a great need for healing, a great need to bring humanity together in love, a great need for all of us to set aside these fears and judgements and error.

Walk the Path Divine, my children. I will be with you, and God is ever with you in His loving care. In His loving care, God is ever with you. Never doubt this, my children. Even when you have despair, never doubt that God is with you, and you may reach out to your Heavenly Father for comfort and love and it shall be there, always, in great abundance.

I call you, my children, to be my disciples, to grasp the truth close to your bosom as best you can, and walk in love with every step, and be a channel of God’s Love always, evermore, in the name of God and truth and love and light. This is my calling to you, my children, and I love you, and I have deep compassion for your struggles, for this world is not an easy place.

There are many who would turn away from you in this light, but you must persist, for many do turn towards you and desire what you have, are eager for truth and love and understanding. And God’s care will always be with you, my children. And though the conditions of the world seem insurmountable, I tell you that love is greater, love is greater than all the darkness and all the suffering.

You are my children, my family. I love you as if you are family. And there is great joy in the heavens that you have come together in joyous love and that you sit in prayer together in this way. And I see many such gatherings, my children, and many more coming. This is just the beginning. The door has opened. The Love will rush through. The Love will pour into your souls and give you what you require to be channels and instruments of change in this world. It shall be so. For those of you willing to step forward and be my disciple, my invitation stands for every soul in this room, to walk with me in this loving, loving effort to heal this world, to bring light to all the children of this world, and to bring a future of light for all the children coming to this world. The road will not be easy, but the work will be mighty and beautiful and filled with great promise and loving acts and healing words.

I am your brother Jesus. I have come. I am here. And God brings great light to this room. The light of His Love, the light of His presence, the light of healing and peace. Open yourselves to this benediction, this blessing. ...God bless you.
