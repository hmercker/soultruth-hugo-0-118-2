# Have Compassion for those who are in a negative Condition

July 13<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Blessings to you, my children, I am Confucius. Each of you (struggles?) for the light. Each of your souls has great desire to be in the light of God. Yet there are many forces that conspire to pull you from this light. Some is of your own choosing, my children, but some is from the general conditions of this world and the desire of others for power (and control?)

When you are a true channel of love, my children, you elicit a response to every soul you meet. And at times this response is not what you would wish. And I say to you, my children, have compassion for those who are in a negative condition and who are judgmental and afraid. Pray for them. And for you, my children, be sure not to engage in these conditions, to allow this negativity to pierce your heart, for you are claimed by the Father. You are loved by God. You walk in light, my children. Only that which you allow to pierce you, will pierce you. Walk in strength, in love, compassion and a surety that God’s hand is upon you, my children, and His protection enfolds you and His Love burns within you.

And yes, you may make errors and you may say those words that may cause a negative response in others, but I say forgive yourselves, forgive those that respond to the negative for they have (learned?) Walk forward in strength, in love, knowing that every moment is an opportunity to grow and to love, to understand and to be in the light of God’s Love. There is nothing to fear. There is goodness within each one of you, my children. And that is what you must acknowledge and walk in light with the awareness that you are beautiful, for you are beautiful, my children. Your souls shine with the Love.

And yes, you do struggle in this world as all the children in this world struggle, to find their way. Be at peace and know you have truly found your way, my children, the way to redemption and love. The transformation within you makes for a powerful manifestation of love. And at times you find it difficult within your mind to reconcile and to catch up to these changes within and this is a learning and growing process that shall take place all the days of your life, my children.

The light of the soul steps forward before the enlightenment of the mind, but they will come into harmony, and awareness will bring great insight, and understanding and compassion and love. Yes, there is much to learn, a great distance to walk, but you are well on your way and (love?) will blossom within you and love will flourish and you will be strong and bring light to many with your strength and your love as God uses you as an instrument of love and wisdom and change in this world.

And when the barbs are flung on you do not flinch, do not retreat, but ask God to lead you forward in His loving protection and the wisdom will be given, the strength will be given, and your eyes will open. And although you see before you a time may seem unbearable, you will be strong enough to bear these awarenesses. And with this will come great light and healing and ecstatic connection with the Creator.

Yes, the spiritual path is a double-edged sword, my children. You shall go forward and your eyes are opened. And the true reality of the conditions of this world and the conditions within each suffering soul on this Earth will bring pain to you and these perceptions, but with this comes wondrous insight, glorious love, mystical rapport, the truth and beauty and love and God’s great soul. There is a price, but I assure you the price is well worth the payment. And there will be a time when even the most (nasty?) conditions will not touch you and you will see clearly and accept and have compassion.

Blessings to you, my children, beautiful souls, I love you. And Jesus loves you and I am with you often. I come to you often. Your souls know this. God bless you.
