# Your Efforts, your Commitment, your Love has created a great Light

January 9<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine. And I wish to say that this instrument and his dear beloved partner have learned much upon their travels and seen much. And they have come back to their home in gratitude for what they have. So many gifts have been bestowed upon all of you, my children. So many blessings. To have this fellowship, to have the strength of love that resides within this gathering, to be in the light as you are, to have the awarenesses that you have, to walk upon the path as you have, has brought a great deal to each one of you.

And what you have, my children, is very scarce in this world. You are blessed, and yes, the light that you carry together, the love that you share together brings those from around the world, for they are drawn to this, and they desire what you have, and they long for this beautiful blessing of fellowship and love and strength and wisdom. Yes, for many years of your earth time you have come together, loyally, consistently, and this has brought about this great gift.

Your efforts, your commitment, your love has created a great light which is seen by many from distant places. Acknowledge what you have, my children, these gifts, this beautiful, beautiful fellowship; and this strength and this light will bring you ever forwards as channels of light and change in the world. A gift from the Heavenly Father. A gift to the world. And through your desire and your dedication and your strength much will be accomplished. A healing, an unburdening, an opening of awareness from the soul will be your gift to the world.

Continue in your efforts, my children. Continue to love one another and support one another, and to walk upon this path with humility and grace and love. Thank you for these efforts, for your commitment, your love, your dedication, your willingness to walk upon the Divine Path, for this has created great light, great potential, and wondrous blessings, a channel of love into the world.

Bless you, my dear sweet children, channels of the Father’s Love, bless you. We are so joyed at your efforts and we will draw many. As they are drawn so the doors will open for you to gather the souls, to bring the healing, and the peace, and the love amongst you. And we will ever be present, and God’s Love shall pour upon you in great abundance and you shall all be carried forward on a wave of love, a wonderful flow of love.

God bless you. I am your teacher Augustine, and I love you dearly. So many of your earth years have I been with you and watched you grow and change and evolve. And watch your souls grow in the Love and the light grow within you and your struggles to release those conditions that are not in harmony with the Love. And I am joyed with your efforts, and your success, and your acknowledgment of the truth. Continue with these efforts, my children. Continue in your supplications to receive the Love. Ask for the guidance. Ask to be led along the path. And God will hear your prayers and lead you forward and place His Love within you in ever greater quantities. God bless you. I am Augustine. I love you.
