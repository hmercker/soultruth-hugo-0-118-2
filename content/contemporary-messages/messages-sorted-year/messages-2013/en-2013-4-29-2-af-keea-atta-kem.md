# One step at a Time

April 29<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

The dear soul you have been with in times past, she is in need of your prayers. As you make this contact with others, other souls who desire the Love and reach out to God as you do, there is a wonderful bond that is created, and with this bond, this connection of love, much can be accomplished. Your prayers are more powerful as you pray for these beautiful souls and they pray for you. This network carries the light and brings blessings forward in a more tangible way, for them and for you.

This is why we are encouraging and facilitating your meeting those from around the world to create this beautiful network, this lattice of light that can be used by God to bring healing and wisdom and blessings to many. Your family grows, my children, and shall grow and multiply and multiply again. These efforts are very important my children. These efforts will in time change the awareness of humanity and bring their attention back to the Creator and His Love.

One step at a time, my children, one step at a time. As you make your efforts, as we make our efforts, as you all come together, in your loving embrace, in your acknowledgement of love, in your acknowledgment of God’s Love. Bless you; bless you for your prayers and your efforts. Bless the children in need of your prayers, bless all the children in need of your prayers, so many prayers to be said, so much love to be given, so much healing necessary, and so many barriers to break until there is love and peace.

Bless you, my children. I am Keea and I love you.
