# Love will flow and Guidance will be clear

October 26<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Precious children, Confucius loves you so.

Yes, a new era dawns for you, my children. Great work begins. And it is for you to live your lives with each day in dedication to God, receptive to the flow of God’s Love. Yes, to be in the centre of this river of Love where the greatest currents and deepest depths of Love flows and takes you to those places that are required in the unfolding of God’s plan to bring peace and love and harmony to this world.

It is a great, great task and much is asked of you, my children, yet you are ready. The doors will open. The Love will flood through and the guidance will be clear. And you will not be shaken by the conditions of this world when the conditions that reside within each soul in this world which can as you perceive them can be disturbing and concerning but I tell you, my children, you will be led through these perceptions and understanding, great compassion and love and awareness. For with these awarenesses, will come what you require to touch the soul in a deep way, to understand the deep struggles, to know the challenges that beset this world are great and vast and deep and very dark, these conditions.

But the Love is stronger, my children, and as you gain your strength, so the Love will flow through you in ever greater quantities and powerful, powerful ways that will dissipate the ignorance, the darkness, the desire for power, the desire for recognition, and for wealth. These things will dissipate as you walk upon this path as a channel of Love. And your perceptions will come through this darkness and touch the soul in a deep way and in this touch will come the awakening and the falling away of these errors and these disharmonies.

Much to learn, my children, as you walk this path and do this work, yes, great changes ahead for each one of you. Be willing to change, to do the will of God with every breath and every step and you will know a great and deep fulfillment as you express your purpose in the world. Each has a role to play, each a gift, many gifts to bring. Do not doubt this. And when you doubt turn your eyes to the Heavenly Father and say, *"Father, I want to understand in a deeper way what you ask of me. I wish to understand myself and my fears and my prejudices and my judgments. I wish to release these to you that I may be a clearer channel of your Love in this world"*.

Yes, we do not show you the full picture, my children; for we fear you would run away. For these challenges are great and what you must endure will be difficult but God in His love, and his caring, in His beautiful, beautiful care for you, will bring you into greater light always, always if you desire this. So, you may walk in this direction of Love, my children, each step, one day, each day and we will hold your hand and show you. Ah, my little birds, beautiful, rather than run, you will fly. Lovely, lovely.

God bless you, Confucius loves you. I am with you always in this great effort as are many, many Angels and we congratulate you on your efforts. And you have worked together in love and you have followed your guidance, even those who were reluctant followed your guidance and we are very happy about this. And have you not gained greater insights, a fuller understanding of what it is to live in the grace of God’s Love, to walk in faith, to know peace. Blessings to you, my beloveds.
