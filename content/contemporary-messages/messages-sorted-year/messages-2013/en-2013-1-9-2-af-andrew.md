# Be at Peace

January 9<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

Deep peace, my children, deep peace. A blessing for each one of you. You struggle for the light. Your souls open to the Love. This path is destined for at-onement with God. Deep peace to you, my children. And may your efforts be blessed. And as you make these efforts, as you question and converse and cast about for your direction, know that we will guide you, my children, that there is a path, a plan, a destiny. And you are sure to follow, for your souls desire this. You desire this. Do not let that which is within your minds diminish the desires of your soul, for your soul knows, my children, knows the (a?) great deal. And you struggle within to acknowledge this, to find this deep wisdom and understanding and desire. Be at peace, my children.

For as the Father’s Love flows into your souls, so all shall be revealed upon the path of light and joy and understanding and love. Do not allow your minds to clutter and to twist this deep knowledge. For that which is in your mind, my children, is distortion. That which you take within you from the human condition causes barriers, confusion, and doubt. Do not doubt. Allow your souls free reign. Allow God’s Will to flow through you freely. Be joyed at the opportunity for this flow, this expression of God’s Love, to touch every aspect within and without.

There is no need for fear or doubt or confusion, for when you walk in faith, faith in the Father’s Love and guidance and care for you, all will be revealed, every step will be lovingly placed, every door shall open with ease, and you shall walk in light, in humility, in love. Pray to be used as a channel of God’s Love with each day and those opportunities will be laid before you and with each expression of love, with each step forwards in light, you will learn and grow within and understand your path, the path that each one of you must take individually. Yet collectively you walk together in love and respect and acknowledgement- acknowledgement of that which is within you, my children, this love residing within: it is a beacon, your guiding force, your inner voice, your loving, loving directive from the Creator.

Be at peace, my children, and know you are loved and cherished and guided, protected. That as you embark upon this journey, this opening, this opportunity, we will guide you, the Father’s hand will be upon you, and there will be a great work, a work that can and will change the world.

This is possible, my children. For as you walk, humbly, obediently, with your guidance and your awareness and your strength, there can only be light and harmony, though the struggles may be great at times; and though you are challenged by the human condition, there will be great light, and rewards, and successes, and surprises. Be at peace, for God has blessed you with his Love. You walk in light, my children. You will know the steps you must take, and those steps will be revealed as one is placed before the other. Be at peace.

God bless you. I am Andrew. And I love you. So many love you. And God, in His Love, is a vast ocean of love and light and blissful joy. Swim within these waters, my children. Drink these waters, these living waters. All will be well. Be at peace. God bless you. God bless you. I love you.
