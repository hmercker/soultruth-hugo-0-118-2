# Your World needs you deerly

August 10<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

God bless you, beloved souls. You are uniquely beautiful and wondrous, and your world needs you dearly and deeply. I am Jesus. I have deep love and regard for you, my beloveds. Yes, the world, your world, needs you so much, so strongly, beloved ones. Know this and carry it with you as you continue upon your path. And the Love will deepen and Godʼs hands will uphold you and guard you and bring you peace and love that you will spread as you continue upon your journey in light. Know this, beloveds, and welcome this, for you will bring a deep and loving care for all life we know of. Precious and beloveds, I will always walk with you. Good night.
