# Pray for the Power of Divine Love to fill your World

February 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

I am Jesus. I ask you to pray for the power of Divine Love to fill your world and protect all life with love and peace, so deeply needed in your world. Each of you, my precious souls, are channels of our Heavenly Father’s Divine Love; and each of you have this gift and deep purpose which you must, must allow and bring this to the attention and the conscience of as many lives as you can in this troubled world.

You are beloved souls, precious and beloved souls. And each of you has this deep purpose to understand the power of Love Divine and depth, that it is needed in your world. I am Jesus. And I shall always uphold you my precious, beloved souls. Know this. Praying the peace from each of you that is so deeply needed, my beloveds. You are channels of the Creator’s Divine Love, and this is a blessing you can bring into your troubled world. God bless you, my precious souls, God bless you.
