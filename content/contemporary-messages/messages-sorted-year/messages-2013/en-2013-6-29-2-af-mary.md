# Many Opportunities and Experiences to come

June 29<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

Bless you, my dear, sweet children, it is Mary, and I come to greet you, to love you, to encourage you as you embark upon your spiritual journeys, as you pray for this blessed Love to fill your souls.

My dear, dear children, the winds of change blow across your planet. And there is upheaval, and disruptions in the natural cycles, and all of creation upon this dear planet feel this disruption. And this shall continue, my children, as the conditions in this world change, as the world experiences a leap from one vibration of existence to another. And it is beginning. And there will be disruptions within your lives as a result of these changes. But you shall be held close to the bosom of the Heavenly Father, and protection will be given and guidance will be given and understanding will be given; for these changes and this disruption and this transition is necessary and a blessing from the Heavenly Father to all of life upon this planet. For too long mankind has influenced and manipulated and caused imbalance upon this place, and there is a need to neutralize and heal and balance this influence and this disruption.

Mankind does not understand the power that has been gifted to all peoples in this world with their free will, and the gifts of perception and understanding that come with the development of the material mind. So much has been gifted to humanity, yet mankind does not understand their own true selves, the power of their souls, the possibilities of their connection and rapport and openness to the Divine, to God. And you, my children, come together to experience this communion with the Heavenly Father. And in this communion, and in your gathering, and in your unity, in this desire to understand, to know, to be open to the inflowing of the Divine Love within your souls comes a great power and a great healing and a great channel.

This world, where you help to usher in these changes, you assist as channels of love to bring healing and balance into this world. And parts of you do not yet understand and are fearful of the power of these changes and the power of your collaboration in this. Yet your souls rejoice in your role, in your opportunity to be a part of this wondrous change.

There is much to come, my children: many, many experiences and opportunities and channels for love and healing and change in this world, for each one present here. You are gifted with these opportunities, my children, for you have prayed to be used as a channel of love in the world. And you shall be used in this way. To know the Will of God, to be in alignment with the flow of this Will, to be a channel of love, to speak the truth, to walk in light; all of these blessings and opportunities are available to you with the inflow of the Divine Love into your souls and your willingness to walk this path, to be open to the guidance, to listen to the voice of God within, showing you the way.

Do not fear the future but rejoice in this dynamic and powerful change that will bring harmony and love to this world, will break down the barriers and the ignorance and the strife, will bring the balance, will bring the truth. And as you make your plans and gather the souls together this will be the bell toll for these changes, and God’s wondrous power will manifest in beautiful ways as those of you willing to walk the path and be the agents of change in this world will have what you require, will be given the power to make these changes in the consciousness of mankind.

You will be given the words to speak; the love will flow in a mighty channel, a river to this world. You will know, my children. You will know you are on the right path. You will speak the right words. The flow of love will be abundant and clear and beautiful. You will embrace your brothers and sisters without inhibition or fear, but with great love and a surety that the channel of love is clear, profound.

Pray for this Love, my children. Pray to be used in this way. Pray to be guided. Pray to slough off your fears and the old ways of thinking and doing, for you enter a new place, a wondrous journey, a powerful and glorious and beautiful journey. And yes, you need to be strong. You need to be fearless, for there will be great resistance in many, and fear of the changes, and confusion, and a lack of understanding of why. Yet within each one of you is a true perception of why and how, and if you allow this understanding to come to your consciousness it will, and the fire will be lit, and the passion will flow, and the ability to strike the awareness in others will come to you as a blessing from God. Such a mighty channel of love and harmony this world will manifest through each one of you.

So yes, you make your plans, my children, and you speculate about the future, and I tell you that none of your perceptions at this time are clear, that you must walk in faith and trust that God will lead you, and what is within the Will of God will manifest for you to bring these necessary changes in the world will ripple out and cause mankind to open their eyes to their true selves, to the true nature of their creation, to the truth of their wondrous beings in this place, this wonderful creation of God. And so your eyes must open for their eyes to open.

Pray for this, my children. Pray that God will open your eyes, that you may fully understand, you may know why, that you may be a channel of love and clarity and strength and power and beauty. And we call you, we call you to step forth. To be in fully in the flow of this Love, the Will of God, to release your fears and to open your eyes.

God bless you, beloved souls, beautiful souls. There is nothing to fear, and so much shall be yours as you walk this path. Your needs will be met. There will be great love, and joy, and openings within you, as you will truly know who you are, your purpose, your place in this wondrous plan to heal humanity, to bring love. God bless you. I am Mary and I am with you often. And I pray for you constantly. And I love you always. Blessings to each one. You all have the strength to walk this path, to do this work. It shall be and will be. It is the Will of God. Bless you. I love you.
