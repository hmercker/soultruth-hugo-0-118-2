# It is not a Reward; it is your Birthright

April 6<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children, I am your teacher, Augustine.

Bless you for coming together to receive the Father’s Love, to beseech the Creator for this great blessing of Love. And yes, you come together with sincerity and consistency, with a desire to be close to the Creator, a desire to grow within yourselves and within your souls in this Love. And you reach for the light; you reach for the light. Remember that this Love is available to you always. It is not parcelled out to you, but is available as you desire it, as you ask, as you open yourself, as you allow yourself to receive. It is not given because God deems you worthy, for all souls are worthy of the Love. Each and every soul is given this blessing if it is desired. There is no criteria, there is no obligation. It is not a reward; it is your birthright. It is a gift given freely, given without intention to receive anything back to the Creator, other than your love and gratitude.

Please be aware of this, my children, that you are given this gift freely, and you are all worthy and beautiful, a creation of God, a wondrous creation of God. And God’s love for you is limitless, His intention to nurture you with His Love is vast and endless and bounteous and glorious. Seek the Love, my children. Ask and you shall receive. And be joyed at this knowledge. For so many turn away because they believe they are unworthy, because they do not believe that God would love them, for they do not love themselves. And they place barriers in the way of this gift. And for you to receive this gift, express this gift, be in the flow of this Love, you become the examples, the beacons of light, to show your brothers and your sisters that God loves all, all His creations, all the beautiful souls, the vast array of souls- unique, gloriously beautiful.

And it overcomes all cultures, all impediments. Any soul who asks shall receive. Any soul. And that which is within the mind has no bearing upon this, my children. It is the true desire of the soul. And this is why we ask you not to judge, to place any criteria upon this gift, for it is given freely. And what of the soul that receives the Love? The transformation within as this Love grows, so the mind is affected and healed and brought into harmony with this Love. The impediments fall away. The judgements become as smoke drifting in the wind. That which is a feeling of unlovedness becomes love. And then the true miracle begins, the transformation. The gifts emerge, that which God has placed within the body of flesh manifests in light and power and beauty and glory. And a channel of love, a flow of love, a touch, reach into the lost souls, the compassion and the joy, the healing and the peace.

What a glorious path we walk, my children, what a glorious path. A path that can and will change the world if you allow the flow of this Love, if you accept the flow of this Love, if you put aside your feelings of unworthiness and doubt and fear, and allow the Love to carry you forward beyond the realms of your own limited thinking to a place that is in harmony with the will of God. This glorious path, this wondrous flow, this miraculous healing.

Drink deep, drink deep, my children. Beseech the Father with all your will and all your desire to receive this Love, to allow the expansion of your souls for your soul’s thirst for this. There is a great thirst. Quench your thirst. And kneel down to the Father and say, *"Father I am tired of the burdens, I’m tired of feeling of unloved. I wish to fill this empty place with your presence within me. I wish to be healed completely and for my soul to be resurrected in the Love."* These prayers will be answered. This Love will be granted. This miracle will be given, powerfully, powerfully, in such great force that it will feel like all is shaking within.

And yes, there is a great and powerful inflowing that feels like all is moving and shaking. And this is a reaction to this wondrous blessing. Come with us to this wellhead and drink and drink and drink, for the parched souls to find the nurturing that it desires. God bless you, my children. Oh, we love you so. And we want you to be in joy and acknowledgement of this light, this love, this beauty. God bless you. I am Augustine and I love you.
