# Reach for a higher Place

June 10<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

May the Heavenly Father lift your burdens. May the peace that passes all understanding pour into your beings bringing comfort, bringing respite. And may your souls open to the inflowing of the Divine Essence bringing you strength and insight, bringing compassion for yourselves and for all those around you. With these blessings you will walk in light with this light you will help to heal those around you for many carry their burdens so heavy and life is a struggle upon this plane for so many.

And for each of you, my children, there is an opportunity to be a channel of light, of love, of healing, of wisdom, strength for those in need around you. As you pray for the Love, so shall the Love flow through you. As you beseech the Heavenly Father for His touch, the Light will be around you. As you seek to be guided, the Angels will surround you. And the flow of your lives will be that of beautiful works, of healing, of love, of nurturing, of truth, of wisdom, and peace. For it is your choice and your desire and your efforts and your prayers that will bring this about.

And yes, it is easy to succumb to the negative, to the conditions in which you live. It is easy to judge and to be disparaging towards yourselves and towards others. This is so much the way of the world. But my children I ask you, to reach for a higher place, to be in that flow of love, to love yourselves and forgive yourselves and to be at peace. And we will support you and uphold you and surround you and protect you as you make these efforts for Light, as you hunger for the inspiration and the Love, as you over-reach the darkness into light we will be with you always. And our love shall uphold you as God’s Love upholds you. And blessings to you, my children and blessings to your loved ones and blessings to all those drawn into this circle and blessings for this world.

We love you so dearly. You are precious, beautiful, and in the light of the Father’s Love. I love you. I am Mary. I love you dearly. God’s peace, God’s presence is with you. Feel the love, like flowers to this beautiful light. Breathe in this air. Open your souls to this Love, absorb this healing. All these blessings are here for you. God bless you. I love you.
