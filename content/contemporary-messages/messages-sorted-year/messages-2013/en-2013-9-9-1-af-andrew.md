# Step into the Light

September 9<sup>th</sup>, 2013

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

Oh precious children, beautiful souls, beautiful souls of the Father’s Love. You come together so eagerly to receive this blessing, to be together in love and desire for the soul to reach your Heavenly Father to receive this great benediction (?) of love within. And yes, the Love pours into your souls, my children, in abundance as you gather together in unity, strength and prayer, and you come from many places and some from different cultures, and yet the Love flows abundantly to each soul here because of your desire and your willingness to step into the light.

And we beseech you, my children, to step into the light. And of those conditions of the Earth that encompass you and hold you back from this light, we ask you to release these conditions and to beseech the Father to heal you of these conditions which are not in harmony with the Father’s Love, that they may fall away, that you may know great joy, freedom and understanding and awareness of the Father’s Love and presence and care for each one. That you may stand in the flow of the Father’s Love and be a channel of love for the world. This is my prayer. I beseech you, my children, to take these simple steps, to walk this path of Love, to be in harmony with the laws of God’s Love.

God bless you. I am Andrew and I love you. Precious souls, you are loved. God bless, you are beautiful. God bless you.
