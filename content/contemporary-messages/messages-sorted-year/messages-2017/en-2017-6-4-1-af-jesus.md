# God’s Blessing Through Jesus

June 4<sup>th</sup>, 2017

First United Spiritualist Church, Burnaby, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

*(Jesus standing)*

God bless you beloved souls. God’s Hand is upon you, beloved souls. His Love pours upon you. His Light enfolding you. His Healing soothing you. The great Comforter is with you beloveds *(wind chimes start ringing and then stop).*

When you come together in this way, seek God. Seek for the highest. Seek for the nurturance of your souls. For it is in this, you find what you seek. You find the healing, the peace, the grace that all souls seek in their lives. Indeed, this is a sanctuary for you, a blessing from God. And it requires each of you, my beloved souls, to give your love to this place and all who dwell within. To embrace one another in harmony. To walk in peace and to listen to the Will of God which comes through the whispers of your soul.

Each of you knows the great Truths that are within you. Listen to these Truths, to God’s Whispers, to the flow and power of Love and you will walk a route that is straight and filled with Light. All that is required in your life will come to you beloved souls, but every soul must seek Light, must seek to know God in a pure and simple way. For God Loves each and every soul. His Love is a vast ocean and it awaits for you to swim there within, to be immersed in this wondrous Light, healing Love, Peace beyond understanding. It is yours for the asking. It is yours in prayer. It is yours in your longing, your desire, your opening.

May each of you be blessed, beloved souls, each of you receive a gift from God. May your souls flourish, your spirits be resplendent in Light, your beings in harmony with God.

Be a channel of Love beloveds. Walk in grace. Allow the Truths to permeate your beings. Allow His Love to touch your heart.

God bless you. God bless you. I am Jesus and I have come to bless you, for each of you is precious to me. Each of you is beloved by God. Each of you has a tender heart. May that heart be filled with joy and gratitude. God bless you and keep you. I walk with you. God bless you.
