# You Will Know When God Places a Soul Upon Your Path

April 10<sup>th</sup>, 2017

Onlie Circle of Light, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloveds, it is Confucius. Oh, how my little birds love to flock together do they not! And how God loves to hear your songs put forward to Him, seeking that Touch of the Divine within your souls. Yes, we have guided this dear soul into your midst as we have told you many times that we will guide the earnest souls upon your path and many more come upon your path, my beloveds. And this shall be, at times, a torrent of earnest souls seeking, who will come upon your path seeking the wisdom that you have to share of your souls awakening and your life’s experiences.

Each of you can contribute to this, my beloveds. As you continue to open to God’s Love, as you continue to allow your souls to be awakened with this Blessing and Touch of Love. So you shall share with your brothers and sisters the joy of this awakening, the beauty of this awakening, the flowering of love within your souls. You will draw in many souls who feel the warmth of those burning fires within your souls, who seek to be close to you beloveds because you seek and desire to be a channel of love in this world.

It is very simple. It is a beautiful unfolding of gifts and intentions all illuminated by love. You will know when God places a soul upon your path, you will feel the connection of your soul to that soul and you will know that it is a gift. It will be a blessing to you to be used to help and love another and in this, you will find joy. In this, you will find your true selves as you share this part of you with another and as they are blessed through you, you shall be blessed, as you give, you shall be given to, as you seek to serve, so God will fulfill your desire to do so.

Allow the love to flow my beloveds. Do not place expectations of the mind upon this but be open, accepting, embracing another soul, no matter the injury or the error, or the lack of love. Be that channel, beloved souls, clear, abundant, joyful. As you walk this Earth you will bring light wherever you go. Though it be one soul placed in front of you or a hundred souls in front of you, know that God will use you to touch those souls and in this touch there is a beautiful blessing that goes out into the world and in your desire and effort to love, a beautiful blessing comes to you, beloveds.

You will not want for anything, my beloveds, for your souls will be full and joyful. Your experience in this world will be rich and rewarding. When it comes time to pass into the realms of spirit you will look back and see that you have done well in your efforts to serve mankind.

God will guide you. There is nothing to worry about, my beloveds. Seek each day in joyful anticipation and know that God will bring the opportunities to you, surprising in their manifestations, joyful in the unique qualities of each experience. Share your love, beloveds. Share your love and know that you are loved and that that love shall grow for all eternity upon the Path Divine as you seek at-onement with God.

Your lights shine forth, beloveds. Your lights shine forth. God bless you. I am Confucius and you know that I love you. God bless you.
