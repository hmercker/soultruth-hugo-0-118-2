# On the Name for the Divine Love Movement

August 17<sup>th</sup>, 2017

Prayer Session, Swakopmund, Namibia

Martin Luther & Jesus received by no name

Good morning my dear, it is **Martin Luther** and I have been with you and you are contemplating in seriousness the attempt of giving your movement a name. I have to giggle a bit because….. aren’t we human….aren’t we human in our approach, as for most things we like to use our language to express ourselves. This is what we are taught, yet this movement is about expressing your soul, expressing yourself through that unseen part that people cannot see with their eyes or hear with their ears. That which is yet so alive within each person and I am talking about the presence of a soul. We do know that some souls are -as is mentioned in the Padgett Messages- *"dead"*. I want to express that each human being is having implanted a soul and I am talking from that aspect.

It is within the awakening of the soul - in its desire to find a closeness to its Creator- to seek this Love, to seek Truth, (answers to) the question about its existence. We had many philosophers of past who were inspired through the spiritual gifts they had, through their soul knowing or awareness they had and yet much was debated from the mind; as it was in the history of Christianity. Thus my dear, dear daughter and charge, I would recommend to approach this topic from your soul knowing.

You did put forth your contemplations within your prayer and in your call for help. I am here to answer this call and give you advice. I will not give you the answers directly as you need to seek it yourself from your soul. I wish to put forth however: what is the benefit of having a name? what is the benefit if you can't even put the whole teachings into a long name? No, my dear, the foolishness of the human mind has created many wars and I caution about that restriction. If someone doesn't like this and another one doesn’t like that (name), yet you all know what you seek for, you all know from within your soul where you want to be. So, I also would recommend to seriously consider what is being discussed, to seriously contemplate this, because of the great restriction you impose yourself under, if indeed there is a name given (declared).

Each individual’s path is different, each individual has a different background, each individual soul has different gifts and abilities to express themselves in this world. Have we not asked you to plummet your souls, to go to the depths of your souls to find that connection, that soul connection to reveal that to the world, to reveal your gifts? God is working through these gifts in this world and they cannot be summoned under one name. No my dear, you sense my sentiments, however I wish for you to engage your own searching in your own communions with God and I, through my experience of life, can only present the pitfalls and the dangers of a human trait, of using their language in describing  something that is incredibly individual and beyond words. It is beyond words this Love, it is so powerful and it would be truly self-restricting to give ONE name to this.

*[…longer pause…]*

I am here, **Jesus**. My daughter, I thank you for your contemplations and your desire to find the right answers of the questions posed. My love, my dear sister, have I granted a name to my movement? Have I given a name to what i present, to what I do, to what I live for, to what i work for? Have I constructed a church or a temple when on earth? How have I lived my life- but for a name? You know the answers. Live your life how I lived it. Live your life like you know it: from the depths of your soul in communion with God, in at-onement with God, in carrying His Love into this world and have we not spoken of being a channel of Love, obedient to His Will. Yes my sister, you know the answers, you know the answers. Can they be placed in one word? No, they cannot. Why than would you give another person a word, a crumb of something that is not reflective of my teachings, that is not reflective of the Truths that are available? No. You want to share your bread, you want to share the table with all the delicious foods that are presented. You want to share the word of God, you want to share the knowledge of the depths of prayer - this wonderful nourishment for each soul - to explore the Living Waters in their own journey.

My dear sister, the temptations of this world put (on) restrictions, put you in dilemmas and as you know, always reach for the highest. Seek the Highest, seek the Love, seek to be a servant of God's Will and wishes and desires and nothing can stand in your way - not even the name of a movement. So thus my dear, it is your choice how you wish to express yourself and it would be nice for this movement to be bonded in a deeper sense, but that sense needs to be acknowledged. What is unifying this movement? You are not on the *"same page"* because not everybody is inclined to use the same words to express that inner feeling, that inner knowing of God's Love. How can you express yourself in one word or one sentence of what it is that you follow?

My dear, have I been restrictive in the way to think? What is the use of parables, like I did? I wanted to connect with the common man, with the person in front of me using the topics and themes that they were familiar with and demonstrating the Truths through that – it was my gift. So, as you have your gifts- it is demonstrating God's Truths through that- is it not? God is doing the work, God is doing the work (for the one) who desires to be in His service, who desires to be a channel of His Love, who desires to take on its soul fulfilling potentials, who is willing to let go of all restrictions- be it through names, be it through the environment, be it through encrustations- to break free and yearn for the nearness to our Father and yearn for this Love with an ever growing awareness of its presence, awareness of its power- ever ready to be in service, to be ready to live the Truths and speak the Truths for the individual to understand that stands in front of you, that touches your soul. That person is ready for the Truth and God is doing the work in their souls.

So, my dear beloved sister, I am Jesus and I have walked this earth many, many years ago. I am present to continue my work of bringing Gods Truth into this world. Do I bring a name when I come? Do I bring an announcement when I come? No my dear, I am drawn by the Love, I am drawn by the desire of the individual to be with me and the individual who knows my Truths as I have taught them and teach them, that is when I am drawn. You know yourself, through the messages of our beloved brother Padgett, how I shy away from congregations of people who pray to me as a god, who pray and believe in me that my blood sacrifice redeems them. Oh, you know how erroneous these teachings are and I understand that you do not wish to be identified with these teachings. However, they are prevalent in this world and there is a necessity to bring the correct teachings, to bring the Truth into this world. So yes, your search for Truth, your desire for Truth caused you to investigate. You have drawn to you my teachings, you have drawn to you the Truth and this is what souls are seeking for: Love and Truth - the nearness to their Creator.

So my dear, continue in your discussions, continue in your prayers, continue being engaged with people and reach them so that they can listen with their soul, so that they can listen to you with a soul knowing, with a soul understanding, with or without an awareness, that there is something deeper, there is something greater in this universe that they seek to love.

I greet you for this day, I am Jesus, your brother, your friend and the Master of the Celestial Heavens. I have spoken to you and through you. I hope my words bring you some relief and I thank Martin Luther who is here with me, that he granted our connection, that he was able to bring me close to give you the answers you seek for, as indeed, this is an important topic. In a way also a caution! My love for you is great and I come again. I am your brother and friend. It is Jesus and I have come. God bless you and I love you. May God continue to bless you and I wish you a good day.
