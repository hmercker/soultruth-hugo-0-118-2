# Lambs of God

May 8<sup>th</sup>, 2017

Retreat, Indio, CA, USA

Luke received by Al Fike

I wish to express an allegory to teach you as to why some souls are adventuresome and wish to experiment in their spiritual lives while others are happy to stick with the basicTruths and be content. For I believe that you are observing this reality within your larger group and you wonder about this. For each of you have a way of living the Truths in your lives.

Think of yourselves as the lambs of God. Each of you are tended by God, each of you are reliant upon God’s Blessings for your spiritual growth. Now God has laid a field before you and within that field is a paddock defined and protective, and those who desire to be very strict with the Truths of God’s Love desire to live within this paddock, this defined space, and are content there. Indeed, God does nourish them and God protects them within this place. He does not say *"go out of this paddock to the wider field"*, no, He says *"I am here to love and protect you. You have nothing to fear I am here"*.

And then there are those lambs that go in and out of this paddock. They venture forth and they seek more luscious grass on which to feed upon. They are curious of the outside world. They go forth and search, but they always return to the paddock, the safety of God’s Love within that place.

And then there are those who venture forth, often getting lost within their little journey of discovery. But still God ushers them back. His angels, the shepherds, encouraging them to return to this place of safety and defined ideas.

So my beloveds, you must have tolerance and have love for each of these beautiful lambs of God. Some who desire the safety and the restriction of that small paddock while others venture forth and they must satisfy their curiosity, while others get lost within their ambitions to learn more. But God is watching over each of these beautiful lambs of God and you are all lambs of God. Do not expect others to walk the identical path that you walk. No, my beloveds, this is not possible, for God gives each of his lambs free will, to walk as they wish. He offers His protection and Truth and Love to each and it is their choice where they will graze in this field of God’s Truth. This is the wonderment of God’s Creation, so much variety, versatility and there is no judgement, there is only Love. Even those who graze upon error are still loved, my beloveds. This does not mean that they will never know Truth, no, they will know Truth, they will come home eventually. God will guide them back.

And there is no one path back to God but many, many, back to the Source of Love. Every soul desires this to some degree and since every soul has a mind curious, and willful, and imbued with free will, they must take their journey whether that route be circuitous or straight, cautious or venturesome. Every soul is unique, every path unique. You, my beautiful lambs, have discovered the Great Truth of God’s Love and because of this, you will never stray too far from God’s beautiful, protective paddock of Truth. Yet God allows you to continue to explore the world of Truth and He shines His Love upon

you. How blessed you are, how blessed every soul is, who ventures forth to seek the Truth and know that each soul on their journey towards God is your brother and sister. Give them Love, accept their path and give them Love. If that opportunity arises, you may show them the beautiful sweet grasses of His Truth of Love and share this. Beloved souls, may you be blessed with the bravery to go forth into the world, amongst those who are different and be blessed with tolerance, love, and acceptance for your

brothers and sisters. Knowing that God shall always usher you back to His Truth, His Love shining upon you, His protection always with you. As you mingle amongst those who have different idea of the mind, whose realities and experiences continue in many ways, foreign ways to you at times. You can, with the eyes of your soul, recognize the beauty of that soul, the magnificence of a unique creation of God.

Step forth, beloved souls, into the world, step forth. God is with you. Thank you, my beloveds, and I love you. I am Luke. God bless you.
