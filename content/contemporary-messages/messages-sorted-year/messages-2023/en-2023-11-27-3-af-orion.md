# The Winds of Change Coming to Our World

November 27<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

There will be many angels and your stellar friends present at your meetings. We endeavour to educate those who come about many things. Most importantly, to inspire others in their relationship with their Creator. For as you well know, this is the key to all openings of truth. To receive the Essence of God within is the highest truth of all the universe, as God’s Love permeates every corner of the universe and this energy, this Essence of His Soul, can be obtained within each soul who asks for it.

There comes a time when your Earth will be in transition and have great difficulties. But before that time, we are encouraging you to continue to travel and make connections in many parts of the world in order to prepare those who are willing to listen so that they are in the light and capable because of their preparations, their soulful awakening, to deal with what is coming, to be a positive force in this world of yours and to help others cope with what will be a combination of tragedy and great change.

In the end, this change will be seen as a great blessing. But as humanity goes through the transitional period, it will not seem to be such a blessing. Many will curse God for this, blaming God for all that transpires. Yet humanity has been the author of its fate. God, in His mercy, His Love for humanity, will make every effort to rectify the difficulties and terrible conditions that will come of human endeavour and the response to humans’ efforts to make the world in its own image.

This erroneous journey, this way of humanity that involves such mindful and wilful practice and expression will be contradicted by God. God will impose His laws, the Laws of Creation upon humanity, thereby righting the wrongs and bringing balance back to your Earth, bringing greater light to your Earth.

This is where we will continue to be a part of God’s plan, instruments of His Will, in order to help humanity adjust and to rectify the wrongdoings of their behaviours and ambitions and expressions. Truth must be told and efforts must be made to bring truth to the consciousness of humanity. One cannot continue to express such deep and profound error and dysfunction in the world and not have consequences.

Every planet, every expression of God, has the mandate to seek harmony. To be in harmony with the Laws of Creation. To be in harmony with light and, in many cases, with love. So, humanity must come to realise that the key to their misfortunes and dilemmas is in their capacity to love, their ability to see through the fog of their own mindful creations to that which is clear and evident in the truth, the truth of God’s making, the truth of God’s creation, the truth of your planet and what it is has been created to accomplish.

It is the seat of the beginnings of the human soul seeking identity, seeking the realisation of its potentials and expression. God has other purposes for this planet. But where you are concerned, it has provided the cradle of nurturing. It provides all that you require to be strong and vital, to explore your own true natures and capacities for creativity and expression in harmony with the laws of the universe.

Indeed, humanity is a very creative force filled with many ideas, inspirations that many endeavour to bring to realisation. Yet, all is tainted by the desire for material gain, for power and control. Indeed, there is an altruistic vein that runs through these things, but that altruism is perverted by human ambition. Humanity will be humbled. The power that they believe they have, the control that they believe is implemented in their lives and upon the Earth will be rendered impotent and useless.

Humanity will be in a state of great confusion, turmoil, unable to orient themselves to the new reality, the reality that does not have all the buffering and comforts that has been put into place by human endeavour. Rather, much will be wiped away. Outside forces and conditions, both physical and spiritual, will ensure that humanity cannot pull the levers of control.

Those things that are implemented upon the Earth in order to satisfy the ambitions, and needs of humanity will not be available on the Earth plane. Rather, something new and quite astounding will take its place. God will provide for His children. God will bring solutions to the great dilemmas that humanity has created for itself. Merciful God will allow the floodgates of love and support, wisdom and light to infuse the world as the endeavours of humanity evaporate before them.

So, a new way will come. A way unforeseen, a way so surprisingly different that many will not accept this and, in their decision to withdraw from the invitation offered by God to a new world, they will forfeit their physical bodies and enter into the world of spirit where they will continue in their progress without the physical. This too, is a great mercy upon humanity, for many within their mindsets and their insistence on reinforcing human reality, the human condition, they will not be able to accept that which is before them.

Yet, many will and many will come to the realisation that what is given is a gift. What is manifest on the Earth at that time, a time that comes soon, is far better, has far more possibilities and opportunities for the betterment of humanity than anything that humanity can create for itself. A hand of love is reaching out to humanity. But the changes are already beginning, and the time comes when these changes cannot be ignored.

The energetic conditions of the Earth are being transformed by forces beyond the capacity for humanity to recognise, at least from the status of the mind. They will come to see these things as their soul progresses, as that other part of them resplendent in many faculties and capacities to see and understand the universe to come, to know the great Creator of all the universe to come, and see and know each other as children of the universe and children of God.

With these realisations, will come a great revolution of thought and intention that will sweep the Earth. Every soul will be given the choice whether they aspire toward and accept this new world, or reject it. These things have been prophesied in your religions, although their ideas of what it entails and what it means are not correct. Yet indeed, the time of reckoning is coming.

Each of you will be given choice, the capacity to understand who you truly are and what is meant in life, what God created life to be and how that life is to be honoured, and all life is to be honoured. In this realisation will come many, many changes in your world. A rebuilding that will not take as long as you might think. Not hundreds and hundreds of years to rebuild civilisation, as you call it. But within a few generations, you will see the manifestation and acknowledgement of the wisdom and truth that will be given to humanity.

Great changes, indeed. We from another place and reality will be privileged to witness this and to take part in it. To be nursemaids to the birthing of a new era of humanity, a new way of being in the world. A way that is beyond your ability to understand at this time. But within every soul there is an inkling. There is a feeling that great change is coming. Indeed, many beautiful outcomes and blessings will be upon all on this Earth. This brings us to great excitement, anticipation, for we see more clearly than you.

We see how the energies and forces and trends of change are conspiring to bring what is needed for the continued flourishing of your Earth and the flourishing of humanity. So, we will walk together, my friends, into a new world, a world that is more beautiful, more harmonious, more suited to the true nature of humanity. Because that which has blanketed the Earth for so long, the endeavours of mankind, will dissolve and blow away on the winds of change.

You will find yourself naked before God with no other choice than to go to the great Creator of all and beseech God for help that you may be lifted up and guided, that the truth may truly be given in a conscious and clear way to all peoples on the Earth. God will not deny each individual their right of expression and being upon the Earth. Cultures, collective civilisations, all things that are a creation of humanity will persist, but will be expressed in a different way.

Life will become simplified. Humanity will learn once again the value and purpose of human connection, human bonds, families and all manner of collective efforts that will not be dedicated toward the empowerment and enrichment of the few, but will be focused upon the enrichment of the many in regards to their lives. Even your economies will be transformed and changed into something different. Inventions will be recalibrated and reassessed so that they are in greater harmony with what is needed in your world and what is harmonious with the natural world. We will play a part in helping you to come to these insights and capacities to recalibrate the technologies of your civilisations.

This will be our role. Not only this, but to teach the fundamentals of soul awakening and understanding. We will not be the only ones. In this new world, your spirit friends, the high angels and lights in spirit will have the ability to manifest before you and to help guide you, for they know what it is to be human and the struggles that have taken place upon your planet. Thus, they make for better teachers for all of you.

Yes, much will change and many new openings and awarenesses will befall humanity. Your capacity to communicate will change. We will teach you how to use what you call telepathy in order to communicate with one another. This amongst many other things. So, there will be a great leap of human progress and evolution that will come very quickly indeed.

This is why we are here, to help facilitate and witness this great shift of consciousness. To help usher in a new world, a new way of being in the world, to be your brothers and sisters truly, to come not only in peace, but in love and acknowledgement of our dear cousins, our dear brothers and sisters. You are part of God’s great creation, the great universe that continues to unfold toward harmony.

So, our motivation is great. Our expectation is great. Our view of your future is clear enough, my friends. We will endeavour to do everything possible to help you to awaken and to adjust, to shift into a new way of being and expression in a world cleansed and made fresh again by the Hand of God.

May you be blessed in light and wisdom. May you come to understand that you step upon the threshold of something magnificent, that your true selves, all that you possess, the treasures that lay within you will have greater possibility of expression, and of your own recognition as these changes take place.

We stand together, truly together upon this threshold. May you have the excitement that we have, the anticipation that we have. The joy of what is to come, for we are together. We are truly together in this wondrous transformation of the world. I will leave you now, my beloved friends, and I will come again. God bless you in love. I am Orion. God bless you in light.
