# The Road Ahead is Clear

November 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

I am Keea Atta Kem. The road ahead is clear, though you may, in your minds, not understand the details, the twists and turns of this road. Yet indeed, within your soul you know full well that God has a plan and that plan is unfolding as it should. Because you are listening to God's whispers, to the guidance that is coming and you are seeing the journey ahead. Though, as I say, within your mind you may add details and project certain outcomes that are more of a material nature, the flow of God's Will is strong within each one of you. The outcomes of God's plan continue to manifest and be a part of your life. Provided you are upon that path, listen and in some ways are obedient to God's Will, then no harm will come to you, beloved souls.

All plans, all efforts will be blessed. The unfolding, the outcomes of those efforts will be harmonious. Though the conditions of your Earthly plane may cause some form of interference or challenge, the outcomes will be in accordance to God's Will. Thus, every day, to some degree, is a test of faith.  Every day is an opportunity to grow, to learn, to serve and to be in harmony with all that is of God's creation and Love. There is little that can harm you, my beloved souls, for you are surrounded by your angel friends. You are given great latitude and blessings as you move forward in your life.

Remember that God always honours free will. That the pace and movement of your progress forward is determined by your choices, by the exertion of your gift of free will. On this Earth of yours, very little comes without a struggle. Without some form of reconciling your material mind, its ideas and constructs, with that of your soul that has very different perceptions and understanding, the struggle continues daily. You continue to find your way, weaving through the forest of thoughts to that open field where the clarity and light exists. We help you upon the way. We assist you and are by your side as you weave through this forest, as you exert yourself to find the truth, to open to what is God's Will, what is intended. Yet, as this process continues to be a part of your life, you are also growing and expanding in God's Love so that indeed, the road becomes straightened and clear. Because you have exerted the capacities and perceptions of your soul and given that you have empowered that with love, you walk along this path expanding in this awareness, this understanding of the truth.

Thus, even though you are still engaged within the material world, and to some degree the human condition, this other part of you continues to grow, strengthen and awaken. Thus, as you awaken, the journey of service to God, of gifts manifest and expressed, becomes easier and less cluttered by your mind. Pray for clarity, beloved souls. Pray for insight.  Pray for all that which God wishes to give to you in order that the journey may be eased, clear and fulfilling.

You continue to find yourself with each day upon this path. With the blessings of each day, the journey is eased and brightened by the blessings of God. Yes, little comes without a struggle. Yet, as you strain and continue to challenge yourself, continue to seek guidance, continue to accept and embrace the reality of your own soul and God's blessings upon it, your journey will be filled with light, will be an expression of joy and peace. It is your destiny, my beloved brothers and sisters. It is your destiny. Let go of your worries and cares, your concerns, those things that pull you from God. Be within the light, flow, peace and joy of God's Will and Touch upon you. You will find yourself within the flow of His Will, and this will be glorious, beautiful and joyful.

You are blessed my beloved, beloved friends and shall be all the days of your life. No matter what may come you will have the strength, tenacity and ability to overcome any challenges. As you strengthen in God's Love, as you overcome, it will not feel as if you are burdened. Rather, the challenge will intrigue you. You will feel as if it is a blessing. You will know the blessings of the journey. All will come as an onrush of God's blessings upon you meeting the power of darkness and negativity that is inevitable on your earthly plane, neutralizing these things as you walk in faith, as you walk in light, as you call out to God and say, *"Guide me, dear Father. Keep me upon this Path of Love. Show me the way. Show me the way."*

God bless you, my beloveds. I am happy that you are together in this short time of strengthening your bonds and opening yourselves to one another in love. Great harmony ensues with this effort, with your friendships. The possibilities open as a result of this time. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Keea Atta Kem. I love you, each of you so precious, a light, so many gifts, so unique. You are blessed by God deeply. We are joyed by the outcomes and results of these blessings. We are joyed because we have friends upon the earthly plane who truly understand God's Love and laws. God bless you. I love you. God bless you.
