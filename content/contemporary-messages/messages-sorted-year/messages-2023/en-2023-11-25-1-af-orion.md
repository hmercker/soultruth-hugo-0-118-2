# Acknowledges Our Soul Progression

November 25<sup>th</sup>, 2023

DV Dozen, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God’s blessings to you, my beloved friends. I am Orion and I wish to talk about your progress, not necessarily the progress we have made in developing what you call Direct Voice, but the progress you have made within your souls, your attitudes and your minds. It was not that long ago that you would not entertain the idea that someone like myself would be involved in the project that you are engaged within. Yet, I have found acceptance and love among you and this warms my heart greatly. Do you not think this is progress?

Your minds are open and your souls receptive. Therefore, you are allowing a greater expansion of consciousness and understanding of the universe as you progress with your souls’ awakening in Divine Love and small but important patterns within your life have changed as well. Many of you are not as judgmental as you were, nor as fearful. For these two things often go hand in hand. You’ve come to accept the Will of God and accept the earthly conditions for what they are and not be so invested in controlling and desiring to control others who are making their free will choices in the world.

Acceptance is important. Allowing yourself to be a loving channel no matter what condition you are encountering, being somewhat detached from the behaviors of others that you know are destructive. This is not to say that you have developed a callous perspective or a hardened heart. Rather, it is the opposite. You see more and you feel more. Yet, you are not willing to invest what you see and feel as an outcome of changing another individual. Rather, you are respecting the process of change and evolution within that individual’s life. You may bring some words of wisdom forward and that is encouraged but when done so in love and respect and there is not that edge of expectation and neediness within yourself to see the outcomes that you desire to see. It is with your own view of yourself. You’ve come to accept the humanity within you but also to have faith in the process of change and evolution that is happening within you. Therefore, you have patience for yourself and not so much judging and having great frustration with those human patterns that are so established within you. In this way, I would suggest that when you are aware of patterns that you know are not to your benefit, and do not help you to grow in love, that you may stop yourself and change those patterns.

For God gave you the gift of free will. God allows you to choose and so you might choose something of greater light. If you do not, I do not suggest that you beat yourself up, that you point fingers at yourself or blame others for your behavior. Rather a prayer is important, a prayer to God to ask that He help you to overcome these small barriers and conditions within you that often cause you grief. In the great scheme of things, the progress that you have made is remarkable in the short time that you have been on this earthly plane. Indeed, you will reap the rewards of this progress when you transition into the spirit world. Each of you is assured of a place of light. None of you are in the darkness or the semi-light. None of you will progress into lower spheres. Rather, you will see yourselves in places of joy and light. Of course, what the final outcome will be is entirely up to you and your decisions and choices to be made from now until that time. Yet, you understand the laws. You understand what it is that you must do to improve your own spiritual condition, your light.

These things are not difficult to understand. Nor are they that difficult to implement. It is when you allow yourself to accept and be within that condition so familiar to you that you make no effort to change. Effort is required, as always. We have spoken of effort many times. You understand that in order to make progress or in order to accomplish anything on your earthly plane or even in the planes of spirit, you must make the effort necessary in order to accomplish the goal. It is your will that determines this. It is certainly what is in your minds’ ambitious ideas that you possess that will govern the course of action. I’m not suggesting that you become a very willful individual for this, too, is not beneficial. For when you are in alignment with God, your will and God’s Will merge into a harmonious flow that is of great benefit to you and also serves God. This is ideal. Humility, being in the grace of God, knowing the Will of God, receiving greater inflowing of God’s Love, awakening further within your soul, gleaning the wisdom within your soul, expressing this wisdom, being true to your soulful self. These things we encourage and you know this.

Yet, in this message, I wish to acknowledge each of you in your efforts. None have fallen so hard that they cannot pick themselves up and they have. None are so misguided that they have strayed off the straight and narrow course that has been laid before you, each of you, that unique course that God has laid out for you. None are so immersed in darkness that they cannot find their way to light. I know there are times when you feel very dark. You feel the conditions of darkness powerfully and you feel somewhat hopeless when you are in this condition. You know when you are in that condition, that you are subject to those pockets of darkness within yourself.

In doing so, you have opened the door for dark forces to reinforce and strengthen that condition. Yet, this is where the great struggle is. Is it not my friends, to overcome these conditions, to look them squarely in the eye and say, *"I do not wish to be a part of this any longer. I chose light. I choose to be free from these old conditions that have so powerfully and subtly invaded my consciousness. I am strong. I am with God. Though I may have fallen, I know that you will help me rise up into greater light?"*

This is God’s great love for all humanity, all the beautiful souls that populate His universe of creation. That love is always there. That compassion is always there. That forgiveness is always possible for each of you. For when you receive the great gift of God’s Love, you are engaging in the process of forgiveness, of transformation, of wholeness, of brightness. The capacity to love grows within you. Many of you have great capacities for love and express this daily. Some of you are shy and do not express these things readily to strangers and yet, you emanate the light. You emanate the glory of love within your soul. This is the most powerful blessing and gift you can give another, that you can give the world, to emanate that light of love, a soul resplendent in the great gift of God’s Essence sparkling, shining and glowing, a great channel of love created by this transformation, these changes that are happening within you.

Each of you is well on your way on this path. Each of you sees yourself differently. Each of you sees the world differently now. Look back. Look at your life. Look at the changes that have happened through decades of effort, prayer and desire. Soon comes the culmination of these efforts where God will need you and God will enlist you as His channels in powerful and awesome ways. For you are being prepared. You are being cleansed. You are being strengthened and you are being educated of the ways of truth and love and light. You continue to grow in these things. You are the beautiful souls emerging. Like the butterfly from the pupae, the chrysalis, you emerge in all your glory and beauty, each one unique, each one wonderful and beautiful in your being. For God has created you and God has given you many gifts. God is awakening you to those gifts. As those gifts shine forth, as you shine forth in your true self and being, so the beauty is evident and the wonderment of you as God’s unique creation is glorious.

May you be blessed upon the journey, my beautiful friends. I am honoured to witness the changes. All that you release and all that you gain, all that is you emerging, new and fresh, vulnerable, yet, is strengthened by the love of God. You are a wonderment. You are truly glorious. God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and I am humbled to witness your transformation. God bless you. God bless you all. I love you.
