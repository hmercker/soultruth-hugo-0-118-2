# You Each Choose Your Soul’s Journey

November 1<sup>st</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine. May your souls open wide to the inflowing of God's Love so that you may be awakened more fully to the Truth and reality of God. My beloved and beautiful students, God lays before you many possibilities, many options, many roads to take. Because, in God's universe, the flow of free will is accommodated by the many possibilities that God lays before you. Therefore, you are able to choose. You are able to step forward in many different directions. Yet, each one of these paths will lead, in time, to the same place.

Indeed, the timing is determined by you and your choices. Are you going to take a meandering path or one that is straight, clear and direct? It is for you to decide. In this, God will accommodate your choices. We will accommodate your choices. For we have existed long enough that we understand that God's Will is not done by direct fiat and expression. It is done by the meanderings of humanity, headed toward the direction that is intended by God.

Indeed, the timing of such cannot truly be determined, merely the trends and directions of human endeavor and expression. Therefore, when we speak of changes coming, when we speak of these conditions that are changing, we speak of many things in which the world will change. We cannot pinpoint a date for this. We merely share with you the trends that we observe through our soul's perceptions. Indeed, we can see the outcomes more clearly than any of you, but we are all subject to the great force of free will. Therefore, the many choices that are being made, millions and millions of moments of time, will determine the direction and flow of God's Will realised, God's plan unfolding.

God does not orchestrate people. You are not puppets on a string. You are each endowed with a powerful gift of free will. You have desires. You have perceptions. You have conditions within you, conditions that, at times, inhibit you and, at other times, propel you forward. These conditions are exhibited because you have chosen so. You have chosen an expression of something within you. Thus, we are urging you to choose the highest, to be within the flow of God's Will, to know the great blessings of God's Love within your soul, to acknowledge your soul, to be upon the path toward the fulfillment of your purpose and destinies, and to do so with the clarity and desire to know God's Will regarding your life, who you are, what God has placed before you, which is unique to you.

Much is given to each of you, beloved souls. Many blessings flow forth. You are uplifted and upheld in love and light. Indeed, as we make our efforts on your behalf, as God blesses you with His great Soul touching yours, then the possibility of making choices for the highest is far greater than if you were left merely with your own resources, your own perceptions. Indeed a great deal of effort has been made to help you along the path.

I urge you not to be engaged in worries and cares about the condition of the Earth, the condition of humanity and these things that are dark and heavy. For this is God's problem, so to speak, not yours specifically. Although we do encourage you to pray for the world, pray for your loved ones and pray for humanity. This is important. But to get caught up in the anxieties, worries and cares of the human condition is not what you were intended to do, though it is easy to do so. We understand this. It is more important that you are with God, and you are guided by God. That your gifts, your very being, becomes an active agent of God so that these conditions may be ameliorated and diffused. So that darkness may turn to light, that sorrow may turn to joy, that ignorance may turn to truth, that those who feel so unloved may come to know love. These are the things that are important, not the prattlings and endeavours of humanity in many different avenues.

I say release your concerns, beloved souls. Come to God in all the fullness of faith, desire and openness that you can express toward your Heavenly Father. In this way the burdens of the world will dissipate and the openings to God's Will and to your own soul will be strengthened.

My beloved and beautiful children of God, students of truth, seek God and His Love. Seek to know this with every breath. Seek to be in that place of grace of which our dear daughter speaks, that this may be your lot at all times. That whatever may be within your life, whatever the circumstances are, you may approach them with love, with a stillness within, a peace and wisdom. This is so that you may come to some resolution, come to greater harmony with all things in your life.

Yes, God will guide you. Yet, it is your choice how you will express that guidance, how you will move upon the pathways that are presented to you, how you choose with every moment, every breath. These things are important to understand and to realise in your lives so that you may follow the path that is not circuitous, but straight. It will bring the greatest rewards, blessings, opportunities to serve and the greatest light in your life. Understand that that choice is with you at every moment. Understand that when you choose God and choose the grace of God to be your life, your experience in life, then all goodness, all that is meant to be, all that is in harmony will come to you. Those things that are not in harmony, that are also an expression of this world, will not lead you to darkness or despair, or even a reaction of anger and distress. Rather, you will be at peace. You will see these things from a place that is not engaged. Although you will see, you will not feel these human reactions that are so common and accessible to your mind.

Be at peace, my beloved and beautiful children of God. Know that you are loved, that we care for you, that God is caring for you, is protecting you and guiding you, so the outcomes of your life will always be in harmony. No matter how long it may take to get to that place of harmony, you shall arrive. It shall be so. Each of you has your paths to take. Each of you has your choices to make. God has His Hand upon you, each one. You are blessed. You shall be blessed, and the road shall lead to greater light, greater service, greater harmony. It is given in love. It shall always be in harmony with the Laws of Love.

May you be blessed, beloved souls, truly, deeply blessed. Know this from the very core of your being to the consciousness of the present. May you know this truly. God bless you. I am your teacher Augustine. My love is with you always, beloved souls, always. God bless you.
