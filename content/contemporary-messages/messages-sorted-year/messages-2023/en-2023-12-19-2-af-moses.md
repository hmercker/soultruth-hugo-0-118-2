# We Are All Innocent Little Children in the Eyes of God

December 19<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Moses received by Al Fike

The world waits for change. It is pensive. The souls of humanity wonder about their future. I am Moses and I come a great distance to be with you. Yet I come to join the ranks of those who pray to be with you in this light, this blessing from God. For it is God's desire to calm the fears, to bring reassurance and love to humanity.

Though the world waits for change, it is important that for each individual soul that they set forth upon the path of change for themselves, overturning the darkness and becoming the light, putting aside the judgments and being a channel of love, rejecting all that is not of truth. My beloved, beautiful friends of Earth, this must happen. The world must change. You, each one of you, understand the journey that must be taken, the road that is ahead. I come to encourage you upon that road that you may find your way beyond conditions that are so very human and of material mind, that you will clear the way towards the soul and become a child of soul, a child of God, a child of truth. I use the word child because to walk this path, to be within the soul and to know the truth is to adopt innocence, faith and trust as your foundation upon the journey, seeking the Love of God that will transform your soul, seeking all that which is of your innocent soul, all that which is of light.

You must release conditions that do not reflect love, light and truth. A tall order for you on Earth, so much easier for us in the realms of light. These realms are not polluted by human thought and conditions that detract and distract you. Be strong, my friends. Walk upon this road that is of light. God will ensure that your feet are firmly planted thereon, that you are upheld, that you walk with great confidence and faith on this road.

You are needed now more than ever, for this pensive world continues to spiral into fear, confusion and darkness. Many lost souls are drawn into this darkness, many out of confusion, many out of ignorance. You must be stalwart in light, strong, steadfast so that those who are seeking light, those who are lost, those who are afraid, they find comfort in you and your gifts and capacities to be God's channels of Love and Light.

You are needed. I beseech you, my dear friends of truth and of love, to continue to feed the hunger of your soul, to desire the awakening that comes with a great gift of Love from God. In this way you will be steadfast and you will not be drawn into the darkness. Rather, light will be within and around you always. We will be close. God's Hand will be upon you. I direct you toward light. I insist that you choose light above all else. This is what you desire. This is the road you must travel. Do not falter. Do not be distracted, but walk steadfastly toward the light. Beautiful souls, children of God, we are with you, shall continue to uphold you. For we love you so and God loves you so.

May you be blessed, beautiful children, blessed in light, innocence, joy, love, truth and wisdom, all things good and of God. May they come to you in an onrush of God's caring, blessing Touch upon you. God bless you. I am Moses, and I love you. Beloved friends, I love you.
