# Blesses and Closes Running Springs Retreat

December 4<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Running Springs Retreat, Sunning Springs, CA, USA

Augustine received by Al Fike

Beloved souls, I am your teacher, Augustine. Yes, you have accomplished much. You have made valuable contacts and have received many blessings. What more could you ask from a small group such as this than what has been blessed, opened and accomplished?

My dear son, you must listen to your guidance. Do not allow the pressures of this world to detract you from what you know is right and the journey that you must take. When you are on that path of change in your life, all things will open readily and there will be no struggle. Possibly the struggling mind accepting change, but this is for you to grapple with, a test, so to speak. I ask you to listen, my dear son. Listen to God’s guidance. Know God’s blessings. The way will open and you will know great joy in that opening.

Dear daughter with the flaming hair, you are a light. I am pleased that you have listened to your guidance and have entered into the chapel. Pray to be a light for those dear souls who are seeking, those who are in need, even the man who preaches. He needs your light and your counsel, dear daughter. Ask before your day begins to be guided and to hear the true guidance that might come, and your days will be filled with purpose. You will be a channel for blessings for many.

There is much work for you to do. There is much need all around you, dear daughter. You will find your way along this road of service and find fulfilment there, fulfilment like you have never had before, fulfilment that will fill your heart with joy. Seek for the Love of God, His Divine Essence to flow into your soul. Do so with earnestness daily. Several times a day, ask for this gift. This will ensure that you will be guided by the highest, that you will be protected, that you will be blessed deeply, beloved daughter.

Dear mistress of the house, you continue to seek the truth and find your way. Indeed, you are observing and using your capacities to feel, observe and understand to find your way. Know that you have an angel with you. Her name is Martha. Ask for her help. Ask that she may assist you in your daily efforts and your life will become easier and filled with light and times of grace and peace. For God loves you so, beloved daughter. God loves you so. You will know this Love, as the deepest love of all, will give to you all that you require to awaken in truth, to live in truth and to know the peace that passes all understanding. You are blessed, beloved daughter, and shall be blessed.

Beloved daughter to my right, the one who doubts herself so and yet, beyond these doubts is great work and great service to others. Can you not feel the momentum of God’s blessings upon you, the momentum of God’s guidance for you, the expression of your soul reaching out to many others who have great love and admiration for you, dear daughter? All these things indicate that you are truly blessed, beloved daughter, truly blessed upon the road, and you are well upon this road.

Do not allow the obstructions to hinder you, for in this earthly plane there are always obstructions, some great, some small. But it is for you to rise above the earthly condition and be in harmony with God and be at peace within yourself. You have been given blessings and gifts, beloved daughter, to help sustain you. Listen to your own heart, your own soul and its guidance that it gives, and you will find your way. You will know peace beyond understanding, joy beyond joy and love beyond love. It is for you to discover, to know and to experience all these blessings.

My beloved daughter, whom I have been with for many, many years, you know me and I know you. We love one another, do we not? Upon this journey we have been partners serving God in love and so it shall be for the rest of your days upon this earthly plane and beyond. God bless you.

Dear daughter whose gifts have opened, who has become a true and loyal servant of God, bringing the truth to whomever will listen, bringing comfort to many. In your quiet and gentle ways, you bring comfort. You bring truth and light to many and you are willing to listen to the word of God, the blessings of God, the guidance of God. You are guided. Your life unfolds as an expression of God’s Will and God’s Truth of Love. So, it shall be, my beloved and beautiful daughter. So, it shall be. It shall intensify and increase in magnitude and in your capacity to bless, as God’s channel, many, many souls. So, it shall be. You begin the opening of more gifts that you may be more equipped to do God’s work and to travel the world as His emissary of light and truth and love. Listen, listen to God’s urgings. Know that you are upon the path that you are destined to be on and you will know the outpourings of God upon you and through you to many souls, many souls.

When your time comes to pass into the world of spirit, you will be well satisfied with your ministry, with your efforts to serve. This time is not soon, my beloved daughter, I assure you. For there is much work for you to do and many places for you to visit. You are truly blessed with your guides and gatekeepers and angels that minister to you. God bless you, beloved daughter. God bless you.

My beloved friends, I am your teacher and you are my students. I shall continue to bring my words of wisdom to you. I shall continue to be a light for you, to uphold you and protect you, to guide you along the path, beloved souls. So much work to do, so much work to do. Listen and be in harmony and alignment with the Will of God and you will never go without a purpose, a blessing, some mission to accomplish, some way to reach out to another, some possibility to open greater gifts and, in so doing, bless others more deeply.

My beloved and beautiful students, you are cherished by the angels and loved by God. Acknowledge this within your life and live by the truth of love and all will unfold in harmony, in peace and in joy. God’s Love shall continue to pour within you, beloved souls. As this Love pours in and quenches the thirst of your soul, you will know more deeply the guidance, the purpose enfolding, of your journey of service for God.

God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine, and I love you dearly. Beautiful souls of light, beautiful souls filled with goodness and desire to love their brothers and sisters, God bless you. I honour you and I love you. God bless you.
