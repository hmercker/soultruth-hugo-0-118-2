# Comes to Reinforce the Desires of Our Souls

November 26<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Matthew received by Al Fike

The Love of God pours upon you at this very moment, beloved souls. I am Matthew and I come to pray with you and uphold you in your prayers. To help reinforce the desires of your soul to be with God. For how often do you have the opportunity to truly pray together, and to pray and be with God in the peace and quiet tranquility of your place in prayer? Your world is such a hectic place, having so many demands and calls upon you. Yet your soul requires its nurturing. It desires the nurturing of God and you must take time to feed your soul. To be with God within your soul so that you will find your way through the twists and turns of the human condition. That you may be freed from this condition, if but for a moment, and be with God in prayer, receiving His Love. Opening your souls so that His Love may pour in.

As you do so, your soul is strengthened. Your capacities for prayer are more intense. The blessings that come are more readily felt as you put into practice the way and means of receiving God’s Love as you have been taught and as you put into practice the expression of love within you, through you and around you as you have been asked to do. These things are so simple. But in your complicated world, its simplicity is lost and surrounded by the human condition. Thus, your life and what you truly need in your life, becomes a complicated matter. So, we come to remind you time and time again to seek that gift as often as you might. It is important to set the foundation of your soul in light and love and the Truth of God’s Love so that as you live your life, it is determined and informed by this Truth within you.

When your time comes to enter into our world, you will be well prepared. The workings of God’s Love upon your soul, cleansing and releasing the encrustations around it, will have great benefits for you. No matter where you are, whether you are on the Earth plane or in the spirit world, you will know the benefits of the power of love working within your souls. Do not take this for granted, my beloved friends. But seek it earnestly, with great desire so that you may receive in great abundance this Love and know the Touch of God, the grace of God upon you, the peace of God upon you. The Light of God, the Light that is of God’s creation may be with you always, protecting you, upholding you and bringing to you many good and wondrous gifts and blessings.

These things I say are simple truths. But indeed, for you to progress and understand things more complex, you must come to know these simple truths to be your own. To live by them. To be in the grace of God because you have accepted and integrated these truths into your life. In this you will be greatly blessed. You will have something that few have in your world. That is peace within, wisdom within and a greater capacity to love not only yourself, but others. These things are important. These things are necessary in order to truly find the happiness and joy that you seek. So, we pray together, my beloved friends. Pray for this gift. Ask God to open your souls wide, that you may be blessed accordingly. Within yourself, go deep and seek out the true longings of your soul. In this way you invoke the laws and receive the blessings.

May God bless you, beloved souls. I am Matthew and I love you. I honour you, each one seeking for the highest, seeking to be redeemed in Love, transformed in Love, awakened in Love. God bless you. My love is with you. God bless you.

Jesus: Brother Jesus has walked amongst you and blessed each one of you. Take this light and blessings and use it in your life to be a light in the world. Do not withhold love from your brothers and sisters, but be strong in light and love, in wisdom, in truth. Though that strength may be quiet and unassuming, the strength of light, the strength of your presence in the world will have its impact and effects upon your world. Be in harmony with all you meet. Be a loving channel for God and all will be well, my beloved friends. All will be well.

I am your brother and friend, Jesus, and I come to again uphold you, to again be with you in prayer. And to bring the blessings of heaven upon you. God bless you, my beloved friends. I am with you always. Never to forsake you, my friends, but to walk with you for all eternity. God bless you. God bless you all in His Love
