# We Ask You to Shine Bright as God’s Instruments

November 5<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father’s Love continue to flow into your souls, my beloved brothers and sisters. I am Jesus. I come to be with you in prayer. The fabric of darkness that enshrouds your world is being torn away by your intentions and prayers. Do you see how you are spread out around the world to many places? Many avenues which God may work in order to dissolve this darkness, in order to bring the truth of love forth. Though you may in your lives have very few who listen and understand the truth that you bring, the effects of your prayers, the dissolution of darkness around you, is having an impact upon your Earth and shall continue to be so as you continue to pray and are steadfast to the truth. Thus, you become my disciples, beloveds. Thus, you become God’s instruments in the world.

This work that you take on by merely being one who prays for and is dedicated to the gift of God’s Love, opens the gateway to greater light to the world. Be that instrument, beloved and beautiful brothers and sisters. Be that light in the world. Recognize this within your souls, within your being, that you are truly connecting with God and all that is of God. All that is meant to flow from God through you and into you will continue to be and to flourish in light. Carry the torch of truth wherever you go. Consider yourselves God’s instruments. Even when you go about mundane matters in the world, you are being used by God as an instrument of light. When you pray to be close to God, you reinforce this light. You awaken further light within you and further truth as you receive, drop by drop, the blessings of His Love.

Soon, many of you will know and realise the transformation is taking place. It will be a conscious thing that lives within you. As it does live within you, so your consciousness will change, shift and come under greater alignment with truth. Your capacity to love, your capacity to understand truth, to express the wisdom of truth, to be a channel of love will grow. With each breath, God is continuing to assist you upon this journey of awakening. Angels are with you always, my beloveds. We endeavour to ensure that our beloved brothers and sisters on Earth are surrounded, protected and blessed with light. Not only do you benefit from this gift, but as God’s instruments, the power of your instrumentality, your gifts, is magnified by the presence of angels. With this beautiful relationship with your Heavenly Father and your Celestial friends, there is a great power and flow of light that will continue to help tear the fabric of darkness away and bring greater light to the world.

Do not take this for granted, beloved souls. Do not think of yourselves as unworthy of this great blessing and possibility of instrumentality. Rather, walk in the world with your head held high, with a sense that God is with you. With a listening ear that you may hear the guidance that God has for you. With open eyes, so that you may see the effects of God’s work through you. With an open mind, so that you may not block any possibility or opportunity to serve as His instruments.

Walk in the light, beloveds. Know that you are beyond just flesh and mind. That indeed, you possess a soul, the great jewel, the great gift that makes you truly who you are. In this journey of awakening through the Father’s Love, you will find yourself in the truth and light that is meant for you and meant for the world. So much awaits those who are willing. So much work for those who ascribe to God’s Will and wish to be His instruments.

Be in alignment with God’s Will, beloved souls. Know your way with God. Do not allow the material obstructions to hold you from God. Be pure and true to your soul’s desires. Set forth on the wings of prayer that you may truly be in alignment with your Creator. In this, all of us who are by your side, who continue to minister to you, who continue to protect and guide you, will find the ways and means of doing so, reinforced by prayer, faith and desire within you.

You are His children. We are all His children. We walk the Path Divine together. You are benefitted by all the blessings, all that God wishes to give those who are truly His children, those who make that choice to come to God in this simple, pure and powerful way. This highest of truths is yours. Our desire, as is God’s, is that many, many will follow the way to at-onement with Him. We ask you to shine bright, beloved souls. To be that beacon of truth in the world, to truly share the word, the Truth of God’s Love to many. You may do so by your actions, your thoughts, your light, your words. In many ways, God will use you to bless humanity, to be that instrument of love, truth and light.

May you be blessed, beloved souls. Blessed, guarded and filled with a sense of how loved you are, that peace may be upon you, beloveds. That you are embraced by God, fully embraced by your Heavenly Father and taken on the journey of truth, love and joy that is meant for every soul. Accept the gifts. Accept the blessings. Accept all that God has for you. Your life shall be an expression of His blessings upon you as greater harmony ensues in your life and deeper truth grows within you. It is given. You are blessed, beloveds. We shall continue together upon the path.

I am your brother and friend. I am Jesus, and I love you. I love each and every one of you. You are truly a part of my family. You are my disciples to bring truth to the world. God bless you. My love is with you always. God bless you, beloved souls of the Earth.
