# A Embrace and Walk the Truth of God’s Love

September 26<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circles, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come to you, beloved souls. I am Jesus, your brother and friend. I walk with you as you continue to seek the Love of God within your souls, to affirm this truth that I taught so long ago. I walk with you because you are the light and the truth that the world needs. Though many of you do not see yourself in this role, I say to you, embrace this role. Embrace the truth that lives within your soul. Embrace the love that is building and its expression comes as you seek to serve God. Much comes to those who are seeking the highest of truths, the greatest of God's blessings. Though you may approach this with some reservations and you walk somewhat timidly along the path, I say to you, as the love grows within your soul, so you will gain confidence, so you will come to know the truth in such a way that there is no doubt and that there are no barriers that restrict you as you walk this path and serve God and love.

The flow of truth will change you, beloveds. The understanding of God will embolden you as you walk in the world or you will feel and know that God's Hand rests upon you, that you are truly blessed by his Love, the Essence of his Great Soul transferred to your soul and thus, the law of transformation takes place as you are being reborn in love, reborn with the Essence of God within. This cannot help but change your attitudes, your thinking and your actions. For the power of God's Love is the greatest power of all. The transformation that comes is deep and all-encompassing so that when you walk along life's path, you will be changed and you will act in greater harmony with the Laws of Love.

Unless you make a concerted effort not to obey and be a part of this change, you shall be changed and you shall walk upon a path that indicates and is an expression of this change. This is the law, beloved souls, the Law of Love. It shall be so with each of you. You may feel you are not progressing, that there are times when your heart is heavy and the conditions around you are not as you would like them to be. I tell you, beloved souls, that as you sincerely seek this gift of Love, such conditions will not last, nor are they truly a part of you. As you grow in love, as you are transformed in love, so the sensitivities and perceptions of the soul grows and with these sensitivities and perceptions, you come to know conditions that are unpleasant, that are part of your world, but are not truly a part of you. There are times when you may encounter these conditions and feel them and think of them as a permanent part of your being. I can assure you, beloved souls, they are not and you will come away from these things in light, upliftment and joy.

Do not think of yourself as a victim of the earth and the earthly conditions. Think of yourself as one who speaks and lives in the Truth of God's Love. In so doing, you continue to free yourself from the human condition and, in time, will be completely detached from these things. The power of love, beloveds, is greater than any condition, anything upon your earthly plane. The power of love will not only transform you, but will transform all conditions around you and in many ways evoke a desire within those whom you love, those whom you encounter in the world that touch will ignite a desire within their souls to seek this gift. It is not your responsibility that they will act upon this, though you may encourage them and I encourage you to do so. Indeed, you become the one who carries the invitation from God to be a part of, to open themselves to, the great flow of God's Love, that it may change them as it changes you. Some may answer that call and some may not. Indeed, you have done God's Work and you have become one of my disciples in doing so bringing the truth of love to the world, beloved souls.

Know that as you walk and live in the Love of God, so you become his instruments of truth, light, change, healing and love. See yourselves in this light, beloveds. Know that you are truly a part of this truth, that you walk and are an example upon the earth of that which is the salvation of humanity. Be true to this. Do not allow your minds to misdirect you from this. Have faith in your true selves and that journey which you are walking upon that God is directing you upon and the angels accompany you upon, this beautiful journey of love that is meant for all. Indeed, God gives this abundantly and powerfully to those who are willing to embrace it, accept it and desire it.

May you all be blessed, beloved souls, on the Path of Love. Know that I walk with you always and that many angels are with you as you journey upon your earthly life. God bless you, beloved souls. Know that you are loved. I love you. Many love you. You shall know a life of love, not only on this plane, but as you journey towards at-onement with God, entering into the Celestial Kingdom, you will know the wonderment of how deeply loved you are and how precious you are as God's Child. God bless you, beloveds. I am with you. I will always be with you. I am Jesus and shall continue to be your friend and brother forevermore. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you. May the power of His Love encompass and flow into your soul. Open, open, beloveds, to this gift, for it is given freely by God. Accept and embrace and revel in this Love that shall change you forevermore and uplift you into light. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you.
