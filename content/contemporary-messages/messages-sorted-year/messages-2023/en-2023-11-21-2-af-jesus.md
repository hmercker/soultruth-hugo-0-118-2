# Mighty Blessing Proclaiming His Love For Us!

November 21<sup>st</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come to pray with you, beloveds. I am Jesus, your brother, your friend. As we are all one in this truth, so we are all within the family of truth, of God’s Love. I come because I love you. I come to be with you, to share in this experience with God. To be blessed as you are blessed. To love as you love. To know God as you know God.

Put aside all your troubles and cares, your worries of the material life, and know the freedom and joy of God’s Touch upon us. Each of us is worthy, as every soul is worthy, of God’s Touch of Love given freely without restriction. Given because God loves us so. Given because the world is in need of such a gift. Each soul has been born to receive this gift and yet so few know of it, so few feel it and experience the touch deep within their souls. Yet here we are gathered together to acknowledge and to practice the truth. To truly be God’s children in the light of truth. To walk the way of love. To be love. As each of us grows in this light, we are a channel of truth and love for God. Our lights shine bright, illuminating the darkness, bringing peace.

My beloveds, continue to walk this path, dedicating yourselves each and every day to God, to service, to be that channel of love that you were meant to be. As you progress upon your days, the gifts and the power of love will increase and multiply so that, in time, you will not know yourself. For what has emerged is what is hidden within your soul, awakened by love, awakened by God’s compassion and Touch upon you. Each soul has something remarkable and extraordinary to give. Each soul is unique. Each soul shall express their gifts in accordance to the expansion that comes with God’s Love, the transformation that is given and works with this Love within you. Each soul finding itself as you acknowledge your true self.

My beloved brothers and sisters, we walk a path of light, of truth, of love. We shall for all eternity, walking together, a family of light. Never to forsake one another, but to be together, our true selves shining forth. As we do so, each soul who walks with us will acknowledge the other, acknowledge the beauty, acknowledge the light. Reach out to your brothers and sisters, my beloveds, for they are in need of love. They are in need of all that God has to give. As His instruments you make this love more powerful. You demonstrate it in such a way that they may understand and feel it and see that it is real. Let God use you in this way, beloveds. Do not forsake your destinies as God’s great instruments in the world. Seek to do so in humility and grace, to walk the world with a humble touch and demeanour. In this humility is great power and expression of light.

You are graced by God’s Touch, my beloved souls. I come to you because the laws dictate that I may come in light and power and love to pray with you. The Law of Attraction allows me to do so. As you grow in love, I come closer. I am with you more often and in the fullness of love. My beloveds, beloved brothers and sisters, I love you with all my heart and soul. I appreciate what God has created in you, your unique self, a soul made bright and beautiful by His Touch. You will glow and express all the beauty, all the gifts that lie within. You are truly His children and we walk together in this truth and love.

I love you. I walk with you and we pray together. I am Jesus and I am with you always. God bless you. God bless you, beloveds. I love you.
