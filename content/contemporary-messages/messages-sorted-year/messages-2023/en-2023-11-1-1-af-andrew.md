# Your Service to God will Grow with Your Soul

November 1<sup>st</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

The Father’s Love be with you, each beautiful soul present, all those who are connected to this group and all those who are seeking the great Father’s Love. I am Andrew and I come to pray with you, to encourage and hopefully inspire you upon the journey. For there is much work for each of you. If you desire to be God’s instruments in the world, there is much to do. Yet, that doing must be predicated upon the light within your soul, for this is the way in which God will guide you. This is the way in which you will be God’s instruments in the world.

It is that Light that glows, that Love, the Essence of God which is eternally there within you, that will continue to grow and expand within you, that will inform the journey. The journey of service, the journey of your own awakening, the journey of the insights and experiences, the many blessings that will come as you seek His Love. As you are in the flow of His Will, as you are in the grace of His Touch, it is given freely and abundantly.

My beloved friends, seek the Love of God always as your great anchor to light, to truth, to love. For as you do so, all the yearnings of your soul, all the potentials which God wishes to be expressed, will come to be. It is in these gifts, in these blessings, that you will find your way upon the path of truth and love. It is a faith in this journey, in what God has to give to you, in exploring and understanding your true self that all will be revealed, all will come to pass that is of grace, love, peace and joy.

Beloved souls, we are here together today to seek this great gift. May you feel it pouring into you. May you feel the effects of it changing you. May you come to know the results of this blessing, bringing you to a level, a place of true perception, understanding and light. Each of you has an angel with you in this prayer. Each of you has the potential to be a powerful instrument for God. Each of you are being blessed at this moment. Walk upon the path. Open yourselves fully and be the light, the light of truth, of love, of healing, of peace, so that you may venture forward into the world and be God’s instruments, God’s channels of light and love.

May God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Andrew and my love for you is great. My love for each of you continues to grow in the love of God. God bless you. God bless you upon the journey. I love you.
