# Encourages Our Instrumentality

November 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

…pray with you, beloved souls. I am Seretta Kem. What a gift you have in your fellowship, your friendship, your loving acknowledgment of one another, that you have shared in a common and deep experience of receiving God’s Love. You have shared many hours together in prayer. You have been together in many ways upon this Earth plane. Indeed, God has gifted you with one another. He will continue to bless you, beloved souls. No matter where you are upon this world, you will be blessed. You will feel and know the blessings of your bonds with one another, this Lattice of Light that continues to grow, continues to spread and connect others to you and to many others. This web of light is important and powerful on your Earth plane.

God works at this day and night, this effort to unite and to bring greater light to your world in the Truth of His Love, in the power of love expressed, connecting one with another. Though these things seem subtle and you are not always aware of the workings and how this complexity of light is built, it is a powerful instrument to bring healing to your world, to bring light and love to your world, as are the Portals of Light that continue to proliferate in your world.

These two instruments wonder about their travels at times, wondering about its purpose, wondering whether they are effective instruments or merely responding to their own imaginings and desires in this regard. I say to them, wherever they go brings the opportunities of building greater light, Lattice of Light and Portals of Light. This happens through them. It is part of their ministry. I urge them not to doubt their efforts, but to carry on and continue to be those instruments, which come naturally and beautifully through their bright soul’s administrations, wherever they go.

Indeed, each of you have purpose and have your missions, your instrumentalities of work for God. As you travel about the world, remember that God is using you at every turn, at every moment. I would urge you to pray for this, to open yourselves more fully to it. Though these things are not readily seen or understood because your focus is on the material world, they are nonetheless active and powerful as God’s channels and instruments for the benefit of the world. You will see at some point the outcomes of these ministrations of your guided travels, of all that you do in your daily life. Yet for now, it is a matter of faith and trust in God’s guidance, in God’s purpose for each of you. I urge you to be aware, to consider that what I say is true, that what you do with each day is of God’s doing and your instrumentality at work.

As you grow in God’s love, the awareness of this will grow. You will see the many layers, the multiplicity of efforts, ministrations that are accomplished through you. For it is far easier for us to work through instruments such as yourself than to try and connect with souls who are not aware. Nor are they prayerful or in this alignment with God that is so important. You become the channels. You are the nexus for the light. Indeed, we are most grateful for your efforts, for your lights and for what God has made possible through you and through others in your dark world. You will continue to awaken to these things and know them as second nature, a reflexive response to life. To be that channel, that instrument, so beautiful and wise and loving.

It all comes in the flow of love, in the flow of your desire to be within that great river of love and to be an instrument for God, that your soul and God’s soul may be in alignment and beautifully so. In this way, great harmony comes, great joy, many, many blessings to you and to many others. It is all part of the work, my beloved friends. All part of what is to unfold in God’s plan. You will see yourself as an active agent each day within that plan. Be true to yourselves, the knowings of your souls, the great gifts that God has placed within your souls, in the knowings and the experience, in the understanding. You will grow in your instrumentality, in your capacity to be a channel of love in the world, to be a great light. Indeed, as you continue in your prayer practices, you are connecting with God. This too strengthens the channel, widens and clarifies it.

You will continue to find your way. We are with you. Many angels are with you at this moment, as we have utilised the portal that is here to be with you. Not only to assist you and help you to further develop in your gifts, but to utilise this light for the benefit of many others. Continue in your prayers, beloved souls. Remember wherever you are in the world, whatever you are doing, this bond, this connection, this strand of light, brings you all together in loving harmony. In this way, God can utilise you individually and collectively to bring blessings to many others. These things come on the heels of the great light of God’s Love within your souls. Indeed, that light grows and each of you grow. The light that you create together grows. The Law of Progression is obeyed in this way as you continue on this journey toward greater light and love and truth, deeper and fuller instrumentality.

Beautiful lights, beautiful lights, may you be blessed. My love is with you. I am Seretta Kem. Many are with you today in this gathering of lights. God bless you. God bless you all.
