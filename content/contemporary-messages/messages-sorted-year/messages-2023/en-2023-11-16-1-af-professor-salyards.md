# Expect the Unexpected!

November 16<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Light Bringers, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Professor Salyards received by Al Fike

May the  Father's Love continue to infill you, my beloved friends, my friends who bring this truth to the world, who are dedicated to this matter and this effort. I am Professor Salyards. Indeed, you must pray for inspiration and guidance, for what will bring about the unfolding of this truth and the interrelationships of individuals in this truth, then efforts to bring people together in many different ways, through many different means.

Expect the unexpected, my friends. As God guides you, things will come to you that you would not expect. You would be surprised as to their coming and guidance to direct you.  My friends, there is much work to do. Do not think that you are in the trough of a wave. Rather, you continue to head upwards into new things, new possibilities, new opportunities. Open yourselves accordingly. Allow yourselves the luxury of God's inspiration to wash over you and awaken you to the possibilities. Indeed, God has a great plan and you are a part of it. I suggest you continue to listen, to awaken and to find your way upon the road that is designated.

Each of you may contribute to this. Each of you have your gifts and abilities. Each of you is no less important than the other. Do not consider yourselves as a minor player in this group. Rather, each of you is significant and important. Your ideas and what you contribute adds to the whole in a significant way. My friends, be open to guidance, be open to the inspiration. It will come. You will find revelations unexpected, ideas significant and brilliant. Each can receive and are open to these things. Exercise your capacity, your ability to be guided. Seek the Love of God, for this opens the doors, does it not? Be united in your efforts.

God bless you. I am your Professor Salyards.  My love is with you. I congratulate you on your efforts of the past, your gathering, which indeed was significant. What may come of it will be significant. God bless you, my friends. My love is with you. God bless you.
