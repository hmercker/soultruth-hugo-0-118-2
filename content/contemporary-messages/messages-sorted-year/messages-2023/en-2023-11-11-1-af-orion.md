# We've Told you the Truth of the Soul and God's Love in a Thousand Different Ways!

November 11<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Yes, you are children of God as are all of us and all who inhabit your planet, ours and the universe. Those of us who possess a soul are truly children of God. The rest are certainly creations of God and beautiful creations at that. Each of us who possesses a soul is something different, something unique, something beautiful that only exists because we have a soul. We are destined, one day, to find our at-onement with God.

This is what has been a part of your destiny and ours. It is said that some of you upon this planet will not reach that goal. Indeed, the possibility exists, because of the gift of free will, that you will and will decide not to. Still, God honours life. God honours each child. Thus, He gives ample opportunities for that child to be in greater light, greater harmony with His Laws of Love and, in time you receive the great gift of His Essence, the great gift indeed. The gift that you must proclaim to the world. The gift that your beloved brother proclaimed and forfeited his life in doing so. You, my beloved and beautiful friends, must continue this work to awaken humanity to the potentials of their souls. For this is the next step forward. This is how humanity will progress. It is not through the faculties of the mind and the inventions of their creative minds. For these things have their limitations. Yet, when one is blessed and awakened by the Essence of God then the possibilities and potentials of creativity of knowing truth, of knowing the Universe have no limits. It is for you, my beloved friends, to talk about how the individual may find limitless growth and potential, awakened by Divine Essence.

Humanity is stuck within its mindful considerations. In many ways, you are a part of this. You continue to inhabit your material, mindful consciousness to such a degree that the things that I speak of become merely abstract principles rather than reality for you. Yet, you also dance with God back and forth between the material mind and the soul mind, the experience of a soul close to God and the experience of a mind close to man. This is your lot. This is your challenge and you continue to struggle. I see that progress is made, this incremental journey toward greater understanding and soul awakening, at-onement with God. Thus, we are with you and many others to help you upon this journey, to encourage you, to protect you from the powerful influences of the dark conditions of your world. We uplift you. We nurture you. We speak to you. We stand beside you. We pray with you. You continue to dedicate yourselves to this process, these blessings, the efforts being made, so you ensure our presence and the presence of your angel friends and the touch of God upon you.

These things continue to become a formidable force in your life and they continue to impress you upon the road of love, the development and awakening of love, the foundation of love that grows and makes possible many other blessings and expressions of your soul. My beloved friends, continue to put your eyes, ears, thoughts and intentions upon God. For as you go for the highest, as you seek out all that is of love and all that is of the Divine. You will reach your goal, you nurture those parts of you that are a part of this goal, those places within you that need love, those parts of you that are out of harmony that can be corrected by love, those avenues that you may take that will still those human aspects of your consciousness, those fears and concerns, judgements, anger and many other things that humanity has created within their consciousness.

These things are eradicated and diminished by love. Continue to consider this, my friends. Continue on the path of love, the path that leads to God, the blessings of God, the great Essence of His Soul and you will find yourself in a place of joy, of wisdom, of peace, that love may flow reflexively, powerfully, beautifully, that you may walk through life in harmony of love, that you may express yourself in light with those gifts and abilities that come with love that are opened with God’s Love.

So much can be given, but you must give as well. You must express those deep desires and longings that come from within, putting aside the distractions that are so dominant in your life and truly be with God. This we have said a thousand different times and thousands of different ways. We reiterate the truth that you must come to God and seek the great gift of His love. All else follows in its stead. All else is given with this love. Do not take for granted the truth but honor it, live it and sustain it in every way that you can. The truth will set you free, my beloved friends and so love will bring its rewards. God bless you. I am Orion and my love is with you all, beautiful friends of the truth. God bless you. God bless you all in His love.
