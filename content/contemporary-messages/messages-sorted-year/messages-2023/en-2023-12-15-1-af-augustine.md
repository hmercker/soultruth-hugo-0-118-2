# God’s Love is Unlimited

December 15<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Online Prayer Circle, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

The universe is filled with love, my friends. I am your teacher, Augustine. Never limit God’s capacity to love, for you have no idea, no possibility of understanding the depth and breadth of God’s Love. The One who created the universe, all within it, and has done so in love, the expression of the Love of His Soul, is greater than all the universe combined. Indeed, as you continue to explore the universe, you are coming to realise how vast and unlimited is that universe of God’s creation. Yet, God is love. What being is capable of creating the universe in all its depths and breadth and infilling the universe with love? This is God. This is God’s capacity.

Indeed, even we, who have traveled this road for far longer than any of you, only get a glimpse of how wondrous is God, how magnificent is His great Soul, how beautiful is God and His creation. Set before you upon this road is the possibility of knowing God more deeply, understanding God’s creation more fully, and knowing His Love more deeply. All of these things will bring you great joy, my friends. For what is it that brings joy to a soul, but love, but coming to know God, and in coming to know God, coming to know the universe of God?

This is what awaits you, my beloved students. There are no limitations. There are only limitations that you place upon yourself. Indeed, in this very limiting consciousness of your world, it is difficult to see beyond it. It is difficult to be in that place of complete openness and perception that allows you to see beyond the earthly plane, beyond the mindful conditions, beyond all those things that take you not into God consciousness, but away from it. But here, you fill up your cup, your soul with His Love, the elixir that heals, breaks down all barriers, and brings you into that transformative place, that consciousness of God.

You have found the right road, my beloved friends. You travel it uniquely, individually, and yet, collectively you understand, as you acknowledge one another, what a wondrous road we are all traveling, what a beautiful gift we have all been given. We will continue to expand in our understanding and our perceptions, in our capacity to love, in the truth, in all things that are of soul and all things that are of God. This is our journey, unlimited, wondrous, and awesome in its scope.

We are His children. We recognise this. Part of something so magnificent, so wondrous that, can you not feel the joy in your heart when you come to know this? When you get a glimpse of the magnificence of God's creation, the magnificence of who you are as God’s creation, and the magnificence of God, the Being, the Creator? All things come as you allow yourself to open to them, as you pray for greater blessings of the Father’s Love, as you come to know yourself within that blessing and come to be changed by that blessing.

My beloved friends, we are all linked in love, brothers and sisters in the magnificence of God’s creation. We will journey together. Not just for the few moments on this Earth, but for all eternity, all eternity we will walk this path together and acknowledge with one another, in joyful recognition, the magnificence of God’s creation and the magnificence of the journey that God has set us upon. We are blessed, beloved souls, truly. We are loved deeply, beyond our capacity to understand, even my capacity to understand. It is beyond this, beloved souls.I come closer, and you come closer, to that true understanding and awareness.

May you be blessed upon the journey, my beloved students, children of God. For we are loved, and I love each one of you. I am close to each one of you. God bless you, beloved souls. I am your teacher, Augustine, and I love you truly, deeply, and shall never leave you. For I am your brother upon the Path, as are many who join you and love you. God bless you. God bless you all in His Love.
