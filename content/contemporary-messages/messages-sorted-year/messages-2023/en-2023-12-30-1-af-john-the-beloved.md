# Introduces Himself to Jose Miguel Sanchez as His Guide

December 30<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Dominican Republic Retreat, Dominican Republic

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

May Divine Love fill your souls. Beloved friends, I am John, John the Beloved. I come because my dear friend and charge asked for my presence. I wish to reassure him that I am his guide, that I am close, that he has a destiny to fulfil. His time on earth will not be cut short, but will span decades of time so that God may use him for the work at hand. There is much work to do, many souls to reach, much light to bring. God’s work is great, but those who work with God are few. Those who are willing are held close to the Bosom of God. His Love continues to pour upon you, dear son. His intentions for you are continuing to be known by your soul. It is for you, my beloved son, to know and understand the Will of God.

This is a great challenge for you, as it is for most on this earthly plane. But you have the gifts and perceptions within you, the sensitivity and understanding that will come as you seek out God more earnestly. As you seek to know God’s Will for you with greater depth and understanding, you will come to know what God has in store for you, the gifts that God has put within you, within your soul, the meaning and purpose of those gifts, and how you may utilize them to not only awaken yourself but to awaken others. It takes courage, focus, faith, and dedication for this to be so.

God implants His Will and His plan within many, but many are distracted. Many do not understand. Many do not seek this to any great degree. Therefore, the seeds that God has planted are lost because the individuals who have the gift of God’s intention within them do not understand nor are they able, in a conscious way, to grasp the Will of God. My dear son, you have sought the Will of God. You seek the great blessing of God’s Soul to your soul, the Love that comes, that opens the way and opens the doorway to your understanding, to all that is a part of His Will for you.

Continue to pray earnestly for this gift. Continue to seek in your thoughts, in your prayers, to know God. Indeed, you may ask for my presence and my support on your journey. I am most willing and able to walk with you, my beautiful son, beautiful instrument of God. But you must clear away the distractions, worries, and cares of the material life and be wholly with God to be truly His instrument. These precious souls before you are examples of what must be in your life. You are unique and different, and you walk a life that is unique for yourself, but the foundations, the commitments, are expressed through these instruments before you. Take heed of their advice and their example, as you will with others who will come into your life and help you upon the path.

My beautiful and beloved friend, child of God, son of the truth, you must recalibrate the intensity of your commitment and your thoughts toward God, to pray diligently each and every day and to ask to be used as an instrument for God. In this way, God will utilise you because you have made clear your intentions and your desires. This is most important. One may pray for the great blessing of God’s Love. Yet, if that love is not expressed in one’s life to such a degree and extent that they are indeed God’s instruments, clear and full of love and light, then where shall they travel? What shall they do?

It is not something that the mind conjures up. It is something the soul understands and comes to know from the Will of God, the guidance of God, the Love of God. You must go to your soul, truly. Ask God to ignite the gifts, perceptions, and blessings thereof so that you may truly be God’s instrument, clear, concise, powerful, beautiful in your capacity to serve, in your ability to love, and to bring truth to others. Your nation and those people of this area of the world are in great need. They are lost, and often they are conflicted with various ideas and various perceptions of truth. It is for you, my beautiful son, who is a teacher at heart, to teach your brothers and sisters, to teach the children. Teach your children about this gift. Pray with them and be with them. Pray with others and be with them.

I know it is difficult. This path is not easy. God does not open the door and the lost children do not readily follow, but if you are strong, loving, and a great example and light for others, they will be drawn. There will be those who come to you, and God will guide them to you, my son. At first, it will be small and humble. In this way, you will learn much. But as you grow stronger, as you are in greater light, so others come, so you will build a community of light, of the Truth of God’s Love. In so doing, those such as the blessings of these beautiful souls and others will come and assist you in these efforts.

My beloved friend, I will mentor you and I will be close. Ask for my presence. As you pray for God’s blessings of Love within you, you may ask for my presence and the presence of the angels to surround you and protect you, guide you and be with you during your day. As you walk in the Light, as you draw the Light, the Love, and the Truth that is of God’s Love, the Light shall surround you. You will have the opportunities to speak, to heal, and to bless others in light and truth. It is given freely to you, beloved son. It is for you to accept and to be serious about your commitment, clear and unclouded with thought, but in harmony with truth that lies within the soul. It is the expression of the soul that is in relationship with its Creator.

You shall find your way, my son. You shall find your way in love and I shall be with you to help you. Many others shall also be with you. You are blessed. You are given blessings. You are eager, and you desire truth. These things will come as God forges the way for this journey toward greater truth, light and love. It shall be given freely and abundantly. Awaken to this, beloved son. Awaken to this.

I am John. My love for you is great. I shall walk with you. For all the days of your life, I shall be with you. This is my commitment to you and to God, and it shall be forevermore, your friend and helper, your guide and teacher. God bless you. My love is with you. The Love of God surrounds us and shall pour into you in abundance. God bless you. God bless you, beloved son. And you, beloved, beautiful children of God, God bless you in His Light and Love.
