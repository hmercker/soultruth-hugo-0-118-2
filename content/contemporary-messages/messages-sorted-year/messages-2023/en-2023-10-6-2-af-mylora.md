# Healing Mother Earth

October 6<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Monthly Healing for the World, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mylora received by Al Fike

I am Mylora. I come to bring peace and healing. I come because I am privileged and honoured to come to minister to you and to minister to this Earth. It is my dedication to bringing harmony to this world of yours. It is the reason why I come. For I love this world, what God has created. I love you all, the most wonderest of His creations. When we come together in prayer seeking to gift the world in light, to be a channel for the world, an instrument of God, then we are fulfilling the desires of our souls, the highest inclinations and capacities of our souls to be God's instruments, to be lights in the world.

Consider how you might be a light in the world, beloved friends. Be a friend to your world, to nature, to nature’s creations, God’s creations, so that all may come back into harmony. So much imbalance exists in your world. There are only pockets of your world that are not influenced so powerfully by humanity and that are still in the flow and freedom of the natural order. Some of you live in this country that is full of forests, streams, lakes and oceans. You are very blessed to not have such a population impinging upon these parts of the world. You feel a sense of freedom, a sense of being a part of the natural order, where so many in your world do not. They are encased in the creations of humanity, a world on their own, separate from the natural order. This is most unfortunate, for how else can you appreciate God’s creations if you are not a part of them? How can you truly be an instrument in harmony with all that is if you see the world from a very specific view point, one that has been created by humanity, your minds becoming a part of a reality that is not of God’s reality but of man’s?

This must be rectified. For all humanity must come back to an understanding of the natural order, the natural world that God has created. For if they do not, how can they truly be sensitive and understand the needs of your world? You cannot continue as a species to take and to be engaged in all that you can get from the world and yet not make efforts to give back, to help restore those parts of the natural world that have been removed by the will of man. Every system that God has created, everything in God’s creation, is sustainable and is sustained in love, sustained by the order and rhythms that God has created. Yet, man lives outside of these rhythms and orders because God gave humanity a great gift, that of free will. They have the privilege, the possibility, of creating their own reality, your own reality. They do not see nor do they feel an obligation, a responsibility, to the beautiful world that sustains them. Some of course do. Some see very clearly what is required. Yet, the vast majority continues to live their life in the blindness and the rhythms of their own making and do not honour God in loving acknowledgement of His creation.

You must change this, beloved souls. Change it within yourself. Change it within the world. Be outspoken. Be an example for others, so that they may see that you care, that you care for the world and that you care for them. In this caring, you become part of the force, the impetus to bring about harmony, to be a part of God’s plan for the salvation of your world and all within it. Consider these things, my friends, for not enough time is given to consideration of the beauty and wonderment of God's creation, of the needs of God’s creation, of what is required to bring harmony and to rejuvenate this beautiful world.

Some are more fortunate than others, living on the edge of the natural order while others live fully, completely, within a human creation and condition. Those of you within and close to nature are very blessed indeed. For what benefits you have when you walk in forests by streams, breathe the air that is fresh and know the animals that live around you. Great gifts given. This is in great harmony with God’s intention for humanity to find their way within the natural order, but also to be in the flow of God’s Will and God’s blessings. All are possible, all can be achieved with the right attitudes, the right desires and building within the soul the great Love of God.

I encourage you, my beautiful friends, wherever you are in the world, appreciate a flower, a bird, a butterfly, the sparkling waters, the beautiful skies, the mountains, the fields and the forests. All these things of God’s creations, for they are a gift to you. This planet is a gift to you. Honour that gift from God and walk within the harmony of God’s creation, God’s Love. We all work to bring and restore harmony to your world. We encourage you as you grow in God's Love to perceive and acknowledge the beautiful creation that is of God and to honour your own place within it. My beloved and beautiful friends, we are God's creations, God's children. We live within this beautiful Light and creation of God. Continue to seek ways and means of bringing greater harmony, bringing light to the world in all kinds of ways, all levels of expression, of effort.

I thank you, my dear and beloved friends for listening to me. You know I love you. I love each one of you. God loves you so much. God has given you so much. From life, to a place to live within this material sphere, to a soul that can know God. So much, so much is available to you, gifted to you in love. May you come to appreciate all that is given, the wonderment of creation, the wonderment of God’s Love. God bless you. I am Mylora and I love you. I love you all. God bless you.
