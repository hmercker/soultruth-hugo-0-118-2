# Blesses José in Dominican Republic

December 30<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Dominican Republic Retreat, Dominican Republic

Mary received by Al Fike

I too come to bless you, beloved soul, and these two beautiful souls who have travelled a great distance to be with you and to uphold you. I come because it is important that you be a light in this world, all of you, to be a light in this world, to stand forth as light and truth and love. I will be close. I will guard and protect all who bring the Light and Truth of God’s Love to the world. I am close to you all and many others who have a deep desire to bring the truth that my son brought, and so will you, into the world.

I am here to be with you in prayer and shall continue to be with you in the great blessings and Love of God. I am with you and I love you dearly. I love you as my own children. I love you because you are a light and indeed, God sends His instruments, His angels, His blessings to those who seek the truth. It shall be forevermore, beloved souls. God bless you. I am with you and I love you.
