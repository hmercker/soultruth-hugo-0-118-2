# Do Not Get Swept Away in the Dark Conditions of the World

December 12<sup>th</sup>, 2023

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Greetings and blessings to you, my friends. It is Orion. The conditions of your world will worsen before they get better. This, I can assure you, to be true and you are feeling the pressures, the heaviness of the earthly conditions upon you, each one of you feel this condition. Some are able to cope better than others but, indeed, it is a struggle. You must be vigilant. You must not allow yourself to be swept into the darkness, the negativity that is all around you. If you are in this condition of discontent, I suggest that you continue to pray and put aside these thoughts that are so dark, so resonant with the human condition.

Each of you has been chosen because you are strong, because you have the capacity to overcome these things. I implore you to apply yourself in such a way as to be separate from the darkness. Each of you has many angels surrounding you, stellar friends, beautiful lights, but they cannot make your choice for you. They must stand by and wait if you are choosing something other than light. They will come as close as they can, but they are not able to penetrate a condition that you make for yourself that is not of light.

I am not saying these things to be critical. I understand the earthly conditions. I observe them. I am horrified by them. Yet, I persist, as do you, to overcome these things and I will continue to persist, as I know you will, to be in the light. Each day brings its challenges and rewards. Some days are easier than others in order to combat the darkness and be in the light. It is for you to struggle with each day, to triumph with each day, to be in harmony with God with each day. Free will is, indeed, a double edged sword. On one hand you receive, by choice, the great blessings that God has to give. On the other hand, often not by conscious choice, you are embroiled in the darkness to some degree. Your thoughts play a big role, a major role in this. Your thoughts are powerful things. As you are tired and weary, often, you allow those old patterns of times long ago to dominate your consciousness. These patterns often open the door for darkness, for negative influence, for judgments and patterns of mind that entertain unloving thoughts.

Indeed, I’m not saying anything new to you, my friends. Some of you have been taught these lessons multiple times for many, many years. Yet, as the darkness persists and strengthens, you are engaged in a dance between light and dark that is quite intense and quite powerful. I urge you to not take any notice of this darkness. When you entertain thoughts that are dark, put them aside. Do not indulge yourself in these things. When you entertain yourself with thoughts of light and love, joy, give yourself acknowledgement for this. For in doing so, you are some of the very few in the world who are cognisant and expressive of light, of these things of light.

As you continue to win this battle, as you continue to creep toward greater light, more light, light that is persistent, light that is expressed in the majority of your life, then you are and you become the example for others. Do not feel shame because you have fallen in this dark place. Instead pick yourself up, say a prayer, ask for help, release yourself from the hold of the dark conditions. In your desire to do so, you enlist a great force, a great many resources to help you to come to that place of light.

The world continues to spiral into dreadful conditions. The world continues to be asleep and in a multiplicity of choices and actions expressed in the world, these dark conditions are reinforced, over and over again. You may see this as a losing battle. For the majority of humanity is engaged to some degree in the dark conditions of which I speak. In your life, that which surrounds you, there is great light, light that is stronger than the darkness, light that will overcome the darkness, light that will ensure that you are immune from the darkness provided you allow yourself to entertain that which is of light. Because you are human, you stumble into these conditions often without even knowing, but here we are, and many more who will reach out to you and help you get back on your feet and get back into the light. This is the power of love, my friends. For you are truly loved, each one of you and you are truly blessed.

Powerful forces of light continue to exert their influence upon you, surrounding you with protection and light, but no force, including God can countermand free will and the choices that you make. So be aware, my beloved friends. Be vigilant and know there is always a solution to your dilemmas. There is always a contradiction to your mistakes that will bring light. There is always someone to be with you in light. God provides this with every breath. God will continue to walk with you and have great compassion for you. God’s desire is that you and all will be in the light someday, a light that persists, a light that disperses all darkness. It is coming and you are progressing.

All will be well as the light continues to be shed upon you and the light continues to grow within you as you seek the great gift from God, the Divine Love of the Creator. God bless you, my friends. We will never leave you and are always there to uplift. Seek us out and allow yourself to release all conditions that are not in harmony with God’s Laws of Love. God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and I love you all dearly, dearly. God bless you.
