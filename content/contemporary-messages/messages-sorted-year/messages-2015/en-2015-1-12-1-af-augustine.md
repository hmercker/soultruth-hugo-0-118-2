# Do not confuse the Divine Love with the natural Love

January 12<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike


God bless you, beloved souls, your teacher Augustine. I have come expressly to teach

the two new souls who come to this circle a fundamental truth, and that is that Godʼs

Love, this essence from the Creator, is a gift which resides outside of the human soul

when it is born. Do not confuse the Divine Love with the natural love, which each soul

is born with, precious students, for this Love Divine comes with the invitation from you

for its reception within your soul. This is a fundamental distinction, beloved souls, which

you must recognize if you wish to walk upon the Divine Path, the path that leads to atone-

ment with God in this special way. And of course, the choice to walk in the way of

the natural love is always yours to take, my beloveds, but in order to make this choice,

you must understand the distinction between these two forms of love. Both are

beautiful, but each has its particular qualities and destinations; for the soul purified and

perfect in its own natural love will reside in the heavens of the natural love, the sixth

sphere. This is the final destination for these souls, and the vast majority of humanity

will take this choice. And for those dear and precious souls who choose Godʼs Love,

the Divine Path, they will, in time, as the soul, as the love purifies their souls, reside

within the Celestial Heavens, which is where we who come to teach you reside, and

these heavens are infinite. They do not end, as Godʼs great soul is infinite and does not

end, and as such as you receive the Essence of God, His Love within you, your

progression shall not end. I would encourage you, my beloveds, beautiful students, to

choose the Divine Love, to pursue this opportunity and open door, to come to know this

Love intimately, to feel it working within your soul as you invite it in, in your prayers and

your longings, to allow it to do its purifying and beautiful work of transforming the soul

from human to Divine. The mind has trouble recognizing the distinctions. The mind

assumes that it has all that is required. And this is true upon the natural path. But in

order to walk upon the Divine Path requires this gift of Love from God to transform the

soul and ignite the soul and open the soul. I hope I have made myself clear, beloveds.

It is important for those of you who sit in this circle to understand what it is that you are

praying for, and to experience this gift in a deeply personal way. It is available to you at

all times. You may receive it in any circumstance if you are able to open your soul to its

reception. And these beloved souls who sit with you who have sat and longed for this

Love for many years may share with you their own personal experiences in receiving

the Fatherʼs Love, of feeling the closeness of the Creator, of being guided by the angels,

of walking the Divine Path. Beloveds, walk with us in this light. Understand that God

compels you, beseeches you, beckons you to receive this gift, to open yourselves to the

inflowing of the Divine Essence and to allow your minds to be at peace and bring your

souls to a place of awakening and understanding of this highest of truths, this most

precious of gifts. God bless you, beloveds. May you place your feet firmly upon the
