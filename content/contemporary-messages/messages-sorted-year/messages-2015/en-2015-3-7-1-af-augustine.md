# Hope was indeed given to some and Healing and Comfort to many

March 7<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Farm Elim, Gobabis, Namibia

Augustine received by Al Fike

Dear students, this is Augustine.

So you see that things have unfolded in ways that you did not expect and attitudes have shifted on both sides amongst yourselves and amongst those in the village. And many souls were touched on that day, and hope was indeed given to some and healing and comfort to many. You were all used as channels of Love. And I believe that you all could see the effects that this had amongst those you prayed for and embraced. Indeed, it is difficult to see such depravation. To reach out, to put aside your shock of what you see. But what you have seen is an outward manifestation of the depravation which is all around the world. For those who have material comfort they have a deep deprivation within their souls, so many. And yet, many who have material deprivation have a light within their souls, a joy within, an innocence, a loving nature. The darkness that resides within mankind manifest in many different ways. And you my children as you expose to these different conditions will be able to discern this with your soul’s perceptions and God will prompt you within many ways to express His Love to all that you meet, all the conditions that you move amongst. God’s Love will clear away the darkness, and your love will bring comfort to many. Continue in your efforts my dear and beloved children. Some of you have learned much through this experience and were deeply moved. And this is a blessing from God. So much more to experience and understand, so much work to do. Indeed, this work at times may feel overwhelming but as you greet each day with the desire to be a channel of God’s Love, you and your Heavenly Father, and we in spirit, will chip away the darkness, piece by piece, day by day, breath by breath. And those of you who are strong enough, persistent enough, loving enough and have a deep faith you will make change in this world though you may not see the breadth and depth of this change, it will manifest. Now the fires that sweep this world to cleanse, to purify, they are small ambers but will be fanned by the breath of God to a mighty flame. And you will be purveyors of this of Truth of Light of Love. Each day that you challenge yourselves will overcome that which inhibits this.

You become stronger and in the greater flow of Love and your lights will grow my beloveds, they are growing and much is falling away from you. And when you reveal what is in your hearts to one another you are taking a step to release those conditions. Allow God to heal you completely. And lift you up to that high place beyond the human condition so that you may be in the world but not of the world. And though you may feel that you are alone, you are never alone. And though you may feel you walk a lonely path, you walk with many Angels. And there are those on earth who walk with you as well. You understand what it is that you strive for and desire for and hence your Heavenly Father has brought you together in fellowship and will do so again and again to help you to support one another, to bring greater clarity amongst you. This is God’s Will and the means for this will be provided. None of you can truly know God’s plan, yet you all are part of this plan, and it is your responsibility and challenge to realize the purpose you have within this plan. To put aside your judgements and your fears and your preconceptions, but to be open to God’s Will and to manifest this as clearly and powerfully as you can. You have great support my children, great support in this and you will succeed and in time you will become unassailable by those who would tear you down. Your faith will grow to such an extent that you will know in a clear way your Heavenly Father’s Will for you. And this my children is what you strive for and pray for and long for. It shall be given and is given.

Though you see through glass darkly on this day, each day will become clearer each day the joy within will be stronger and there will be no doubts, no worries or cares or burdens merely opportunities and awareness that you are with God.  This is possible my children, it is coming in your lives, and each day will be a joy, a discovery and a wonderment. We in spirit are so happy with the efforts you have made together to help those in such need and yes there will be more opportunities to manifest this Love in this way. Follow your guidance, walk in the light, long for the Love listen and act with pure intention and loving grace, humble but strong, simple but powerful. So much awaits, you are loved, so dearly loved. Walk in the light my beloveds. God bless you, your teacher Augustine loves you and is with you often. God bless you.
