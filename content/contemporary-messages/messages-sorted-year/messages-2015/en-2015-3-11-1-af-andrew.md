# So few Places in the World are free from the Human Condition

March 11<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Dolomite Camp, Etosha National Park, Namibia

Andrew received by Al Fike

God’s grace envelopes you my beloveds as Love pours into your souls and in this pure and beautiful place you are nourished and healed and inspired. So few places in the world are free from the human condition in such a way as this and you are blessed to be here my beloveds, to have this time together in communion and fellowship. Let this pure and true peace be absorbed by you in every way, allow God touch your bodies, your souls, your spirit, your minds to bring you deep and abiding peace. The stillness is so important for each soul to allow this peace that passes all understanding to open new avenues to God, bring nourishment for your bodies, allow your souls to expand and enter your consciousness. Yes, this time together is for you my beloveds, to nourish you. And be at peace, be truly at peace and know that God loves you, is with you, envelopes you, embraces you. We are able to accomplish so much when the mind is still. Allow your souls to be open and receptive. We bring gifts to each one of you. A blessing for each one unique and beautiful.

Be at peace and know that you are loved. God bless you, Andrew loves you as do many, many Angels.

….as you are ready within yourselves to accept, to embrace and to move forward with this knowledge and with this insight which comes from the soul. And this will come more in a knowing and simple understanding as the soul mind embraces the Truth and your minds allow this to be your truth; it is simple yet so powerful. What is incomprehensible to the mind, it is so clear to the soul. And as the Father’s Love pours into you the soul has the capacity to understand to truly understand, will expand and bring you what you require to walk God’s path and know His Will and be in harmony with this.

Be at peace and know that you are loved. God bless you.
