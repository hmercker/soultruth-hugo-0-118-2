# God’s Love will attract the Healing

March 20<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

Mary received by Al Fike

God bless you and I love you. When you do love the children you do Gods work. The innocent ones who wander aimlessly without love and nurturance. When you love these children you do great work for God who loves all His children. Continue to embrace the lost ones, the orphans, the hungry, the sick. So much need, so much depravation. But love heals all and God’s Love will attract the healing and what is required to nurture the lost souls. Continue to be in the Love to seek your guidance to be with God and in this the Angels will be with you and all that you require will be given. Beloved, beloved souls bless you for your efforts in your desire to bring light in this world, bring healing and comfort. You are my children and I am well pleased with your efforts to be pure and gracious channels of Love in this world. I am Mary and I love you.
