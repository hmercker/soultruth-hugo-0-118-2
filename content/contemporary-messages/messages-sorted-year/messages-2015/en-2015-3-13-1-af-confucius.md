# Walk through the World to be loving and accepting is a powerful Testimony to the Power of God’s Love

March 13<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Casa Piccolo, Windhoek, Namibia

Confucius received by Al Fike

Precious and beloved souls, it is Confucius. God bless you beloveds.

We have been with you upon your journeys within your vehicle. We have been within that transport sitting beside you and at times we have influenced you to turn down certain roads so that you may see these animals, and we have influenced others to come to give you the message to come to the watering holes.

[personal ]….walk through the world to be loving and accepting is a powerful testimony to the power of God’s Love. And as you overcome the barriers within yourself, so a power of this influence will be greater. You find yourselves in situations that you may deem difficult but indeed the Love will break down those barriers in the darkness, and you will become more comfortable in these situations and allow the Love to flow.

Beloveds, do God’s work. Indeed, each is unique. Each soul and personality. And this is a blessing from God, the uniqueness of the soul, the uniqueness of the individual. And like each flower in God’s garden appreciate the beauty within - see how God creates such beauty and no two are the same and you my beloveds are beautiful, indeed precious. And we will continue to accompany you upon your journeys and God’s loving hand is upon you, my beloveds. Does this not make your life rich bringing such deep meaning to every step that you take, do you not gather up these experiences and hold them close and in so doing to praise God for what He gives, each moment, each breath? For your lives are truly blessed and meaningful and filled with love and do you not see how souls are drawn in so many ways and there is a touch of light and beauty, of comfort, of healing, of love given as you touch each soul?

May God carry you forward in this way in the flow of His Love going to every corner of this world with His Love. Your journeys will continue my beloveds, do not resist this guidance to travel through this world, it is so important for you to be in these places and to love all those whom you are able to reach and this will expand and your journeys will become more complex and many more souls will be drawn into this enveloping light. And with each day and each journey you will grow, become more powerful and clear channels of the Father’s Grace in the world. This is so needed my beloveds. This is doing God’s work, the work of our beloved Master, bringing these truths. For of each day you bring forth this Truth because of who you are and how you are in this world. And indeed there will be times where you may speak these Truths of Love and you will feel impressed to do so to give in this way. To profit these little books, to bring food to the hungry, to speak in loving ways to those you meet and in some ways these little conversations you may have with strangers seem trifle, but in other ways they are deep and meaningful. This is God’s Love flowing through you. It is important. It has purpose. You touch many souls in this way.

[ personal ] …So it is in your world. So many do not feel loved and are alone and you bring this message that they are loved and not alone. Be God’s Lights my beloveds, do not allow your judgements to dim this Light. But embrace all in this simple way. And appreciate their own beauty, their uniqueness and love them for as you are a channel of Love in this way you increase the capacity to love within you and the capacity for God to love others through you. Do not judge but be as a child, innocent and loving. Like the dear soul who patted this instrument’s arm, his love was felt. Is he not a channel of God’s Love? This simple and vulnerable child gave love. Bless the children. Bless all those who are willing to give love, bless all those so down-trodden and lost, bless this world which thirst for love and is lost in such confusion and darkness, bless humanity who were made in love and shall in some time find this Love. Bless those who are beacons of Love for they are the bell-toll that shall show the way to God and bring the comfort of the Truth and shall be God’s Hands in the world, touching the lonely and the separate, the lost and those in great pain. Be God’s hands my beloveds, allow the Love flow through you. Listen to our guidance, be innocent and true and there will be great rewards for those willing and open and true to His Will.

God bless you beloved, beloved children. May peace be with you always and His Love finds a constant inflowing into your souls, beloved children. And your Angel Confucius will be with you upon all of your journeys for God has given me this purpose and blessing to be your guide to be with you as you learn and grow as God’s precious channels of Love. Beloved beloveds, I love you and always be with you. God bless you.
