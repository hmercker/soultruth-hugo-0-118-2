# Let your Soul Consciousness take precedence over your Minds

August 18<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Divine Love Retreat, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike


My beautiful birds, loving souls, in the beginning of these prayers, I promised you clarity and understanding. And for those who still remain confused I tell you that you are still entrapped within your minds and have not come to that place of soul awareness and as long as you continue to give your minds precedence, you will not find the clarity of which I have promised.

We have encouraged you to feel and know that place deep within you and many of you have felt this. Do you not feel the joy? Do you not feel within you the willingness to go wherever your Father leads you? And do you not feel a deep peace that tells you all is well and there is nothing that can take you from this path of joy. And for those of you who do not feel this, I say there is still time. Prayers are still coming and time for you to choose to release your concerns and be at peace. For from that place of peace will come the answers that you seek and the knowing that is truly known. I know some of you complain how difficult it is to release this mental condition. And I tell you, my beloveds, there are many angels at your disposal who will come and help you with this and bring you the healing and bring you what you are required to let go. And this is determined by your prayers and your desire. Are you willing to release this condition? Are you willing to enter a place so unfamiliar yet so beautiful and comforting and to walk in Light without the burden of your mental condition? For your mind absorbs much of this human condition and reinforces this within you.

Indeed, some of you fear losing your minds, but this is only a myth. In releasing your thoughts, you gain the universe, you gain great knowledge and peace and joy and knowing your Heavenly Father. This does not mean that your mind is lost, it merely means that your mind is no longer in precedence. You have found the balance of which we speak and ideas will flow in this river of Love and find their harmony and place within it. Your beings will glow in Love, acceptance and be at peace. Let go, my beloveds, let go. And we shall all flow in this river of Love together.
