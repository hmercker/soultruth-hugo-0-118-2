# How may I serve you dear Father

March 4<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Safari Hotel, Windhoek, Namibia

Augustine received by Al Fike


I wish to comment on your journey thus far, my two precious souls. Do you not see, how the souls of those you have been in contact with each place that you have travelled and journeyed, they have been stirred, they have been inspired, they are questioning and learning and growing and you see that this is your gift and part of your purpose to bring the power of God’s Love in greater measure into the lives of those you touch. And the comments that they have made and the love they have given is but the tip of the iceberg of what has been accomplished in your journeys thus far. And you ask this question, *"how may I serve you dear Father"*, are you not serving God in this way? Is this not a great blessing in purpose to come to your brothers and sisters in hopeful love as they see your dedication and your faith manifest, as you make your efforts to travel great distances to be with your brothers and sisters and God has provided the means for this and this will not be the only journey, nor the only purpose in your lives. My beloveds, each of you here has great gifts to share and works to perform and love to give and God shall answer your prayers my beloveds, when you ask to be of service, God will use you in many ways and propel you upon many paths, to serve Him, to bring love and change in this world. And what is it to answer a prayer, is it not for God’s Will to filter down into your lives, that you may do His Will and answer the prayers of those who reach out to Him and ask for a blessing. And this is how God manifest in many ways through His Angels, through His instruments on earth. And yet it is difficult for you to see this. God’s Will manifest through you, when it is indeed done in many ways maybe it is subtle in your vision, but it is powerful in its effects. Aah….what awaits you my beloveds, what awaits is a wonderment. Your souls are joyed by this feeling, the life of God’s Will sparking within you, manifesting and flowing.

God bless you, beloved souls, instruments of the Creator’s Will. God bless you. Augustine loves you.
