# To be chosen in this Way is a great Blessing

March 19<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

Augustine received by Al Fike


Your teacher Augustine.

There comes a great period of change in the world. Crumbling of the old and the bringing in of the new. And the great efforts made to assist you to come to the level of awareness and love are made in preparation for this time of change and flux within the world. The Angels put forth great effort on your behalf my beloveds, and many others in the world. To bring them to that place of clear consciousness of understanding. What it is that they must do to be a part to shift the world from such terrible darkness and confusion and error to a place of light and peace and love. It is a mighty work, and it will be indeed a great demand upon your persons but it will be a great fulfilling venture of joy and love, of unfolding, awareness and acts of Love. To be chosen in this way my beloveds is a great blessing for you and indeed each is part of this great plan and there are many (who) have chosen this work before they have even incarnated into the world. To have a clearer understanding is indeed a blessing. Forewarned of what is to come, gives you responsibility and power to prepare yourselves to be in that condition of spiritual awareness and Love. To know that you have dedicated yourself to your Heavenly Father and to be open to whatever God wishes for you to do in this world. This is not a life normally lived, common. This is exceptional and uncommon. And in this many look at you in confusion or anger and not understand but you must be strong enough to allow these onslaughts of judgement to fall away. Indeed as your Master has said, to be in this world but not of the world, for the world is destined to slough off these worldly….created by man and to exist in a different way. A way in greater harmony with God’s Laws and Love, and in these transitions there is great pain and resistance but it is God’s Will, This must come about and you my beloveds. you have come  to usher in this new world. And you know this from deep within you, you know this. Be at peace. Enjoy this life for what it has to give and know what comes is greater life and greater gifts.

Beloveds, you are being called and within your souls you rejoice in this calling. And this opening of your purpose, this opening shall be wider with each day as your souls (are) filled with the Love and you come to understand this bigger picture, this greater calling and purpose and as I say others are being called. Many. And you my beloveds will bring clarity to them, many others who in turn will bring light to many others and this light will spread. And the will of mankind will harmonize with the Will of God, in time.

Do not place great expectation upon this but merely follow your guidance with each day that comes and many surprises await and opportunities and doors will open. Oh my beloveds, brothers and sisters, we in Heaven rejoice in these opportunities of great unfolding of the Father’s Will in this world. For too long has mankind suffered so and lived in darkness. It is time to raise your brothers and sisters up.

The Truth to all who are willing to listen, to walk in this world as examples and beacons of light. And everywhere that you go you are being noticed my beloveds, there is something which you bring which awakens within the soul of another. That something is God’s Love.

Continue to walk in this world in this innocent way going about your life (?), being gentle and kind and generous, loving and engaging. In this way God will use you to touch another soul to touch many souls. I am so very pleased with you efforts and dedications and I will be with you if you walk upon this world and I will make my attempts to teach you, support you and love you. God bless you my beloved students and may you always walk in light. Augustine loves you dearly.
