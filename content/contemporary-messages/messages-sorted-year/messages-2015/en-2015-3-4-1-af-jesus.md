# Blessings

March 4<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Safari Hotel, Windhoek, Namibia

Jesus received by Al Fike

I have come, God bless you, I am Jesus.

I come to bless you my beloveds, and bless your journey together, for this is God’s Will my beloveds. It is God’s Will that you to be together, travelling this land, being a channel of God’s Love wherever you go, and so it shall be my beloveds. You shall be a channel of God’s Love wherever you go with each day that comes upon your journeys. You shall be blessed and so shall those around you. This is doing God’s work, to walk in faith, to follow God’s guidance, to welcome all those who come within your place and in so doing you shall learn how to spread the Truths. It shall come within your consciousness, your understanding, the words you speak, the actions that you take, the Love that flows. And so it was in my day upon this earth. I followed God’s Will and I performed the blessings with those around me. With the blessing of God’s Love all is possible my dear beloved children. Do not limit yourselves in this, but allow all barriers and judgments, fears and doubts fall away from you. And walk the path of Love. Do my work, do God’s Will. And every day shall be a mighty blessing, and in every day there shall be many blessings, beloveds.

God bless you, I bless you, I am with you, God bless you.
