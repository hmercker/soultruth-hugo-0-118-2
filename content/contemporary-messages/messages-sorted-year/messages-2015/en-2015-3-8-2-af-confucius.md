# Wherever you are in the World you will be united in Love

March 8<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Casa Piccolo, Windhoek, Namibia

Confucius received by Al Fike


Yes, you are always united my children, in Love, wherever you are in the world you will be united in Love. This is a gift God has given you. For you are all sincere souls seeking to do the work of God and the Master Jesus. And as such wherever you are in whatever time, there will be a connection, an awareness and understanding that you walk together as brothers and sisters in this world, and this shall be for all eternity my beloveds, all eternity you will walk together in love helping one another and working together to bring light in this world and the next - you will work together, bringing light and understanding to many.
