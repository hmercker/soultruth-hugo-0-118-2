# Acknowledge the Power of your own Will

February 21<sup>st</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

Confucius received by Al Fike


God bless you my beloved, beloved souls.  This is Confucius and I give to you my joy and encouragement.  To hear you pray for the great inflowing of God’s Love makes my soul soar.  To hear the sincerity of your prayers is like the flowers opening to the warmth of the sun.  Beautiful, each one, each soul present here is beautiful, a unique flower, a creation of God.

Indeed, do not underestimate the power of God’s Will and to acknowledge the power of your own will my beloveds, for each has free will, each beloved soul may choose.  And yes, there is much pain and disillusionment in mankind.  And it is for you my beloveds to overcome these conditions within yourselves, within the world, to have faith in the power of God’s Grace and Will and Love.  No matter how you perceive it within your mind, it is real and is powerful and will transform the soul from mortal to immortal, from human to Divine.  It is the Great Gift, the Saving Grace, the Redeeming Love.  And I ask you my beloveds, to seek this Love with all your hearts and souls, to know this Love so completely that you shall walk in this Light and Love every step and every breath you take, every moment you are in the world, this Love will carry you and guide you and heal you and inspire you.  It will take you down unexpected roads, surprising and beautiful, open doors you cannot yet imagine, bring into your consciousness, awareness of God’s Creation in the most beautiful and awesome ways my beloveds.  We walk together in this Light hand in hand, beyond the _____ and the shadows cast by this world into a place of joyous communion.  Shall we walk together in faith and Love and be a Light in the world, beloveds, beloveds?
