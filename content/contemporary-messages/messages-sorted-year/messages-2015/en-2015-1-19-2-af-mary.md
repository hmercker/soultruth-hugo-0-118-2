# Go with God

January 19<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

I am Mary. Go in peace and know that God’s Love and care for you is always reaching towards you, enveloping you in this light. You cannot stray far when God has you in His vision, when the angels surround you, when your souls yearn and long for His touch within, you cannot go far, and you shall not go far from this light, as God reaches and carries you back when you may stray. Bless you, my beloveds. Go with God. Be His channels of love and be at peace. God bless you. I love you.
