# It is important to bring Reform to Mankind

March 21<sup>st</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

Martin Luther received by Al Fike


God bless you, I am Martin Luther and I come to say some last words of encouragement for you, my brethren.

In my day I was a reformer, I brought change to the church, and many whom were within the structure of the church resisted my efforts to bring a clearer understanding of what it is to seek God, yet I persisted and in this persistence I gained greater support and I weathered the storms from those about me who would have me stop my efforts, yet within my heart I knew the Truth. I understood that the old rituals and the old ways could not persist for they become hollow and without meaning. There needed to be change, needed to strike within the hearts of my brethren, a feeling for God rather than rituals performed again and again without meaning or feelings. And today there is a need for reform within the minds of mankind for they have lost sight of God. They do not see the benefits, the Love, the blessings that are available to them through their prayers beseeching to God to bring to them what they require within their souls, this great Divine Essence. This is the highest of Truths, the most powerful of blessings. So my brethren, you become the reformers, you bring the message the new way for the tired souls who perform the rituals and the believes which are hollow. Their understandings within their minds are so many which merely pertain to the material world, yet the development of the soul lags far, far behind in these idle pursuits of pleasure and accumulation. Do you not see how important it is to bring reform to mankind? And how just one man can bring great change, one person, with dedication in their heart and a clear knowledge of the Truth within their minds and souls can bring reform. Continue to persist in this effort to bring the Truth of God’s Love to the world. And when those storms of anger and rejection come, be true to your God and know that you are loved and not alone. Indeed, your family grows and those who would support you will be there in these efforts to walk the earth and bring the change which will bring greater harmony to this world. Walk with your head high my friends and know that I, Martin Luther, stands with you and I will lend my strength, my love to help you bring change to the world. Reform is needed everywhere; change is a constant in the world. Walk with God, be prayerful and have faith and you will succeed. You will succeed to do God’s Will in this world and within your lives. So much work awaits and so little time to express this change to stir within the consciousness of mankind. The understanding that change is necessary. That their ways bring great disruption and disharmony to the world. Their thoughts are powerful as are yours my friends. Always think in positive ways, always have God in your consciousness and be strong, be strong and God will bless you mightily. As you go about your lives with this desire for light and change, grace and harmony, Love from God. Bless you, each one. Precious souls in the eyes of God. Bless you and may you be within that flow of His Love always. Your friend Martin Luther is with you in your efforts. Bless you and I love you.
