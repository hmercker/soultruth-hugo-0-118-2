# God’s Will is stronger

June 9<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike


I am your sister and friend, Faith. I have been with you in your conversations regarding

my old home in the centre for prayer and healing. And I wish to reassure you, my

beloved brothers and sisters, that God is working towards Light, solutions will be found,

Love will prevail within that place. And yes, there have been some distractions and there

are those there who would detract from this Light, and the forces for evil do indeed work

against what is meant to be in this place. But I tell you God’s Will is stronger. The efforts

of the angels will prevail and Light will abound in this place meant to be a centre for

Light, for Truth and Love. And so I ask you, my beloveds, be prepared for what is to

come. Be open to the invitation which was made a while back and never completely

fulfilled. You will fulfill this invitation and this purpose for you to be within this place

acting as channels of Love. It is a step that is necessary. And yes, this dear instrument

is meant to begin his ministry under this roof, a ministry that will have far-reaching

effects in the world. And I know that you perceive all the dynamics and conditions there

and those conditions within this country; you are not enthusiastic and worried for what

you must encounter and work through in this place. But I tell you God will clear away the

obstructions and is clearing away the obstructions so that the work may flow freely, and

you will have the freedom to express yourselves in great Light and beauty, and you will

be effective, and you will do God’s work. So I tell you, worry not but have faith that all

will unfold as is required and is within the Will of God. And those whose minds are

clouded with uncertainty and error, this will lift, clarity will come. Be compassionate and

gracious with those who turn to you. Be loving, and kind, and generous. Do not allow

negative thinking to cloud your reasonings and what you know within your souls to be

true. And be prayerful always, for God will guide you. And you have not wasted your

time in preparing and waiting. Indeed you have been patient and in your patience and

waiting you have been strengthened and gained greater insights, greater love and

compassion. You now have more clarity as to your role and this will open many doors

upon your arrival to this place of work, God’s house of Light. Blessed children, beloved

friends, beautiful souls, you are in God’s Grace and your souls wish to comply to God’s

Will, and you will do so and this will carry you through the world, and you will be a

channel for much love, comfort and healing for many, many souls. You stand on the

verge of a great adventure. Do not fear for those elements whom you feel will suffer

from your commitments, those souls around you who need your love and support. God

will take care of all of these aspects of your lives in harmony and Grace. Walk in faith

beloveds, walk in faith and know that all will unfold in love and harmony as you walk

with humility and obedience to the Will of God, all will unfold in harmony and love. God

bless you, my friends. I am with you so often and we in spirit work tirelessly to bring

about the Will of God, and you will find your way or walk this Path as you were meant to

walk. Be at peace and know you are truly loved, truly loved. God bless you, Faith loves
