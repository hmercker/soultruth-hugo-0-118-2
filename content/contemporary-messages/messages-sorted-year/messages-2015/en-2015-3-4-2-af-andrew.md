# Yes you are eager, and we are pleased

March 4<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Safari Hotel, Windhoek, Namibia

Andrew received by Al Fike


It is Andrew, your brother and friend.

Aah…. you are so eager my beloveds, my precious friends, to do God’s work and will in the world. To bring the truth of God’s Love, this great news to every soul. Every day brings you closer to that great flow of Love, the flow of God’s Will in the world and each day is a blessing and a gift with many opportunities, many possibilities and each day is a learning and a preparation, so that you may fulfil your part in Gods great plan of salvation for mankind. Each day you step closer to this, my beloveds, each day with yourselves being open and eager and receptive to what the day gives/gifts you. You learn something, you are strengthened within your souls, you receive a greater inflowing of God’s Love. And as you converse together and reveal your souls’ longings and your minds’ questionings to one another so you gain more clarity and understanding as we are with you impressing upon your minds what is true, what you need to know and yet you have this great eagerness to step many steps ahead, and to be at that place where the flow of God’s Love and Will is strongest and clearest and most powerful. My children it is one step at a time. It is one day at a time, it is one blessing at a time and you will grow and learn and this accelerates as time goes by and the days accumulate and you bring into yourselves the clearer and beautiful Truth and your vision changes, the wisdom of your soul grows, the gifts that lie within you flourish and blossom. Yes, you are eager, and we are pleased with this eagerness and God gives to you all that you require but indeed it is a journey and each step is important each day each moment each breath as you make the choices for light as you yearn and long and come ever closer to your Heavenly Father. Each day is a blessing, live this day to the fullest, and be receptive to God’s guiding hand and all manner of blessings and surprises and ……. ? and love will come your way. An open and wiling heart, a desire for the highest, a soul filled with this Love will bring the miracles you seek, the clarity and the purpose which is within each one. A great purpose will unfold as is meant to be in God’s great Will and this desire for you to fulfill this Will, this purpose, this hidden gem within you.

God bless you my beloveds. Andrew loves you and steps with each step upon your journey, I am there as many Angels who pray for you, in constant prayer, who love you and protect you and journey with you for you are so precious in our sight, in God’s sight. And loved beyond measure, God bless you precious souls, may you journey well and all of God’s blessings be yours. God bless you.
