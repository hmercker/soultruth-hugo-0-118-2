# Blessings

February 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

Blessed are the children of the Heavenly Father who seek His Love, who have a deep desire within their souls to know truth. Blessed are those who are humble.  Blessed are those whom in their lives, they express love and are generous of heart and spirit.  Blessings to you my children who make great effort to rise above the conditions of this world and the conditions that lie within yourselves and try to walk in the light.  You are blessed.

Continue on your journey and your search and your prayers and efforts to reach your Heavenly Father.  There is no greater work than this.  And in this, comes your salvation.  And all that you accomplish within yourselves, you shall bring into your light and spirit and carry on, progressing through the spheres into the Celestial Kingdom.  As you accumulate God’s Love within your souls and your souls flourish in this, carry on my brethren, carry on and do my work, for you are loved and you are guided and you are watched over and you are so needed in this world to bring light and truth and love and follow me, for I shall lead you and we shall work together in loving harmony.  Be at peace and know you are loved.  God bless you.  I am Jesus and I love you.
