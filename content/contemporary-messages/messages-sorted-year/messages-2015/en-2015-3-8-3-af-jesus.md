# Be humble and true within yourself

March 8<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, The Pinnacle, Windhoek, Namibia

Jesus received by Al Fike


…… and the kingdom is God’s Love. It is the inflowing of this Divine Essence that shall be the key that shall answer all of your questions and bring you to that place of harmony and peace of true knowledge and awareness. So simple my beloveds so simple, yet this is the most powerful truth of all, to know of the love of God, to be in the flow of His Love, to walk in this Grace, to be humble and true within yourself. And in this quiet walking of light and love comes a strength and a faith that is beyond your understanding at this time, that builds and grows, it comes with this light and shall continue to grow for all eternity. Your souls long for this my beloveds, you long for this and in your prayers and in your efforts to receive these blessings you will find your answers, and you will receive as you long. This great desire within you must come to your consciousness and allow this to go to God. Speak it, feel it, know it. And in this acknowledgment of your true soul longings comes the blessing, the opening, the knowing.  Can you not feel this now, my beloveds, do you not feel the opening of your souls, the warming within you, the peace surrounding you. This is what my brethren seek, and have sought for many years, this is what humanity longs for and so many do not know, cannot see, are blind and deaf, and mute. They only see with the faculties of their minds but you my beloveds, must see with the faculties of your soul. And this comes with the inflowing of the Father’s Love. True sate, true knowing, true feeling comes in this way. And you may read many books and ask many questions my beloveds, but truly it is in this rapport with God, this holy communion that you find the answers you seek. What is written is just a pale, pale description of the Truth, what you feel is what is real and true. And upon this road one must in time lay down all the error and all that which you have learned that is not in harmony with God’s Love and in doing so you will know a joy beyond imagining, in doing so you will find your Heavenly Father in such a deep and powerful way, that all that you have let go will matter not. Walk in Love my beloveds be that child of God and know that God is Love. Do not forsake the longings of your soul, the desires of your mind, but know that in time those two parts of you will come together in harmony. And when that comes you will walk a life that is powerful in truth and beauty and rich in experience in Love. It is available to all mankind, to all the souls in the world and all the souls in creation may receive this gift of Love. But it must be asked for, it does not come in any other way, but to say to God:  *" I long for Your Love, I long to receive Your Love"* and in this true asking you shall receive in great abundance. No greater gift can you receive, no purer blessing, no more powerful a touch that shall change you from every part of your being.

God bless you beloveds, I am Jesus, and I love you. This is my message and my message of Love needs to spread around the world. Speak this Truth, live this Truth, and you do my work.  God bless you my beloveds.
