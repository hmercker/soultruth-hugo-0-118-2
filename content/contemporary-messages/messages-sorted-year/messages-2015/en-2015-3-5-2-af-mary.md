# As you give in Love, so you grow in Love

March 5<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Safari Hotel, Windhoek, Namibia

Mary received by Al Fike


.... I will be with you when you are amongst the children. This work you do my beloveds is a work of love, a manifestation of God’s Will and as you give in love, so you grow in love. As you release your resistance and barriers to the flow of God’s Love through you and you embrace the children and the lost souls you bring great blessings to yourself and great blessings to those about you. This is doing God’s work, and this does indeed bring change in the world. Small steps lead to greater steps. Simple love leads to great change. And the road that you walk upon will widen substantially and there will be many travels together to bring this light through the world. When you love the children, you love yourself. When you feed the children, you feed your soul. When you give in this way the light brightens around you and you become a beacon which will draw many. God bless you, I am Mary and I love you so.
