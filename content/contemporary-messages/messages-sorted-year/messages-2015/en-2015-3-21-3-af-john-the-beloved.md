# The true Journey begins in the Discovery of the Soul

March 21<sup>st</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Lake Oanob, Rehoboth, Namibia

John the Beloved received by Al Fike


I am John, John the beloved.

Indeed, the winds of change blow through all of your lives and shall continue to do so. As the changing conditions of this world bring about a response by each one to adjust their lives, to bring greater harmony within their beings. And as God’s Love flows within your souls you will find the right road, you will know the steps to be taken. The soul knows the journey to take and exerts its will within you, yet you have the gift of free will my beloveds, it is your choice. Indeed, the mind makes great effort to rationalize the desire of the soul. The mind makes great effort to understand the nature of God and the nature of life and its meanings and purpose. Yet these questions are answered within your souls my beloveds. The true answers lie within that place, and the true journey begins in the discovery of the soul and that discovery is ignited by the inflowing of the Divine Essence within. You each have your journeys to take, you each have your unique questions to ask, and in your unique journeys you will find the answers. They will flow and unfold as you make your prayers to your Heavenly Father. And you consider what it is that you truly desire, what you need, where you must go, how you must act. And if you ask your minds these questions you will receive a response that is based on speculation and information gleaned by others, information they have put within your mind, so you act in some ways through the will of others, through the understanding of others. And I ask you is this being true to yourself? Is this being true that place that does have the answers you seek and does know the journey you must take. Yes, we in spirit do guide all of you and we help you to come to these conclusions from that place deep within you and we encourage you go to your Heavenly Father to ask for the sustenance you require that will give you these answers. It will open the door of understanding; it will carry you to light and enlightenment. Each journey is unique; each soul is unique. And each soul must take its own journey. But the Truth my beloveds, stands on its own. And will always stand. And it is for you to discover. To truly take within yourselves and hold it dear, these precious jewels of Truth. And express this in your lives and your beings. This is your responsibility with the gift given of life to seek the Truth, to live the Truth, to seek who you really are and who God really is. And when these understandings flower and flourish within you, you will then become God’s channel of light and love in the world. God beacons you all upon this road. When you partake of this journey, it is your choice, the timing where you begin, how you begin. But all is given with the clear desire to begin, to know, to understand. And God knows your desires before you know them, God responds to your prayers before you utter them, for you are soul and the great soul of God are in communion and it is for you to discover this, to know this relationship, to understand it, to exercise it, to benefit your soul growth. And the unfolding of your true selves. Walk in joy beloved souls and know that you are truly loved, and this Love is infinite and will carry you for all eternity, to at-onement, ecstatic beyond your imaginings, my beloveds. So as these gentle breezes caress your flesh think of them the breath of God and upon that breath is Love. God bless you I am John the beloved and I am very pleased to see you together in prayerful communion with God and you will come together my beloveds for further prayer and conversations ad in time you will work together to help to usher in the changes that are required for humanity to know peace, to be in harmony in this world. Yes, the ways and means are a mystery to you now, but in time all will be revealed nothing left to chance other than your desire and will and choice.

God bless you and keep you upon the path Divine my beloveds and carry you far into lands unknown to you now, both within you and on this earth. A wondrous journey of discovery, surprise, joy and deep understandings which will be revealed to you with God’s light within. Blessed are you beloveds. Be at peace and know that you are being guided. All is well, all is well.
