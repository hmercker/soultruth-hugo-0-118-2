# Blessings

March 21<sup>st</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Lake Oanob, Rehoboth, Namibia

Jesus received by Al Fike

It is your brother and friend Jesus.

I come to bless you upon your journeys. I come to encourage you to walk in the light and listen to your inner promptings. I come to say that I am with you. I come to encourage you to uphold the Truth of Love, of God’s Love, and to live this with each day of your lives. I come to tell you that much awaits you. Each one here has a wondrous journey to take and a purposeful life to live. God’s hand is upon you. He will guide you and the Angels will be with you my beloveds. And may His Love find a constant inflowing into your souls and (remove) all that stands in your way from fulfilling your purpose and finding the true source of all life and all goodness and all power and all joy, all this awaits. Open yourselves to this blessing. God bless you, your brother Jesus loves you. Walk with me and I will light the way. God bless you.
