# You cannot do this on your own

February 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

John the Beloved received by Al Fike


Carry this light with you as you journey home. Contemplate the words that were spoken, the conversations you have had with one another, the blessings of this time together and bring them deep within your heart and soul, and may they open up the awareness of greater Love and inspire you forward as God’s channels of Love in the world.

You cannot do this on your own.  You cannot do this from your willful nature.  You must have humility.  And you must support one another in love and in this way you will influence many and bring the truth to many.  You have been called my brethren, you have been called.  Now it is your choice to listen to this calling from deep within and follow God’s Will, which is to bring truth to mankind, to bring the truth of what it is that will truly bring salvation to those who are lost and without Love.

It is a great work, but it is a work you cannot do alone and you cannot do through the eyes of your mind, but must do through the eyes of your soul.  Seek ye the Kingdom my beloveds, seek ye the Kingdom and all will flow on to you and through you and about you and the way will open and you will know a great joy in service, a service of healing, of teaching, of praying, of supporting and loving your fellow man.  You have been called.  Take up the banner and walk forthright into this world, without fear and with great faith.  You will be guided.  God bless you beloveds.  I am John the Beloved and I love you.
