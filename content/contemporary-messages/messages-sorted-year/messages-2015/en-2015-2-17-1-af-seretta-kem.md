# Great Blessing is a great Balancer of your Bodies and Spirits, Minds and Emotions as this Love flows in

February 17<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Menefee, CA, USA

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

God bless you, this is your Helper, Seretta Kem.

May your souls continue to flourish in the Love for in this, great blessing is a great balancer of your bodies and spirits, minds and emotions as this Love flows in, so the fluctuations which occur on a daily basis within your beings, and the many conditions which you encounter during the day in the early conditions, the influences of spirits, - all of these aspects which influence your consciousness what you experience during the day are ameliorated by the Love of God. And when you experience imbalances within your physical systems, when there are great changes of hormonal fluctuations and chemistry within the body, it is important to note that you will have within you corresponding reactions within your emotions, within your consciousness, within your spirit. For all of these systems are interrelated and require balancing and nurturing. To be moderate in diet, to attend to your feelings, my beloveds, to acknowledge what you feel. And when those feelings are not what you would want, to give them up to God and to ask God to heal you. To replace anger and other difficult feelings, with His Love. And God will respond and will bring you the healing that you request. And your part in all of this is to choose for the Light, to reach for the Light, to long for that Love to heal all within you. For the power of God’s Love is great and all encompassing. It will bring peace within so that when the disturbances of the body manifest you are not swept away and enveloped and pinned down by these difficult conditions.

Walk in Light my beloveds, ask for God to heal you each day. To bring light, to bring peace, to bring harmony and balance within you, to pour His Love within your soul so that the great Light of powerful healing agent may work its wonders in your beings.

So often when that kernel of anger and hurt and judgment is within you it is reinforced by negative influences of the world, and you allow this to happen when you indulge yourselves in these feelings. And they are reinforced and they do indeed bring you to a lower condition spiritually. So, discipline is required. And understanding within yourself as to what is happening and the desire to reach for higher things in your thoughts and in your feelings. You are not a victim of your feelings, your emotions, you can control these things. This is what one does when one matures. To allow the feeling to pass. To acknowledge it and let it go. And to allow Love to take its place. We who guide you and are with you each day are indeed there to help you with this process, to give you strength, to remind you that you may indeed be in control of the situation rather than the situation control you. That you are centered in God’s Love and God’s Grace and in this way, you may move through all the conditions of the world. And when you encounter those who would fling barbs and hurtful thoughts and deeds in your direction, you do not feel the pain of this, merely to respond in compassion and love. Yes, you are human and this is difficult, yet you have working within your being something that brings you from being human to being divine. Do you not wish to encourage this, to allow this process to manifest on every level of your being. To walk in Light and Love. To be that individual that you long to be that is quite accessible. It is there. You are there within your beings. But you allow your mind free reign, and you indulge yourself in negative thoughts and feelings at times. And I do not say this in a form of reprimand or for you to feel guilt, but to bring to your awareness, to remind you that what you truly desires in your souls is Light and peace and harmony. And in order to accomplish this, you need to choose this. And to let go all of that which is not in harmony with God’s Love. And to be cognizant in each day and each moment, that it is a choice. And you are capable in walking in the Light always, if you so choose. And God and the Angels are always there to reinforce the choice for Light and Love.

So will you continue to indulge yourselves in negativity or will you step forward in light and choose the way of God’s Love in every aspect of your being and your life. Not easy, but it is possible. And every day presents its challenge and gives you the gift of this choice. And remember so many subtle variations within your beings caused not only by these inner emotions, of chemistry within your bodies. What you eat, what you think, who you are encountering in the moment the energies that mingle and shift between you. So much to be cognizant of. So many perceptions to hone, so many aspects to understand yet above all, the desire for Love will overcome all of these barriers and problems and dilemmas and lack of understanding the power of God’s Love is great.

God bless you beloveds. May you continue to choose Light to be Light to express Light to be that channel of Love that God desires you to be. And we who are with you will support you with every step, every breath, every thought. Every longing of your souls, we are there. You are never alone. God bless you and may God be with you on your journeys. Your helper and friend Seretta Kem ever at our side in support and love and prayer. God bless you.
