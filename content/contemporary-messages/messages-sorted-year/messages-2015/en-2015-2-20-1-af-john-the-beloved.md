# The Soul is the true Self

February 20<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

John the Beloved received by Al Fike


I am John.  And welcome to this circle of love which will expand in the coming days and will bring many blessings to each one of you my dearly beloved sisters and brothers.  The soul is the true self.  The soul is what you seek to know, to understand, to become truly consciously aligned with the existence and consciousness of the soul.  And until one truly is in that place of awareness and understanding, the authenticity that you speak of is not possible, for much is distorted within the mind.  For the mind absorbs many influences and has been taught many things and is only marginally aware of the desires and inklings of the soul.

And this is your quest, my beloveds, to know your souls, to enliven your souls, to receiving God’s Great Love and in that, comes truth and awareness and authenticity.  In that, you will know great joy.  In that, your faith will grow.  In that, there will be no death nor fear, nor ambivalence.  Indeed this journey to the soul and this journey to God is fraught with many pitfalls and barriers as your mind and conditions of this world and the distortions, which are so much a part of every soul upon this planet, work against you in this journey, cause fear and doubt and pain, but with faith, with your sincere prayers to your Heavenly Father, with your willingness, to walk this path with sincerity and honest faith, to slough off all those conditions that are not in harmony with Love.  Yes, to walk innocently  into the light as a child, trusting in your Heavenly Father, knowing that His Love is the redeeming Love, the saving Grace, the great healing of the soul and so much more.  And so I encourage you my beloveds to seek the Divine Love and along the way you will struggle and you will doubt and you will fear, but this does diminish as the power of God’s Love takes precedence within you and allows you to truly know who you are, that being that Dawn created, unique and beautiful and precious beyond price, glorious beyond words, beautiful, so beautiful.  And you all have Divine Love within your souls my beloveds. That light burns bright within you and you will continue to know this, to feel this, to acknowledge it and to grow with it.  Your beings flower to the Light and warmth of God’s Great Presence and Love.

Trust in this my beloveds and know that no other can walk your path with you.  It is between you and God and we are with you as well in our prayers and encouragement, but indeed it is your effort, your prayers, your desires that will bring you to that place of true knowing.  There is no other way, but you be not alone.  You are truly in that place where you are with God only.  And when you are in that Light and that Presence, lay down your fears, your anxieties and your doubts, and say to your Heavenly Father, I give You this, for I am tired of carrying these burdens.  I do not wish to be in that place of darkness.  I wish to be in the Light, free, free of these human conditions.  I wish for Your Love to redeem me and bring me to a place of pure ecstatic joy, awareness beyond our dreams, a knowing that is unassailable and understanding of the truth, as only the soul is able.

Can you walk to that place my beloveds? Can you trust in the desires of your soul?  Can you trust in the loving care of your Heavenly Father? Can you release the cloak of pain?  It is all within your capacity and you are given the gift of free will and in this choice, you know, you truly know what you desire and what is available to you, such a gift of knowing  such a gift given to you by your Heavenly Father!  And each time you make that choice for Light, you grow stronger and all the other aspects which hold you back grow weaker.  And upon this journey, there will be a time when they will not exist.   They will have evaporated to nothing.  And you will look back and wonder why you held on so long, when such joy awaits you, such true understanding and such honesty and authenticity than yourself emerging in a strong and powerful way.  And at this time my beloveds, you will be unassailable.  You will see the error that surrounds you, but you will not be a part of this, instead, you will be in the world, but not of the world.   And in this way, you will be a clear channel of the Father’s Love in this world, bringing Light and healing and comfort and peace to so many.

All of humanity suffers in this way.  They cannot bridge this chasm of aloneness and pain and doubt.  They are afraid. Yet you my beloveds walk boldly into the Light.  Make this choice.  Make this choice and others will follow. They will know your strength and lean upon you and come with you.  This is God’s will that you do so.  God’s Love is for all who seek it.   Tell your fellow man that this is so.  What more do you need tell them?  What more is necessary, but to tell them that they are loved, that they may receive this gift of Love.  This will open the door to this world of truly knowing at-onement with God, at-onement with all the wonderment of Creation, of Truth, of Light of Joy.  What more would you want?  What greater riches could you seek?  So I pray with you my beloveds, walk the Divine path.  Be earnest in your prayers.  Be loving in your actions and know that God is with you always, always.  God bless you.  Your friend John is with you and loves you dearly.  God bless you.
