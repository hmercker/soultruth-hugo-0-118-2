# God will lift the Burdens from you

February 18<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

Mary & Confucius received by Al Fike

**Mary:** Gentle breezes of God’s Love come through you and into your souls, adding to the light that burns bright within each one of you my beloveds.  You prepare a place for the souls who come and are drawn and who are curious and who long for the Father’s Love.  My beloveds, be strong and clear in what you say and what you do with these precious souls.  They come for healing and love and nurture.  They come to know God.  And you prepared a place of light, of welcome, of serenity.  And we thank you for these efforts.

My dear daughter, God has great love for you, and you have nurtured many over the years and you have done so with humility and love.  And the light grows bright within you my beloved and you will know peace.  Though those around you do not recognize your beauty, there are many who do and though you know not those feelings from them, within their souls, they know, they know that you are God’s instrument of Love.  Be at peace my daughter, be at peace and God will lift the burdens from you and bring the healing which you so desire.  Be at peace and know that you are truly loved that the angels surround you.  So many in your life cry out and you respond and it is your desire to bring relief to so many, yet often you do not see that you yourself require the healing and the peace, yet God desires to give this to you my daughter, in abundance.  And as you are healed you will heal, as you are loved, you will love and the burden of your physical infirmities will be lifted with faith and prayer and acceptance of what your Heavenly Father has to give to you.  Blessed are the peacemakers, blessed are the channels of God’s Love, blessed are those humble and loving.  God bless you my beloveds, God bless you and keep you in His care and light.  And you, you are my charges whom I care for and love deeply.  God bless you my beloved children.  I am Mary and I love you.

**Confucius:** Yes, we have much work to do to help you, my daughter. There is much to accomplish to relieve your burdens, your pain, and we shall work diligently in these days to come to help bring you the healing you require.  Think of these days as a time to replenish, to be given to.  Do not overly concern yourself with those around you.  God will take care of all of you and those simple needs will be fulfilled.  Be at peace and know you are loved and God desires to heal you, beloved, beloved soul, that you may continue His work of the Love in this world.

I am Confucius.  I give you my love and my healing to you precious soul.  God bless you.
