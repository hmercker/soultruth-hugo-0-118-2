# Be together in Love and Harmony and soulful Longing

March 5<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Safari Hotel, Windhoek, Namibia

Peter received by Al Fike


I am Peter.

I am pleased to see you together and you have been gathered together in many places, and this is for a purpose, this has purpose this gathering, to bring light. Indeed, amongst you is a great power for change in the world. Many gifts and potentials to bring light and change and love into this world. And you will continue to come together in places around this globe and work together in this way.  So much you are not aware of my beloved children so much to accomplish that your senses cannot perceive at this time. And there is much work to do, much work, and love to bring to do your Father’s Will to walk this earth in light. Continue to pray together beloveds. For the commitment that you carry together shall bring great change, shall uplift many, shall help each of you to learn and grow, to expand your souls and awareness of Truth. And you replenish one another by your presence. The gifts that you share, and we are happy that you respond to our summons to be together in love and harmony and soulful longing to be a channel in this world of God’s Love. And every step that you take on this small journey together in this place will be blessed, the Angels will surround you and blessings given to many, to the dear children and to those in great need. And when you gaze upon this deprivation, do not feel a sense of guilt for what you have but praise God for this blessing in your lives of such abundance and ask God to show you how you may utilize all aspects of your life to serve Him and to change the condition in this world. It is a great work, requires deep wisdom and a willingness to listen to God’s guidance to know His Will, to act in ways which will have a great effect and ripples of change and longings of the soul implanted in all those that you meet. Much is given to you my children, and indeed much is expected but these expectations are not beyond your capacities but we encourage you to grow in your souls to receive an ever greater inflowing of God’s Love, so that the gifts that lie within you may flourish and express themselves for the benefit of mankind. You walk in God’s Light, you are His channels of Love, you are mightily blessed- every part of you, every part of your lives is a blessing, a gift and God so loves you that He opens the doors to many possibilities, opportunities, work of the soul. Continue to journey upon your paths, continue to nourish the joy within you and the love that you share will grow and expand and you will enfold many upon this path. Relieve those who suffer, bring blessings to the lost souls, teach Truth to those who so desire this, and inspire those who long for God to understand what it is they long for. Great work lies ahead, many facets, many aspects of this work will be performed by you, my children. And you are eager; you desire this within your souls. And you will be guided as you have been and will continue to be. Journey in Love, journey in peace and journey in joy. Serve your Heavenly Father in wondrous ways and a time will come when your eyes are open and you will see all the wondrous beauty at this work and this will bring you great joy, the Love will flow, the Light will expand and the journey will take on a greater depth of meaning and purpose and so full understanding. Each day draws you closer to this, each day a blessing and bring alive God’s Love within that day in your beings into this world. God bless you my intrepid travelers, my beautiful souls, God bless you. Peter loves you and I am with you.
