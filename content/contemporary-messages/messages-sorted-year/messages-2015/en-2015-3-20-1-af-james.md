# Be generous, do not judge but love, be joyous in your Work

March 20<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

James received by Al Fike

God bless you, it is James, the apostle and I come to reassure you my beloveds that you are upon the correct path in your lives and what you do and who you are and how you express God’s Love in your lives will have profound impacts upon many. Continue in these efforts. Beseech your Heavenly Father for guidance with each day and make those efforts to be in good spiritual condition that you may be receptive to this guidance and the flow of God’s Love through you and about you. And in this way what is required for you to express your efforts in the world as God’s channels of Love will be provided and given in abundance. There is no need for worry or concern but to be sure that you are indeed guided and you are strong in the flow of this Love.

My daughter, all will be provided for your efforts with those lost souls, all will be provided. Be receptive to your guidance and consider what is required for you to do this work. And it is not a sin to ask for assistance. For you spread the blessings for those who can assist you, they too are blessed in this effort, they too receive joy and a sense of accomplishment, and this is a gift, this is a gift. These poor and lost souls are a gift to all of you for they allow you to open the doors of love and compassion, and wisdom. To show them the way out of their dilemmas, to assist them in their efforts to struggle from a dark place to a place of light. And it is important to feed those who are lost, for in the feeding, in this giving there is a blessing given to each soul and the nourishment is blessed by the Angels, a healing through it to assist the physical. And when you touch those who are lost and dying and afraid, you bring comfort. When you pray with them, you bring healing and love. And how much you benefit by this my daughter and for all those who assist you in this, benefit. A gift, a blessing. Continue in your efforts. And continue to replenish yourself to make those times of quietness to be with God. For even our Master Jesus in His ministry took time for replenishment, to be with His Heavenly Father. To gain greater insight, to be guided and shown. Do not feel that this is not a gift as well to have these times, for they are a gift and a blessing. You will find your rhythm in life and you come very close to your purpose my daughter. Be at peace, be at peace and know how greatly you are loved. How you become an example for many to express God’s Love in this world.

And all of you bring Love wherever you go. How many in this world do so and how many in this world need this and this flow of Love will intensify and become purer, will have great effect on those you meet and what you do in this world. As you walk in faith in this way there will be a great response from others, a crying out. Have compassion and patience. For even those who cry out in rejection are only expressing the pain within which they long to have healed and they see within you the wondrous effects of Love, the serenity and the flow of healing and comfort. And for some they cannot withhold this cry of pain for they recognize that deep well within you that is of Love. Have compassion. Be generous, do not judge but love, be joyous in your work. And walk this path Divine in great faith and trust. God will guide you as He has done and will continue to do so. And every Angel who has spoken to you, my beloveds, brings this message, assures you, that you do indeed walk with God and you are not in the wilderness, you are not alone but with God and His Angels. And He brings the brothers and the sisters together to affirm and reassure one another of this truth, and what a blessing that God carries His children around the world to come together in harmony and Love.

God bless you my beloveds, your brother James is with you. And I am with you my daughter when you feed the children, when you care for the sick and the elderly, when you bring your love into this world, I and many others are with you in this effort and it is blessed. And many feel drawn and compelled to support you my daughter in this world. Accept this support, accept their love and know that this is a gift from God.

God bless you and God is with you. His Love burns within your souls and carries you into His bosom in loving care. God bless you.
