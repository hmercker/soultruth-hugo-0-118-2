# Open yourselves completely to your Heavenly Father

August 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2015

Divine Love Retreat, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

My beloved children, you have reached out to one another, and you have joined hands in Love, and you have found harmony amongst you, beautiful, beloved souls. Now you much reach God and find harmony with Him. Open yourselves completely to your Heavenly Father, who at this moment pours His Love upon you. Release your burdens. Those who are hiding, come out, come out. We all long for God and He is here for you in His great Love, He is here for you in His deep and abiding care for you. He wants you to know how much you are loved. He wants you to know how deep that Love is. Blessed children, come together and be with God. He is here and He loves you. God bless you. I am Andrew and I love you.
