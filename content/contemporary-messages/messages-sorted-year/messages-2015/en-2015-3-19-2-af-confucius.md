# Know that you come very close to that Time of great Work and the Fulfillment of God’s mighty Plan

March 19<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

Confucius received by Al Fike

Even the beast is drawn to the light my beloveds.

Yes, you do the Father’s Will and the Master’s work in this world. It requires from you a great deal, a willingness to move in this flow of light and love in ways that may not appear to be ……. (?) see your mind but is indeed part of the flow and unfolding of the Father’s plan for the salvation of mankind. Your purpose my beloveds is to ignite others….. (?) into the world, to teach others, to be that one central point of flame that shall bring light and light many candles. ……. (?) My beloveds, this is a great purpose, and you should not feel it as a burden but as a joyful, to your Heavenly Father a loving dedication to what has been given. So much work to do my beloveds. Yes, you will be required to travel to many places, and it will be difficult not to stay in one place to see through and find the completion of the efforts that are started. This will be difficult, but it is required that you continue in your journeys and walk the Truth in loving acceptance. When you are ready and have come to that place of faithful obedience to our Father’s Will and your minds have become subservient to the minds of the souls, then you will be ready and your soul will carry you. That consciousness that resonates with the consciousness of God.

Be at peace my beloveds and know that you come very close to that time of great work and the fulfillment of God’s mighty plan. It comes very close. Pray for God to heal you of all obstructions that may inhibit the flow of this Love. Do not let anything stand in your way for many will cast dispersions upon your efforts and you cannot listen to these voices of humanity that are lost and confused who do not understand the true purpose of a soul’s yearning and flowering in the Love. You must….... which God has laid before you and trust in His great and mighty wisdom which will guide you in ways yet unfamiliar to you but will truly manifest in powerful ways. Listen, my beloveds, know the Will of God. And as this knowledge become sure and palpable you will know in a way the presence of the Angels and the voice of God. Continue to pray for the Love of God to flow into your souls and continue to beseech your Heavenly Father to show you the way, continue to ask that this great healing within you continues and is fulfilled so that you may be truly free from the conditions of humanity and truly know the way.

And in this we shall do a mighty work that shall reverberate through many, many centuries upon this world and bring peace and bring light to this beautiful world and this light shall reverberate into the universe. This is God’s doing my beloveds. Be His humble servants. You must know the voice of God, and you will, you will and you do.

Bless you, my beloveds.
