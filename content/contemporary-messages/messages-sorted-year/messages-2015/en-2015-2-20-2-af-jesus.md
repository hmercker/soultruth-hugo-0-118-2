# Love will indeed bring you all together

February 20<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike


I wish you to know that each soul drawn here for the coming days, hungers for the Love of God.  Though there may be disagreements within their conscious awareness of what this desire is, there is a true hungering for Love. Be open and loving and accepting my beloveds.  Do not insight arguments or disagreements.  This will not accomplish what you desire to do here, but to allow each soul their place.  And when you hear what you believe to be error, say a prayer for them.  And when you hear their souls sing, give praise to God for His divine blessing upon them.  All will be well and Love will indeed bring you all together, for this is what is important, and you know this.  And you will see how God’s Love works it’s miracle within your mist, bringing the souls to a place of receptivity and openness and truth.  Bless you my beloveds.  I am your brother and your friend.  I am Jesus and I love you.
