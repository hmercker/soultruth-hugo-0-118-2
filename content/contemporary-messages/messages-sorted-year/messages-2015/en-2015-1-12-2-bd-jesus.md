# You have a deep Purpose

January 12<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

God bless you, my beloved souls. This is a deep and purposeful time in your world, and you are needed, my beloveds, deeply needed. There is so much pain and suffering with the lost. Yes, it is a deep purpose. I am Jesus and each of you will carry the blessing of this deep awareness and the power of the Divine Love. You must let this flow for all the lives you touch brings peace and love and deep awareness of God’s blessed Love for all the souls in your world. So many are lost, so many are suffering. And you, my beloveds, with your ability to pray (?) with God will bring the healing and love that is so needed in your world. My precious ones, do remember to let this flow and let it come from you (?) to your world, beloveds.

Beloved gifts of God will come from you, my dear ones, and you will brighten the light, and bring the peace and the comfort and love, and let this deepen as you move upon your journey in this world. Yes, my beloveds, you have a deep purpose. Let this flow. Let this help and comfort. This is so needed in your world. Remember you have a deep purpose. Let this grow and let this flow from you as you move upon the path of the Divine Love. You do have this power and the ability to perform what is needed, my precious ones. Yes, you are beloved souls with a deep purpose, my precious, my beloveds. Go forth with the power of the Divine Love and (?), gifts of Love Divine, precious, precious. Yes, you have this purpose, and yes, you need to do this, it will deepen the love and bring the change that is so needed in your world. And I will uphold you, precious, precious ones.
