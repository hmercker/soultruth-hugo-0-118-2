# I pray that you will hear my Words

March 15<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

Martin Luther received by Al Fike

I am Martin Luther, and I have come to reassure my brothers and sisters that God is indeed Love and loves each one of you dearly. And each one here knows God within their hearts, understands this Love and is open to its influence and blessing. Though within your minds you indeed have different perspectives and understandings and experiences, yet within your souls you have an understanding that is common amongst you, and this is that God does indeed love you and that His loving Essence is within you.

In my day I tried to change the church, and I battled and rallied against error and the corruption, and within my heart I knew that I was doing the right actions and words to help change the conditions of the church. And I did bring reform and a new understanding, but there is always a greater understanding my beloved brother, a higher truth. A road which must be travelled to truly understand who you are, who is God, what is the meaning of this life and the meaning of the life that comes.

In prayer, in your opening of your heart to your Heavenly Father you are gleaned much understanding, an awareness of the Truth. And these beloved souls who have come to you speak from their hearts a Truth that is valuable and has much power to bring you close to God. For God enfolds you at this moment, God raises you up to a place of light and peace and joy and love. Drink deep, drink deep my brethren, for those living waters of God’s Essence flow to you and will flow into you as you open yourself to this, this transformative Love, this redeeming Love. This Love is beyond your mental understanding but is recognized by your soul, which craves this blessing and this shall be a desire which will be fulfilled for all eternity and in time you shall reside within the kingdom of Heaven. Walk this straight and simple path and God will bring you the answers you require, the comfort that you desire, the peace that you crave, the connection with your Creator which is indeed available to all souls who wish this. It will be obtained through your sincere prayer to receive.

My beloved brothers and sisters, go with God, go with God! And I await your entrance into the Celestial kingdom which you surely shall enter if you believe my words. No church, no other being, no other man or woman, can bring this about. It is for you and your prayers and your efforts to be close to God that will accomplish this. A simple and powerful truth of Love given freely, given generously, and given to all who ask. Every soul in this world is worthy and in this way are equal and loved equally. Love your brothers and sister my brethren, love yourself, for the world is in great need of love and this will change this world and all the inequities will crumble, all the wrong doings will fall away and harmony will be in this world as it is in Heaven with the power of this Love.

I thank you for this time to speak, and I pray that you will hear my words, and I will be with you in your prayers to seek your God in love and hope and joy, with great faith and trust. God bless you.

Your friend and brother Martin Luther has come with this word of encouragement, and I thank you for this opportunity to speak, God bless you.
