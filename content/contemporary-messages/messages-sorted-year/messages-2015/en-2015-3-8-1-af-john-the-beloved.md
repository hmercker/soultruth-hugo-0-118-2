# You are beginning to feel the Power of that deep Purpose

March 8<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Casa Piccolo, Windhoek, Namibia

John the Beloved received by Al Fike


…..and may the peace that passes all understanding be with you my beloveds and the Father’s Love find a constant inflowing into your souls. Yes, there is much work ahead for each one of you and you are beginning to feel the power of that deep purpose that God has put within each one and as you come together you affirm this, you affirm your awareness of your deep soul desire, that deep soul-purpose. Those things that you have committed to do with God and God is ushering you along the path that these things may manifest in ways that are meant to be in this world. And doors that are meant to open and you are meant to walk through and souls you are meant to meet. And a life you are meant to live, in service, in harmony with God’s Will and Love. And you are past groping along in the dark, your lives are now lit with the consciousness of God’s presence within it. And so you must indeed follow your own deep and inner awareness of where you must go, where you must be and how you must in-act the Will of God, the Truths of God and the Love of God. You are no longer babies but now you can walk. And now you understand the power of God’s Love. And in this you have a responsibility to be in that flow as powerful as you can, to walk in this light at all times, to pull yourself up from that human condition and to release all those parts of you that are dead weight and unnecessary and to release that human condition to God. We do not ask for perfection, we only ask for a desire and an intention to be pure in your thoughts and your deeds. Indeed this is a great invitation and expectation upon you as you live in conditions that are not conducive towards this but for you to be leaders and examples, to be beacons of light. It is required for you to make this effort and it is truly your soul’s desire to do this to strive and aspire to the highest, to this light and in many respects you are successful and at times you fall. But are you judged by the Angels or God? No, you are only love and nurtured, given what you require in guidance and love and healing and support, so that you may make that choice again- for light. The Divine path does not have the harshness of judgement but only love and acceptance and encouragement and an abundance of gifts of love and healing of peace and joy. These blessings rain upon you my children. And as long as you do not turn away or become apathetic you will indeed grow and flourish in this and truly understand the power of love, the power of God’s Love, to change the mortal to the immortal, the human to the Angel. And you are embarked upon this process each transforming at their own rate, in their own time through their own choices in this transformation. And for many that seed within them is nurtured but has not sprouted, but for you my dear ones, you have become saplings destined to become great trees flourishing in the sunlight of your Father’s Love and the living waters nourishing you. And every day new growths are spreading and expanding your beings, every day God tends to you and brings you to fruition. Indeed when that day comes, you will flower in glorious beauty……. a gift to humanity, a gift to yourselves, a gift to God. And such joy it brings us to see you grow, to watch this miracle, to praise God for claiming another child. And bringing this beautiful child into the family of God’s children.

Beloveds, beloveds, be joyful, be joyful……. for you are in God’s garden, tended and nurtured and you are beautiful beyond measure. Beautiful. Bring your beauty wherever you go. Be loving and kind and generous. Allow this beauty in light to spread throughout this world so that others may indeed become a part of this wonder, this garden, this beautiful place where you and God are together in loving harmony. Go find your way my children for how can you not find your way when God’s Hand is upon you and the Angels guide you and the road is clearly lit. Follow your hearts, walk in faith and know how beautiful you are, how loved you are. Be joyous and filled with that knowing, that Love, that Presence of God. God bless you and keep you enfolded, and I love you as so, so very many Angels pray for you and love you. And the light expands. Do not be afraid but push forward in faithful trust that you are being lead and guided. God bless you, God bless you beloved souls.
