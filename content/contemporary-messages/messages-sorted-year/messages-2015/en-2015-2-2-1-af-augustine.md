# You long to serve God

February 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved and beautiful souls. It is your teacher Augustine. Each one of you carries a light. Each one of you has a longing within your souls to be with your Creator, to understand and acknowledge and receive His Love within you; that Essence of God will flow into your souls and bring the answers that you require and guide you along your life’s path, your destiny. It is the key that shall open all doors and bring you that great peace that passes all understanding. Beloved souls, you long to serve God; each one of you has a desire to serve God. And in order to do so in harmony with His Love, the flow of His Love and Will, you must, you must seek this Love with all your heart and souls. Seek it like a thirsty man seeks the water. Seek it like a dying man seeks salvation. Seek it like a hungry man seeks to fill his stomach. Beloveds, this is what you seek and long for. And this desire must become the greatest desire within your being. Its intensity, your need for it, must be foremost in your consciousness. And in this desire and need, placed within you by your Heavenly Father, it will be sated and given in abundance. You will thirst not, hunger not, and you will receive salvation. It is the greatest gift given to mankind, and the highest gift attainable from your Heavenly Father. And it is only you, in your asking, acknowledging the desires within, only you can receive this. Only you can make this effort and open yourselves to this blessing, for it is between you and your Creator, this special benediction that will lead you down the road to greater joy, much happiness and peace, wisdom, and will show you a new way, a new opening with yourselves, of expression and love, of knowledge, truth. My beloveds, seek ye the kingdom and all things shall be added unto you. Seek God within your hearts and souls and you will be filled with this joyous Love. As you seek to serve God you will find your way within the flow of His Love. There will be no mysteries. There will be no questions. There will be a knowing, so deep and profound; a faith so pure and all-encompassing, that you will walk your path with a surety and a strength, and a wonderment, and a joy that so very few in this world know.

Beloved children, seek this Love. It is meant for you, as it is meant for every soul who walks this world, who walks in the world of spirit. It is meant for all. The waters await your thirst, to quench your appetite and bring you what you require to be at one with your Heavenly Father. It flows into your souls at this very moment. Open wide, beloveds. Quench your thirst. I love you. I am well pleased with the efforts that you make in your desire to be a channel of love in the world. And as you walk your path and travel upon your journeys and speak your truths and love your fellow man, God’s hand will be upon you, and His angels will surround you. God bless you, beloveds, God bless you.
