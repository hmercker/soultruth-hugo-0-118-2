# An Example of God’s loving Channel in the World

March 6<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Farm Elim, Gobabis, Namibia

Augustine received by Al Fike


It is your teacher Augustine.

Do you not see the wonderment of God’s Will to bring you to this place. The blessings that are bestowed upon you as you are willing to come to answer the call.

And the dear soul who abides in this place brings light, prepares for you a place of comfort and love and she listens to God’s small voice within her and opens herself to His Will and walks in places of darkness and depravation and does so willingly with love. Honoring all that she meets with love. An example of God’s loving channel in the world. Watch this beautiful soul, my beloveds, as she traverses through the conditions and needs of these poor yet eager souls who wish to be in the light and yet such hopelessness and confusion. She brings hope, she brings light, she brings solutions to the dilemmas. And this is doing God’s work. So my children, walk with her and bring your hope and love. Do God’s work this day, embrace each child this day, every soul you encounter is a precious gift to you to be that channel of Love. To put aside your fears and your judgements, and your misconceptions and be that clear channel of Love this day and in so doing you have fulfilled your purpose upon this journey. And you will bring this insight and experience to others when you return to your homes. And this will be indeed a blessing for them. And to truly gain insight into the reasons and conditions for such great poverty will help you in the future, for the world to be lifted up my children. The very lowest of the low must be lifted. And there is so much in this world, in these bitter and soul-deadening conditions.  So many are trapped in this, require some effort by those of you who have more resources to bring a change. To inspire those who feel trapped, to struggle out of this terrible darkness. Indeed this is a difficult work but it is necessary. For this world cannot be divided in two, those who have nothing and those who have everything. There must be more equanimity. More love and honoring of all souls. This is the Father’s Will. In this work that you embark upon is blessed by your Heavenly Father who loves the little child in the dirt as much as he loves you. All are equal in His eyes, all are precious, all are beautiful. See through these eyes my children and let your love reach out to every soul. Embrace all you meet, be that channel of Love that you declare you wish to be.

God bless you beloveds, God’s hand is upon you and the Angels are with you, and you shall be upheld as you experience this day. A great blessing to each one of you. God bless you. Augustine loves you and embraces you.
