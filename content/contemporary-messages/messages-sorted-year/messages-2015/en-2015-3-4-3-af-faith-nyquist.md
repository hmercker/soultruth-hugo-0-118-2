# You will indeed do the Work as laid before you

March 4<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Safari Hotel, Windhoek, Namibia

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

God bless you. I come to reassure you my beloveds, my precious friends, that you will indeed do the work as laid before you in my home and you will travel to many places in the world to do this work. It is important that you are physically present in these various places in the world for this brings a powerful manifestation of love and blessings wherever you go, given your desire to be this instrument of God’s Will. Yes, you will work in that far off place, and you will work in many places, many destinations. Do not create barriers or speculations but be loving and open and receptive to the flow of God’s Will, for this is not for you to create it is for God to manifest through you and as such you must be humble and open and willing my children, following your Heavenly Father allowing His Will to manifest in this perfect way, harmonious and with great effect in the world. And so you walk between your desire to manifest initiatives and plans and your soul’s desire to follow the Will of God and this creates a conflict and restlessness, yet God has placed you where you are meant to be- has done so with great harmony. Remember this my friends, God’s Will is both clear and a mystery and every day brings a clarity of the sun and the mystery of all the possibilities inherent in this day. Walk in light and discover what God may gift you. Yes beloveds, my dearest, I love you dearly and I have been with you often and you need not worry. You need not worry, be at peace and know that God’s Will manifest in your lives more powerfully with each day, more powerfully with each blessing of His Love.

God bless you and I love you.
