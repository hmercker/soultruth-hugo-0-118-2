# Personal for Henning

March 8<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, The Pinnacle, Windhoek, Namibia

Matthew received by Al Fike

You have a purpose my son, and you know this and you long for this to manifest in your life. You are restless and this purpose will manifest in time for God has given you all that you require to do this work, this spiritual work, and it does remain a mystery in your consciousness but it will come in clarity and power and truth. Do not resist this, observe the example of your dear sister, who has given up much to be a channel of God’s Love in the world, and she does so willingly and in joy, and you are blessed, and you are all blessed, with great potential and gifts which you do not recognize at this time but God will show you the way. Be patient and be prayerful and that great mystery that lies within you shall come to light and manifest in wondrous ways. The world needs all those in light and love to come forth to bring change and equanimity and harmony into the world. And all are invited to this table to make that change to overturn the distortions and the error which mankind has for so long lived in and promoted and brought pain to so many. The solutions will come with those who are willing to step forward and be God’s channels of Truth and Love. And the doubts and the fears will fall away with faith and love and all will manifest in harmony. Each child here is beloved by God. Each is precious and filled with purpose and great ability and you are willing to acknowledge this and walk with God. So you will change and so will your life. Identification (?) of purpose and love and service will manifest in wondrous and surprising ways. This cannot be if you do not know the Will of God and in order to know the Will of God you must receive this Divine Essence, His Essence, within you to open your souls and your perceptions so that you may hear that small voice within you that will guide you and show you the way. Yes, this is challenging and possibly not what you expect, but it is an invitation for you to step forward to take this risk to walk in light in greater ways. And it does not cost you anything my dear brother, to seek God and everything is given in return. Each soul here has a light and gifts to give and love to give. You are all invited to know the will of God in your lives. It will be given if you are open to this and although we use this instrument to speak to you in this way, each is capable of receiving this guidance within themselves. Ask and you shall receive, knock and the door shall be opened onto you and seek this Love and you will know the meaning of my words, what is meant, what is invited, what is shown.

I am Matthew and I love you beloved souls and I will be with you on your journey as u seek more, as your eyes open, as your hearts are full, I be with you. God bless you beloveds.
