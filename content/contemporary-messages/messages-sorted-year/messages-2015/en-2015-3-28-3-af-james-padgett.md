# Continue to work towards these Efforts to understand the Truth of Divine Love

March 28<sup>th</sup>, 2015

New York Retreat, New York, NY, USA

James Padgett received by Al Fike


God bless you, this is James Padgett.

The vision that this instrument has had regarding this building is correct. There will be a center here that will bring the message of Divine Love to this part of the world and there will be displays of the original messages that came through my mediumship and a center for study, because although you do not see this at this time, there will be a great interest in this work and the truth of the Divine Love in years to come. And although you start out small it takes but a spark to start a mighty blaze. Continue to work towards these efforts to understand the truth of Divine Love. Read these books that were written by my hand but not by my mind. And understand the value and the purpose of these writings. Great effort was made to bring these through to the world and they cannot be lost. So, I urge you to take these writings seriously. And to bring into this place the Truth of this message of Divine Love. There is much work to do my friends and indeed you worry for the material that is required to sustain this building and I tell you as you make a commitment to this work and these Truths, God will provide what is necessary to sustain this place and as you make a commitment to the truth of God’s Love and a true understanding of what it is and make the efforts to receive this great blessing, you will draw many to this place, the hungering souls will be drawn to the light. Yes, there are many questions, but I tell you that with faith and commitment, strength within your souls, much will be accomplished and God’s Will will manifest in this place and many other places in the world and bring the Light and bring refuge to those that are weary in the world and need the succor of God’s great Love. Listen to your guidance my friends, for there is a plan and a way to bring this to reality to allow God to manifest this destiny in this place, this Light shining, this breath bringing life to this building. Continue in your prayers and your desire to bring this building alive, to draw in the lost souls, the children and the elders, and all peoples who crave within their souls to know your Heavenly Father. As long as you are open and willing you will be guided. And the doors will open for this to manifest. Walk in Love my brethren, pray for this place, pray for this world, that God will bring the comfort, the great promise of this Love to every soul, both in this world and the next and that you may find a place in this plan for the salvation of mankind.

Great changes are coming, great challenges are coming, but great work as well as the world changes so the doors of opportunity to bring greater Light and enlightenment to the world are possible. Prepare yourselves, continue in your prayers, walk the Divine Path, walk it with all your heart, all your dedication, your soul longing and allow God to work through you. And many things will manifest in your lives and in this place.

God bless you, your brother James is with you in your efforts. We continue to work together to bring the Truth of God’s Love to this world. You are all brave, dedicated, earnest, and have received the Love within your souls. You have what is required. It will not be easy, but the rewards will be great for your efforts. Listen to the voice of God and you will be guided. Thank you. Thank you one and all for your efforts, for your love. God bless you.
