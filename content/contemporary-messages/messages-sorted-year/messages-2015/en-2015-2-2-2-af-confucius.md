# Sing your Songs to God

February 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike


...I am with you, Confucius. I am with you. Beloved son (P______), yes, you have been

guided to this group, and you have the Father’s Love within your soul. Continue to seek

this Love, my son, and great vistas of understanding and joy will be yours in the days

and years to come. You are amongst your family here. Do you not feel this, my son?

A place of comfort, a place of peace and joy.

Yes, my little birds who flock together, enjoying and basking in the radiant Love of God,

continue, continue to be together in love and sing your birdsongs of joy and truth. Sing

your songs to God, and know that He hears every note, and is joyed while you are

beautiful, loving expressions to Him. Do not seek to understand the Will of God in your

minds, but come to know His Will in your souls, and the answers will be given. They are

not complex, but simple and true and shall unfold in the beauty of your Father’s care

and love for you. You cannot stray far from the light, my beloved children. My beautiful

birds, you will fly together in loving harmony, making beautiful circles in the air, bringing

light wherever you go. Be at peace, and know that you are loved, you are truly loved,
