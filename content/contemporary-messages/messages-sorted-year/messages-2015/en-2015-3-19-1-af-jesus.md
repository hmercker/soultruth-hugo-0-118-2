# Trust in your Father and His Will

March 19<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

Jesus received by Al Fike


God bless you my beloveds, I come it is Jesus.

Blessed are you who bring love into your hearts. Blessed are you who express love in all that you do in your lives and all that you are in your beings. God has His hands upon you my beloveds and you are guided and protected in your lives. Do not bring fear into your consciousness but walk in the sure knowledge that your Heavenly Father guides and protects you my beloveds.

Your father and husband is protected and blessed for you bring this condition of Love in your home and in your lives as you beseech your Heavenly Father to receive His great powerful Love which influences all about you and all around you and all within you. You are blessed. Walk with this faith and knowledge, act in the flow of your Father’s Love and guidance, be in peace and act with Love when you know within yourselves you walk in this sure knowledge and guidance.

My beloveds when you are guided in this way, when God’s Will manifests through you, it is both gentle and powerful, clear and all things are possible, when God manifest through you, my beloveds. The only thing which will inhibit this is your minds which doubt it. Allow these doubts to fall and walk this simple path, the trust in your Father and His Will. And many doors will open my beloveds, many possibilities yet dreamed of will manifest in wondrous ways. Do not worry for the material, do not worry for your own being. And when you touch those who are diseased do not worry you are protected. And when you comfort those in distress know that the comfort reaches them in a deep way and when you see the great depravation in the world, know that God will bring solutions to these dreaded conditions. Though they may seem complex and hopeless, through God’s Will all will be resolved, and though you may not see the results of your efforts in an immediate way this will manifest in time. Solutions will be found.

Remember my beloveds, you come as a beacon of light and an example for those who follow and so in this way must trust in God’s Will and what manifests through His Will in the world. And in this great faith, much will be accomplished and many souls will be saved from the darkness and the depravation in this world.

Beloveds, beloveds you do my work for my work is God’s Will. In your own inner knowing have faith that God will guide you and for all those who would detract from this and bring criticism, say a prayer for them my beloveds, love them, for they feel the power of God’s Will within you. And this brings a fear and an unknowing within them and in this lost way they will lash out at you, but they only hurt if you believe them. That is their only weapon and the negative forces of the world would have you turn away from the light. But you are securely within God’s embrace, and you walk with many angels. How can you be hurt in this way, you cannot and you will not. You will be stronger and wiser and more loving with each day that passes and when you are tested ask your Heavenly Father to guide you through to bring you what you require to be strong, to have the wisdom to make your way through these trials and tribulations in life. The wisdom within you, the perceptions within you are growing and you will see more clearly, my beloveds, for which is a mystery to you now, will soon be clear. You will know and what remaining doubts there are within you will fall away and those fears and petty anger will fall away from you my beloveds, you will know deep joy, abiding peace and great love for all humanity. This is the promise of God’s Love and it shall be fulfilled within your lifetimes my beloveds, you will walk that path Divine in great light and freedom. It is yours already. It is but for you to truly acknowledge this and go in all and every way you can and will walk in Love. So few are blessed in this way my beloveds, in this world.

My disciples, you will walk through many lands in this world bringing the message of Love. Your very presence does indeed fulfil this purpose. Your very beings are an example of Love. Raise yourselves up to this place, accept this blessing, walk in the world but not of the world my beloveds. And you shall fulfill your purpose and find your place within the flow of God’s great Will and Desire for the redemption of mankind. There is a plan, you come close in finding your place within this. Be open and receptive and the route will be short to that place of sure knowing.

I love you, you are my brothers, my sisters, and for all eternity we shall know great joy and happiness in the Celestial Kingdom together. This is your destiny for truly you are already redeemed by the Father and you shall grow ever closer to the Celestial Kingdom. Beloveds, I love you and I shall never leave you, never. God bless you. Your brother Jesus loves you, bless you.
