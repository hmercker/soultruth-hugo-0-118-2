# Do not compromise in your Thoughts and your Actions, but reach for the Highest

January 19<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike


God bless you, beloved souls. This is your teacher Augustine, and I wish to support you in

your efforts to bring those together in prayer. And as you do your travels in the world, to

bring those who desire to receive the Love in circles of prayer and circles of fellowship,

you do the Father’s work as you make these efforts, as you traverse the globe, and as

you draw those to your home. Prayer is so important, my beloveds. It is important for

you to make these efforts, to draw like-minded souls together in this way. When you

draw so much of God’s blessings into your midst with your collective prayers and you

become a channel of light in this world through these efforts. Open your souls,

beloveds, open them wide to this blessing of Love. As you beseech the Father for this

gift, you bring an influence, a light, the presence of the angels into your midst, and this

draws many spirits and mortals alike to this light. Walk together in love. Support one

another in your efforts. Reach ever for the highest. And do not compromise in your

thoughts and your actions, but reach for the highest, the highest of intentions, the

highest of desire to do the Father’s Will, to be a channel of His Love, to speak truth

wherever you may, and to emanate truth wherever you go. And in this way we, your

guides and teachers, helpers and healers, will accompany you wherever you are in this

world. And we will help you to reach out to those souls who reach to you. There is

much work ahead for you, my beloveds. Some doors will open that will surprise you,

opportunities that will come your way from unexpected sources and work will flourish for

those of you who wish to work for God. This will flourish and expand and encompass

the world, my beloveds, encompass the world, and bring change and light, comfort and

healing to many. Walk the Path Divine, surefooted, without doubts or fears, seeking

inspiration from the Heavenly Father, listening to His guidance and Will, walking in the

light and being carried by the flow of His Love. Continue, beloveds, and we shall
