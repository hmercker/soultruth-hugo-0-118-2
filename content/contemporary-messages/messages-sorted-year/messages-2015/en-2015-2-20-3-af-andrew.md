# Life’s Experiences bring Understanding, and you all grow within your Souls

February 20<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

Andrew received by Al Fike


And I am so happy to see you all together, such beautiful souls, praying and yearning to be in the Light.  And I have been with this instrument often in his prayers and I have observed his struggles and how he has overcome his fears and his biases in order to come and be a channel of love in the world, clear and strong.  And I wish to say to my friends, you too struggle to overcome those things within you that hold you back from pure and beautiful Love, God’s Love.  And when I walked the earth, my soul knew God’s Love, but my mind was not in clear understanding of its nature and purpose and I wish to convey to you my beloved friends, that this Love is the greatest gift any soul may receive. It does not reside in your soul upon your breath, this is the gift of natural love, which you receive and purify and utilize in your lives, but Divine Love is a gift from God, received in prayer and openness and desire to receive.  And it is the greatest blessing.

Oh, if I knew this truth clearly in my lifetime here, I believe my words would be different, not that I preached error, for I did as best as I could.  And I did do the Father’s work in my life and I do not regret this, but to know the Father’s Love in a clear and conscious way, the Divine Love, this is a more powerful aspect of the soul working with God to be His instrument in the world.  So, I beseech you to look into this, to ask the questions, to ask God to open your soul in this way.   All of you are here to learn, to grow and to share with one another and all of you have something to give, for life’s journeys bring wisdom.  Life’s experiences bring understanding, and you all grow within your souls.  You have light within your souls and may this Light and Love be shared amongst you and I will be with you my beloved friends, with all of you, to help you, to influence you and to pray for you.  There are many angels with you, surrounding you and making great effort to bring you to a higher level of understanding, and an openness within you, that you may see this truth and many truths with clarity.  Our desire is that you come away from this gathering renewed and refreshed, healed of the body, inspired of the soul, that you may continue your journey upon this earth with joyous and a powerful understanding within you of the Will of God.  The Will of God my friends, that small voice within that will guide you upon your journey, will bring you to places and bring souls into your path so that you may be blessed in this experience, that you may walk as a channel of Love for others.

Yes, you desire community.  Each one of you has a longing to be open and loving and spiritual with one another and I applaud this, this desire, for your souls long to express themselves openly to revel in this joyous Love together and to have the support and the strength of fellowship.  And it can and will happen, given your willingness to come together in acceptance and love.  God does not want you to be alone or to feel lonely.  And of course, God is always with you and you are never alone, but on this earth plane, often you feel separate from others and that deep desire to have this connection, to share (knock on door interrupted)   I urge you to share in love and generosity.  You have an opportunity here to express yourselves, to be who you truly are, to reveal to the others, your deep soul longings and desires, to be open, honest and true.

As the Father pours His Love upon you, drink deep, quench your thirst, be ravenous in your thirst and allow this blessing to work its miracle within your beings and give you all that you desire, for all of you have a deep thirst, a deep desire to be with God, to walk in His Light and Presence, to be in that place of communion.  God is there waiting, waiting for you, loving you.  Reach out and be with Him and He will reveal the mysteries of the universe.  He will give you all and ask only that you love Him and desire to be with Him and at-onement.

So very simple to be with Him in this innocence, in this faith, in this truthful way, your soul with His soul, your being together with His great Being.   And the angels will hold you up and help you to open your eyes.  God bless you, beloved souls.  I am Andrew and I am here to be of service, to help you upon this little journey you take together, and many are with me in support and love and prayer.  Reach out my beloveds and take the hand of God and to Pure and Holy Light.  God bless you.
