# The Power of Divine Love is greater than any other Blessing

February 19<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Homosassa Springs, FL, USA

Augustine & Seretta Kem received by Al Fike


**Augustine:** And yes, the Heavenly Father has brought you together.  This is not by happenstance, but it is by plan and intention that you are together.  And the others shall come into the circle of Light and you will all benefit by the Father’s touch within your souls, and you will have greater clarity when these days are finished together, a deeper bond of love, a greater understanding of one another.  It is indeed all preparation my dear students for what is to come and in this way, in this bond of love and in this sharing of prayer in God’s Love together, you will have a deep bond which will allow you to work together in the world.  The time comes for you to share these truths in a greater way throughout the world and the doors will open for this my beloveds as long as you walk in faith, trusting in the Father’s plan for the salvation of mankind, you will find your place within this plan, your purpose which God has imbued within each soul.   And in fulfilling this purpose in being a part of this plan and being a channel of God’s Love and Truth, you will find great and deep and wonderful fulfillment within you.  And those impediments which you seem to face today, will be gone tomorrow.

God will give you what you require to do this work.  It is a matter of being open to what is being given, to be generous with those around you and to be resolute in your knowledge of the truth, each of you desires greatly, to do this work.  It is a burning passion within your souls. It is a mission which you all acknowledge as being the primary purpose of your beings and lives at this time and as such, you have come together. The law of attraction, the laws of love, the support of the angels and the Will of God has brought you together and this will happen many times in the future.  For as you come together in deep and sincere prayer, so you build the light, you build the light amongst you and this is the initial step of many.  Do not fear the future, but welcome what comes and do not speculate on God’s plan, but listen deeply, listen deeply my children and you will hear and you will know the next steps. And as you grow in your soul’s Love, the hearing will be more acute, the knowing will be deeper and the faith will be stronger and the knowledge will come in ways that you may convey this to others who hunger, yet do not know what they hunger for.

Much to learn my beloveds.  Every day is an opportunity to learn, is a day in preparation for what is to come. Be prayerful. Be open and hungering for more.  For what awaits you is a great flow of Love and knowledge, of healing and peace and it is for you to break that dam that holds it back.  For you to truly want this with consciously, with every cell of your being, every yearning of your soul, every openness, it is within your mind to yearn and long and allow this great wave of Love to touch you, envelope you and sweep you away into a higher level of awareness, into a deeper understanding, into a great channel of Love built within you and your souls my beloveds.  Walk the path, walk the path and be at peace.  You are loved, loved deeply. And you will do the Master’s work and the doors will open, the doors will open and you will walk through with joy and peace and anticipation.  God bless you.  Augustine loves you, is ever with you.  God bless you.

My friends, it is **Seretta Kem**.

Beloved sister, you require much healing and we have been working upon your body, helping you to replenish.  There is much depletion in your energy and in the chemistry of your body.  You must take time to replenish yourself.  Be mindful of what you consume.  Be mindful of the energy expended.  Rest and prayer are so important, beloved sister.

Although you may have a handful of eager souls to pray with in the coming days, you will have a multitude of angels and spirits with you.  Remember that much of the work you do in these gatherings flows out to those in spirit who are drawn to this light, so pray, we ask you to pray for these dear spirits who are also curious and desire truth.  So much happens in ways that you are not aware of, on levels of reality that you are not aware of, by means that are not available to your understanding at this time, but as you faithfully enter into prayer and conversation with one another, much can be accomplished.  And we will guide you and inspire you and help you to come to greater clarity and soul growth.

Do not be afraid to speak the truth and to be honest with one another, for in the honesty and integrity, this loving and truthful sharing, you build bonds that will last for all eternity my beloveds.  You begin your journey here, but this journey shall never end. And the glorious wonderment that awaits you is far beyond your understanding at this time.  And your souls eagerly anticipate what is to come and God will give you what you need my beloveds.  Just a small effort from you brings a great work and unfolding of the Father’s plan.  How many angels are eager to assist you, how many spirits observe you, how many mortals feel the power of your soul’s Love.  You are never alone, and God uses you every moment to bring light into the world.  It is a constant flow, a wondrous work.

And what will be accomplished will change the world my beloveds.  And as you faithfully and humbly go about your life, ahh how little you know of what is done, the light you spread, the Love that flows, yet it is so, and one day when the scales are pealed from your eyes and the understanding of the soul is great, you will know, you will truly understand the power of this work, this flow of Love, this healing of the world.

The power of Divine Love is greater than any other blessing and it will turn disharmony and darkness into light and harmony, peace and joy, both within you and all about you.  Continue my children to pray and live as best you can in the Light, to speak the truth, to walk this world as a channel of Love and God will always be with you and the angels will be ever eager to support you upon your journey.  Precious lights, continue to do the Father’s Will and you will be blessed (?).

God bless you.  Your friend and helper, Seretta Kem, is here and is ever with you and I love you.  God bless you.
