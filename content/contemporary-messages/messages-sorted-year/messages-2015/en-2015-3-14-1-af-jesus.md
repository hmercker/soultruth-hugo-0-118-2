# Your Soul’s desire to serve God

March 14<sup>th</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Casa Piccolo, Windhoek, Namibia

Jesus received by Al Fike

Beloveds, I come.

Such great joy I feel as you speak about the desire to serve your Heavenly Father, or is this not the essence of my teachings, is this not the path Divine to serve your Heavenly Father in pure love, in the Love that He gives, in the Love that flows through you as His servants, as His beautiful creations.

Yes, such great blessings are given to you my beloveds, because your soul’s desire to serve God and to know His Love fully. Indeed, God desires for all His children to know His Love in this way and could you but imagine what this world would be like if each soul desire to serve his brothers and sisters. To give of themselves in their own and unique beautiful ways to express their gifts in light and love. To walk God’s path of Love and to express this Truth in pure and powerful ways. Indeed, you are leaders my beloveds, for you aspire to the highest and in so doing you become a beacon of Truth in the world. And though others may not recognize this or are confused, or feel the power but do not know what, from which this comes. Continue to walk your path, to express your truth in beautiful ways. And know that God acknowledges you. And this is what is important. And for you to know this my beloveds, to know how much God loves you and sees your true selves in this knowing, in this knowing you will have great joy and peace. In this living of your truth, of God’s Truth, you will do great works and life will become a joyful unfolding. And mysteries and surprises and miracles will be a part of this journey.

Follow God’s Will each day, my beloveds. My brothers and sisters, follow God’s Will each day, and you are assured to be accompanied by Angels. And His Love will flow into your souls in every increasing abundance. And this will unlock all of the truths you desire to know and will heal all the wounds that are within you and will erase all the doubt within your minds and you will change my beloveds, profoundly and beautifully. You will know your true selves within your life here on earth and when that life is complete you will know the great rapturous joy, ascending through the spheres with Light and Love.

Indeed, God lays before you a banquette with opportunities and possibilities to be His loving servant. It is but for you to open your eyes, to be willing and to affirm within your souls that you are walking the correct path and this is not difficult for you my beloveds, for the Love shines within your souls, shines brightly. All the resources that you require to do God’s work are available to you and shall continue to flow to you. God’s Will is mighty and powerful and beautiful and perfect. It is but for you to know this, to truly know this and walk in faith. It is faith that is a powerful element in the world, that comes with Love and you walk hand in hand in trusting faith, in loving acknowledgement to your Heavenly Father. Please continue to do so and you will do my work. You will live the Truths that I taught. You will be the harbinger of change in the world and it is indeed your purpose. What you are meant to do. And I am joyed, as are all the Angels, that you acknowledge this. Though you may struggle in your minds to clarify this purpose and expressions of your soul, the mere fact that you are willing to struggle in this way, indicates the power of the Love within and the power of the Truth that you carry. And you will resolve all of these inequities within yourself and walk in purity, simplicity and harmony. It is but a breath away my beloveds, so very close. And indeed when all of your doubts and fears and confusions fall away from you, the work shall truly begin and the manifestations of that work and God’s Love and Truth will turn the heads of many my beloveds. You must be the Truth and the Love in this way, to be a leader in the world, a beacon of Light. Pray for this my beloveds, pray for this miracle of complete and true healing within and there will be no doubts, nothing to distract you. You will be unassailable by those who would detract from your efforts. You will be truly within the flow of God’s Love and Will. This is great benediction of Love. It brings you to that place beyond the conditions of the world and you will be in the world but not of this world and you all give a resounding message to the beautiful souls, to all the hungering souls, to humanity who reach for God, who reach for Love. Express Love. Express who you truly are and humanity will be moved given this choice, this opportunity to live in Truth for all eternity.

My beautiful brothers and sisters, my disciples, walk this earth with your head held high knowing that you are loved, you are truly loved and beautiful and you walk in light. God bless you my beloveds, God bless you. I am Jesus and you are my beloved brothers and sisters and my disciples. Journey forth and spread this light. God bless you.
