# Bring the Truth of God’s Love to Mankind

March 21<sup>st</sup>, 2015

Tour 2015, Swakopmund, Namibia

James Padgett received by Al Fike

God bless you, it is James Padgett. I wish to thank you my daughter for making these efforts to speak the words that came through me all those years ago and to encourage others to do so. And indeed you are following the guidance that is given in this great effort to bring the Truth to mankind for this effort will come from many different directions. There will be creative ways in which they will be expressed and this will be but one way and for you to comply with this guidance and put these efforts forth is a great gift and you will be blessed as you pursue this project and you will find that many others will want to support you, for the Angels work on each one to help to bring about the completion and I would encourage you and say that it will go more swiftly than you imagine and will not be a great burden upon you. To help you all help will be available. Bless you for your efforts my dear sister Helge, and for all the efforts all of you make to bring the Truth of God’s Love to mankind. Your dedications and your love in the way that you are able to follow your guidance and you see how God puts you on the right path and the barriers fall away and the gifts are given in abundance. This is an indication that you are following God’s Will. How seemingly insurmountable situations are resolved and difficulties seem to melt away. This is how God works in the world and though at times you may feel impatient and you would like the timing of such matters to be quicker, it is important to follow the Will of God and the timing of God. For what seems like a great deal of time to you is hardly a blink of the eye for God. Have patience and perseverance as our dear friend Martin Luther indicates, that strong consistent effort, that dedication and love will see you through and allow God to work through you.  And God will always light the way. It is for you to open your eyes and see that lit path and walk along it in faith and trust, and dedication and love for your Heavenly Father. Bless you for your efforts and your friend James Padgett is with you as you express the Will of God in your lives. God bless you and keep you in His care and light. My love is with you. God bless you.
