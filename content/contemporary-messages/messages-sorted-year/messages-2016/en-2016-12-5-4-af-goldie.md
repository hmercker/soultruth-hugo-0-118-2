# Dance in the light of God’s Love

December 5<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Hawaii Retreat, Oahu, HI, USA

Goldie received by Al Fike

You are no longer asked to take baby steps. It’s time to stride forward for you are not babies anymore, you are beginning to grown up, you are beginning to recognize your own beautiful selves, to walk in the light and to let go of all those attachments to the world of man and refocus yourselves to the world of God.

Beloveds, be joyful for you are growing up and you are beautiful each one, beautiful. Blessings to you beloveds for God loves you truly and you love God truly. What more can you want but to dance in the light of God’s Love, such a gift, such a beautiful life.

Blessings to you my beloveds, Goldie loves you, Goldie loves you.
