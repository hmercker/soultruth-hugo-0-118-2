# Continue in your Service

September 4<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Augustine received by Al Fike

It is your teacher Augustine. I believe that you have learned many lessons with this gathering of souls in a country that is foreign to many of you and familiar to some of you. And so it will go, my beloved students, you will venture forth into other lands, other countries, cultures, peoples, traditions, languages, for God does not reside just with your own peoples but with all peoples in the world. And it is important for you to be amongst many different peoples in this world, to be familiar and comfortable with all of these different cultures; to walk amongst your brothers and sisters feeling like you are a part of the human race, for truly you are brothers and sisters in this world. And in all of this, love must inform your actions and your words and you each must be strong, filled with the Love, filled with the desire to serve, filled with the flow of God’s Love through you. For it is in your efforts, those of you well established upon this path and of strong faith, it is for you to encourage, to enhance, to pray deeply when you are amongst those who are hungry and have not had the experience and the growth within their souls that you have had, my beloveds.

For we will be calling you to many places in the world. There will be many such groups, circles, discussions, bonding with many people and you must be prepared, strengthened, filled with God’s grace, confident that you are being led and, guided. For to do this work, you come as missionaries, ministering to the people who are eager to learn, to experience this great blessing of God’s Love. And yes, your message is simple, your love is accepting, your actions within the grace and harmony of God’s Will and so when all of these elements align within you and around you the souls are touched powerfully. The Love of God is concentrated within the circle and the innocent and hungering souls are touched and feel this Touch and something is opened within them. But we rely upon you, beloved souls, and those likellke you who are secured in the Love, to bring about these conditions. You draw, to the group, through your prayers, through your intentions and desires and gifts these great blessings so that others may drink deep as the great well of love is filled and filled overflowing.

**Do not argue with others. Be sure of the Love. Do not express contrary opinions, but be sure in the love. Let your heart lead you.** Let God’s small voice within you inform you and be willing, my brothers and sisters, be willing to serve God, not resist, but to acquiesce to the Will of God and in this way we can use you in powerful ways, in ways that will bring great healing, that will touch many souls, that will bring truth forth. And for some of you we have prepared you for many years for these tasks and we are preparing others.

You begin to see the vastness of God’s plan. You begin to open to those whom God has caught within his net of Love and you will be a channel, a mentor, an example for those who wish to serve and are eager, have much to give and need your solid and steadfast faith and knowledge and Light within your souls.

Yes, you serve many in many different ways; when you walk down a street, though you are not aware, souls are touched. When you sit in a circle, though you do not see everything that is manifest through your prayers and your efforts together, there are many, many blessings given to many, many souls far greater in number than the souls within your circle, and when you plan and feel inspired to travel and to seek out the hungering souls it is your willingness to listen, your willingness to pick up and go where you are guided that makes God’s plan easier to enact.

There is a fullness, a richness to your actions, to your lives as you continue to serve God in love and listen with faith and act with a surety and a grace that is informed by God’s Love. There is much work ahead. This is only the beginning of a great journey of service, for the whole world hungers, my beloveds, and we do not expect you to carry the whole world but be assured that God will guide you to where you are needed. And you are not to feel burdened or worried, concerned with the details of this, merely to joyfully follow God’s guidance as he lights your path, encourages you along the way. One day at a time, one step at a time, one wondrous event, one unfolding act of God’s Love in the world, one beautiful expression of your gifts, one gaze upon another with love one moment of open souls receiving this great benediction. Your life is made of these many moments, strings of pearls beautiful to behold, beloved souls.

Continue in your service, your prayers, your desire to be close to God and your desire to serve mankind in love and God will use you, beloved souls, will take you upon a miraculous journey filled with surprises, filled with things unimagined, filled with warm hearts opening, blossoming, to the great Love of God. Continue, beloved souls, in these great efforts. You can not imagine the rewards, the blessings, what you are creating within your soul through service and love, prayer and faith. Great blessings, great blessings that will carry you far, not only in this world but the next. Beloved souls, we are pleased with your efforts, we are very pleased. We love you and will continue to guide you as you walk upon this path of service, as you are strengthened, as the channel within you grows and clears, becomes powerful and beautiful.

Your teacher Augustine loves you, will continue to be by your side guiding you and teaching you and loving you. God bless you. God bless you, beloved souls. I love you.
