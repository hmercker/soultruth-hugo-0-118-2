# Move in the World

September 13<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Serbia Retreat, Belgrade, Serbia

Goldie received by Al Fike

So, you see the healing waters did heal and then God and His joyful tears blessed you with the rain. And then you met the beautiful soul who was delighted to be with you in conversation and who was touched deeply by your presence. And so it goes as God guides you through your days to be open, to be receptive to this guidance, to be joyful as you walk along your life’s path.

So many sit and wait, thinking that God will make manifest His Will without any effort upon their part. This is not true. You must move in the world. You must make your efforts, and make them in love and when you choose love you choose to serve God. God will bring towards you as you move towards His intension there will be many blessings, experiences and opportunities to serve. But the one who sits and waits is lost. The one who engages in love and engages with God ,which together creates the many blessings that will come.

God bless you. I am so happy that you found joy in your day and today you will find more, many surprises are coming. God bless you, beloveds. I am with you, I am with you, joyfully so in this beautiful day. God bless you.
