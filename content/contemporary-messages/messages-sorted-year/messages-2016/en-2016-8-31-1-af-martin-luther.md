# Welcome to Frankfurt

August 31<sup>st</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Martin Luther received by Al Fike

Welcome, beautiful souls. I am Martin Luther, and I welcome you to the country in which I was born and I regret that I cannot speak my language through this instrument who is unprepared to do so, so we will have to make do with English.

Beloved souls, you have been called together, and I have worked with the beautiful souls who have arranged this gathering and the purpose of which is to start a new Reformation, a new understanding of what is truly a journey of the soul.

Yes, I fought for change in my country and that had far-reaching effects. And now, beloved souls, those of you who are willing, must again fight for change, must again bring the truth that you have to your respective countries, to bring a great change, a great Reformation. For this Reformation, this revolution of thought and deed based on love, the Divine Love will change the world, beloved souls, for all those who are hungering, who need from deep within themselves a great cleansing and comfort, a Touch from God so that they may know of joy, that they may open their eyes and see the truth. For so many in this world are blind, so many go about their day without even a thought towards God, without expressing love, without bringing harmony to your world. And you, beloved souls, you will bring change. You will show through your example what God means for all humanity to be, to be spiritually strong and beautiful, to be that channel of Love that is so needed in this world.

In my time my thinking was different, and my efforts were born mainly of the mind, the material mind. Although something within me motived me, something within my soul, God did indeed guide me, strengthen me, show me the way, but now you have so much more clarity. You can bypass all the intellectualism, the analysis, the step-by-step ways in which you reassure your mind of the truth.

Here, beloved souls, you will come to know, through your soul awakening, the Truth. And this is what we set before us for these days to come, to awaken the soul, to bring greater understanding from that place of soul which will influence and change your minds. It is not from the mind to the soul, beloveds, in these days ahead, it is from the soul to the mind. May that awakening happen for each one of you and those who are coming. May that beautiful flowering of awareness have coming to God in innocence, in clarity, in a singleness of desire and purpose. To do so you will bring a mighty, a mighty inflowing of Love into your souls.

Every gathering such as this has the potential to bring the Pentecost to your beings and this is a real blessing, beloveds, a mighty blessing that will change each and every one of you. Are you willing to focus your true hearts upon this great benefit and blessing of God’s Essence? If you are, if you have a sincere longing and you push aside those elements within your minds which cause resistance and questioning. If you are willing to take this great leap of faith, then great blessings will come, for it is the longing of the soul, the focusing of the soul towards God that will bring all that you require.

I was a man of rules, of intellect, but I was also a man who desired that the world change for the better, that all the dogmas and the heaviness of those who would control the seeking souls would disappear and that we would all have freedom to worship God in our own way and this, beloved souls, is still my desire. That you all worship the Heavenly Father in your own ways, in your own words and with a great longing from your soul and in this way, you will turn that key, the door will open and you will enter into a world that is filled with Grace, the presence of God and your soul will sing in joy and come to know wondrous things.  And as you reach out to your Heavenly Father in true prayer, prayer that is not necessarily of words, but it is and must be your deep desire for connection and love, for healing and comfort, you may come to know this great joy.

So, my fellow seekers of truth, my Reformers, shall we enter into this place, seeking Love, Light, healing, joy, and doing so without trepidation or ambivalence but to do so with innocence and curiosity and a deep desire?

Thank you for listening to me and thank you for allowing me to open this circle. Many will come to speak. Much is to be conveyed, and we will together bring ourselves up far beyond the human condition into a place of soulful, joyful dancing with God.

God bless you, beloveds. I am Martin Luther and will be with you as will many angels in these days to come.
