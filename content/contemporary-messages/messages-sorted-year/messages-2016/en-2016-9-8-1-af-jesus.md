# You are my Disciple here

September 8<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Serbia Retreat, Belgrade, Serbia

Jesus received by Al Fike

The hearts that mend in this country are burdened, my beloved brother. They are burdened with such pain and grief, anger and sorrow. They are lost and I have sent you, my brother, to be my disciple of Truth in this land so that you may be used to unburden the lost souls and bring hope to many. Yes, beloved, beloved brother, you will be my disciple in this place. You will walk this land in truth and you must be strong. Put aside your doubts; walk in faith.

You have many gifts which you do not yet understand or realize. You must allow God to guide you and I have sent my beloved brother and sister to support you and to show you the way in this work, and the angels will surround you as you continue in your prayers and service in this land. Although your numbers are few, the power of God’s blessing is great. You must allow this flow to come uninhibited and powerfully.

There is so much healing and work to be done here, in this part of the world, beset by strife for so long, so much confusion, so much anger, so much needs to be healed. You have a great task ahead of you, my son. You must unburden yourself and be strong. You are worthy of this task. God has given you wonderful gifts and you recognize the great power of His Love and you will carry this torch to dark places and speak in your humble words and be an example of Love. Your calling begins, my son. God beckons you to work as His channel of Love. Do not turn away, but face fully into the Light and make this promise within your soul, that resides within your soul *"Father I am your child of Truth and Love, Father I will serve you in love, Father show me the way"* and in this simple prayer you will be guided and you will know, and as you step forward, one step leading to another, there will be an awakening within you, a strengthening, and a cleansing that will lead to many more opportunities to serve as you unburden yourself, my son, as you are clear on the Truth, as you stand up in Truth.

Every soul must face this choice: Shall I be with God and come to know His Will and act upon this knowledge or Shall I join the human condition and turn away? You have chosen to turn towards God, my son. You have prayed for this. You have prayed for support and help, and you have received an answer to your prayers and you will continue to receive this support from the angels as God puts His Hand upon you. Allow the old thinking to fall away and be receptive to God’s Will and Truth in your life.

You will come to know a great sense of freedom and joy in this work and others will join you and I will lead the way, beloved son. I will lead the way. Follow me. I am Jesus and I love you. You are my disciple; you are all my disciples of Truth. May God bless every footfall you make in this world and bring sweet peace as you walk in the darkened conditions of this world.
