# You are cradled in His Care

September 7<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Confucius received by Al Fike

You are wanted and loved, precious children. God has you in His Hands, cradling you with His care and protection, the power of His Love, infusing every part of your being, beloved souls. You are cradled in the Hands of God. Allow His sweet Love and care to infuse you, to carry you, to guide you, for He wishes to heal every part of your being, every crevice of your soul. He wishes to heal you and make you whole, and filled with joy and wisdom and deep perceptions that will bring you into a new world, a world of God’s creation, a world that is revealed in all its glory, that your eyes be opened, that your heart be full and soul resonating with the Love, beloveds. In this way, God will carry you forward in that mighty river that will take you upon your destined paths.

It is so simple, beloved souls, so very simple. Do not clutter your minds with speculation and doubt and fear, for you truly do not wish to be a part of the human condition, you wish to be a part of this reality of God. Your souls long for this. Do not let the conditions of this world distract you from this deep longing and tremendous transformation within, for you are changing, beloved souls. The Truth of God’s Love is change and renewal, healing and opening. You must allow this higher Truth and reality to be your truth and reality. Feel the powerful pull of God, His great soul drawing you close, bringing you all that you require to be whole, and beautiful, and Light, and Love.

For every breath you take there will be a blessing, not just for you but for this world. Every step that you take, you will touch another soul. Every thought that you have, it will be of love. Every embrace you give will be a healing and a comfort for another. And you will think not for yourselves but for the salvation of mankind, and this will carry you to many places to bring God’s Truth and His Love, for you will carry this Light and many souls will feel the power and beauty of this Light and they will be touched, for God will use you in many beautiful ways. Your gifts just begin to open, they flower in this Love and when they are fully in bloom you will carry a great message and confirmation of the Truth to many souls, beloveds, and the souls await your blooming, opening, sharing, confirming the Truth.

Dedicate yourselves to prayer. Be with God and you will find your great purpose. Confucius loves you, brings you a blessing. God bless you.
