# I am your Brother

September 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Jesus received by Al Fike

And I walk amongst you, beloved souls. I walk amongst you and every soul who longs for the Father’s Love I will walk with you, in my love. Yes, I have returned to Earth and I have done so many, many times to help educate and influence the souls of the Earth to the truth of the Father’s Love. And every soul open to this great blessing of the Divine Love will receive this gift, for in their longings, in their great desire to be with God in love, to be with God directly, they will be blessed; they will be blessed.

And I was never God incarnate. No, my beloveds, I was a man who discovered the truth of God’s Love and in my discovery opened the doors to its existence amongst mankind and all spirits. Beloved souls, do not worship me in any way for I am not God. I am your teacher. I am with you in my love for you but I am with God as you are with God in your love for God. And this is a great truth; that it is God you must worship; it is God you must reveal your heart and your souls to and it is God that will bless you in your efforts and your prayers and your vulnerability and your aching heart. You will be touched in powerful ways and you will manifest in powerful ways God’s Love and this will influence and bring Light to many, to be a channel of Love, to be as I was upon this Earth, as God touches you in this way.

I am your brother. I am your brother and I will walk with you as you seek truth, as you seek God, as you are, as a child of God, I am your brother. God bless you, beloved souls, and may you continue to long for truth and be open to this great blessing of His Love and all will be revealed within your souls, beloveds, within your souls, you will know. You will come to know in complete and utter surety that this is the truth. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you and I love you. God bless you.
