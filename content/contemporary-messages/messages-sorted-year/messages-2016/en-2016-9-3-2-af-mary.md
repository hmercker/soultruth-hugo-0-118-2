# Love brings Resolution to Disturbances

September 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Mary received by Al Fike

When the daughter is creating a disturbance you must pray for her and send her your love. Put your love around her and ask for the cloak of protection to surround her and those who are close to her, that this disturbance may be quelled. Confronting her will not create the effect that you wish but will entrench her in her belief, but if you are channels of love, acceptance, humility and prayer you will assist us in keeping the conditions at a higher level.

Judgement does not bring resolution, love always does. Assist the daughter in love for she knows not the conditions that she is delving into and is very vulnerable to all influences and it is for you, beloved souls, and those who are sincere in love to move to protect, envelope, and acquiesce theher troubled heart. Send her your love, send her your prayers but be strong in this. God bless you, I am with you. Many angels surround you.

There will ways be challenges, beloved souls, as you reach out to many in the world, but there are many beliefs, conditions, levels of influence in this world and the highest road for each one of you, beloved souls, is to reach for God and be a channel of Love. Yes, I realize that you wish for each soul who gathers together in prayer to be in harmony, to seek the true Love of God and this is our desire as well, beloved souls, but it is not always possible in this world to be in this place of common intention and soul desire.

You must be the examples. You must walk with your head high, not engaging in judgement or concern but trusting in God’s Will, that His Will, His Touch, His influence, will save the day and bring about what is meant to be. Always reach for the highest, beloveds. Always know that God will repair that which is out of harmony when prayers are said and love is expressed in faith and in the truth. God bless you, beloved souls. Continue with your efforts with these beautiful souls seeking the truth. Express your truth clearly, be loving at all times and allow God to heal whatever is not in harmony.

God bless you. God bless you, I am Mary and I love you.
