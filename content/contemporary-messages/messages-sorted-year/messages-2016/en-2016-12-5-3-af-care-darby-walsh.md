# I do appreciate the Prayers

December 5<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Hawaii Retreat, Oahu, HI, USA

Care Darby Walsh received by Al Fike

And I do appreciate the Prayers and I appreciate all of you who are roses in God’s great beautiful garden and I’m with you often, I’m with you often.

Continue in your efforts and prayers. Continue to love one another. It is beautiful to behold. If you could see your own lights, my dear friends, I think you would be surprised how bright and beautiful they are and how you draw many spirits to you as you gather in your prayers and in this way to acknowledge God and His Love, to bring your joyful spirits together and to love each other. It is beautiful to behold and I am so happy to see this, I am so happy for my dear, beloved Jim whom I am close to always and for all of you who try to bring light to this world.

Beloved souls, God bless you and keep you, God bless you and keep you. God bless you.
