# Pray daily

July 11<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Online Circle of Light, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Once again, my little birds have gathered and oh how you love to chatter together, to share your lives with one another, and now you come into the peace and silence of God’s presence, opening your souls and releasing all those thoughts of your minds to be with God in communion, to feel that deep and peaceful presence of the Creator. My little birds, you must take time each day to replenish your souls, to be in this silence and Light, to put aside all those conditions and thoughts and concerns of your daily life and be with God, for without this Divine Touch bringing to you the message of Love, peace, and the equilibrium that God’s Touch does indeed bring, you become lost in the human condition, in the ebb and flow of all the disparate energies of this world and the disparate thoughts of your own minds, and you do not feel the harmony and the peace that is meant for each soul in this world given freely by God. But you, my beloveds, must take the time, make the effort, say the prayers, sit in silence to receive God’s answers to the longings of your souls, and you must allow the longings of your souls to reach God. Beloveds, it is your souls that will become eternal with the touch of this Love. It is your souls that will create the great foundation of your lives and that which you entertain within your minds, my beloveds, is transient and changeable, but that which you absorb within your souls and the Truth and understanding that comes with this experience with God will remain forever. Remember this, my beloveds. It is the effort to expand and develop your souls that will bring you deep and abiding joy and peace, truth and love, and this is what you must concentrate on for at least one portion of your day – to be with God, to intensify the Light around you, to beseech God’s guidance, to draw His angels with you, to walk in this Light, for when you do so you draw many of God’s blessings within your day, within your being and you encourage and widen the channel of Love within you so that God may use you as His instruments, and channels of Love and healing, wisdom and comfort.

This is so very simple, my beloveds, so very simple. And yet it eludes many, for the conditions of this world are intense and often greatly distracting, and you get lost in these energies, the machinations of life. You must be disciplined, my beloveds. You must take this time daily. You must realize that the greatest nurturing you can do for yourselves is in this way, that all else is subordinate to this effort in prayer and connection with the Creator, and with this all else will come into harmony and will flow in harmony in your lives. And I know for some of you it is difficult to put aside the concerns of your minds, the thoughts and waves of emotions and reactions, judgements and assumptions as you interact with others, as you struggle to bring harmony to your lives, as you encounter the various conditions of this world.

It is difficult to place the priority of God first, but it is necessary, my beloveds, if you are to truly find what it is that your souls seek. Your souls seek your Heavenly Father. Your beings, all that is you, scatter in many different directions. You live different lives. You seek different things. You place within your mind many different ideas and concepts, and in many ways you bring your beings out of harmony through all of these pursuits and distractions. And to truly follow this Path you must put God before all else. You must allow your souls to be expressed fully within your lives, my beloveds, and this can only happen if you are earnest and prayerful to receive God’s essence that ignites your souls, that brings the awakening and the true understandings that will be your treasure and heritage that shall last for all of your days here in this world and in the next.

My beloveds, seek God. Seek God and be with God and you will be a beacon of Light in this world and God will direct you in many ways and will draw many souls to you that you may be His instrument of peace and Love and healing for others. Each one of you has a great capacity and a great desire to do this in your own unique ways, your own unique channels of Love. Seek to know your purpose, beloveds, and you will find great joy and fulfillment in your lives.

Confucius loves you and I am with you often, my beloveds, as you struggle with the human condition, as you pray to be risen above this condition, as you struggle to express your true selves and love in this world. Yes, I am with you, as are many angels, encouraging and protecting, inspiring and showing you the way to true enlightenment. God bless you. God bless you, beloved souls. Confucius loves you.
