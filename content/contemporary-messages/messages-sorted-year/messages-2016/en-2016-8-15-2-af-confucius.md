# Every day can be filled with Light

August 15<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Online Circle of Light, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

How you love your sunny days, do you not? And what a beautiful day it is as you come together in love and joy. Every day can be filled with Light, my beloveds, every day. When you are with God and you feel the joy within your souls as God has implanted within you his great Love, bringing your souls to a place of awakening and acknowledging the true purpose of life, the true power of love, the true touch of joy.

Yes, we will be with you this day. And God has placed his hand upon all of you this day. And each day that passes you will awaken a little further within you, your souls will grow a little more and brighten a little more as you pray for this blessing of Love that comes with your faith and trust in God's Touch, beloved souls.

Feel the embrace of God, his deep and abiding peace, His glorious Love lifting you into that place of communion and recognition to truly know your souls, my beloveds, to truly acknowledge where you are meant to go and be, which is to at-onement with God in His embrace. The embrace and the joy and the journey that lasts for all eternity, my beloveds, is yours for the asking.
