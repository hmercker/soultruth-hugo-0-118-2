# Be open to the Love of God

September 1<sup>st</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come, I am Jesus. Beloveds, I want you to come to know me for I am your brother and I come to show you the way to the enlightenment of your souls. I come to light the way so that you may realize those deep longings within you, to be in the Light, to know deep love, to be in harmony within. Cast aside your doubts, beloved brothers and sisters, and be open to what God will give you as you set forth your longings to the Creator.

You are loved, each one a beautiful soul, each one is worthy of God’s Touch of Love. Each one who allows this deep touch within them will begin a journey to at-onement with God. The only obstacle in your way, beloved souls, is yourselves. For within your minds many of you have a barrier caused by many conditions in this world, many things that were told to you, many betrayals by others. Even your country struggles beyond those conditions created long ago.

Forgiveness, beloved souls, will open the door, forgiveness of yourselves, of all those around you and in place you must put love. Love, my beloveds, is the key to healing. Love will open the door to God and God’s Love will come rushing in like a mighty river bringing you to that place of enlightenment, understanding, and peace.

You all long for this, beloveds. You all seek it and I will lead you as will many angels upon this path. Be humble. Be innocent. Ask; ask God to show you and you shall be shown and your eyes will be open and Love, glorious Love will be yours.

God bless you. I will be with you upon this short journey together. I will be with you as you come to reconcile the truth within yourselves and come to know the great power and glory and wonderment which is God’s Love for you, beloved souls.

God bless you. God bless you.
