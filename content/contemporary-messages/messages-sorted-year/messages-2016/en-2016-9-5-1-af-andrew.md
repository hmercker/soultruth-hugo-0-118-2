# Be secure upon the Path

September 5<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Andrew received by Al Fike

Beloved souls, I am Andrew. The steps upon your path are many, my beloveds, many increments of understanding, of receiving the Father’s Love within your souls, of opening your awareness to the spiritual truth. And each of you must be patient in this journey, trusting that God will guide you upon this path, that these awarenesses and shifts within you, releasing all that is not in harmony with the Love, growing, expanding, feeling the joy that wells up from the soul, relating to all your brothers and sisters in loving ways and allowing that healing condition to fall away from you so that in time your lives are governed by your soul and your soul is governed by God.

This is not an easy path, not an easy road, for everywhere you turn are many distractions and messages which encourage you to not follow these simple ways of Love and faith and trusting God. And some fall away because they are not strong enough. Others excel because within them is a deep trust and, faith in God. But most waver from one to the other; the mind and soul, the mind and the soul, trying in their own way to make sense of the truth, to enact the truth in their lives, struggling as they will for Light. God will use the instruments that are secure upon this path and allow the soul to take precedence within their lives. Yes, he will use you when you are strong enough to walk in this way. When you cast aside your doubts and your fears, when you relinquish your willfulness to God, when you are solidly in the Light of His Love you will be used in wondrous ways. And this is not to say that God does not use those who waver somewhat, for you are human and we do not expect perfection, but the more that you can travel within this truth expressing the truth within your soul, having this Love, this blessing infuse everything that you do, then, my beloveds, you will be carried in a great river, a purposeful, meaningful, wonderful, and loving flow that shall bring you to places, experiences, opportunities unimagined by the mind.

This takes a great faith, a great commitment, a willingness to listen and, a humility.; Ssuch strength comes from such faith, such joy comes from such Love. And so there are few upon this Earth who knowhave the knowledge that you have and fewer still who are willing to act upon the truth that they know. But it is a wonderful beginning for the transformation of this world from a place governed by man to a place governed by God. This is the goal, that all your brothers and all your sisters may listen to God, may express love in everything that they do, might have unshakeable faith, and are true to the laws and the conditions which God has set out for mankind, for all creation.

It is a noble ambition that this be the reality of all humanity and I’m sure within your minds you do not see this as a possibility but then again you do not see as God sees, and you do not love as God loves. Your vision is limited. You are restricted in many ways, but I tell you God has a plan. God has a plan for the salvation of mankind and each of you, if you are willing, if you lay down your own ambitions, if you are humble and you listen and you love in the way that is prescribed, will play your part in this wondrous plan and the world will be in the Light. The world will be in the Light.

Beloved souls, God needs you, the world needs you to carry this truth, this flame in purity and strength and faith. God needs you to be fully in this Love and this truth of his love. He beckons you forth, the master has his hands upon you and all of us within the Celestial Kingdom will put our efforts forth to support you, for you are all precious, you are all precious. And there are others who are coming, who will join you in this great task. Prepare yourselves, beloved souls, for what is to come. There is much work that awaits willing hands, hands guided by God. Pray and pray again. Make this your life’s effort, to get ever closer to God so that you may understand, that you may see and you may appreciate the opportunities that are set before you, beloved souls. You are loved, each one loved, each one touched by God within their souls. Each one has a purpose, a role to play that will bring deep, deep, meaning to your lives and fulfillment and joy. Walk this road, beloved souls. Walk this road with God. Seek ye the kingdom that is His Love and all else will come in the timing of God’s wisdom and Will. All will come to you.

You a’re loved. You are guided, and you will, you will serve God in wondrous ways. God bless you, beloved souls. I am Andrew and I love you, precious, beautiful souls. I love you. God bless you.
