# God has lit a Spark within you

September 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Confucius received by Al Fike

It is Confucius. God has lit a spark within each soul in this circle, a spark that will change you, beloved souls, and will inspire you upon a different path in your lives, will bring you to certain awarenesses and experiences that may not otherwise be a part of your life. The power of a soul that is blessed in this way will bring great change, will motivate each one to walk a path imbued with Light and that which is not in harmony with God’s Love, with this spark of Light will fall away from you, beloved souls. Do not resist this change within you, that which compels you to move forward. Listen to the desire of your soul. Listen to that truth that beats within you, each one of you, and be compelled to act upon that which is within you, to walk a path lit by God, guided by God and in so doing you contribute to the change that is required in this world. For each of you must change, each soul upon this planet must be ignited into change to release the dark conditions that beset humanity. You must become a Light. You must allow the flame which God has placed within you to burn bright, to seek God’s repeated Touch of His Love within you that will fuel the great revolution within your soul, the transformation of your being, which will in turn change your thinking and your doing, which will in turn heal your wounds and your errors.

Change always starts from within and it is the power and the passion, the great desire that wells up within you, beloved souls, that will motivate you to seek God, to seek truth, to seek to love your fellow man. And as you struggle forth beating back the darkness of error and those negative conditions which beset all of humanity you will be the change required, you will do God’s Will in this respect: that you will live by example, by your own being live the truth and that expression will be unique for each individual. For each soul is unique and the path that they walk is unique.

Do not judge another’s path but walk firmly upon your own, knowing that God will guide you forth, show you the way and bring to you what is required. That you may grow within your souls and that that Light will expand within your beings and that this Light will break through and break forth in wondrous acts of Love.

God desires to build a channel of Love within each soul in this world so that His Love may flow forth and bring Light and healing, comfort and joy to those around you, my beloved souls. Again this creates the change that is required. Some of you have much to think about, to digest from this time of prayer and teaching and I tell you bring your questions to God in prayer and the answers will come, the dilemmas will dissolve and you will know the freedom of flight, to soar in the air, experiencing the joy of our beloved Father’s Love, releasing the great burdens of this Earth and being in the sun, beating down it’s Love, warming you and bringing joy within. Beloved souls, I’m Confucius and I bless you in your efforts and your struggles and your prayers. God bless you. God bless you.
