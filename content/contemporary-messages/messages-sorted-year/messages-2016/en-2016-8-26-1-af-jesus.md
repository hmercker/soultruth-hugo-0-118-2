# You are part of God’s Salvation for Mankind

August 26<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Blackpool, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

Dear souls, I come and I am Jesus. The heavens open for you, my beloveds, and God pours his Light, his Love upon you. Open your souls, beloved, beautiful brothers and sisters to this great benediction pouring upon you at this very moment. You have come at my call, and you shall come together in many places in the world to bring these great portals of Light into the world.

For God needs a circle of dedicated souls in prayer, beseeching the Heavenly Father to bring this Gift of Light. And as you do your work together opening up this wondrous Light, this Gift from God, you will come to know the purpose of these blessings, the power of this Gift and you will bring with you wherever you go as part of this Gift and blessing. It will be a part of your ministry and a channel of Love Divine in the world.

God has a plan for the salvation of mankind: it comes with Love, it comes with healing, it comes as balance is restored to this place, this Earth. And each soul here who so desires to be a part of this plan may play a role, may contribute to the unfolding of God's Will in the world. We in the Celestial Heavens are poised and work diligently to bring the healing forward to this world, so in need, to disperse the clouds of darkness, to neutralize the human condition, to bring love, to join all peoples together in peace, to allow acceptance, tolerance, where there is no judgement and fear, but joy and acknowledgement of how every soul in this world is unique, a child of God, embraced in love.

You must see the world in this way, my beloved souls. Look through the eyes of your souls and recognize this truth that you are all loved and those who are blind to this, those who shrink away from the Light, you must pray for, my beloveds, for all who are in darkness, and alone, and afraid, in pain, steeped in error. You must pray and be that channel of Light that will beam upon every soul, that will bring a blessing to every soul as God works through you, my beloveds, in the world.

Continue in your prayers, your efforts, acknowledge that deep longing within your souls always and come to God. For all that you need, all that will come into your life, flooding in abundance, will come in prayer, will come as you grow in the Love, will come as you grow to love God ever more deeply, beloved souls.

Your Light shines. You walk in humility. You are learning to be patient. You are coming to see the truth with more clarity. You see now that all done in love will bring healing and peace and you are poised upon a threshold that will be a new phase in your work, in these efforts, in this great divine plan. Walk ever closer to God. Listen ever more intently to your guidance. Be free of your fears. Be free of that human condition which is within all but cannot exist with the Love. You will find your way. You will know great joy and freedom, great knowledge of truth, and you will be a mighty channel of Love and blessings in this world. And you will come together in ways and means that may seem impossible to you at this time but will be a reality, a great blessing from God as he continues to use you and bring you together in groupings for specific purposes in the world. And you must listen to your guidance, to the upwellings from your souls so that you will understand and know what it is you are to do in service to God, in service to mankind.

Be at peace, my beloveds, and know that you are enveloped by God, touched by God, and you shall be carried by God on a wave of Love. Beloved souls, follow me, be my disciples, walk in this Light and you shall know the glory that comes in this service, in this path that is love. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you.
