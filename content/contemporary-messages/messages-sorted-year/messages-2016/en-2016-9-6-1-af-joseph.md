# Be with God

September 6<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Germany Retreat, Frankfurt, Germany

Joseph received by Al Fike

Bless you, beloved souls. I am Joseph, the father of Jesus, and I am very pleased that you have come together in prayer. Is this not a testimony to the power of God’s Will, that you come together in this way from various parts of the world? Your souls are drawn and. God beckons you into this relationship of prayer and, of love. And tThere is a deep purpose to this, these efforts that you all make to be together, for when you come together in this way you create a Light that not only benefits yourselves but benefits your families, benefits the many souls that are connected to you and in some ways benefits this world.

Answer to the callings of your souls, beloveds, for within that call, that desire, that awareness is the answer to all your questions. It’s the beginnings of a great spiritual journey that will bring to you many experiences with deep, deep, love and healing and many souls will be drawn just as you were drawn to one another and these souls will be drawn to you, my beloveds. And this is how the Earth will change. This is how humanity will change. One soul saying *"I am with You beloved Father; I am with You in Your Love. I will walk with You, I will talk with You, I will listen to Your response, I will be open to the gifts You have to give me, Your Love to flow towards me, Your guidance showing me the way"* and then you walk in Light, you become that example and others will notice.

For each of you in your own way have great gifts and a great Light. Do not turn away from God’s Will, but turn towards the beckoning Light, the Light that will bring great change and healing, peace, and Love. There is no other source. There is no other way for you, my beloveds, to walk this road, this journey that within each of your souls is compelling, within each of your souls is a deep and great desire to walk this way of Light. And each step that you take will bring further awakening and will bring about further events, responses, and experiences, that will help you to grow in your soul, will show you the truth as your eyes slowly open and you move closer to God.

And those things that seem a dilemma today will not be so tomorrow. Those fears that you may harbour within you will fall away and the healing will flow towards you, beloved souls, so that you will know a great sense of physical and spiritual strength within you, that you may walk this world as a channel of Love and Light, Truth and comfort for others. That Light grows within each one of you. Nurture it with prayer. Use the truth that you know and you are sure of to strengthen your faith and the Light within your being, with your sincere prayers, with your reaching to God, for there is no other source that will bring the healing and the answers to mankind. Too long humanity has turned away from God and in this turning away they have done themselves and this world great damage. The answers will come in prayer. The answers will come in inspiration. The answers will come as the breath of God blows upon each one of you and clears away the error and the mysteries so that you will truly know, so that you may truly share with others your wisdom. The understandings that God has placed within your souls will emerge into your consciousness and be your guiding grace, your strength, and clarity and all will be informed by Love, all will be infused with Love, all will be an expression of Love.

Is there any higher calling, my beloveds? Is there anything worth pursuing that is greater than this? For this brings true happiness, liberation from the shackles of the human condition, truth and knowledge beyond your present understanding, an awareness of God that will be central in your consciousness and powerful wherever you go. What other riches can a man possess that are greater than this? Pursue your Heavenly Father in prayer, beseeching Him to place His Love within your soul and you will be a very wealthy, wealthy soul in this world. Riches that you can take wherever you go and when your time to enter spirit comes you will walk in great Light, you will know great joy and you will see that your choice and path was right and righteous.

Beloved souls, be with God and all else will follow. Be with God. God bless you, I am Joseph.
