# Put aside any Resistance and Doubt of this great Gift

February 15<sup>th</sup>, 2016

Prayer Cicle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, my little birds, This is Confucius and I see you alight again in this little circle of Light. You come for your nourishment to be together in this prayer for Love.

Open wide your souls, my beloveds, open wide your souls for you must put aside any resistance and doubt of this great Gift, which you must beseech your Heavenly Father to receive. Feel the longings within your souls, and on this winter’s night, my beloveds, do you not feel the warmth of the Father’s Love flowing into your souls kindling that fire, that warmth, that glow of Light within you? For the Father warms you, embraces you and lifts you up into that place of warmth and sunshine, Light and openness that you may feel His Loving Touch, His powerful presence within your consciouses.

Beloved souls, put aside your concerns, lay bare your soul, my beloveds, for the Father may Touch you deeply within bringing the healing and the comfort, healing the wounds, bringing wisdom and Light, showing you the way to at-onement with Him, at-onement, my beloveds, that great embrace by the Heavenly Father, it shall bring you ecstatic joy and deep and abiding peace. It shall grow your souls that you may be a fountain of wisdom and Light, Love and joy. Be unified in this desire, my beloveds, do not hesitate be together in Love, beloved souls, God is with you. The angels are with you in your efforts to seek Light, beloved souls, God bless you. Confucius loves you, Confucius loves you.
