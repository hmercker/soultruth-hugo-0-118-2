# Becoming Love Through Divine Love

September 12<sup>th</sup>, 2025

Online Circle of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Lotus Blossom received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you, beloved souls. I am Lotus Blossom. What a delight it is to come and be with you in this way where I can use this instrument to share my love, my blessings, my encouragement and inspiration as you open your souls and pray for God's Love. Let yourself receive a mighty blessing, an abundant inflowing to continue the process of change.

I can see the light in your souls, beloveds. I can sense the desires that sit within each one of your souls, longing and yearning not only to receive God's Love, but to be a demonstration of this Love, to truly be that example for your brothers and sisters, so that it ignites a spark within their souls, great curiosity and wonderment to ask what it is, this light that they see in you. This gives you an opportunity to tell the truth, to let them know how you receive this brightness within you that they can notice and sense. You can share with them the simple truth that what they sense about you is also available for them, when all they need to do is, with the sincerity of heart, ask God to bless them with His Love and that will start them on the journey of transformation. Your souls long to be a beacon of light and truth at all times. As you grow in this process of awakening, those burdens that hold you back are being transformed and healed. As you undergo this process, you develop a strong heart, a heart to be open, a heart to be courageous, one that is able to stand in the midst of darkness and not hide, one that is a voice for truth.

Allow yourself. Allow your heart to strengthen in its ability to remain open no matter what comes before you. Acknowledge that you are love, and love is not indecisive. Love is steadfast. Love is constant. Love requires no conditions to be perfect in order to be an expression of love. Love purely is for the sake of being love. My dear friends, you are all growing in your development to be a pure channel of Divine Love. It is not necessarily that you must ensure that your words are perfect. No, what will determine your ability to be a soul of love is the Essence of God that lives in you, the transformation of your nature and your substance. For in this way, you too can only act and be love. Love is who you are. Love is who you be. Love is your demonstration.

You are changing into this. You are becoming. You are becoming love in all manner, in all ways, within every substance of your being. The transformation is making this so. The workings of God's Love upon your soul are making it so. As you grow in this, be courageous of heart to face those things that are yet to be transformed by this Love. Invite God into this part of you, asking for His blessing of Divine Love, His very Substance, to come into this place within you.

As you do this, beloveds, as you move through this process of recognising what yet God is to transform within you, it becomes easier and inspirational, because within your mind, you carry a remembrance of other times that you have moved through the healing of an encrustation. You know well, perhaps, it is quite uncomfortable to feel that which is ready to be healed, but you come to know that in order for it to be healed, there must be a recognition. You must allow yourself, from the mind's perspective, to see what is ready to be healed and to embrace that part of you, that condition, because you know that it is up for transformation. As this encrustation is released and transformed, many of you have had the experience of what that is like. There is great relief, deep, deep gratitude to God for the transformation of a condition that once held you back. Now in that place of your soul is greater compassion, greater love, greater joy, truth, wisdom and so much more that comes with the Substance of God into your souls.

Be courageous of heart, dear ones. Be gentle and kind to yourself, but also be loving. Love is the same as truth, to love yourself, to be honest and truthful with what you are going through. Do not hide it away and turn away from what you are seeing. Look straight into it and bring love to this condition. In truth, all things of fear and worry, concern or doubt are seeking love. All things of darkness, in truth, are seeking love. So let love be the way in which you guide your life and live your life. How are you letting God love you today? How are you letting love guide your way?

May you be blessed as you pray for God's Love to come in, shower you, change you and cleanse you with His Living Waters. I am Lotus Blossom. Such a joy to be with you, to pray with you, to help to guide you and, most of all, to love you. God bless you.
