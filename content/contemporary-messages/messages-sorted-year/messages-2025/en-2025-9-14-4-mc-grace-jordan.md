# Ask God to Establish the Consciousness of Love in Your Mind

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2025

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Grace Jordan received by Maureen Cardoso

Let us rise up into love, into the realms of light. I join you. I am Grace, Grace Jordan. This is a circle that is special to my heart, for it comes from the area of your world where I resided, that I served in and that I did my best to allow God to make His mark of Love through my life. That, dear friends, is the desire that each one of you has within your souls, within your hearts and even your mind, though your mind can create challenges for you through distractions. When it comes into focus and it remembers where it is to align, it serves you well. It shall serve your purpose that is within your soul, to bring formation to the manifestation in the way God desires to move through you.

How is it that you can ensure, as you go about your lives that are busy on the Earth plane and do require you to attend to material matters, that you keep the awareness of God, of service, the Path of Love, in your consciousness? Of course, it requires your discipline and your diligence, but mostly it requires your desire, a desire to be able to hold within your consciousness the Presence of God's Essence, of God's Love, of God's purpose. You may ask, ask God for that help, that rather than the worries or the fears to ruminate in the backgrounds of your mind, that it be replaced with Love, that not only in your souls may there be the consciousness of Love, but the rumination that is in your material consciousness be a circuitous cycle of Love, the possibilities, the potentialities, the wonderment.

As you move through the world, there will grow in strength an awareness of God moving with you through all material tasks, that there is an awareness that your thoughts are resting on the highest and that the way you speak is speaking for the highest. The way you perform your physical tasks, it is the highest Essence of Love that is the force behind all that you do. Ask, beloved souls. Ask to be helped, to have a mind that is able to be still in such a condition, a mind that is insistent on love being its dominant foundational thoughtform and vibration. In this way, you will grow in your ability for the soul to be in ascendancy, because your mind knows where to find the link of truth. It is within its soul. Not only that, as you ask for this gift of your spiritual condition, your thoughts to be of the highest, we, your angels, your guides and helpers, can come and support you, individually and collectively, by influencing you in this way.

You must ask, for we are not able to influence you with that which you are not willing and ready and prepared to accept. Would it not be an absolute delight to be able to have your material thoughts on the concept of love, being able to trust in your process of thought that is stable in love, knowing that the mind and the soul and your entire experience is love and of love? I am here to suggest to you, in your sincere prayer, in your sincere willingness and desire, to ask God to bless you in this way, that whatever it is that needs to occur in order that old ways of thinking and doing and processing, once and for all, can be released and replaced by thoughts, desires and words that are in harmony, harmony with God's Law of Love.

Beloved souls, this I bring to you as an offering. For God is willing to grant you this, to grant His children a mind that is filled with His Love, with a mind that is of light, a mind that sees potential possibilities throughout their everyday occurrences, that nothing that comes before them can break down the light in them. If you truly desire for this to be your way, your new way of existing, ask and you shall receive this gift and this blessing. Once you ask, know that you shall receive it, but also that you must ensure that you enact your gift and bring strength to it, for this is the way gifts strengthen in their abilities. You must use them. Know that this gift shall be given. It is for you to exercise and explore the wonderment of your gift.

I am Grace. My love, my warmth, my blessings and support are for you all. God bless you.
