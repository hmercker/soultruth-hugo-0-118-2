# Having Reverence for God’s Gift of the Body

August 24<sup>th</sup>, 2025

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Lotus Blossom received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Lotus Blossom. I greet you with a blessing of love, with a warm embrace, with a loving acceptance and great admiration. My brothers and sisters on the Path Divine, may you open yourselves mightily, surrendering to that inflowing of God's Love, allowing your soul to be blessed with its beauty and grace in the transformation.

Today's message that I would like to bring to you might be a little different, but one that I think is important and can help you to become more integrated, more appreciative, more accepting and loving to the gift that God has given you: your body. As we observe many on Earth, what we see is the physical body is largely taken for granted. In some ways, it is not cared for. What I hope to be able to convey to you is the love, the respect, the awareness of the instrument that you call your physical body. This is the instrument that you have been given as you walk the Earth plane, carrying your soul to and fro. This is the instrument that God's Love within your soul manifests through. But how well do you know your body? By this, I do not mean the systems of your body separately, but rather the wholeness of your structure, your physical structure and all that it encompasses.

Are you able to sit in the stillness and feel your body, not only the dense material elements of your body, but the subtleties, the flow of life force moving through you, the pumping of the blood, the passageways that your breath moves through, the sensitivity of the watery substances within you? If you are to sit and to have your attention upon the flow, the many things that flow within your body, what do you notice? Where are your thoughts? Are your thoughts in reverence of your body? Are they critical of your body? Are they respectful? Can you sit in that stillness and say to your body, *"I love you, I really love you,"* and allow that Substance of Divine Love to flow to all areas of your body, to bring healing, to bring ease, to bring peace?

Let yourself come into that place where you can sit with yourself, feel what God has created, what God has given to you as an instrument for your soul. Can you vow to respect its function and bring choices of loving support to it, caring for it in a way that is clean, that supports its vitality and that will ensure that its stability and its strength can carry you through all the days of your life? In the nature of the way your body has been created, it is a temporary home for your soul. Of course, as your life moves through the ages, there is a natural deterioration. But largely through your choices, you speed up that process, causing your physical body a great challenge for its natural function. It begins to break down sooner than necessary.

There is always time to reverse that process of ageing. Every day is a new day to bring the experience of love and gratitude, of respect and willingness to care for that physical part of you. I am not asking you or even suggesting to you that you must change everything in the way you have been doing things. But why I come to say this is to help you to see the ways in which you can make it easier for your physical body by sitting in that stillness and truly letting your body know how grateful you are for its instrumentality, and how you desire to grow in the ability of caring for its needs. You may notice a great sense of ease come about you, the harmony and the alignment between the parts of you. This, beautiful souls, as you progress along your path of awakening, can be possible for you, if you so desire.

Remember the laws and how they work. Remember your free will choices and the power of them. When you align these things within the wisdom of your soul, there will be the experience of great integration, an expanded awareness of the love that you are, of the light that you are, in the blessing that you are. Be kind. Be generous. Be attentive and be loving to your body. Soothe it with the blessing, for it to know that it is loved, appreciated, respected. For it is a gift that God has given you, one that largely operates automatically. Your heart beats without you thinking it needs to be. Your lungs move, taking in the breath. Your digestive system and all the systems operate flawlessly. This is how it has been designed.

Yes, nature does take its course, but realise, through your choices, how you exacerbate its flow, causing irritations and inflammations sooner than need be. Create a loving environment within you, speaking words of love to this beautiful instrument that carries you through the Earth plane step by step, in hopes of being able to help you fulfil your purpose. If you come to look at your body and even your mind as blessings for your soul that God has created and given to you, you may look a little differently upon these parts of you and come to understand that all that you are, body, mind, soul, spirit body, is because God has created you to be. It is not because you have created. It is because God has created and given to you this blessing.

It is my hope that a tenderness for all parts of you is awakened and you can see the wonderment and the workings of God through you, in you, around you at all times. May your life be a life of divinity, awakened by the grace of God's Love and in alignment in all parts. I am Lotus Blossom, always so happy to be with you, to encourage you and to pray with you. I love you and God bless you.
