# The Power of God’s Love

September 8<sup>th</sup>, 2025

Online Circle of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you, dear souls. It is Keea Atta Kem. Forgive me if I interrupt you as you receive God's Love into your souls, but I would like to acknowledge the blessing that your circle is. You know how to create a condition, a condition of light, a condition that allows the Celestial Angels to come close and to pray with each one of you. For our instrumentality is to serve God's Will. For everyone that prays to receive God's Love, He sends an angel, a Celestial Angel, to come into your midst, to raise your condition so that it is easier to feel the longings within your soul.

Dear friends, at times you feel us with you. At other times, it is not as easy to perceive us. But I want you to know that we will never leave you. We will never leave you on your own, for that is not how God has designed Love to be experienced within His universe. For in order to be able to really perceive the Love of your soul, it requires an action of giving. You know what it is like to feel God's Love come in. At times, the depth of that Love is so overwhelming that it brings you to tears. That is the power of God's Essence.

Then, you can also have the experience of sharing love, giving love and responding in love. It is twofold. In that giving, it is a beautiful experience to be able to allow the essence of your soul to share its very being, to have the unconditional quality of Divine Love flow from you to others. Not only are you giving this to others, but also you are experiencing your very nature, the nature of your being, becoming more and more loving, so you are experiencing yourself as a beam of love, your very essence, the nature of who you are, growing in the quality of Divine Love.

My dear friends, you know when you are in that state of joy, the unconditional experience of your emotions, these emotions purely expressing for the sake of expression, not looking for anything in return, not requiring approval or acceptance from others. The power of a soul that is filled with God's Love loves because that is its nature. You are growing in this power, in the power of God's Essence within you and the experience of the Divine Love in action, both within you and through you. Dear friends, let us enter back into that space of longing, once again offering God the invitation to help you to open your soul, for His Love to come in and fill you with His greatness. As you experience this response, allow yourself to be absorbed in all that God can give you and all that you can receive. Let everything down, release your barriers and your guards and open yourself to the greatest gift in all the universe.

I am Keea Atta Kem, praying with you, longing with you and receiving with you. God bless you.
