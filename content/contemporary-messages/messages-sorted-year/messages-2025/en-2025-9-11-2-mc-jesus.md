# Immerse Your Soul in Divine Love

September 11<sup>th</sup>, 2025

FUSC, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Maureen Cardoso

My brothers and my sisters, I come. I am Jesus. Raise your hearts up to the Father, dear ones. Raise yourselves high. Open your souls and let God's Touch in. Allow the Holy Spirit to bring into you the very Essence of our Creator, the One who has made each of us. Let this in-pouring fill you, fill you mightily, causing a great change and transformation on your soul, where every part of your nature is brought into harmony with love, with compassion, truth and wisdom.

All that you could ever yearn for is within this very Substance that comes into you now. Let the angel that is standing next to you bring healing through their presence, helping you to feel carried in a way that makes it easy for you to dissolve into this Substance, to become one with this inflowing of God's Love, feeling the at-onement with this Essence, where there is no beginning and no ending, but you are encompassed by Love, swimming in an ocean of Love, nourished, carried, thriving in God's Love. Feel the presence of God's Essence within you and around you. Let yourself go in this ocean of peace. Resist nothing, accept everything and let your heavenly Father touch you, love you and bless you.

I am your brother. I pray with you, drinking deeply the Living Waters of God's Love. God bless you. I am Jesus. God bless you.
