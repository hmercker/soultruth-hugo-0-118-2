# So Much Awaits

November 7<sup>th</sup>, 2025

Online Circle of Light, Fairfax, VA, USA

Elizabeth Barret Browning received by Jimbeau Walsh


I can speak of things unseen

beyond the ken of mortal dreams

Into the realms of spirit

where lie ghosts of past and truth denied

When now is all

and then is nil

one must take the bitter pill

of past regret

and let it fly

beyond the grief beyond the sky

So much awaits

you cannot see

but if you could

you would agree

all the things

you bought and sold

you could not keep;

you could not hold

they were good as zed ( zero)

to heaven’s gold

So treasure every true desire

for heaven’s gift

and God’s (desire)

that every soul become anew

in His love

His gift to you
