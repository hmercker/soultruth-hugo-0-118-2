# In Accordance to Your Gifts You Will Receive the Support and Blessings of an Angel Friend with You

March 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine. As you see, in accordance with your gifts, you will receive the support and the blessings of an angel friend with you, one who also shares a gift and ability that may enhance and support yours. This is part of God's plan and part of God's harmony in the universe, the Law of Attraction working to your benefit, beloved souls. Each of you, as you grow in your gifts, will have a variety of beautiful Celestial Angels accompany you along your journey and help to reinforce your gifts in ways that you may be surprised, in ways that are unexpected. Indeed, you will benefit greatly and come to deeper realisations and expressions of those gifts as you mature, as your soul continues to awaken, as your perceptions sharpen and become deeper.

There is much about yourself you do not yet know, potentials that lie there, dormant, waiting for that igniting Divine Love to touch them and awaken them. The pacing of this awakening, the number of gifts and abilities that can be awakened, is determined by your efforts to receive the gift of Divine Love and to accept what that gift brings. Some will never know many of their gifts. Others will know a great deal of what is within their soul. It requires a curiosity, a desire to truly awaken to those elements and aspects of their being that God has created within them. When that desire ignites openings of the soul and awakenings of the soul, then the journey becomes intense and beautiful. There is so much to know about yourself, my beloved friends. There is so much that God can utilise within you, provided all is in harmony and in alignment, awakened and honed to a beautiful expression that is filled with light, grace and truth.

I encourage you, my friends, my beautiful students, to be awake, to awaken to yourself and awaken to your gifts. Of course, we all know how this is accomplished, by seeking out the Father's Love consistently and with great desire. This way, all that is meant to be and expressed within you will come about in harmony, in the flow that is consistent and in alignment with God's Will and desire for you. Much awaits each one of you. So much is not known, but can be known in accordance with your desire and efforts to awaken to your true self.

God bless you, my beloved students. Your teacher, Augustine, loves you, is with you and continues to help you to grow, to express yourself in all its trueness, beauty and intricacy. God bless you. Augustine loves you.
