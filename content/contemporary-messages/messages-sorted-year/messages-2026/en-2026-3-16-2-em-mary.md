# It Is Time to Rise

March 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Fort Worth, TX, USA

Mary received by Elizabeth Mack

God bless you, dear children of God. I am your sister, Mary. I bring with me many blessings and so much love for each soul. We are bringing you together now as one unit that joins together your desires and your longings. Listen to the Father, that you may feel His blessings as they pour upon you. For it is time to rise in your expression of your soul. It is time to rise in your development, that you may be in harmony with all that is happening in this Earth that is of God, that you may share with your brothers and sisters the Love that is within your souls already and at the same time receive more and more Love from your Father.

It is time for you to truly know your Creator, to be at one with God's Will, to be true in every sense of that word, with your desires, with your soul, with the laws and with your yearnings to be with God. It is time to walk this path fully and completely, knowing with each step that you will be taken care of, that you know where you are headed, that there be no distractions, no misalignments, that you weather these changes with the grace of God, for that too will raise you above the human condition and bring you to a more peaceful, a more loving and a more harmonious existence. It is time, dear children of God upon this Earth, for you to walk in faith, truth and love. It is time. Let this be your prayer now and going forward, that you live in truth, faith and love.

I am your sister, Mary. I bless you with my love for each one. The depth of my love for you is great. I ask you to open and receive it and I shall be with you always. I am your sister, Mary. May God bless each and every one of you.
