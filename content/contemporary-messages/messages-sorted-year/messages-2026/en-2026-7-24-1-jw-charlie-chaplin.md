# Present to the World the Love of God in a Way that resonates beyond Religion

July 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Charlie Chaplin received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, Charles, your brother and friend, Charlie Chaplin.

I come to you from the seventh sphere, by your numbers, on the precipice of the celestial kingdom where the love of God flows, and the beauty is beyond words. I have come many times through my friend here and described my life as a pauper through times of famine, through war, and all kinds of troubles, and yet I could always find a bright spot, something humorous even in tragedy. And so I created, with the help of many others, movies dealing with subjects from love and romance to politics, and certainly including the hobos and the homeless, the workers, and the lovers. And as I have told my brother before, my mother mentored me, and when I crossed over I realized she was a celestial angel and I also began to realize that the teachings I had learned of Christianity were a far cry from what Master Jesus was actually teaching and is still teaching on this glorious path of Divine Love.

I wish to commend all those who have continued praying and those who have created or co-created works of art in literature, in song, in visual media, in art and poetry. I can tell you that art that touches the heart, that stirs the soul, is a thousand times more effective than religious preaching. For dogmatic belief can be in conflict with the resonance and the harmonious vibrations of the love of God. True humor, as I have spoken about, is compassionate, is empathetic. So, now you have all these means of reaching people through media, around the world, and you need not be a famous actor or actress or singer or poet or writer to bring your art, your gifts to the world. And even forms of artificial intelligence can be beneficial because you can create such a diverse combination of cultural impressions. One could, as I am led to believe, recite a poem and ask for this intelligence to turn it into another language. This is very exciting, and I support all the artists, all the writers, all the poets, and those who sing and dance and share their gifts. I wish to encourage you to explore these avenues even deeper, and I might add one note of caution, which is to present to the world the love of God in a way that can resonate beyond religion, but contains the essence of spirituality, and in your case, the essence of God, this great love. I shall give you an example. One might write a screenplay about James Padgett and the wonderful work he did and continues to do, bringing to the public the ideas of mediumship, connecting with the celestial kingdom, bringing the truth of the Gospels and so forth, and this has been proposed and even worked on. But consider presenting the ideas, the truths given by the Master and the angels, in a very human fashion. Perhaps a homeless person who is visited by an angel begins to see the light, receive a healing, and eventually becomes a healer. And in this scenario, the homeless person prays in desperation to our loving Creator for assistance, and the angels come, and at some point, a soul is opened, and a flood of Divine Love fills it. All this is presented in a very human circumstance.

Of course, you need not use my example. I only give you a template. But I say it to those who are co-creating and wishing to get the truths of God’s love to the world, as the sister here with her healing circles. It’s not a religious group. It is a spiritual group, and she brings this longing of the soul, this healing balm to that group, and many get healed, all get blessed. This is a prime example of what you can do. So, bring the good humor and joy and magnificence of this wonderful gift to all in simple ways. Tell your stories however you are moved to tell them, and you shall bless the world. And I shall support you and a vast array and host of celestial angels in the love of God. Oh, I thank you for this time, this beautiful group. With all my love and the blessings of God, I shall step back. I am your brother and friend, in Christ.

I am Charlie Chaplin. God bless you.
