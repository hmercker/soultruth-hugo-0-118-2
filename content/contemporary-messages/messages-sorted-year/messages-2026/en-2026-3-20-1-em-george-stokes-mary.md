# Awaken Your Gifts and Walk in Divine Love

March 20<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Fort Worth, TX, USA

George Stokes & Mary received by Elizabeth Mack

**George Stokes:** Good evening, my brothers and sisters. I am George Stokes. I come to join you in this prayer at this sacred time when true children of God come together and allow their love to flow to the Father. The Father responds by bringing His Love and blessings into your souls. It is such a pleasure to be with you, you on the Earth plane, who are growing the light in this world, who are stepping forward, becoming Love Divine and expressing that Love in so many varied ways.

I know you all are individuals. I see your souls so bright and so talented. The Father has blessed you with so many gifts. I encourage you to explore what they are and to utilize them in your time upon the Earth, for the benefits will be great. The manifestation of all that the Father has given to you will change many upon this Earth to direct their attention to our Heavenly Father, their Creator and the one who loves them so dearly. You have many things that occupy your minds, but during this prayer and during your individual prayers and during your days, when your thoughts are raised to God, there are many opportunities for God to utilize the gifts that you have, the love that you have and the blessings that you have.

Turn your thoughts to the Father. Feel His Love and let that Love be expressed through you to those around you, to those you do not even know. Bring forth those gifts. Bring forth that love and join together often to bring about those changes upon the Earth that will be impacted by your love, as well as the Father's Love through you. How we cherish you children upon this Earth. How we see your beauty, see your truth and see the work that you do to continue along this path. You know we are with you and will be with you. Many of you feel our presence and many more of you will also begin to feel us, sense us and even see us. Come to know that we are with you. Come to know that we are giving you our love. Even as you sleep, we are with you.

I am your brother, George. I have great love for you and great admiration for all that you are doing. May God bless you and may you feel the arms of all of us around you, encircling you, lifting you higher and higher above the Earth condition. God bless you. God bless you, dear, true children of God. God bless you.

**Mary:** I am Mary, the mother of Jesus. I come forward to give you my love and my blessings. May you all walk tall in this love and express this love daily, moment to moment, that you may feel the love in you and being expressed. God bless you. My love is with you now and always. God bless you.
