# A Gift of Communication

March 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

George Stokes & Timothy received by Elizabeth Mack

**George Stokes:** I am here now. I am George and I bring a friend, a stellar friend, who you all know and who has been with this group from time to time.

**Timothy:** I am your beloved brother, Timothy, from another planet, far, far from this Earth. As we come closer to you and we bring our love to you, we open these channels of communication with you, as a blessing from the Father of all of us. We desire to be able to speak to you and have you hear us, so we bring this blessing now to each of you, that you may follow our directions to assist you in your lives on a regular basis.

We come in peace, as you know. More importantly, we come in love at the direction of our Heavenly Father. You know you may trust us. Recognise my voice, which in each of you will be felt and heard as we speak to each of you and all of you. We have come a long distance to be with you, to uplift you, to help you in these times that grow more and more chaotic and it is the blessing of God that we come and help in every way possible, protection, upliftment, guidance and valuable assets that will impact your lives in a most loving way.

So do listen to us. Open your willingness to hear us when we speak to you. It is for your benefit and the benefit of this world when we direct you. We will be around for a while to assist you. Do call upon us and if, for some reason, you are unsure of what you are hearing, turn to God and ask that you hear truthfully and more clearly. There will be no one to hurt you. We are here to help.

God bless you. God bless you, you workers for God who live upon this Earth. God bless you. I am your brother, Timothy, and I will be around. I love you all. You are my siblings, just as those upon the planet where I live are my siblings.
