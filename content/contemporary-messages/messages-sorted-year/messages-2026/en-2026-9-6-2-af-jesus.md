# Soulful Ministry is Accomplished Through the Sharing of Your Experiences and Making Personal Connections

September 6<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come, my beloveds. Once more, I come to be with you in prayer, to be with you in love. I am your brother and friend, Jesus and I come to speak to you about your continued progress in the Love of God. I was here when you spoke of your love of God. I was here when you spoke of prayer and your journey to come to be with God. It brought great joy to me to hear you speak in this way, to be true in your speech, to share of your heart, to be clear and loving in your way. For this is being an instrument of God.

When you teach from the heart, when it is truly of your soul, then you are being God's instrument and emissaries of truth. Allow yourself that luxury of opening, allowing and being in that place of soul consciousness. For this is how God will use you, now and in times to come. When you are truly in alignment with God, then all flows in harmony and all is of truth. When there are those who listen to you, you as you speak, as you share of your heart, then indeed they feel the power of it, the sincerity of it and the love that flows with it and this is how you must teach, my beloveds.

This is how I taught in my day. This is how all true teachers of the spiritual teach. It is not an intellectual exercise. It is not having the most words and the most things, aspects of truth crammed together, so that you do not miss that dialogue, those aspects of truth in one opportunity to speak. Humanity does not take in these things in such a way as to truly integrate them. They take in that sincerity.

They hear the simple words. They understand from your example and your sharing. This is how truth comes. It is what you emanate in what you share. It is the light that comes through as you share. It is the honouring of each soul as you are in a Circle of Light.

It is as of now in prayer. As you receive, this is how God wishes for you to teach and to share the truth in the world. As you do so, as you ask those questions that come from deep within you and as you share the truth from deep within, then the true learning is accomplished in your circle. I am very pleased with what you have done this morning, how you have done it, how you have overcome the intellectual need to bring forth information as much as you can.

Rather, the seed of truth has grown by watering it with your light, sincerity and simplicity. The truth that God gives you truly requires these elements, these parts of you, opening and being expressed to one another. These are the elements. These are the aspects of true teaching. By example, by the beauty of your soul shining forth and your material mind following suit within the flow of that beautiful condition, an aspect of your being, allow yourself that luxury. Be confident and faithful in what it is that you are giving to your brothers and sisters, and ask God to guide you.

Your angel friends will be with you. What will be given, what will flow, will be in the harmony of God's Will and the power of truth and the surety of your own testimony and giving of your heart to another. This is how truth truly is shared from one to another. It is a very good lesson of how this is done and how it can be done in such a way that the soul is moved, the soul feels and knows what is being shared is true, authentic and powerful.

The ways of God are like this, my beloveds. The ways of man continue to be filled with words and details. Each competes with another, being more eloquent, having more words, complicated and sophisticated, but what does this accomplish, my beloveds? Merely the expression of the mind and what you call ego. Yet, what must truly be in your circles is the humility of the soul expressed in the ways and language of the soul. First and foremost, the flow of love must precede the flow of words. You are learning, my beloveds. You are learning to speak in this way and we will be with you to assist you to speak truth from that place deep within you. We love you, my beloveds, oh how we love you! How we care for you! How we nurture you! How we accompany you!

This is done because God, in His wisdom, in His Truth, makes it possible for you to have your angel friends close. As you desire more of this Love, as you receive more of this Love, so the rapport that we may make will be closer, deeper and clearer. This is how it goes, my beloveds. The journey of the soul. This is what we are all teaching, is it not?

The journey of the soul and its relationship with God. Speak in these terms and simply, clearly, with confidence and with faith, speak of the truth of the soul. This is often done through the example of your own experiences, through the clarity of your own faithful knowing of these truths. I urge you, my beloveds, to speak in this way, to walk in this truth, to be that light shining bright. For this is what will attract many and many will be drawn to the love that you have and share as God's channels of Love.

This is what will be the hallmark of your ministries, your teachings and your presence. May God bless you in this way. May you see clearly the ways and means that you may speak from the soul and be true to that which is within, expressed in light, beauty and love. God bless you, my beloveds.

I am here with you and I encourage you to continue to be that light and to learn the words of truth that are spoken through the soul. Thus, we may do our work together. For in this alignment of expression, of truth, there will be the power of clarity and love brought together with the blessings of God's Love. I love you.

I am with you and I feel great joy when you are with God and you speak of God in the ways of truth. Your ministry reflects this and shall be as you walk the Earth, beloveds. We shall walk together, pray together, speak together, bless your brothers and sisters together in the truth of love. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus. God bless you.
