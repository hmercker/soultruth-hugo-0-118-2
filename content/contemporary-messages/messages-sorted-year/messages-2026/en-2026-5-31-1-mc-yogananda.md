# Allow Things of the Soul to be What Stimulates You

May 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Starpoint Circle of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Yogananda received by Maureen Cardoso

I am here. It is Yogananda. Your discussion draws me near. This topic is one that, for some, stimulates the mental faculties, gives the opportunity for deducing and analysing and logic. For some it does stimulate, but I ask you, if you speak on evolution, what is it that truly evolves? It is your soul, the Law of Progression drawing wisdom, truth, knowledge and soulful facts into your being. It is through the mind of the soul that you can know absolute truth and the material mind is not equipped to understand what the soul mind knows.

Yet, as the soul progresses in Divine Love, there can be an influence on your material mind as it comes into harmony, where these two consciousnesses merge and there is a flow of oneness, so to speak. The truths and wisdoms that come within the soul, because you carry the Substance and Essence of God's Love, can inform your material mind. While these debates can be somewhat stimulating, you can truly further your progression if you come into the study of your soul and God's Truths, for there is much that science does not know about God's Truths. Yet in ways it can be made that there is an understanding, one that pleases the mind.

As you listen, read or involve yourself in these discussions, I ask you to consider what part of you feels stimulated and what part of your being feels alive. Do you feel truths within the soul, or are you largely contemplating, making suggestion and wonderment based on your experiences, your education, what you have been told and how you have been subjected to learning? Those things that should inspire you, especially at this stage of your progression, are those things of the soul. When I say *"should"*, I do not mean this to be something that is harsh, but maybe something to allow yourself to be stimulated by. Do you really know yourself? Do you really know the attributes that make up your soul? Do you understand your human condition and how to surrender these conditions to God, asking God's blessings on those things that continue to distract you from progressing in soul wisdom and having time to receive this mighty flowing of God's Love? This is what will help your evolution and progression of the soul. You must go to the Source, the Source of all things. You must take action from within the soul and invite God to be your educator, invite God to stimulate your soulful mind to awaken the desires. The true science is the science of the soul. This is your path. You have all subscribed to the Path of Love Divine.

I am here to encourage you, to bring you back to the centre of your existence and feel from this place within you what is true. In much of what you do in your lives, you are chasing something. Do you know what your motivation is for doing anything? Is your motivation to be in harmony with God, or are you seeking some external response that would give you a short sense of peace, or belonging, or comfort, recognition or approval? When you are motivated in these ways, you are all chasing something, but when you allow your consciousness to come into the depths of your soul and you immerse yourself into the ocean of God's Love, turning to God for all answers, you will experience and can find the peace, the acceptance, love, sense of belonging, all through this beautiful Essence and union with God.

In these times of change that are happening in your world, this is the great currency of life, to be filled with God's Love, to ensure that you are able to be receptive and God can deposit into your soul His Love, ensuring that you are well in hand, you know who you are and you need not prove anything to anyone. You are so involved in coming to know who you truly are that those things that are not in harmony begin to fall away naturally. They no longer interest you. They no longer have a gravitational pull upon you. For you are anchored in soul consciousness and you aim over and over to remain so.

A soul that is buoyed in God's Love is one that is alive, is one that can help others, to throw a line to others. For they are strong in the immersion of the ocean of God's Love. In changing times there will be many that seek help. You must be strong to be able to be held by God with the capacity to cast a line to all those that you meet and those who come before you. God will bring to you the many who can learn through you and from you. You will have the capacity to guide others, because you know the path that has brought you to where you are. These are the things that really require your time and these are the things that are worthy of attention. It is only you that can walk your path, and it is for you to know what is for you and what is not for you.

Many things are changing, as you well know, upon your Earth, but also within yourself. All souls who pray for God's Love are under constant transformation, so allow yourself to be buoyed in God's grace always and keep this as your priority in order to fulfil your role that God has bestowed within you. For it is through prayer, the inflowing of God's Love into your soul, that this shall be revealed. You will walk as a soul lit with God's Love and God's Light, like a lighthouse with the ability to attract the many that God intends to send to you in time.

I am Yogananda. May the Breath of God's Love flow into you as you sit in prayer and receive this great blessing. God bless you all.
