# You Develop in the Ability to Express Soul Consciousness

March 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Peter received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Peter, Peter the Rock. I have not spoken through this instrument. I greet you all with a blessing of love and peace as you come together, strengthening your bonds, offering your instrumentality to be developed and understanding to a greater degree the workings of your soul. Each one is progressing in this way, experiencing the greater consciousness of soul. I encourage you to speak about this, to share this with one another, because as you do, it brings into your material consciousness the opportunity to express what you feel and know from your soul. For often, it can be that words evade feeling, sensation and understanding in its verbal expression.

As you develop and awaken and go out into the world, having that ability to express yourself in these ways will be important. For you recently, you had the experience where the dear soul Albert gave his talk on the soul. While he did not necessarily prepare, he trusted and had faith that we would help him, providing the words, because this is his prayer, *"Dear God, give me the words and put the words into my mouth so I can convey these truths and help beautiful souls come to understand themselves in a deeper way."*

It is important that as things shift and change within your consciousness, that you share and that you are open, for it is the next step on the journey. While the journey may not feel overly ritualistic, these awakenings that happen within you are unfolding in harmony because you carry the essence of God's Love in your soul. Through this transformation, these channels of communing and communication and the uploads from your soul into your consciousness can happen. The downloads from spirit can also happen. It is a working both from within and without. It is a great pleasure to see your progression, to see the road ahead that each one through your choices will take. It is becoming easier to have both feet on the Path Divine, because you are transforming and because you are awakening. You are showing and experiencing evidence within you where your nature is changing and how there is a greater desire to check God's Will before enacting your own. Where, at times, it was easy to enact your own will and periodically check with God's Will, now your soul consciousness grows within you and awakens within you, knowing that when all choices are made in all circumstances with God's Will in mind and followed, harmony will ensue.

You will feel strong. You will feel grounded. You will feel aligned within yourself, where you are not going against your own will, because every will within every soul is to be with God, to follow God and to serve God. As the obstructions of your human condition are being transformed and healed, you are seeing the greater evidence that true desire within the soul is able to lead you into following God's Will and help you to discern it in any particular moment, any decision and any circumstance. The more you allow yourself to tune in and attune to your soulful consciousness, the more you will find that that communication between your soul and God's soul grows in its purity and clarity in your perceptions and understanding. The filtering of this wisdom that moves from the soul spirit to the material becomes clearer and clearer because your obstructions and your filtration are being purified and cleansed.

Dear friends, be strong, be true and be sincere. Receiving the Divine Love within your souls makes this so. I am Peter and I am happy to be with you, to share with you, to guide and to pray with you in God's Love always. God bless you.
