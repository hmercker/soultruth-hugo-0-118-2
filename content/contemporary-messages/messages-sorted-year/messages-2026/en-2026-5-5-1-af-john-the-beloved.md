# Be an Island of Peace in a Troubled World

May 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

May the peace that passes all understanding be yours, beloved souls. I am John the Beloved. I cultivated peace in my ministry. It was important to be an island of peace in a world of turmoil. Even in my day, there were many problems, many conditions that were harsh and unbending toward the needs of the common man. I brought comfort as God's island of peace and love. I brought light. I prayed with many and I walked with many. I brought the Light of God's Touch and Love into the church, into my ministries, with all that I met upon my road of life. I was a dedicated servant of God.

Though this may sound simple and unadorned, it is a powerful truth that when your intentions are set upon light and love and harmony, then peace will ensue. Peace will be a part of your daily life. It must come as the outcome of your prayers and desires to serve and to be a light in the world. Light is not frenetic, demanding and harsh. Light is gentle and peaceful and gives people room to breathe and to be themselves, to relax and be with God. Many came to me. They were drawn to this island of light and peace that was my ministry. They were drawn instinctively. It was their souls that longed for this condition of light. It was their souls that knew there was safety, comfort and love in my midst and in the places in which I ministered, refuges of peace, of light.

My beloved friends, wherever you are or whatever dwelling or place that you do your ministry, make sure there is peace there. Make sure that others feel that and know it, for it is palpable. It is powerful and it brings a healing balm to the troubled soul. It opens the way and means for God to work. It is the way of the soul being utilised by God to be that channel of light, of healing and comfort and love. As the individual rests within these conditions of love and peace, they are receptive to your words and they are receptive to the truth. My beloved and beautiful brothers and sisters, be that light. Be that island of light in a dark world. Be true to God's Will and God's Touch upon you. Allow it to shine forth and all will flow in deep and wondrous blessings. Healings will be performed. Those who are weary in sorrow will be uplifted. Those who carry anger and darkness will find peace and release from these conditions. Those who are seeking truth will find truth and all will be under the umbrella of God's Touch.

My beloveds, if you walk in this humble way to serve God, you will find that many blessings will flow and much love will be given. Often it is not what you say, it is how you are in the world. When you say words, it is how you say them, how this reflects the inner light within you, the simple truth that you carry, that great torch of love and truth that is yours. Let it warm many, many souls who are seeking and weary, who are tired and forlorn. Be that light, my beloved friends, be that light. What will come will be peace that passes all understanding. All else will flow in accordance to God's Will and intention. In any gathering, any connection made, when God is put first, what will follow will bring healing and resolution. It will bring peace and comfort. It will bring truth and wisdom, all in the way of love, all in the expression of Divine Love.

May you be blessed, my beloved and beautiful brothers and sisters. I am John, John the Beloved, bringer of peace and light. I bring this to you, my beloved friends. I am with you as a friend. We walk the same road and we know the same light, the same peace, the same Love, all of which comes from God. God bless you, my beloved friends. May you walk in light always and know the Truth of God's Love burning in your heart and speak and express that Truth without hesitation, without reluctance, but boldly shine forth so the peace may manifest wherever you go. God bless you, loved ones. God bless you.
