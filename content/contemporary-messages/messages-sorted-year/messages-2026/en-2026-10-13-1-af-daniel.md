# Do Not Complicate the Simplicity of God’s Love and Truth

October 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Daniel received by Al Fike

Blessings to you, my dear friends. I am Daniel, one who has not spoken through this instrument often, but one who continues to work with you all. So many of us sit in the background and do not speak often, but we are with you. We assist you. We pray with you. We minister to you. Our hope is that you would not put up obstructions to our ministrations, that your thoughts may be pure intentions of the highest. You continue to pray for this gift that God has offered us all, His Essence flowing through the Holy Spirit to each of you.

Allow this to be the focus of your life, my friends. For this is the key to all spiritual progression of the soul. Yes, there are many pathways in your world, many options, many thoughts, many constructs of truth. Yet, there is only one truth that leads to at-onement with God and that is receiving His great gift. I urge you not to obscure that truth with teachings and ideas that are not in harmony and sync with this truth. Rather, simplify your path, make it truly reflective of what God intends for each individual, each soul who is seeking the Truth of His Love and the awakening transformation of his soul. For if you can do this consistently and daily, then you will find exactly what I mean by being true to the truth, by being simple in its pursuit.

There is no need for all the mental machinations, all these complications. It is truly a simple path and God will help you along that path, provided these obstructions do not exist. Yet, this is what is difficult for those on the Earth plane, the mental sphere, this place that is so full of thought. So many cannot understand the simplicity of it. So many want to adorn the truth with complex ideas that have no place to be added unto this truth. There is nothing truly that will obstruct you from it. God makes a clear passage for each one of you. God provides the Celestial Angels to help you and assist you along the path. God awakens you to this truth. You feel it within you, a burning, a desire, an awakening of sorts that says, *"Yes, this is the truth."* That feeling, that sensation, that sense of truth is all that you need, applying this to your prayer life, opening yourselves up with innocence, desire and a heart full of love, you will find the greatest blessings of all. You will find God there, your Creator, the one who loves you beyond imagining.

I urge you to awaken to the simple truth, those who are listening, those who are wondering, those spirits who have lost their way. There are many surrounding you, my friends. Many seeking, many lost and so they rely upon your purity, your dedication, the simplicity of the path you have chosen. For when it is simple, it is easy to follow. Those who are lost see the light. Those who are in spirit are not as subject to these mental conditions, at least those in the higher spheres, and so they can see and understand more clearly.

It is your fellow mortals who have great difficulty, great challenges. They do not know anything more than the mind. It dominates. It controls. It dictates what it is you will do and think and be. Yet, you have challenged the status quo and have walked a path that does not necessarily negate the mind, but says there is something higher and greater than the material mind and this is the message you must convey and demonstrate. Each of you, to continue on this Path Divine, must battle with this challenge, must get your head straight, as you would say, priorities set, boundaries made, where ideas have their place, but the sense of the soul, the wisdom of the soul, the feelings of the soul, the perceptions of the soul are predominant and have the upper hand in this journey toward truth and toward alignment with God.

Each of you has been on this path for some time. None of you are ignorant of it. I urge you, with each day, dedicate yourself to being in alignment with this truth, to living it, expressing it, being a channel of love for God in the world. You are needed, my friends, more so than ever. For you see the troubles in your world. You understand the ignorance, the darkness and the pain. You have lived this to a certain extent yourself. Now that you see the light, understand the journey, I urge you to be that bright, shining example, to speak the simple words that God loves you and that God is Love, that you may know this Love as a true and personal relationship with your Creator. Simple words, my friends, but the most powerful words you could ever speak. I urge you to speak, to share, to show your brothers and sisters that there is truly something more than the mediocrity that man has made of this world, overshadowing God's creation and God's intention.

Clear the darkness away. Walk in the light and be God's servants in truth. In this way, you will have the most fulfilling of lives. You will have the most wonderful experiences. You will find yourself in the flow of God's Will and intention. You will know His Love to an ever greater extent, day by day. What more could you ask for? You have seen that the material path is not fulfilling and the people are most unhappy. My friends, bring the joy, bring the light, bring the truth and bring the love. That is what we ask of you, beloved friends.

I am Daniel. I thank you for listening to me today. I know many have said these words and yet they bear repeating once more as you seek this great gift of Love. God bless you. My love is with you, beautiful souls seeking in the light. God bless you.
