# Your Transformation in God’s Love is Possible While Still on Earth

January 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Retreat, Christ Church, Barbados

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Mary. You are all children of God. What does that mean? When we speak of a true child of God, we mean one that has chosen the Path, the Divine Path, one whose soul receives the Essence, the blessing, whose soul moves through the transformation where the elements of its original creation are transformed, becoming something completely new. This is why it is referred to as the New Birth. The soul that is transformed, changed by receiving the Substance of God, has completely awakened anew and has the capacity to be at-one, its substance and God's Substance being at-one. You come to experience many different things upon the journey from within the soul. In this transformation, the soul mind awakens and informs the spirit mind and the spirit mind relays to your material mind, creating in time pure alignment, where what you know within your soul you also know with clarity within your material consciousness.

While you are on Earth, there is the possibility for your transformation to occur. You need not wait until you transition into the spirit world. A soul who has received the New Birth while on Earth is a demonstration, an embodiment of God's Divine Essence on Earth, where there is no separation and where the individual's will and God's Will are at-one. The desire within the soul is fulfilled because it has attained at-onement with its Creator and the relationship between God's Soul and the individual’s soul amounts to the experience of the ultimate progressive way of becoming and being, living in joy, in alignment.

In your great efforts to pray for God's Love, you work towards your transformation of becoming a divine soul at-one in Substance with the Creator. Some of you feel this powerful desire within your soul that longs and yearns and seeks to be at-one with God in true experience. We come near you. We inspire you and influence you, for it is possible. It is possible. Those of us in spirit who guide you also desire your awakening, your New Birth, while you are on Earth. It will create a rapport between you and us that is also clear. You will know us as closely as you know your physical friends, but even closer. You desire for us to manifest, to be able to fulfil the harmony that God's plan has for Earth. As your instrumentality deepens and your soul progresses in Love, we can come close.

This is all in your hands. For your desires to be fulfilled, ask God to help you to purify your desires so that His mission through you may be fulfilled. Ask Him to help you to know with every moment the alignment and the harmony, to live within the laws and to demonstrate for your brothers and sisters the potential of being a transformed soul on Earth. Let this desire, that truly is within your soul, every soul, be lived through you. You are our band of angels in the making on Earth. We pray with you and for you more intensely now than in other times, because the shift and the awakenings are very close.

I am Mary and I thank you for letting me bring these words of truth to you, in hopes that your soul can absorb the current of love that forms these words and the truth that has been spoken. God bless you all. My love is with you. I am Mary.
