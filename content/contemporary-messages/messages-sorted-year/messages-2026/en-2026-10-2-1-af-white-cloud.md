# Reverence for the Earth

October 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

White Cloud received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am White Cloud. Indeed, the Earth is replenished by God and yet humanity continues to take from the Earth, so this contradiction continues to happen with every breath. The Earth is alive, it is full of life and yet humanity chooses not to see this, nor honour it. I am not suggesting that you, my friends, dishonour the Earth. For I know you have great reverence for it, but generally, the vast scale of humanity that exists upon the Earth continues in their attitudes of ignorance and their desires for material. In this, they bring the Earth to its knees. So much destruction, so much out of harmony and indeed, the problems, the conditions are overwhelming and they are overwhelming the Earth.

My friends, continue to pray for this beautiful world. Continue to pray for yourselves within it. For you need all the upliftment and support that you can receive. For the world continues to fight back. The world continues to change and respond to the collective efforts of humanity, so my friends, there is a struggle happening. There is a deep contradiction expressed with humanity and there is great effort by the Earth and by those who are allied with the Earth to heal and to prevent further destruction. Think of this when you go about your daily life. Are you loving the Earth, or are you taking the Earth for granted, or are you actively doing that which creates disharmony and destruction in the world?

Humanity is filled with free will. The choices are yours. God gave you, those who have a soul, the creatures of His creation, that great gift and in the purpose of that gift is to come to God willingly and wholeheartedly to be with God and to know His Love and in knowing His Love, see the world from the eyes of the divine. Understand the world from the perceptions and wisdom of your soul. When you do this, how can you desecrate your beautiful world, your home? You cannot. Yet, each of you, unfortunately and all of humanity are caught up in such a web of disharmony and ignorance that it is difficult not to engage in these conditions.

You must be wise, my friends. Walk in the world with love, seek out God's Love that you may have that wisdom, may have that reverence for the world. Now, as you continue to come closer to God, so these perceptions, this attitude, the Love will flourish and predominate in your life. This is the healing effects and the necessary blessings that must come with your prayers and your desires to be those formidable instruments bringing the healing and the correction that must come. My friends, we will work together. Many of us desire to heal the world, for it is the starting point for all the souls who are yet to come and incarnate in this world.

Indeed, if God's plans were realised, there would be no concern and humanity would continue to flourish upon the world. Yet, unfortunately, the suffering, the inequities, the trampling of the world and the trampling of each other has created a terrible condition, one never intended by God. Bring the ripeness of God's Touch within you, express in the world. Be that channel of love and wisdom. Act in accordance to your soul's understanding and be gracious in the eyes of God and in the eyes of your brothers and sisters.

You will find that what you are will teach others. How you are in love will be an example for others and this is how the change may happen in the world. Your dedications, your commitment and your love will bring healing and change, my friends. Continue on this path. Continue to be with the Great White Spirit, the Heavenly Creator and know that in this, God will use you to heal and bring that which is needed. May His blessings continue to touch you deeply, my friends and bring the peace, the joy and the light that will nourish you, nourish your soul.

I am White Cloud. I come to encourage you, my friends. For how else will the world heal but with instruments whom God can use to bring about that healing? God bless you all in His Love. I am with you in your efforts to bring light to the world. I will continue to be with you. God bless you all. God bless you all.
