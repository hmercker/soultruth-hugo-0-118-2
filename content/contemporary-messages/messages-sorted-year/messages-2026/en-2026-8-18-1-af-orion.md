# You Have Tremendous Support Behind You

August 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. The workings of the universe are often misunderstood or not seen at all by humanity, obscured by a mind that is so directed in certain ways that it cannot open to the wonderment of creation of the universe. All must be proven in a physical way, material way, before it is accepted. Yet, so much of the universe is not of a physical nature, it is of a spiritual nature. Until humanity can understand this and accept this fact, they will be limited. You will be limited in what you might do on this material world of yours.

It is a very concrete, inflexible and difficult world to be in. The heaviness is quite astounding to those of us who live elsewhere. Conditions are oppressive. So much is not of light or of truth and yet this is the condition that you live in. This is where you reside and you struggle each day to get beyond these things to that which is of the spiritual and soulful nature, the reality of God's creation.

You are the forerunners of what is inevitable, the opening of the minds and souls, consciousness of humanity, to all that which is in the universe, but even you struggle, my beloved friends. I see it in your daily life. I see how you acquiesce to the heaviness of the material world. You struggle to maintain your equilibrium in a spiritual way. You seek to be uplifted and enlightened and yet most of your day is the opposite. It is reinforcing the material consciousness, the reality of man, rather than the reality of God.

I do not say this in criticism. I say this in empathy and sympathy for what you have to deal with in your daily life. Yet, you are surrounded by angels, spirits, stellars who are seeking to help you remedy this situation. We will continue to do so until that situation is remedied or until you pass into spirit, which would be the ultimate remedy for you. Yet, as it stands, you are in a condition of struggle, conflict and difficulty that continues to contradict your soulful desires and efforts to be with God.

You do not do this willingly. It is part of the nature of where you live. Yet, indeed, because of the power and influence of these conditions, it becomes second nature to you to accept them and to live with them. You have done so, many of you, for a number of years and that struggle, though it has diminished, is not over. Yet, as you continue to grow in God's Divine Love, this holy Essence of the Creator, the most powerful remedy in the universe, you then come closer to harmony and peace because closer soul consciousness and God consciousness become closer to all those things which you desire.

You must be diligent, stalwart, patient and faithful in order to make that steady pace toward the conditions that are the opposite of this worldly state of being. At times you step forward. At times you step back. It is indeed a human thing. At times you seek truth and find truth. In this finding, you often take what you have seen and understood from a soulful perspective and change it, transmute it into what the mind might accept. Yet, you continue to work toward the goal of enlightenment, of freedom from the human condition, of truly being a channel of love and light in the world and in this commitment, this dedication that you carry, we are there and many are there

My beloved friends, if you had any idea of the support that is given to you, from the Creator all the way down to your friends and all that which is in between. It is massive. It is potent. Without it, surely you would capitulate into the human condition with your brothers and sisters. Yet, because you have chosen this path, because you have reinforced your soul and changed it with the great gift of Love, you are able to move forward, even if the footfalls are difficult. It is like walking through mud, but you do so nonetheless.

The stalwart strength that you carry, that which is of your soul, continues to guide you forward and give you the impetus, the momentum to move forward. We are there pushing as well, helping you, assisting you, holding your hands, healing and blessing so that your condition may continue to be uplifted. It is a very large task, not readily performed by any of us. Not easy, but we are there in greater commitment than even you. We are there to help and to help you stay immune from the conditions that would hinder you.

My friends, you have my empathy, my love and my sympathy for what you must endure day in and day out, but know that in our love for you, my love for you, you are upheld. You are protected and indeed you receive Divine guidance. It enters your soul. Often you are not aware of it or it takes its time to enter into your consciousness. It is there nonetheless for each one of you. God cares for each one of you with such love and tender care that you are indeed cradled in the arms of God. We continue on our journey, do we not, seeking the fulfilment of our soul's purpose and desire, seeking a way through the maze of the human conditions, seeking to find fulfilment, not in the material sense, but in the soul and spiritual sense?

This is where we stand together each day. Remember this, my friends. Each day, we are there. Your angel friends are there. God is there and all the goodness of the bright souls, all who love you, are there with you. When you begin your day, take stock of what is around you, what is given to you freely, the love that pours upon you and sit in prayer, contemplation, gratitude and joy for what God may give to you and what God has given to you.

This is the sure and most effective antidote to the stodgy conditions of the material world. To find yourself free from this, to extract yourself from its influence, you understand the laws that pertain to these things. I need not remind you completely of how you may do this. It is your continued routine and effort to do so. I merely say we are there with you. We are there with you.

Our desire is that you acknowledge it. You are aware of it. It is not difficult to come to this awareness when you are inhabiting your soul. For the faculties of your soul are all too able to see, feel and be aware. Seek this out. Forge that path between your material consciousness and your soul consciousness.

Make it well trod and easily engaged with it. You will find us there, us and many others. You will come to understand truly how amazing and how wondrous is God's Love for you, care for you and guidance for you. It all comes together as your soul awakens to what is truly there for you. What the reality of God looks like, feels like, is seen, felt, heard, acknowledged and understood. It comes with His Love and it comes with our love, many souls who love you. You are surrounded, my friends, in love.

Feel it, absorb it. Seek that highest of Love. All else will come into clear perspective and blessings for each of you. God bless you, my friends.

I am Orion, your friend, the one who cares for you, the one who will never leave you, the one who is excited by the journey that you are taking and is there to help and support, to be with you upon that journey. God bless you, my friends. My love is with you. God bless you.
