# David of the Bible Provides Guidance

February 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Sechelt, BC, Canada

David received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved souls. I am David, David of the Bible. Indeed, God takes advantage of every opportunity to bring Light, Love and blessings to all who are receptive and all who are willing to gather together. It is not so much the words you speak, my beloved friends. It is what you bring in your hearts, the light that you carry in your souls, that has the effect upon those children who gather, who are seeking truth.

In your world, words are valued greatly and those who speak well are listened to and believed. Indeed, each of you has a gift of your words and your ability to speak. Yet, what is more important is that light that you carry and those who will be gathering in your precious church, my beloved friends, are eager. They are thirsty for love, for acknowledgement, for light. They desire God, but many of them do not know how to reach God and to be with God. You must be the living examples for them, the blessings that will come to open those faculties, inspire them to come to understand truly who and what God is and how they, within their souls, have a reflection of God, and that reflection can become substantial and real and infilled with His Love, His Essence.

This is what must be taught. Indeed, if some in your group carry this Truth with them and practise this Truth in prayer, then their lives will change and deep blessings will come to them and they too will become a light, a light to help others. This is our mission. This is what we seek. All of us within the Celestial Kingdom are eager that lights will brighten, that understanding will deepen but most of all that the acknowledgement and the experience and the faith that comes with this experience of receiving Divine Love will be in the hearts of many.

For what will change the world, my beloveds? It is not words. It is the Essence of God and the power of that Essence to change the hearts and souls of humanity. This will change the world and this is why we gather you together. These are the opportunities that God has set before us and this is where these dear and intrepid souls will come and travel into your midst and bring this message forward. Yet you, my beloveds, to all of you, are beautiful channels of light and love and we wish to utilise those gifts in powerful and effective ways to change the hearts and souls of humanity.

May you be blessed upon that journey, my beloved friends. We will be with you as you do so. I am David and my love is with you. God bless you.
