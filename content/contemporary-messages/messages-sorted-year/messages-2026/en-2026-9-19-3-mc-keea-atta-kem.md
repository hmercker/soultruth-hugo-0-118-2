# If You Allow This Love to Work Upon You, It Will Bring Harmony and Wholeness

September 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Acton Spiritualist Chruch Retreat, Acton, UK

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Keea Atta Kem, so very happy to be able to speak to you and invite you into the Circle of Light, where your singleness of purpose is to open your soul, to allow the longings to flow out from your soul to your beautiful Heavenly Creator. With the sincere desire of your soul, moving in that way, in communing, there is a response where this Love is carried into your soul through the Holy Spirit of God's Soul. This begins a conscious relationship between you and your Maker. There is no higher Love. There is no higher experience. There is no greater fulfilment that can come to you than through this Love.

We, the Celestial Angels, stand in your midst and each one of you has that angel at your side. For we are the instrumentalities that God sends to these beautiful souls who yearn and desire to grow and awaken through the Divine Love coming into their soul. We can help you, uplift you, influence you in ways that will turn your attention upon your soul, upon the building of relationship with God, in order that you may become at one, where all of those places within you that are not in harmony with love are transformed, fall away, and where Love becomes the substance. Divine Love becomes the substance that your soul is made of.

As you have heard, it is an incremental journey, one that you must continue to desire, to use your own free will to attune to the desires of your soul and begin to align with this place within you, to allow your soul to lead the way. At the moment, there is a great duality within the consciousness of your material mind and the consciousness of your soul. Yet, this Love will awaken the consciousness of your soul and bring to you God Consciousness. While you may not feel as though that is the road that you want to take, what I can promise you is if you allow this Love, this Essence, to work upon you, it will clear away the human conditioning that keeps you separate. It will allow the harmony, the wholeness, the beauty that you are, the jewel that God has created in you, to awaken.

As you sit in this circle, allowing your mind to fall back into your heart and to perceive the longings of your soul, that true desire that longs to be with God, you will experience an inflowing, a Touch of God, like God can only touch you, that blessing that is reserved purely for the Divine. I am Keea Atta Kem and I am so happy to see beautiful souls here, longing, searching, awakening on your journey. God bless you all.
