# Rest in the Embrace of Divine Love and Blessing

April 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

I come to be with you, my beloveds. I am Mary. I come to pray with you and to love you. It is my blessing and privilege to be with you, my beloveds, to know that there are those of you on Earth who are sincere and pure in intention and desire to be with God, to know His Love, to be free from the burdens of this world for these moments in prayer together, to know the glory of God uplifting, taking us into that great Hall of Peace, and that you, my beloveds, may open your souls wide and receive the great gift of His Love, to know this grace, that it enfolds you and uplifts you. You may walk in the Light of the Father's Hand upon each one of you, His blessing healing and transforming each of you. In your unique journey, your unique lives and personalities, this Love enhances, awakens, transforms and makes you a greater light, a more beautiful creation of God.

You learn the lessons of love. You learn what love truly is and what love can truly bring into your lives. It is not a love that is human. It is a love that is Divine. It does not carry the human traits and characteristics. It is always consistent. It is always there for you and it is without judgement or restriction. It is yours forever and shall be yours forevermore. What a beautiful gift that God has given you. Each of us has received this gift and continues to receive it. This journey of the expanding soul, the transformed soul, will never end.

There is no greater gift, my beloveds, no greater gift, than that of the Father's Love. My dear son came to the Earth to speak of this and he still does to whomever will listen, that the redemption of the soul is available to all. You, my beloved and precious children of light, must speak of this, must be Jesus's disciples on the Earth and come to know the blessings and the benefits that your efforts and your prayers will bring into your lives, into the lives around you and into the lives of humanity. It will come and touch deeply all who drink of these waters, this blessing.

My beloveds, we are as one in the Father's Love and shall journey in this way into at-onement with God. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Mary and I love you. God bless you, beloved children of God.
