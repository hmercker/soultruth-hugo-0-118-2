# Obstructions of the Spirit and Soul

August 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to be with you in your prayers and to assure you that the road of service is clear and well lit. Indeed, angels are with you and accompany you along this path and God lights the way for each of you, though it may not seem so clear to you and that the road is full of obstructions. Yet, as you do your work as one soul to other souls, as one soul, a channel of God's Love in the world, you are doing much work. As you continue to grow in the Father's Love, so you are powerful in light and ability. The obstructions that you see are that of the mind, but the obstructions that I speak of do not exist and they are of the spirit and the soul.

I say to you, my beloveds, have faith that you are serving God. Though you do not see the outcomes of your service, they are there nonetheless. You are spreading light in many corners that are dark, bringing comfort to many who are in pain and giving truth for those who are receptive to hearing it. I say to you, my beloveds, you are God's instruments. You walk along this path of service, the Divine Path. Opportunities will continue to present themselves and your understanding of your work and what you do on a daily basis will become clear and you will not see the road as littered with obstructions, but indeed clear and beautiful.

I say this to you because you grow in the Father's Love and that your perceptions of these things will grow with this blessing. Do not allow your mind to create obstructions, but have faith in the service that you provide, in the love that you have for God and the Love that God has for you. There is still much work to do, my beloveds. Still, so very much. I urge you to allow the awareness, the blessings of your soul, to rise up into your consciousness and predominate within that consciousness and you will find freedom and joy there.

Understanding will grow, clarity will come and much will be accomplished. Where you do not see your accomplishments to date, we see, God knows and this is what matters. For you, it is walking in faith, my beloveds. Walk in faith and trust that you are being guided and shall be guided for all the days of your life and beyond. Many blessings are yet to be bestowed upon you and upon others.

Much will be given and awakenings upon awakenings will be yours, beloved souls. I come to pray with you, my beautiful friends. I come to be with you in love. I am your brethren. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you.
