# Accept and Embrace the Coming Changes as God Restores Harmony to the Earth

June 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Michael Collier received by Al Fike

I thank you, dear daughter, for your wise and beautiful prayer. I am Michael Collier. Indeed, the Earth is changing, life is changing and I urge you to be flexible and accept change as change comes, for if you are able to bend and respond in positive ways to change, then you will be happier and more joyful through the changing world that will insist upon breaking routines, expectations and desires of the mind. For God's Will is that the will of man may be broken. The will of man, which has for too long determined the course and fate of this world, can no longer be sustained. For the will of man is misguided. It continues to seek out that which is pleasurable, that which brings to the individual material gain and pleasures of the flesh, but neglects spirituality and the soul.

My friends, it is time to ignite the desires of the soul and to acknowledge the needs of the soul. For it is in this the true and beautiful change will come. All the rest, the changing of systems and structures built by man, will not hold, will not be your daily lot anymore. You will see how quickly things can change. You will see that when one piece of the dominoes falls, many will collapse with it and it could be very swift indeed. I do not say these things to make you frightened or nervous, but indeed the mind reacts in this way.

The soul knows this change is necessary, that to bring the world back into harmony and peace, these old structures, these old things of man must fall, for they do not bring peace and they do not bring harmony. Indeed, the world cannot sustain what man demands of it. Indeed, humanity will be lost in this new order, those who cannot see beyond their own mindful needs and conditions. If they cannot see with the eyes of the soul and the intuition and wisdom of the soul, then they will be confused, they will be disheartened and they will feel that all is falling away and there is chaos.

Yet, indeed, the chaos is now. It is the conditions of the world as it stands. God's intention is to heal this, to push aside the will of man and the effects of man's will, to bring about a new order, a new way, a way that is ancient yet new, a way that is in harmony with the Laws of Creation, a way that is of love, rather than those conditions and thoughts and intentions that are selfish and continue to seek gain rather than to seek in a way that will sustain and bring harmony to the world. Yet, within every individual there is wisdom. There is the capacity to know the difference, to see what is required to bring that harmony forward. God will ensure that many will have the insights, the revelations, the ways and means of understanding what is needed to bring true change and positive change to the world.

There is no such thing as correcting and finding solutions within the mind. These things have been tried and they are not true. They are unsustainable. They are mental constructs that continue to add structure upon structure to the human condition and ideas of how life should be. This is the life of man, the life that man creates, the life that is contrary to God's creation, so all must fall. It will not collapse in a moment. You will not be threadbare and without shelter and food. No, God will not make the condition so that all will suffer terribly, but those who cannot accept change, those who are not flexible, those who do not seek the wisdom of their souls and the Will of God, will be completely disoriented, will not understand why these things are happening, why change is so intent and intended to remove the barriers to harmony and to peace.

My beautiful friends, it is for you to continue to pray and seek out the wisdom of God, to seek out the Love of God, to seek out the ways and means that God has for every soul to find their way upon that journey of reconciliation with God and reconciliation with Mother Earth, so that there is peace, there is harmony and that the generations that are to come will find a new life, a life that is resplendent in light, love and harmony, that there will be an Earth that will be plentiful and have all that is required. Indeed, humanity will come to the realisation that there must be balance. There must be the capacity to walk in the world without damaging, taking and doing its ways and means that are unsustainable.

God will give the wisdom. God will provide what you require, my beloved souls, so that you may be in that place of sustainability, harmony and peace. Indeed, as you pray for the Father's Love, you have within you the capacity, the ability and the perceptions to understand these things. They come in prayer. They come with your desire to know. They come with your questions for God and your love for God. They will come in abundance, these answers, these ways that are necessary to bring all of us into the harmony that is required into the future that is bright and beautiful and full of love.

My dear friends, you have heard these things many times. I merely wish to remind you that indeed the times to come may have their challenges, but will also have their benefits and rewards. You will find yourself appreciating God's plan and fitting into His plan for you and for the world. It comes step by step, day by day, but it is relentless, whether you appreciate or comply with this plan. It will be, my beloved friends. It will be, for the alternative is too terrible to consider. God intervenes and God gives to humanity what is required for humanity to thrive and be in harmony with His Laws of Creation and His Laws of Love. Love is the sustaining force.

Beloved souls, may you come to know this in all the ways that it exists and it applies its beautiful conditions and truth into your lives. May you know peace, my beloveds, peace beyond understanding. I am your friend Michael Collier and I pray with you for the upliftment, healing and sustaining of humanity and the generations that will come. God bless you. My love is with you. God bless you.
