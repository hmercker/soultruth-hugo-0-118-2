# Remove Those Mountains Within You

June 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

John Hughes received by Elizabeth Mack

I am your brother, John Hughes. I look at each of you and see beauty in your souls. I see truth and light and I see those things that are impeding your progress. In some, there is little. In others, there is more. Whether you have little or more, God is willing to remove anything and everything that is within your souls that keep you from moving closer and closer to Him. If you are willing, if you make the choice to receive God's Love, if you are willing to receive God's Truth, then open. Open widely and let rush in the Love that will transform your very souls and remove those mountains which need to be cleared.

God's Love for you is great, immeasurable, beyond expectation, beyond imagination, beyond conception but for now, trust and open, knowing that you will feel peace and love. You each have a purpose in this world. If you look deeply within your souls and allow that consciousness of your soul to express itself, you will come much closer to your Creator. Let us all be there in our souls, the safety of our souls that is filling with God's Divine Love, the gentleness of the Love, the care, the comfort, the stability, the joy and the Truth of the Love. Let us bathe ourselves in all of this, as our Father blesses us with His gift.

God bless you, brothers and sisters. May the Hand of God be upon each one of you now and forever. I am your brother, John Hughes, and my love is with each one. I thank my dear sister for allowing me to speak to you today.
