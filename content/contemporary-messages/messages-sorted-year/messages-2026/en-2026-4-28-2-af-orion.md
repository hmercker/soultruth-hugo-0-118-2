# Persist in Reaching Those Who Are Lost in Distraction

April 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. The world waits for your commitment. Each step draws you closer to that purpose that is within your soul, that unique journey and expression that is you. Although God is infinitely patient, the conditions of this world dictate that you may indeed apply yourself as diligently as you can. The world grows weary in the darkness. The conditions worsen. Suffering is intense, confusion and fear are rife. What can be done, my beloved friends, in such conditions and such situations in which your brothers and sisters find themselves on a daily basis? Their way of coping is often just to be numb and unable to feel, for when they do feel, it is not pleasant and those feelings are intense. Many lose themselves in distractions and substances that continue to bring them away from their true selves.

The world continues to drift in this miasma of confusion and darkness. Yet, you are anchored in love. You are anchored in the Light of God’s Love. In that anchoring, it is for you to reach out to others and say, *"You may hold on. I will grab you. I will embrace you in love. I will help you to find security in God."* Yet, few even listen to this offer. They are so distracted, so unable to hear, that they continue on in this terrible plight that is the human condition. You must persist, my friends. You must persist. For how else will the world change if it is not for those who know the road to change, to express it, to live it and to share it, not in a way that points a finger and preaches and tells others, *"thou shalt do this and not do that"*. No, this is not the way of love. It is in that quiet expression, that true harmony, that you bring to yourself in your prayers and blessings.

The power of God touching your soul is not only a personal experience, but it is also an emanation of love and light that affects all those around you. At the very least, you may emanate, my beloved friends. You may bring that light forward in your life, not allowing the darkness to impinge upon it, not allowing yourself to capitulate into it, but to be that strength, that anchor in the world, so that you are truly free of the human condition. It is a difficult journey to take. It sets you apart. You are not altogether comfortable with it in every situation. For that human part of you wants to belong, wants to be seen and wants to be recognised. Yet, what you truly are cannot be recognised by your brothers and sisters, who are blinded by their own condition, or unable to truly see. God has given you the blessing of one another, that each of you sees one another, each of you knows one another and loves one another.

Is this not all that you need, my beloved friends? The world itself cannot fill your needs. It is not capable. Yet, the blessings of God upon you, your angel friends, your stellar friends, the bright spirits, all the wonderful influences around you and your friends of the material world, we all conspire to bring greater light, to bring peace, to bring truth and to bring love. We will continue in that great effort to build the beachhead of light in the world, so that in time, others will feel and see it for what it is. You will have your opportunity to speak, share and embrace your brothers and sisters and they will feel it and know it for what it is. Then you might teach, my beloved friends. Then you might minister to others. Then you might shine bright and that brightness will help to brighten others, a channel of love for God.

This is what you aspire toward and this is what you are. I merely repeat these things that you already know to assure you that as you grow in the light, as you grow in this great gift of Love, then you become that anchor, that nexus of light in the world. It shall reach out. It shall emanate. It gets brighter and stronger and reaches others. God will utilise your gifts, your beautiful soul, awakening, transforming in love, to help awaken others, to show them the way. Listen to your guidance. Listen to God’s plan. For there is much to be done, many souls to connect with, much to be shared and given in love, much harmony to express and to demonstrate. May the truth be your conscious, beautiful expression. May love be the reflex of your soul expressing itself each and every moment. May you truly come to know and love yourself in a way that God intends for you to be, for in this, all that is intended by God, as an instrument of His Will and plan, may be realised and expressed in wondrous ways.

May you be blessed, my beloved friends, all the beauty that you are. May it come forth and shine and be truly a gift to others. God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. My love is with you. I am with you and shall be so for a very long time to come. God bless you, my friends. God bless you.
