# Tell City Pentecost

April 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Tell City Retreat, Tell City, IN, USA

Jesus received by Elizabeth Mack

Feel His Love coming through us. Feel the strength of that Love and of that blessing that is being bestowed upon us at this time. Each one is receiving in their souls this gift from God as we ascend to a higher realm, where there is more love, more peace and more attributes to receive. This is a great time of reception. Open, heal and allow the flow of God's Love and grace to touch each soul. As His Breath comes upon each one, a Pentecostal moment for each who is here, the Fire of Love over each soul is bestowed upon each one that you may burn with this desire to serve God and to follow your ministry as given.

God bless you. I am your brother, Jesus. You have been bestowed with the Father's blessing and gifts with which to do your purpose upon this Earth. Go out into the world with His blessing and give your love. Touch those souls who are in need, who are seeking truth, who are looking, looking for God and show them the way. Thus, you are blessed with all that you need to perform this work.

You are not alone. We go with you. We will be with you. Do not be afraid. All is in hand. All is of God. I am your brother, Jesus, and I too bless you, each one with my love. This retreat is blessed. You are all encircled and connected. My love is with each one of you as I kiss each one of you.
