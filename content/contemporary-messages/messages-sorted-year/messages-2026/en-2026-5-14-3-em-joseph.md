# Our Growing Family

May 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Joseph received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Joseph, the human father of Jesus. My young friend talked about family and I would like to expound a little bit about your families, the love that you feel for them, the kinship that you feel for them, is strong. It is a bind that you bring with you everywhere you go, to influence your human families. You are connected. When you pray for them, you are even more strongly connected and their souls feel the connection of these prayers that you offer for them. They may or may not understand, but their souls, their souls can feel it and your family grows, your Divine Love family grows and those connections are very strong. As you pray for your brothers and sisters across the world, their souls feel it and they are uplifted and they are connected. As your brothers and sisters across the universe pray with you and for you, your souls are touched and you are connected.

This is the true family of mankind. This is the true connection that the Father intended for all of His children to be connected by this Love that cannot be broken, cannot be taken away and cannot be changed. Now you begin to see how loving our Creator is toward all of His children. As you progress in this Love and you pray for your brothers and sisters, wherever they are, your connection grows stronger and truer and you build your life with your Divine Love family, God's family.

I hope you will contemplate this relationship that you are building, not only with God, but with your brothers and sisters, a relationship of true love and kinship. I thank you for listening and I thank you for hearing. I am your brother Joseph. My love is with you and our Father loves each and every one of you deeply, as deeply as you allow Him to love you.
