# The Way that is Spoken is not the Eternal Way

June 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Lao Tzu received by Jimbeau Walsh

I come again. I am Lao Tzu, your brother and friend in the celestial heavens.

My brothers and sisters, when I wrote so long ago, *the way that can be spoken is not the Eternal Way*, I meant to communicate that the universe could not be contained in a box. Today, I wish to coalesce that into our path of Divine Love, meaning not only that the Creator cannot be put in a box but that all the words used to describe God’s magnificence fall short of experiencing Divine Love. Yes, it is somewhat paradoxical that I used words to communicate their shortcomings, but words are wonderful in that way, because when you are moved in your soul by a truth that is written or spoken, you have realized it is beyond your comprehension, but not your feelings, and even your experience in receiving this great gift cannot be adequately described to others who have not.

So, the way of Divine Love is, of course, prayer. To be still and open in the longings of at-onement with your Creator. The love of God comes, as is said, as a still small voice, as a whisper to the soul, as the wind of the spirit comes upon you. The words be still and know that I am God can be comprehended by the soul but cannot be adequately explained with the mind. This is why the heart and soul are so inseparable. For although all the words of love can truly touch one, it is the feeling, the presence of love, that makes all the difference. As you set aside the world in this circle, as you set aside your busy day, all of your pending interactions, plans, may you allow the flow of grace to overwhelm your mind that your very being may be filled with the love of God. This is the way of Divine Love. At its core, it is simple beyond words. It can come in a moment of crisis, the heart and soul calling out. It could come in the stillness of the heart, awaiting the great love, as the lover awaits the beloved one. All things come into harmony, come into the great will of God when one is aligned in love.

I wish to convey not only the simplicity of The Way but also its greatness. The magnificence, joy, and freedom that come with the inflowing of the essence of our Creator; All this, for the asking. You needn’t climb a mountain, but you do need to be humble in the asking so you may be blessed in the receiving.

Be of good cheer and joy. We will not abandon you, and we always come when called. I thank you for allowing me to come again, and my brother again for taking this message. With all my love and blessings, I shall step back. May the blessings of the great love of God be with you all.

I am Lao Tzu.
