# Attribute of Joy

May 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Goldie received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. It is Goldie. My dear friends, I bring the joy of my soul to you and shower you with this blessing, this great blessing of expanded light and peace. The expression of the soul that is imbued with God's Love grows in its awareness, its development and its capacity to radiate joy. Joy is one of the strong attributes that is the very centre of your being. It is not dependent on outside circumstances, but it is a quality of your soul that is held within you and one that you can experience and tap into at any moment in time.

When you are experiencing hardships or confusion, things that take you away from the awareness of God and His Love and His blessing that you hold within your soul, stop a moment. Pause and allow your complete attention to centre into the soul, going deep into that place within you created by God. Feel into the joy that sits within your soul. Allow the expression, the consciousness of joy, to fill you. For any spark, any droplet of joy that you can feel will multiply if you allow yourself to remain steady and focused upon it.

It is something that is available to you at all times, this joy that rests within your soul, that lives within your being. You can always experience a little joy, no matter what is happening in your material world through the circumstances that the life you are experiencing on Earth brings toward you. For when you centre in on joy, you experience the power of your soul, the power to rise above exterior circumstances, the power to rise above your emotional turmoil, the power within you that knows, in truth, all is very well, that God is near you, that God's Love is always available for you. Through that great blessing of Divine Love coming into your soul, these beautiful attributes gain power, stability and your consciousness grows in its awareness. Joy is an infectious light. When you allow that quality of your soul to emanate through you, it affects many. People can see the light that shines upon your face when you are aware of joy and you are connected into your joy, experiencing the brightness, its expandedness and its lightness. It puts light into your eyes. It brings a smile upon your face. It generates a demeanour that is easy. It is filled with light.

Dear souls, know that the attribute of joy grows in its power within you every day, with every drop of Love that comes into you. It is one of the beautiful qualities and as it grows, those things that are out of harmony begin to be transformed and fall away as though they never existed. For it is joy that replaces these conditions within you. Many of you can tap into your joy at any given moment. I encourage you to experience joy every day, merely by feeling its quality within you, allowing its light to affect you, affect your mind, your thoughts, your actions and your way of being. It puts a skip in your step, a lightness, allowing any heavy burdens to dissipate. Bring joy, the light of joy, the blessing of joy into your life and watch the changes that occur simply by allowing this blessing of God's attribute that is within you to show the world, to shower the world and for your experience to be one of great expanded joy in the light and through the light of your being.

I am Goldie. It gives me great joy to be able to come and convey a few words on the attribute of joy that is of the soul. God bless you all on your continued journey to at-onement with God and the awakening of your soul. God bless you.
