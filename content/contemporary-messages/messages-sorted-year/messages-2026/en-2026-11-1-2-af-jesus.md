# Be Living Examples of the Truth that You Know

November 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Yes, it is time to rejoice, my beloveds. Rejoice in the fact that you have found the truth and that you have found life everlasting within that truth, receiving the great gift of God's Love. I come to be with you, my beloveds, your brother and friend Jesus. I am here. Indeed, as the world continues to blame God, to blame another for their own misdeeds, you have come to understand how God's Will may be implemented within your life.

I urge you to have the discernment, the awareness of what is God's Will and what is yours. Humanity must learn the difference. For humanity finds it easy to blame another, to not accept responsibility for their own predicaments and actions. Indeed, some are victims of the human condition, but you all play your part in the choices that you make and how you are in the world. Where there is the choice to love, often that is not expressed. Where there is the choice to accept, to encourage, to bring light into any given situation, many are ignorant of how they might do this.

Yet, you, my beloveds, have been taught well. You have been given much in the way of truth. You know the difference between that which is of light and truth and harmony and that which is not. You must be living examples of that truth that you know, channels of that Love that is of God and teachers of truth that has been given to you. As you affirm the truth by your own experience in life, the changes that have come to you, the joy that wells up within you, the expansion of your souls in God's Love, so you are well equipped to spread this word, to bring this truth to others.

Indeed, as you monitor your own thoughts, choices and conditions, as you continue to choose toward light and that which affirms the truth, then you become powerful emissaries of God's Truth, channels of His Love, teachers of this truth. Not in ways that are so common in your world to blame others for their wrongdoing, to tell them that they are misguided, for this is the human way of correcting the world. Yet, the Divine is different, is it not? You know God's unconditional Love. You know God's forgiveness. You know that time and time again, as you ask God for His blessings, no matter what conditions you have brought upon yourself, when you do so in faith, you receive the blessings that you desire and that you ask for, at least those that are in harmony with the Laws of Love. In this truth in your life, the stalwart blessings of God's Love that come to you, be in this condition and this state of forgiveness for all you meet, a channel of love for all you see.

It is easy to see the error and the darkness and how so many create dark and disharmonious conditions around them by their thoughts. It is easy to judge them. It is easy to withdraw from them and build around yourself a cocoon of light that insulates you from all of this wrongdoing in the world, but is this truly being God's channels of love? It is in your fear and your judgement that you withdraw, but within your soul is the desire to love and to express love. It is merely the machinations of your mind that keep you from doing so.

I urge you, my beloveds, to make those efforts required to amalgamate the loving nature of your soul with your material mind so that you may truly be clear channels of love wherever you go, that you put aside your judgements, whether that be self-judgement or the judgement of others. Instead, replace this with love. Each of you is capable of doing this. Each of you has many, many opportunities to do so. Whether it is with a family member, or with a colleague, or a stranger, whoever it might be, when you express love, even if it is without words, but a look of compassion and loving acknowledgement of your brothers and sisters, then this is what changes the world. You bring into that situation and condition a state of love, a light that washes over those who you are with.

My beloveds, this lesson, so basic but so crucial, is important and needed, not only in your lives but in the lives of humanity. Choose love, my beloveds. Choose love always. God always chooses to love you. We always choose to love you and in this, there is light and harmony. You are refreshed by our presence and our love. You are refreshed by God's Love, the Holy Spirit that conveys it and you are refreshed when you choose love. Is it not worth your effort, your choice, your time to be that channel of love in the world and to follow God's commandment to love and to be love? For as you do so, you do God's work. You fulfil God's plan for the salvation of humanity. As you do so, God will open doors so that you may be that channel of love for many souls in many situations. As long as you are willing, as long as you choose to love, then the opportunities to do so will be great and varied and joy will well up within your soul as you come to know the ways and means you may serve God and you may be that channel of love in the world, true and pure and beautiful.

I will come to help you in this, as will many angels. Ask for our help. Ask that you may be that channel of love. Ask for inspiration from God. Ask for wisdom of the soul and to know the love that dwells within you, truly know it and express it. You will find that a different life unfolds before you, one that does not have the burdens, the pressures, the recriminations and the discord within you that will be washed away by the blessing of God's Love.

Be in this blessing now, my beloveds, as we all enter that Hall of Peace and find our refreshment in the holy Touch of God, our souls open wide, our desires laid bare, our humble and graceful selves kneeling at the Feet of God. We are together in that light, in the grace of God's Touch. We are together, my beloveds. I pray for you. Will you pray for others? We shall pray together, being a powerful force for good, a channel for blessings and love for humanity and for ourselves. God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother, your friend and I am close. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you.
