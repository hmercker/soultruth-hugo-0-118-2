# The True Message of Easter

March 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Andrew received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Andrew. I was once an Apostle of the Master and I still follow his lead that brings us to the Divine Soul of God. This is and has been our will, our choice, our desire, in the deepest sense, to be at-one with God. Our Master leads us all to those Heavens high above where we all are.

Soon there will be a recognition of the day that the Master told the world that there is life after life on this Earth. You call it Easter and resurrection. We call it the Truth of God's universe, that life continues. For those of us with redeemed souls, it will always continue. This is the true message of his rising from the physical death, that life continues in service to God, in at-onement with God, and in God's Love. We celebrate this Truth that the Master expressed after his physical body was separated from him, for he was soul and spirit in this separation and his soul continues to brighten the Heavens. When he comes to the Earth, it brightens the Earth.

We ask of you on this day of celebration that you pray for the world and all of its inhabitants, that they be raised in the spiritual sense, to come to know the Truth of God's Divine Love that allows them to live forever and in at-onement with Him. We ask you to remember that God is Love and that Love is the Truth. We ask you to pray deeply, sincerely, truly, for humanity, that they turn away from the great misery and unhappiness that they are expressing and living at this time and allow Love to enter their souls, their minds and their bodies. For this is the Will of God.

There is much that can be said about events upon the Earth, but that is not really the most important thing to say. The most important thing to say is that possessors of Divine Love, such as yourselves, along with all of us who are redeemed souls, can impact the lives of people upon this Earth. So pray. Pray for these souls upon the Earth that they turn toward God, learn the truth and live the Truth of God's Divine Love. Your prayers will benefit many. Do pray often, especially at those times when human beings are more receptive to prayer.

May God bless you. We are with you. We are many who are with you in your prayers today and always. My love is with you. I am Andrew. May our Father bless each of you and all of you abundantly. My love is with you.
