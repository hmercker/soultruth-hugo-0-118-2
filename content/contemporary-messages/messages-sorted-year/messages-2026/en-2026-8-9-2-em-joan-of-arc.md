# Teach with Love

August 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Joan of Arc received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Joan. I come with my brother Moses and many other souls who are redeemed in God's Divine Love and we bring our light to add to your light to help those upon the Earth who desire to find God, who seek truth, who desire to know the truest love that there is. You, true children upon the Earth, are light bringers and bring forth your light to help your brothers and sisters upon the Earth.

A greater effort is required to touch more and more lives. Coming together in prayer more frequently brings more light and brings many Celestial Angels and other spirits who walk this path together to illuminate the Earth plane, which at this time is still dark, but becoming lighter, as God pours His Love, energy and blessings more and more.

Of all the souls upon this Earth, you are among the few who know the Truth of God's Love, who know how to obtain more of this Love. It is important that you open yourselves and express this Love, that you may, by the Laws of Attraction, attract those souls who are seeking and trust yourselves. Trust yourselves when they come to you to speak the truth to them and to speak it with a great deal of love, touching their souls as well as their minds.

It is extremely important that your love be active and outgoing at all times, that you may attract these souls, for there are many, many upon this Earth who are seeking, many who desire to walk the path, but cannot find it at this time. They need someone to show them, as our brother Jesus showed you. They need someone to show them. Take this to heart, dear brothers and sisters in the Earth plane. Take this as your mission. Stay open. Bring the love to every conversation and trust yourselves that you are speaking on behalf of God, your loving Father, who sent you here with this mission.

I am your sister Joan of Arc and I thank you for coming together like this in prayer as we are being raised once more into that place where your love may shine brighter, where you will feel more peace, where you may commune with the Source of all love. May you be open to receive all of God's blessings. My love is with you, as is the love of all of us in the Celestial Kingdom. God bless you.
