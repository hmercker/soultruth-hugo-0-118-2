# Welcome All in Love and Use a Light Touch When Teaching Others This Truth

June 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

The flow of Love and blessings to you all continues to increase and intensify. I am your friend, Orion. I, as an observer of you and your planet, see that indeed the efforts that you make to bring blessings and light to you are succeeding in doing so. The efforts of humanity to struggle and find their way will indeed bring the results that are part of God's plan to awaken humanity. Many are asleep. Many do not know. Yet, in their souls there is a stirring deep within them, there is a desire for love and light, harmony, peace and all good things. Though they may not know how to attain these things, they may not know that prayer is a powerful antidote to the darkness, they do desire nonetheless. Indeed, it is our work and yours to help them find the way to fulfilling these soulful desires, clearing away the obstructions of the mind, the inhibitions that come from the mind and the emotions of fear and mistrust, these things that come from a life lived in a dark world.

Welcome in your brothers and sisters. Welcome all who are seeking, all who make that tentative step toward you. Encourage them. Help them to see that they have come to a safe place, a place where love is the currency and is the way in which you conduct yourselves, the way in which you give and serve God and serve humanity. There are great opportunities awaiting each one of you, my beloved friends. So much is out there in your world, in the universe, that is seeking connection, seeking light, seeking harmony and seeking peace. Indeed, as you open yourselves more fully to your own soul's perceptions and desires, you will come upon the realisation that you are not alone, nor are there those out there who would spurn you or distract you or cause havoc in your life. Rather, it is the opposite. There are many who wish to engage, who wish to add to, who wish to support you and to be with you upon this spiritual adventure, the awakening of your soul. God will protect you from the dark ones, those who are fearful, those who judge and those who see you with eyes of contempt and suspicion. These ones are plentiful, I know, but as you continue in your work and you continue upon this journey, you will draw by the Law of Attraction those who are good and wholesome, beautiful, those who really do wish to find the light, the light of truth.

It is your steps toward them, the way in which you conduct yourselves, what you say and how you say it, which are crucial in the first meetings, the first connections. Do not come heavily upon these individuals with doctrine and ideas that they should know this and they should know that. Rather, listen. Embrace with love. Allow them to speak. Allow them to share their stories. In time, there will be an opening for you to share yours, for you to share your truth, to give this and to pray with them, to help them understand what it is that you have experienced, to help them to see that if they open themselves to these things, they shall experience something similar, something that will take the burden from them, something that gives them joy and awakens them, enlightens them to truth.

The power of love with the power of truth added to it is a great antidote to many things that ail humanity. You have this gift, this power, this truth, this light, this Love. It is important for you to share, to connect and to be guided by God in this regard. There is so much to do, so much work, so many souls seeking and yet lost, unknowing how to find the way. It is for you to be that good friend that points the way to the awakening, the salvation, the transformation of the soul and the light of love, God's Love. It is a simple way. It does not require a lot of words and a lot of convincing. It is your countenance, your light, that is the testament to this truth. Provided you continue to nourish yourself in God's Love and to live the truth as best you can, you will be that powerful example, that beautiful teacher that will touch many souls.

Yes, indeed, there is much work to do, much for you to aspire toward, to desire in service, to be that light, truly to put aside all worries, doubts, reticence and fears and step forward. Step forward without judgement, without the intention to change another, to convert another. Rather, it is to be with another in love, whatever that might look like. When you give comfort, when you give friendship, when you offer a helping hand, when you are a light for another, then what must come is the curiosity of that individual, wondering why and how, what gives you the impetus, the strength, the power, the light, the grace that allows you to be as you are. Then you might answer their question. Yet, to come on strong to begin with is not how this world works. When there is that insistence and that power that is displayed in the insistence, then many recoil in fear and suspicion. It must be a light touch, a beautiful, beautiful gift given in love, given with humility and grace and given with gentleness that says, *"You are precious to God and precious to me, another brother, another sister in God's universe. How blessed I am to make connection, to have this opportunity to be with you and to honour you for who you are and the journey that you have taken."* This is the attitude. This is the way to connect. This is gentle and it is clear and it is loving.

Consider this, my beautiful friends, as you walk upon the Earth. Consider how you are, how you might be and how God will guide you in the direction of service, of reaching out and connecting with others. It is a lesson, it is a challenge and it is a gift. God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and I love you so, beautiful, gentle souls in the truth and light, God's Love. God bless you all. God bless you all.
