# God Has Created a Wondrous and Unique Journey for Each of You

April 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved and beautiful friends. I am Orion. Your awakening continues. I see with each one of you a brighter light. That light is growing. With that light, comes the awakening of soul consciousness, that beautiful gift that is given from the Creator of the universe to each of His beloved creations who possess a soul, a soul that is unique, individual and a permanent part of your being. Of course, there are other creatures in the universe, but they are not created as you are created. They do not possess a soul as you possess a soul. They have consciousness of some description. They are an integral part of God's creation. Yet indeed, each that has a soul throughout the universe has a purpose to fulfil, an expression given that is unique to them and the opportunity to journey into a form of consciousness that is available to the soul and the relationship between your soul and God's Soul.

You are blessed, my friends, deeply blessed. Everyone on your planet is deeply blessed, for they have the potential within them to find a special relationship with God, a conscious one, one that is changing, evolving, growing and transforming. Each of you is on a journey that shall last for eternity, my friends. Can you imagine the length of eternity? I think not, but within your soul you have a sense of that eternal journey and progress. At-onement with God will never be complete. It will continue to grow and become intense. It will consume you and yet you will not be consumed. It will bring you to awarenesses, insights and capacities to love that are beyond your imagination. You will find your way upon a path of light that will be unique to you and yet you will be joined with many others. How blessed you are, my friends, to know this journey so early in your existence, that your conscious self becomes something transformed and beautiful with the Touch of God upon you.

My beloved friends, we join hands as we journey forward. We are truly brothers and sisters. There are many, many in the universe who are willing and able to join you on this journey. Yet, the journey is not just for yourself. The journey is a spark that shall awaken others. As you continue to grow in light, so the influence, what you radiate, what you transmit in the world, will help others to realise and be inspired toward their own journey to at-onement with God. You have a deep purpose in this world, my beloved friends. In this deep purpose, you draw in many who are there to uplift you, support you and to join you upon this road. God gives the gift of support, of fellowship, of love, of knowledge, of insight and perceptions. As your soul grows, so these things grow with it. As you are upon that road and as you journey forward, then others will join you and yet more will join you until you feel like you are at one with all. That feeling will bring you immense joy and immense awareness.

How wondrous is God's creation. How wondrous you are within it. So much awaits you, my beloved friends. The journey continues. Awareness is growing. Perceptions grow as you continue to find your way to your own soul and your soul continues to find its way to God. This is how it works in the universe, my beloved friends. This connection, this relationship with the Divine, is the key to your evolution, growth, awareness, awakening and transformation. So it is for all of humanity. It is not new in the universe, where many know of this and those who have truly grown and matured spiritually are aware of how they may be in alignment with this beautiful creation that is of God and with God, who has a being, a personality, an existence within the universe.

We continue to discover, my beautiful friends, what awaits us, what is available for us, what is given abundantly to every one of us, a beautiful journey of awakening, of light, of love and joy. It is given every day. My beautiful friends, may you avail yourself each and every day to what God has so offered to you, what is available as nurturance to your soul, what is given in the grace of God's Touch, the peace that passes all understanding, the truth that shall set you free, the love that shall bring endless joy to your heart. Each of you has subscribed to this journey and indeed, I walk with you, as do many, as you continue to grow in this Love, in this Light and all that comes with it.

Blessings to you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. I love you truly. I love you and I walk with you as a friend. I walk with you in support and prayers, all good things that come in the universe, created to create further harmony, deeper love and light. God bless you all. Orion loves you.
