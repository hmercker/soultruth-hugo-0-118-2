# Creativity of the Soul

May 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Bartholomew received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Bartholomew. It is my pleasure to join you as you come together for your weekly gathering. How wonderful it is to hear your discussion, to use the opportunity in your circle to study the thousands of messages that the Celestial Angels and stellar friends have brought through mediums. This is a wise and worthy effort, for when you read these messages, not only do you consider them with your mind, but there are parts of them that speak to your soul, as you have all discovered. You receive knowledge and truth through these messages.

But how does it become wisdom within you? How does the knowledge transform into wisdom? It is by the experience of applying the knowledge, the teaching, of following through, of reaping what it is that the guidance is asking or directing you to. For when you do such, you will create an opportunity for your own experience. When one speaks from their experience, no one can take that wisdom from them. That wisdom can continue to grow and expand and flourish inwardly, so that you are able to perceive life, the creative essence of life, more deeply and more purely through the essence of your soul.

Creativity is a high manifestation, the Soul of God through your soul. Yes, when done in concert for goodness, then the strength and the far-reaching effects of these actions that are creative bless many. Most importantly, they move through you, so you are deeply blessed by having this wisdom of the soul become an expression of you. What you put out into the world, whether that be in harmony or not, causes a rippling effect. It is out there forever and can never be destroyed. Be wise, be attentive, be conscious with your thoughts, with your actions and with your deeds, for thoughts are things and they create.

My dear friends, it is good to put your considerations into what you are creating. Look over your life. What have you created? Look over your day. What did you create in your day? In the morning, look forward in your day. What do you desire to create this day? When you are present within you, attentive to what you are doing, allowing the Love within your very being to flow through you, you will be well-pleased at the outcomes. You will feel great alignment, purpose and fulfilment from all that you have done. Take good care with this idea of creativity, for it is a deep topic and one that you all partake in, one that life is based in. As you pray creatively with the longings of your soul in your own unique way, opening yourself and inviting God in, we shall be with you in prayer as we all grow and develop, expanding in the great Essence of Divine Love.

I am Bartholomew. It is a pleasure to be with you, to help guide and teach and pray with you. God bless you all.
