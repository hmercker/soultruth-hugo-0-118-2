# Be Ready and Prepared for the Imminent Shift in Humanity's Reality

August 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

The awakening is upon you, my beloveds. I come. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come once again to speak to you and to pray with you. The awakening of humanity is imminent. Those who are willing and desire to be awakened will find great joy and fulfilment in this awakening. Those who resist will find it very difficult indeed. Yet, the world must awaken, awaken to truth, awaken to the reality of what humanity has created in the world and awaken to all that which is disparaging to God and God's creation.

Many things need to be acknowledged, my beloved souls, beautiful children of God. You desire to be awakened. You desire the truth. You desire to know your true selves and to know God. You are well on your way to being awake and to know the truth. Yet, even you, with your desires, aspirations and efforts, have times when you are unaware and lack the knowledge and perception of the truth. You experience the human condition as all do upon your world and you desire to rise above this, to be in the holy grace of God's Touch, to know His Love entering your soul and to feel its power and transformation within. This is the beginning of true awakening, true being in harmony with God's Laws of Love and Creation. My beloveds, I applaud you for your efforts. Know that we come because of your efforts. We are there with you because you desire truth and you desire to be with God and to know His Love. You draw the angels close. You bring light to you, to your lives and those around you.

My beloveds, this is just the beginning. The world is on the verge of truly being transformed and healed of these transgressions toward your beautiful planet, toward God's creation and toward God. Much of this darkness is proliferated through ignorance. Many do not know what they do and how they denigrate their lives and the Earth itself. This awakening is necessary so that humanity may look with clarity, with honesty, at what is happening in the world and how it all is so derogatory to God's laws and God's desires. God has given each free will. The choice to look or not to look is given. Those who will not look will suffer the consequences of continued ignorance. Those who desire to look, who are brave enough, strong enough to look, will reap the benefits and rewards of God's blessings upon them to help them overcome the conditions that continue to burden humanity.

Although the majority will refuse to see, those who do look, those who are willing, will become a formidable force for change in the world. Indeed, those in the light will rise up and bring truth, bring healing and bring comfort to the great majority of humanity that will suffer from the consequences of this ignorance and darkness. God does not wish this upon humanity, but humanity has wished this upon itself through the exercise of free will, desire and all that humanity does that is contrary to God. The consequences build. The tension arises and the darkness is great.

We are here, my beloveds. The entire force of the Celestial Kingdom is on the ready to step forward and to intervene, to bring light where light is needed, to help those who are ignorant to realise, to be educated, to understand and to come to the conclusion that each thought and each action is important and adds to the great conglomerate of human endeavour and another choice is given. What will each individual think? What will each individual do in order to bring harmony to the world and to themselves? Each soul desires God, for this is the natural inclination of the soul to be with its Creator. Yet, that understanding is so weak and feeble within many that they do not feel and know the power of that desire and that need within the soul to be with God.

This is where, my beloveds, you come to share the truth. You come to ignite within these individuals the understanding, the awareness of their souls' desires. In this, you are God's instruments and we will be with you to help you inspire others. Yet, first and foremost, you must be well planted upon the Divine Path and know this great Truth as your life. This way, when the time comes of great change and the shift of reality that is inevitable, the truth revealed, then you are at the ready. You are open and receptive to God's Will. Continue to pray for this great gift, my beloveds, the gift of God's Love. This will strengthen you. This will awaken you and, in time, transform you completely. This is the Truth that I taught. This is the Truth that stands for eternity. This is the Truth that is throughout the universe, the Truth of God's Love and its power to change, to heal, transform and to bring each individual in harmony with creation and with love.

Are you ready to proceed as God's emissaries of truth and channels of love? We are ready. We stand beside you. Each one of you has an angel by their side. Each one of you may be blessed accordingly, in accordance to your desire, in accordance to that which you hold dear in your heart and your minds. What is your reality? What is your perspective? What is your desire? Consider these things, my beloveds, for they are truly important at this time in the juncture of human existence. The survival of humanity hangs in the balance. It is crucial that those who are strong in light and truth may have the weight to tip the scales toward greater light, to bring humanity from the brink of its own destruction, chaos and darkness.

It is a formidable task, my beloveds. It is one that is not easily attained or brought forth into the world, but you have God on your side, your angel friends, your stellar friends and all the bright spirits, all the efforts being made to help change the course of humanity's trajectory from darkness to light. It is a tremendous effort and will continue to strengthen as God brings His blessings forward, as God gives you what you require to be those instruments of light. Have faith in this, my beloveds. Have faith in yourself, in your soul, in your relationship with God, soul to Soul. We will proceed forward. We will be ready to confront the darkness and the evil in the world. You must be strong. You must have great faith and you must be a channel of love in the world. God has given you great blessings, my beloveds. He does so, so that you may give and be a channel of blessings for others. This is part of the flow of His Love and the Truth of His power to transform and to heal.

You all possess this Truth. It lives within you, a light burning, a flame within you. May its warmth warm others, warm many and bring comfort to many. For many are feeling lost, hollow and empty within and they are seeking solace and comfort. Who will give this to them? Who of you are willing to step forward and be a comfort to the lost ones? Every time you do so, you do God's work. You have an angel assisting you and working with you to bless that other individual, to be a channel of light in the world. Much is being given, my beloveds. The battle is not lost, but it shall be gained in light and light shall proliferate in the world, bringing peace, comfort and truth.

This will happen, I assure you. Yet, as you apply yourselves and desire to be God's instruments, then you quicken the process, you quicken the journey and you add comfort and peace where there would otherwise be great turmoil and suffering. My beloveds, stand forth for the truth and be that light. God will bless you as you do so and we will be with you as you make those efforts in light. God's Love for you is greater than anything you can imagine or understand at this time. Open yourselves to this gift and blessing and you will truly be blessed in these moments together. I am with you always and will continue to walk with each of you and encourage you upon this path of service, light and truth. We are with you. The angels of God are with you. All is being blessed at this moment. We rise up into that great Hall of Peace and know its blessings.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus. I am with you and I love you. God bless you.
