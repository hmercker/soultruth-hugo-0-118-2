# Maintain a High Intensity of Desire in Your Prayers and Purpose

July 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. The dark conditions in your world continue to intensify the many challenges each of you faces in this world of yours. I have heard the term *"survival mode."* Indeed, many of you are in spiritual survival mode, seeking to tread water in the positive and light-filled conditions that you are in and that we and your friends in the Celestial Kingdom and elsewhere work diligently to help you maintain that condition. What is most important is that you strive beyond that condition, that stasis of light, into something brighter and more powerful, rather than treading water.

We wish for you to make strides ahead and beyond where you are now. That takes a great deal of effort on our part and yours. In prayer, you must be intense and frequent. In attitude and thought and desiring for the highest, In soul condition, you must continue to pray for this great gift of Love from the Creator. For when you can bring all of these elements into alignment, they bring together a culmination of intention, desire and action that will propel you forward, but you cannot take this for granted. You cannot slip into routine that lacks intense desire to be with God, a desire for it to inhabit your souls, desire to make that connection between you and your Creator. Although the conditions of your world continue to detract and distract, your effort to resist these conditions and your efforts to be above them must not only equal the intensity of the distractions, but go beyond them, so that you are indeed progressing swiftly.

For the time grows short. You have heard this many times. Indeed, the time for you to step forward as God's instruments is coming very close indeed. Those remnants of darkness, of inharmony, of lack of love, must dissipate, must be healed and corrected. Working on your mental condition is important, but it is not the most important. The most important is working upon the condition of your soul, cleansing, purifying, awakening and bringing you toward greater transformation. This is what is important, and then making an effort to serve, to be out in the world as light. This helps to exercise your gifts and abilities. It hones them and perfects them. You are required to do many things to step upon this path in concerted ways and efforts to make yourself pure in thought and filled with the desire of the soul to be in light.

You cannot take these things for granted, my friends. It is important, but do you consider these elements required for progression each and every day, that they may be your priority, that they may be your life? If you are to accomplish the gifts that you pray for, direct manifestation, light, healing, blessings for others and a pure channel for God, these things cannot come because you utter a prayer and say, *"this is what I would like, God. This is what my goal is."* This is all very well. Indeed, for your mind's comfort, you need to say these things, but it is in the action. It is in moving forward day by day. It is in introspection and expressing yourself in concerted ways that are in harmony with Laws of Love that you will progress.

I remind you of this, my friends, do not be lackadaisical in your efforts. Be strong and purposeful. Be faithful and desirous of that which will heal and transform the soul. Seek out God. Seek out God in your mind and your soul, in all levels of consciousness. May your awareness of God be there and truly be a part of you. It is not easy in this world of yours, but it is what is required if you are to accomplish your goals. It cannot be done any other way. When you put the effort in, you will receive the benefits, but those efforts must be consistent, heartfelt and intense. They must be full of the desire to be truly in alignment with God.

Here we are, my friends. I speak as one who has always been redeemed in the Father's Love, as you would describe it. It is easy for me to tell you to follow that road, one who has never struggled as you have, one who knows the truth from my very conception to the many years that I have existed on my planet. I know these things. They come not only as second nature, but first nature. They are who I am and they are who my brethren are on my planet. Your planet is destined to also be in that condition. Yet, it begins with the sober few, those who truly see what they must do and act upon it with great intensity and desire.

You will, my friends. You will. You have all the support, all that is required to get to that place, despite the inhibitions and conditions of this world. I am here to help you, as are many. We will continue day by day in that great sojourn toward truth, light and love. Indeed, our wishes are that your strides will be great, swift, deliberate and powerful. Continue on in your efforts, my friends. Continue in your prayers. Continue in your self-reflections. Continue to see and know the truth and you will arrive at your destination. At that place you will come to know these are only the first steps toward eternity, but the most important steps of all.

God bless you, my friends, Orion is with you and loves you. God bless you.
