# Love is the Answer

September 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Confucius received by Al Fike

Indeed, God does love you, my beloveds. I am Confucius. You speak of love. You affirm love. You live love. Is this not the remedy that the world requires for spiritual sustenance and upliftment?

It is love and you, beautiful souls that you are, have come to recognise that this is what is required in your life, in your being and you see that this is what is required in the world, in the universe. It is love, so elusive and yet so powerful, so wonderful and yet many people fear it. They do not understand the power of love. They are afraid because love brings vulnerability. Love brings honesty. Love requires these things to be real within the individual.

There are those afraid to go beyond that which restricts them, the armour of the soul, which continues to bring isolation and brings pain, sadness and loneliness. The mind, the material mind, continues to defend and make their world in their minds safe. Yet, what are they truly feeling and experiencing in their life? You, my beloveds, you are opened to greater experience, greater life and you feel the joy of this, the beauty of it, as God's Love continues to bring that opening, that realisation to your life, your reality, as you call it. You must share this truth with the beleaguered world, so defended, so unhappy and yet you may teach them how to fly, my little birds, how to know the beauty and wonderment of the soul soaring to God and feeling its place within God's universe.

Oh, my beloved and beautiful souls of Earth, you may be used to liberate humanity. For you have the key of that liberation. You understand the needs of your soul, you understand what is required in the world to heal and to bring peace and harmony. You carry a great truth and yet many do not want to hear you, understand what it is that is freely being spoken. Yet, I tell you as you continue to walk in the world and sing that beautiful melody of truth, you will touch many souls.

Seeds will be planted in many souls and in time, as God continues to nurture this world, those seeds will sprout and the realisations will come. Yet, if nothing is spoken, nothing is shared. No truth is set upon the winds of human thought. Who will awaken? How will they awaken? They may feel the Touch of God, but have no understanding and no way of applying this truth to their lives.

You must instruct them, instruct them well. Give them hope. Show them the way to their salvation. For without brave souls such as yourself, how will the world change? I know the winds buffet against you, the judgements pour upon you, at times anger and frustration with you, but you must be steadfast, beloveds, and strong and to go into that wind, that condition of life and be that light and speak and demonstrate the Truth of God's Love. For then the catalysts, the spark, will set the world aflame.

It will cleanse and purify and bring peace in time. Are you willing, beloved souls, to walk as a light boldly? Do not hide away. Do not worry about the judgements, the slings and the arrows sent toward you. No, for God is with you and you will be protected. Yet, it requires bravery, your desire and will to set forth into a hostile world and yet you bring love, you bring the remedy that will bring peace.

It will unfold according to God's plan. It will bring all of you into the flow and the purpose that is God's plan. Have faith in this, beloved souls. Have faith in your own instrumentality, the strength and beauty, the light and love that you carry, the wisdom that you may offer to the world. For light shall overcome darkness. This is a law and the light that you carry is formidable and shall indeed overcome these terrible conditions that humanity must endure every moment.

This is not what God wishes for His children. This is not what shall be. For in the time to come, the cleansing will take place and the darkness will capitulate and light will reign in the world. Have faith in this, beloved souls. Have faith in what God will work through you. The light that shall shine, the wisdom that shall be shared, the love to embrace your brothers and sisters. There is nothing to hold you from this purpose, this ministry of love, other than your own fears of walking forward into that dreadful wind, afraid that you will be blown into oblivion, that you will be rejected and reviled.

Can you release these fears and allow the joy of God's Love to replace these things that are not truly of your soul, but manifestations of your mind and imaginings? The reality of Love must be your breath, your life, your being. Allow it to be so and you will find your way upon the path that God has designated for you and it is magnificent. It is purposeful and it will bring much to your brothers and sisters, much relief and comfort and truth, where they themselves may come to know love as you know love. May come to be comforted as you are comforted. May come to that place of peace as you have come.

This is how the world will be changed, my beloved, beautiful friends. Not through will, not through the mind, but through Love, a great healing and transforming agent that is God's Essence shared and received. This will change the world. Be ever close to God, beloved souls, be in the grace of God and all else will follow as you receive His gift of Love. You will be guided. The openings of your soul will inform your steps.

The gifts of your soul will manifest in all its beauty and glory and power to change the world. God bless you, beloved souls. I am with you in your efforts, as are so many. Know this. Rely upon this. Be strong because you have so much strength around you, light and all that is good is with you and shall never leave.

God bless you, beloveds. Confucius loves you. Oh, little birds, little birds, fly to God and sing your melodies and God will rejoice in what you express from your beautiful souls. God bless you and keep you in His Love.
