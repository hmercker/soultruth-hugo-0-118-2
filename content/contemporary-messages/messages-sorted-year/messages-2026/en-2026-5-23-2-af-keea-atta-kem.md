# How This Blessing of Love Creates Conscious Awareness of Your True Self

May 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

North Vancouver Monthly Circle, North Vancouver, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours. I am Keea atta Kem. I come to assure you that as you continue to open yourself to this gift, it is having a profound impact upon your being. You are being changed by it. That change starts within your soul, awakening ever so slowly to the blessings of this great gift of Love. Though the mind may be rigid and predominant in your consciousness, in your daily life, something else is taking hold, something powerful and transformative. When you allow this power, this blessing, this awakening to come to your conscious self, you will find that your attitudes, what you desire, what you do in the world will change radically.

It is not that your mind has set forth an agenda, an idea as to what is right and what is wrong. Rather, it is this mind of the soul, the awareness of the soul, that is giving you the information, the guidance, the perspective and the inspiration as you deal with your daily life. This is God's gift. Even a few drops have their effect. When you continue to receive this, the effects will be equal to the reception, the quantity of this gift of Love. You will not lose yourself in this, my beloveds, but you will gain much. You retain who you are, your personality and much of your perspective on life. Yet, how you are with others, how you are with yourself, how you walk in the world will reflect the power and Light of this Love that enters into your soul.

The soul is the core of your being. It is who you are. It is important that you understand that you are more than just your mindful conscious self, your material self. You are much more than this. You are a being that was created by God, starting with the soul, adding to that soul an identity that is the form of a spirit body and a material body. As you come to realise that these manifestations, these experiences that you are having in life are layered and that indeed the physical sensations are but the tip of the iceberg compared to the spirit and its experiences in life and the soul. Each one has its own experience and what can take place in a rather fractured and unknown level of experience in all of these places and categories, when it becomes integrated, oh, how your perceptions, your experience in life, will grow and expand in many ways. The proper intensity and power of these experiences will begin with soul awareness and be complemented by spirit and body.

This is a truly spiritual perspective and one that can be attained when you receive the great gift of God's Love, for in empowering the soul you are starting a process of transformation and awakening. As you continue in this process, the shifting of perspectives continues to awaken you to something more, something different, something greater, something more beautiful, something more joyful, something more harmonious with God's Laws of Love. You will come to know the Creator in such a personal way that you will find great comfort there, great Love and great wisdom as God continues to awaken your soul, as He sends this blessing forth into your soul.

May you truly find yourself, my friends. It exists there within, often hidden and with many neglected, but as you continue to practise this way of Divine Love, you will empower the soul in ways that you cannot possibly imagine, but will experience them first hand and know the power of it, the beauty of it and the grace of it. As God touches your soul deeply, He heals your soul of its impediments, stark memories and difficult conditions. As this happens, more joy rises up. A deeper perspective on life comes to your conscious self and you will come to know us, your angel friends. For we are eager to know you and to be with you. There is so much that can happen to a soul who seeks to be transformed by this great gift of Love from God and one of the greatest gifts is eternal life, not a life lived in the body, but a life lived in the spirit and the soul. For that Essence of God given will never diminish, will exist in you forever. How wondrous is this?

Indeed, the progress that is made by the soul, as the soul continues to seek the Father's Love, will be eternal. What you perceive and experience now will be very, very different from what may come in the future, what will be the result of your efforts, your prayers and your actions, the applying of this Truth to your lives. You will change and you will have great appreciation and gratitude for the changes that may take place within you. Your journey will be unique, because you are unique. Your understanding and experiences will come to you in their own way, fashion and timing. As you progress, as the barriers fall away, God will be there as a palpable, true and powerful ally in your life. God's Love is forever and will be forever given. May you find and know the foreverness of this gift and find the great peace that surpasses all understanding as you are with God and your soul is ignited in Love.

God bless you, my friends. I am Keea atta Kem. I come in love and I come to assure you we are here, that God is here to bless you. Open your souls wide, beloveds. Feel the yearning there and express it to God. God bless you, beloveds. Keea loves you. God bless you.
