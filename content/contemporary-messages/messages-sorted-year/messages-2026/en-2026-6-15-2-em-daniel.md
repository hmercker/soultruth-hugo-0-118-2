# We Work Together

June 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

Daniel received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Daniel of the Bible. I come to join you in your prayers. You may feel my strength, my strength of soul. I bless you with this gift of strength of longings for God's Divine Love, strength of purpose to be at one with God, strength of conviction to know Truth, all of these things you have prayed for and many more things.

The Father has sent me to you to help these qualities grow in your soul, for that is how it works. You ask God and God sends us. We come close and your souls are stirred and open and feel the energy. With love that we express to you, your soul desires these qualities.

Each of the Celestial Angels come to you at various times, bringing his or her own quality of soul to strengthen that particular quality within you and these qualities bring you closer and closer to the Heavenly Father. You see, we work together, God, we redeemed souls and you developing souls. We are here for you in every way possible, every way that you allow us to come close. We are here for you. All you need to do is ask and ask God, for God will send exactly the right soul to you.

Sincerity in your requests, reception in your soul and desire. Desire is the key. What do you desire today, dear children? What do you desire today and throughout your day, throughout your nights? What is it that you truly desire? Allow your desires to reach to God and allow God to answer that prayer, that desire.

Delve deep into your souls, dear children, so that you can come to know what it is you truly desire and what your longings are. Open up and allow God to respond and invite your angel friends to come and just the right, the perfect angel will come and be with you. This is not a mental exercise. This is a soul exercise. This is truth between your soul and God's Soul. Delve deeply. Open, receive and allow all that the Father has for you to help you become a fully redeemed soul. We could use a few more of you over here in the Celestial Heavens to help your brothers and sisters on the Earth, so delve deeply, search for the truth within yourselves and then ask God for the Truth that He has to give to you and the gifts that He has to give to you.

I am your brother Daniel. It is my honour and my desire to be here with you today and to serve my Heavenly Father in this way, for His Love that flows into this circle of souls is great and I love you all. You see, it is my pleasure and honour to be here with you today. Let us travel a little higher, a little closer to the Source of all Love and receive all that He is giving to us now. May God's blessings fall deeply into your souls and may you grow in strength, love, wisdom and truth this day. I am your brother Daniel. I will pray with you now and receive the Father's Love in greater and greater abundance.
