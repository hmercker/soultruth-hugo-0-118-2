# Awaken, Receive and Become the Light

April 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come once again to address my beloved brothers and sisters. The door to God's Love is open to you. You have discovered the greatest and highest gift that God has to give to His children. I urge you to continue to take advantage of this knowledge that you possess, to awaken more fully to what this gift will bring to you, to allow yourself to be immersed within the holy Touch of God's Love, so that it may do its work, it may change your soul and transform it into what it is meant to be. For when God gives you a soul, much of it is a reflection of the potential that is available within, but without this gift, this Touch of Love, this Essence of God, that potential is unrealised, the seed does not germinate and become what it is meant to be.

Each of you continues to ask for God's ministrations, the great Divine Gardener who must water and tend to His beloved and beautiful souls, His Garden. You are willing, open and desire this ministration, so you will feel and know the results of it. Yet, that is not the entire story, my beloveds. God requires you, when you are realised within the Love of God and understand it from your very heart and soul, to bring that Love forward, to allow the gift of that Love to be shared, to be an example of that Love, to not hide away in some corner and not express and be that beautiful example for God and for humanity. For it is in that expression, that true example, that the world will know the power of this gift and what it might do for each and every soul upon this world and the world of spirit.

Those who dwell upon this world, they have a great gift, the gift of free will. Also within this world are the possibilities of knowing many things and experiencing many things. In this way, it is a powerful nexus point to teach and demonstrate the Truth of the Father's Love, for here you intermingle with many others, some in darkness, some in light. Yet, indeed, all are creations of God, souls with great potential. When you are guided by God to be with another, to have that connection, the flow of love given, there is purpose in this and potential as well. Please understand that every day brings a gift, a possibility of being a light in the world for another and for many others. Do not squander that gift, my beloveds, but be my disciples of truth, be God's harbingers of light and truth. In this way, you will do God's work. I know that you, each of you, prays for this, asks to be used as an instrument of God. This may be given in the fullness of your desire and willingness to step forward in the world, to truly be God's instruments in the world.

My beloveds, there is much work to do, for the field has been sown and God awaits the harvest. Yet, He relies on each of you to be that instrument, that gardener, that will help nurture the seeds and help nurture the truth in the world. Are you willing to toil for God and for humanity? It is well needed, my beloved friends, for look around you. There is so much suffering and chaos, so many who are lost and yet you hold the key, the key to redemption, the key to light and truth. You yourself were once lost, but have found your way into the light. It is for you, my beloveds, to show the way to others. I know you seek the truth in your heart. You seek out God's blessings. Indeed, God responds accordingly. What great blessings await those who bring this truth and love to others, for this becomes a jewel on your crown. It is a blessing that you carry and will carry for all eternity. Are we not destined to carry those who are willing, show them the way to the portals of heaven and how this may be reached?

My beloveds, each carries the truth in their heart. You glow with God's Love within your soul. Let it shine. Let the truth be uttered through your lips. Let the love be expressed in your daily life. God will do the rest, as He has done and shall do. My beloveds, may a great blessing be yours, an awakening of the soul, a Pentecost, a great flowing of Love to awaken you further and to strengthen you and enlighten you, so that you may truly be that light in the world and have the strength and wherewithal to be God's channels, true and pure and beautiful.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and I am your friend, always. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you, my beloved friends. God bless you all.
