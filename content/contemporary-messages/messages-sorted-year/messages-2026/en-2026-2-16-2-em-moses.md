# Fulfill Your Promise to God

February 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Moses received by Elizabeth Mack

God bless you, dear children. God bless you. I am your brother. I am Moses and I come to you with my love, my great Love that has filled my soul, redeemed it and continues to flow in, as I love you, as I love, and as my Father, my maker, my Creator, fills my soul even more and will never stop filling my soul with His Love.

What a future, what an existence, what a joy, what peace, what happiness to know that forever we will exist and we will exist as Love, at one with the Creator and the Source of all Love. Who could be sad? Who could cry, except tears of joy, to know this blessing, this gift, that our Master brought to us long after I lived on the Earth, long after many of us lived on the Earth?

We, who came before him, struggled with what was even then the human condition, which only became worse with each generation, until our Father blessed us with the presence of the soul of Jesus and, with him, the opening of the doors to the Celestial Heavens. Now, even though there are many, many souls who have been redeemed, there are still souls who have not yet reached the Celestial Heavens and those who have not even become aware of what the Master brought when he came into the earthly flesh.

It is now your turn, dear children, to receive this Love in abundance, to transform your souls, to live this Love. Yes, it takes a while, because of the Earth conditions, because of mindsets and habits that exist within you. Believe me when I say those habits can be reversed. Those beliefs can be shown to be false and even the mind can become dominated by the soul while you are still in the flesh. Believe me, children, when I tell you that with Divine Love, everything that is of God is possible and it is your turn to be on this Earth to spread the love with your brothers and sisters, as I once spread the laws that were presented to me, teaching people how to live in harmony. Now you have the opportunity to teach the greatest law, this Law of Divine Love, through your prayers, through your thoughts, through your speech and through your love and behaviour. What a gift you have been given. What a promise to fulfil for God. Such joy I feel at the prospect of all that you will do, sometimes knowingly and sometimes unaware.

God bless you, you special children. God bless you and all the others who are living this Love, who are receiving this Love, who are learning about this Love. What an opportunity. What a gift. May the Father bless you continuously, uphold you and keep you above this human condition to the greatest extent possible. May you love as greatly as the Love in your soul. Continue to pray, dear ones. Continue to pray for this Love. Continue to grow in this Love and learn to be this Love. My love is with you. God bless you. It has been a privilege, a privilege to come to you today. God bless you and may all of the angels surround you and keep you this day and night in the Love of God.

2026-02-16-EM-Moses (G)
