# Called to Serve: Walking the Path of Love

March 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Faith. Oh, there is so much work to do in your world, so many cross-currents of thought and action that are not in harmony with God's laws, so much suffering and pain, so much confusion and misunderstanding, so much fear. Yet, you have created an oasis of light in the world, a place where fear cannot reside, for you have replaced that with love. You have put your faith forward, trusting in God's Will and God's Love, so that you create light all around you, a place where fear cannot exist, where truth is upheld and where love flows. This is the antidote to all the darkness.

You, my beloved friends, are called upon to step forward in light in the world, for you are qualified. You are endowed with wisdom, truth and love. When you pray to be used as God's instruments, God will indeed answer that prayer, open the door to service and show you the way in which you may serve. It may not be in the way that your mind expects or can readily join with, but it is the way of God. With humility, grace and desire, you may be a part of God's plan. You may truly be in that flow of His purposeful Love and intention for the world. What you do may be simple or it may be great, but in terms of the weight in which God puts upon your service, it is the same. For when one walks out into the world in love and does those small things that reflect love, is the love any greater than when one speaks to many and reaches out to many others, speaking the words of truth to them?

When you walk in love, when you enter into the world with love and that love is expressed in some simple yet important way, you are reaching the souls of those around you. You are God's children, His instruments. Indeed, in your lives, there may be simple tasks or there may be grandiose tasks. Both may happen and be a part of your service. Do not judge this. Do not expect that which gratifies the mind and the ego, but with faith, expect that whatever you are guided to do, it will be the right thing, the thing that God wants of you. You will be blessed as a result of your actions and your response to that guidance. Every day is filled with opportunities. Every day is a gift that God has given you. In that day, much service may be accomplished. It may be small, delicate and subtle, or it may be powerful and obvious. Whatever it is, you have been called by God to serve humanity in the ways and means that are part of His plan.

Walk upon that path of service, my beloveds, the Path of Love, of God's Love. In this way, all will unfold and flow in accordance with God's Will. We are all God's servants. We all serve in many ways and different ways, being unique as we are, but truly lights, lights in the world, lights around the world, lights in the spirit world, light everywhere. This one is important and this is God's intention. I encourage you to serve, to be humble in service, to find those simple ways that you may touch another soul. In this will be great joy, gratification, greater love will flow and beautiful light will build.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Faith. Oh, I love you so. I am here to serve, as you are all of us together in this great flow, to dissipate the darkness and to bring forth the light. God bless you, my friends. Faith loves you. God bless you.
