# Miriam Speaks of Her Progression and Home

April 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Tell City Retreat, Tell City, IN, USA

Miriam received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Miriam, sister of Moses. I am a Divine Angel, redeemed soul, redeemed by God's Divine Love and placed high into the Celestial Heavens. Giving up my brother, I never truly gave up. As he floated down the river I prayed for him. I prayed that God would allow us to see each other again and he became the one who freed us. It was not such a sacrifice once we were all free.

This helped me… once I crossed over into the spirit world and where I progressed. Those things within me that had to change were not so difficult because, in the end, I knew that I would be a redeemed soul. It took a while. Sacrifices were part of it. Then, the Love came, the Love of God filling my soul, changing the essence of who I was while retaining who I am. I am my Father's daughter.

You too, will have struggles, but remember when you enter the Kingdom of God's Celestial Heavens, all your struggles will have been worth it. My Father's house is most beautiful, loving beyond comprehension. The door swings open and there stands our Master Jesus to greet each one majestically. He gives his love to each one and welcomes them home to their homes. So filled with Love is this environment that one considers the children upon the Earth as they need assistance, but there is no remorse regarding having left the Earth, having left the spirit world, having left behind those things that are struggles. His Love is everything. His Love is fulfilling, completing and draws us closer. Our love for Him is eternal. You will see one day. You will see how complete we are in this Love.

May God bless you and bring you forward, closer to your future home in this Love. I am Miriam, lover of God, caretaker of many children. My love is with you.
