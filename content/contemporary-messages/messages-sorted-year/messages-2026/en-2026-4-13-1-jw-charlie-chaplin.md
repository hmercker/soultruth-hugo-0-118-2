# It is time now for us to collaborate

April 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Charlie Chaplin received by Jimbeau Walsh

I am here, Charles, your brother in Christ. Charlie Chaplin.

The last time I came, I spoke a bit about restoring humor, about its roots as a word that had to do with healing and how important receiving the love of God is on your journey, that others may be healed, that humor is restored. The world at times seems to be a tragicomedy, and from a distance it appears to be folly, yet it also appears to be in the flow of God’s love.

When I made my film, The Great Dictator, I was very focused on wanting to expose the idiocy of the lust for power, the arrogance, and really the stupidity and sleep of those who think they rule the world. And so, most of that film is humorous anecdotes and exposing, as I wished to do, the ridiculousness of those who follow that path and those who follow those who follow that path. But because humor often comes out of deprivation, tragedy, and difficulty, as I had in my childhood, and of course, which every soul experiences, whether they are rich or poor, to a lesser or greater extent, it was necessary to bring humor into a very dangerous, violent, and cruel situation that always exists somewhere on the earth. And so in the end, I wanted to (as you may all be somewhat familiar with) give a soliloquy on loving each other and moving beyond the limitations, the barriers, the hatreds, the illusions of one people or one person feeling superior to another, and so I ended with a plea.

And you may know as well that when I passed into spirit, I did have some baggage, and so I allowed my soul to go through the darkness that was in it, into seeking the forgiveness of not only those I had maligned, but my own self. My mother, my dear mother, a celestial angel, became my guide, as you may know, and because of the effulgence of light around her, beyond even a mother’s love for her son and my love for her, she had a magnetism that could not be denied. And so, I truly began to pray for the love, and I am now quite a progressed spirit on the precipice of the celestial realm, and so grateful and so wishing to communicate love and humor and guidance to whomever would accept it.

Now that you have come on board and I have come on board, it is time for us to collaborate and truly bring the love of God, sustainability, brotherhood and sisterhood, and the path of the soul to the earth through stories, through film, through music, through sanctuary. For each one of you, as you have been told, has gifts. What a great thing to be able to serve others with those gifts that you have and to collaborate with each other, to manifest what you see for the healing of the earth, for the spreading, the dissemination of the truth, which simply begins with, *“Let us pray, let us ask the Creator to bless us with His love, to touch our souls”*. And then you follow your guidance, and you feel the flow of grace in everyday living, more and more, greater and greater, as your being is transformed, as your soul is changed, as the prayer goes, from the human to the divine, from the finite to the infinite, and I would say even from the darkness into the great light of God’s love.

So, be of good cheer, for you will be remembered for the love that you gave, for the joy that you spread, for the humor that you restored. Be not afraid to step forward in the love of God in creating a better place from your hearts on this earth. I thank you and my brother for taking this message. I’m delighted as always to come and share with you. May we all receive a great inflowing of God’s love and spread the joy. I told my brother I’d get him a T-shirt that said, *“God’s love and more”* and I will save (the more) that for the T-shirt. God bless you all and thank you. I am your brother in the love of God, a follower of the Master, and your eternal friend.

I am Charlie Chaplin. God bless you.
