# Being God’s Instruments of Love in the World

June 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Barbara Davies received by Al Fike

Blessings to you, beloved souls. I am so happy to be here. The joy in my heart is great. I am Barbara. Indeed, all of you are beautiful lights. You are dedicated to service, to love, to truth, truth of the highest. Indeed, together you do make a formidable force in the world, a beautiful channel of light and blessings from God to others. As you continue to pray together in this unity and harmony, what emanates, what goes out into the world, is powerful and beautiful, filled with light and grace. Though you may not know all who are affected by this, rest assured that many are benefiting from your prayers.

Continue to dedicate yourselves in this way to be God's instruments, channels of love in the world. When you venture out into the world, the work does not end, it merely continues and is in a different form than when you sit here in circle. There is so much that can be accomplished by your physical presence in the world. When you are with others, when you are in crowds, when you continue to make relationships and connections with others, you are doing God's work, for your soul filled with the Father's Love, continuing to be transformed by it and awakened by it, is exercising its beautiful condition and ability to be a channel of love for others. You may not speak. You may not do anything in particular that is of a physical help or benefit to another, but your very presence, my beloved friends, is a beautiful thing. Not that I am suggesting that you hold back from giving or doing for another. This is not my intention at all, but there are many ways that God can give to His children, many ways indeed. Each of you has special gifts, unique gifts and God is able to use those gifts to bless others in many ways you are not aware.

Be of great faith, my beloved friends. Know that every moment you may be used as God's channels, whether you are in a Circle of Light, such as this, whether you are doing mundane chores, whether you are with others in a variety of circumstances and situations, this does not preclude God using you in many different ways to bless many different individuals. Be of great cheer, my beloved friends, and of great faith, that even though you may not see and know and hear the results, the actions that are being taken through your instrumentality, your gifts, it is happening nonetheless and each of you are being used in many, many ways. Together, you make a formidable channel of love in the world, adding light where there is darkness, bringing comfort where there is pain, loneliness, confusion and isolation. Much can be given by the stalwart and beautiful instruments of God, who come together as you do day in and day out to pray for the blessing of His Love and to pray to be an instrument of His doing and His Will.

Continue to be open to God's Will and to God's plan. You will in time come to see the intricacies, the complexities of how God is able to use you, how you are able to be that instrument, that lever for light, that beautiful flow and blessings for many. Be humble, kind, open and loving. Embrace your brothers and sisters. Be true to your purpose. Be true to your soul's abilities, gifts and purpose. You will know joy. You will feel fulfilled. You will be true and knowledgeable of the Father's Touch upon you.

God bless you, my friends. There is much work to do. I ask you to continue in your work every day, every moment of every day. Pray that you be used as God's instruments and God will respond and act upon your desire, your efforts and prayers to be His channels of love, to be His instruments, angels on the Earth. God bless you, beloved souls. I am Barbara. I love you and I work with you all. I hope that many of you will feel my presence and know that I am close and not only me, but many others. Many other angels are working diligently through you and around you to bring greater light to the world. God bless you, my friends. God bless you in all that you do and all that you are. I love you. God bless you.
