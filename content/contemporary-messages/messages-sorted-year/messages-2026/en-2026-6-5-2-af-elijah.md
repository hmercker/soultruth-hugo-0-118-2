# The Time Comes When God’s Will Shall Be Done

June 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Johnson City Retreat, Johnson City, NY, USA

Elijah received by Al Fike

Blessings to you, beloved souls. I am Elijah. The world is poised, waiting for the change that is coming and for the awakening that must be. For the conditions of the world have come to such a terrible place and intensity that it will cause a fracture in the consciousness of humanity. Through that place that is fractured will come the blessings of God, the awakening that must be. The world is poised. In each soul, there is an awareness that what is at this moment, at this time, is not what God intends. For the time comes when God's Will will be done, beloved souls. Indeed, are you not being prepared for this? Are you not awakening yourself to the possibilities and potentials of your souls? Are you not travelling that road of purification and transformation that comes with the blessings of the Father's Love?

God is preparing humanity for a great change. Ahead of this, God prepares each of you and many others who will help usher in that change, bring humanity back to God and bring greater harmony to the world. There is much happening under the surface, roiling about, shifting and changing. Many of you feel this and are aware. Many of you wonder. I say to you, beloved souls, children of God, allow His great Love to infill you to such great abundance that you will not wonder, but you will know. You will not speculate, but you will be guided. You will find purpose within you that you did not know existed and that you were not aware of, gifts that will manifest, blessings that will come, influence that will pour through you like gushing waters flowing, flowing out into the world. Be God's channels of love and of light. Indeed, embrace the truth and do not shirk from your responsibilities, for God has tended His flock. God has shown you the way. God has intentions and plans for each one of you.

Listen. Have great faith and allow God to utilise the gifts that you have and the purpose that He has instilled within your souls. Great purpose, beloveds, great purpose. Indeed, understanding that purpose will be revelatory and will change you. There is great work ahead, beloved souls. Continue to prepare and continue to be in alignment with God. Seek out His blessing of Love and all else will fall into place. You will know. You will understand. You will know joy in the understanding. You will be strengthened and in resolve with the purpose that God will show you. It will be clear and unambiguous. Be strong, my beloveds. Be of good faith and know that you are truly loved.

I am Elijah. I pray with you this day for the great awakening to come, for all to change and transform into light, harmony, love and peace. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you all.
