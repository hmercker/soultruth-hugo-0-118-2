# Following Guidance to Come Together Makes for Great Possibilities

March 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Matthew received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am Matthew. The work continues, does it not? The efforts, the coming together of desire and action as a result of guidance, are making for great possibilities and great work to be accomplished. We are very pleased with your efforts, beloved children of God. We are very pleased with your desires and your humility, your loving grace expressed toward one another and the goodness that is here amongst you. It is important that this harmony be sustained and that the work that you may do together, the potentials that come from your gifts and your collaborations doing God's work and God's Will, will continue to flourish and awaken in all its forms and potentials.

There is so much that needs to be done. Indeed, there are few workers on the path. What you have together, what you share in this beautiful potential and gathering of gifts is extraordinary. Our wish is that you will see this for what it is, appreciate it and act upon it, for it does require your will, my friends. It requires your desire and the enacting of your desire in conjunction and in harmony with God's Will. When these things are in alignment and the power of your gifts and collaborations come together in harmony, then God might do beautiful things and many will benefit from them.

My beloved and beautiful friends, children of God, do not underestimate the power of God and His angels to orchestrate and to bring together various opportunities and scenarios that will take advantage and be a clear expression of the potentials and the gifts that are a part of you, my beloved friends. There is much work to do and, indeed, the work continues at a different pace, at a different level and you are feeling the difference. You are seeing and recognising the shifts, attitudes, perceptions and the gifts of your love-filled souls coming together in singleness of purpose and expression, reflecting God's Will and plan.

Yes, there is much to do and you are willing, my beloved friends. We are also willing. Together, the flow of these blessings will be tremendous in scope, far-reaching, reaching many souls. You will see. You will come to understand and realise the intricacies, the power, the depth, the magnificence of God's plan, how He has woven your beautiful lights into that plan and made a tapestry of service that is unique, powerfully beautiful and filled with much potential to help change the world as it must be changed, to help heal the world as it must be healed and to help open the doors that need to be opened, that love may flow freely, that all may come to pass as is ordained by the Hand of God.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Matthew. I love you, I honour you and I see your lights, so beautiful and bright. Indeed, these lights draw many into your midst through your instrumentality service to God. God bless you, beloveds. Matthew loves you. God bless you.
