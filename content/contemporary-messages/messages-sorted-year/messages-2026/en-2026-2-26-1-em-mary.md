# The Importance of Prayer for Healing Souls Upon the Earth

February 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Mary received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am your sister, Mary and I bless you each with my love. I bring to you your Father's Will to bless you even more and to show you, to assure you, of your well-being in this state of prayer and the condition that you step into with each prayer. For each prayer brings you closer to our Heavenly Father and each prayer draws you into a condition of love where your souls are nurtured, where you learn what you need next, where you are comforted and feel secure and gives you a reprieve from your daily lives upon the Earth.

This is one reason why we urge you to pray often, that you may enjoy those moments, that condition of the Father's Love coming into your souls, that you may feel the Truth of this Love and the Truth of your relationship with God. This is loving yourself to be with God as much as possible. This is how to get your guidance and know what steps to take. This is everything that you need, individually and together. Yet, when you are together, you bring more into this world of this Love from God. The peace that you feel contributes to this world and this world needs much more peace, much more love.

Come together often. One, two, three of you, come together as many times as possible and come into this condition where the Father's Love may reach many, many more people. You are powerful as a group. You are powerful as individuals. Allow God's Love to come through you and heal and transform this world and help those souls to learn of God's Love so that they too may come to know this peace and this Love. You are working very well. Each moment that you are in prayer, the work you do for God is accomplished. We urge you to pray often, whether by yourselves or with others and bring the truth into this world.

You are blessed, so very blessed, to know of this truth, to know how to pray, to know God. I will continue to pray with you, as will many others who are here. We will help uplift more of your brothers and sisters upon the Earth. My love is with you. God bless you, dear children. God bless you. I am Mary.
