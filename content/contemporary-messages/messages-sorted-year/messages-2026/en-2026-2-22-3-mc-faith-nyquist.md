# You Are On A Trajectory of Great Change

February 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Faith. How wonderful it is to hear the prayer of the dear sister, praying for the Pentecostal inflowing of God’s Divine Love. You know we hear this more and more in your prayers. That is because your desires have deepened and your willingness and even ability to receive such a deep inflowing make it so. Each of your souls has progressed and changed by the blessing of God’s Love. This shall continue, as you well know, for eternity.

You, beloved souls, are on a trajectory, a phase of great change, a time where many of you feel that deeper call. You feel more deeply the yearnings of your soul and the change that is underway within each one, the rumblings of your soul making its way known into your consciousness. You spend more time in wonderment of soul perception. You have the experiences of truth and wisdom bubble up into your consciousness, having greater *"aha!"* moments and moments within your soul that you feel a great depth of awareness of truth, of God’s Substance, of your very nature changing. This is all because of the Essence of God’s Soul that you pray for, and the response comes in through the great blessing of the Holy Spirit, His agent of Love.

It is wonderful to experience what you may consider the highs of this journey, where you are riding a wave of love that is vast, expansive, full of clarity and awareness. You love these moments. You yearn for more of these moments, for they inspire you because you feel the blessing, the changes and the awakenings come to you. You recognise your progression. You see in the midst of your journey how far you have come, the changes that God’s Love has created within your life. These waves of love have you high, high upon the journey. Then there are times when you feel less so in this way, even somewhat downtrodden or a little low. You are seeking for that wave, for God’s Love to rush in so that you can ride that wave again.

My dear friends, these times of experiences that are less than the high that you seek are so very important. You must remain steady, deep in prayer, attuning as best you can to the longings of your soul, though it is during these times that the longings seem distant and you are trying to reach for them. Fear not, for this is all part of the progression of your soul changing, releasing, surrendering to the blessings of God’s Love and the Hand of God working upon you, bringing harmony into your very existence. Know that while the waves of love, being in the midst of the bliss of God’s Essence, are wonderful, they are only part of your journey. Those places and times when you feel a little down, even anxious or in some depression, these are your conditions coming into your awareness, prepared for change.

Be sure to be thankful and grateful for this journey of awareness and know that in time, you will be riding another beautiful wave of God’s Love high upon its Waters, deep within the immersion of this Love. It is an ongoing ebb and flow, high and low until you are fully transformed. I am here to encourage you to understand the dynamics and the duality of your journey, the highs and lows, the ebbs and flows and to embrace it all, to thank God deeply in the midst of all that is changing within you. For in that, beloveds, this makes the journey of transformation one that is whole and one that is full.

We pray with you and we are here to encourage you. We are here to stand strong with you, to love you and to bring you our blessings and gifts as you walk this most magnificent journey of awakening and becoming a divine soul. I am Faith. God bless you.
