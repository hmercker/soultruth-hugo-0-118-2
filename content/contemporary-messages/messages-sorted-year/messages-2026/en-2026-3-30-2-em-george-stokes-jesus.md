# Jesus Comes to Facilitate God's Blessings to Humanity

March 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

George Stokes & Jesus received by Elizabeth Mack

**George Stokes:** I am your brother George. I am here to assist you in this prayer to help you open your souls, to help raise you, to love you, to bring you closer to the Source of all love. There is one here who comes forward to speak, one greater than all of us, who brings his love, truth and majesty.

**Jesus:** I am your brother, Jesus. I come to pray with you, children, true souls upon this Earth, true souls in the Celestial Heavens, brothers and sisters, all who are here. There are many, for your prayer, your desires, your longings to be of service to God brings us to you. Indeed, the Father's blessings for His children flow upon this Earth, flow into the souls, all the souls, those here in this circle, those who walk upon the Earth, those who love God, those who pray, those who seek to know the truth.

The Father gathers all around and brings them closer, closer as a loving father would. He blesses them all in ways, in ways and means that they may not even know, but will experience. His Love pours upon you all as you open and allow His Love to enter. Great is this work. Greater is His Love, bountiful for His children. May all these children receive the bounty of His glorious Love.

God bless you all. God bless you all. My love is with you. I am your brother. I am Jesus. My love for you is so great and the Father's is incomprehensible. Stay open, dear children, as the Father continues to bless your siblings upon the Earth in many, many ways.
