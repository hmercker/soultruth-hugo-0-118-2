# Acknowledges God's Creation of Woman on Mother's Day

May 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Anna received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Anna, the grandmother of Jesus. I come to you who live on Earth, celebrating in certain areas of Earth what you call Mother's Day. How special it is to be able to acknowledge God's creation, how the design of humanity on your Earth has been designated to ensure that creation continues to progress, the beautiful design that God has made. When He created a woman, one of her highest gifts is being that portal for souls to incarnate into their world, their physical world, the design that within a woman's body, her biology and creation to carry and develop a physical body for a soul who has chosen to incarnate, the automatic intelligence of the body, knowing how to nourish, to love, to insulate, support and protect as this new soul is coming into form, into physical form.

If you consider the blessing that God has created woman to be is very powerful and also it is good to make consideration that every soul born here on your Earth has come from a woman, so it is not so much as woman is better than man, or man is better than woman, but each one needs to be respected, honoured and revered for who they are in God's creation and how they contribute to God's creation in this very special way.

My dear friends, as you open your soul and invite the great blessing of God's Love to fill you, give thanks to God as well, for it is He who has started creation, the design and the way in which humanity has come to be into form. My dear friends, as you open your soul and invite the great blessing of God's Love to fill you, give thanks to God for His Love of creation, for His design and His perfection. For each one of you are tenderly loved, have been created and designed to be in your own unique way, all born from the realms of love.

I am Anna and may the blessings of God's Love touch you deeply, tenderly, creating awakening after awakening, so that you may become the vessel of love, the vessel of purity that you so desire to be a demonstration of. We hold you in love, in peace, sending you healing and blessings as you walk your journey toward God, the at-onement, your soul and God's Soul. God bless you all in this most miraculous venture. God bless you.
