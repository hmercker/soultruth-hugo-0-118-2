# The Value Of Living the Love While On Earth

March 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light

Clare of Assisi received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Clare of Assisi. I come to join you in this sacred prayer to God, our almighty Soul, Creator of everything and source of all Love. I am truly who I say I am and I live in the Celestial Heavens with many of my divine brothers and sisters, who also are transformed souls. I bring with me many other Celestial Angels, some spirits from the Seventh Sphere and even a few from the Fifth.

We come to join you in this prayer. For it is a beautiful, energetic, loving group of souls in the flesh who come together each week to pray to God and ask for an inflowing of His Divine Love. This is what we do at all times, we who live in the Celestial Heavens, and it is our pleasure and our joy to join you. Yes, my brother, St Francis, as you know him, is also here, of course. Together we desire to bless you with our love. May each one of you receive this gift from us, which in many ways will benefit you as you continue to walk this path toward God.

We learned a long time ago the value of life upon the Earth when you are truly walking toward God and serving God. There are many souls who have learned this lesson, whose lives were lived differently than ours, but nonetheless, the Love is the same as it fills our souls and brings us into a deeper communion with God. There is such joy, such peace, such love, always available.

We touch each one of you, bringing you some of the blessings that have been given to us by our Heavenly Father, that you truly know how blessed you are, that you experience the depth of the blessings that are bestowed upon you each time you pray, that you learn to walk with these blessings and express them more and more, sometimes not even realizing how you have been changed. But when you come over to the other side of the veil, you will see more clearly the blessings that you have within you, the gifts, ways of expressing the souls that God created within you. There is much more for you to learn, much further to go, but you must see by now that we walk with you. We are with you and you even feel us at times, especially in prayer.

May the Father bless you more and more as you progress and walk this sacred path to join God in His Love. I am Clare and I so appreciate being able to speak with you today and give you my love. May God bless you. I am Clare.
