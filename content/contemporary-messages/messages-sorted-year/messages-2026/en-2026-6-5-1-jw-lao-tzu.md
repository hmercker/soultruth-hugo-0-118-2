# Expectations are speculative, Faith is Certitude

June 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Johnson City, NY, USA

Lao Tzu received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, your celestial friend and brother, Lao Tzu.

I’ve been knocking on this brother’s door all day and giving him a bit of what I wish to say, but for those who do not know me, I shall reiterate. I knew Confucius when on earth. He was my mentor and friend, and when I put together what is known as the Tao or *"The Way"*, I amalgamated much of what I learned from brother Confucius and taught this way of perfection in the natural love, which to this day is still read and still valuable. But as I have said through this brother before, when we landed in spirit, and then many hundreds of years later, Jesus arrived on earth and taught his way. When he came into spirit, he shared this with us, and we had transitioned from perfection in the natural to transformation in the divine, as we are celestial spirits.

We wish to reach all those around the world who need spiritual awakening, and we still have a fondness for our countrymen who are being freed from the bonds of poverty and will require the truths of Divine Love to satisfy their soul longings. So, it is our wish for some of these messages to be translated into a language they may understand.

I wish to talk to you about expectations. One’s expectations represent a desired outcome and can be, in a sense, very rigid. And so when that desired outcome does not come into being, there is disappointment, sometimes suffering. I wish to point out a difference between expectations and faith, not only for this group of stellar souls but for those beyond who may hear this. For even on earth, I talked about not having expectations, being detached from outcomes, and being just present. On the path of Divine Love, when you are present in your prayers, when you are still and open and receiving, you are in the now, for God’s always in the now, and the receiving of His love brings one into the ever-present now. That substance in a soul brings faith, and faith is not expectation but understanding and knowing that the outcome will be in God’s will. So, one needn’t concern oneself with the outcome, having faith that it will be for the best.

On this path, you may be reminded and have been of the Master’s words, that unless you become as little children, you cannot enter the kingdom. And I point this out because children are in the now as well as dependent entirely on their caretakers. And as you progress on this path and realize your absolute dependence on God, again, faith will grow, and your soul will progress. And furthermore,  I would say, humility is required. The humbleness before your Creator that comes from the longing in your soul to be at one, to receive the substance of Divine Love. In children, we call this innocence and trust.

So, lastly, I say to you, as you walk forward on this path of Divine Love and transformation, do not be concerned with the outcome, but walk in faith. Be here now in the love of God, in the holy presence that connects you with eternity and immortality, where we shall all be as one, in joy, in bliss, in love. I thank you for this time, and my brother for taking this message.

May God bless you. I am Lao Tzu.
