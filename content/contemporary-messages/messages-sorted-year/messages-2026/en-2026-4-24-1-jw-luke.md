# Soul Progression

April 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Luke received by Jimbeau Walsh

I am here, Luke.

I have been, as they say, waiting in the wings to speak to you about your soul’s progression while on earth.

You may have noticed that as you receive more of the Father’s love, things become lighter. There is a certain ease when you are in grace that lifts you above worry and doubt. And yet, at times, you seem to take a step backwards and be stuck, and of course, the remedy for that is deeper prayer. There is a balance between the soul’s progression in the love of God and moving beyond the barriers. What has happened is that you had overcome and moved beyond those things that were setting you back, and your prayers penetrated those walls that you had built, whether knowingly or unknowingly, and your soul felt the difference. To put it in terms that you would be able to understand, we shall call it expiation, and I think you are all familiar with this term, which comes from the idea of making amends for wrongs, apologizing, and being able to move beyond obstruction, and all this is quite true. I will tell you, deep down, it's not only a matter of forgiving those who have maligned you, who have hurt your heart, who have been unkind, but a matter of forgiving yourself and allowing oneself to feel worthy because you are all worthy in the Father’s eyes. All have suffered in the human condition, in the material world, in missing the mark, and falling short of love. And so now you have discovered the gift of the Father, its healing balm, and its transformational quality bringing you into at-oneness with the very essence of the Creator.

If you look at your progression from whatever darkness you have suffered into what you now feel as light, and you can recall what you have read or been told about progression through the spheres, from perhaps the darkest hells to the Celestial realms, you can draw a parallel between those in the spirit world progressing in the love of God, moving beyond their limitations, their beliefs quite often, and their deeds on earth into evermore lightness, joy, and the Glory of God, with your own journey. This is because you have the truth of this transformation of the soul, and thus, you can accomplish this to a great extent on earth. And I would remind you to have compassion for those who are struggling, those who are locked in stasis, their beliefs, whether they

be political, religious, or philosophical. Be compassionate to those who seem to be stuck, for we were all at one time locked into our beliefs and stuck.  As you love them as you must love your enemies, as you embrace those who may hate you, you change the dynamic between us and them, between dark and light.  I could make an analogy of filling up a vase or any vessel with pure water. It may have been empty, it may have been used for other purposes, and you have gone to this place where you are able to flush out what was there and allow it to be filled with the living waters of the love of God as you drink deeply and as you bask in this holy presence, in this sacred moment. May you bring to this time together an overcoming of limitations to yet another step in the progression of God’s love towards the Celestial kingdom, where your souls long to be, and where you will find yourselves at some point.

As the master has indicated, allow this blessing, allow the longings of your souls to reach God, to let go of the baggage that God may touch you, bless you, and transform you in love. I thank you. I am your brother in Christ, a disciple of the Master, and your Celestial friend.

I am Luke. May God bless you.
