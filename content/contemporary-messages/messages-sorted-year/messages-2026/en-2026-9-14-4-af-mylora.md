# The Seed of God’s Love Has Been Planted; Continue to Nurture It

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Wimbledon Spiritualist Church Retreat, Wimbledon, London, UK

Mylora received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. My name is Mylora and I come from these parts many, many centuries ago. The seed has been planted now. It is for you to water it, to nurture it, to find out what it brings into your life, to know that God has given you a gift. In that giving, you will find many delights, many things emerging as you explore the power, beauty and wonderment of God's Love, that tangible Substance from the Soul of God that you may apprise yourself of at any time in any condition and place. You may ask for this gift.

If you are willing to water this seed with these Living Waters, then it will change you, my friends. It will heal you. It will bring you peace. It will bring your relationship with God closer and more understandable. Many insights, many understandings, many perceptions, many blessings will come of this. For when the soul is awakened, so the whole realms of this consciousness, the consciousness of God, the consciousness of soul in relationship with one another, will come and awaken you to all the blessings that God intends for you to have, so much given in love, a love divine, a wondrous love, a love that brings all to you that you require, a love that changes you and makes you one of God's children, truly, truly.

My friends, water that seed of truth that has been planted. Give yourself a moment to breathe and be with God, to pray and to open to God. You will not regret those moments, but they will become more precious than life itself when these moments expand into true conscious expression and understanding of love. It is there for the asking, my beloved friends and so we offer to you a gift beyond price, something truly divine.

God bless you. I am Mylora. I love you all and I will help you. I will be with you as you continue to seek out this beautiful blessing. God bless you all.
