# Boundless Joy, Boundless Love

August 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Goldie and you draw me because you were speaking about joy, this beautiful attribute that is of the soul. An expression of expandedness for true joy knows no bounds, just as pure love has no bounds. It gives for the sake of giving. It showers itself for the sake of life, the sake of creating and expressing. It is so beautiful, as you all know, when you are aligned with your soul and in the deep experiences of purely being in the oneness of the soul. In those times when you are able to hold this consciousness, it ignites a great desire within the soul to receive more of God's great Love. For it is awakening all of the attributes of your soul and your soul's expression. As the soul continues to progress in Divine Love and its faculties, the attributes open and function in a way that has no bounds.

You feel free. You are no longer guarded or protecting anything. You are free to love. Your soul is no longer under the shelter of your armour and you experience the power of the love of your soul and how this love has a growing force awakening you, the soul, in all its beauty, in all its wonderment. You are developing a new consciousness. Even your material consciousness is changing because you can see beyond the material, beyond the limitations of a material life and the density that trying to create purely from a material perspective is slow, is heavy and dense. As your consciousness rises, you are able to perceive from a higher vantage point and you can create your life from these realms beyond the material, from different dimensions, different dimensions of consciousness.

You have the consciousness of your soul and you have the consciousness of your spirit mind and your material mind. As these patterns of fear that hold you back are transmuted and transformed by the Divine Love, you can perceive higher, you can see through different eyes and you can walk in the world, in the density of the material world and at times, not be affected because you are attuned to a higher dimension, a dimension of the soul, the dimension of your spirit. This can affect and does affect your material mind because these new patterns of optimism, of potential have found their way into form within your brain. So, these new patterns of creation have stabilised, materialised and actualised in your existence and this is an exciting place to experience and at times many do. It may be for one day. It may be for a moment. It may be for numerous days.

This is how you see your nature progressing. In that progression, you are living and experiencing life from a different vantage point. In ways this is connected to faith. Because faith within the soul, as it expresses itself, your spirit mind can perceive this as well as the material mind. There is a connection, a conscious connection and awareness of the faith that lies within the soul. You experience the harmony of these three minds, the oneness of, in harmony. As you move along your path and you experience your awakenings, there is a light that brightens within you, a light that helps you to see the way forward, a light that sits within your thoughts that holds truth, that perceives wisdom, that receives guidance. You are then free to harmonise with that, where your material mind chooses to allow the boundless joy and the boundless love to flow purely because it can. No need to have a reason. No need to have a motive.

This love, the joy, it is alive in you, enlivening your experience of life. Welcome these blessings into your experience. Observe them as they take over your experience in a way that you, out of your material consciousness, are not trying to control, but you are purely allowing the gift of life, the gift of love and the gift of joy to be alive and to exist in you and through you. May you be blessed deeply as you continue your joyous path of at-onement with God. I am Goldie and not often do I present myself in this manner. Yet, it is my joyful experience to be able to share some wisdom and insight to each one of you as you walk your path.

May love be boundless. May you receive it openly and may you also give it openly. God bless you all. God bless you. I am Goldie and I shower you with sprinkles of joy, each one. God bless you.
