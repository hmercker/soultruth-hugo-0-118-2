# The Angels' Hope for Us From the Bassingham Retreat

September 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

God be with you. I am Keea Atta Kem. What a delight to see how you draw one another together, your pure essence communing with God and with one another.

While you have this beautiful time together over these many days, it is our hope that each of you takes away a clearer understanding of who you are as a soul, that beautiful creation of God, that the way that you come to expand in your consciousness is so evident in your lives, that you know your efforts of travel, but also how your efforts of yearning and longing from the soul are changing you and that you can perceive within yourself these beautiful changes. It is not for others to acknowledge the change that is within you, but more so that you see the change, the changes, your transformation, because this ignites a great fire within you. It drives you forward in your longings, in your awakening of desires to be with God at all times.

Perceive that union of your soul, the Love of God flowing into you and the manifestation of that Love flowing from you, allowing your eyes to change. In this way, you can see beyond the material. You can see beyond your human conditioning and the human conditioning of another and the power of your soul, the love, the Divine Love that is within your soul, guiding you, breathing you, speaking through you and acting through you. These are the reasons that you sit in prayer for God's Love. It is to be able to experience the wholeness, the transformation of Love Divine.

We will be with you and we are with you. Allow your soul perceptions to be a part of you at all times, your feelings, the sensations, the truth of who you are, to lead you forward in every day. I am Keea Atta Kem. May the great inflowing of God's Love fill you, pierce you and awaken you to the truth of all you are and all you can be through God's Love. God bless you.
