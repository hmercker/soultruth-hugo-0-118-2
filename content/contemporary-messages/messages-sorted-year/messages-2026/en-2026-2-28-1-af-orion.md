# Q&A: Orion Opens the Floor to Questions, Addresses Soul Consciousness and How to Discern Between Different Stellar/Spirit Influences

February 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. Before we go further, I would be interested in opening the floor to any questions you might ask of me, to give that opportunity if there are concerns or queries that you have regarding the efforts that we are making and what is coming in terms of those efforts. I will give you a minute to consider these things. I know it is difficult when the floor is opened in this way, rather than giving you some time to think about it, but here we are today, having an opportunity to be together and to communicate together. I ask you to verbalise your question, if you have one. I will give you some time to do so.

**Question:** *Thank you, Orion. What can we do to expedite our leap into soul consciousness?*

Well, there are many overt things you can do. Many of the challenges that you face have been explained numerous times regarding living in a world that is mostly of the mind, conditions that are contrary to soul and situations that often challenge you to be within the mind and to deal with those conditions from the mindful perspective, rather than to be from a place of soul and soul wisdom. Those essentially are the challenges that you face daily.

How do you expedite soulful awareness? Well, one thing that is required is discipline. When you discipline your mind so that it is not free flowing and dominant and, in time, subordinate to the soul, then the passageway of soul communication toward consciousness is opened more directly and clearly. Indeed, the human condition, which involves emotions, thoughts and actions that are centred in the mindal field of awareness, need to be brought into a place of harmony with the laws which you know, the laws that have been taught. With each breath, with each thought, there is a choice, a choice of releasing that which you know at this stage of your development is not in harmony with love, or to change that thought or change that intention toward that which is in harmony with love.

This too will expedite the passageway between soul consciousness and everyday consciousness. Although you cannot expect yourself to flip a switch, as they say, and go into soul consciousness. This would be quite convenient for all of you, although in a way it would retard your growth, because it would not require any great effort on your part to be in that state. Rather, our desire and what is most beneficial is to be constantly in the state of soul where the mind is subordinate and the intellect, the wordiness, the mindfulness is less appealing and less expressed in your daily life. I am not suggesting that you remain or become ignorant of the world around you and to release any sense of responsibility and involvement in that world. Rather, the way you approach it, the way you see the world, how the world interacts with you and how you interact with it can be determined by the choices you make about soul consciousness.

Are you able to do this, or are you still comfortable and stuck, shall we say, within the modalities of the mind and all that that brings to you, that which gratifies the mind’s desires to be dominant and to make those crucial decisions in life that reflect a mindful perspective? Indeed, you have the mind of the soul to consider. That mind is far wiser and more perceptive than that of your material mind and your everyday mindful ideas. Again, it is for you to choose. We cannot choose for you. No one can. God can’t even choose for you, my friends. It is your choice.

Expediting soul awakening and awareness comes in prayer, as you well know, but what you do outside of prayer is very important and is decisive in terms of how expressive and powerful your life can be in relationship with truth, in relationship with God and in relationship with your own soul. You have all gathered together because you desire to be God’s servants, to be in the flow of the awakening of your soul and the transformation of it through the gift of Divine Love. Indeed, each of you are at various stages of progress. Each of you has your dilemmas and your issues to face. Each of you has your inner world to deal with, or to engage with in a way that brings deeper soul awareness and communication.

The foundation is easily stated and has been stated very many times. Go to God. Establish a relationship with your Creator, open your soul to the blessings that your Creator has to give to you. Yet, for you, prayer is easy. You do it numerous times. You are a master at prayer. It is how you apply the experience of that relationship with God, the experience of that blessing of Divine Love within, the experience of allowing the soul to communicate with you consciously and the experience of being a channel, an instrument for God. These are the great challenges.

I cannot give you a simple formula other than the one that has been presented numerous times, but I will tell you that discipline, forthrightness, desire, intention, all of these things are an important factor in how you express yourself in the world and be in the world as a channel of love. I hope I have answered that question to your satisfaction, my dear son.

**Question:** *I would like to express my deep gratitude to you and your cohorts for your presence and guidance in my life. I look forward to the physical manifestation that you have indicated will happen at Happy Days. My question is about other star friends from other planets. I understand they also will be coming. Well, there are many influences that are around each of you. How do you recognise that influence? How do you recognise one of your angel friends? How do you recognise me or one of my cohorts? How do you recognise other spirit influences in your life? It comes with soul discernment, the awakening of those faculties of the soul that allow you to know and to recognise various what you call “vibrations” or presence within your life. I would suggest that you do not doubt your capacity for perception. I would assure you that around each one of you is a protection afforded by the higher levels of rapport and communication that you have established with all of us. As a result, you have the benefit of that protection. If you are worried about lower influences, then I would suggest that what will ensure that there are no lower influences is a reflection of your mindful condition, what you desire and what you focus upon.

Yes, there are many influences in the universe and many influences upon your world. You have elected to reach out for the highest. You have put into action various laws to ensure that the highest comes to you rather than something that is deceitful or dark. Thus, the influences that will come to you will always be of that calibre. Indeed, those that will be permitted to connect with you will be those whose souls are like mine and yours, seeking that great gift of Divine Love and transformation and in light. Of course, you may derail that system, that condition of protection, by your own willfulness, but this is doubtful. You will find your way, each one of you, and things will be revealed to you in the timing that is right and in the way that is possible with the opening of your gifts.

We do not wish to confuse the issue with you by presenting many other beings and influences that may cause some confusion and some hesitancy, but some whom you know that are part of what you call all the Divine Love community have communicated with these entities and understand them well. This medium has communicated with a few of them and it has not been a traumatic experience. On the contrary, it has been enlightening. This door will continue to open as you are ready and in a way that is productive and helpful to your progress and to the work that we are engaged upon.

Whether these beings will manifest in your world is a good question that cannot be answered at this stage, because you are not ready to have manifestation. You are not in the condition or situation where this would be possible or even helpful. That question remains to be answered, but I can assure you that you are well protected and that the influence and guides that are around you have purpose and are deeply committed to your wellbeing and the upliftment of humanity in truth, in light, in the ways in which humanity will evolve and grow in light and harmony in the world.

For this is the way of the universe, to bring everything into greater harmony and peace, to have each planet a home that nurtures, uplifts and guides each soul that incarnates there toward higher realms and higher levels of consciousness and expression of soul. We labour hard and long for you, for this world, and will continue to do so. Many are involved. Indeed, your world is not an isolated place. It is known by many other worlds. I hope this answers your question, dear daughter.

If there are no other questions, shall we go into prayer together to receive the nurturance of our soul, God’s blessings upon us, this universal Love that continues to awaken not only humanity here on Earth, but humanity throughout the universe?
