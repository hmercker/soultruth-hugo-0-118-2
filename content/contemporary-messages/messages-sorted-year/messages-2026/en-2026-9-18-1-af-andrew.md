# The More You Can Resist the Human Conditions of the World, the More the Angels Can Utilise Your Gifts

September 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Andrew received by Al Fike

Oh, beloved souls, may the Love of the Father continue to inflow into your beautiful souls. I am Andrew. Your connection with God is never diminished. What often happens in your life is your conscious understanding and experience of that connection is diminished by your condition, by your ability to be attuned to this, but be reassured that your soulful connection with God is always there, continues to grow and strengthen as you receive the great gift of God's Love. Indeed, it is our hope that your conscious awareness of this will grow as well, that you will feel in that grace of God with every breath.

This would be ideal, would it not, my friends? Yet, indeed, the power of the material world and the human condition continues to override this subtle yet powerful awareness of God. You are all eager to experience this connection consciously. You are all eager to be in God's grace and to feel it. I suggest to you that you continue to open yourself, to open your sensitivities to this, even though you may be in environments that are contrary to it.

The more you can do this, the easier it is for us to utilise your gifts and abilities in the world. For there is no obstruction in this regard, but as you close yourself off, entering into these conditions, these human conditions, the depth and breadth of your instrumentality is also affected. Are you strong enough to enter into the world without being or feeling victimised by these conditions? Can you engage with the world and with that flow of love being most evident and expressed powerfully? Each day you go out into the world, it is a challenge and a test. Each day you open yourselves more to God's Will and less to your own mindful will, then you will find yourself being guided and directed in a way that often would be surprising, yet beautiful in its outcomes.

There is so much to learn, my friends, regarding being God's instrument. You pray to be this and to be in the condition that will allow it to be. As you do so, the opportunities will increase and the possibilities will multiply. Yet, you hold the key to these expressions and realisations. It is within your consciousness where you decide to be in alignment with God or not. There are many things that God will utilise you for that you are not even aware of.

Yet, the most powerful things require your awareness. The most subtle things are difficult to be aware of, but you will find yourself becoming more aware, more sensitised. Your perceptions growing and awakening. All of these things flowing as a natural outcome of your soul condition and soul desire to be God's instruments. Beautiful, beautiful blessings to flow. Powerful blessings that will come through you, that will uplift your brothers and sisters and at times, blessings so powerful that you will change their lives through your instrumentality.

Indeed, when you organise these retreats and others come often with curiosity, sometimes with a guardedness and trepidation, where the love melts away these conditions of resistance, these are the times when God may work powerfully and effectively. Continue to build these conditions within you, the ability to be that clear channel, to be humble yet clear in truth, to be loving always and to understand what being loving is. It is not just a few kind words.

It is what is behind those words. What is behind your motivation to speak and to direct others? Many need a very soft touch, a clear voice, but one that does not threaten, nor where the individual will pull away, feeling afraid, or even judgemental, or being judged. It is in the soft gentleness of your voice, the clarity of the truth you are speaking. It is in your embrace of another and the flow of God's Love through you. These are the things that will break down the barriers and open the individual to the grace of God's Touch.

Each soul longs for this, but very few understand it. They do not realise that it is in their vulnerability, their openness, that the true Touch of God comes and in that vulnerability is great strength, great capacity to be truly who they are meant to be, to be truly themselves. Continue to teach the truth with humility and grace. Foster peace and harmony and most of all, love. You will be successful in your ventures and strong in your presence.

God bless you, my friends. I am Andrew. May God's Love continue to pour abundantly within you. This will inform everything and guide everything, power this Love to awaken and transform. It is the most magnificent blessing of all and it is yours, my beloveds, for the asking. God bless you. Andrew loves you. God bless you.
