# Listen For the Truth That Is Conveyed with This Divine Love

May 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

John Hughes received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am John Hughes. As I come closer, you recognize me as being your soulmate, your brother in soul, as well as in this Divine Love. We come forward to speak. Sometimes you are at a loss. Sometimes it is that you are not hearing the Father as He speaks to you. Yes, I am speaking to all who are here. For hearing the subtle voices of the Celestial Angels and the even subtler Voice of God is a challenge when you live upon the Earth, but it is possible.

As you develop this Love in your souls, you truly are becoming one with God. Go to your souls and listen and ask that you hear God, that you hear God's Will for you and that you hear any guidance that you may be or have been receiving. Go deep and allow your focus to be on your soul. Release the thoughts that clutter your mind. Release your material expectations and desires and listen for the Truth, the Truth that is conveyed to you with this Divine Love.

Your soul will know it. Your soul will feel it. It may be a whisper to you, but the Love that it comes with is unmistakable. It is healing, it is pacifying, it is gentle and it is so welcomed by your soul. Allow. Allow this to come to you. Open and allow the Love of God to come to you and with it, everything that you need and everything that your soul desires. This is today's lesson on communing with God.

God bless you. My love is with you, each one. I am John Hughes and I am often with this medium as she is learning.
