# You Each Add a Dimension that None Other Brings When You Pray Together

August 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Retreat of the Four, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved students. Your teacher, Augustine. It is good that you take time to pray together in the flesh. There is a quality and substance to your prayers when you are together like this. It creates a light around you and through you that enters this world and dissolves much darkness. Because you are all a part of this great network of light, the Lattice of Light, together you charge this lattice effectively and powerfully.

It is also good that you pray alone to be with your heavenly Father and communing in this way. For that is the staple, the foundation of your daily life, your prayer life. Yet, as you build those conditions around you in your daily life, as you continue to grow and prosper in the Love of God, so you bring something with you when you come together in these Circles of Light. You add an aspect, a dimension that none other brings and thus, together you are more complete. There is more power, beauty and light in these Circles of Light than if you were praying alone.

Yet, each is important, necessary to ask God for this great blessing of His Love, to do so with all earnestness and desire, to walk in that light that is God's grace, to be in alignment with God's Will. You, my beloved, beautiful students, know these things. Yet, I say this to remind you also, for the benefit of many spirits that you bring with you into this Circle of Light. I wish to bless this instrument, who is on his way upon another journey, helping to form other Circles of Light. He must know that we are close, that all will be well. He should put his anxiety away and be at peace.

As for the rest of you, those things that cause you disturbance, that bring ripples of disharmony into your consciousness, put them aside and replace them with faith. Replace them with the Light of God's Love and you will truly be at peace. You will truly benefit from your choice and desire to be in the grace of God, not to be outside of that grace, not to be fully engaged with all the concerns of the material world, but to be in the peace that passes all understanding, to know that you have your angel friends with you. You have God's Hand upon you and you are in the light. It is only these concerns and anxieties of the mind that diminish this experience, this condition. You must learn to control these thoughts, to put aside this anxiousness, this desire to please and in that desire to please, the fear that if you do not, you will be reprimanded or ostracised or some other reaction from another.

Yet, when you are with God, these things do not matter, even if they do exist. God will never ostracise you or reprimand you or bring you from light into darkness. Rather, He always brings you from darkness into light and so you must have faith that this is the case, that indeed you are a part of this great network of light bringers in the world, bringing your truth, your part to play, your condition and light to give, your faith to uphold and to demonstrate the truth. May God bless you, my beloveds and beautiful students, upon the journey of transformation through God's Love. It happens as rapidly or slowly as you determine. It is your will, your effort, your desire, your consistency and your prayers that make the difference, that add to the journey.

May you all find your way within the harmony, beauty and love that is God's presence with you and God's Love within you. God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine. My love is with you. Truly, I am with you. God bless you.
