# Make an Authentic Choice To Be the Resounding Bell Of Truth In an Erratically Changing World

March 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Greetings to you, my friends. It is Orion. Today I wish to speak about the nature of change and the Law of Progression in the universe. These conditions and expression of laws can be an individual thing and it can be a universal thing. For like any law in the universe, it is applied equally to all things. In your world, there is great change at this time. Your world is a very dynamic place, far more dynamic than many other worlds in the universe, because humanity has taken the choice of free will seriously and has applied their choices and their expressions accordingly to such a degree that there is much change in your world, especially in these times. On my planet, change is very slow, because the direction which we take is toward harmony, peace and continued progression. Because these elements are not disturbed greatly by our behaviours and our choices, there is no need for dynamic responses on my planet.

There is great disruption upon yours. Indeed, each of you are trying to counteract and nullify those disruptions in your life. You desire to have peace and harmony, and so your battle is more about dealing with those influences and elements in the world that may cause you disruptions and disharmony. There are outside forces, yet within you is a great momentum toward harmony and there is great momentum toward true change, the recalibration of the soul that comes with this great gift of Divine Love within you. It is changing everything and continues to do so as long as you continue to desire and receive this great gift. Though outwardly in your lives there can often be disruptions and disharmony, within your soul is building harmony and peace.

The awakening of your soul is transformation. It is happening with every breath. We have asked you to be aware and cognisant of what is happening within you, to make that your guidepost in life, rather than looking around you at outside forces that you believe are making the decisions for you in life. In fact, you are in control, not the sort of control of the mind, the material mind, but in the form of Divine influence and guidance. Thus, you have a pathway, an ability to walk forward, to progress forward in harmony with the Laws of Progression.

As long as you listen to what that guidance might be and feel with the feelings of soul, what is right for you and your daily choices, then all will be well, despite the outward appearance of the world, where there is chaos and darkness and difficulty. Even when you are in that mode of listening to God and being in alignment with God, you are influencing those around you and your loved ones. Wherever you move in the world, you are bringing that light and that harmony with you. You are important instruments to bring peace and harmony into the world.

Yet, the world contradicts all of these things. The world is intent on self-expression and willfulness that is often contrary to the Laws of Harmony and Love. Yet, change is coming to the wider world, just as change is coming to you, my beloved friends. God is pouring light, influence, the influence of angels, the influence of your stellar friends, such as myself and many more. We are focusing on your world. This is not a casual application. It is an effort to save your world as it continues to plunge into greater darkness, as wars and threats of wars and the behaviours of governments, institutions and individuals conspire to continue to cause depravity and difficulties for everyone upon your planet. We are working diligently to help counteract these conditions and choices.

You are a great focal point for us, because within your hearts and souls, you too wish to be an element, an aspect, a force to counteract darkness and bring light and harmony. Thus, the elements of change are within you and around you. Because of your world, change does not come readily. It does not come steadily or in an upward projection always. It is erratic. There is change and then there is a decline and a reactivity within the human race that tries to pull conditions back to what is familiar and what is common. This is done through the collective thought of humanity. Yet, that trajectory is upward, if somewhat erratic.

There are great efforts to help humanity move forward. Often this is done subliminally and from the level of spirit consciousness and soul consciousness. The material consciousness is the most dense of all and most difficult to deal with, but with the spirit consciousness and the soul consciousness, what we have to offer and what is being offered by God and His instruments is an influence within these two centres of consciousness that are working their way toward material consciousness. So much of our efforts are not seen or heard, even though we attempt, as we do at this very moment, to bring truth to humanity. How many listen? How many are attuned to this?

There are some, of course. Humanity is made up of a wide variety, a great disparity, of individuals. Some are of light. Some listen. Some are contemplating these things and some are very much attuned to the efforts that we are making. The vast majority continues to follow the road that is dictated and become lost within the dark conditions of the human populace. We are diligent, as are you, as are some in the world making those efforts daily, to be a light in the world, to be an agent of change in the world. It is for you, my beloved friends, to consider how you might do this, how your gifts may be utilised to accomplish a great task.

As you have been told, there is a great plan afoot. There is much effort being made and indeed the most crucial element of that effort are those of you who are doing so consciously in the material world. This must be strengthened and increased in numbers if we are to be successful. We may have all the influence in the material sense and God may pour all the Light and influence in the Divine sense. Yet, if there is not a response in the material sense, then it is very difficult indeed to move the forces of humanity toward greater light and away from chaos, darkness and ultimate destruction.

We continue in our work to try and influence many. It is a difficult work indeed, an uphill climb, so to speak. We make our efforts, as do many others. This focus upon the world is immense and the power of that influence indeed is breaking through here and there. There are cracks in the veneer of many whose consciousness is dulled and unable to truly understand spiritual laws, but it is coming slowly. You and your efforts are helping, as are the efforts of others. I do not mean to tell you that these efforts will come to nothing, for this is not the case. God’s Will will be done in whatever way it can be done. That expression of Will is often circuitous, but it is expressed nonetheless. The opening of consciousness for humanity is meant to be. It is divinely ordained and so it will be.

How this will be accomplished has many twists and turns. We have talked about the coming disruptions and Earth changes, which are but one element that will bring about a shift in consciousness for humanity, but there are many other elements as well. Indeed, my friends, your times will come when you will speak up and talk of truth in simple terms, yet direct and powerful. All is coming together, the fruition of which will be determined by the choices humanity makes. Yet, the forces that are being exerted upon the world are great, and indeed the powers for influence and change are being exerted daily and increased daily.

Change must come. Each individual must recognise the changes they must make within themselves, the choices that they must make toward greater light and harmony. Obeying this law is crucial, yet so very few understand this law and the importance of it. Your role, beloved and beautiful friends, is to speak of these things, speak of why it is important to consider progression, change and shifting of consciousness and how this will benefit the world more than anything else, to be able to make conscious decisions that resonate with the soul wisdom that is within each individual, and to have that wisdom magnified, increase and transformed by Divine Love is the ultimate goal. Yet, indeed, humanity must start somewhere, in some way, one step forward, for change to truly happen. Indeed, some exert themselves in this way, but many do not. It must be a resounding bell of truth that is shared with humanity. You, my beloved friends, along with others, some you have not met yet and some you know, will be that resounding bell, will be that voice speaking of truth.

You must be and you must be willing to be that voice, despite whatever inhibitions there are in your life. God will push away the obstructions if you are willing, if you have within you that true choice, not one that your mind makes, saying, “This is for the better”. It is one that your soul has made, saying, “this is my destiny. This is my purpose”. When that is clearly known and the commitment toward it is made, then many changes will come to you, many changes in your life at present. Much in the way of opportunities to speak and to share will be evident.

Indeed, this manifestation that you so long for of what you call Direct Voice will be a part of it. Yet, that decision, truly from the very core of your being to the surface of your consciousness, must come and be your daily mandate, your daily cry, saying, “Dear God, use me as your instrument this day. Whatever that may be, show me the way that I might be effective and a powerful channel for truth, love and light in the world.” Is that a resounding voice within you, my beloved friends? Ask yourself this. Am I willing to make any and all changes necessary for that voice to be clear and expressed in the ways that are divinely guided? This is your dilemma at this very moment. This is the turning point of your lives. As I have said, you may think within your mind you have made those choices, but you must go deeper and be circumspect and wonder, *“how am I truly in alignment with that statement, that willingness to be God’s channel and instrument in the world?”*

This is true change. I am speaking of authentic, powerful, clear and unambiguous change. This is a change that must come to each one of your consciousness, each one of you living a life that is one foot within the material world and all its demands and one foot within the spirit and soul and all its implications. Look at these things, my beloved friends. It is important. These laws that I speak of need your attention and obedience to them. Like all universal laws, they cannot be ignored, for they are in operation whether you are aware of them or not. It is important that you have the power, you are empowered with that awareness.

May you be blessed with true wisdom, awareness, love, peace and harmony, all these beautiful things, these spiritual elements in your life. We are here to help you find your way to truly be in alignment with these things. God is always there to nourish you and to help you along that road. May you be blessed, my friends. I am Orion and I love you so, my beautiful friends, I love you so. God bless you in His Love.
