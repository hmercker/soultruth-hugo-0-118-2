# Tahlia Describes Her Healing Gift and Explains The Soul’s Path to Communion with God

July 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Tahlia received by Al Fike

I come to be with you. I am Tahlia. I have not spoken often through this instrument, but I have been with you. I am part of the team of angels that come to uphold you, to heal you and to bring light to you. I lived in the Inca Empire many years ago and I was a shaman, as you would call it, a healer, as I would call it, a long line of healers, generations of which helped my people. I continue in the healing practices while in the spirit body and I have indeed earned my place in the Celestial Kingdom, as everyone does, receiving this great gift of Love, the greatest healing agent of all.

I come to help this instrument who recently had a physical trauma. I come to give healing. I also come to give you all healing. When I touch you with my walking stick, you will feel possibly a jolt, like someone has poked you with something powerful. Indeed, I have. That power and that feeling is the result of healing energies that come through me to you, God's blessings from me to you. Of course, it comes through me, from God, to you. You may ask for my presence, the healing that I give, if you so desire. Remember, the Law of Desire is important. When you pray for something, you wish for something and it is in harmony with God's Laws of Creation, God's Laws of Love, then it will come. You will receive this blessing. Indeed, many of you have infirmities and many of you suffer one way or another. I do not just bring healing for the body. I also bring healing for the mind and the spirit. I am happy to help, happy to give you something to uplift you, to help you in this world that is so heavy, heavy with the human condition.

I am glad I did not have to deal with such a complex world. Our life was simple. Yes, we had our ideas of things, our dialogue regarding the origins of ourselves and of the planet. All of these ideas were formulated over many years, but it did not cover my soul in such a way that I could not be with God. We did not understand God as you do, but we understood God enough to be able to communicate and be with God. My heart, my soul, understood what my mind had trouble comprehending. Is it any different for you, my friends? You may have ideas that are clearer and simpler, but indeed, what is important is the understanding of the soul and the relationship between your soul and God's Soul.

When you are in that place, when you understand in that way, then the whole universe opens to you. There is nothing that God holds back from you, but you must be open, to be receptive to seek out the truth, not from the mind and its desire to formulate, to create everything in boxes and ideas, paradigms, as you say, of thought. Rather, this is different. This is the fluidity of soul perception. It is an openness, an expansiveness, a way of being with God that is not restricted by the mind, but open with the soul and that wondrous gift that God gave you, which is your soul and all its faculties, all its abilities.

When I was on Earth, we had potions and ways to open the individual to these experiences. Now, you have the way of prayer, the way of opening your soul to the inflowing of the Essence of God, the greatest Essence of all. There is no need for potions, only the need to sincerely go to God, to open yourself fully to God and to be liberated by God's Touch, that you may fly through the universe, truly experiencing it for what it is, truly knowing God for who God is, truly knowing yourself for who you are.

Is this not the authenticity that you seek, the Truth that will uphold you? It is not written in books per se. It is written in your soul. I urge you to seek this out, to trust your soul, to be in your soul and to allow your soul to take you on the journey to at-onement with God. When you say a prayer, if it is the right prayer, it is opening, allowing that door to open, allowing yourself to enter into the consciousness of soul and the consciousness of God. In this, the communion between you is far beyond anything that your mind can imagine. It is ultimate joy, ultimate freedom and ultimate truth.

Shall we go there, my friends? I will help you. I will touch each one of you, help you to be liberated from the flesh for just a moment and truly be in the Spirit of God and your soul, together. Shall we go and fly and be truly in the embrace of God? I am Tahlia. I am here to assist you and I love you all, beloved friends. God bless you.
