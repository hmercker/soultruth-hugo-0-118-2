# Works on Healers' Development

May 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Keea Atta Kem received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here now. I am your friend and your guide at this moment, guiding each of you in this circle. I am your sister, Keea Atta Kem. I bring my love to each one of you, as you have been told, you are a group of healers. I come to assist you in the development of your healing.

There will be times during this circle [^1] when you will feel your body, your soul, even your mind will become aware of feelings as we make adjustments within your being to help clear away some obstacles that remain in your path of your healing energy. This work is to be done by several of us who are healers. Not all of you will feel the same healer, but we will come to each individual and remove particles from your being. This will allow you to bring forward more of those healing energies, for today will be a healing day. Call it practice, if you will. It is an opportunity for you to discover more of what God has given you in this gift of healing.

So children, open wide and let us begin our work as you offer your love to our powerful, beloved Creator and give gratitude and thanks for His Will in your lives. We begin.

The structure of your being will need adjustment. Allow us to do that.

We will attend to your focus next (soul).

We will bring you into a greater integration of your being.

Your energies are mixing only slightly.

Now, we pull from you those things that need to be removed.

An adjustment here and then there.

Now, a blessing from the Father.

We welcome you now to give thanks for God’s provisions for you, for these healing arts that have been developed (in you) to a small degree.

Now, we strengthen your rapport with one another.

I am Keea Atta Kem and I bless you with my love and will continue to pray with you in thanksgiving to our Heavenly Father for His beneficence.
