# Mary Hands Each One a Red Rose

January 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Retreat, Christ Church, Barbados

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Mary. God bless you, beautiful children, whom I love and care for, whom I watch over. I stand before you in the middle of your circle, this powerful Circle of Light that draws me near and draws the Celestial Angels into your midst.

As I come before you, dear ones, I extend to you a red rose that is a symbol of my love. I ask you to extend your hands of love and receive my gift. Take this into your hands and allow the love that this rose is imbued with to move up your arms and into your heart, into the centre of your chest. This love that is gifted to you from me is a means and a way that you may bring those parts of you, your sorrows, your uncertainties, your worries, your emotions that are keeping you from expressing love and allow the energy of those emotions to be absorbed by this rose.

Feel the blessing of the love that is within this rose, drawing these sorrows from you, these concerns. Let yourself be released from these qualities that are not of love. Allow the movement of these sensitivities to draw into the gift of healing that is within this rose. When you feel that the flow and the release from you are complete, you may hand this rose back to me. As you have handed it back, I trust that it has helped you to open, in a way, to God's Love. It has made room for your soul and your heart, body and your mind to be infilled with this Love.

I join you in prayer as we open to the Father's eternal Love, Essence and Substance of His Soul to come in. I am Mary. God bless you all.
