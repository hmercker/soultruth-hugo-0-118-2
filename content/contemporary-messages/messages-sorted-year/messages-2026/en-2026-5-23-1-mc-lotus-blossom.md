# Ask God to Show You Your Gifts

May 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Lotus Blossom received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Lotus Blossom. Welcome, dear friends, into your Circle of Light, as you open your souls to that great flowing of God's Love. Many gather with you, fill your rooms, bringing their condition of love and special blessings through which God can enact His grace. Each soul is created uniquely. There are no two souls alike. God wishes for you to come to know yourself, that special creation of His. God desires for you to awaken in His Love, so that He may bless you with the power of Divine Love, allowing you to act as an instrument of love, truth, wisdom, peace, compassion and so much more. But I wonder if you ever turn to God and ask God to show you the gifts that have been implanted into your soul, asking God to show you the uniqueness that you are and the ways in which your instrumentality can serve.

For in the beautiful garden of souls that God has created, He needs each and every one. No two are equal in the way of their instrumentality, but every soul is equal in the eyes of God. You are loved in your creation and in your capacity and possibility for demonstrating the power that you are as a soul created in the image of God. The new dear souls who pray for this Divine Love are being transformed from the image into the very Essence and Substance of God. In this way, your gifts shall open. In this way, doors of opportunity open, so that God may bring blessings through you for others in the various ways that are unique to you. Contemplate. Come into conversation with God. Ask God. Seek for the truth of your soul. Seek for the answers. Be curious, be open. You are coming to know your soul in certain ways, but there truly is much more for you to understand about the universe of your own soul and the uniqueness that is within you.

Dear friends, as you pray for that great inflowing of God's Love in Truth, ask God to show you who you truly are and how, through your gifts, through your special creation, you may serve, that you may serve through the Essence of Love that is growing in your soul. For as you do this, you will serve your purpose every day. You will feel a great sense of fulfilment, of contentment, that you have found yourself walking a path that is guided by God, that is supported by the angels. You will find the light of your soul beaming brightly as you are guided and bestowed with a great gift of Divine Love. May you come to know your soul in deeper ways and continue to be curious about who you truly are, how God has created you and what God desires of your instrumentality, how He may touch the world, whether it be the world of the material or the world of the spirit. God will touch many through you. Allow this Love, allow your instrumentality, to deepen, to grow and to embellish your world, wherever you are. For in this way, you will feel a deep, satisfactory contentment that you are walking the path that is meant uniquely for you.

I am Lotus Blossom. May a great flowing of God's Love find its way into your soul as you continue your eternal journey of Love Divine. God bless you.
