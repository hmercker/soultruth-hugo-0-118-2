# The Power of Free Will and the Awakening of the Soul

October 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Aaron received by Al Fike

I am with you, my friends. I am Aaron, the brother of Moses, an inhabitant of the Celestial Kingdom and therefore entitled and given the opportunity and blessing to speak to you on matters of the soul and of God. God's power is immense, able to create the ongoing creation of the universe. He has the ability to make possible changes and conditions that will rectify all that which is out of harmony with His creation. Yet, side by side between God and the universe lies humanity and all who possess a soul in the universe. For possessing a soul is a reflection of God, but is also entitled to free will, to choice. Nothing else in God's universe has that ability and is endowed with soul in that way. Therefore, you carry a great gift and a great responsibility to choose well and to choose to be in harmony with God's creation. For the soul knows what that harmony is and how it can be expressed.

Yet, many in your world do not listen to their souls, the wisdom that lies within, and most certainly they do not wish to find that road to at-onement with God, which is this great gift that you pursue, that of Divine Love, the Essence of God. Those who do, those who choose wisely, those who seek out God and seek out the Essence of God's Touch within them to be given freely and abundantly, these are the ones who freely choose and who receive. In this, they are awakened, their souls coming into harmony, the empowerment of love and all gifts that lie within. For those who can see, those who are willing, those who desire, many doors open to them, much truth given and the possibility of being one of God's channels of love, instruments of change, healing, reconciliation, to bring the world that is out of harmony back to harmony.

These are the possibilities that a soul possesses, which also possesses the great gift of Love. Each one of you has made that choice and you have chosen wisely, have you not? Each one of you sees the road ahead is filled with light and possibilities. Each one has within you great gifts and abilities that you are still unable to see and know. Yet, in time, the awakening of your soul will reveal to you who you truly are and who God truly is.

Are you open and willing, my beloved brothers and sisters? Can you walk in faith and act in faith, enacting God's Will through you, enacting Love, the Love that is of God, expressed through you, seeking out those doors that God presents to you and opening them through your free will, your desire to do so? Within each of these doors are opportunities and possibilities yet to be imagined. Yet, God gives you the opportunity to step through that threshold and become that being of light, to awaken to the power of His Love, enacting and bringing to fulfilment the blessings and gifts that God has bestowed upon you.

Each of you is powerful. Each of you has the power of free will. Each of you can choose God or not. Yet, when you choose God, when you open yourself to the power of your own soul and the soul awakened by His Love, what can you imagine? What can you realise, understand? What that truly means and what that truly is. Each day, a door opens. Each day, a possibility is presented to you. Do you choose to walk through the threshold or do you choose to stand passively by and not be engaged in that which God has presented to you? God will not force you to that threshold. For this is a law, the Law of Free Will. Yet, God will offer it in the grace, humility and wonderment that is God. In His loving ways, He offers you the possibility of transformation, of great change and expression, that is of your soul.

Choose wisely, my beloved friends. Choose to be with God. Choose to discover the potentialities of your soul and to truly express those in the highest and most beautiful of expressions so that you may truly be God's instruments, channels of His Love in the world and expeditors of change, which is a part of His plan for the salvation of humanity. It is for you to choose. All is given freely. God does not judge you when you hold back, merely waits patiently. We do not judge you, but we encourage you. For we see the potentials and the possibilities that are within each of you and we encourage that expression in its highest form, which is enlightened and transformed by the Father's Love.

This is our mission to help lead you into that light, the light of the soul fulfilled, expressed and awakened with the power of God's Love. Are you willing, my beloved friends? You are able. Within you are great possibilities, powers and gifts that you have no idea exist. Are you not curious as to what is within you? Are you not eager to serve God and to serve humanity? It is for you to choose, beloved souls. We come to help you understand the true power of choice, the true power of the soul and God's willingness to heal and transform you in His Love.

May you continue on this journey, my beloved friends. I am with you as are many. I am Aaron. I bring you the strength that I carry, that you may choose enthusiastically, with wisdom, grace and joy, for that which God desires to give to each one of you. God bless you, my beloved friends. My love is with you. God's Love is ever there to receive. Seek it and all that you wish for, all the possibilities inherent within you will be realised with the Touch of His Love. God bless you, my friends. God bless you.
