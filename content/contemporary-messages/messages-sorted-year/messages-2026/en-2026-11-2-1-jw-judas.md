# All Souls’ day

November 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Judas received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, your brother Judas. Greetings to all, my brothers and sisters on this All Souls Day.

My brother here has been thinking about his ancestors and his family and his friends and even his enemies who have crossed over into spirit and so I wish to speak about honoring these dear souls. You may be aware there are many cultures, many religions, many tribes around the world who honor their ancestors. This is sometimes mostly erroneously labeled ‘ancestor worship’ which is only true in a very limited sense. For, most cultures around the world wish to keep the memory of those who have passed on before them and honor them, some seeking favor from them, some seeking intervention, and sometimes even material gain. This is a very ancient devotion, and it is still practiced in Chinese culture for instance, which of course would be non-deistic but could include Buddhism, Confucianism, Taoism, all which bring honoring the ancestors to the forefront. In Japan there is the largest group called Shintoists and they also honor their ancestors but it is slightly different as they believe in supernatural spirits, Kami, and they believe that sometimes their beloved ones have become these spirits and so they will offer gifts to appease them, to delight them, to ask for favors. Yet in other cultures, for instance many places in Africa, there is a belief that the ancestors who have passed on are more helpful in procuring favors than celestial beings. But in all these cultures there is, of course, the possibility that spirits who are not friendly, who are not harmonious, will need to be dealt with in forms, in ways of being protected. And I shall mention one more in Latin America and some of South America. There is the belief that simply remembering those who have passed into spirit keeps them alive and should they be forgotten, they will no longer exist. Of course, this is an erroneous belief but a loving one, nonetheless.

Now we have a very different perspective on those who have passed over. We seek for them to progress, to be healed, to be redeemed and forgiven, to forgive themselves, to forgive us, and us to forgive them and as we pray to progress our souls, we also pray for the progression of their souls. When we think of those in spirit, we should always send them love. When we pray for them, we pray for their souls and as we pray, those who look in on us receive the benefits of our prayers encouraging them. Those who have progressed in spirit and those who are our celestial friends, they are praying for our souls and encouraging us.

It is one thing to consider the end of life on earth, but it is quite another to consider eternity and how you wish to spend it. We have not lost those who have passed into spirit and they have not lost us. So, on this All Souls Day, let us send, with all the love in our souls and in our hearts, our love to our ancestors, to our friends, to our enemies, both here and in spirit, that all may be blessed in the love of God. I thank you for your time and my brother for taking the message. May the love of God be upon you and in you and through you. I am your brother and friend in the celestial kingdom.

I am Judas. Go in peace.
