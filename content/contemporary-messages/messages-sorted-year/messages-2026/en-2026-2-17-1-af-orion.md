# You Cannot Afford to Be Casual in Your Commitment to This Path

February 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Manifesto of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Greetings to you, my friends. I am Orion. There is a great need for shifting consciousness now, for the time grows short for you to have the luxury of sitting as you do in these Circles of Light and absorbing all the blessings and light that is given within these circles. Indeed, it would be easy for you to sit in the comfortable place of receptivity and prayer, knowing and feeling the blessings that come from the Creator and from all of us who are here to minister to you and to uplift you. We do not begrudge that opportunity. We are happy to be of service in this way.

Yet, change must come. A shift of consciousness must come, not just something that is minor and subtle and not well understood nor felt, but something dramatic, powerful, that will bring you to a place of truly comprehending and understanding your journey and what is happening within you. The mind continues to be dominant. You continue to allow this dominance to rule your life, to make the decisions, to make certain perceptions and understandings that are acceptable to the mind and yet can be quite contrary to that which is of the soul.

My beloved friends, you have designated your journey, this exceptional awakening that must come with the soul in alignment with God, a soul receiving the Essence of God. This is your choice. You have dedicated yourself to this journey, but you still are afraid. There is still resistance to that which will shift and recalibrate the focus of your consciousness. You wish to walk these two paths, one of the natural love, as you call it, and one of the Divine Love. Although you may have a reaction to my statement, it is true. To truly walk the Divine Path means that that great shift of consciousness must come to you and be accepted by you. That way, the perceptions of the soul, the understanding of the soul, the blessings and abilities of the soul must come alive and be expressed.

Many of you do not even know us or comprehend your angel friends. You have a slight and mild feeling of their presence, but you are not sure. You do not see. You do not comprehend. Yet this very foundational perception, this gift and ability to know our world and our presence and that of God’s world and God’s Presence must come to you in greater depth and experience if you are to truly realise a shift in consciousness and a shift in the way in which you relate to life and respond to life.

It does not come with the dominant mind and a subordinate soul. Rather, it is the reverse that brings what you require. Though your soul is strong, has received much Divine Love and that Divine Love is doing its work, affecting you in many ways, through the power of your free will, you choose the predominance of mind. That shift must come beyond this rather mild dedication to soul consciousness. It must come with extreme dedication, great faith, desire and willingness to go beyond this rather ambivalent state that does not allow extreme dedication to either one, but puts you within the middle and unfortunately, this does not place you in any condition and state that is useful.

Yes, you have experiences and insights. Things come to you. There are perceptions that are working for your benefit and we are happy that this is happening. Yet, because of the timeline that we are dealing with and what is required of you in the near future, you cannot afford to be casual upon this journey anymore. You must look at your own attitudes and desires, the way in which you have compromised toward truth. I do not say these things to criticise you and engender a feeling of failure or disrespect toward truth. Rather, I merely say these things to suggest that now is the time to step forward, to put aside the distractions, the mental reckonings, the compromises that keep you from God, keep you from your soul. If you are to be successful in all the ventures that you desire to be successful in, this shift of consciousness must become your priority at this time. The efforts that you make, the energy that you apply, the focus, the desire, the prayers, all that which is of the soul must come to the fore so that the awakening may begin and the shift is permanent. Rather than this meandering back and forth between these two states of consciousness, you must be firm and anchored within the consciousness of soul and the consciousness of God.

This will be your saving grace. This will assure that as you journey forward and as changes come in the Earth, you will be safe. You will make that journey toward greater height and pursuit of service for all of humanity. We dedicate our time toward you and for you, for this very reason, to help you find the ways and means to be uplifted beyond the mediocre, beyond this duality that exists within you, to that place of light, that powerful place of soul, that consciousness that comes as you allow it to emerge and be a part of your daily conscious self.

It is time, my friends. It is time. Can you take my message seriously, one that I have given you before? Are you willing to step forward without pretence, without your mind rationalising and compromising so that you are neither with nor without the deep comfort and wisdom of the soul. These things must come now. I urge you, I plead with you, I encourage you. I do so with love. I do so because there is little time and a great work ahead.

God bless you, my beloved and beautiful friends. I am Orion. I am with you on your journey, as are many, countless many who walk with you, who pray for you, who seek to uphold you. How sad it is that we cannot change you. That can only happen with your own free will and desire for that change and that shift that must come. God bless you, my beloveds, Orion loves you. We continue to be by your side always. God bless you. God bless you all.
