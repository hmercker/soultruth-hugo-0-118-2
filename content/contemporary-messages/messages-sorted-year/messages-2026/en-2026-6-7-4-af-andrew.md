# The Power of Love Bonds You as Brothers and Sisters on this Journey: Andrew Closes Johnson City Retreat

June 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Johnson City Retreat, Johnson City, NY, USA

Andrew received by Al Fike

The power of love will bond you forever, my beloveds. I am Andrew. As your souls seek to grow and expand with this great gift of Divine Love, so your propensity to reach out to another to build relationships, to be in that state of love for one another, will continue to grow and many others will come into this fold. Many others will benefit from your efforts in love, the bonds that you carry, the strength that comes from this love, for each of you carries a beautiful light, a part of that bond that makes it strong and durable and eternal.

Be truly brothers and sisters on the journey of Love. You will find joy there, great satisfaction amongst you, to realise that you truly have brothers and sisters to be with and to share life with. You have come together. You have broken bread together and mostly you have prayed together. In this, you have strengthened your bonds and created light, greater light, not only in this place but amongst one another. All that you do in the name of God will bring joy, harmony and peace, will bring you truth and the knowing of your own true self. You are children of God and you are brothers and sisters together. How beautiful your light shines. How beautiful we walk together in peace and harmony.

God bless you, my beloveds, my beautiful friends, for you have great friends in spirit in the Celestial Kingdom. You have great friends. Together we walk upon the Divine Path forevermore. God bless you, beloveds. I am Andrew. I am with you, your friend. We shall pray together many prayers, many hours with God. God bless you. My love is with you. God bless you.
