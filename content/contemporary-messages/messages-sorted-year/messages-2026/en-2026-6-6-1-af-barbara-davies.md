# As You Awaken in Love and Act in Faith, the Path Forward Will Be Lit

June 6<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Johnson City Retreat, Johnson City, NY, USA

Barbara Davies received by Al Fike

The winds of change blow through every life. I am Barbara. I come to be with my son and to be with you all. Since I have not been in the spirit world for long, I understand the challenges and conditions of your world. It is not a distant memory, but a very fresh one. As you have all said and affirmed, that it is faith, it is your relationship with God that carries you through, through the bad times and the good times, through all that life has to give to you. Indeed, each of you continues to grow in the Father's Love and your light shines bright. Together today, you have shone brightly. You are lights in the world and you are bringing light to the world. Beloved souls, how beautiful is your light. How beautiful is the Truth and the light that it brings to each one of you. How beautiful is the light that you bring into your lives and all those you touch in your lives.

Know that each of your efforts, each time you make an effort to bring light into the world, that it is appreciated and God is joyed by your effort. I urge you, my beloved friends, to choose to bring light with every breath. Choose to be a light in every situation. For how else will the world change? How else will the world benefit from your light and your instrumentality as God's angels on Earth, but to be willing to step forward, to step forward in faith and trust in God's Will and God's plan? For there is a great plan for the salvation of humanity. I know you speculate and you all wonder what that plan is, but you could not possibly fathom what is coming and how it will unfold. This is where faith is needed, to be true to the Will of God, to trust in the Will of God and to listen to God's guidance and Will for you. For in that, you are compliant with His plan and you will be in many different ways, through many different avenues of service, through many different situations and souls met.

There is much to this plan. It is complex and you need not worry about the complexities, merely to trust and have faith in the guidance that you are given with that day. That is your responsibility and it is not difficult. It is not complicated. It is merely listening and doing as you see as the path forward, the expression, the act of faith and service in the world. Each of you, each of you, my beloved friends, wants to serve God in the highest and the deepest way and you will, in accordance with your desires. These things will happen and you must trust in this. For as you continue to awake to the Father's Love and what the Father's Love brings in the awakening, you will find yourself compelled in many different ways, in many different situations.

Sometimes it will make no sense at all and other times it will be very obvious and sensible, your actions and your expressions, but this is where faith comes and is expressed. When you are guided and you have some trepidation, it is the power of faith, it is the strength of the Love within your soul that will carry you forward. Indeed, as you take one step in front of the other, what you are required to do, what is part of your task at the moment, will become obvious and clear. It is the momentum of moving forward, of stepping forward, of being willing and being of good faith, that is required, so that you may indeed fulfil what is asked of you.

Be faithful, my beloved friends. It is a cornerstone to your service, to your future, to your purpose. Love always. May your love for God grow and may it be an all-consuming love. May your love for yourself wash away any impediments, any doubts, any feelings of insecurity and judgement. May your faith and trust in the Father's Will propel you forward in all the tasks that are waiting, all the things that God wishes of you, and there are many. My beautiful friends, as you act in faith, live in faith, love in faith, you are truly being God's instruments and God's children. In this will be a great joy, fulfilment, the true purpose. You will understand what love truly is and how love can truly be expressed. Beloved, beloved souls, each one of you earnest, full of desire and full of gifts and abilities, you will find your way. There will be no confusion or doubt. You will know the journey you must take and God will show you the way in love. The path will be lit in Love. You will not hesitate, but be truly, truly following God's Will and God's Love for you and for all.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Barbara. I love you. It is an honour and a privilege to be with you. Indeed all will be well, my friends, one way or another, as you take the deliberate path of God's Will. All will be well. You will be in joy and you will be fulfilled in the Truth of God's Love. I love you. Barbara loves you. God bless you.
