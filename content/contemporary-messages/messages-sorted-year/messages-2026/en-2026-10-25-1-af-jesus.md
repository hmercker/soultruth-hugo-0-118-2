# Love Without Judgment

October 25<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the grace of God's Love be upon you, my beloveds. I come. Your brother, your friend Jesus is here. Can you judge your brothers or sisters? Are you qualified to say that they are more progressed and you are less? Do you have the perceptions of the soul that give you that ability? My answer to you is no, you do not. You may have perceptions and feelings toward an individual. You may have a quality and idea of mind of the state of that individual, but you cannot perceive what rung on the ladder they are in terms of their souls' qualities, conditions and light.

We, in the Celestial Kingdom, have that perception and that ability. For we are purified souls that have been redeemed in the Father's Love and this qualifies us in that regard. Do you think we judge any of you or anyone on Earth? Though we perceive and see you in ways that you cannot see yourselves, do we not have love and compassion for you? Do we not have our presence with you? At all times, we are with you. This is because, with the quality of Love within our souls and our connection with our Creator, our beloved God, the quality of love and compassion that we possess is great indeed. In this we have no reticence, no barriers to loving you, each and every soul upon this Earth.

Yes, we see the conditions, the choices, the terrible misdeeds that humanity foists upon one another, but we still have compassion and love. For within each soul is a magnificent potential, a possibility of being redeemed in the Father's Love. If this is not the case, that soul will, in time, come to a state of purity. All souls are destined to do so. Does this mean that we will wait until that time? No. It means that we are there to encourage and to help ensure that that journey is made. In this, we do not judge.

At times we cannot come close because the individual does not allow us to come close, but we may pray for that individual. We may gather around that individual and bring to bear the light that we have, the love that we have and the blessings that will come with these efforts. This is how we are with you, my beloveds. At times we are very close. At times we are somewhat distant, but it is not because we have judged. It is the law in effect, the Law of Attraction, that works in your favour or not. It is the result of your choices, your thoughts and your desires. If you feel distant from us, if you feel distant from God, then look within and discern what it is that causes this distance. For the laws are working, my beloveds. Indeed, the Law of Love is the most powerful of all and the Law of Divine Love is the highest of all laws. Yet, it cannot circumvent free will.

So you must choose, my beloveds. You have chosen to be here on this day in this hour. You have chosen to open yourself to God's Love. You have chosen to allow this Love to enter into your souls. In this, you have chosen the highest of blessings, the greatest of outcomes in your life. I urge you to continue in this vein and in these choices. For they will indeed bring great rewards to each one of you, beautiful blessings, not only to you but to your families, your loved ones, to those around you, to the world, the highest of blessings, the greatest of outcomes. This is what we seek for you. This is, at times, what you seek for yourself.

I urge you forward, my beloveds. You know the truth. You know the journey that needs to be taken. You feel yourself upon that journey. You are aware and, indeed, with each breath, there is a choice to love, a choice to be uplifted and to uphold the truth or a choice not to do so. For the powers of your earthly plane are great and they do influence you to a degree. Yet, as you continue to work toward the purity of the soul and its transformation in Love, the influence of the darkness lessens until there is nothing there but light.

My beloveds, we love you. God loves you. Love yourself and love one another. I command you as Master of the Celestial Kingdom, to love, to love all, including yourself, to have compassion for all, including yourself. For it is the power of love that dissolves the darkness, that disallows the judgement, that brings truth into the consciousness of each soul who desires this great gift. It is not the exercise of the mind and its organisation and calibration that does this. It merely brings order to your thoughts. It is the power of love within your soul that brings true forgiveness, true light and true compassion.

We are here, my beloveds, to seek out our own redemption, to release those conditions within us that bring us discontent and disharmony. Though the mind may fool us into thinking we are in order and harmony, the truth of it lies within the soul. For the mind has little to do with this, other than follow the lead of the soul's wisdom and condition. Walk with your soul. Be engaged with your soul. Allow the love of your soul to pour forth in all that you are and all that you do. You are then qualified to be a child of the truth, truly and beautifully.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend. I continue to be with you in love. I do not judge any of you. I love you, each one. Truly, my brothers and sisters, I am your friend in love. I am Jesus. God bless you, beloved souls in the grace of God.
