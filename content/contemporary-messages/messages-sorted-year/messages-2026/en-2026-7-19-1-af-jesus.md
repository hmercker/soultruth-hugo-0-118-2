# Walking Together Toward the Celestial Kingdom

July 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

**Jesus:** I come. I am your brother and friend, but I am also the Master of the Celestial Kingdom. I come with my garments and robes that designate this position, though I am not a king. That designation does not befit my standing. I am your brother and friend, as you know. Yet, I am also the leader of this great place and Kingdom that is where the Celestial Angels reside. I am with you in this way, the blessing and power of this position displayed for all those to see. As I come to speak, there are many spirits who see how I am with you. Indeed, I wish for each of you to see how I am, for in this regalia, there is a sanctity and a beauty that is revealed in a blessing of my presence.

I do so not to display power. It is not something that the mind, the material mind, can appreciate. It is that of the soul displaying its own standing with God. I stand with God, close and abiding with my Heavenly Father. I stand with you as my brothers and sisters upon the Path Divine. I am with you, each one. I walk with you. Though you may not know that I am with you, your soul responds. Your soul knows of my presence and my love for you. I act as God's emissary to bring the blessing of His Love that emanates through me and around me. I pray for each one of you, a hand of blessing upon you, for I am God's greatest instrument, the most powerful in the Heavens. I utilise my gifts, powers and blessings to uphold you all and to help all those in the world, the world of material and the world of spirit, so that they may see, know and realise the manifestation of God's Love incarnate in the soul that I am. For I represent the power, glory and potential that each soul may reach and may come to be.

In this eternal journey of soul progression, as I am, so you may be, my beloveds. As I express the Light and beauty of God's blessing of Love upon me, this is my own personal and unique expression. Indeed, each of you may express in your way, in the particular beauty that you are, your souls resplendent in Love. For we walk a path unique and yet together. Our path leads to at-onement with God. We may be truly brothers and sisters upon that path, awakening together, sharing our blessings, our gifts and our love with one another. This upholds the great movement toward the Celestial Kingdom and the completion of that Kingdom, which will one day be complete and one day we may all reign as God's beautiful redeemed souls, our lights bright beyond imagining, our joy deeper than the ocean, our gifts, like jewels, flashing and bringing light and blessings to many.

We shall never end our endeavours to uphold the Truth of God's Love. Now we work together to uphold this Earth, so tired and weary of the weight of human endeavours and disharmonies. We work together to utilise our gifts, our light and our love to help awaken humanity to the potentials of their own souls and the possibility of at-onement with God. We endeavour to walk in the purity of this light, to walk in the glory of this light and this truth. We are truly beautiful channels of love in the world, my beloveds. We are as one body, moving, bringing light, peace, healing and love to the world. How the world hungers for this, though they know not what they hunger for. How the world is in great need of relief from these burdens, from the lack of peace, the lack of harmony and the lack of love. We do our work together, seeking to do God's Will in the world, seeking to be a channel of love in the world.

My beloveds, follow me upon this path of service. May you know the gracious Touch of God, that peace that passes all understanding, the glory of His Touch upon each one of us. May you come to know this, my brethren, truly know it and understand the depth and breadth of His power, His blessing upon you. That you may be redeemed in the Truth of God's Love. That you may be transformed by it. That you may be truly blessed by His holy kiss upon your soul, given freely, given through love and through God's desire, that you may know Him truly, that He may be within completely, that you may know the glories of that relationship, the oneness with your Creator.

We walk this world together, my beloveds. We are as one in the glory of God. We shall be that light that shines bright, those blessings that flow, that love that is demonstrated with every breath, every moment that you exist upon this Earth. May God's Love flow abundantly in His plan for the salvation of mankind. May you find your way, my beloveds, truly express your purpose and that which is within you in all its beauty and glory. I will help you to see this. I will show you the way, my beloveds, for I am your brother and friend. I am also Master of the Celestial Heavens and shall be so for all eternity. I lead the way. I do so with joy, with true humility, with grace, with love. I am with you and I love you truly. Come to know me, my brethren. Come to know me and you will know how much I love you, how much I honour and appreciate the work that you do, the efforts that you make, the light that you strive toward and the Love that you really long for to enter into your souls. I honour this and I love you, each one.

My beloved friends, I love you. God bless you. God bless you, my beloveds. In the glory of His Love, we shall know ourselves, know one another and know God.

**Al’s description of his vision:** Today, during our Zoom prayer session, Jesus came, as he often does, but this time he appeared differently to me. Rather than coming in his usual long white robes with a blue-coloured coat, he appeared every bit a monarch. He wore a long magenta cape that sparkled with flecks of gold and diamonds sewn into the fabric. Along the edges of his robe were patterns and symbols, which I understood to be Celestial language, embroidered in delicate gold filigree.

When he turned, flashes of gold shone like stars, and streaks of colour from the diamonds filled the space around him. His auburn hair was crowned with a gold band, which also bore symbols outlined with rubies. At the front of the band was a small peak of gold, filled with a starburst symbol at the centre of the crown. In his right hand, he held a golden staff topped with an oval stone, which acted like a magnifier for whatever light and blessing he wished to send to those around him.

His appearance was stunning and visually dynamic. The light that radiated from him, and from his vestments, created a spectacular display of spiritual energy. All the spirits around him bowed or knelt in his presence. The atmosphere was charged with regality and authority. I was overshadowed, and he spoke of his position as Master of the Celestial Heavens. It was a memorable and vivid experience, one I hope never to forget.
