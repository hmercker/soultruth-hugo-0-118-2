# God Never Intended For Suffering to Exist

May 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

North Vancouver Monthly Circle, North Vancouver, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

I am Mary. I have heard you speak of the suffering in the world and I know that you realise that God wants the suffering to end. I know that you understand that we too wish the suffering to end, for the suffering is not necessary. So many believe that the suffering that they carry and have experienced in life brings meaning, brings a depth to their soul and because of the conditions of the world, this can be the case. Yet, it was never meant to be, my beloveds. Suffering was never meant to be. For God created the Earth with great potential for harmony, for peace, for all things that are of light. For all that God creates has this potential, this possibility to be a light, to be fulfilled in light and harmony, to progress forward in the beautiful ways that God has made possible.

Yet, for humanity to know God and to truly know themselves, God gave the gift of free will. In this gift, there has come a great price. That price is suffering and disharmony, for humanity chose to walk that road, often unwittingly, but indeed this is the case. We come to help humanity to lift up beyond this human condition, to lift up into a place of light and enlightenment, that there will not be ignorance, strife and a lack of love. Rather, the healing balm of God’s Touch may be given to every soul.

We pray for this. We in the Celestial Heavens, you on Earth and many, many more and work diligently to bring about the healing of this world of yours. For here the soul starts in its progression and in that foundational experience of Earth. Would it not be wonderful if it were a place of harmony, peace and love, rather than all the challenging conditions that continue to beset humanity and bring strife?

My beloveds, beloved and beautiful children of God, continue to make your efforts to awaken to truth, awaken to your own true self and awaken to God. You will come to know your destinies, the trajectory of your life and the true purpose of your being. Is this not what you seek, my beloveds, to truly know who you are and where you are going? It comes with a soul awakening and that awakening comes with God’s Love. Seek out the blessing. You shall know the rewards. It is given freely. It is given to each of you. Open, awaken, transform, heal and be in the light. What more would you want in this world? All the distractions and material gains mean nothing if you cannot take it with you as you progress into the spirit world. Yet, these things that I encourage you toward are the treasures that you will take with you into the world of spirit and they will benefit you greatly.

Be true to what your soul longs for. Be true to what you know is true and be in the grace of God, my beloveds. We will be close as you seek out light. You will draw those of light to be with you, to uphold you, to protect you, to guide you and teach you. This is how God works in the universe and it is a wondrous creation, wondrous laws, wondrous expressions, expressions of God’s great creativity. My beloveds, I am with you. I am Mary and I love you so. Indeed, many are with you at this time. God bless you all. May you receive His Love in abundance. May you know that we love you. God bless you. God bless you.
