# You Have Everything You Need

May 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Peter received by Elizabeth Mack

My brothers and sisters, ahead of you, you have much work to do, much joy to have and much love to give. This is part of your future as disciples of the Master. You have learned much here. You will learn more as you go about your time on this Earth plane, giving your love, teaching your brothers and sisters, helping them to hear you, to understand, to feel the truth and to come to know the Love. There is much that you will be doing and doing so successfully, for they are eager to learn, they are eager to find God, they are eager to follow the example of Jesus, his praying, his loving, his teaching and his ministering to all those upon the Earth and to all those in the spirit world. You have the additional help of the Celestial Angels. You have the additional help of the stellar friends. You have the protection and guidance given to you by God through the archangel and you have Divine Love growing in your souls.

I am Peter. I began with less than what you have now. Yet, my soul drove me to let people know what the Master was trying to teach them. My love burned inside of me. I prayed and I prayed some more and I taught. My brothers, the apostles, and I went separate ways so that we could teach as many as possible. You will not be alone. You will have many unseen, redeemed souls with you. You will have God directing you.

Keep your eyesight on God. Keep your heart open. Let your love flow outward and let God's Love flow into you. This is a recipe for success in this endeavour to reach as many souls as possible. Remember you are protected. Know this in your souls, your hearts, your minds and your bodies. Know this, that God is leading you. While some will follow, some will not. Continue to give your love and seek only the Heavenly Father's Love and guidance.

I am your brother Peter, sometimes called the Rock. I am one of many who will be with you with encouragement, guidance and love. Open now to the Father's blessings, which come with this Love, which is so great and so continuous. May God bless you. Know that I am with you.
