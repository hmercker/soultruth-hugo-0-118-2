# Pray for These Tender but Greatly Hurt Souls

January 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

Bartholomew received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here, my beloved brothers and sisters, in the Light of God. I am Nathaniel (Bartholomew). Worries of this world are not necessary for you to concern yourselves with, although praying about it, about the lost souls and all that you can do to assist God in the transformation of this Earth is more in line with your purpose. You need not worry. God has everything in hand. Yes, there will be strife and conflict. These are mostly in the souls of humankind, manifested in behaviours.

This can be your focus. Pray for these souls who are so contorted that they truly do not know where the truth is, what their true goals are or what their real purposes are. You must have compassion for these souls who are unknowing, blind and so confused. Consider how forward-thinking you all are, how you all know the truth and you have been equipped with God's Divine Love and all that comes with that, while these other souls, some aimlessly walking upon the Earth and some very, very confused and hurt are unknowing.. God will absolve them when they are ready to be absolved, because God loves them as much as God loves you.

So, dear ones, pray. Pray for these tender but greatly hurt souls who cannot find the way. Pray for them. Sometime in the future, your prayers will be answered for them. Hold on to that truth as things descend into more chaos. Stay true to your path. Stay true to God and true to your own being, which is becoming at one with God. This is where your focus is and will keep you in harmony as well as safe.

May the Father bless you abundantly, this day and all days upon this Earth and beyond. I am your brother, Nathaniel (Bartholomew). I am here with you and will continue to be with you. My love for you is great for each and every one of you. May God bless you, my dear, dear children.

2026-01-04-EM-Bartholomew (G)
