God bless you, my friends. I am Orion, here once again. The world is waiting for this, my friends. As we continue to make our progress, as we continue in our dedications, in our efforts in development, we inch closer toward that time when we will answer the world and answer the world in ways and means that will catch their attention and their wonderment.

My friends, we continue upon this arduous journey. You continue in your own personal development, which is crucial. We all continue to grow in soul awakening, the nurturance that comes from that universal Love of God. It is here with us now. It continues to be a part of our lives. The goal is to inform and point the way for this Love, this grace, to be a part of every life. This is why we come from such a distance to your lovely planet, in order to spread that word, that understanding, that awakening that must come. For every planet that has evolved and grown in light, has understood the way of doing so.

It is the way of the soul, awakening and developing and transforming. For the faculties and abilities of the soul are far greater than that of the material mind. When these two aspects of your being come together with the intermediary of the spirit mind, the sky is the limit, my friends, in what you may accomplish, even in the material sense. For as you continue to grow and awaken on all these different levels of life, so the wisdom, the perceptions and the abilities to understand that come from the soul consciousness are informing and enlightening the rest of your being. As you and others continue to grow and to develop in your creative ways, avenues of living and being in the world, so this wisdom helps you to moderate and to understand in a way that brings harmony, the creative aspects and tools of your being, to assist humanity upon the road of progression and to provide for humanity comfort, peace and harmony.

As you have seen, merely developing the mind, the material mind, does not bring you to that place of harmony. The missing component is that of the soul, so we stress and continue to educate, inspire and direct you toward soul development. This is the key to everything. As you continue to aspire toward these things, you continue to long for and receive that great gift of God’s Essence, so the capacity and abilities of your being to have its effects, its light, its inspiration, its creativity in the world increases. Its alignment with soul and mind makes for a formidable avenue of expression. These days upon your world require true understanding of the power of love. With love and acceptance and peace and all things that are of light and that follow the Laws of Creation, then you may create an atmosphere, conditions in which other things may progress as well, technologies and ways of living that will bring harmony to your world.

Yet, you first must understand the key component and that is love, not just the human love that you experience in your life, but the spiritual Love that comes from God. When the power of love is exerted through you into the world, then it changes the atmospheres, the very molecules and components of your material existence. It changes everything and it brings it into greater harmony. Thus, with harmony comes peace and with peace comes a thriving environment of light that will bring about all of these manifestations, gifts and awakenings to humanity. We focus primarily on the key component of love, that of Divine Love. Until that is truly understood and aspired toward, then all of these other things that I speak of cannot possibly be realised.

The foundation must be set. Here we are, my friends, creating that foundation, allowing ourselves to be in alignment with the great truths of the universe, the great Truths of the Creator of the universe, the great truths of the soul. As you inch closer to this, so the possibilities of other realisations, manifestations and accomplishments may become possible and probable. We are still at the beginning point, the first stage of true spiritual awakening and development. Your evolution awaits you. The world awaits you to evolve as you should.

I urge you on, my friends, so that we may step beyond the first stage and carry through to others that you will be astounded by and truly impressed with, not just with the mind, but with the soul, awakenings that may come, understandings that may flow, truth that will be yours and embraced wholeheartedly. So much awaits you, my friends, and so much of the world awaits your efforts, the conditions that you carry, the awakenings and revelations that come to you. We continue. We continue, and Love, that component so vital, continues to grow within you and you continue to understand its power and ability to change and transform everything.

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and my love is with you and I seek to assist you, to help you upon the road of truly understanding what love is. Blessings to you, my friends. Orion loves you. God bless you.
