# The Unlimited Blessings of God’s Love

May 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours, my friends. I am Faith. I am happy to be here with you today as we pray together, forming this Circle of Light. Even though you come from different parts of the world, you are united by the great Lattice of Light, by the yearnings of your soul, by the blessings of God and the presence of your angel friends. For much is given to those who are sincere in their prayers and desires to grow within their soul with His gift of Love. We are drawn because of it and we are happy to be with you and to pray with you in this grace that is the Father's Love.

My beloved friends, do not hold back in your desires and efforts to be with God, for when you are clear and strong in this faith and prayer and desire to be in the grace of God, then all else will come. All blessings are given. There is nothing that God will not give to you if it is in harmony with His Laws of Love and that which is in harmony with your life and your desire of soul, so the potentials, the possibilities of gifts and blessings, are unlimited. I urge you to be open, to awaken to what God can give, to be in the grace of God always, to walk with Him in all that is light and love, harmony and peace.

My beloved friends, there is so much waiting for each of you, so many blessings to come upon you. Put aside all material concerns and be with God today, this moment, for He is here, the angels are here and we are all here together in the oneness of this gift of Love, in the oneness of God's Love for you and all that that is and all that that carries into your lives, blessing you abundantly, helping you along the road, helping to release all that is not in harmony with His Laws of Love. It is given freely and abundantly, my beloveds. Breathe it in, feel its Touch, feel the upliftment that you may come into that Hall of Peace and know the blessings therein.

God bless you, beloveds. I am Faith. I love you. I am praying with you, as are many. God bless you all in His Love.
