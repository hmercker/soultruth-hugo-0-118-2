# Memories of Your Prayer Circles Are Within Your Souls

June 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

Mary received by Elizabeth Mack

God bless you, dear children. I am your sister Mary. I come to bless you with my love. I am welcomed into this circle by your love and the Love of our Father. It is a joy for me to be here, closer to each of you.

How you are progressing, how you have progressed in these times, as you look to God, as you allow your souls to be open wide and receive His blessings! This is simple, is it not? To open, to receive, to express your love. How complicated some human beings make this and how simple God has made it for you, as His Love flows continuously, looking for a soul to touch, an open soul that will receive His highest, most sacred gift.

In the many, many, many months to come, there will be times when you recall these moments with your brothers and sisters in prayer. You will know as you reflect upon it that the Love was ever present, the Love from God flowed into your souls, the union with your brothers and sisters, where there was love, was ever apparent, and the presence of your angel friends, stellar friends expressing their love to you. All of these things are within your souls. When you pray by yourself, although you are not ever by yourself, but when you pray without being on the wires or being in the presence of your brothers and sisters, these memories will be in your soul and you will be able to reflect on them. It will be as if you are here praying together, sharing the love, feeling God's Love and growing in this Love.

These memories are part of you and you can recall them anytime you choose. When you are praying by yourselves, take a moment to reflect on prayers that you have shared with your brothers and sisters, those seen and those unseen. You will feel their love once again, as well as the Father's Love. Your soul knows, your soul remembers, your soul loves. This is the experience that your soul desires at all times. Reflect on this and May God bless you. My love is with you and I do so love you dearly, so, so dearly. I am Mary. I will step back and pray with you now, along with thousands of Celestial Angels. God bless you, dear children. I bless you. Heaven awaits you.
