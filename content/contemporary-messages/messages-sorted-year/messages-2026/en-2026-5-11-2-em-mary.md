# Expression of God’s Love Within You

May 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Mary received by Elizabeth Mack

I am with you in love. I am your sister Mary, mother of Jesus, my beloved son and the first beloved son of God. I welcome all of you into this Circle of Light and give you my blessing of love. Those who are here because of the light that has drawn you, we welcome you. Those who are curious, we invite you to pray with us. Those who are here, who walk this path, know that your open souls will bring about more of God's Love just for the asking, just for the desiring, just for the longing, for you know the peace that it brings, the joy that it brings and the Love that it brings.

We in the Celestial Heavens understand, comprehend and are conscious of how meaningful is this Love to the growth of our souls, to the development within of those gifts that our Father has bestowed upon us. There is much to be done with these gifts and in you too are gifts given with every prayer. Seek to know what they are. Find your way to deep within your souls, longing to know what God has asked you to do with these gifts, for this is the fulfilment of God's Will for each of you, to receive this Love, to receive the blessings that come with this Love, to utilize this Love and these blessings on behalf of all of your brothers and sisters all, including those in the spirit world, including those upon the Earth plane and including those across the universe, for in this Love you are all one.

Words cannot express how deeply this experience is of this Love in its expression. Regardless of where you are, only the experience of it manifested from your souls through your spirit body and, those of you who are in the flesh, through your flesh as well. Regardless of where you are, regardless, the expression of this Love from your soul is the most exquisite and unique expression of God's Love through you. Do not hesitate. Do not draw back from these expressions of love. Remember, express this love first to our Heavenly Father and ask that you be shown how to express it to each of your brothers and sisters, wherever they are. With these expressions you will develop in ways that you cannot imagine and you cannot understand until you do so, until you express this love.

May the Father's Will guide you and bring you closer and closer to receive more of this Love. May you be open to receiving everything that our Father has for you. My love is with you and I bless you with my love. I am Mary, your sister, and I remain to pray with you. God bless you, dear children. God bless you.
