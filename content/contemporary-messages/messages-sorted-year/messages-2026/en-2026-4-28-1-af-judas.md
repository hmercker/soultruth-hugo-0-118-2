# The Power of Free Will to Reinforce or Counteract the Harmony of God's Universe

April 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Judas received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved souls, I am Aaron (Judas)*. God created a universe filled with benevolence, a universe that is designed to progress in harmony, a universe that, by rights, should have peace and love within it. All of God's creation follows this edict other than those who have free will, those who have been created with a soul. For those who possess a soul can progress beyond obedience to the laws and be independent in their actions. Indeed, you on Earth know this well. The purpose of this independence, this free will, is so that the souls of humanity may progress and have the potential to progress beyond the limitations of God's creation. Potentials that reside within the soul can only be realised when the individual's will is involved and when the individual desires and asks for the greater Love that comes from the Soul of God.

Thus, it is a double-edged sword. The individual's will can bring hardship or bring great blessings. It is up to the choices of those individuals, each and every soul, to realise that their thoughts and deeds, their desires, their efforts, their prayers, all have their effects within them and around them. Each individual soul is imbued with great power, great ability to grow and progress spiritually and mentally. Indeed, they can exercise their gifts and attributes to great benefit to them and to others. Yet, if the laws of God's creation, the Laws of Love, are ignored, then there, too, are consequences to this. This is why humanity suffers as it does. For you have not grown and matured spiritually. Humanity does not know its own self and its own potential. Humanity merely responds and reacts to the circumstances that are a part of a life that is a reflection of free will, so there are great struggles and great disharmonies as a result.

Again, the power of man shows up in adverse ways, but it does not need to be so. The power of man to choose light, harmony, love, knowledge and truth can indeed be utilised to uplift one another. You are all upon that path of upliftment and to be an instrument to uplift others. You have chosen wisely. You are coming to understand the power that you possess, the power to choose, the power to receive and know the great gift of the Father's Love. In this, you become liberated from this dreadful human condition that holds so many people in darkness, ignorance, confusion and pain. You are discovering the levers of freedom. These levers are truth. These levers are the understanding of how to utilise your gifts and free will to your advantage, to align them with God and God's creation, to be fully with God in your desires, in your intentions, in your thoughts, in your prayers, in all that you exercise as a human being.

You are able to step into light rather than encourage the darkness and be engulfed by it. Thus, you are different and you are precious in the world. For you carry the torch of truth with you. Indeed, that torch must be held high so that others may see and know it, and that you may express with love, grace, with wisdom, knowledge, harmony and peace the truth that is your creation and the potential that lies within you, your soul beaming forth, seeking to be in alignment with God and God's Will, seeking to be God's instrument, seeking to be utilised in ways that may bring truth, enlightenment, light, love, all the goodness that is part of God's benevolence, His Love for humanity and for the universe. So much in the way of potential continues to lie hidden within you. Yet, you will find your way, my beloved friends. You are finding your way upon this Path Divine. Indeed, you will become leaders and teachers, healers and servants of light in the world. You are learning the ropes, so to speak. You are testing the waters, understanding what it means to be that channel of love, that instrument of God. Each day affords its opportunities and possibilities to do so.

My beloved friends, you are truly blessed. You are blessed not because God chose you. It is because you chose truth and you chose to pursue truth. In this, you have forged the path in this world that will bring light, that will be guided and that will afford the opportunities to serve humanity. This is inevitable because the power of Love within your soul is drawing to you the blessings and helping you to understand and abide by the Will of God. It is not that your free will is subjugated. It is that your will and God's Will bind in alliance together where you may serve and know the way of service, the path of service, and understand the opportunities that God lays before you to bring blessings to others.

My beloved and beautiful friends, you are a light, each one. Indeed, you shall be a brighter light with each day that passes, for you are well on your road to truly being transformed in light and love. In this, you will be a formidable instrument, a powerful servant of light, truth and God's Will. May you be blessed upon that road, my friends. Your feet are upon it. Each step is important. Each hurdle that you rise over brings you greater strength and wisdom and a greater capacity to love and have compassion. Each choice you make will bring rewards, provided those choices are in harmony with God's Laws of Love. Many blessings, my beloved friends, shall continue to be yours, shall continue to guide you along the road and shall continue to bring you opportunities of service and the joyfulness of being a light in the world.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am here with you. (This instrument felt Aaron close at first, but I am Judas*). In truth, it was me. Indeed, at times, the medium can be somewhat confused as to the identity of the speaker when he is not familiar with them. I bring clarity and I thank this instrument for allowing me to speak. God bless you all, beloved friends. God bless you.

*The medium initially believed it was Aaron speaking. At the end of the message, the angel made a correction and advised it was, in fact, Judas. The recording dipped out at this exact moment, but a paraphrase of the missing words have been added in consultation with the medium.
