# Your Ability to Dispel The Darkness of This World is More Powerful Than You Think

May 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. Such a disharmony is in your world. The power of that disharmony can be overwhelming. It is the result of human will and human desire, exercising their free will to create chaos in your world. Such a contrast between yours and mine. Because we put love first, because we understand the Laws of Creation and the Laws of Love, harmony comes as a result.

In your world, it is a very different scenario, creating a miasma of darkness all around your planet. The expressions of humanity in all its different forms and ways bring this disharmony in such a powerful form and expression that it is difficult to penetrate. It is difficult, as you well know, to live within. Yet, as you seek God, seek out His Love, seek out harmony and peace within and around you, you penetrate this condition. It reaches God and it clears the passageway of light, a benefit to you and for those around you. This allows us, who are creatures of light, to make contact, as it does for many others who are of light.

The mechanism is simple. The power of God's Love, the power of your desire to be in light, to be in harmony with truth and with the Laws of Creation make this so. You realise that even though you are humbled by the conditions of your world and by your own life and its somewhat mediocre expression, can you not realise how powerful you have become to neutralise these incredibly difficult conditions? This darkness, so potent, is a great expression of free will and desire. Applying the laws that you have been taught and the Truth that is Divine Love has worked to your benefit and penetrated the darkness into light and brought light through the darkness.

My beloved friends, you are far stronger than you think. You have a potency that comes from your souls and the desires of your souls. It is only your material mind that considers all the aspects of this material world. At times, you feel impotent, helpless to change this world. Yet, you have found the key that will do so. You have found the power of Love. With utilising this blessing, the faith that comes with the experience of this blessing, the guidance that comes, the understanding and perceptions that come. These things of the soul are what will change the world, not of the mind, as you have been taught, as is the general perspective of your world. No, the things of the mind are intransigent and incapable of truly finding solutions that are in harmony with universal harmony and creation.

You were given the gift of a soul and that is for good reason. Within it, you may unlock all the potentials, the perceptions, the ways and means of bringing harmony to yourself and to the world. The very foundation of this is love, not merely the love that you possess and were born with, but the Love from the Creator of the universe, the Love of God. These two loves combined create a formidable force for change. It is changing you at this very moment. It continues to awaken you and transform you, to cleanse you and to equip you with the knowledge, perceptions and wisdom that you require in order to be a channel, an instrument for change, an agent for God in this world of yours. Thus, we are concentrating our efforts with you and all of you who are seeking this great and potent transformative gift of Love.

For this is the way in which the world may change. This is the avenue of true change. As you apply your experiences and your perceptions, your wisdom, your love toward your daily life, then great change may come. Expect this, my friends, and do not think of yourself as unworthy or incapable. Rather, as you build that great light within you, as you continue to pray and receive that great gift of Divine Love, you must sit in that knowledge. Look back upon your life and see the changes that have come thus far. It becomes obvious that a force is building within you that is truly changing your perspective. Your entire being is being blessed by this gift. The potency is greater than anything else in the universe. It is the Essence of the Creator. It is the power of change, transformation, to bring harmony, to bring progression toward greater harmony. It opens the faculties of the soul and awakens all that is within the soul. Without it, the soul is a rather two-dimensional thing. There are capacities and abilities of the soul that may be awakened through natural progress and cleansing. Yet, the true ability and potency of the soul is not realised other than through this great gift, given, desired, received and desired again and received again, until there comes a time when the soul is resplendent in Love and light.

As you well know, it requires your desire, your efforts, your prayers, your time with God and we honour you for what you have applied thus far. Again, I encourage you to go further, to seek, to know, to experience, to put aside the worldly concerns and to truly be with God. For as that portal between you and God continues to grow and be strengthened, so your capacity to be receptive to the gifts and the blessings given grows. That channel is cleared. There is no more distraction. There is no more desire for other things that are less important and less potent. Rather, all revolves around this gift of Love and continues to have its effects and continues to change you, or does the change that is required, the change that comes from the cleansing of your soul and then the awakening of your soul, change that comes when the faculties of the soul awaken and arise into your consciousness and become a living, breathing part of your experience of life.

At times, you are not so attuned to these things, but those times lessen and you become more attuned as you progress forward, whether you realise it or not, whether you are mindful of it or not. It continues to be in the forefront of your consciousness, although you may interpret it differently. Your material mind may see things from an entirely different perspective. In truth, the consciousness and mind of the soul is emerging, is growing stronger, is making its presence known and is being expressed with every breath. Listen, absorb, observe, acknowledge and love what is happening to your being. Be attuned to this. For this will not only assist you in days to come, but will assist others, as God puts you in the forefront of leadership during times of great need, chaos and trouble. You will be called upon, my friends, and it will not only be you. For you are merely the spearhead of a great retinue of bright souls who will accompany you in your ministrations in the world.

Yet, you must be ready. The readiness is well underway. Yet, you continue to resist within the mind. You continue to entertain many fanciful, mindful things, so the passageway, the clarity, is not clear as it should be and needs to be. It is time to truly accept who you are, what is within you, to be clear and intentful of what you need to be and how you need to express your own self and how you need to be in alignment with the Divine expression and Will, so that when the time comes, there is no faltering, there is no back-stepping, but merely stepping forward. In this way, a potent force for change and light will clear away this darkness, to bring truth, to be clear channels of love and even to manifest your beautiful angel friends and ourselves and others. Yes, all of these things will come about, but it requires the solid and clear dedication of what is important and needed, the awakening of your soul. For there lies within all the tools, all that is required to be that channel of light, to be that potent force in the world that is guided by the Divine and a channel for all that is of God.

May you be blessed, my friends, upon that journey. We are with you. We continue to uphold you, to encourage you, to show you who you truly are and will continue to do so until you no longer inhabit this planet, but in other spheres of reality. We are with you, as are many. May you be blessed with this gift of Love. Open yourselves now, my friends, that a great inflowing may come, may wash away all the impurities, all the doubts and fears, biases and prejudices, all that is not truth, to bring truth and love, harmony and light to you all. God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and I love you. Oh, how I love you. God bless you. God bless you all.
