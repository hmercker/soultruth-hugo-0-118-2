# Though We Start with the Physical, Many are Blessed in Spirit

September 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Retreat of the Four, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you. I come. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. Those who are my disciples shall be blessed accordingly, protected and guided, inspired and opened as God's channels of love in the world, as each of you makes your commitments to serve, so these blessings shall come, my beloveds, so you shall know the light and the upliftment, the fullness of God's presence with you. All that is required to serve, to be that channel of truth and love in the world, shall be given.

You see this with this instrument, this beautiful child, this disciple of mine. It will be with you, my beloveds, as you make a deeper commitment to serve and to be within that flow of service in the world, a world so in need of you, a world so bereft of light, truth and love. We continue our journeys together. The blessing of God's Love and Truth shall be carried on the wings of love, on the blessings of God and my personal protection and blessings for this instrument and all those who participate in bringing the Truth of God's Love to others.

When they enter the hall, it may appear that the numbers are meagre, but do not forget that the spirits are many. Although those who represent the flesh are often not so cognisant of the truth, nor do they bring it into their hearts and minds in such a way that will change their lives, the spirits that surround them, the many hundreds and thousands that surround them, are being blessed deeply, profoundly. This is a great part of the work that we do. We start with the physical, but indeed, much of what is blessed is in the spiritual. We continue on our sojourn to bring truth to the world, not only the material world, but the spirit world. This is and shall be the way until such times as humanity opens its ears and eyes, and is eager to hear, hear and learn and come to know God.

Although those in the flesh may not be so cognisant in a way that reflects their wakefulness, their souls are receiving the truth. Their souls shall know the truth and shall respond to the truth accordingly. My beloved disciples, brethren of truth,  it requires patience and humility, the capacity to serve humanity without judgement, but with a desire to love and bring comfort, to comfort the many, comfort the lost. Comfort the ignorant. Comfort all those who are seeking to bring the warmth of God's Love into their midst, that they may bask in this light and know its blessing that it may tease open their souls, that they may receive a portion of this gift and, in time, it shall leaven the soul and bring desire, conscious and true to God.

Indeed, blessings. Even those of you who are not within this blessed circle who travel may add your prayers of support and love. They will be appreciated and gifted to these beautiful souls who travel and the beautiful souls who are eagerly awaiting their presence. God bless you, my loved ones, beloved brothers and sisters upon the path. We shall carry on. When one moves forward, all move forward. A blessing for all. A gift of Love from God. God bless you, beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I love you. I love you so.
