# We in the Celestial Kingdom Wish to be Close to You

February 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved souls. I am Mary. I come to love you, to embrace each one of you, for you are my beloved children and I am God's channel of love for you, as are all the angels of Heaven and my beloved son who comes and is with you now.

The angels wish to be close to you, my beloveds, not just in an abstract way of your thinking and believing that they are there, but in a real and palpable way that you may feel our presence. Know us as individuals, as God’s angels who come to love you, to be with you in prayer, to help you and to help guide you. There is so much that we can give to each one of you. The closer our rapport together, the more you are aware and can benefit from the blessings of our presence. There is much that we can do to help you, to uplift you, to heal you, to protect you and to inspire you. We yearn to do so, for we are God's servants and, in being God's servants, we wish to apply our gifts and our light to those whom we serve.

I ask you to be aware of us. I know that each of you are humble servants of God. Each of you are seeking out God and to know God in your prayers. I do not wish to discourage you of this, but I say that in the time you take, may a portion of that time be set aside to be with us and commune with us, for this will help to build the rapport, will build the light, the blessings and the communication with us. We do not ask each of you to be a medium and speak as I do through this instrument. This is not our intention, but our intention is to be close, that we may speak to you directly, that you may have the powers of perception, the clarity of perception and hearing that may allow you to know us, to see us, to feel us and to hear us. Each of you will have that experience in your own unique way. Each of you will come to us as a friend, a teacher and one who loves God. We will be those things for you, not just in these times together, like this one, but in every prayer, every time you reach out to God, may we come to you.

You have guides, gatekeepers, teachers and healers that all come and help you. Would it not be a beautiful blessing that you will know us and feel our ministrations, our presence and our light in your lives? There is much for you to accomplish and realise in your lives as you walk this Path Divine. There are many lessons to be learned, many releases of conditions and healing of conditions that are required and much love to flow into your soul, fueling all of these blessings. When you are ready, we will be close. You will know us and you will be guided by us in a conscious way. We will help you in your life, even the material things of life. We will assist you.

My beloved friends, beautiful children of God, this path has many layers and many aspects and facets to it. As you grow within the gift of the Father's Love, these facets are available to your conscious self. We wish for you to focus upon these things, rather than be overly focused upon the material things and the consciousness of the material mind. When you are focused on us, you are more focused upon your soul. We will help you to come to understand your soul, to be aware and to open to it. Oh, my beloveds, there is much for you to experience upon this eternal path of love. There is so much available to you. We encourage you to open your eyes, your ears, your mind and your soul to all of that which is in harmony with God's Laws of Love. In this, much will come to you, many insights, feelings, impressions, communications, visions and awarenesses, so many things of the soul to be conscious of, to nurture and to live your life with and by.

My beloveds, God has many blessings in store for you, much to be given in all the ways that God can give to you. First and foremost, you must build the foundations of your soul and, in this way, open the faculties, open to God and open to all the blessings that God has to give to you. He is here with us all, empowering the angels, empowering you, mixing us all in the unity of His Love and making us true servants of His Will and His plan for the salvation of humanity. If you are to be an effective servant, my beloveds, you must have these faculties available to you in a conscious way to live your life so that you are with God and with your angel friends working in collaboration to bring greater light to the world.

Be that light, my beloveds. Be that beautiful light shining forth with God's Love, pouring through you to many other souls. It is time for you to awaken to your potentials, to that which is within you, that which desires so much to flow through you, to be a part of your consciousness, to be gifts that you may use for your own awakening, for the benefit of your families and loved ones and most of all, for the benefit of humanity. God's plan waits for all of us to be in that condition, that state of grace that we may act flawlessly and fully within the parameters of God's Will and plan for the world. It is for you to choose, as always, for you to pray for this, to long for this and to be within it, in the reality of God and the reality that is God.

My beautiful, beautiful friends, I am with you in love. My son is with you in love. Many, many angels gather around you and bring you into the Hall of Peace, that place of nurturing where you are free from the conditions of the world and truly in alignment with the blessings of God. God bless you, beloveds. I am Mary and I love you. I am with you, always nurturing, uplifting, loving you, loving you all. I do so within the flow of the Love of God, which flows over you at this moment, blessing you deeply, my beloveds. God bless you. God bless you all. Mary loves you. God bless you.
