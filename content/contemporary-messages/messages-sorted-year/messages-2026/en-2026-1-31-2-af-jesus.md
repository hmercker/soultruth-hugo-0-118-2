# I am God's First Son Who Has Been Transformed by His Love into an Angel

January 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved children. I am your brother and your friend. I am God’s first son who has been transformed by His Love into an angel. I am Jesus. I come to affirm the words of my beloved sisters and brother. Indeed, the gift of His Love, that redeeming grace from God, will transform you, heal you and bring you upon a road of love and soul transformation that is beyond anything your mind can conceive.

It is a place of peace, healing, harmony, wisdom and much more than this. The road of God’s Love is the highest road that one can take in their spiritual pursuit. There is nothing else like it in your world or in the universe. It is a gift that shall give to you for all eternity, if you but aspire to receive it, to know it, to feel it in your heart, to be close to God and to know that tender Touch of God within you. If you can open your hearts, my beloveds, feel that Touch and ask to receive this gift within your souls, then you will know what has been described and taught, beyond that of the mind, but of the true feelings of the soul, a soul that longs for this. Every soul wishes to be close to God. Every soul feels and knows that direction, that place that is God. God does not judge nor restrict. He does not hold back His Love, but He gives it unconditionally to every child who seeks it.

Be that child, my beloveds. Be that child who seeks the Touch of God, the power to transform and redeem. It does not require books of learning or ascribing to certain edicts or ideas. It merely requires your longing and prayers for God and in this your prayers will be answered. Your longing will go directly to God’s Soul. In this, the law is answered. Its response is given through the Touch of the Holy Spirit. It awakens your soul in Love.

My beautiful beloved brothers and sisters, it is the message I have taught for all of this time, since dwelling on the Earth and now dwelling within the Celestial Kingdom. This message continues to be shared, taught and given. Even those who do not understand the words will feel it in their hearts as true and divine. They are touched and shall continue to be touched by God’s Love as they pray for this gift, as they long for God. My beloved and beautiful friends, be that child longing for their Father, longing for their Creator, longing to be in alignment with God. It is given to you here in this room. Light has formed. Angels surround you. This is a perfect environment to set forth your prayers and longings so that you may hear, know and feel the Touch of God within you, given by this mechanism of the Holy Spirit conveying the Love to your soul.

It comes from outside of you. It comes from God. It comes within you and resides there and will never leave, will never leave, my beloveds. It is a gift given for eternity. As you continue to long for this gift and seek it out, each prayer given to God, each longing put at God’s Feet, then you will receive more and more of the Substance of God. It will truly transform you and bring you into alignment with all of the laws and edicts of God’s creation. You will know God like you do not know God now. You will know love like you do not know love now. Your natural state of love will be added to and transformed by the gift of God’s Love. You will be a different person. Your soul will be awakened by this gift. You will come to see and know the universe of God’s creation like you do not know now, but can know by this gift, by its transformative powers to awaken your soul and bring it closer to God, the Creator, the Heavenly Father.

My beloveds, we gather today to learn this truth, to not only learn it but to experience it for all its wonderment, glory, depth and breadth, to expand your soul like nothing else can do, to transform your soul like nothing else in the universe. This is a direct gift from your Creator and it is offered in loving kindness, mercy and grace. Ask for this, beloveds, as we pray together. Ask for this gift. You will feel the burning, the inflowing, the presence of something truly blessed, unique and powerful.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come because each of you are seeking. You seek the truth and I come to give you the truth in the way of love and the wonderment of this gift. God bless you, beloveds. My love is with you. I am present with you all in prayer. God bless you. God bless you. “Open my soul to the great inflowing of Your Love, beloved Father.” This is the only prayer that may be spoken that opens the gates to your soul and awakens you to this gift, to seek it out with all your yearning, all your heart and all your desire. It will be given in proportion to the intensity of your longing and your desire for this gift. Together, may we ask God to open our soul to the inflowing of his Essence, His Love. God bless you.
