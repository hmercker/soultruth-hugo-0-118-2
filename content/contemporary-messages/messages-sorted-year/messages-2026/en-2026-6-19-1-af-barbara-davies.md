# Awaken the Soul to God’s Love

June 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Barbara Davies received by Al Fike

Oh, yes, we do, Darlene [^1]. I am Barbara. We all love God, for God is the Source of all Love. When we open our hearts and open our souls, we are asking for that gift to flow into us, our soul, which will be there forever. What a beautiful gift this is. What a powerful blessing for our lives. Each one of us continues to long and gather and long and pray and gather, and again and again, to be a part of that great flow of Love that is God's gift to us all. My beloved friends, there is so much within you that longs to be awakened. There are so many parts of you that you do not even know exist within you. There are gifts, truths, insights, experiences and connections that are all a part of your soul. Your soul continues to desire to be awakened by God's Love, so that it may all be conscious and alive and truly a part of your life and your consciousness.

My beloved friends, we come a long way to be with you and we do so in love. We do so because we are enthusiastic and excited. We desire for each of you to follow the road that we have taken, for you to truly understand what it is that is awaiting you, for you to truly feel the joy of it and know that this journey is the greatest journey of all. As you continue to awaken to what is offered by God and awaken to what is created by God within you, and as God brings us together in love, so the joy builds within you, so the strength and the perceptions, the abilities to see and to love, to assist others, to be wise and to be joyfully giving to others, to uplift them, to help them to see their way beyond the conditions of the earthly plane to something far greater and more wonderful, something that is for everyone. Be sure and tell your brothers and sisters that God wants all His children to know the joy of His Love. God wants all His children to awaken to His Love and be truly that soul that they are meant to be. So few understand this, so many are distracted, downtrodden and unhappy. Yet, you have found the key that dissolves all of these things and makes life beautiful and fulfilling.

My beloved friends, I am happy that we are together today. Know that there are many spirits that join you. Indeed, many layers throughout the spirit world, there are circles upon circles joining you and reinforcing the light, the blessings from God and the capacity for the angels of the Celestial Kingdom to come and join you and to come and join all who are together in this great beam of light that goes heavenwards and to the Creator of all. My beloved friends, thank you for taking the time, for being together as you are. May you know the fulfilment of your soul's desires. May you feel the changes being instilled within you by God's Love. May you know your true self and have that powerful and wonderful relationship with God. All this is available to you, my friends. All this is in accordance to your desires, your willingness, your prayers and your openness. Be open, beloved friends. Be open to God and you will know the joy of it, the wonderment of it, the beauty of it, the depth and breadth of it. His Love awaits you. Sing to God your desires, the desire of your soul. God will respond with melodies you have never heard before, with the music of the soul that is the most beautiful of all.

God bless you, my friends. I am Barbara. I will sing with my soul as you sing with yours. We will reach out to God and open ourselves to a great inflowing of His Divine Love. God bless you. Barbara loves you. God bless you.
