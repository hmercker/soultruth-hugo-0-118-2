# Spiritually Cleaning Up the Environment

February 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Isaiah received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Isaiah, a prophet of old, as you would say, one who was on the Earth before the Master came and one who attempted to enlighten souls upon the Earth at that time, to change the ways in which they were expressing themselves. Many years ago, before the advent of the coming of Jesus, we were in a deplorable condition, we, the human race, were in a deplorable condition, hurting brothers and sisters upon the Earth, creating havoc in the environment with our hatred, jealousy and lack of connection with our Creator. All of these things were part of the universe, of the Earth, its atmosphere and environment. All of these things contributed to the necessity of the Father sending our beloved master to the Earth.

Yet, even then, the souls upon the Earth were unwilling to change their ways and ignorant of what was being taught to them. So this plan that our Heavenly Father has at this time to bring forth more of His Light into the world through many children upon the Earth, many of His children upon the Earth, is going forward. The Earth is changing, becoming a more habitable place for souls who are progressing. This is the only way it can be, to progress, to integrate into greater harmony, greater light and greater love being expressed among the human beings who live upon this Earth.

There is much that needs to be removed from the environment of the Earth. Hatred continues to exist. That must be reversed and love must become predominant. The jealousies, the pettiness, the anger, all of it needs to be eliminated and love, whether natural or divine, must be present in much greater quantities and expressions upon this Earth. It is an imperative so that souls may continue to live on this Earth.

I speak to this now slightly differently than when I walked the Earth, but the message remains. People who walk this Earth must change and, with God's grace, God's sacred gift that is available now, it will happen. Yet, you who live on the Earth, you are the ones who must help others to understand, to comprehend the importance of letting go of all of those things that keep them from receiving God's Love. I and many other Celestial Angels are here to pray with you to this end, that souls living upon the Earth and souls in the spirit world open and recognize God's gifts for each soul. We come to pray with you, to uplift you, to motivate you to continue in your progression on this Divine Path and to help your brothers and sisters come to know of the sacred gift of God's Divine Love.

We are with you, true children of God. We will continue to be with you. Our love is with you and the Father's Love is always available to you. Open up now and allow that sacred, transforming Gift of Love directly from the Soul of God to enter into your souls and transform you a little bit more. Praying often and deeply each time will increase that transformation of your souls and activate the love for your brothers and sisters, which will help them to learn how to live in love upon this planet.

I thank you for listening. I thank you for hearing. I thank you for your contributions to the transformation of the souls upon this Earth and those in the spirit world as well. May God bless you mightily this day. I am Isaiah and I am happy to be here with you today and be by your side as you pray to our Father, the most glorious soul that exists. May God bless you and I love you.
