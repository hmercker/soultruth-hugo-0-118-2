# Lifting everyone in the Spiritual Collective of Divine Love

September 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Judas received by Jimbeau Walsh

You are all familiar with the saying attributed to the Master: *judge not, lest ye be judged*. If we make this applicable to our interactions in the world, it opens up a door that always looks for the good. It opens up a door of soul perception which, unlike the mind, which is an organ of discrimination and can compare one thing to another, (the soul) always chooses light and love when it is activated, as in this prayer in the heavenly Father’s essence. For every soul has something to offer another soul. Every interaction can be a lesson learned in progression, in growth, when it is delivered in love.

You come together in this beautiful circle, lifted above the earth plane in this celestial connection, which informs you and guides you in these days together of the best ways for each unique individual soul to move forward in community. I encourage you to take this opportunity to share your guidance with all. If we begin to see through the eyes of our souls the preciousness and value of every soul, we are part of lifting everyone up in the magnetism of the spiritual collective, as I shall call it. No matter where you may have your abode upon earth, the love of God connects you all, for there is no distance in it. The separation that one feels when removed dissipates in the great love and great light which the heavenly Father bestows.

So, we welcome you in your time together as you explore the progressions of your souls and move forward as a community. When you ask for assistance, it will be given. So, allow your hearts to be open to each other, that God may bless each soul, that we may be at one in His love. May the blessings of God’s love be upon you each, and may the joy of your communion and your community pervade the atmosphere as you spend this precious time together. With all my love and blessings, I shall be here for you and many, many others. I am your brother and friend, for all eternity.

I am Judas, once of Kerioth, now your celestial brother. God bless you.
