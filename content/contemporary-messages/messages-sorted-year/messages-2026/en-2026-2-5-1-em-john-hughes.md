# Praying Together Is Joyous

February 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

John Hughes received by Elizabeth Mack

We are pleased also that this group comes together again. I am John, John Hughes. My sister Joan, Joan of Arc, is here also and there are many others who join us in this prayer. We are grateful for this time together that we spend in prayer with our brothers and sisters who live upon the Earth, in the flesh and those dear souls in the spirit world who come and join in prayer.

It is the Father's Will that we pray together wherever we are. The veil is thin, our love is deep and the Love of God’s children is growing in their souls. As such, we are becoming as one and one with God. How joyous, how fulfilling, how much in accordance with God’s Will that we come together and pray and express our love to one another. This is the greatest gift that the Father gives that we have love to share with one another and it is His Love, His Essence, that makes it possible. We are grateful, we in the Celestial Heavens and we know many of you upon the Earth are grateful. Many of you share the understanding that you have about your gratitude toward God for all that God provides. This is good and as you express your love to those souls who are unaware, they too will grow in gratitude as they experience this Love.

We thank you upon the Earth, those of you in the flesh, for the work that you do and we see the joy that your soul experiences as you do this work. It may not feel like work, but it is as the Father desires that this Love is spread across the Earth to all of His children. Those of you upon the Earth who are knowledgeable about this Divine Love, who are receiving this Divine Love, every time you pray, every time you think of God, every time you open to receive His blessings, are doing this work. You are sharing and expressing this love. It is joyous, is it not? Well, I just wanted to say that it makes my heart, my proverbial heart happy to gather again with my brothers and sisters upon the Earth.

God bless you, dear true children of God. May you be open always to the blessings that are always available to you. God bless you. I am John Hughes, soulmate of this medium and great lover of our Heavenly Father.
