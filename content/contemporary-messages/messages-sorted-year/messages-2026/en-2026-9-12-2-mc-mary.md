# Allow This Living Substance of Divine Love to Transform You

September 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Mary, the mother of Jesus, the one who is the Master of the Celestial Heavens, the one whose mission and purpose was to come to Earth to teach the Truth of God's Love. This beautiful gift that has been returned to every soul, whether on Earth or in spirit, to be able to create that personal relationship with their Maker, the one we have all been created by. Within your souls, each one of you is a uniqueness where no two souls are alike and oh, how God desires for you to come to know that precious jewel that you are in His creation. How He desires for every flower to blossom in a way that only they can. How God loves to watch you, to care for you, to love you and guide you to awaken you in His Love, the Divine Love.

As you begin to listen, feel and perceive your soul in its longings, its desire for truth, you will find yourself awakening to the longing for God. As you learn to listen and to respond to the wisdom of your soul, the true nature of who you are, you begin a journey of soul to soul with God, awakening the light, receiving the Essence and Substance of God, transforming your very nature, where in time you are love, you are love divine and it is only love that emanates from you in all of its beautiful facets and ways of creation.

My dear friends, I am here to stand in the middle of your circle to bring healing to you, to shower you with the Love that my soul has been transformed in, allowing my instrumentality to serve God, God bringing through me the blessings that each one needs to help you to awaken to your longings and begin your journey, or to strengthen your journey directly to God. The blessing of a Celestial Angel can walk with you if you so desire to take up this mantle of allowing God's Love into your soul and this beautiful angel will support you, will come to influence you so that your thoughts can be in the highest nature possible toward God. Ask God at any moment to help you, to awaken you, to release you from your struggles, to have those things that operate within you that are not of love to be transformed, to be put down so that this Love, this living Substance of Divine Love, can live through you. I can promise you that it is a journey that only you can take and only God can bless you with.

Come to know our beautiful Creator in all the glory, the wonderment and the great peace that is awaiting you merely by attuning your material mind into the heart and soul of your very being. May you be blessed richly and deeply in this Love, these Living Waters, cleansing you and awakening you. I am Mary, conveying this message of love to each soul, whether in the flesh or in spirit. God awaits your longing and your desire, the invitation to inflow His Love into your soul, making you anew. God bless you.

God bless you all. I am Mary and oh, how I love you! How I love you! God bless you.
