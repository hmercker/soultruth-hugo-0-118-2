# As You Are Less Distracted by the Material World, the Flower of Your Soul Will Bloom

October 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

May the blessings of God's holy Love continue to inflow into your souls. I am Orion. I come once again to speak to you about the soul, about what is available to each one of you and to all souls within the created universe of God. This gift is given, this blessing, abundant and freely given.

My beloved friends, you see your own development, your own awakening in fits and starts, in revelations and often dry periods of nothing. Yet, in truth, this development, this awakening, is happening every breath. The power of God's Love continues to have its effects within you and within the consciousness of your soul. I would urge you to be attentive, to be aware, to be sensitive to these subtle changes within you. For if you allow yourself to consciously be aware of these things, then you will progress more quickly. Rather than this interrupted journey of awareness, it will be constant. It will be a blessing, an awakening that is like a flower. Although subtle as it opens and may seem slow in your mental processes, in truth, it is constant.

I ask you, my beloved friends, to be aware of your own journey. Be aware of the blessings from God that are pouring upon you. As you accept them, as you embrace them, so the effects are felt and the effects are evident. As you are less distracted by the material world and less enthralled by the needs of that world, the needs of your body, then your awareness, your experience of God's blessings, God's Touch upon you will become stronger and more palpable. That door continues to open, not just a crack, but to open further and further, allowing the Light of God's Love to flow and to envelop each of you. Allow this to be your daily experience. For it is your daily life. You have established this relationship with God. You are continuing to awaken to this. You are at the stage now in your development where this constant awareness, this flow and the awakening, may happen more smoothly and readily in your consciousness.

Consider this, my beloved friends. Consider what may be yours. For if you do not allow this to be, then you are missing out on a great journey, a great benefit that comes with God's Essence. There is much for you to discover, much for you to discover about yourself, about the universe and about God. Indeed, I encourage you in that discovery. There is so little that holds you back at this time, but because of the exercise of free will, it continues to impede you. Some more than others. Some have very busy minds indeed and that busyness is a great obstruction to the clear sensitivity, the quietness of the soul and your going deep within that quietness and that awareness of soul.

Yet, as you continue to make efforts and strides in this direction, it will become easier. You have seen how it has become easier. Yet, you must guard yourself against the distractions that come from without. Much of this is orchestrated by the needs that are around you, by the obligations and conditions, but you will develop a strength, a condition within you that is somewhat detached from these things. You will be free of its burdens. You will know yourself as truly a child of God rather than a child of man.

This is the goal. It is close, my beloved friends, so very close. Allow it to be your reality. Allow it to be the cornerstone of your life. In this way, the gifts that you possess, the instrumentality that is you, the expressions and outcomes of your day, will all be centred on God rather than centred on ego, mind and material wants and needs. It is available to you. As you continue to inch your way forward into these conditions of light, love and harmony, following the Laws of God's Love, so what God asks of you, whether that be manifestation, materialisation or merely a gentle touch upon another soul, or continued and greater love toward yourselves, all these things will come more readily. For they will come through the consciousness of soul. As the consciousness of soul becomes your condition, what you long for and what you express within your daily life, so these things will manifest. These things will flow. They will awaken in a deeper, wider, more fulfilling way. The wonderment that you have about your purpose, your abilities and your guidance will be transformed into knowing, feeling, perceiving and expressing, all of these things.

You are close, my beloved friends. Continue to seek the gift of the Father's Love. Continue to be on that path, that flow of His Touch upon you, this awakening of soul, ever seeking its effects, grounding and integrating within you, in your consciousness. It is there for you, my beloved friends, always and evermore. For you are on that path and will never leave it. Yet, the pace at which you travel, the pace at which you become truly a part of it, is entirely up to you, your will and choice. It always is and always will be, my friends. Yet, as you find your way upon this path, so your ability to teach others will grow. Your example will be evident. Your light will shine. All that God wishes for you to do will flow in a powerful, gentle and graceful way in the world. Many will benefit from your efforts, your decision and choice to be within the flow of God's Presence, God's Will and God's Love.

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and I love you so. Beautiful flowers, budding, petals opening one at a time. Oh, we are excited to see what unique and precious flower you become, that you are. Indeed, God creates you all unique and so it intrigues us as it must intrigue you, who you truly are and what you will truly become. God bless you, my friends. Orion loves you. God bless you.
