# Soul Consciousness Over Mindful Beliefs

January 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Grace Jordan received by Maureen Cardoso

Good morning. I am Grace. I too am in great anticipation for the days ahead as you gather with the beautiful souls of this nation. As your instrumentality changes, you will experience within your soul that nudge, that desire to go deeper, the wanting of your service to be expressive of the truth in deeper ways. As you travel this path of soul awakening, the experience of the change in your instrumentality that you feel from within your soul is much different than your own projections of your mind.

If you find that you are mindfully making your decisions void of the perceptions of your soul, your journey will be one that has more obstacles on it. If you allow yourself from your soul consciousness to experience the wisdom and the guidance, the directive from within this place of you, then harmony is certain. You walk a road that is paved with grace, with peace, a path of service that feels aligned, that is joyful. These are markers on your journey, that you are being guided by your soul and that your soul is informing your actions, your mental perceptions, your wisdom. Allow the grace of God's timing to move through you, to move you and you will be certain that your journey will be filled with opportunities of service that are in alignment.

Each one of you grows in your ability to be in harmony from your mental constructs. There is more soulful awareness coming into your consciousness. You are observing a change of your mindful understanding of your experiences, of the path forward, how you see the world and how you see yourself. These small changes that occur within you are necessary, for the path is step by step. This is a path that walks within the Laws of Harmony, the spiritual laws of God. If you are struggling within yourself, you must check which part of you is in the forefront, your mind's free will choices, or your soul's will informing your choices.

It is not difficult. It is a simple path. You are learning, growing and coming into alignment and merging the mind and soul’s communication. Allow yourself to come to really know, experience and feel your life from the inside, your soulfulness. Allow your soul to inform the decisions that you take. In this way, all things unfold with God's Hand, with God's wisdom, through God's Love. Take your cues from within. This requires your ability to be still in order to perceive what is within you. It is a blessed journey and one that I am so pleased to be part of, helping guide you, loving you and shining my gifts and my love upon you.

I am Grace. May your day be filled with the Light of God's Love. God bless you.
