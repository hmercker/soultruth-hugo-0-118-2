# Encourages Us to Engage With the World and Confront Its Many Tests Head On

May 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Blessings and greetings to you all. I am Orion. I realise how difficult it is on your earthly plane to continuously be focused on your spiritual condition, to be attuned to God and in alignment with the great Creator of the universe. This is not so difficult where I live, but I see great difficulties where you inhabit, in this very densely material plane and condition that is your Earth. Yet, there are remedies to this condition, the situation, if you but utilise the Laws of Attraction and blessings to you that are given and known by you.

My beloved friends, when you are wrapped into a condition, a condition that carries you into disharmony, there is a choice available to you, a choice to either continue to be enmeshed within that condition, or to step back, say a small prayer, realign yourself, attune yourself to your soul, and have the blessing, the awakenings, the dissolution of dark conditions and thoughts in your state of being. This can be done quite readily if you so choose to step back from these conditions. Yet, if your choice is to continue to engage and to be attached to these conditions, then there is no remedy available, for you have chosen otherwise.

I know how difficult it is on the earthly plane. You must engage in a great variety of conditions and a great many individuals as you conduct yourself with daily tasks, errands, travels and situations at home. Yet, each of these are not as powerful as the power of your own free will to choose beyond the mundane, the grayness, even the darkness of human interaction. I am not suggesting that you remove yourselves from these conditions. You cannot be a monk in a cave and pray and be with God always. This is not a balanced life. Yet, as you go out into the world, each moment, each time you are there, engaged with life, there is the possibility of a blessing and the probability of a test that you must endure. As you practice spirituality in your daily life, then you will be able to hone your condition, your thoughts and your choices to a place toward harmony, light, peace, wisdom and love. Yet, it must be a conscious choice, because in your world there is so much that happens that is unconscious. There are so many interactions that are reflexive, a reflection of past experiences and patterns of behaviour.

We are asking you to change, to recalibrate, to be in a state of mind and spirituality that is of a higher calibre and of light. In order to do this, you must practice and engage in the life that is presented to you and consciously choose that which is of light and harmony, that which is loving and which brings a resolution toward harmony and peace. This is not easy, with the cross currents of thought and emotion. The entire atmosphere of your Earth is polluted with dark matter, thoughts and conditions that are contrary to what I am speaking of.

My beloved friends, you have quite a challenge to be that light in the world. Yet, you have asked to be that light in the world. You have prayed to be a channel of love in the world. You have prayed to be an agent of change to bring greater light and harmony to the world. That practice is very important. It is meaningful. It is how you may move from one point of perception and expression to another. It is constant and relentless in its challenges and its conditions. Yet, you are up to the task. You have much in the way of support. You have your angel friends, your stellar friends and bright, beautiful spirits all around you. God is with you. You could not have any more than what you receive now. It is a tremendous influence, a tremendous condition of light that is surrounding you, my beloved friends.

Yet, often you choose otherwise as a result of those mindful patterns that still exist within you. We cannot countermand these thoughts and actions. That would be against Laws of Free Will, but we are there to help and if you ask for help, it will be given. A small prayer will change the complete condition and atmosphere which you are in, if you but say that prayer. If you ask for help, then you are allowing a different condition, condition to formulate and to infuse the atmosphere around you. It is not difficult, but you must remember. You must choose. You must realise the support that is given, the blessings that are all around you and the love that is poured upon you. In this way, you become a strong, formidable, clear and beautiful instrument in the world.

Yet, if you do not practice, if you are not challenged, then how do you grow? Yes, there is a certain amount of symbiosis and blessings that come as you pray and are with God. This helps you and this supports you. It grounds you in the light. In the atmosphere of your own home, you may have the blessing of that beautiful condition, but you cannot be at home always. You must venture out into the world and we wish you to do so. God wishes you to do so. For how else can He utilise your gifts and instrumentality if you are not out in the world?

Human contact is crucial. Engaging in different atmospheres, environments and conditions is important. For if you are to be an agent of change, how might you change these conditions and atmospheres if you are not present? Yes, you may pray for various individuals and this is important, but this is not what I am speaking about. I am speaking about actual connections and contact in the world. It is important for you to realise that even though it is difficult at times, even though with many of you who are extremely sensitive it can be challenging, it is still important to counteract those inhibitions, those fears and those uncomfortable experiences that often accompany these interactions.

You will get stronger, my friends. You will get wiser and rather engage and be of the world of engagement in this way, you will be in the world, but engaged differently. You will be a strong and formidable force that others who easily cause disturbance and are disrespectful will think twice in your presence. Those who wish to foment negativity will not come close to you, for they will feel a discomfort, a difficulty in being with you. Yet, those who are in need, those who are emotionally distressed, those who require healing, those who are lonely and desire comfort and connection, these are the individuals who you need to reach out to. These are the conditions and situations which God will guide you toward, that you may be that instrument in a condition that may be of great need and maybe great distress.

Yet, you are strong. You are full of light and you have compassion and love. You are just the individuals that they need to connect with. As you are willing and as you grow in your ability, the wisdom of soul that will bring about a healing or a resolution or possibly a look or an embrace that is needed at that moment, then you will realise that God will use you in many, many different ways. As you walk in these humble and small ways and become strengthened by them, then the possibility of greater impacts, more powerful instrumentality and more wonderful expressions of your beautiful soul will be an expression, a true instrumentality realised within you and through you and around you. It takes time. It is incremental, as are so many things in one’s spiritual progress. Have compassion for yourself, but also be brave and willing to step forward and to be that beautiful instrument that you are meant to be. Those humble situations are potent with possibilities and with opportunities.

Walk in the world, beloved friends, but not be of it. Be true to the wondrous capacities and gifts and abilities that you have stored within your soul. Allow these things to reach your consciousness and your awareness. Honour them. Allow them to be expressed, to be truly a part of your condition at that very moment. You will find many blessings will come. Many interesting, sometimes strange, but often gratifying and rewarding situations will come to you. You will be shown and guided. My beautiful friends, it is important to act, to be that channel of love in the world, to truly listen to your guidance, to be God's instrument and channel of love. There are many opportunities available to you. All you need do is step out the door with a prayer, *"God guide me. God show me the way. God protect me. God help me to be an instrument for another."* With this simple prayer, you will open up a whole world of experiences and connect with many other souls.

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. I encourage you to be yourself, to use your gifts and to be a light in the world. Be that channel of love, my friends, and all will come to pass that is meant to be and blessed by God. God bless you. God bless you all. Orion loves you. I honour each one of you, beautiful and unique creations of God, as am I, as are all who possess a soul. God bless you, my beloved friends.
