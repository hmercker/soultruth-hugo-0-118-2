# Don’t Limit Yourselves

June 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Liverpool, NY, USA

Joseph received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Joseph. Yes, I live in the Celestial Heavens. Yes, I am the father of the Master, as well as his brother in this Divine Love. We come to grant you your prayer requests in accordance with God's Will. We pray for these souls. We touch these souls for whom you are praying. As you pray we also lift them up in accordance to their will.

We seek what God seeks, which is the transformation of the souls of His children, so it is good that you pray for them. Pray for them often that they may begin to recognize that there is so much more than what they have been used to so they may begin searching for the truths that their souls long for.

This is why you are blessed again and again, because your souls realize that there is so much more, so much more on this Path of Divine Love, so much more to be had: greater happiness, greater Love from God, communing with God and having that sense of peace, of contentment and of joy. We Celestial Angels honour you souls who are not yet redeemed, but are on the path to soul redemption. We honour you. We pray for you and with you. We come close to you that you may feel the love that we have for you and it is our privilege to love you as we do. And dear children, you do the same when you go out and about and be close to people and pray for them. Your love touches them also. You make their days a little bit brighter and you stir something inside of them, something that they want more of.

So children, continue to pray for your brothers and sisters. Don’t  limit yourselves. Pray often, pray for all of them. When you go out, allow your love to be expressed, that your brothers and sisters may have that experience of true love expressed. We are guiding you and you are being protected as you go out. Continue to pray for the Love, for your progression and to love your Heavenly Father with everything that you are.

I welcome you to this sphere where there is much love, a special place for you to pray and commune with God. I am your brother, Joseph. My love is with you and I will be with you as you pray. I will pray with you while I love you at the same time. Much is being done on your behalf at this time. Trust in your Father's Love.
