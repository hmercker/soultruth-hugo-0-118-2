# Your Primary Purpose is to Receive Divine Love and Come Home to God

June 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

James received by Elizabeth Mack

My love is with you as the Father's Love flows deeply into your souls. Open, receive, allow. I am your brother James. I come with a cadre of souls, some of whom live with me in the Celestial Heavens, some who are high spirits along this Divine Loving Path and some of whom are visitors from other places in this universe. We all bring our love to you and we all partake of the feast of God's Love, which is pouring down upon us all. For is that not why we are here, to receive this blessing, this connection with our Creator, our Father, our generator of Love?

This is our purpose in gathering together. It is our purpose, even when you pray alone. It is our sole purpose to receive the Divine Essence, to experience connection with God, to fulfil our mission upon this Earth and well into the spiritual realms and the Celestial realms. Always, it is because of the seeking of this Love that we are able to continuously progress, continuously connect with God, our Source of being, our Source of Love.

Take this deeply into your souls, dear children. How important is your connection to God, your purpose to receive this Love, to exercise this Love upon all of your brothers and sisters, all who God has created and to express that love first and foremost to the one who created you.

This is your mission. This is your Truth. This is your purpose, to come home to our Creator. All else are expressions of this Love. All else is secondary to this longing for God's Love. Drink in His Love now, children, as He continuously pours it and makes available this Love.

It is my pleasure, my honor, to come speak to you of this Truth. I am your brother James. I will always love you and the Father will always love you, carry you and bring you home. Bless you, dear ones. I bless you with my love and may you receive all that God has for you. God bless you.
