# This is a Path that Encompasses Everything and There is Nothing Higher

September 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Augustine received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. It is your teacher, Augustine. We enjoy witnessing your curiosity, your knowledge and your wisdom as you ask, as you respond and as you share because it is in this way that you progress to some degree. There is a stirring within the soul that responds to something that has been said. In this way, you are all helping one another. For when a question is asked from one who you may consider *"new"* to this knowledge, to these truths, then those who have had some experience, some soul progression and carry some wisdom within the soul can respond. In this way, you are helping one another because it is one thing for the soul to carry wisdom and another thing for that wisdom to be expressed through the words and the concepts of the individual.

This path, as you know, is a path of the soul. When you speak through your essence and you allow your material consciousness to reside in your soul consciousness, you can inform greater truths than when you potentially use the words that you express through the material knowledge. It does not touch as deeply. You are all growing in this experience of your soul awakening in truths, where the facets and the qualities of your soul are developing through God's Substance. As you bless yourself with the opening of your soul and receive that great inflowing, part of that Substance that enters into you carries the wisdom and knowledge of truth.

This is why a soul who chooses the path of Divine Love will also obtain universal truths, those places, those aspects of truth that one who travels the natural love path is very drawn to and spends a lot of time in developing their mental faculties. One who follows the Divine Love path by absorbing and being transformed by God's Substance will also obtain spiritual, universal truths. In this way, it is a path that encompasses everything, has no limitations and provides eternal at-onement with the Creator. That does not mean that you never arrive at at-onement, but it means you continually expand in truths. For there is no end.

Bless yourselves every day with a great inflowing of God's Love. Have faith and exercise the faith that you, as a soul, are being nurtured in Love and Truth by God directly because this Substance is becoming who you are. You will experience the transformation of your nature, becoming all loving, compassionate by nature, not having to manufacture patience and not having to manufacture joy. For these are the qualities that you are when your soul is being awakened in God's Love.

There is no higher teacher. There is no higher transformer. There is no higher comforter than the Love Divine, the very Substance of God. The power of sitting every day and at moments throughout your day in taking the Substance of Love will carry you on a path of awakening that is true. It is filled with joy. That changes you and releases the human conditioning that you carry, transforming it, releasing you into blessings of joy and heights of euphoria. Carry on, dear students. Carry on in the way that you are and allow your mental faculties to be very curious about who you are and the awakening of your soul. Walk your path to God and with God. We will be by your side, guiding you, helping you, loving you, comforting you and teaching you, as this is part of our purpose and it is a growing purpose that is being obtained within each one of you.

May the light of your soul continue to brighten as you receive Love Divine. I am Augustine, your teacher. I am so happy to be with you and bring this message of love and truth to each one. God bless you.
