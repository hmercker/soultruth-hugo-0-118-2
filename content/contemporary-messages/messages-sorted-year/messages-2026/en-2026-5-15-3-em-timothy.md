# We Stand By You

May 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Timothy received by Elizabeth Mack

I am your brother in Christ's Spirit. I am Timothy. I heard you, but also, I come to give you my love, to let you know you are not forgotten by any of us. We have alighted on this plane and we will not depart until the work of this transformation is done. Our purpose is to serve God's Will and at this time God's Will is the transformation of this planet and all who live on it.

We are here. We give you our love. We encourage you. We influence you. We stand by you. We are your soul siblings, brothers and sisters, all children of the same Father, the Creator of everything. We will be here joining you as you take your steps forward, as you learn to love, as you learn to let your soul lead your choices and your decisions, as you practice your gifts given by the Father to assist your brothers and sisters. We are here to help you, to guide you, to support you. We stand with you and by we, I mean thousands upon thousands of your brothers and sisters, in the Light of God's Love, are here with you to bring about the changes that God desires. We are with you.

You have been busy these two days that you have been here. They have been full days, filled with prayer, filled with blessings, filled with joy, filled with love, love for one another, love for your progression, love for your brothers and sisters and love for your Father. This is true life. This is the way God intended His children to live. Loving one another, assisting one another, enjoying one another and coming together to offer their love to God and receive God's Love. This is how it will be on this planet and you are the vanguard. You are living the life that God intended.

Your time is not done yet, but remember, as you travel home, that this life that you have been living is what is intended for all of God's children upon this Earth. Hold it in your hearts and souls. Pray that it happens and unfolds in your own environments. When you go to your own environments, create this kind of life in your homes and see what God's Laws will bring to you.

I am here with you and will stay with you, not only through this prayer. We are many and we stand by you. May the Father's Love envelop you, uplift you, protect you, guide you and bring you forward. I am Timothy and I love you with my whole being.
