# Engage Your Soul

June 29<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Ann Rollins received by Jimbeau Walsh

My dear ones, it is I, your sister, Ann Rollins.

I am with you in this prayer. I am with you in your longings.

What a wonder it is to connect with our heavenly Father, the presence of His love permeating this place, inflowing into your being. What a wonder! When you find it difficult to traverse the path of Divine Love while in the world, you can become very frustrated and annoyed. But I tell you, do not let the world disrupt your peace. You cannot stop the world, but you can engage your soul when you choose love, when you choose prayer.

As you have been informed, conventional prayers may reach no higher than the lips and the mind. But the wonderful thing about the challenges of the world and all the cacophony, all the troubles, all the negativity, is that, to the love in your soul, it is so transparent that your response is inevitable; opening your heart and your soul to God’s love and, as we say, this love has conquered the world. This love has lifted you, awakened you, and embraced you in peace, in joy.

Let the healing balm of this blessing touch you. Allow the magnetism of the Creator’s love, that your soul so wishes to be at one with, allow it in, that the heavenly Father may heal you, change you, guide you, and bless you. Then, you can go into the world and be the blessing you wish to be, loving all. For do not the ones who are the most out of harmony need love the most?

I know it can be difficult to reach those who are at war with themselves and perhaps each other, but when you shine your light in a dark room, those things which were not apparent are exposed, and a choice can be made, whether to be in the light or resist it. This is a matter of one’s free will, and all we ask is that you bring this love to all, to bless all.

So, I shall step back in this luminous circle, that the heavenly Father may bestow His great love in abundance upon all. Stay the course, my friends. Keep a prayer in your heart and a song of joy in your soul. May God bless you. I am your sister, in Christ, and your eternal friend.

I am Ann Rollins. Go in peace.
