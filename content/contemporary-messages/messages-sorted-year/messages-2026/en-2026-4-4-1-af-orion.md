# The Gift of a New World in God's Love

April 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

The power of this great Love is building within you, beloved souls. I am Orion. I come once again to encourage you upon this path, this path so obscure in your world. So few understand it and yet you have the clarity and the vision that does not obscure the journey you are taking. The strength that you carry is the strength of knowledge, the strength of dedication, the strength of faith that your Creator, the Creator of all the universe, is blessing you deeply, powerfully, with the Essence of His own Soul, that you are upon that focus of God's Love for you. You feel it. You are aware of its warmth penetrating through all the layers of darkness in your world and touching your soul directly and powerfully, healing you of all the conditions and the darkness that you may possess within you that are out of harmony with the Laws of Love.

This is the power of prayer, the power of your desires deep within, coming to the surface, conscious and expressed in prayer. You are truly connected to the Creator of all. In this is the key to that which you pray for, the goals that you have as a group, the possibility of bringing further awareness and awakening to the world. You cannot see at this time the plan that evolves, what God intends, the river of His Will flowing ever forward. Yet, you are within that river. You are a part of the intended plan because you have the knowledge of truth within your mind and within your soul, and the light of truth is glowing within your soul. Each of you grows stronger in this light. Each of you carries a lamp that indicates that you are a part of a great plan that will, in time, change the world.

The reality of human existence will be transformed and will come in ways and means that you cannot truly know at this time, but it is unfolding. The momentum is building. The light is increasing. In this increasing light, there is resistance, fear and misunderstanding amongst humanity, for each soul intuitively knows change is coming and change is being expressed as a result of this light flowing upon the planet. So many are fearful and confused. So many are lost in their material condition, their mindful consciousness that insists that this is the only way, the only consciousness that can be utilised for life. Yet, you know differently, my friends, that indeed a consciousness builds within you that is quite different from that which many have and this brings a sense of separateness. Within that consciousness is compassion, is a desire to love, accept and embrace your brothers and sisters and so you do in your own ways. Do not emphasize the difference between you and another. Rather, emphasize the similarities. We are all children of God. Whether you come from my planet, yours or another, we are all children of God. This is the great bonding truth, that each of us carries a soul that connects us with the Creator and all that is of creation.

My beloved friends, we venture together. We venture in love, singleness of purpose and a desire to help God bring the change that is required, the change that shall heal, that shall brighten and awaken humanity and the entire world, all within it. We are being lifted up by God and the resources of the great Soul, the Oversoul, are beyond comprehension. The power of God's Love, the power of His healing Touch, the power that indeed creates the universe and can bring change and will flush out the darkness in time and bring to you a gift, the gift of a new world, a gift to restart the progress and the journey of humanity toward light and enlightenment, toward knowledge and truth, toward the capacity to love and to understand the power of love, toward many things that shall bring harmony to humanity. The potential is there. It has always been there. Now it is time to understand, to bring it forward, to truly acknowledge and embrace that deeper self, full, so very full of possibilities.

May you be blessed, my beloved friends. May you be in light always. May you be in relationship with your Creator. For as you well know, this is the key to all that exists in harmony and peace and that love will bind you with God, with one another, with all humanity. The oneness of love is your great strength. The knowledge of it guides you forward. We continue, my friends. Your friend Orion walks with you, as do many. May you be blessed truly. Know the peace, the deep peace that comes and the knowledge will flow, revelatory and deep and filled with insights that will create a new reality for you, my beloved friends, a new reality for humanity. God bless you all. My love is with you, always, always. God bless you.
