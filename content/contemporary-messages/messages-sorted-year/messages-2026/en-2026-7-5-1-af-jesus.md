# Opening to the Father’s Love and the Coming Transformation of Humanity

July 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours, yours for eternity. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come once again to join you in prayer, to be with each one of you as your loving brother, that you may know that you have me as a friend, that you may know that, as a friend, I will bring all that I can to uplift you and to envelop you in love. For God provides much for His children, given that His children will respond to His beck and call to partake of this great gift that He offers, the gift of His Love. God offers all that is and can be given to uphold you, my beloved brothers and sisters.

I urge you to open your souls wide to the great inflowing of His Love. I urge you to avail yourselves of all that God has to give to you. It requires your faith, my beloveds. It requires your dedication to prayer. It requires some time and some effort, but oh, the rewards! The rewards are great. A small effort from you will bring great rewards, my beloveds, and all that your soul can be will be ignited by the Father's Love, that part of you that still, to some degree, is a stranger, still is a mystery.

Yet, as you continue to pray and receive this gift of Love, much will be revealed. You will come to know your true self, come to be in alignment with God. The Creator of all the universe will become a familiar and beautiful addition to your life and consciousness. God's gift to you through His Love is the awakening of this personal relationship with Him, as if you are the only one in the universe. You will feel God's attention and Love upon you. You will come to know the grace, the upliftment and the healing that comes. You will be showered in light and awakened in Love.

My beloveds, there is no higher nor greater pursuit than this gift, this blessing and this relationship. I show you, I point the way. I do so because I love you as well, as do all the angels of the Celestial Kingdom. They know of each one of you. Many pray for your continued soul progression, awakening and transformation. Many are dispatched to assist you in your daily life. You have opened the door to great blessings, to magnificent support, upliftment, inspiration, protection and guidance. So much is given by a simple prayer and desire to be with Him. We pray together, my beloveds. Upon the altar of love, we gather in that Hall of Peace. We pray together. All that is holy, beautiful, of light and harmony with God's blessings and creation is yours to experience, to behold and to embrace.

This is the true spiritual journey of the soul. It is available to each individual. All who seek shall find this if they do so with sincerity. Even those who have been told error and have distortions of the mind will feel the rightness of the soul and come to know God and His Love, given their desire to do so. The barriers that humanity has erected toward God will fall in time. The floodgates will open. They will wash clean the conditions of this Earth and bring something new and beautiful in its place. God's mercy and redemption shall be felt by all of humanity. In that, there will be a choice to be with God or not. Too long has humanity fallen away from this sacred union, this beautiful blessing. They have done so and caused great pain to themselves and to this world. Now is the time where God beckons His children back into the fold of His embrace, Love and guidance to know the Truth in its simplicity and power, to discover the blessing of Love and what it might do for each one of you and all of humanity to awaken to their true natures, the nature of the soul, to feel the power of God's healing Touch in His Love that will break away the obstructions and the encrustations, as humanity longs to be saved from themselves.

God hears those prayers, as He does your prayers, my beloveds. God responds in ways that begin so subtly, so gently. Yet, they will build into a powerful force for change, healing and harmony. It all comes within God's plan for the salvation of humanity. Each of you may partake of this plan and be a part of it if you so will to do so. My beloveds, great change is afoot and wondrous healing is coming. Allow yourself to be swept away by the great waters of change and healing, so that you may be refreshed and come to your life anew and born into the Truth of God's Love. You are a part of that plan. May you, your loved ones all around you, come to be in thoughts by God's intention, God's ministrations and efforts to humanity. May your eyes and the eyes of those around you be opened to the truth, to who they truly are and what life is meant to be. May all the pain and suffering, all the ignorance and strife, the lack of love, the lack of insight to truth, may all these things be washed away within God's plan.

The healing waters shall flow, my beloveds, the waters of life, the waters that bring new life to the soul. These things shall come in abundance and help to right that which is out of harmony and out of balance in the world. We will all work together, God's servants, to help usher in this new age, an age of life, an age of peace, an age where your children and your children's children may come to know a good life, a life that is in harmony with God's Laws of Creation and will be given in a conscious way the choice between the path that leads to the heavens of those who are in harmony with purification and the Heavens with those who are in harmony with transformation. Such will be given.

It is beginning, my beloveds. The changes are coming. Yes, they are subtle to begin with, but will intensify and strengthen in such ways that none can deny it. It will bring confusion and fear, for humanity is resistant to change and to truth. This is where you may do your work to give to those who are curious and desire the truth, the reasons for what may come about and why God must bring harmony to the world. It is the law that every world of His creation must seek greater harmony, though humanity may resist this and may push against the efforts that God and His angels make, they must acquiesce. For the power that humanity possesses is minuscule compared to the power of God and the forces of the angels to bring about harmony for the world. Pray to know your place in God's plan. Pray to be strong enough, clear enough, right enough to truly be an effective instrument within this great changing world, to bring greater light to you and to the world.

My beloveds, I ask much of you. God asks much of you, but there is much to do, my beloveds, so much to do. It must take place in order to free humanity from the shackles of its own doings and darkness. May we work together, my beloveds, truly work together to be those instruments of change. You will have all the blessings, support, protection and guidance that you require to do this work, provided you are sincere and dedicated and are open to the guidance that will be given. I am here to uphold you, my beloveds. Many are here to uphold you. You shall be upheld, guided and blessed as the blessings of the Father's Love pour into your soul at this time. May you open wide to this gift, for this is what will fuel your instrumentality and in turn will fuel the changes of the world.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend. I am Master of the Celestial Kingdom. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you, beloved souls.
