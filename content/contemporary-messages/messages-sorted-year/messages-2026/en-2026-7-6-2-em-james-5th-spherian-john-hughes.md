# Divine Love Will Carry You Through

July 6<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

James 5th Spherian & John Hughes received by Elizabeth Mack

**James 5th Spherian:** I am James, a soldier of truth. I live in the Fifth Sphere. I come because you are in need of the truth that even though many disasters impact those souls upon the Earth, still the Divine Love will carry you through those disasters, those hardships. Even those situations where people lose their lives. The Divine Love will carry you through to the other side and bring you a truer, more accurate picture of what life is on this planet, in the material and in the spirit world.

I know you typically bring through Celestial Angel guides but I am one who transitioned recently. I was a soldier fighting in a war that was not my choice to do, but I did choose to defend. My human life was taken as a result and yet I was one who prayed to God, even while on the battlefield, even while stoking the fires of hatred of my brothers upon this Earth. I regretted it and am sorry for it. Let me say, it is a useless endeavour to fight, to hate, useless and I regret that I did that while I was on the Earth. Through prayer, intense prayer, my Father has forgiven me. My Father has shown me a great deal of Love and as I grow brighter and lighter, I see the Truth of the Love that exists and is readily available to all souls. If only men could see that hatred, that greed, that the horrible things that they do to each other is a useless endeavour. The only way they can see it is through the eyes of love.

Dear children, if you have these feelings of hatred, of greed, of angst, of confusion or anything less than love, it is a waste. It is useless. Pray. Pray to remove all these things that inhibit your loving your brothers and sisters upon the Earth. There is much work to be done in removing these great errors that exist. Open your souls. Receive the Love. Accept the Love and express the Love and you will be rewarded tenfold.

I thank you for allowing me to speak and with this, I will step back, as I am instructed to do. May the Father bless you all and each.

**John Hughes:** I am here, John Hughes. We are bringing you higher. We are ascending closer to God, that you may be healed of your worries and concerns, for much, much is happening in your Earth plane at this time. Feel the love. Be at peace and bring the truth of your love forward.
