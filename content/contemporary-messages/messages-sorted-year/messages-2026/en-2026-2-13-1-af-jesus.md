# Faith and the Soul 6: Have Faith in the Power of God's Love to Bring You into Communion with Angels and Alignment with God 

February 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Manifesto of Light, Christchurch, Barbados

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the peace that passes all understanding be yours, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to be with you on this last day of prayer and to acknowledge each one of you as my true brother and sister. My beloveds, you have reached out to many since you have been here on this island. You have complied with God’s Will and plan as you travelled, connected and prayed together. I am well pleased with the work that has been accomplished. I am well pleased with what may be as a result of this work, as souls have shifted in their understanding and their consciousness, as love has flowed abundantly within many souls and that steps may now be taken because of the benefits and results of your prayers and your efforts.

Indeed, there is always much work to do, God’s plan to unfold, His Will to know. I say to you, my beloveds, each of you has the capacity to know God’s Will. It is only because your minds do not believe fully, are in doubt. Your minds are so well engaged with your consciousness that it is difficult to hear the sweet and small Voice of God whispering to your soul. You must overcome those things. This is your challenge and your test. To truly know your soul is to acknowledge the power and the supremacy of your soul over your mind and to live in a way that demonstrates and indicates that supremacy within that mental framework and those thoughts that you carry. It is difficult to see how you might do this, how you might walk that road where being in alignment and consciously so with your soul is difficult and unobtainable. Yet, as you feed your soul with the Father’s Love, as you continue to grow brighter, as your soul is strengthened and transformed by this Love, as the impediments fall away, so the consciousness of soul will come to you. In that consciousness will be the ways and means of communication with us, your angel friends and with God. That communication will become tangible and reachable. It will become obvious and without distortion. You must have faith in the power of God’s Love to do so, for all that is required is given. All that God wishes of you will flow to you as a gift and a blessing to each one.

Yet, as you live in this formidable and difficult world, so the impediments are great and the lack of trust and faith continue to dog your journey. I ask you, my beloveds, as you carry on with your life, to truly question why you do not have this understanding and gift of communication and awareness, for it resides within your soul. It is alive within your soul. I ask you, my beloveds, make the effort, walk the path, have the faith, seek out the gifts of God’s Love and all will come in good stead. Because you might utter a prayer to say, *“I want to know and hear and feel in ways that others do,”* does not mean that this prayer will be answered overnight, but it is the beginning and is one more step closer. As you express your desires to God in heartfelt ways, in truthful ways, you will find the answers will come, often in ways unexpected and surprising, but they will come. You will know and live within the truth of your soul and the faculties and capacities that reside there.

It is part of the process of your redemption and transformation. The awakening of your soul comes with every breath. With every day that passes, there is a further awakening, a greater awareness and greater joy, for the soul knows no bounds of joy. It is the hallmark of a soul that is in alignment with God, the joy that comes, the appreciation, the gratitude of life that comes, the love of your own true self, the recognition of the wonderment of God’s creation that is you. These things come on the heels of His Love that is working ceaselessly to transform you and to awaken you.

My beloveds, we have spent some time together. Doors have been opened. Awarenesses are trickling into your consciousness. Be aware that as you continue to feed this progress and process of awakening, the subtleties and the fragments will grow into sureties and a continuous flow of awareness, knowledge, joy and love. It is for you to nurture these things in prayer and to practise expressing this truth in your daily life, as you put the effort forth, as you commit yourself to this path in all ways, in all the tasks and deeds and expressions of your life and all the thoughts that come to you and all the expressions of your instrumentality as God’s instrument. As these things are strengthened in the Love of God and the Love of God influences and makes harmonious these parts of yourself, these expressions that flow through you and within you, then the question of knowing your guidance, knowing who is close to you from the spirit world, knowing how to proceed and how to be in life will be answered and will become obvious. In that observation, you will laugh with the realisation of how truly simple this is.

It is merely that which you hold on to, my beloveds, those paradigms of thought that are so precious to you, those aspects of self that you have put forward in life because you believe that is what is required or desired, but are not truly a part of you. These will fall away. You will come to know a self that, in the beginning, you will not recognise. You will not truly see it is you. You may even believe this is the spirit pretending to be you, but in truth, it is who you truly are and that is truly magnificent. That is the personality, the being, the individual which we are encouraging and we come close to nurture and to strengthen, and say, *“Be who you truly are. Be that channel of love. Be that gifted soul that God has made and given as a gift to the world.”* As you come to recognise these things within you and truly be a part of them and express them, often rather haphazardly to begin with, but as you practise, as you move forward, as you gain acuity in your expression, then you will find great fulfilment in who you are and what God wishes of you as that individual, as that beautiful being of light, truth and love.

May you be blessed upon that journey, my beloved friends. You know that I am close and with you. I wish for you to be aware every moment that I am close and that your angel friends are close and that God is close. This way, doubts cannot arise. Mental projections and judgements cannot be. Instead, they are replaced by the surety of faith and the power of knowing from soul perceptions, feelings and existence. May God bless you, my beloveds, I am here with you. I am your brother and I am truly your friend. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you, beloveds.
