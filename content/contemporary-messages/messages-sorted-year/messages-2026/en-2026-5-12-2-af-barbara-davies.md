# The Power of Love to Unite Souls and Heal the World

May 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Barbara Davies received by Al Fike

God bless you all. I am Barbara. Yes, isn’t it wonderful how God weaves a beautiful tapestry of all of you together, connected in love. My dear friend Lisa, I know you well. You and I have our conversations in the sleep state. With all of you, I have had a connection, a bond. I am part of your family and my intention is to be part of your family for all eternity. This is the power of love, to bond and to create such a cohesion, such a harmony together, that it cannot be broken. The power of love, my beautiful friends, is the most powerful agent of all to bring harmony and peace to the world. Even though you may all have a different experience in life and a different perspective, soul to soul, we are all brethren. I want you to remember this, that when you meet someone, if it is a stranger or someone you do not know well, know that there is the potential for an eternal bond that may come with God's Love creating that connection, that beautiful relationship between you two.

This is how the world will change, one bond at a time, one beautiful soul connecting to another, connecting to another, creating that beautiful Lattice of Light, bringing us all into harmony together. Love is like that. It does not sow division, but brings connection, harmony and peace. It allows each of you to overcome your biases and your fears. That which may cause a repulsion rather than an attraction will dissipate and you will come together in harmony. You will grow with the eyes of your soul to appreciate the differences and to love them. You will feel, with a full heart, gratitude for what God has created and the potentials that are within each soul. They are magnificent, they are awesome and they are within each one of you in your own unique ways, expressions and personalities.

You can never be bored, my friends, when you see life through the eyes of the soul. Appreciate the variety, the variations, the beautiful expressions and creations of God. It brings delight. It brings a fascination and a joy to the heart of a soul awakened. God gives us so much and God gives us the ability to perceive and see the complexities, the intricacies of His creation through the faculties of the soul, the mind of the soul. So much is revealed. So much can come to you, revelation upon revelation, truth upon truth and in it all is love, the power of love to bring you to that place of recognition and understanding, of truly comprehending the power of love to bring acceptance and to bring peace within, so that the disturbances of the mind are not so great and can be overcome with love. The variations and things that the mind may find distasteful are actually seen in the light of appreciation, understanding and compassion at times, so that when you gaze upon another, especially the lost souls, great compassion comes, understanding of their plight.

You may even have the capacity to see their history, what has brought them to the place that is so difficult, disharmonious and painful. Rather than judge, you may say a prayer, a true prayer, that God may deliver them from the darkness into the light of truth, that they may grow to love themselves, to have compassion for themselves and for others. This is also the power of love, to dissolve the differences, to bring harmony, to bring connection. The power of love is great. Even the natural love breaks down barriers, but the Divine Love does more than that. It adds truth. It adds joy to the experience of a love expressed and flowing through you to another. The power of love, my beloveds, the power of love is great and all comes from God, His beautiful, merciful and abundant blessings upon us all.

May you receive a great inflowing now, my beloveds, a beautiful benediction, a blessing to wipe away the distortions and bring clarity and bring insight, so that the veil is lifted and truth may come into your consciousness in all its beauty, glory and wonderment. God bless you all. I am Barbara. I love you all. We are friends, we are family and will be so for all eternity. God bless you. Barbara loves you.
