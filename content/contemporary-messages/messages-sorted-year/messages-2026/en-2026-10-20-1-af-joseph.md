# Blessings Are Abundant and Powerful During This Period of Change

October 20<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Joseph received by Al Fike

Amongst all the darkness, the world is awaiting greater light. The world is on the edge of turmoil and disruption. Great change does not come otherwise upon this material world. It has been set this way because humanity will not change any other way, at least not en masse. My beloved friends, I am Joseph. It is the time of change, but it is a time of greater light and greater blessings coming. As God smiles upon this Earth of yours, then there are blessings, abundant and powerful, that will come and help you to awaken to your souls and to your souls’ destinies, help you to awaken to who you are and what is within you.

All of these beautiful things, beloved souls, are coming on the heels of change. Though you may not appreciate the inconveniences, the difficulties and the reactions that will come with this change, you will find the benefits wholesome and nurturing to your souls and to your lives. Thus, it comes with a double-edged sword. Indeed, you must be prepared for both, that which is difficult to bear and that which is joyful to bear. For that is how it will be, my friends. The Earth will have much pain and much joy. This contradiction of terms is because, on one hand, God gives His blessings. On the other hand, humanity reacts with resistance and with anger. The changes will force humanity to change with it.

My beloved friends, there is so much to prepare for. We have done a great deal of preparation with each one of you. Each one of you is feeling the power, the strength and the light that comes with the Father’s Love, the presence of your angels’ friends and your stellar friends to be with you. They have promised much to you. We have all promised much. Indeed, those promises will be fulfilled. We will give what we have dedicated ourselves to give to you, beloved souls. We will give our love, our protection, our guidance, and our gifts will be utilised for your benefit. Those who are open and willing to receive of these great blessings from God shall receive them abundantly, shall receive them with great measure and purpose in their lives.

Beloved souls, you are all on the verge of great change within you. The power of God’s Love, bringing its changes, bringing its healing, its peace, its harmony, these things too will come to you. Each personally will have their own epiphanies, their own awakenings and transformations. As you open further to the Father’s Love, as you allow yourselves to awaken to all that which is of God, you will find yourself in the bliss of His Love, a bliss and a strength that will help you weather the storms, will help you reach out to your brothers and sisters and pull them in to the warmth and safety of God’s Embrace.

When there is trouble, when there is heartache and pain, when there is chaos and confusion, your brothers and sisters will seek shelter from the storm. They will be open to what you have to offer. I know at this time many do not listen to you. They do not give you credence. They are not interested in spiritual things. Yet, they will be, my friends, they will be deeply. They will abide by the desires of their soul, the anguish of their soul, as they see all that they expected to insulate them from a painful life disappear. Many will be naked and afraid, whipped about by the changing Earth, the changing conditions, and you will be a rock and a fortress for the many.

For you will not change. You will not react in the way that they react. Rather, you will see the purpose of it all. You will understand how these things must take place in order for the new regime of light and love to establish itself, a cleansing of the Earth, my beloveds, a cleansing, recalibration and harmonisation of the Earth. This is what God will give. This is what will be given to this Earth. Those who understand and embrace God’s blessings will be able to show, to demonstrate and to clarify for others what it is that is happening and for what reason.

My beloved brothers and sisters, you are the tip of the spear that shall penetrate the darkness. You are the light that shall dissipate the chaos. You are the comforter, the teacher, the healer and the one who carries the great light of love above you. So you will, my friends. So you will. You will be known by your light and many will draw to you because of your light, carrying that great torch of truth into the world. Beloved souls, continue to pray, awaken and strengthen. Strengthen your faith. Strengthen your resolve to be in harmony with all that which is of God and the Laws of His Love. You will know the peace that passes all understanding. You will release the vestiges of doubt and error within you and you will become purified by the Father’s Love. Further, you will develop and transform within it.

May God bless you, beloved souls. I am Joseph and I come to reassure you. On the eve of change, great change, I come and many others come to assure you that God embraces you all. You are protected and you will be guided. God bless you, beloved souls. Joseph loves you. God bless you.
