# Pausing Your Material Life Helps to Strengthen Soulful Insights

February 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Anna received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. It is Anna. Shall we rise up from the material into the spiritual, into the realms of light, as you come into the awareness, the centre of your being, the soul, feeling its expanded energy, the spirit that moves from your soul to the spirit body, the lightness that you experience when you are in prayer with soul consciousness? When you feel the presence of the angels around you, you feel a sense of lightness through your physical body. Let us take you up into these realms, higher and higher, allowing the inflowing of God’s Love into your soul, to illuminate and to infiltrate your very being, where the essence of your soul merges with the Essence of God’s Soul, where your love and God’s Love meet. In this inflowing of God’s Love into your love, your nature is transformed, creating elements of change.

As you are in these realms of light, it helps to elevate you and to strengthen you. Your soul mind can bring insights into your material consciousness. You can find resolution and solution to your circumstances when you allow yourself to connect with the perceptions of truth and wisdom, letting your material life pause for just a moment and be rejuvenated, enlivened and enlightened by these moments that you pray and spend with God. For it is in these moments of prayer that you grow in your understanding of truth. That wisdom is given to you, further knowledge where your gifts are developed and where your soul is awakening. You can receive these experiences in a very real way, filled with light, yet grounded in presence and awareness. For this is not a fantasy type experience. These are experiences of transformation and change where you are awakened to greater truths, knowledge and understanding of who you truly are.

When you have completed your time in prayer and you return your awareness into the physical world, you can bring with you these perceptions of truth and light in hopeful ways of retaining this awareness, staying attuned to the realms of light, staying attuned to the faculties of your soul, the facets of your being, the intricacies that are awakening, coming alive in you and experiencing these openings in your physical body and through your physical body. You can tell when there has been a shift, where you feel through your senses something is new. You may not fully understand what is new, or be able to speak about it clearly. Yet, as these blessings that are given when you are in prayer integrate into your experience, your very nature is of this change. You walk in the world differently with greater light and understanding. You perceive your life and the way you move through your life with different light and different awareness. For it is the soul that begins to inform your actions, activities, your words, your perceptions, how you are in the world and how you are with yourself.

This all takes some time. Yet, when you put your attention upon the unfolding and awakening of your soul with great anticipation and joy, you will keep yourself in more soulful consciousness than in the dense measures of your 3D world. You will be what you call *"in the world, but not of the world."* You are growing in your capacity to be able to see through the eyes of your soul what really is happening here on Earth. At times, it is very disturbing. Yet, when you are strong, steady and faithful in God, when your soul is aligned with God and you allow the angels to be in your midst, teach you and guide you, you can walk in this world, this world that is in transition from darkness into light.

There is much that is underway within each soul, within the earth and within the atmosphere of the Earth, so much changing. You, beloved souls, are fortunate, so fortunate to know of God’s Love, how you are loved beyond measure by the One who has created you. The Creator will never forsake what has been created in harmony. Understand that the more you allow yourself to be carried by God, the more God can carry you. Place all things in your life, big and small, at the Feet of God and you will receive God’s helping Hand upon you, showering you with wisdom, bringing forth doors to open for gifts and instrumentality to be of service.

All things that you need shall be provided for. There is nothing that God cannot provide for you. As you grow in your faith and understanding of this truth, your life will change. Your faith will expand in ways that, at this point in time, you could not imagine faith to be so strong. Your knowings are growing, your stability is grounding and your truth is guiding you forward. So take your prayers, your time with God, when you open your soul and allow Him to love you very seriously, knowing that the relationship that you have with God is the ultimate relationship that you will experience. In time, beloved souls, you will find yourself a redeemed soul, divine in nature of God’s Substance and Essence, at-one with our Heavenly Father.

May you be touched deeply as you open yourself to the inflowing of God’s Love. I am Anna. Thank you for being in this circle and for giving your time, your attention and your will to God. God bless you.
