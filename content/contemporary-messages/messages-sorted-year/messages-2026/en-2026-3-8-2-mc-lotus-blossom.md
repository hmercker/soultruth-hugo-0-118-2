# The Creative Flowering Of the Soul Through Divine Love

March 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Lotus Blossom received by Maureen Cardoso

I am here. It is Lotus Blossom. As you come into that rested place within you that allows the longings of your soul to open to God's Essence, drawing in the Holy Spirit, that agent of His Love, may it mightily flow into all parts of your soul, especially those places that are not familiar. May there be a crack, an opening within your soul, so that this Love, the Substance of God, may find lodgement and the transformation of that aspect that is not in harmony. Come into realms of this Love, awakening your soul to new awarenesses, greater light, permeating your spirit body into your physical consciousness, so that your embodiment of these deeper awarenesses becomes a truth, one that you live, one that there is faith held in.

For as you continue walking this path of at-onement with God, it must be that you awaken to greater change, that you see through the eyes of love in broader ways and that your perceptions fill your material consciousness. Your soul is becoming stronger, a more dominant operator in your existence. When you think about this, how you are growing in greater awareness, it brings a smile to your heart. That smile in your heart is reflected on your face. Your eyes are brighter. The expression of your face is of more light. There is less tension and you have the perceptions of seeing the potentials and possibilities of your life, a life given to you by God, a life that is growing in its demonstration of truth, a life that is awakening in desire to be in alignment with God's Will. For as this grows in you, within you is great anticipation of the possibilities of every moment. These moments of alignment, of harmony, instill greater desire, soulful desires, to be expressed, your creative energies to move through you, to be set free and to be expressed. There are many avenues that the soul can walk in its creative expression and uniqueness. I would encourage you each day to allow your creative expression to be experienced, to put this knowledge of Divine Love into creative outlets, releasing only an intellectual understanding of Divine Love and to allow a creative embodiment and outflow of this Love.

By doing so, you are living through the essence of your soul. Your mind is aligning and the integration of the experience of allowing these creative outflows will build and will grow to where, in time, potentially, your creative expressions become the full way that you live life. You incorporate these into your material experiences. It is through these avenues that you allow the demonstration of your life to unfold, giving greater expression and airway to the desires of your soul. What sits within you that brings a powerful flow of life to you, igniting joy? What sits within you that desires your mind to say yes to? These discoveries are for you.

This is what it means to be living a life in harmony, allowing the Divine Love that is within you to lead you, to guide you, to move you, to thank you and to cause action in your life. At times you can feel your soul in a way that these creative energies shoot out or sprout up and move through your life. You can feel the centre of your being, moving and breathing and its will trying to guide you. Here, if you should put your material consciousness in this part of you, intertwined in soul consciousness and desires, then there is the greater chance for these possibilities and opportunities to be a part of your life, your expressed life.

Do not wait for your outside circumstances to come together. You may be in some storm or another, whether that be mental or emotional. It is in what you may call the messiness of life that you instill your focus upon the calm, the truth and the wisdom of your soul and allow it to inform you how to address and engage these mental and emotional outbursts or disharmonies. Truth will rise up and wisdom, compassion and humility will rise up from your soul. The parts that you consider unruly will receive new insights. These will bring to these uncertainties hope, a place to trust and the realisation that there is a consciousness of Divine Love within its soul, where God's Substance can guide and inform. This can bring great relief to your material consciousness because it experiences where it can turn to, what it can lean on, where it can seek guidance from. In this way, those patterns and habits that are stored within your brain and your material body have a chance, have a chance to be healed because what will come as guidance and wisdom is from the Substance and Essence of God's Love, Divine Love that you hold that is a part of your embodiment of your soul.

You only need to choose where to put your attention and where to find support for your fears. It is in truth that all things shall come into alignment with love or harmony through your thoughts and your actions will be part of God's Will for you, displaying the alignment of your soul and God's Soul through the outpouring of your physical being. It is worth coming to that place of rest within you before making your decisions and taking action and seeking wisdom and guidance to ensure that you are acting inside of the Law of Love. As you do this, there will be no discrepancies, no consequences to atone for, because your light and your actions and your love are in harmony.

May you be blessed on your journey walking in the grace of God's Love, filling your soul with His Essence, keeping yourself open at all times as best you can, aware of welcoming God into your soul, where you do not close the door of your heart. You keep it open and God finds His willing child able to receive His blessing and Touch at all times and it is certain that God will fulfil your longings always. I am Lotus Blossom, so happy to be with you, to share this time together, speak to you, to feel you and to see you, the lights of your soul, the brilliance of your joy and the radiance of your light. God bless you all. I am Lotus Blossom. God bless you.
