# God's Love Will Awaken Greater Soul Desire & Receptivity

July 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Luke received by Maureen Cardoso

My dear brothers and sisters, welcome. Welcome into this Circle of Light, where God blesses your souls with His Divine Love and your soul continues to develop in perception, attributes, truth, wisdom, longings and so much more. The road ahead is filled with absolute wonderment, peace and joy, a union with God that is growing within each one of you. My prayers are for you and I come to pray with you. I am Luke. I come to you from the Celestial Heavens with a reminder: seek ye first the kingdom and all things shall come to you. Respond to the promptings of your soul, for as your soul grows, awakens and develops in God's Love, through the laws of God's creation, all things come into harmony.

It is a process. At times, you feel deeply awakened, feeling the essence of your soul, the beautiful Love that is between your soul and God, how much you truly love God and how you can perceive through the eyes of your soul and see with love, allowing this love to outflow from your being, where your presence radiates the substance of your soul. This is such a beautiful experience to have. For in doing so, it ignites greater desire for more. There are other times when you feel distant. You are more wrapped up in the material world and what is directly in front of you, perhaps even distracted. But then it flows again back to the steady strength of the awareness of your soul and its growing capacities to maintain its ability to commune more and more with God.

As you experience these two parts of you, I want to assure you that the workings of God's Love within your soul can make it that there is more desire, more receptivity, frequent prayer, where at any moment you can cast your love up to God. That response in return is the mighty inflowing of the Love Divine, this communing back and forth. Know that as you progress on your road of awakening, this is how it goes, the ebbs and flows of the spiritual journey. If you are one that is not conscious of God's inflowing into your soul or even potentially being able to perceive your soul, do not fret. Do not worry. Have faith in the longings and your outcry to God, for that is how God responds to you. Whether you can feel this inflowing or not, God's laws operate in such a way that not even God can forgo or act outside of the laws of His creation. In time, as you continue your progression and the inflowing of this Love comes into your soul, this may change. But perhaps rather than looking for the sensations to be a gauge that you have received, be steady in faith and know that, so long as you have given a heartfelt longing, whether silent or loud, God is responding. The laws are working upon you.

Have great faith. Know that God loves you and cares for you, provides for you, supports you, walks hand in hand with you, sending His angels and your guides around you. Be attentive to how you engage in the world. If you are feeling very caught up in the material realms, give yourself a break. Allow yourself to rest from the turbulence of the material world. Deepen your light, illuminate your light, allowing God to release those conditions that stifle the awareness of your longings, whether that be of the soul or even your material desire to be in prayer with God. Allow these conditions to be lifted from you.

But you also must participate and be active in your awakening and in your development. Be honest with yourself and come to know where you can extend yourself toward your progression a little more. Do this always lovingly, not in judgement, but with the understanding that every effort that you make will ignite greater awareness, more results and greater receptivity. It is a path that requires your attention, rather than to sit and ask God in an intellectual way to bless you. By doing it this way, you have not created an opening within yourself. The mind is not open. The heart is not open. How can the soul open itself into your awareness of its longings? Unlock these doors within you and keep them ajar, knowing what distracts you, knowing what closes these doors. For it is through the door of receptivity, God's Love coming in, that all things awaken within you.

May you each be blessed on your journey. May you listen intently within to the little niggle that is calling forth. That is your soul. That is your prompting. Attend to it like you would an infant needing help. Respond fully to this niggle, to this outcry, and you will find that your spiritual journey moves forward in a way that you consciously experience the change from within. My dear friends, let us open. Let us open the mind and the heart. Relax yourself in your body. From deep within you, open yourself, allowing God, in His blessings of Love, to continue the transformation of your soul. I am Luke, always happy to come and to convey a message of truth. May you be blessed, beloveds, mightily, mightily blessed. God bless you all.
