# Conversations with God, Prayer, and Spiritual Magnetism

April 20<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Care Darby Walsh received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, Care, your celestial sister, and friend in the love of God.

I humbly ask you to take this time together to open your heart, that God may open your soul, and to let go of all those things that occupy your mind and your concerns. Let the longings of your souls go to the great Soul of God that you may receive this gift.

I shall give you, if I may, a little advice about prayer, because we are constantly asking you to pray, and some even say unceasingly. What does that mean? Does one need prayer beads or a rosary, a prayer book? Well, if it brings you into this Presence, then yes. But rather than belaboring your heart, I recommend you just talk to God. If you start your day in gratitude, asking for our guidance, asking for the heavenly Father to bless every soul you come in contact with, to protect you on your walk, to help you serve others, you begin to see your day unfolds in miraculous ways, and in a flow that does not come up against insurmountable odd, walls, and barriers, but rather walks through them in love. This ongoing conversation is really a very intimate form of prayer, and as you persist in this, you begin to feel this joy in your heart, this radiance, and you do not wish to leave that, and so the Law of Attraction begins to manifest those things that are your heart’s desire and even more so those things that are your soul’s desire.

There is a certain effortlessness about the state of grace that comes through your intimate conversation with our loving Creator. I can give you a rather earthbound example. One goes to a casino and desires to gamble, hoping that the odds will be with them, and one is feeling quite good about this, quite positive, not concerned. And so, all of a sudden, as the wheel spins, every time it stops in this game of roulette, it lands exactly in the right place. And confidence grows, and suddenly, one is surrounded by onlookers seeing this soul hitting the jackpot and creating a certain magnitude and magnetic field. And at some point, the winner of all this is no longer even concerned about the jackpot but simply basking in the glorious winning of things going their way, and everyone feels it, and all are quite drawn in. This, of course, is a material example and a very human one, but I tell you, if you put this into spiritual practice, whatever you do in love creates good and serves the world. Whatever you do in the love of God brings the greatness of His blessing, not only to you but to all, as you draw in the spiritual magnetism of the glory of God.

So, let your prayer be easy. Let go of all these things that, in the big picture, have no weight. Let us be in the weightlessness of the light and love of God. I thank you for this time and for my beloved to take this message, and I wish for each one of you a great inflowing of Divine Love into your souls, that we may all be one in the love of God. Go in peace. I love you.

I am Care.
