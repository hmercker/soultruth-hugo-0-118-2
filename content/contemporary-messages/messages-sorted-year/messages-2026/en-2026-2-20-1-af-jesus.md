# May You Understand How Loved You Are

February 20<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the grace of God's Love be with you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you this day. I come because I love you so, my beloveds. I come because God loves you so. All the angels have their love for you. May your universe, your consciousness, be filled with the knowledge of how loved you are. May your soul receive that beautiful blessing, the Essence of God pouring into you, infilling you and warming your soul with this beautiful, beautiful Light and gift from God.

In this way, we are all brothers and sisters. We are all part of God's family of love. We shall indeed be joyfully together in the Celestial Kingdom and walk amongst each other in such light, joy and happiness that you cannot possibly conceive of this. Yet, it will be yours, my beloveds, as you continue to strive and pray for this gift, so it shall be yours and so the benefits of the soul transformed will come to you and be your life in times to come in light and joy and happiness.

Oh, my beloveds, may we enter the Hall of Peace and know the benefits of this place. May your souls open wide to the gift of His Love. May we all be in the joy and rapture of His Love, awakening us further, bringing us closer to at-onement with our Heavenly Father. We are loved, each one, loved beyond measure, loved with such depth and breadth that it would be impossible to see and know how much we are loved. Yet, God pours this upon us as we pray together and we are captured in the Light of His Love and Presence.

God bless you all, my beloveds. I am Jesus and I love you. We pray together in this blessing. God bless you. God bless you all.
