# Jesus Opens the Harrow Retreat

September 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the Father's Love find lodgement into your souls. I come. I am your brother. I am your friend. I am Jesus. I come to be close to you.

For each one here, I know. I know you. My desire is that you will know me and that we may have a friendship together. For as you trod this path of love, one encounters their friends, those who, along with their souls and their desires, are bound together in this journey of discovery, this wonderful transformation of the soul that comes with the Father's Love. I desire to be close, my beloveds.

You are all children of God, as am I and we are all making that journey together toward at-onement with our Heavenly Father. Open yourselves, my beloveds, to the miracle, the wonderment of this gift of Love, given freely and abundantly. My beloveds, we are in the grace of God, the holiness of His Touch. Feel its warmth. Feel the breath of God upon you and may you be uplifted into great light.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am with you, as are many. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Kingdom and your friend, truly. God bless you. God bless you.
