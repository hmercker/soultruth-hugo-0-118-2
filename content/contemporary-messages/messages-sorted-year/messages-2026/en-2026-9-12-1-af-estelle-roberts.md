# Ground Yourself in God Before Entering Spirit

September 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Estelle Roberts received by Al Fike

Greetings to you from spirit. I am Estelle Roberts and welcome to this beautiful church, this little sanctuary of light in a very busy world. How unfortunate that more have not come, that they cannot take time out of their day to seek solace of the soul, to seek truth that these dear and bright souls who have come to share and to bring comfort and truth for you and for this world. So many do not believe in God and yet our seven principles are very clearly stating that God is real and that God is here. My friends, if you are not grounded in God before you enter the spirit world, it is a great hindrance to your progression. It is important to understand the power of God’s Love, God’s Touch and blessings for every soul.

I am very pleased that you come together to discuss these things, to share amongst yourselves your understanding of these things, to honour each other as God's children, to be in that Light of God's presence in prayer and to bring that Light, that blessing that is given out into the world.

For what is a church? What is a gathering? If one cannot share these things in the wider world, God does not want you to hold tight your light, your understanding and your love. God wants you to express these things in everything that you do and everything that you are. For when you do so, you do God's work.

You bring light into the world. You are God's emissaries, God's instruments. All is predicated upon God's Will, my beloved friends. When you come into spirit, you realise how very free that statement is. How important it is to realise how God's Will is the great motivating element factor in life that will bring truth, harmony, peace, love, healing, comfort, so much God wishes to give to all His children.

Are you willing to receive? For it is in your willingness, your desire to be in alignment with these things, these elements of light that will not only change you but help to change the world. The world is in desperate need of all of these things. Many go about life with that emptiness, that sorrow, that aloneness that makes for a very unhappy life. Yet, you are being taught at this very moment the keys to a good life, a full life. Your life must have the foundations of these truths that are being shared. When this is truly a part of you, when you are truly in alignment with these things, then you will have a good life, a joyful life, one full of wisdom, love, peace, one that is meaningful, one that is bright in light.

I thank you for listening to me, my friends. I continue to help those who come to this church. Though I am not of the flesh, my spirit continues to be fond of this place and to work with those who I am eternally grateful for, who help, who sustain, who nourish this beautiful church. I am close to you, my friends and will continue to be so. This church is meant to stand, to stand in light, to be strong in all that is true and to give to those souls who come through the doors and are seeking solace, truth and all that is of goodness of God. Be those instruments that God wishes to use to help your fellow man, to help ease the suffering, to help bring comfort and to guide the way into greater light.

I am here to help you, as are many who surround you this day and the beautiful angels of God are here this day. Take full advantage, my friends, of what is here, what is now, what is given in the fullness of love. God bless you. I am Estelle and I am happy for you, for you have chosen light. You have taken the time to gather, to nourish your soul, to question that which is not of light and to embrace that which is. God bless you all. My love is with you.
