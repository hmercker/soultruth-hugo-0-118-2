# God’s Will is Not a Mystery, But It is Something You Must Truly Desire to Know

September 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

State College Retreat, State College, PA, USA

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Keea Atta Kem. I am so happy to see such beautiful, thirsting souls come together to be nourished, to serve and to awaken. It is wonderful to hear you speak about and consider the Will of God in your life. What I want you to know is that it is not a mystery, God's Will, but it is something that you must truly desire to know and to not fear. For God is harmony. God is Love. God is patient and compassionate and would not ask you to follow Him if it was something that was not in harmony with your progression.

Allow His Will to unfold with grace. It is like moving across a pond of ice with ease in the flow, allowing the movement that God has to give you from within to guide you and glide you upon this pond of ice that is deep, sturdy and has a solid foundation. As your life solidifies with God and in His Will, you will be shown. You will be carried. You will walk hand in Hand with our beloved Creator, who cares for you, each of His children, in a unique and special way.

Open yourselves to the desire that is within your soul and allow the consciousness of that to bubble up, warm you and carry you. It will require your willingness to know, to feel and to perceive. Bless yourself each day with that communing with our Heavenly Father, His Love inflowing and awakening your soul, that jewel that He has created. Allow the blossoming, the flowering, to unfold and to be awakened. Be in joy as you experience the depth of your comprehension of God's Will. Always know that it will be loving. It will be harmonious. It will feel like the next right thing to do. Be at peace as God continues to awaken you and show you the way to Him in all His glory and Love.

Drink deeply of these Living Waters, beloved souls, and allow us to unfold these days with you, all in accordance to God's plan and God's Will. I am Keea Atta Kem. God bless each one of you as we open our journey in these days together and discover a little more about who we all are. God bless you.
