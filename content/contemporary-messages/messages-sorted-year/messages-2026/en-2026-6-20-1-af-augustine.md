# This Truth Gives You the Opportunity to Change Your Perspective on Life

June 20<sup>th</sup>, 2026

North Vancouver Monthly Circle, North Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

The gate is open to you, beloved souls. I am your teacher, Augustine. You have before you, in these moments together, the opportunity to change your perspective on life, to awaken to something deeper within you, to allow the Divine blessings to come to you, not just for these moments but for many moments to come. For when you establish a prayer practice, a connection with the Creator, and you ask for this high blessing, the highest blessing of all, which is the Essence of God's Soul given to you, each one of you, freely, no matter your condition, no matter the life you have led. It does not matter what has happened in the past. What is important is the present and what it is that you desire.

My beloved friends, do you wish to progress in light? Do you wish to have the answers to your questions? Do you wish to be loved, to truly know love? These are the blessings that God makes available to each of you and we, who reside within the Celestial Kingdom, are available at your beck and call to help you on that road to progression, but it requires an earnestness within you, a desire to seek this blessing and to seek the road of soul awakening and development. This is not development of the mind. This is not development of psychic faculties. This is development of the soul, the very core of your being. When you make that choice and do the simple procedures that are required to receive this gift of Divine Love, then you enter upon a journey like none other, an experience, an opening, a shift of consciousness, a blessing, an upliftment and a sure knowing of how loved you are by God. These things come. If you so desire them, they will be yours. God does not hold back anything from any individual, but God gives freely and abundantly in the graciousness of the Divine Touch.

So here you are, opening the door, awakening to the possibilities and feeling the upliftment and Presence of God's Touch and your angel friends around you. It is a gift and a light that is not common in your world. It is a blessing given freely to each of you. Take full advantage, beloved souls, full advantage of what is offered. You will come away from this place changed and also a decision to be made. *"Shall I follow this Path leading to at-onement with God? Is this what I desire? Is this what my soul desires?"* You will have your answer, beautiful souls. It will come to you and it will be definitive and powerful.

God bless you, beloved souls. I am Augustine, once the bishop of Hippo, now an inhabitant of the Celestial Kingdom. I went through this process of which I describe and this dear soul has told you about. Everyone must go through this process, this way, in order to enter into that place, so beautiful, refined and lovely. May you find your way there. I is your decision and your decision alone. God bless you, my friends. I bring my love to you. I honour each one of you. God bless you.
