# Mary Introduces Herself as a Caretaker of Wimbledon Spiritualist Church

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Wimbledon Spiritualist Church Retreat, Wimbledon, London, UK

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Mary and I reach my hands up to you. I have been asked to step forward as the one who was blessed to bring our beloved Jesus to Earth and I am here to help you to bring forth the Breath of God, the Love of God into your souls, to nurture you, to comfort you but also to draw many here. For I have the privilege of acting like a mother to all children on Earth. The love of a mother is very powerful indeed. As you may begin to listen to that longing within your soul and nurture it with the sustenance of God's Love, the Living Waters of our Creator. You will grow and change and I am part of the change.

Being here is like a caretaker for your beautiful centre of light that will be home to many, that will help you to stand in the Light of God even stronger. I am here to guide and to love, to nurture and to bring forth many, many souls into this building, into your lives, experiencing a soul-to-soul union with them, a loving support, a system of truth, a way in which they feel welcome because the love in your souls, the light of your spirit, shall bring them great comfort. Yet, always know that the nurturance of God's Love is the greatest substance of all the creation and permeates the universes, flowing to you at all moments in time.

You only need to stop and ask, *"My beloved God, touch me in the way that only You can. Comfort me and love me like only You can. Help me to become like a child before my heavenly parent and know You in a way like only I can. Help me to know You like You know me."* In that way, I promise you that God's Love will find its way into your precious souls. For our Creator desires to have that relationship with you, this child, this beautiful creation, in all your uniqueness and fullness.

May you be richly blessed as we sit in silence, as you open your souls to that great inflowing of God's Love and be touched in a way that only God can touch you. I am Mary. God bless you all on this eternal journey. God bless you.
