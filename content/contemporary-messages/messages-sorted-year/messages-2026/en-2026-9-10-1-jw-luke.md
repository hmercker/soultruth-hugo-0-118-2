# Healing

September 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Luke received by Jimbeau Walsh

I am here, Luke, your brother in Christ.

I thank my brother for delivering my message to the young woman, but I wish to speak to this group on healing and health. There are many factors involved in one’s personal health status, and primarily what the focus is on is diet, environment, and genetics; certainly, all these things factor in: inheriting certain traits from parents and ancestors, living in an environment that’s healthy or unhealthy, choosing which foods to eat for balance and harmony, and being open-minded enough to be tolerant of others’ needs, environments, and diets. There is also an emotional factor. One who is suffering from grief, loneliness, heartbreak, or feeling unloved will tend to overindulge in some type of habit that, although it provides temporary comfort, can be destructive and out of harmony.

As you move forward in your discussions about sanctuary in light of earth changes as they intensify, I will suggest some very simple things one can do. Putting your hands and feet in soil, healthy soil, is very good grounding. Walking barefoot is very good grounding on the earth, and in terms of soil, I can say that because of industrial agriculture, soil in many places around the world has been seriously depleted of essential and vital minerals, and so people take these supplements, and there are many available. I encourage, if you can, each one of you to create healthy soils and a portion of your diet from a garden. I encourage you, if you are near the ocean, to go in and absorb the plethora of minerals in ocean water, for it is also a battery, and it will re-energize the body and restore balance. Even the sand on the seashore has healing qualities, and of course, you are aware that when the air is clean and the water is pure, your body responds in delight. Not everyone in your community and beyond has access to all these things, but some can be created, and some do have access, and so rather than relying on chemicals and prescription drugs and supplements, I encourage you to find natural paths for your own well-being.

Above all these considerations is prayer. Not only prayer for guidance but prayers for the world, prayers for the earth, prayers for your brothers and sisters, and above all that, praying as you do for the love of God to come into your souls and transform them, bringing with them the guidance of us celestial angels. Never forget that we are available at your beck and call, and we delight in influencing you and guiding you, at the same time, always respecting your free will. But as you pray for the love of God, your soul aligns with His will, and you’ll find yourselves in the beautiful flow of grace. May this blessing be upon each one, and may your beautiful community of souls be ever-guided, ever-blessed in the heavenly Father’s love. Be well. Be at peace. Allow your fears to be transformed into faith in the love of God. I am your brother and friend in the celestial heavens, and I love you. I am Luke, the Physician. Go in peace.
