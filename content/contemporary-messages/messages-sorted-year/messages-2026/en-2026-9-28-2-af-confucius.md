# Awakening and Transformation Come With Practice, Application, Prayer and Dedication

September 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

State College Retreat, State College, PA, USA

Confucius received by Al Fike

Oh little birds, it is I, Confucius. I have come to speak to you. You seek transformation and change within. You seek release from all conditions that provide disharmony within you. You seek to be a powerful instrument of God and you are all seeking to be in the light. As you have discovered, these things do not come readily, easily or instantaneously. They come with practice, application, prayer and dedication.

If you seek these things of the highest order, the greatest blessings, the most wonderful gifts, then the power of your own dedication and application must be great. The focus must be truly there. Indeed, what is required will change your life. You cannot walk the path of the material mind and focus and expect the gifts and blessings of God to be bestowed upon you, like some whim of God's Touch upon you. This is not how it works. It is your faith, trust, desire and effort.

Like anything in your material world, you see the progression. You see the progression of a baby turning into an adult. It takes time. It takes effort. It takes the effort of the parent to raise that child, to bring that child to a place of independence, an expression of their own free will and personality. It is the same with your souls.

You begin at the beginning. You seek to be transformed by God's Love and it shall be. It is a truth, a truth that shall truly transform you. In some very rare cases, that transformation may be instantaneous. Yet, truly, when I look around this beautiful group of beautiful souls, I see that each one of you has struggled, has applied yourself and continues to seek purity and then transformation. For transformation surely cannot happen without purity, without releasing the conditions that will stop you from being in that place of godliness.

I encourage you to continue in your dedications and also to be realistic about the possibilities that lie ahead. There are many obstructions upon your earthly plane, many conditions, many who would stop you from your goal. There are also things within you that stop you from realising this goal of transformation of the soul. Yet, as you continue, as you strive, as you pray, as you long for God, as you are indeed walking upon a path of salvation, then God provides for you that which you require and at times you are tested. At times you struggle. At times you feel pain. Maybe even be disheartened upon the journey.

These are the reactions to the conditions of this world and the conditions within you. Yet, I assure you, my beloveds, that every effort made, every prayer sent to God, every desire acknowledged and true, will bring you closer to God and closer to your goal. Do not give up. Do not take your efforts casually in their expression but be truly dedicated, truly focused, truly seeing the benefits, understanding what is required of you and what will come of your efforts. My beloved and beautiful birds, who seek to fly high into the heavens and come to that place of atonement with God, you are flying above these dreadful conditions. You are making progress.

You are seeking liberation from this human condition, from within and around you and you shall indeed receive the blessings of God that will help you to find your way. My beautiful birds, do you not see what God has given you thus far, do you not understand the process and the struggle? It happens for every soul and in very many cases. It happens when the soul goes from the material to the spiritual, transitioning from this world to the next. This is where true focus begins for many. Yet, you, beloved souls, have decided and are dedicated to achieving these things in the material plane. Thus, you draw to you the beautiful spirits and angels that come to uphold you, uplift you, protect you and guide you along the way. Much effort on our part to be with you, beloveds.

Indeed, if humanity could change its ways, change its feathers and fly into truth, as you have done. Then the world will truly change and all of these struggles, all of this pain and all of the efforts that so many in the spirit world must make to rectify the conditions that they have created within the Earth plane and in the spirit world. If these things can be preempted with truth, with the truth and the power of love, creating light, releasing darkness, then your efforts will not be made in vain, my beloveds, but will be celebrated in heaven. Then Heavenly Father will bestow on you great blessings in appreciation for what you have done. Carry on, my beloveds. Carry on in the great efforts to reach beyond the darkness and errors of this world to that of light, purity and grace, the Love of God given to you with such desire on God's part to have you transformed in Love, my beloveds.

We understand your struggles. We see them in a far deeper way than you do, but we know the potentials of your soul. What may be accomplished? What may unfold in your gifts and who you are, your purpose, the blessing of your true self revealed to the world. We are with you. We shall be with you for all the days of your life. We have great hope and desire, constant prayer, that you may break this barrier of error and darkness in your own lives, that you may serve as God's servants in the lives of others and, in time, the change will come in the world that will wipe the darkness away and light may thrive, love may thrive in a world of peace.

Continue in your efforts, my beloved and beautiful children of God, and we will continue in ours and together we will be a formidable force to change the world, so in need of these blessings. I am Confucius and I love you, beloveds. I am with you. Do not doubt that I and so many others from the Celestial Kingdom continue to support you, pray with you, uplift you and protect you. We have great hope for the efforts you may make to open the door of truth to humanity. God bless you all. Confucius loves you and I bring a blessing and peace to you all, beloved souls.

God bless you. God bless you.
