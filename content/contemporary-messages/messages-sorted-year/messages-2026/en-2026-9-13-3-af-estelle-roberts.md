# Divine Love is Everything You Need and so Much More

September 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Estelle Roberts received by Al Fike

You see the power of God's Love manifest in this place amongst you people who are seeking truth. I did not know this Love when I walked on the Earth, when inhabited this building, but as I progressed in the world of spirit and what this instrument has described is quite accurate, I began to learn about the qualities of the soul and the progression of the soul. I indeed chose that road, the road of loving God. Those of you here who have deep feelings, who have deep sensitivities and who have souls that yearn to be alive, I suggest that you follow this path, the path divine, seeking this gift of Love, this gift from God. For it will put you in good stead when you land here in this world of spirit.

For many who come across are confused and do not understand any sort of pathway, but you who prepare yourselves now lay the road clearly and beautifully for the future. Of course, as the beloved Master has said and we are all honoured that he came to this humble church of ours, it is this Love that shall change the world. It is this blessing that is transformative, powerful, most powerful. Nothing else truly matters in comparison to this.

There is so much within the blessing of God's Divine Love that brings to you spiritual awakening, awareness, truth, wisdom, peace and joy. Love is all of these things and much more. Allow yourselves the luxury, the blessing, what God has to give to you and indeed luxuriate within it. For it is a gift like none other and it is a blessing that shall be with you forever. What more could a soul want than to be in the bliss of God forever?

It is given to you, beautiful, beautiful souls and it is yours to receive. Ask for it, long for it, allow it and it is yours, beloved souls. Yours forever. God bless you. I am Estelle Roberts and I come to pray with you as well.

So many are here. Many thousands are here and many saw the Master come and speak. It has touched their hearts and they begin to listen more intently to what is being said. You are the tip of that iceberg, the beginnings of this gathering and indeed the power of this gathering shall have its implications for many years to come in our humble little church. God bless you all and keep you in His Love. God bless you.
