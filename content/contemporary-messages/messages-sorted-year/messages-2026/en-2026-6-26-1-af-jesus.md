# Receive the Abundant Gift of the Father’s Love

June 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the Father's Love enter your soul, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you. I come to be with you, my brethren on Earth. All the blessings that God has to give to the Earth, all that is available to humanity and yet so few take advantage of what is there for each soul and what is available to each one in this world. You count the millions upon billions of souls. Yet, if each were blessed abundantly in the Father's Love, it would not diminish God but would bring joy to God to be able to uplift and nurture His children, His beloved children. You have come to drink of the Living Waters. You have come to seek your soul's desires and longings to be fulfilled in prayer and in the Touch of God upon you.

My beloveds, we are all His children. We are all seeking this blessing, whether it be those who have walked in the Celestial Kingdom for many hundreds of years or you who just begin your journey. The journey does not change. The perspective, the abilities, the gifts, the blessings, the constitution of your soul and your being changes, but the way in which you may reach the Father does not. It is always the same, seeking with earnest longing, seeking to be with God. I am with you as your brother to seek this blessing. I come with love for each of you. I am truly blessed to be here with you who are longing and seeking with your soul, for how many on your earthly plane do so and are truly in alignment with this great Truth and blessing? It is my privilege, it is my blessing, to be with you, to pray with you and to be in the grace of God's Touch with you.

My beloveds, it is a simple path, yet the most powerful of all. Do not allow yourself to stray from this path but seek it with all the earnestness of your souls. You will reap the glories, the blessings and the joy that comes with this great gift of Love. It is for you, my beloveds, an abundance, a great onrush of the Father's Love. It is for you, God's gift of Love for each soul. You are here to receive and to take advantage of what is given. It is yours in all its abundance. Embrace God. Be open as wide as you might to know His gift, to feel it flow within your souls, to know its blessing that will infill you and transform you and will bring the awakening that will set you on the course firmly and resolutely, the path of love, the Path of God's Love.

My beloveds, we are here together to receive, to know and to be blessed in this gift. Know that I am here with you, each one. I pray for each one of you. Each one has before them a wondrous journey to take, a beautiful path to tread upon, which God has laid before you in all its glory and wonderment. All that can be of this path is available to you, beloved souls. God does not withhold these blessings. God merely gives abundantly, unrestricted and without judgement. God gives all to those who are willing to receive. Put aside any reluctance. Put aside any self-judgement or judgement of others, but be open and receptive in the innocence that is of a child asking its Father:

*"O beloved Father, bless me with Your Love. Embrace me with Your Love. Open my heart with Your Love. Open my soul that it may flow in abundantly and I may know You as not a stranger, but my beloved Heavenly Father, who loves me so. I am Yours, dear God. I am Yours for evermore and I love You. I love You and I feel Your Love for me."* It is the greatest blessing, my beloveds. Seek it out, know it, feel it and be in alignment with all that it is and you will find the glories of heaven, the wonderments of God's blessings upon you. It is all given. It is all there waiting. Seek it out, my beloveds. Seek it out.

God bless you, beloved souls. Precious to God and precious to my heart. God bless you and keep you close in the light and protection, in the guidance and joy that is God's Hand upon you. God bless you, beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to be with you today in the prayer that you may know the peace that passes all understanding, that you may also enter into that Hall of Peace and know its glories. God bless you, beloveds. I will guide you there. We will go together. Reach out to my hand and I will take you, beloveds. I will take you. God bless you in His Love.
