# Choosing the Path of Divine Love and Soul Awakening

April 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Isaiah received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be with you, my beloveds. I am Isaiah. I come to tell you that God's Hand is upon you, all of you, and that your refuge from the darkness of this world comes from this blessing, this great blessing of God's beautiful Love that pours upon you, this Light that cleanses and removes darkness, this Light that shall uplift you and uphold you. It is the highest gift that God can give. Indeed, you have sought for the highest and shall benefit from that seeking for all eternity.

My beloved and beautiful friends, there is much turmoil and darkness in the world, much suffering, much ignorance and strife. Yet, you have struggled through these conditions to come to this place, seeking light and enlightenment, so the blessings of God are upon you. It is not that you are chosen. It is that you have chosen God to be your saviour, your blessings of soul that will indeed transform, heal and bring you into light. God is always willing to give to those who seek, but they must seek first. They must want to be blessed. They must desire this blessing. It is not given randomly or without the laws being obeyed. These laws are simple and are understandable to every soul. For within the core of each individual, within that soul, is the understanding and the desire of this gift. It is for them to bring it to consciousness and for them to pray to God to receive it. As they do so, so the awakening begins and the transformation takes place, drop by drop, step by step.

Each of you is undergoing a process of soul awakening and soul transformation. Each of you is on your own journey. It is particular to you, my beloved souls. You have chosen it. God is all too willing to show you the way, but the pace, process and journey that you take is up to you. It is up to the power of your own free will and desire to follow this road. God will lay the road for you. God will light that road for you, but you must want and desire to walk upon it with all the faith, desire and impetus that comes from your soul, that seeks out God, that has been with God and now seeks to be reunited. Indeed, in this union and in what you are claiming for yourself, this reunification will be changed and different, for you are different and changed. You have personality. You are unique in your way and means of being in the world and shall continue in the spirit world.

As you continue to grow and awaken in this way, so the capacities, abilities and gifts that God has given you by the fiat of being incarnated into the material world will propel you on a journey that will bring these unique gifts forward. It will bring your unique personality, who you are as an individual, forward, and that will grow, transform and evolve as the gift of God's Love is given to you. In time, you may encounter your soulmate, your other half. This too will bring you great joy and great fulfilment.

God has a plan for each one of you. God will lead you upon the road of enlightenment. God will continue to give what you require, this great gift of Love flowing abundantly. As you catch every drop that is rained upon you, as you feel every infusion of His Love within your soul, you will know a joy like none other. You will know yourself like never before. You will know God as a true and palpable being who is your Heavenly Father, who is there to uphold you, support you, love you and awaken you. You will know us, your angel friends, who are well aware of these things and are eager to show you and help you upon this way of soul transformation, awakening and enlightenment. You will learn to love, my beloved souls. You will learn what soul love is and the power of that love that is the magnification of God's Love through you. It will reach others and will show them the way on this magnificent path of soul awakening.

Much is in store for you, my beloved friends. Many things are coming, many experiences, many insights and revelations and much joy. It is all waiting for you. It will continue to await you, my beloved friends, dependent upon your desire, your prayers, your faith and the journey that you take, for God honours your free will and will show you only what you wish and what you desire upon the path. May you desire everything that God has to give you. This is our prayer for you. This is what must come to you, all the blessings of the universe and of God's great Soul waiting to be given in the fullness, in the wonderment of God's Hand upon you.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Isaiah. I come to proclaim the truth once again to you on Earth who are seeking, who desire, who are willing to walk the Path Divine and seek out the Father's Love. It is given this very moment, so it is given. God bless you all upon this path. Isaiah loves you and is with you as you journey forward.
