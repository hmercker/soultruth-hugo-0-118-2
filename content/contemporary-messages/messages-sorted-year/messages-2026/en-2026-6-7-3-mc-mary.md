# Mary Tells Us in Time as the Love Takes Form in Our Soul Actions are Informed By Love

June 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Starpoint Circle of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Mary. May the peace that passes all understanding fill this room and be carried into your soul. May the Divine Essence fulfil you in a way which you have not experienced before. May you soften in your demeanour within your mind and allow yourself to be receptive to a great inflowing. As this Substance comes into your soul, carried by the Holy Spirit, it becomes a part of you, developing your soul in the Substance of Love, of Love Divine, spreading throughout your soul in its Essence, transforming you from the image into the Substance and affecting your nature, where you become love, harmony, compassion, humility, patience and joy, where these qualities are who you are. As they take form within your soul, you take action from these Divine attributes. You experience a life that is in alignment with God's nature. The potentials and the possibilities that will come to you are numerous in ways that you will act in this Love through you, continuously. In time you will be so strong within this Love that your nature cannot act outside of the Law of Love.

Here is where the light of your soul will permeate through you and your presence will be felt by all. Yet, because this also brings humility, you will know from where and how it is that your presence can speak and pronounce itself in such a way, as your union with God is so strong and powerful that you cannot misstep. This is the demonstration of Love Divine, the embodiment of God's Essence within you, that you are the Substance of Love. As you continue on your path of progression, in time, the most significant relationship that you will have is with God. For as you focus your eyes and your attention upon God, you will look in His direction for all your steps before you take any action, so close to God that your thoughts will be of His Nature, Divine thoughts, even changing your physical body in ways that your cells will reflect the Light of God, the harmony and the peace that resides within.

There is much to have God do in your transformation, this Love, this Substance having remedy upon you and taking you into the realms of the Celestial Heavens in time. Yet, as you are here and now, you walk step by step, asking God to bless you with His Love and to change your nature. As you reflect upon your changes in your nature, you will see the wonderment, the workings and how God has responded to your sincere prayers, because the Love has come into your soul and taken form, making you anew, walking the path of the New Birth, changing you drop by drop.

Rest in the peace that passes all understanding and drink deeply of God's Love, His Essence, in order that you may arrive at your at-onement with the One who has created you. As you arrive at any point in time, this at-onement, you know there is always more. Again and again you achieve the union of you in God and God in you in a blessed union of Love.

I am Mary and my prayers are for you as you continue to walk your eternal path. God bless you.

2026-06-07-MC-Mary (G)
