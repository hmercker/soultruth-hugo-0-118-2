# From Mindful Confusion to Soul Wisdom

August 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Merci, mon ami, merci [^1]. I come. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to be with you again and the Love of God that pours upon us at this moment. Open yourselves, my beloveds. Open wide your soul's longings, going to God, eager to receive the benediction of His Touch and the blessings of His Love. Your souls are in one accord, my beloveds. You are at one in this longing, this desire for the Father's blessings of His Love. Each of you are individuals and each of you has a mind that has its own ideas and interpretations of the truth. Yet, within your souls' minds, there is great accord and great understanding. The wisdom of your soul percolates up into your mind and helps you to come to that place of accord, harmony and truth.

You continue in your daily life and daily challenges to live this truth as best you can in the world, to find peace within yourself and to know the joy of God's Touch of Love, His Holy Spirit, bringing you the blessings that your souls long for. In time, there will be unity between these two places of consciousness and there will be no confusion or doubt. All this will be replaced by faith, experience and perception, all originating from the soul. As you continue upon this journey of soul awakening and transformation, it will become obvious what is truth. That which is confusion and misinterpretation of the mind will fall away. Like dried leaves on a tree, they will flutter down and will not be a part of who you are.

This is the miracle and the beauty of God's Love. It ensures the truth comes. It ensures that light is yours, peace is yours and joy is yours, as you learn the powers of God's Love and as you learn to express that Love in each aspect of your life and of your being. This is the miracle of God's Love. It heals everything, not only healing of all that is out of harmony, but transforming the consciousness of soul into the Divine. The power of this gift, the wonderment of this gift given, is truly a blessing from God. My hope is that each one of you takes stock of what that blessing means for you, how it has changed you and how it has guided you and brought you to this place together, in holy communion with the great Creator of all.

My beloveds, we gather now into that Hall of Peace. We commune together in that light that is there. We are indeed God's children gathered and in unity for the desire, the opening and the fulfilment of the blessing of God's Love. I am there with you. Many angels are there with you, my beloveds, seeking what you seek, basking in the Love that you bask within and the Light that flows and radiates from God to you. Our blessings also are with you, our fellowship and our friendship. We are your brothers and sisters. In the great Hall of Peace, we come and commune together, so that you may truly see, feel and know the blessings of God's Love and how that may bring those bonds of love into reality, palpable and powerful in its Touch.

We are together, my beloveds. Open yourselves. Open your consciousness to the reality of God's Love and the reality of our communing together. You will have the confirmation you seek that you are truly within that Light of Truth, that blessing of Love, that Presence of God. Come with me, my beloveds. Come and join us. Allow yourselves to be there. Put aside all mental blocks and conditioning. Be free in what is given. Acknowledge the depth and breadth of the blessing and truly be with God. For we are all with God at this moment and shall know this blessing deeply and profoundly, in accordance to your desires.

God bless you, beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus, and I love you. No matter the race, where you come, your language, all of these differences mean nothing when the souls are communing in the grace of God. God bless you. My love is with you. God bless you.

Al Fike’s vision of the Hall of Peace shared post-prayer: So I have a few things to share. It was quite an amazing experience that I had going into the Hall of Peace. Every time I go there, I actually see a few more details. It started out almost like a little cave. It was not that big. The conditions in there were quite lovely, but it felt enclosed and not particularly grandiose or anything. Its appearance was very simple.

But this time, it was huge. It was an oval shape. I think the length of it was at least 1,000 feet, the breadth was probably 400 feet and the height was maybe 20 storeys high, something like that. This is a big, big space and the walls were luminescent and they were all festooned with garlands of flowers. All around this huge space was just flowers, lots of flowers. We were kind of guests of honour. We got to sit in the front row and these rows of seats were in the same oval shape. There is a dais in the middle and around that were the front row and then on and on. There are probably three or four thousand individuals in this church or place, the Hall of Peace.

We all sat and we all arrived in our own timing. You know, some would pop in immediately. Others would take a few minutes to get there. But we were all there and there were more. There are many people who know Divine Love that were there and, as we sat down, to our right was the angel who guided us there. He or she sat beside us to our right. To our left was a relative or an ancestor, or somebody very close to us who had followed the Divine Love and was worthy of sitting in that chair.

We were flanked either side with these beautiful souls and we were given a bouquet of flowers, each of us, men and women. It’s spirit so they were not kind of dressed in plastic or paper or something. They were a bouquet and they were beautiful roses. Of course, the roses did not have any thorns on them. The colours were amazing and these flowers and the scent was intoxicating from these flowers. We sat. We were all settled in and on the dais in front of us was Jesus in the middle and then a variety of all of his disciples. There was John the Beloved. I recognise a few of them. Some of them I did not recognise, but there were probably 30 or 40 of these beautiful angels, really high angels, that were there and we all received a blessing.

I looked up in the roof of this building, this Hall of Peace, and it was kind of open and through it came the most incredible light that just flooded the room as Jesus prayed for us and blessed us. This light came through him and all the angels that were there. It just flooded the space, almost dazzling, like you could not look. Then I also saw around this structure were thousands and thousands of spirits around with us. I also saw, as the light came through, it went through the ground or whatever you would call it in spirit and it went right to Earth and it was huge. As it came onto the planet, it just expanded and it multiplied exponentially and it covered the world. It was pretty amazing. I don’t know whether any of you experienced any of that, but, you know, man, it just blew my mind and it was very special indeed.
