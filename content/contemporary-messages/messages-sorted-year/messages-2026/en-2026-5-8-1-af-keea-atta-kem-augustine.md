# The Lattice of Light: Strengthening the Network of Divine Love

May 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem & Augustine received by Al Fike

**Keea Atta Kem:** May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours. I am Keea atta Kem. I come to encourage you in your adventures to bring light into the world. When you do so, you establish and reinforce that wonderful Lattice of Light in the world, a lattice that is far-reaching, a network of connection, soul to soul, that comes with the Father's Love enlivening and establishing these bonds of love, bonds of love that you feel together, bonds of love that reach out tendrils of light that reach into the world, connecting with others.

This is happening every moment, my beloved friends. These tendrils are reaching out and drawing in the souls that are yearning for truth, for light and for love. As these connections are established, as the Lattice grows, so the light grows upon your world. In this, we are able to work through this Lattice of Light to help those who are seeking and hungering and to help those whose souls are open. You become those nodes of light where many tendrils reach out and touch others. Through your instrumentality, God reaches out and establishes a bond with that beautiful soul, his yearning and desiring the Divine Touch, the Divine Love.

Yes, much work is being done, even when you sleep, every moment of your existence. We work through you and with you as God's agents to bring greater light and truth into the world, to bring comfort, healing and upliftment to many. You do not see the vastness of this work because you lack the eyes to see, many of you. Yet indeed, I wish to tell you that the work that you do and the efforts that you make are far-reaching and a great blessing to many. Do not allow yourself to be restrictive in your thinking and your prayers, but be open to God's Will and God's efforts to touch the souls of humanity. He needs you as those nodes of light, for they are powerful and they help to invigorate and to grow these tendrils of light reaching out into the world. In this way, this level of reality, this work becomes very crucial. It is subtle. Many do not understand or realise what is taking place, not only in your consciousness, but in the consciousness of those around you and those who are being touched, but it has an effect. It inspires and uplifts. Eventually, the consciousness of knowing will come to them and they will find a way to connect with you or someone who has the truth, who is willing to share the truth, who is willing to pray with them and be with them in the light of truth, in the Light of God's Love.

Yes, the work continues and it is vast, with many angels involved, bright spirits, even your stellar friends are involved in weaving this great network, this Lattice of Light. We are grateful for your consistency in prayer, for the beauty of your longings and your desires to be with God. In this, you are empowering much work and many blessings for others. My beloved friends, continue to be a light. Continue to seek for the highest. In your life, may it be an expression of the highest, the greatest light and truth. In this way, you become effective instruments in the ways that I have described and in many other ways.

Blessings to you, my beloved friends. I am Keea Atta Kem. I love you all. I am with you often. God bless you all in His Love.

**Augustine:** I am your teacher, Augustine. Your dedications, my beloved students, are crucial in this work. When you are attuned to God and desire to be with God in a conscious, soulful way, you are empowering this great network of which beloved Keea speaks. You are giving validity to the truth, for as you live it, as you pray for this gift, as you are used as God's channels, so you are a testimony to the power of God's Love working through you and with you and within you.

Be true to your soul's longings, beloved students. Be true to that which is of light. Be a channel of love wherever you are and wherever you go. It is important that you keep your thoughts and your intentions toward the highest at all times. Practice this and apply it to everything that you do, all of your life. You will know a sweetness, a harmony and a peace that cannot be had any other way. This light, when allowed to be expressed, allowed to infiltrate all parts of you and all parts of your life, will be a great beacon for others to be drawn to and others to benefit by. In time, as you are an example and as you teach this truth to others, they too will become a light. They too will become an instrument of God to bring this light to others. Be true to God's Will, beloved souls. Be true to the truth. Be joyful in the truth. Be clear and focused on your journey of discovering truth and living it.

May you truly be blessed, beloved students of truth, and truly find joy in it. God wants this for you and God needs this of you, to be His channels of love and light in the world. Be true to this and God will bless you mightily. God bless you, my beloveds, beautiful students. I am Augustine and I love you. God bless you.
