# It Is in Service that Fulfillment Comes

September 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Andrew received by Al Fike

The sun shines upon you, my beloved souls. I am Andrew and the sun shines within you, a sun that is brighter than any other sun, that shall increase in measure for eternity. That inner light will continue to grow as you receive more of this blessing of the Father's Love. Oh, my beloved friends, rejoice in the light that is given. Rejoice in the opportunities that God gives us all to serve, to be a channel of love in the world, to be a light.

For it is in our service, done in humility and grace, done without a thought for our own reward, done in love, that fulfilment comes to our soul as its gifts are expressed, as we step forward in that expression with clarity, humility and grace. Today, you have the opportunity to express your gifts into the world. Think of your day in this way. Think of what may come. What souls may be drawn to you. What conversations you may have.

What light may be spread in a world so desirous of light, a world that thirsts for the Living Waters, a world that hungers for truth. My beloved friends, today is the opportunity to serve. I urge you to think of it in this way and to be in gratitude, rejoicing in the possibilities, in the flow of love, laughter and joy. It is a gift this day and we will be close. My friends, expect the unexpected and feel the excitement of what might come in a day, in this day.

My beloved friends, I am Andrew and I will be with you, as will be many. God bless you, beloved souls.
