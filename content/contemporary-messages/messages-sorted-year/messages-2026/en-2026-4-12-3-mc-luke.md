# The Universe is Contained in God's Love

April 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Luke received by Maureen Cardoso

May the gift of God's Love and its inflowing meet your longings and this great inflowing find a way into the beautiful soul that you are, allowing the Essence of God to fill you, awaken you, to bring harmony and peace within you. I am Luke and I am happy to be here to pray with you, to share my love, presence and gifts with you and to bring a message of truth, hope and understanding to each one of you that hears this message.

The universe is contained by the Divine Essence, God's Love. It flows and touches all aspects of God's creation, holding things in harmony, allowing things to move in the finite way that has been designed for each planet, each galaxy, each universe, moving in harmony. Because you, dear souls, have opened yourself to receive this great gift of Divine Love, not only is that energy around you, the Essence of God around you, but it is within you. For through the Holy Spirit, this Love has made its mark upon your soul and is awakening the universe of your soul. When you are progressing on your path and your consciousness is shifting and changing, it is good to ponder the idea that you too are contained within the Divine Love, this beautiful quality of God's Essence flowing around you and in you.

So how could it be that harm would reach you? How could it be that you step too far off of the path? For this containment is like a magnetic draw within your soul that brings you back into the realms of God's Love and the dimensions of God's Love as you continue to progress on your path of at-onement with God. It is good as you are walking your life, making your choices, perceiving the changes that are happening, with the awareness of how important it is to give great consideration to what you do and how you do it. For in that, when you understand that the containment of Divine Love is around you, it is good to be able to perceive that auric field, that energy field of Divine Love that is around you, awaiting your desire, awaiting your asking, awaiting your longing for its inflowing.

When you can begin to look at your life from the inside and perceive and feel what it is that your soul truly desires, you will come to a greater sense of ease for longer periods of time. When you have the ability to be within yourself and see how you are responding and reacting to the world that is of the material, and you choose to allow the Essence of Love that each one of you contains to act for you, to inform you, to see through your own eyes, to breathe you and to experience the Love that you are, this is a sign of progression. This is something that each one of you will grow in your ability to ensure that your life is enacted through the Divine Love that is within you.

Great harmony will be your experience. For you have the understanding that God's Love is the most powerful, the most harmonious, the most giving Substance of all the universe. That love is a part of you, that love desires to flow from you, that love desires to be expressed. As your consciousness has the opportunity to really be able to feel this Love, its qualities, its way of responding, its personality, you can receive more and more, because you are open, you are receptive and you are experiencing that Divine quality of you moving through your life.

So, dear ones, as you move along your journey, be still from within and live from this point, this centre of you. In that, no harm can come to you. All uncertainties will be and are resolved when you attune yourself to your centre point, that point of your soul that is in union with God, that is becoming at-one with God, that contains the Divine Essence of God. In this way, your life unfolds in a most powerful way, a most peaceful and harmonious way. You will not need the world or people or circumstances to be your way because within you, you are your way and your way is God's Way. God's Way is Love and your way is love. This is where you are growing and this is where you are all headed to, no matter what is happening around you. Within you is a powerful light, a powerful energy and spirit that is contained within Love Divine. It is who you are becoming. It is what is informing you and it is what shall guide you forward. Always be open, receptive and willing to receive that which is around you. Your life is contained within God's Love. Always be open so that this magnetic force of God's Love can flow into your soul and attract more and more of this Love.

I am Luke and I hope my words have been able to find a way into your soul, into your mind and into your body, so that this small truth can shift your sense of consciousness and help you to walk in the world as the light you truly are, guided by God always. God bless you as you sit in prayer and allow this Love in, allow its power to protect you, to guide you and to live through you. God bless you.
