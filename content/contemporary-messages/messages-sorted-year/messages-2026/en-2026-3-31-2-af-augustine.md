# When You Have Faith, You Can Step into the Vast Ocean of God’s Creation Without Fear

March 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine. The universe is filled with truth. It would take an eternity to truly understand this and to absorb it. You lay the foundation for this journey of awakening to truth, to God's creation, as you seek the Father's Love. You prepare yourselves and equip yourselves with what is required, the soul perceptions, the soul faculties and gifts that will allow you to take that journey, to truly see, understand, feel, integrate and know the truth.

If you were to understand the vastness of this journey and the wonderment of God, who gives you and equips you with the ability, the potential, to go on this journey, then you would be truly humble. For how else can you appreciate and understand the power of God's blessing for you than to see for yourself the scope and broad spectrum of knowledge, perception and experience that awaits you? It is like you are entering into a vast ocean. For many of you, to do so without faith would bring fear and a wonder as to how you might survive such an experience. When you have faith, when you trust in your soul's perception and you trust in God's Will, then entering into that ocean is merely a part of the journey, the intention that God has for you.

I beseech you, my friends, step into that ocean of awareness, of understanding and of truth. You will find yourself immersed in the magnificence of God's creation. In that, you will sing your Hosannas to God and you will thirst and long for more of His Love. For it is in what he gives you with His Love, the awakenings deepen and broaden, the capacities to know are exercised and awakened by His Love. We in the Celestial Kingdom know of these things and have all experienced them in our own way, in our own time. We come to you, urging you along the road, because we are well aware of what will come to you, the blessings that are waiting for you. We want you to truly be immersed in these things. You will not be lost within it. You will not lose yourself. You will not lose anything, but you will gain the universe of God's creation, the universe of God's Presence, the universe of your own soul's ability to truly understand and experience these wonderful things.

My beloved and beautiful students, we carry on on this journey, not merely for your own enlightenment, education and awakening, but so that you may be teachers, healers and lights in the world to lead the way to this new way of knowing, of experiencing and being in the world. For this is how humanity must approach life if there is to be true progress, true understanding in the world, true joy and true love. We beseech you time and time again, and you enter ever so gradually into this great ocean that I speak of. At times you retreat, for even at the depth that you experience it, you feel uncomfortable. You are suspicious. You think that your mind is playing tricks. Yet, in truth, your soul is truly finding its way to where it is destined to be and you must follow your consciousness, catching up with your soul's experience, awareness and desire.

It is time for you to abandon any reticence, any fear, but to plunge headlong into this truth, this true awakening, this true revelation that comes as you understand who you are, understand the capacities that you carry, trust in them and feed them with the faith that comes through prayer and receiving the gift of His Love. My beloved and beautiful students of truth, that ocean awaits you. It will carry you. It will anoint you. It will help you to understand and to be inspired to teach others the true blessings that come with the Father's Love, the true blessings that are available to every soul. Seek and you will find this. Pray for it. Ask for it. It will come like a Pentecostal experience, enveloping you, not only in Love, but in knowledge, perception, understanding and vision. All of these things come as you receive this gift of Love and qualify you with the awakening of your faculties, so that you are truly immersed and know the awesomeness, the wonderment of God and His creation.

God bless you, my beloved ones. God bless you in His Love. We will journey together. We will play within the ocean of truth and knowledge. We shall do so with great joy and abandonment of the material mind's fear and restrictions that hold us from this true experience which you are destined to have at some point in your life, either here in the material world or in the spirit world. It waits for you. You determine when and how this will happen, but it must happen. For as your souls awaken, so they are prepared for the awakening. God bless you, beloved students. Your teacher, Augustine, loves you and is with you every step of the way, eagerly anticipating the next step, the next choice, the next blessing. God bless you. Augustine loves you. God bless you.
