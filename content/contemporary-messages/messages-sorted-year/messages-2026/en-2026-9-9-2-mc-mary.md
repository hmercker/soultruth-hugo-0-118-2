# When You Find Yourself Out of Balance, Take a Moment to Return to the Peace of the Soul

September 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Mary and I have waited to be able to touch your hearts in this way. I encourage you to be at peace, to allow peace to reign over your life and to live a life of peace. When you find yourself outside of those realms, take a moment and allow yourself to come back into the peace that is within the soul. For in this way you will find the strength, the desire and the love of the soul to stand as a light in the world, no matter where you are or the circumstances that you find yourself in. For peace is what reigns over your life, a beautiful attribute of the soul, the soul that is filled with God's Love, the calm Living Waters that reside within.

For when your mind is filled with peace, there is nothing that can rustle your waters that would resemble a storm or be of disturbance. It is a beautiful way to live from the presence of your soul when you are conscious of your essence and you allow this deep blessing of God's creation, your true nature, to lead your way. Breathe in the peace of God's Love as you travel forth into the world, leaving this sanctuary of light, furthering your service and instrumentality into the various realms of humanity. You are strong in numbers and you are blessed with the angels by your side and God's Love always available only for your asking. Walk with an open heart and experience that inflowing at all times.

Allow your life to be led in this way. For as you do so, there is peace within. My love goes with you. My presence walks by your side. You are the light bringers on a mission, setting forth, setting sail into the light as God's instruments of love.

I am Mary. I bless each one of you with the strength of peace, a heart that is soft and open and a mind that is in harmony with your heart and your soul. God bless you all in these days ahead. God bless you.
