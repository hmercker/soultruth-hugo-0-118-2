# Queen Victoria Describes Her Journey in Spirit and Shares Her Hopes For God’s Truth to be Shared

September 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Queen Victoria received by Al Fike

Indeed, may the Father's Love pour within your soul, changing you, healing you and transforming you. I am Victoria, once queen of this country and I come to encourage you, beloved souls, seekers of truth and light, to bring this Truth of God's Love to this country so in need of it. So many walk this land bereft of love and in much pain. Though there are material comforts, very much more than when I was on the Earth, there is little comfort for the soul, little comfort for one another. Like all of this world of yours, it is awash with materialism and the desire for more, to assuage the pain and loneliness that is so common in the hearts of men and women.

My journey has been a long one. I am not Celestial, but I am close. I have come into the Seventh Sphere, after much struggle and many prayers. When I passed over into the spirit world, it was a great shock to me. For I assumed, and I was a spiritualist of sorts, I assumed that I would have the comforts and conditions of life that I had on Earth. What a shock it was to be like anyone else, to be in the spirit world without the accoutrements of my station and my sovereignty. Yet, in time I became used to these conditions and adjusted accordingly. Then I began a search for truth. Many assisted me upon the way. Now I know God's Love as my saving grace. Indeed, that has healed me of much of the compensation that I needed to accomplish after the rule that I was a part of in your world.

Much harm was done in my name, much suffering of many around the world because of the arrogance and the ignorance of my people and myself. I wish to encourage you upon your path, seeking the great Love of God. To do so in the flesh gives you great advantage, beloved souls of Earth, great advantage indeed. For you will not have to suffer the pangs, the realisations of a life that seemed well-lived and of light and yet, in many respects, was not.

Many come into this spirit world and do not realise the consequences of their actions until that time that they have become aware and have realised, often in horror, what they have done through ignorance, neglect and a lack of true spiritual understanding or health. It is, my beloved friends, that you must be very diligent in your efforts to help others in this world. The task is tremendous indeed, overwhelming. Yet, you have such wonderful help, many beautiful angels and the beloved master at your side. Very few in the world have such advantages. I would say, if you were to think of the advantages in a material sense that I had while on Earth, you have the spiritual advantage that is equivalent and this is tremendous and beautiful to behold, beloved souls of truth.

Take full advantage of what is given and do not keep this to yourself. Do not hide away from humanity, but assert yourself in the world. Speak of your experience. Speak of how God loves you and how you know that God loves you. Speak of prayer and how in prayer you may receive many blessings and benefits and receive this great gift of God's Love. These are the treasures of the soul that may be accumulated through your efforts in prayer and through your efforts to share with others.

So much can be gained in the way of light and spiritual advantage when one walks the world as God's servant, when you increase your light daily and share your love with all. Be true to your soul's desires. Be true to God's Will and you will live an extraordinary life, not one caged by obligation and situation like I was, but one free and filled with joy and filled with many, many opportunities to serve, to bring the light to others. You have a great advantage, my friends, beautiful and powerful opportunities, filled with God's blessings, filled with angels by your side, resplendent in the gifts from God. So much you have, my beloved friends, far beyond the scope of your vision, a light so bright and deep that you do not recognise for yourself what I say to you. As you continue to love your brothers and sisters and to love yourself, that light will become evident indeed, and you will be well-pleased with the results of your efforts to receive the gift that is God's Love.

Those of you who begin this journey, take heed. For it is in effort, it is in dedication, it is in growing faith and desire to serve that you will know the benefits, you will reap the rewards of these things. Much is offered. It is in abundance beyond your imaginings and so open yourselves with great desire and earnestness to seek these blessings from God and God will answer your prayers with such fervency and beauty and power that you will be overwhelmed by love, overjoyed by love, taken beyond the confines of a mind that has been filled mostly with irrelevancy, to a place of true awareness, understanding and love. It is yours for the asking, my friends. Take full advantage and you will find yourself in the glory of God's Touch and His Love forevermore.

I am Victoria and I come to you to encourage you. I thank those who have travelled to come to this land and share the truth. I will be with you, as will many and you will indeed feel our blessings, our strength and our love and support. You will know deep peace as well. God bless you, my friends. May His Love continue to pour into your soul with abundance, a great torrent of Love washing over you and in you, healing you, uplifting you, changing you and bringing great wisdom to you. God bless you, my friends.
