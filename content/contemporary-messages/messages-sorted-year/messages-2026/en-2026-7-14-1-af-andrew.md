# Acknowledge the Many Milestones Along the Divine Path

July 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Andrew. The journey of growing within the soul with God's Love is endless. You begin this journey right at the beginning of your incarnation. This time on the Earth plane is so important, so crucial, deciding the direction that you may go and the journey that you may take. When you choose God, when you choose to commit your souls to God, that you may receive this great gift of His Love, you choose the endless journey, the one that shall take you beyond the limitations of your mind, beyond the conditions created by humanity through endless choice, endless experience and mindful accumulations that determine another road, the road of natural love. You have chosen the Divine Path. In this, you have made a wise choice, one that is compliant with your soul and the desires of your soul.

Many in your world do not understand this, do not recognise that there is this part of each of you that has an inclination, a desire to be at-one with God. For many, it gets covered up by the world and the experiences of this world. Yet, you, my beloved, beloved friends, have chosen otherwise, though each have had your struggles, each have been distracted and each have gone down various roads to test and research the truth. In the end, you are here together, beautiful souls that you are, in harmony and alignment with the Truth of God's Love, seeking to be in harmony and alignment with one another and of course, seeking to be in alignment with God.

There are milestones along the way, my friends. For many of you, life has been a struggle. It has been full of heartache. It has been a place where much pain has been experienced. Yet, as you move along this path of Love, do you not find that the pain eases and that the heartache is no longer there, that you have found something so substantial, something that lasts and continues to uphold through any conditions, any situations? This is the path of God's Love. It is powerful. It heals. It brings the soul to consciousness so that your mind is not the only instrument that determines your fate, but you will come to know the wisdom of your soul and the guidance that God gives you through your soul. This ensures that you are upon this journey, this road of love, that the wisdom, the impetus, the desire within you grows with this Love, setting forth another marker along the road, no longer distracted by the material, but strengthened by the spiritual, with a deep desire to further grow and awaken in this Truth, to be at one with God, the soul's true desire, at-onement with the Creator.

There are many, many other markers along the way, my friends, but suffice to say that I have spoken of a few. Indeed, they are important. For what else will keep you steady upon the road but these milestones that say you have grown, you have struggled and yet you have grown? You have become stronger, your mind clearer, your soul's wisdom coming forward into your consciousness. Love, love, my friends, is in you and may be expressed in many ways through you. This is the greatest marker of all. It is to know and learn how to love, to love oneself, to love your brothers and sisters, to love the world, to love God, even to love us, your Celestial friends. Where love is present, so is light. Where light is present, harmony ensues. In this way, you will find your life more harmonious than it was and you will find joy there, my friends, great joy.

God gives you so much. God allows you so much latitude to explore, to seek out, to understand the truth. God does not insist. God merely offers in Love, in humility and grace what can be in your life, in yourself and what you can express in your life. So much is offered. So much is given and yet so little is actually asked of you, my friends, merely to have faith, to set forth an earnest prayer, to make some time to listen to God and to be with God, to ask for that gift of Love and in this, you mark your progress. You mark the way toward greater light, greater harmony, greater love and greater joy. How blessed is this simple road? How wonderful is the journey?

May you be blessed, my beloved friends, acknowledging with the awareness both of the mind and the soul of how you are loved and how God's Love has guided you along this road. God bless you, my beloved friends. We continue to mark our way. We hold the staff of truth to steady us along the road. We have faith and vision to see us forward as we make this journey and seek the truth, ever greater, ever more wondrous, deeper, wider, full and resplendent, as God gives to us all the truth that we are able to receive. Thank you, my beloveds. I am Andrew. I love you so. Thank you and God bless you.
