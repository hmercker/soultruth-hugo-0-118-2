# This Great Truth is the Road to At-Onement With God

September 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Acton Spiritualist Chruch Retreat, Acton, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be with you. I come to you as your brother and your friend. I am Jesus. You may doubt that I might come to you, but in truth, all those who seek light, all those who are upon the journey toward God, I am your brother and friend and I speak not only to you within this room in the flesh, but to many who surround you in spirit, that this great truth that I once taught and continue to teach is the redemption of the soul. It is the road to at-onement with God and it is available to each and every soul who seeks it.

No matter your perspective, no matter what you have done in your life that may be difficult for you to acknowledge, any condition that you carry, God will forgive you and God does. God loves you and shall love you for eternity. You may indeed take advantage and receive that gift of Love, if you but desire it within your heart and soul. It is given freely and abundantly, without conditions, without stipulations, merely a gift.

This gift shall set you free, my beloveds, from all conditions, dilemmas, pain and anguish. These things shall fall away with the blessing of this Love bestowed upon your soul. I come to you to beseech you to receive, to rest in the arms of God who loves you so. May the blessings be yours, my beloveds, be yours for all eternity.

I am Jesus and I come in love. God bless you all in the Light of His Love. God bless you.
