# Become Aware of Your Reflexive and Unconscious Impediments

August 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

The act of your soul's awakening is an act of faith. It is your soul's desire to know God manifest in consciousness. My beloved friends, I am Orion and I come to encourage you to awaken, encourage you to become that beautiful soul in a conscious way, where you have within you the light. Your soul is charged with God's Love, and it is in the process of awakening. Yet, there are impediments, as you know.

A great impediment is the very environment in which you live, this earthly plane, is a difficult one. Then, as a result of this environment and your living within it, there are many impediments that are put in place in order to protect you from the harshness of the world, impediments that have come because of your social conditioning, impediments that are there because of fear. impediments because of mental biases. These are all things that may be remedied if you are willing.

It is not as if you are stuck within these conditions and cannot move. You have free will and you can choose what it is you want to do. The greater problem is those things that you are not aware of, those reflexive conditions within you that are so common and so much a part of you that it is difficult to see them. They are hidden within your daily response to life. Yet, you must come to the realisation, the consciousness of that which holds you back from being that light in the world, that beautiful soul redeemed and free. This is why you have your friends, most certainly your angel friends and we, from my planet and so many others. We are here to help you understand, help you to realise what it is that holds you back from that true soul's consciousness, that awakening that is there just at your fingertips, so very close and yet, you hesitate. Even that hesitation is often reflexive and you are not aware of it, but it is hesitation nonetheless, and you are continuing to grapple with the impediments of which I speak.

Yet, there is hope and there is the possibility in the near future that you will be freed of these things, for the awakening is close and the impediments are not so strong. They are weakened by the power of Love. As you continue to receive this great gift of the Divine Love, you are strengthening the very core of your being which, in turn, in its way, informs your consciousness. Our hope is that the core of your being, the awakened part, will infect the unawakened part and therefore a transition, a shift of consciousness, may take place as you continue to grow, as the power of that Love continues to have its effects, as you are awakened slowly from the slumbers of the children of Earth who sleep.

You will become different. You will see the world differently. You will know who you are, truly. You will know who God is truly and you will understand the layers and complexities of life. Not all of the layers and all the complexities, but enough that awakening may be a permanent thing. There is unfortunately a harshness to that awakening, for you begin to see in a clear way the darkness that is of your world, the air that proliferates in your world, the thoughts and conditions of your brothers and sisters who inhabit this world and have no inkling of why they are so unhappy, why they are so alone, why they grieve within their soul that emptiness that exists.

This is where you may help. You may help to inform your brothers and sisters, to show them the way to heal that emptiness, that hurt, that pain and to bring joy into their beings, true awareness and true truth, so that they may be freed from the shackles of the human condition. My friends, it is a big task, I know. Yet, you are well on your way to your own healing and it would be a pity if you did not share the journey, what has happened to you, your own testimony and witnessing of what is truly the Truth of God's Love. You will continue to grow, my friends.

The eternity of growth, of awakening, transformation, all these things come on the heels of God's Love. May you know this deep joy. May you know this fulfilment that erases the emptiness. May you know the Truth and live by it, that you may be free, my friends, truly free, my beloved friends. You continue to walk toward greater light, to come to know the Creator of all the universe and to know your part within that universe. It comes on the heels of this gift of Love. It awakens you and then it transforms you. We journey together, my friends. We are happy to see the light growing within and we are eager to help you transition from the condition of human error and darkness to that of light, joy and freedom. It is at your beck and call, my friends, your fingertips. It awaits your desire, your eagerness to be in the thrall of God's embrace and God's Love.

God bless you, my friend. Orion loves you. God bless you all, the world and all of humanity. God bless you. Orion loves you.
