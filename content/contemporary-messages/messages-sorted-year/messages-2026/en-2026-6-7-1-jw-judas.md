# God does the heavy Lifting

June 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Johnson City, NY, USA

Judas received by Jimbeau Walsh

I am here, your brother Judas.

I come in the love of God.

My brothers and sisters, who have come to share in the bounty of the Father’s love, all of us here honor you for your persistence in prayer, for your willingness to be open, and to share as a Divine Love family.

Today, I wish to speak about gravity, gravitas. The gravity of each life, and of the human condition. Not only what is a part of the nature of Creation, but the gravity that sometimes is created to protect oneself, created for comfort, for safety, sometimes for avoidance or numbness. And that gravity, the weight of the world, that is so often created by individuals, for their own protection. And yet, you come here in this circle, amidst everything that is going on politically, environmentally, socially, which can seem like chaos, disruption. You come together in this prayer, and the love of God gives you a weightlessness, lifting you up, if even for a moment. And isn’t that really why you come, to feel that atonement that lifts you up, that heals you, that changes your soul, and brings you progression, community, communion, and joy.

Ahh, but to be lifted in any moment, this is the gift of the Father to you. What will you do with it in the world? For when you bring this in your walk, whether knowingly or unknowingly, you shine a light on everyone you come in contact with. You bring that grace, and you have the ability to give those in the world a moment of weightlessness. Consider when you fall in love, your troubles disappear, you’re not thinking about your bills or your issues at work, or your family. You’re thinking about the one you love, and you are in bliss, and you wish to stay in that place. This multiplied beyond a number is the gift of God’s love, and once you feel it, you wish for it to be part of you, you wish for it to be in your being, and as you have been encouraged, all it takes is prayer. You need not have prayer beads or a rosary or a minister or a church, just a conversation with God, whether with words or simply the longings of your souls, and this you know.

So, as you walk out into the world after this sacred, holy, and joyous gathering, stay in grace as best you can, and you will see your light shining upon all those souls, for a light in the darkness is what the light in your souls truly is. May you shine it upon every being. Also be mindful of the obstacles, not only those you have created but those that are seemingly put upon you, such as grief, the loss of a loved one, which tells you how much you love that one. Addiction, which tells you that you have a wish to numb yourself from the pain of existence, and yet coming through that and leaving that baggage behind is the greatest freedom. So, whatever you believe your burdens to be, simply take them to God. Allow the longings in your souls to bring you freedom, to bring you joy, and to bring the world a blessing that God so wishes for it and you, as God’s instruments, and we here in the celestial kingdom as your helpers. Together we shall be part of this change and remember lastly that when you engage in prayer from your soul, God does the heavy lifting. All you need to do is to be in the love, and we are with you, and we love you. Go in peace in the blessings of God.

I am your brother and friend, I am Judas.
