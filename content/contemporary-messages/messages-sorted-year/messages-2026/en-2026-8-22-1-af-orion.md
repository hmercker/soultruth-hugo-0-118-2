# A Lesson on the Three Minds: Material, Spirit and Soul

August 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. Today, I wish to address the reality that you experience in consciousness, the three minds that the dear child has mentioned in her prayer. Indeed, this separation of consciousness into these three parts was never intended, but they became the natural outcomes of the decision made at the beginning of your existence to have that separation from God. We do not have these separations of consciousness as you do. For we are fully of soul and soul consciousness. At some day in the future, so will humanity be only of soul and soul consciousness. Yet, that day will not come soon.

You have these levels and layers of consciousness. The deeper you go, the closer you come to soul. Yet, each functions well. The material consciousness is full of curiosity, intellectual abilities and the capacity to deduce truth in its own way. These things are a gift and they are indeed important in your material life. For without them, you would have a great deal of difficulty integrating into this material life. God, even though humanity has taken that journey of separation, has been given the opportunity and the capacity to engage in life in such a way that all may potentially be in harmony.

Then there is the bridge between the soul consciousness and the material consciousness, which is the spirit consciousness and this too is a great gift from God. For this is where your intuition lies. This is where you are able to add to the strictly material deductions that come with the material mind and this adds its part. It displays its part on the journey of life. With this comes deep emotions, certain insights and aspects of perception that are not strictly of the material. It is beyond the five senses. Often it is called the sixth sense, but it is more than six.

There are several that come with the spirit opened and developed. When those who go into the spirit world continue in that development, they come to that place of perfection, including those sensibilities and senses of the spirit and they reside within what you call the Sixth Sphere. Yet, that does not mean that the soul is opened, developed, awakened and transformed by this great gift, the Divine Love. When this happens, then greater perceptions and abilities come and you find yourself in a different world, a different reality than that which these other two centres of consciousness can provide. It is the reality of God. It is the capacity to understand God and to communicate with God in a way that cannot be with the other two centres. Indeed, when soul consciousness is refined and transformed, the other two centres of consciousness are absorbed by it.

It becomes, in its own way, consciousness complete. This is the consciousness that we have, that your angel friends have and others in the universe who have attained this level and this awakening. My friends, there is a lofty goal ahead of you, the integration of all of these aspects of your being into one. In this, they are not just singular components added together, the three into one. It is more than this. It becomes far more than this. As this gift of the Love is added for eternity, then indeed the abilities and gifts of the soul become virtually limitless and the reality of the soul expands for eternity and at-onement with God is achieved.

Within this at-onement is continued experience, insight, capacity to love, capacity to create, to be and to feel. All of these things expanded infinitely as we all travel this infinite road. That is what you call the divine path. My beloved friends, you have reached for and desire the highest road, the road with infinite possibilities, with the capacity to be, to shine in light with such brightness. Even the stars begin to fade compared to this brightness. You have elected to come into at-onement with God and that level of consciousness cannot be described.

The feeling of joy and love, all the wonderment of the soul's reality, experience and sensibilities cannot be described to those of you who live upon this earthly plane, so limited, so stuck within the margins of the material and the material mind. Yet, I know within your soul you feel an excitement when you think of, when you acknowledge the journey that you are on. That soul knows that in time, its capacity to be alive, to truly take its place in the firmament of God's creation, of the reality of soul, brings a great expectation, a joyous desire to enter into that reality and consciousness, bringing with it those other two that are now separate but destined to be one. My beloved friends, you will find yourself upon a road that will bring many, many experiences, insight, knowledge, perception, joy, feelings of love, awareness of what love truly is and how it may be expressed. All the light, the joy and the wonderment will be yours, my beloved friends, upon this journey. I know you want to share this with others and you are making efforts to do so.

Indeed, it is difficult to explain and to share with someone with limited perceptions what it is that you are truly experiencing and the journey that you are on. You will and you will be a great example, a shining example of that, of the outcomes that will come by receiving this great gift of Love. The infinite journey, the infinite development, awakening, transformation, all that is involved in soul consciousness, will serve you well as God's instruments, lights in the world, bringing comfort, healing and peace to many. We continue on this journey, my beloved friends, truly in the wake of God's Love, in the opening and expansion that comes with this gift, the reality that is yours for the asking. As you receive, so you benefit greatly, infinitely, without true measure. For how can you measure the infinite, my beloveds?

We continue step by step to shed that which restricts you and to open that which expands you. We continue, beloveds. I am Orion and I love you so, as you join me and others on this magnificent journey to at-onement with God.
