# Live Each Day as God's Instrument

March 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Matthew received by Al Fike

I bring light and blessings to you, my beloved, beautiful brothers and sisters. I am Matthew. I urge you to seize the moment of each day, to be in the light, for God gives you the gift of a day, the gift of life. What you do with that gift, how you respond to that day, determines much of what you might do in your life. For life is lived day by day. The opportunity to be a light in the world is given day by day. There is no grand plan or scheme that you may aspire to that is greater than the opportunity of the moment that is now. It is for you to choose what you may do. You come together in this Circle of Light. You pray together. You pray and ask God to bless you and bless this world and God responds in accordance to the depth and intensity of your prayers. You are exercising your free will and praying for a blessing, a gift given by your Creator, your Heavenly Father. You seize the moment in whatever way, whatever means, is available to you.

My beloved friends, there is so much you might do in one day. There is such power in what you can accomplish in a spiritual way with prayer and with effort and guidance, with all that you are as a soul, a fledgling soul on the journey toward at-onement with God. Not only can you seek the nourishment of God's Holy Spirit, bringing to you His Love into your soul, but you also are full of gifts, full of capacities to bring the blessings of God's great Spirit to many in the world. If you are able to do this, then there is no need to be confused or to wonder about your day. Rather, as you pray yourself through your day, God will guide you. God will utilise your beautiful light, your beautiful soul, to bring blessings to others. That way you become an angel on Earth, an instrument of God's Will upon this Earth.

Do you see how God uses you? Are you aware of the potential that you carry, each of you, within your souls, within your beings and within your life? With that awareness, you become an active agent for God. As you grow in the Father's Love, that Light grows around you and in you. The capacities and the power of that agency is expressed in many different ways, through many different means, through many different prayers and through many different actions. You may serve God and God will utilise every opportunity available to bring greater light and blessings to the world. You are no small creature upon a vast planet filled with many souls. Rather, you are a node of light, a beautiful blessing for the world and that carries a certain power and ability to bring blessings for others.

Pray to be utilised as God's channels, my beloved souls. Pray to be utilised as a light in the world. Pray for your families and your loved ones. Pray for the world, for the world is in great need of your prayers and your actions, reflecting prayer and reflecting God's Will. There is much work to do, my beloved friends, so much work to do. The world is in such great need. As you live your day moment by moment, remember that within you are many gifts. Within you is a great light and all of these things are being ignited by the blessings of God's Love. As you receive more of this blessing, the power of your gifts, the power of your light, is increased and new gifts are awakening with this beautiful blessing.

My beloveds, my friends, beautiful lights all, think of the potential that lies within you. Think of the possibilities that are all around you. Think of how you may be utilised as a blessing for another. It may be simple in its expression, but the power of God's Touch through you will bring a change, a shift, something that is not lost, but something that that individual can build upon and increase in its power for light and love, as you are His instruments to help ignite this journey in another soul. My beautiful friends, be that light. Walk that road of service upon the Divine Path and you will be greatly rewarded. There will be joy in your heart and an understanding of the true purpose and meaning of life and your true purpose in life. What more could an individual want than this awareness and this expression that will fulfil the soul in so many ways and bring joy to your heart and bring meaning to your life?

Walk the world as God's channels. Be that light, beautiful, deep and profound, bright and powerful. God will work through you, my beloveds. God will work through you in many ways. Seize the day, my beloveds. Walk with faith in what God can do through you in a single day and all the days of your life. May this be true. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Matthew and I love you. God bless you.
