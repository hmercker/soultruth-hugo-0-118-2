# Circles of Light: How They Unite Us in Divine Love Beyond Physical Presence

April 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours, my beloved friends. I am Keea atta Kem. I am always glad to see earnest souls coming together in these Circles of Light. This instrument mentioned the Lattice of Light. Indeed, you are all connected. Yet, when you envision a lattice, you think of a grid. In truth, this lattice is like a circle. It connects one to the other to the other and interconnects back and forth, across, around, all through these interconnected souls who are with God in this way. For you are a special category of souls. You have received this gift of Divine Love and in this you have gifts and abilities that are different from many others.

Yes, you are a family, a group of individuals who share an experience, a blessing, a light that is unique to your group, your interconnected family, one that is not exclusive, nor do you see yourselves as separate from others. You are all most certainly willing to embrace and include others. Yet, it remains a truth that you are connected in a special way. When there is talk of these retreats, these gatherings of light, you are most certainly welcome to join, even though you may not be there in the flesh. You are welcome to join in the spirit and indeed, you will benefit greatly from it. If you set aside your time in prayer as the others in the material world set their prayers to a certain schedule, then you are a part of it. These connections ensure that you are a part and that you are being blessed as well and you are contributing as well.

There are many dimensions and aspects to these gatherings. There are few elements that you truly understand or recognise while you are together, but they are operating in many different ways, many different levels, soul to soul, my beloved friends, soul to soul. This does not require physical presence, but indeed, when you are together in Circles of Light, then we are able to do development work with each one of you so that your gifts may open and be strengthened as you come together in this way. Yet, indeed, each of you have been blessed deeply and many of you have been together in these Circles of Light, so you have benefited from them.

I urge you all to come together in unity and harmony, in desire to connect and to be this beautiful family of light. You will find that blessings will come to you as you sit in prayer and contemplation, as you ask to be drawn in, as you are open as much as you can, to receive the blessings from God. They will be given, my beloved friends, in abundance, in intensity and strength that will be recognised. You will feel the changes being brought within you and opening within you, so that you are truly sensing that your soul is enlivened and is changing as a result of these gifts, as a result of God's Love pouring in as you ask for this gift.

Be open, my beloved friends, open to all that is offered. The many ways those souls that have made the effort to come together have been guided and take their time honouring the Truth of God's Love in such a beautiful Circle of Light. They are doing so not only for their benefit, but for yours as well and for the Earth, for all humanity. They come together and seek to serve and seek to receive. God is wondrous in the ways in which He blesses us all, draws the angels close to you, draws the Holy Spirit into your midst, bringing the gifts of Love, healing, Light and peace to you all. You will know this, feel it and be a part of it. How blessed are you, beloved and beautiful angels in the making. Indeed, you are a special group, a beautiful light. You continue to benefit from all that God brings to you, surrounds you with and blesses you with. So much is given, so much light around you all.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Keea atta Kem. I am the one who orchestrates and sees over all the Circles of Light, whether they are in this fashion or in physical circles. I am with you and I ensure that the angels that are needed are present. I also work to inspire you with the desire to come together and to make that effort. Yes, I have much work in this regard, but I am very happy in my efforts, in what is accomplished and what shall be accomplished in the future. God bless you, my beloved friends. I love you so. Your friend Keea is with you. God bless you all. God bless you in His Love.
