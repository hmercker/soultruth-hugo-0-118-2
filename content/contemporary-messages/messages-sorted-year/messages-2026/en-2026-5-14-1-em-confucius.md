# You Have Been Given Gifts for Healing

May 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Confucius received by Elizabeth Mack

Good afternoon, my dear children, my little birds. I am so grateful to come to speak before you. I am indeed your brother, Confucius. As I have not spoken through this beautiful soul before, she is struggling, but now I am here.

I too wish to welcome you to this retreat and tell you about the healing opportunities that you are bringing to mankind by being here. Each of you has a gift, a gift for healing. Each of you express it in a different way, but you have it. You are able, some by touch, some by thought, all by love. It is the Love in your soul which will grow and develop this gift of healing that can be applied not only to your brothers and sisters upon the Earth, not only upon your brothers and sisters in the spirit world, but upon this Earth herself, which in many cases needs healing.

Some of you already know what I am talking about in terms of healing of the Earth. Some of you have a twinkling of understanding, but as you grow in this Love, as you allow more to come in, as you allow it to go deeply in your souls and percolate, for lack of a better word, you will find that you begin to do things with your hands, do things with your eyes to see what can be done to heal this Earth with water, with the air. There are many, many, many elements that can be used to heal the Earth. Each of you will come to know what it is that you are gifted with. You will be applying that gift and, in some cases, not even be aware of it, but you will be aware of the results.

This is a special group of souls whose work includes healing of the Earth. It may be a small piece of the puzzle. It may be a larger operation that you are part of. But each of you, each of you will help to heal Mother Earth and bring her back to a place where she too can thrive.

You were given a peek this morning at some of the work you will do and where you will be working. Some of you saw it as soil, some of you saw it as clearing the brush, some of you felt it in your hearts. Yes, we work together. We are a team, as the Master said this morning. We are all working, all of us. We, the angels, our stellar friends, our brothers and sisters on the Divine Love path in the spirit world and those human beings who knowingly are working to heal this environment. God blesses them for doing so and God is blessing you for your work, so again these gifts will be brought forward. You will be cleansed, you will be healed and you will heal.

Open your souls and receive that which will cleanse you, that which will activate the Love, activate the gifts to help you grow in this gift of healing, which you will find very soon to be very important, very strongly needed where you live and where others live. Stay open, stay awake, stay aware, for some clues will be subtle. You will find inspiration in those clues to ignite a greater passion for healing, to find answers to problems that currently exist in this world.

What a wonderful group of healers I see before me. Wonderful, wonderful. I am pleased that you hear with your soul. May God's Love pour upon you now in great abundance, in a great shower of His Love into your souls. Let the rains come, the rains of His Love fall upon you. I am your brother, Confucius, and I bless each one of you with a kiss upon your forehead, that you may see more clearly.
