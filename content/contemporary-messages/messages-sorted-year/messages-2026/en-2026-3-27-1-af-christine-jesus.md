# Becoming Instruments of Light in a Changing World

March 27<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Christine & Jesus received by Al Fike

**Christine:** God bless you, beloved souls. I am Christine. Some of you know me, some of you do not, but know that even I, who come from far away, am part of your efforts to bring light, harmony and joy to your world. God has enlisted many. Indeed, that retinue of light souls, beautiful spirits, are from many different parts of the universe and many different parts of your universe and your world. It is a great variety, a great collection of beautiful and gifted souls.

We are happy to participate. We are happy to be ones who have been chosen to be a part of this great effort to help shift and awaken humanity toward higher thoughts and conditions and love, that their souls may be awakened with that universal Love that is God's Essence, that their minds may be opened to the possibilities of what can be in your world, in your reality, that their spirits may be full of joy and light, that all of these parts of you may be in harmony and synchronised together to bring an awakening, a level of consciousness that is far greater than what is in your world today. This is our goal, to help humanity evolve into something beautiful and of light, so that humanity may truly understand their own potentials, but also the potentials of the universe, that they may come to realise and use for their benefit.

First, all must be at a higher level of consciousness, with greater love, greater insight, greater appreciation of what life is. When that can come to each individual on your Earth and in your lower planes of spirit, then truly change will come. Truly harmony will be yours. Truly, deep insights and understanding of truth will come to you and you will be able to move forward, but first the foundation of love must be set. The understanding of the power of love must come to each one. As they connect to the true Source of Love, which is the Creator, then light will shine, truth will come and the actions of humanity will be in harmony with the Laws of Love and Creation.

It is a formidable task, my friends, to be in this world so full of pain and distortion and to try and make it right, to be God's instruments of light, so that you and all around you may find joy in life. The goal is simple, but the task at hand is complex and challenging. As the spirits, stellar friends and angels gather about you and gather around this world, this formidable force for change will turn the tide and you will find great changes coming, great awakenings. The masses of humanity will finally listen and come to realise who they truly are and what they are truly capable of.

First, must come humility and faith, a desire to be within the deep recesses of the soul and in that, to be with God. If each individual can truly come to that place of understanding their true nature, the core of their being, then the transformation begins and the light pours in where there was once darkness and deprivation. A healing of your world will come and the true purpose and goal of humanity's existence in this world will be realised and, in this, there will be harmony and peace and joy. The power of love will do this, my friends. It has done this throughout the universe and will do it in many ways, through many channels, to help heal and diminish the darkness.

Oh, my beloved friends, yes, the need is great. The plan is formidable and complex, but as you find your way to God, as you become knowledgeable and truly understand your relationship with God, then you become formidable, beautiful and filled with light. In this, the truth of your existence and purpose will be revealed and your souls awakened and then transformed in love. This process is universal. Many souls have undergone this process and are undergoing it at this moment, as are you, this transformative light and love that brings a true healing to the individual.

My beloved friends, you are truly blessed. You know the power of love. You have felt it and you are making every effort to express it. I say to you, my friends, know that you have beautiful friends and spirits who surround you and who help you, uplift you and inspire you, who protect you, who are there when you pray and who are there with every breath. This is God's way of upholding His children, humanity, the humanity that exists throughout all the universe, and so it is and so it shall be, my friends. Do not fret and worry. I know there are many terrible things happening in the world, but I urge you to stay focused on God, to be higher in truth, light and love, so that you may be the antidote to all this darkness and suffering. It is important that you remain steadfast in the truth. Be free of that worry and concern. That concern that you have, direct it to God, direct it to light and be a light. You will find yourself being used by God as a beautiful channel of love and light in the world.

May you be blessed, my beloved friends, blessed deeply, profoundly in love, the Love of God. I am close. I come close to be with you and to help you. I am Christine. My love for you is great, beautiful souls struggling and urging the truth forward, day by day, step by step. God bless you, my friends. Christine loves you. God bless you.

**Jesus:** We come to bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus. I come in prayer and love to be with you today, to love you, to uphold you, to tell you that you are not alone and many are close to you and many are here for your benefit, are praying for your blessings. It is so, that God uplifts His children, brings us all into the light, shows us the way to nourish our souls and to grow our light.

We are today in this place of prayer, to be with God, to honour God and honour our own souls, God's most glorious creation. Indeed, we are His children and shall walk the Path Divine together, always and evermore. God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to bless you all and to pray with you. God bless you. God bless you.
