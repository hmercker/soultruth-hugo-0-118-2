# Wondrous Things Will Come About Once You Are Completely Attuned to the Will of God

August 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. You often face the dilemma of acting upon your guidance, of acting upon God's Will. The most comfortable position for you is to sit and pray and be in the grace of God's Touch, to be fulfilled in this way. For there is nothing you need to do other than pray and to be receptive, to long for this Touch, the blessing of the Divine Love to enter your soul. Yet, God asks of you more than this.

God gives you much, but also there is an expectation, a desire from God for you to act as His instruments, to be active in the world as a light, to be a demonstration of the Truth of God's Love, its existence within you and how that truly affects all that you are, so there is a tension between repose and action. Often, with this tension, you are stretched to a place that may feel uncomfortable, or in this new way of being, a way so contrary to the material world, you are challenged to act in accordance, in alignment with human expectation and also in alignment with Divine expectation. This may cause all manner of doubt and confusion as to how you may be in the world.

Yet, when you are aligned with your soul and your soul's wisdom and consciousness, these actions may appear simple and doable in your life. It is that ever-going conflict between your material mind and the mind of your soul that causes the discomfort, the tension. As you continue to grow in your soul, as your soul awakens and becomes transformed, the dilemmas will lessen and the understanding will grow as to how to achieve this balancing act between the conditions of the material world and the expressions of your soul as God's instruments in the world.

As you continue to walk in the world, it is important that you relate to people and people relate to you. If you stand out as someone very different or unusual, often people will avoid you or not pay any attention to you. This is the first part of the dilemma, how to be in the world but not of the world. Yet, as you grow in the Father's Love, as you continue to awaken within your soul, the words and expressions come quite readily. If they do not, surely you are aware of your angel friends and stellar friends who help you to express yourself in a way that is in harmony and is in sync with God's Will. God provides all that you require to be that instrument, but God cannot provide for you the impetus to be that instrument. This is what you must provide for yourself, a willingness to step forward, a willingness to engage in the world in the way that I describe as God's instruments.

This is your responsibility and opportunity. Indeed, as you step forward tentatively in the world in this way, you come to practise and become familiar with what is required and what is guided. As you do so in greater measure, as you practise and become comfortable with this aspect of your life, then the words flow, the actions flow. All flows in harmony with God's Will, God's plan. This vignette of action, that is all part of God's plan, is guided and protected as you enact it, as you are willing to enact it.

My friends, there is much work to do. There are many actions to take. The world does not change overnight, nor does it change if there is no impetus for change. You, along with your cohorts, are providing that impetus if you are in alignment with God's Will. I know it is difficult for your mind to comprehend, to plan, to even understand what it is that God wishes of you. This is where the perceptions of the soul and the wisdom of the soul bring to you the information that you require. Yet, you must have a friendly relationship between the mind of your soul and the mind of your mind. For without this, without the free flow, the trust and the conduit by which communication might happen, there will always be confusion.

There may indeed be a feeling, an impetus within you to act and yet not knowing how to act or what to do. This is where forging this link between the two centres of consciousness are important and necessary. This is where we come into play and many others. We help you to find your way to connect with clarity and with power, to communicate these centres so that they are clearly understood. At some time in the future, this duality will not exist and we have spoken of this.

For now, this is the reality that we all must live with and work with and we do so in loving appreciation that you are willing to step forward in this way. Indeed, as you continue to forge your relationship with the Creator of all, the guidance will come swiftly and powerfully to you. In fact, each day may be a deep blessing of service if you are in alignment and attunement with the Will of God and allow the Will of God to subjugate your own, or at least to be in harmony with your own, so that you may act with confidence and with finesse. There are many working parts to this. Indeed, you must find your way through the challenges of aligning all of these parts, all of these elements of your being into one solid desire to serve God and in so doing, serve humanity. Do not be reticent, my beloved friends, or fearful, for serving God is the most wondrous and fulfilling effort you may do in your life and it indeed becomes your life and it indeed becomes your life.

Service in this way is what we all desire of you. Indeed, in time, as that service grows and is more fluid, more powerful, more filled with light and love, then the prospect of manifestation becomes accessible and readily available to you and to us. First steps first, however. The effort to move forward, the effort to pray, to listen and the effort to enact the small requests that God has of you. As you grow, these things grow as well.

More is asked of you as you continue to find your familiarity with what is God's Will. The process is one of development. It is one of learning, growing, acting, refining and being in the flow of God's Will, always with every breath. This is what a true instrument of God looks like. It is not a casual affair. It is not a part-time thing.

It is your life and in it being your life, then wondrous things may manifest, may come about because you are attuned always to the Will of God. Consider this, my beautiful friends. What would it be like to be attuned to the Will of God with every breath and what could be accomplished with that attunement? Much indeed. This is why we ask of you to make these efforts, to find your alignment, to be in alignment within your soul, to be in that light, that grace of God that moves you, guides you and protects you at all times. In this way, what you desire to accomplish and what we, God and your angel friends desire to accomplish, will be great indeed and you will find deep joy, fulfilment and true purpose in this.

May you find your way, my friends. For you need to step forward, to grow, to develop, to awaken and be transformed. You do this not only for yourself, but for your loved ones, your children and their children to come and for all of humanity. This is the deepest service that you can partake in and it is magnificent. The road ahead is magnificent and you, beloved souls, the light that you carry, the truth that you live by, is magnificent. May you come to understand this, my friends, and know how truly wonderful and magnificent that you are.

Potentials that lie within are tremendous. Come and discover these things, my friends. Allow God to work through you, His beloved channels, to bring peace, harmony and light in the world, as well as truth and love. God bless you, my friends.

I am Orion. I continue to dedicate myself to your journey of discovering who you are and expressing who you are, in service, in love and light. God bless you, my friends. Orion loves you deeply. God bless you.
