# Orion Describes the Process of Receiving Divine Love on His Planet and Expresses Admiration for Our Tenacity

May 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

May the great gift of the Father's Love continue to enter your souls, my beloved friends. I am Orion. I have said in the past that on my planet, there is no need to set aside formal prayer to receive this gift of Divine Love, that indeed we do not pray for this gift, but we receive it nonetheless. There are those who have read and acknowledged this message that say this cannot be true because you have been taught a way in which to receive this great gift, this Essence of God.

What they fail to understand is that the conditions of my planet are such that we are born into this condition of Love. It saturates our atmosphere. It is truly a part of us, a part of our reality and part of our consciousness. In such conditions, there is no need to make ourselves known to God or to bring our consciousness toward God in the way of prayer and supplication, as you do. It is there. It exists. It is truly a part of our world. Thus, we are receiving this gift of Divine Love, this holy Essence, constantly and reflexively, for our souls are receptive, open and desiring this blessing. We are great beneficiaries of this constant response from God.

On your world, there are great barriers to receiving this gift. The conditions are opposite to ours. Rather than receptivity, openness and the saturation of love in your environment, there is a lack of receptivity, a lack of openness and certainly a lack of love. Therefore, you must make great effort to receive this gift. You must clear your mind. You must be in touch with your soul and activate the laws that are universal and that bring the gift of this Love to you. Indeed, you must work hard to be in alignment with this gift and to live a life that reflects this gift and this reality.

We have deep compassion for you, my beloved friends, for everything on your planet requires great effort. Nothing is easy or easily obtained. Much that you desire requires your focus, your energy and your efforts to obtain it. Even this gift of Divine Love requires your focus and efforts to receive it. Indeed, this is why we make great effort with you to help you overcome these barriers, to work toward creating an environment where love will thrive, not only the natural love, as you call it, but the Divine Love. This is part of God's plan. It is part of a plan in the universe that this great Essence of God's Soul may be available to all freely and given to all abundantly.

You toil away, my friends, seeking and contemplating, assessing and calibrating, all the things that you require, all the efforts that you must put into this. Indeed, I admire your efforts. I admire your tenacity. I admire your struggles, for even though it is not easy to receive this great gift, you persist nonetheless. You continue to walk this road with dedication and with faith. It is faith that is your strength. It is faith that gives you that strength and that tenacity. It is faith that opens the door to this incredible gift given by God. As you grow in faith, as you truly trust in God, as you truly trust in the journey of your souls, so the gift is more readily available and flows more easily into you. That corridor, that channel of love, is nurtured by your faith and your efforts in prayer.

You continue to make this a priority in your life. Beloved souls, this draws to you much support, many who come to uphold you, protect you, bring you into the light and help you to be in alignment with this truth. Indeed, you are in alignment. Barriers still exist. Conditions within you and around you still inhibit, to a degree, the flow of this great gift of Love. Yet, you persist, as do we, my beloved friends. For this is the key to the next stage of evolution of your species, of the human race on your planet. We have seen this over and over again, how when the human race comes to understand the power of this gift, to utilise it and to apply the laws pertaining to it, then everything changes, not only the individual but the environment around the individual and, with many individuals, in time, the environment around the entire planet. This is another reason why we persist with you, for we see the potential. We see the possibilities that might arise if there are enough souls willing and consistent in their desires and efforts to receive this gift. The world will truly change and reflect the blessing of this gift.

Our planet is the ultimate example of this. It is completely transformed, completely in alignment with what is truth, what is love and what is God's intention for all of humanity, whether it be in this world or my world. We struggle and continue to make our footsteps forward in the great venture of transforming your planet. It is not we who have wilfully done so. It is we who have listened to God's Will and plan and comply with it with humility, grace, enthusiasm and desire. You too join us in this, this great venture to reclaim your world in light, to bring harmony and peace to your world and, most of all, to understand the power of love to transform and change it. I urge you forward, my friends. It is not an easy task. In fact, it may be the most difficult task, but it is one that brings the greatest rewards, the greatest blessings, the greatest outcomes and the highest of outcomes. As we all continue to listen to the Will of God and bring it into our hearts, minds and souls, then we do our utmost to fulfil this mandate, to bring forward these efforts and gifts directed within this great Will of the Divine.

We are with you, my beloved friends, on this great journey. Indeed you are links in a chain. There will be others who will link with others and, in time, transformation will take place and peace and harmony will come to your world. This is God's intention. Indeed, when God has a plan, when God intends for something to be, it is inevitable that it will be. The journey of what will take place may be circuitous and may be difficult, but the outcomes will always be more harmonious and more of light. This is what we strive for, you and I, in this world so in need of light, harmony and peace, that with love, the world may be transformed, changed and become truly what is meant to be. We continue on this journey. We are God's instruments, channels of light and love. All of us are linked in this great effort.

May you be blessed, my beloved friends, upon this journey. May the Love of God truly be the focus, that it may be evident beyond any doubt, its power, beauty and ability to heal and transform. It is always there for you, my beloved friends, as are we. Take advantage. Seek, desire, pray for and open the door for the inflowing, the blessing of this gift of Love. As you have been told, all else follows in its stead. Blessings to you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. I come to you in love, deep compassion and appreciation. I see your struggles. I wish we could erase them all, that the spiritual progression of your planet may be as easy as ours. In time, it will be. In time and persistence, the possibilities will be realised. God bless you, my friends. Orion loves you. I love you dearly.
