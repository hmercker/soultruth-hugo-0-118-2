# You Are Each Crucial to the Transformation Process

August 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am your friend, brother and cohort in this beautiful journey. I am Orion. We continue to make efforts in your individual development and your development as a circle, efforts made to help transform this world of yours, this planet, from darkness to light, to disseminate and eliminate the veil of darkness and the illusions of man, and bring the reality of God forward for each and every soul upon this world of yours. Each of you who has elected to do this work are integral to the work. Each of you is required in your efforts to be a light, to release the darkness within you and to allow all that is of the purity and goodness of God to be yours.

My beloved friends, we continue on this journey. We continue to seek out that which is truth, to express that which is truth in our lives, to be in that place of harmony and peace within, to be that channel of light and love in the world. All that you do and all that you are, may this be a part of God's plan to indeed refurbish this Earth with that which is in harmony with creation and that which is within harmony of God's Laws of Love. For this is the way in which the darkness will be illuminated. This is the way in which humanity will be transformed. It is through love. It is recognising, acknowledging and expressing the true power that is love and all that you are that expresses that love. You become a generator of light, that which dissolves the darkness, my friends.

Indeed, we are with you in this, always with you to help uphold, to help inspire and to help you upon that journey, so that, indeed, as each day goes by, you are a little closer to that purity, to your relationship with the Creator of all and that harmony that comes with love. For as you continue to walk this path and strive toward something higher, more beautiful, with purity and with power, that you walk in this grace, the grace of love, then you are fulfilling your work, your mandate to be those instruments of light in the world. We all work toward these ends, we who come to visit, to help and uphold, and you who are representatives of this planet, this place, important and crucial to the transformation process that is established and continuing to be established in your world. As the process intensifies, so will be the challenges that you face to release all conditions that are not in harmony. As you do so, so you become a more powerful instrument, a beautiful channel, a beautiful example in the world, helping to change what has been desecrated and out of harmony for so long, changing the course of humanity, its intended course recalibrated and directed toward light and harmony.

Each of you is a part of this correction. You are living this correction and you are indeed teachers and examples of this correction. As you receive this great gift of the Father's Love, this Divine Love, so the work that you acknowledge and embrace will intensify and change will come. It will open the doors for greater work, greater connection with humanity, greater possibilities of what might come as your instrumentality flourishes, as your being comes into greater alignment with the Divine. My beloved friends, oh, this world is in need of you. This world is desperately in need of you. Continue in your efforts. Continue to seek out the Creator. Continue to be transformed by your relationship with God as you receive more of this Essence, the key that will bring the change, the key that will open and transform the soul.

My beloved friends, we walk together in this light. You have all the support required. You are blessed and doubly blessed with God's Touch upon you. All the beings, your stellar friends, your Celestial friends and bright spirits, gather around you, for you represent the hope for the world, the change that is coming and God's plan for the salvation of humanity. You are a part of this, integral. Indeed, as you continue on this path of service, you will be surprised at what will come, the doors that will open and the possibilities that will manifest.

May you be blessed, my beautiful friends on this journey so wonderful, so important, so powerful. I thank you for your efforts. I am with you always in those efforts and those I represent. We are all with you, my friends. Blessings to you. His Love to you, beloved friends.
