# Truth Is Required

July 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mark received by Elizabeth Mack

Truth is yours, dear children. Truth comes from many sources. It comes as a result of your requests, your desires. It comes to you in many forms, but truth, real truth, God's Truth comes directly from God into your precious souls as you open to receive God's blessings.

I am Mark of the Bible and I come to you as a result of your desires for healing of this planet, for healing of the so-called future of this planet and for healing of the people who live upon this planet. Truth is required and lived in order for this request for people to change. As souls are suffering in many ways, in so many ways, upon this Earth, they are beginning to turn. Ever so slowly, they turn to their beliefs about God. Some blame God and some question God when, in truth, they only have to ask for God's help and God gives it. As you have done so, the Father is already responding to your prayers, touching the souls of many, many souls who are open to receive this desire, to move forward, for it is God's Will for His children to move forward in their progression, all of His children.

You are so loved. Each of His children is loved greatly and all each needs to do is open to receive and accept the blessings that God is more than willing to pour into their souls. Take this message to your brothers and sisters to accept God's offer of Love, progression and harmony. Take this message and share it widely. Pray for those souls that they receive what you have to tell them and what you have to share with them, which is your love. This is a great work. This is the work you are meant to do upon this planet. This is the work for which you will be richly rewarded and this is the work which will satisfy your very souls, minds and bodies. Go out among your brothers and sisters and love them and let them know from where this Love comes and that it is available to everyone for the asking. My prayers are with you, dear children, my prayers and the prayers of thousands upon thousands of Celestial Angels and other redeemed souls still in the flesh. You have all of us behind you and in front of you. May God open your souls widely and deeply and receive all that your souls can accept. May God bless you. I am Mark of the Bible and it has been my privilege, my honour to pray with you today. God bless you.
