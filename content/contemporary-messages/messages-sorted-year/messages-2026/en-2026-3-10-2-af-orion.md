# The Road Less Travelled

March 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

My friends, my name is Orion. It most certainly is the road less taken. Yet, in the universe, it is a road that is commonly taken. When the individual understands and discovers their soul, there is a deep desire for at-onement with God, the great Oversoul. Those who take the intellectual road miss out on this experience and this relationship. They are often bogged down by their reasoning, by their material minds and their intellects. They find it very difficult indeed to go beyond that. On your world, this is the most common path and many, even in the spiritual realms, take a road toward the Sixth Sphere, the perfect man, and stop at this juncture.

Yet, you are taking the road less travelled, my friends. You are looking for something deeper, wider and more profound. Even though you have your own minds distracting you and begging for your attention, you still persist. You still want to know and want to be a part of this beautiful journey of soul awakening and transformation. Thus, you attract this very high calibre of spirits, star friends and Celestial Angels. It all comes with with the Law of Attraction. You elicit this response and benefit from your prayers and desires and thoughts toward the highest. Indeed, you have travelled this road a certain distance, but, oh, that road is eternal and you are merely a few footfalls from the beginning. Thus, you require our assistance and you require God's guidance.

I urge you further in being aware and open, receptive to these things. Because without them and without the protection that we offer you, your life would be much more difficult and complex and without the ease of connection between your soul and God. For in order to do this, you must travel through many layers of conditions, thought conditions that inhabit your world. Instead, we help you to make that clear passageway to be in true connection, rapport and communion with God. This is all that is truly required, my friends. The rest, including your relationship with us, your angel friends and all the others, comes on the heels of this powerful connection with God. My friends, we are here and the more powerful your connection with the Creator, the more powerful our rapport with you.

This law is simple and you will understand it. I merely reiterate it to refresh your memory, to help you to see that the path is not altogether complicated or difficult, but it does require your dedications, your sincere prayers and your desire to be in alignment with God. As this desire is fulfilled, so God utilises you as a light, as a gifted soul unique in the universe, so that many things may be accomplished, much given through you to others and much given to you. Oh, it is a wonderful journey. This road less travelled is magnificent. Would you turn back, my friends? Would you take a different road? I doubt it. I think you understand, as I do, how blessed this road is, how magnificent, one that I encourage you upon, one that God beckons you toward, one that is full and resplendent in light, insights, blessings, revelations, true and abiding Love that is not human but Divine.

Who would not want such a thing? If others understood, truly understood, what it is you are seeking and what it is we are encouraging, they would follow this road too. For the soul yearns for this. Even when the mind is so engaged, the soul yearns for this. It is for you to help encourage the individual to bypass the biases and prejudices of the mind and go to that yearning of soul. Each of you, in your own way, have gifts that will help others. Each of you, as you continue to grow and flourish upon this path, will be beautiful examples of what this path has to offer. Each of you continues to be on the threshold of deeper insights, wonderful experiences, soulful awakenings and transformations. It continues to come to you.

The question is, are you paying attention? Are you allowing this awareness and this level of consciousness to be recognised and lived within? When you are able to do this at least more fully, then the way in which you will serve God and serve humanity will be much clearer, easier and more direct. It comes with your efforts to open your soul, to acknowledge your soul, to communicate with your soul and to communicate with God's great Soul. It is so simple and yet on this earthly plane, you find it very difficult indeed, but we are here. Many are here to help you on the journey. You come closer. You inch closer, day by day, to profound revelations and awakenings. It is that close, my friends. It is that close. Be inspired, uplifted, encouraged, assured that this is your destiny and you step upon the threshold of such. It is yours for the asking and so it is yours to decide.

I am your friend, Orion. I love you so, my beautiful earthly friends. I am with you, close, eager, helping, inspiring, uplifting and loving. I am here. Orion loves you. God bless you all.
