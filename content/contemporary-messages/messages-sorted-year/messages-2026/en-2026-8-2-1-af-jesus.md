# Beacons of Light in God's Great Plan

August 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

In the name of love, I urge you to love, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come once again to be with you in the grace of God's Love and Touch upon you all on this Earth plane. There are pinpoints of light that are growing and brightening because you have come together in prayer. You have come together to be with God. Your souls bring this light forward, your beautiful beings reaching out to God, your souls receiving this great gift. At this time, heaven and Earth are connected, are together in the Truth of God's Love. For it is in His Love that the healing takes place, that peace comes, that light spreads and that great Lattice of Light is fed by your prayers, desires and blessings.

My beautiful brothers and sisters, each of you is needed in the world. Each of you carries that spark, that beautiful acknowledgement of Truth, that is the Father's Love glowing within. As you strengthen, you will be a beacon of light, a beautiful channel of love in the world. It is inevitable that your instrumentality, your gifts and your being will shine forth in the beauty of the Father's Love conveyed through you, the beauty of truth that you are a living example of and all the goodness that you exude and express as your souls are awakened and transformed by the Father's Love. As this light that is you brightens, as this pinpoint in the world has its effects where you live, then the angels are able to work. The circumference of their ability to touch other souls and to have influence in the world widens because of your dedication and your beautiful soul awakening in the Father's Love. It becomes the conduit from which God may work, which the angels may indeed minister to many.

We see you as very precious indeed, very important in the work that we do, for this one plane informs so many others as souls are incarnated and then move on. If they can know, feel, experience and be blessed by this great Truth of the Father's Love, then their journey may be swift through these spheres. Indeed, as they become lights, they affect others who affect others and the entire world will be infected with love and light. There will be no need for wars, suffering and lack of love. Rather, it will be replaced with harmony, peace and light. This is our goal, for this is God's Will and desire. God has the plan to establish peace on Earth, to establish light, to eradicate the darkness and the suffering in the world. This is God's plan and each of us, no matter how humble our position in the world, we are engaged in this great plan, this great work that continues to manifest in the world.

I urge you to have faith, my beloveds, faith in what can be, faith in what can manifest through each one of you, faith in the power of God's Love to transform, heal and uplift, that you may indeed be that light in the world, that you may indeed walk as God's instrument, God's channel of Love. It is not out of your reach. It is not impossible to be within that Truth of Love, for you are already in it. You are already part of God's great plan. As you grow and awaken, the understanding of this, the depth and breadth of this, will come to you. You will be inspired, uplifted and carried. You will have great opportunities to spread light and truth in the world. It may not be in words, but it will most certainly be in action, as light is around you and influences all around you. The more that are touched by this Love, the greater will be the influence and the power of God's handiwork through you to eradicate the darkness.

Know this, my beloveds. Know this, in the very depths of your being, that you are God's instruments, channels of love and that light has an effect in the world. Each of you, as you grow, will continue to do your part and your work in this great plan. You will be guided, inspired, protected and blessed deeply. All that you see in the world now, your perspective will change and be transformed by your soul's awakening by God's Love. It is what is intended, my beloveds. I walk with you upon that path of service, the path of light, path of healing and peace. I am with you as Love grows within us all, for that Love is eternal. The blessings of that Love are eternal. We walk the eternal path, the Path of Truth, of Love and of Light. We shall be those instruments. All of us, we in the Celestial Kingdom, you upon the Earth and many more, work and collaborate together to awaken humanity to their true selves and their true potential.

May we all be blessed in this great work. May God's blessings be with us all. Open your souls wide, my beloveds. Know that God is eager to bless you. May you be eager to receive His blessing of Love, so that we may be transformed in that Love. God bless you, beloveds. I am your brother and your friend. I am Jesus and I love you. God bless you all. In His blessings, in His grace, a blessing and Love.
