# Although Not All May Listen, Be a Light as You Travel These Lands

September 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours in great abundance. I am your brother and friend Jesus. I come to bless you this day. I have been with you each and every day as you continue to foster the light, the blessings of God through you, wherever you are and whatever you are doing and so you are my disciples. Even though you do not proselytize and bring this message clearly to your brothers and sisters, because it is evident that they cannot hear you, nor are they interested in your message, or at least many are not, but the light, my beloveds, the light shines, comforts and heals as you travel in your way through these lands.

Beloveds, continue in your ministrations and consider that what you do is important. How you are as you travel and connect with many, as you ride the various modes of transportation, as you walk the streets, as you enter various places, you are God's emissaries of light and love. Be that to the most that you can be, my beloveds. Be that channel of love without limitations and expectations, merely to be in the flow of God's grace and God's Will and you will accomplish much. Even though it may not seem obvious to you, it is obvious to us that God is using you in many ways to help many souls. This is our ministry, is it not?

Why do I walk with you, my beloveds? Because you and I are continuing upon the same path for the same purpose and objective and so we are truly brothers and sisters. We truly walk in that place of humility, grace, peace and joy as the Father's Love continues to infill us and uphold us. You know that this path is infinite, eternal, so why would I not receive this gift as you do? Why would I not continue to progress as you do?

Indeed, our path is different and the place that I reside is far beyond this place, but we are still together, soul to soul, on this great journey and of discovery. This great journey of soul awakening and transformation and we shall be upon that journey together for all eternity, my beloveds. I am with you. I am truly your brother and we are truly upon a magnificent journey beyond the limitations that your minds may have. Far beyond that, my beloveds. Allow the perceptions and openness of your soul inform you as you travel.

Your senses and insights of the soul informing you, informing you of all things. The awakening of your perceptions and abilities comes as the Father's Love continues to inflow and awaken you. We continue to make this magnificent journey. You are accompanied by the angels, by me, by God's Hand upon you. You are protected, guided and we shall indeed do the work designated, the flow of purpose that God has for each one of us. May we all be humble upon that service, that way of serving God and serving humanity.

My beloveds, I speak to you as my friends, as my confidants and I am joyed to have such beautiful friends upon the Earth, who truly understand my mission and ascribe to my mission to bring the Truth of God's Love to humanity. We will, my beloveds, in all the ways and means that God will provide for us, we will fulfil this mission. We will bring this Truth to all those who are willing to listen. It is our destinies together, beloved souls, and I embrace others upon that destiny and I will introduce to you others who are destined to serve in this way.

Your group will expand and absorb all the different souls, all the unique individuals who will come into your orbit and your lives and you will be the better for it, my beloveds. The blessings of what others may bring into these Circles of Light, the blessings of their gifts, their insights, their experiences and their personalities, all of these things added to the great amalgam of souls that is being created by God and we do our utmost to make and facilitate these connections and opportunities for each one of you. Open your minds, open your eyes, your souls to what God guides you to do and the opportunities made available to you and you will find yourself amazed at the possibilities, at the opportunities, at the blessings that will flow.

My beloveds, we are humble yet powerful. We are God's instruments in the world. May this day be an example of such, a blessing that brings insight, allows you to see more clearly the power of your own instrumentality, the grace that is there, how these things affect the world, how the world responds.

May you be blessed, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend Jesus and I walk with you this day, as do many. God bless you in His Love, eternal Love, eternally blessed. God bless you.
