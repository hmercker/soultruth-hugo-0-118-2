# Awaken from the Sleep of Materialism

February 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Faith. It is important that the world wakes up. So many are asleep. So many are seeking a good life by encouraging material gain and physical pleasure. Yet, this cannot and never has brought the individual to happiness and a good life.

It is important that peoples around the world realise that the values that are being promoted and espoused these days are bringing the Earth to its knees. The Earth cannot possibly sustain what humanity is asking of it. You cannot sustain each other in this erroneous belief that more material goods, more technologies, more avenues toward pleasure, will bring happiness. It only encourages people to continue to be asleep, to be out of harmony and out of sync with their true selves and their true purpose in life. For when you are engaged in your mind only, then there is no understanding that goes deeper and the many parts of the individual that God has given them as a gift remain dormant and asleep.

The consciousness of many lack awareness, lack understanding of who and what they really are. It is comfortable to be asleep, unaware, unconnected with God. It is what is being asked of you now in your world to pursue the material in order to enrich those few that are offering these things. Yet, it proliferates darkness and disharmony. There is great sadness in the world, great difficulties, great disharmony. It is time for awakening to begin. God has a plan for this to help awaken humanity, even though many wish to slumber and be detached and unaware. God wants his children to awaken to their true selves, to the true reality of God's creation, to the reality of love and the power of love to bring harmony into the world.

You are on the forefront of this awakening trend, this flow that will force and cajole many to wake up. You are awakening, my beloved friends. You seek truth. You seek to be awakened within your soul. You seek out God. You seek out true meaning in life. You are on the road that God wishes for all. He is guiding you forward. Each day becomes an opportunity for further awakening. Each day prods you forward and challenges you to awaken further. Each day gives you the opportunity to see the errors and the misunderstandings that you carry within you. Each day is an opportunity for your soul to grow in God's Love and therefore wash away these conditions, this misunderstanding, this darkness.

You are so loved by God, so loved by many. It is important that you carry that love as foremost in your consciousness, that the power and strength of that love will guide you with each day that comes and you may be that instrument, that channel of love, light and truth, so that you may help to awaken others toward this great Truth that they are loved by God and that God's Love is the power to transform and to awaken and to heal. All of these good and wonderful blessings are given by a mere prayer and desire from the heart to seek out God.

My beloved friends, continue on your road to awakening. Continue to look and be brave, forthright upon that road and seek to release all things, all thoughts, all actions, all memories that are not in harmony with love, so that you may be pure of soul, beautiful, transformed and awakened by Love. In this way, you will be that instrument to help many awaken. You are needed desperately and the awakening of humanity is necessary at this time to avoid much pain and sorrow, heartache and much destruction in the world. The awakening in Love will cure all that which is out of harmony with humanity. Yet first, humanity must want this and desire it. If they can do so with the true longings of their heart, then they will find great rewards, great healing there and great joy.

So much is offered by God. I urge you to take all that God has to offer, all that is available to you and pray for more, ever more of this beautiful Touch from God. You will find such happiness and joy, such truth, such ability to understand yourself and the universe of God's creation, that you will wonder why you have not made greater effort to be in that place, a place so powerful and deep and wondrous. My beautiful friends, I urge you to continue on the journey of your own awakening, to continue to help others to awaken. It is needed now. God desires this of all his children. God has the plan to help all of you to awaken further. It is given out of love. It is given because of your needs and God's desires to bring harmony in the world. It will be one way or another. God will ensure that harmony and peace will be in your world and that all the disharmony, all the violence and disruption, will dissipate like clouds on the horizon. The warmth of God's Love will melt away these conditions. It is given to you and to all humanity.

May you come to know it, the blessings that are available to you and all the goodness that awaits you. Be true to your soul's longings, my beloveds. Be true to God's desire to awaken you, heal you and transform you in Love. It is here for you at this moment and in every moment. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Faith. I love you. I pray with you, along with many. God bless you all in his Love.
