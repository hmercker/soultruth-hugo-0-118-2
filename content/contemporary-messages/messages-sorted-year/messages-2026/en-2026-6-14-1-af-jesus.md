# With Your Efforts and Commitments, God Will Bring to You the Hungering Souls

June 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the peace that passes all understanding be yours, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come once again to be with you in prayer. I lead the way to the Hall of Peace, that you may appreciate and be blessed by this condition that is truly of peace. My beloveds, despite all the conditions of the world, you continue to break free from these things and seek out the peace of the soul that comes with our Father's Love. In this, you are mightily blessed. In this, you come to a place that is centred and strong within the Love of God. In this, you are my disciples, for you understand my true message and you live by it. As our brother has prayed, God will put those in front of you who are seeking this Love. There is a yearning within their souls that is bubbling forth into their conscious self, wanting truth, wanting love, wanting peace and wanting to nurture joy within themselves.

You have the key, my brethren. You are my disciples on Earth. I urge you to act accordingly, to be that channel of love in the world, to bring the words of truth to those who are willing to hear and to be a light. Yet, the world needs many more like you. Indeed, who will toll the bell of truth? Who will speak truth? Who will give the opportunity to others to hear the words of truth? Not in a demanding way, not in a way that causes people to fear or to feel that they will be punished if they do not follow the truth. Rather, it is in the way of joy, in the way of love, in the way that your eyes sparkle when you speak of this truth and that those who are listening will see for themselves that you are touched by the Holy Touch of God, His Love flowing into your soul each and every day, changing you, awakening you and bringing you to that place of pure truth and pure being.

I need you, my beloveds. I need your efforts. I need your commitment. I need all that you can give toward this truth. For how will the salvation of mankind be accomplished, but through the efforts of those sincere, faithful and filled with the inspiration of the Father's Love? As you humbly go forth, my brethren, you will be guided. You will be protected. You will be shown the way. God will bring the hungering souls to you. This will happen in wondrous ways, in common ways, in all the ways that humanity is capable of being with you and connecting to your soul. There are many ways that God may move and guide another and there are many expressions of individuals who are guided.

Be patient and be open and accepting to all those you meet. Be in that condition of prayer, Light and grace that God has given you. As you move in the world, this will affect the world. This will bring blessings to the world. As you are God's channels of love, as you climb closer to your Heavenly Father, as your souls expand with His Love, then your capacity and ability to reach out to others will grow with it. You will find yourself speaking in ways that you have never spoken before. You will find yourself embracing others where in times before you would be repulsed or fearful. You will have the bravery that comes with being a child of God and one of my disciples. You will put aside your reticence. You will put aside your fear of judgement. You will put aside your hesitations of being truly who you are, your authentic selves, my brethren. For within your soul is the authenticity, the true self that is meant to be expressed in your life in every way, every aspect. It will be with the inflowing of the Father's Love, strengthening you as you are, as God made you, so that you may be His channels of love and that you may be that light.

My brethren, we walk together. I am with you always and evermore. The angels walk with you. God's Hand is upon you. What more do you need, my brethren? All is available to you, every aspect, every blessing and condition of light that you require is given freely and is given with His Love. Seek out ever greater blessings. Be open and be available for God and for that guidance that God gives. You will find your life changing in ways that you have never anticipated. You will find opportunities opening that have never opened in your life before this time. You will find yourself connecting with many souls. They will intrigue you. They will delight you. They will bring compassion through you to them as they tell their stories, as they reveal their pain and their vulnerability. Then you will come to see that you too have followed your road to pain and vulnerability until you found God and until you have found the truth.

This is your opportunity to bring the truth to another and say, *"I know a way. I know a way out of your dilemmas. I know a way to heal this pain. I know a way that you may grow and expand in Love and feel the true joy of it"*. It is given to each of you, the possibility of being that messenger, that angel upon the Earth. May you realise and truly embrace this, come to understand what it means and the responsibility that it carries, and to act accordingly, to act with integrity, to act with humility, to act in grace, love, compassion, kindness, all the goodness and all the light that your souls can bring, that you, as God's children, may bring to others.

There is so much work to do, my beloveds, so much that needs to be done. The Earth, your brethren, are desperate. They are lost. They are in a pain so great that they insist on being numb and not feeling at all, for if they felt the anguish of their souls, it would be unbearable to them. Have compassion, my beloveds, for all the lost ones and for all who are seeking truth. May God provide the opportunity for them to be uplifted in truth. If you by chance are that instrument to do so, do not resist, but embrace it wholeheartedly with gratitude that you are given a great blessing of service. You are given much in the Light of God's Touch upon you.

My beloveds, we walk together. We are brethren and we shall be so for all eternity. I love you, my beloveds. I love you so much. I want you to feel how much I love you, how much I care for you, how much I pray for you and how much God loves you and holds you in His Love. God bless you, my beloveds. I am truly your brother and your friend. I am Jesus and I come to you in love. God bless you, beloved souls. God bless you.
