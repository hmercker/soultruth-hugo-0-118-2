# It Is Time to Gather Humanity Around You and Abandon the Fears of the Mind

November 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

There is change sweeping across your world, my beloveds. I am Orion. I come once again to speak to you. That change comes in fits and starts. It accelerates and decelerates. It is not predictable, but it comes nonetheless, as your world is in flux and life responds accordingly. Within you there is also change. As this great gift of the Essence of God's Soul finds its lodgment within your soul, it must bring change. This is inevitable. It is a law that who you are today will not be who you are tomorrow, nor the next day or the next, because within you there are fundamental shifts, healings and recalibrations happening in your soul. There is greater perception, deeper, broader perception. There is a greater capacity to love, to forgive and to have compassion. There is greater truth coming into your consciousness and you come to understand a world that is not merely of the material, but a world that encompasses the spiritual.

This comes. It creeps into your consciousness day by day. It is not revelatory often, but it is there nonetheless and it has its effects upon your behaviour and your thinking, the condition and the state you are in each and every day. This is what you have prayed for, to welcome the journey of the soul, to truly be within the realms of soul consciousness and to acknowledge this and be a part of it in a conscious way. These are the changes that are happening within you and they will continue to flourish and blossom within your being. My beloved friends, so much is waiting for you. Your consciousness will shift and change, not just temporarily, but permanently. As you open your eyes to a new world, a new way of seeing the world, you will experience more joy, more wisdom and more love and compassion. There will be less of the mindful machinations, the judgements, fears and errors, all that you deal with daily but will in time fade away like dust in the wind. It shall blow and scatter and be no more.

This is the miracle in the power of God's gift of Love. It changes everything. God gives love to this world of yours. It is not the Love that enters your soul, but it is love nonetheless. A love for His creation, a love that will help heal and make true His creation, a love that encompasses all that was meant to be upon this planet, a love that allows all who intervene, such as ourselves, to operate here, to help, to help restore harmony in this world. A love that brings light, a cleansing light, a powerful light that heals that which is in disarray and out of harmony, that which is out of balance, that which lacks the cohesiveness of what God has created.

These things are being influenced now. Forces are being awakened, forces that will cleanse the Earth, forces that will help humanity come to see truth as it must be seen and understand their role and place within this great system, this great creation that is God and His handiwork. Awakening to these things is crucial now. It must be something that you must focus on and be a part of. Allow consciousness to infuse into your awareness, consciousness of soul, consciousness of love, so that not only will you be happier and more joyful, but you will also be more perceptive and accepting of the laws in which and by which you were created, by which you live. These laws are not difficult to comprehend, especially if you are open to it. Your souls awaken to them.

Yet, obedience is often difficult for humanity. You beat your drum for independence. You beat your drum to say, *"I am an individual. I shall be heard as such."* To some degree, this is a powerful stance, one that is important, but when it overshadows and contradicts the laws by which you are created, by which love may flow, then there must be an adjustment, an amalgamation of independence and obedience. This sounds like a contradiction, but in terms of your soul consciousness, it is not. It can be done. Yet, when your mind feels it must have its say, it must enforce its edict of independence, then this is where you experience the tension and the disharmony that comes with this attitude.

We will help you find your way, my friends, as will many. It is not that difficult. When the wisdom of the soul intervenes and corrects the waywardness of the material mind, then the understanding comes, the acceptance comes and peace comes. It is when the mind and the emotions, which are so full of hurt, pain and defensiveness, continue to enwrap the individual with a shroud that is hard and full of challenges to the outside world, that it does not allow in the softness, the gentleness and the power of God's Love and blessings to soothe the troubled heart and mind, to dissolve the barriers and the armour, replacing it with something far stronger, more resilient, wiser and more loving.

This is the process that each of you are entertaining at this time. Indeed, there are times when you feel the tension within you, the difficulty of accepting these new ways, the flexibility that comes with obedience to God's laws. It is not a rigid thing. It is something that is incorporated into your consciousness. It comes with wisdom, with soul awareness and is accepted because it all is sensible. It all is required and necessary if there is to be harmony in the world. You do not lose anything by it, but you gain so much, my friends, so much. That wisdom, that perception of knowing and accepting is crucial now. You need to let go of the harshness of your minds, fear and rejection of that which the mind finds disagreeable, the rejection of vulnerability, the rejection of the softness and openness of love. You must embrace these things, not reject them. You must come into that harmony and peace of soul consciousness.

Embrace it, welcome it, reinforce it, live with it and live by it. That way, you will be formidable instruments of light, truth and love. You will be beautiful in your countenance, formidable in your presence and glorious in the love that will flow readily to everyone. You will come to feel and know these things if you but release the armour, release the old ways, the defensiveness, the anger, the jealousies, the resentments and the fear. All these things are irrelevant now. They do not work for you. They work against you. Allow yourself the tenderness of a soul redeemed by God's Love. Allow yourself the brightness of a soul thriving in the way of God's Love. Allow yourself the perceptions, the vision, the realisations that come as your soul opens and becomes truly what it is meant to be.

These things come now. They may seem somewhat distant, but in truth, they are not. It is that, at times, you make distance between yourself and your soul consciousness, a way of defending yourself in the world, a way of being strong enough to withstand the things of the world that bring difficulty and hurt and pain, to be impenetrable by the conditions of the world. These things are not what you think. You cannot defend yourself with the old ways. You must adopt the new. For in this you have the gracefulness, the beautiful ways, the gentleness, the light, the beauty of a soul responding in God's Love. That even though the slings and arrows are sent to you, you are within the grace that allows you to move away, allows all of these things not to truly touch you, but to be truly unscathed by such things.

This is what is waiting for you, my friends, to be that beautiful light, to show it, to express it and to live within it. It is time. It is time, my beautiful friends, to show yourself to the world, to not worry about their reactions, but to truly be that light. You will draw in more than those who will reject you. You will draw in many. It is time to gather humanity about you, speak the truth in those warm and beautiful ways that are of the soul, embrace your brothers and sisters in love and indeed be a light that shall help to heal the world, that will be a part of God's expression for the transformation of this world.

God bless you, my friends. You are all lights, truly, and I love you. I love you dearly. I am Orion and your friend on the journey of soul transformation and awakening. Blessings to you, my friends, always and evermore. God bless you.
