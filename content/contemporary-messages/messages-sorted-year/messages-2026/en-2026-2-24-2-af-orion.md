# Revealing the Existence of Stellar Friends Will Open Doors to Your Instrumentality

February 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion.

The consciousness of soul is growing within each one of you. You are awakening to this reality, the power of this perception and experience. Though you are each unique and the journey that you take is unique, the level of this depth and breadth of awareness varies. Yet, it is inevitable that, as you continue to pray for this great gift of Divine Love, the Essence of God, so the awakening of these perceptions, the reality of soul awareness, must come to you and so the consciousness grows within you and it becomes more powerful, its influence affecting you, maybe in subtle ways, but in ways nonetheless to determine your decisions, your thoughts and your life. As you continue to focus upon soul, this awareness will become more evident and you will know the trueness of your soul’s experience in life. You will know the trueness of your soul’s relationship with your Creator and you will know the trueness of your soul’s awareness and communication rapport with us, both your Celestial friends and your stellar friends.

The time is coming when the world will wake up to the reality that they are not alone in the universe. This is a great leap forward in consciousness because it allows for many things to happen. As we work with you and as many from other planets work with those on Earth, then the ability, the doorways, the consciousness will open so that we may be able to teach more readily and to show the way toward true self, the self it is the soul and the self that is soul consciousness. As we have been saying and emphasising, it is the awareness of soul that is needed. For within that awareness is much love and wisdom, perceptions and light come as you grow in your soul’s awakening.

If we can influence and communicate with many others on Earth who are so very much grounded in the material and seek proof, then the revealing of the truth of our existence will help many to accept and to listen. We are eager for others to listen. We know that we have your ear and you are all too willing to listen to what we have to say, but you are just a handful of billions of souls. Though you are a beautiful light and precious to us, to the world, they do not know you exist. Precious few know, but not enough to truly make a difference.

Within this great plan that God has for the awakening of humanity, we play our part. We have our wisdom to share. I know that many think of us as deep intellectuals with much technology and things to give that will help bolster the technological advances of humanity. In truth, without spiritual foundations, without love, without harmony, these advances and this information is useless. It does not carry humanity forward. It merely distracts and inhibits humanity in their pursuit of what they call progress. In truth, it is not progress at all. It is merely a continuation of a material pursuit that brings humanity away from all that which is harmonious in the universe, all that which is of love, all that which is of our Creator, the source of all love.

It is important when these doors open that the right information is shared and that you, my beloved friends, will step forward and tell of your experiences, what you have been able to experience, see, perceive, feel and know on this pursuit of soul awakening, so our part in this aspect of awakening humanity is close at hand. You will have opportunities to speak to others, for the information that will be given soon will ignite great curiosity and wonderment and at times fear. Your role is to quell the fearful thoughts, to add that which is truth and of light that will uplift humanity. Indeed, the struggle continues because many do not wish to believe what you have to say. Yet, there are many who will and are open to the words you might speak, to what you may demonstrate to the world.

We will be close at hand, also there to manifest truth, to reinforce truth, to bring light, to bring peace and assurance to humanity. God’s plan is vast and complex, involves many players, many individual souls. You know that you are a part of this and we ask you to continue to be forthright and persistent in what you do, in the growing of your soul, the awakening of your perceptions and your gifts. In this way, you will play an important part if you are at that place of soul awakening. We are with you in all that you are and all that you do. We are with God and all that God is and all that God wishes to be. We are with your angel friends as cohorts in a great and wonderful plan. As we have been telling you of recent times, the time grows very short indeed and the doors are beginning to open, the opportunity to speak will be given to each one of you.

Do not turn away. Do not feel that you are inadequate or that you lack the abilities to do as you have been guided. This is far from the truth. When you walk in faith, you are able to do anything. Indeed, you are capable, my beloved friends, truly. You will find out how capable you truly are as you step forward and grasp the opportunities given to you. Continue to have your spiritual eyes and ears open, to be willing to listen to your guidance, listen to what your soul has to tell you. Keep struggling and persisting in merging your material consciousness with that of the soul. In this way, things will become much easier and clearer, and you will see your way forward without hesitation or confusion.

There is much work to do, my beloved friends. The time is now for that work to begin in earnest. Consider these in the days ahead. Consider and ask for guidance as to what you may do your part to play. You will find that the path will be laid clear and concise and true to you, who you are, the gifts that you have, what you carry within you, the purpose of God has placed there. All these things await your acknowledgement, your faith and your desire.

May God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. Oh how I love you, beautiful souls willing to take up the banner of truth and walk forthright with bravery, with the strength and faith of a soul that knows God. God bless you, my friends. I walk with you as do many. God bless you in His Love.
