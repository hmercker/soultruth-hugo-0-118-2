# Through You, We Will Be Working to Bring About More Harmony in This Earth

May 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Joan of Arc received by Elizabeth Mack

Thank you, dear children, for gathering together, for allowing the blessings that our Father has to give to you to enter your souls, your minds and your bodies. I am Joan. I bring with me many souls, those who are Celestial Angels, those who are on the Divine Love Path in the spirit world, some of our brothers and sisters from beyond this Earth plane and who travel far to join us. We welcome you to this retreat that will bring greater peace and love into the world, for that is truly what is needed at this time.

Through you we will be working to bring about more harmony in this Earth. Open wide. Allow yourselves to be used as channels of love, especially during these prayers throughout this retreat. It is an opportunity for you to grow, an opportunity for you to use your gifts, an opportunity to serve God and all of humanity. This is an opportunity for each one here to develop their souls in greater Love and greater service for God and for your brothers and sisters upon this Earth. We will be with you throughout this retreat. You may feel us at different times. We are close and will remain close. Allow us to influence you in these days, as we draw you all together as a unit.

God bless you each and all. May we work together to uplift humanity. I am your sister Joan, and my love is with you, as is the love of all of us who are here.
