# Songwriters: Keep a song in your Heart

October 25<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

David received by Jimbeau Walsh

I am here, David. Yes, David of old. The writer of many psalms, many songs. When you find a melody that touches your heart strings allow the words to align so that both the message of the song and its melodic intent will delight, inspire and touch the listener.

Though I did not know of the Divine love, my heart was full of praise for God.  And this is why, to this day, much of what I wrote is still read and sung and appreciated by souls wishing to connect to the Heavenly Father.

My humble friends, you have received much and there is much more to receive.  Consider every hungry soul, every ear, waiting to hear that which is beauty, that which is heavenly, that which is created in grace and co-created in love.

I give you all my blessings and appreciation for what you have done and what you will do. Many, many, many musicians and composers are available to you should you wish to receive and we shall never discount your gifts in the glory of God.  So with all my love and blessings, keep a song in your heart.  I am David.  God Bless you.
