# I Am with You: Awakening the Soul Through Divine Love

May 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. The aching of your souls is evident. The power of that desire within to know God motivates your ascension into light, for as your soul aches with desire, as you feel the opening of your heart, as you know the fulfilment of the blessing of the Father's Love in response to these things within, so you fulfil the highest purpose and blessing in your life, so you are in true alignment with God and God's desire to cement that bond between you, so that nothing may detract or sway you from this great journey, that your feet may continue to be firmly upon this Divine Path, that you may have and know the discoveries of the soul as it awakens and finds its way to onement with God. There is no greater blessing, my beloveds, nothing more powerful nor transformative, nothing that will bring the highest of truth and the greatest of insights, as your soul is awakened with this gift of Love.

My beloveds, know God in the way that God wishes for you to know Him. Put aside all the thoughts and desires that are of the mind, these projections and ideas that lead you down a road that is not true. Rather, open yourself to the perceptions and knowings of the soul. You will find God there, not an image, not an idea, but a true and living God, that is your Father, your Saviour, the One who loves you so. There is nothing that will impede your progress, my beloveds, other than that which you put in front of you. I ask you, put aside these things that inhibit the understandings and experiences of the soul with God. If you are able, then the purity. grace and joy of being with God will be yours, for God intends this to be so. He does not put anything between you and Him, but gives freely and abundantly to your heart and soul, so that your hopes may be fulfilled, that the desires that are impending within you are fulfilled and awakened in the Truth of the Touch of the Holy Spirit, His Hand upon each one of you.

We are truly His children, His creations and the highest and greatest of all creations. The potentials that each of us carry are without limits when ignited by the Love of God. Know the flame of His Touch within your hearts and souls. Allow it to burn all impediments away, that you may know the freedom, the expansion, the joy of a soul redeemed, that you may truly be at one with God. I am here with you. I walk with each one of you. You are my brother, you are my sister, upon this path that we share together and we shall walk this path for all eternity, a friendship that will grow, a love that will expand, a bond that is eternal.

My beloveds, walk with me upon this road. You will know a life like none other, for you will be enlivened by the Father's Love and come to know life eternal. It is God's promise to you and I too make my promise to be with you upon that road and to walk eternally toward the light ever brighter. My beloveds, you have chosen God. You have chosen His blessing of Love and it is the greatest thing you will do in your life, not only here, but for eternity, for you will recognise the power and blessing that you have chosen and you ascribe to the Truth of His Love, a gift that will never cease, a journey that will never end.

God bless you, my beloveds. Oh, how I love each one of you, unique and precious to God, precious to me. I am with you. Come to know me. Come to feel my presence and my love for you. As you come to know God's Love for you, that awareness of my presence will increase and deepen until you truly know that I am your brother and your friend. I love you, my beloveds, beyond measure. I love you. I am with you. Open your heart to God and you will know me there as well. We shall forever journey in truth. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Kingdom, and your friend forever. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you.
