# Set Your Burdens Down at the Feet of God

May 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Starpoint Circle of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Jesus received by Maureen Cardoso

I come. I am Jesus. May the blessing of the Father's Love find its way into your soul, allowing your spirit to be of light. I ask you to put down those heavy burdens, those worries, the resistance that you carry in your spirit, the heaviness and burdens that distract you from the true nature of your soul, which is love, peace, compassion and joy. These attributes of your soul are what you long to be able to feel within your spirit. In order to do so, beloved friends, you must bring your burdens to the Feet of God in a sincere way, willing to have God remove these qualities from you and bless you with a spirit of light. In this way, you may walk your path in a way that carries soulful knowing and confidence, and you have an understanding, a deep appreciation and acknowledgement of who you really are.

In the eyes of God, you are one of His beloved children, his perfect creation, whom He so desires to return into the perfect man or the Divine Angel. All answers within you, there yet to come into your consciousness, will come through the blessing of your spirit being cleansed and your soul being awakened in God's Love. Take this time, as you come into this Circle of Light, to be intentional with opening yourself to the blessings that God has to give you and to celebrate your life in a way that is filled with joy, with grace and with ease, where you move throughout your daily experiences, flowing from one activity to the other with the presence of your soul in connection with God's Soul, aligning your soul with God's Soul. As you do this, and as you put your conscious attention upon this achievement, within your mind, your spirit mind and your material mind, you will be able to experience the blessing of oneness with God. All that you do, all that you think, all that you say, all that you see and hear will be through the filter of love, of truth and of wisdom.

Let this be your experience. Allow yourself to be healed in God's Love. All of your pains, whether they are emotional, mental or physical discomforts and diseases, shall truly be healed and alleviated. Listen deeply within you, through the ears of your soul, to know what is needed in any moment, at any time, upon your journey. For when you are attentive in this way, it is God's promise that answers shall come. Put your attention on the wholeness of your experience, where your body and your mind, as a reflection of your soul and soul condition, are of light. Allow yourself this blessing. Allow yourself to be close to the One who has created you in His image. Let His Love bring you back into at-onement. Let His Love show you the way. Let his Love demonstrate through you, permeating your soul and all that ripples out from you. Acknowledge that God's Love is the answer that you are seeking. God's Love is the antidote to all things out of harmony. Bring yourself back into harmony again and again until it becomes your nature, that you are Love Divine in soul, body and mind.

I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Heavens. I come to bring you a blessing of healing, of wisdom and of understanding. May you read the words that I have spoken and allow yourself to use them as a gauge of bringing alignment and harmony in God's grace. God bless you all. I am Jesus and my love is with you. God bless you.
