# Accept the Coming Earth Changes as Necessary and Blessed by God

July 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. Great change is coming to your world and each of you is aware of this. The depth and breadth of that change is not so well known amongst you, but you are feeling the influence and the signature of these changes. You are coming to understand that you are changing as a result, not merely the infusion of the Essence of God into your souls, which is always an agent for change, but the external influence as well that is coming as the actual universe is responding and changing to God's directives and God's plan.

You will become more aware of this. There are some obvious signs in terms of how the sun is reacting and expressing itself, but this is a minor aspect. There are other energetic influences and overshadowings of the Earth that will have far greater consequences. Yet this, in accordance with the great Divine plan, will not be an immediate thing, it will be gradual. For God, above all, seeks harmony and so harmony must come. God does not intend nor wish that humanity suffers greatly as a result of His doings and efforts. Humanity suffers enough from its own efforts, outcomes from error and lack of understanding and love, expressed by humanity.

You are on the cusp of great change. You have been told this before as well. There is so much that will happen. This is inevitable, my beloved friends. It will happen, that all you might do in preparation is prayer, to open up your faculties, to ask God to peel the scales from your eyes, asking that you may have insight, perception, strength and the ability to pivot in situations and coming Earth changes that will affect your physical life and how you live in that life. There is still much to do for each of you, releasing obstructions, barriers, biases, prejudices, fears and error. God will help you in this regard. Has not God sent you all the help that you require, not only from your Earth plane and the Celestial Kingdom, but even those of us from other places and habitations?

Yes, the support builds and it builds daily, helping to uphold you, to inspire you, to protect you and to help you in every regard. I urge you to be open and receptive to what might be offered from all of this help, all that God sends to you in the way of blessings, spirits, angels and stellar friends. It is all at your fingertips. It is for you to be aware. For it is difficult for you to ask when you are not aware of what you are asking for. It is difficult for you to understand when you do not have the perceptions, the capacity to truly understand, to see and to realise the depth and breadth of what is available to you and why it is available to you.

For you often do not understand why the Earth is changing, why you are changing and why it is necessary for change. Your mind is very resolved and comfortable within the conditions that you know and desire, but this is not what I am speaking of. I am speaking of conditions and changes that start from the soul, start from that deeper place where you may truly come to understand that those changes are permanent, steadfast and important.

My beloved friends, there is little you can do to be an intervening force in the world, in regards to these changes that are coming and the blessings that God is sending to the world. Yet, as the world changes and as you come to understand the changes within you, then you may share your experiences. You may help to comfort and uphold your brothers and sisters. You may be God's agent of change, in whatever way that He has deemed necessary and in harmony with the gifts that you possess. That, you might certainly do and we encourage you to do so. I know when individuals see a crisis, something that is looming, something that is foreboding, there is a desire to change, to run ahead and make those changes, in order to circumvent the results and these conditions that are coming, but you cannot. You might change yourself. This is, of course, a very important task. Yet, to set forth and change the world, that is God's doing.

It is for you to listen, to be aware, to accept and to embrace that which God intends, even when, in your minds and your attitudes, it may seem unacceptable, strange or lacks logic. These are perceptions of the mind. Indeed, we are speaking of perceptions of the soul that you may dive deeper and accept more readily those perceptions, those experiences of life, to the point where you are joyful and most accepting of what is coming. This is a formidable shift in consciousness and it is not easily attained. Yet, each of you continue on this road that will invariably bring a shift of consciousness. You are feeding your soul, seeking to know it, to nurture it, to accept it and to embrace it. In this, step by step, the doorway opens with just a crack, but it will continue to open until it is completely, widely opened, that you may walk through it easily and readily.

My friends, continue to seek spiritual, soulful growth. In this way, change will become obvious and you will readily embrace it. As you become stronger within the consciousness of soul, then the mind will acquiesce, it must, as that true strength builds and the truth grows within you. So much must come yet, but you are eager, open and willing. We are there to help you, as are many. Seek to open that door of consciousness that your soul may truly dominate your awareness, experience of life and your inner life. That way we will make great strides together, great strides indeed, that you will know truly the wonderment of your own soul, the wonderment of God's creation, the magnificence of it all and the universe of God.

This is what we wish for you. We continue to help you to inch toward this. As you get closer, my hope is that you will feel joy in anticipation rather than fear and foreboding. For it is important that you embrace this wholeheartedly. Give up your reluctance. In a way, give up your mind, for that domineering bully of consciousness needs disciplining and needs to be put in its place. so that is in harmony with your true self. We will help you with this as well, my friends. Oh, so much to do, so little time to do it in, but we will carry on, my friends, on this journey of soul awakening, service and love.

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and my love is with you. We are eager to continue on this journey of love and transformation. God bless you all. God bless you all.
