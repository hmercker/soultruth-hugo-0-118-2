# Faith, Service and the Blessings of Love

August 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

I come to be with you, my beloveds. I am Mary to pray with you, to pray upon this earthly plane, a privilege and a blessing for those of us from the Celestial Kingdom to be able to be with you in your prayers. For you draw us to you, you draw us close and so we may pray with you as we do with one another and that blessing may go out through you into the world. The world, so in need of such blessings, a world that needs peace, joy, healing and comfort, all these things we come to convey as God's instruments and you align yourself with this effort and become those channels of love in the world. Where you live, how you live, your faith and your efforts to serve God, all these things accumulate greater light and draw us ever closer, drawing God's Hands more closely upon you as you pray with deep faith and desire. As blessings come and so we come to accompany you on that great journey of awakening the soul, to experience the New Birth of the soul, as my son came to teach and continues to teach in the world.

We are living examples, are we not, of that teaching of what it may do for the soul and for the being that you are. You are beautiful children of God and I see you all as my children. I gather you in love. I gather you in light. We pray together. As we do so, we ascend into that great Hall of Peace and feel the blessings that come, as God makes this great chamber of light for us to commune with His great Soul and for you, my beloveds, to be lifted from the earthly plane into something more ethereal and beautiful and closer to God.

There are two things happening here, my beloveds. You are being channels of love in the world, and you are being loved and blessed as you commit yourself to service, my beloveds. Have faith in this. Truly open yourself to it and you will see, know and feel the beautiful blessings that God has to give to you and you will find the joy there, the beautiful joy and upliftment of God's Love for you and God's blessing for you and the world.

All will be blessed as you continue in your pursuits and your desire to serve. All will be blessed, my beloveds. We are in that great Hall of Peace. Feel the power of these blessings, this Love, this Light enveloping you, God sending His Holy Spirit to bring the blessing of Love into your souls, deep and abiding, powerful and transformational for each one of you.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Mary. I love you. I am glad to be with you. We are all happy to be with God in this time of prayer. God bless you, my beloveds.

2026-08-18-AF-Mary (G)
