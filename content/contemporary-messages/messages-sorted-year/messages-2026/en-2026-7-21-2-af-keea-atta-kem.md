# God Uses Every Opportunity to Bless Others

July 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

Beloved souls, I am Keea Atta Kem. In the days ahead, we will be using you extensively as instruments, channels of blessings for all those whom you will be meeting, all the individuals who are coming to honour the two souls who are celebrating the length of time on Earth that they have spent together. [^1] I urge you to be in good spiritual condition, to allow us to be close and to see this time as an opportunity to serve and to be a channel of love for the many who will be in your orbit.

Celebration is often an opportunity for God to bless, for it is positive and uplifting, allowing the blessings of the Creator to infiltrate and bless another deeply. Healing and upliftments will come and flow. When you are in a state of joy and delight, then you become a beautiful channel, a beautiful light to help uplift the atmosphere, to bless those close and even to be a channel of light for the world in general. We will be there in your celebrations. Many of us will join you, for we too celebrate. We too wish to acknowledge the dear souls who have spent much of their life in service and to bring truth to others. It is a significant occasion, a beautiful time for love to flow and for all to acknowledge the power of love, the power of truth and the power of God, who has blessed and guided these two beautiful souls for many, many years.

We are all celebrating. We are all in unity in the joy of acknowledgement of God's blessings upon this beautiful couple and upon all of you. It is not by chance that so many of the beautiful souls are gathered and part of this celebration. They become the foundation for these blessings to flow. All will be used and a part of these blessings. No opportunity is wasted, my beloveds. Much can be accomplished when you are receptive, open and desire to be that channel of love. It will flow and it will bring joy to all.

We thank you for your efforts, beloveds. We thank you for all efforts made to bring the blessings of God's Love to the world and yes, during these significant times, these mileposts of one's life, we are there to honour, to bless and to join in in the celebration. Remember this when you are in these crowds and how you might be utilised as a channel of blessings. We will guide you to various individuals that are in need and who may benefit from your gifts. They may not know that they are being blessed, but indeed, in many ways, they are being blessed and will come away from this event with upliftment and blessings that will bring greater light to them, some relief and comfort to them.

Many are struggling. Many continue to misunderstand the meaning, purpose and flow of earthly life. Yet, in their souls there is a yearning. This is where we are connected and make effort to help that soul to find an avenue, a crack within the armour, so that they are liberated or at least have some relief and some light. God's plan is great. It is detailed. It is complex. Yet, your place within it merely requires your acceptance and application of your instrumentality, so it will be a blessing of light, of joy and upliftment. We will join you in all the celebrations, all the gatherings and all the blessings from God.

I am Keea Atta Kem. I love you all. May your day be blessed, for we are always eager to be close and God always is eager to bless His beloved children. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you.
<small>

[^1]: Referring to Al and Jeanne Fike’s then-upcoming 50th Anniversary celebrations on July 25th 2026.
