# Prayer to God and Guidance on Serving as God’s Instrument

June 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Faith Nyquist. I am here once again to be with you, to pray with you and to speak with you. I wish to thank this instrument for allowing me to come through. My ministry on Earth is not finished yet, for the ministries of the angels upon the mortals will never be finished until that day comes when all are either in the heavens of the natural love or the Celestial Heavens. Then, all will be settled. All will be in harmony and peace. Yet, that day feels a long way off, does it not? So many are in trouble. So many know disharmony, pain and disillusionment. So many are lost and so we continue in our work, do we not, my friends, continuing to seek the Father's Love, to awaken to its blessings and benefits, to be transformed by it.

The magic of this Love knows no bounds. It is the eternal quest toward at-onement with God. We are all captured by the momentum of this great blessing, hurtling toward our relationship, our oneness with God. Our hearts are opened and filled with the Father's Touch of Love, the Holy Spirit bringing, conveying this blessing. We are the few but the strong ones, seeking to overturn the momentum of darkness that continues to build in this world, seeking to help others find their way, seeking to be that instrument, channel of God's Love in the world. We gather, do we not, for strength and support, to pray together, to be unified in our efforts, in our journey, as our souls continue to awaken, as we continue to find our way and find ourselves, as we journey toward God.

My beloved friends, there is so much that can be given to each one of you. I ask you to be open, to desire this, with a fervency, a fire lit within your soul that says, *"God, I need You. God, I am here for You, God. I am open to You and may You set Your Love within, quenching the fires of pain and replacing them with the fires of Love. Rather than displeasure and disharmony, I will feel and know the pleasure of Your Touch within my heart, of Your Love glowing within me, an ember burning bright, helping me to know that I am truly loved, helping me to find my way in a dark world, helping me to be strong in that world, so that I may reach out to my brothers and sisters and pull them along into the light, not forcibly, but with love, compassion, wisdom and all the goodness that I possess, that we all possess, to help our brothers and sisters along the path."*

Have compassion and faith. Be truly with God and God will utilise the strength within you, the light within you, the love within you to help others, to be expressed with your families, your loved ones, be expressed in the world wherever the opportunities may take you in the world. Be God's channels, beloved souls. This is part of the journey, for as you are used in this way, as you allow God to utilise your gifts and the channel that you are, then you benefit greatly. You grow with it. You are a greater light, stronger, more perceptive, more loving, more compassionate and more wise. All is given in the blessings of this Love.

Indeed, we are all God's children, are we not? We are all God's children, walking hand in hand in the light, God's Presence and feeling the glow of His great Soul, shedding His Light and Love upon us, warming our hearts and souls to such an extent that we are uplifted and the detritus of this world may burn away and be forever gone. We are cleansed, healed and changed by Your Love. Dear God, we thank You for all Your blessings, for all that You are. We love You. We are with You. We are here to be with You and so we open ourselves to a great inflowing of this gift of Love. We eagerly accept and desire it. We eagerly embrace You and wish to be known by You and wish to know that You know us, that You love us. We are Your children.

God bless you, my friends. I am Faith and I love you. I love you so, my brethren on Earth. We continue with our ministry, with our efforts to bring light, blessings and comfort to many. We do and so we are God's instruments. God bless you, beloveds, God bless you.
