# The Abundant Grace and Blessings of God

October 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds. I come, your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you and be with you, my precious brethren of the Earth. I come in love, as the grace of God has infilled me and infills you. We are one in this blessing and so uplifted into the great Hall of Peace together. We are together. As God's great treasure trove of blessings is open before you, waiting for you to choose, beckoning you forward in light, showing you the way to eternal peace and upliftment. We are together, my brethren. In the grace of God, in the blessings of God. All that God has to give, so it is given, the power of which you will come to understand, the grace of which will be yours for eternity, all blessings of His Love, given freely and abundantly

The angels surround you, my beloveds. We are all in harmony. We are all gathered, seeking the same thing, seeking the blessings of God. It is given with the power of His Touch upon us, the beauty of His Light shed upon us, the magnificence of the Holy Spirit, blessing each soul individually, bringing the depth of His Love within you as you open yourselves and greet this blessing with joy and desire. It is given, my beloveds. All those things of the Earth, all the distractions, all that causes you pain, disappear in these moments of grace.

Open yourselves wide, my beloveds, beyond the constrictions of your mind to the expansiveness of your souls. Be in this light. Together, we are blessed. Feel the peace that passes all understanding. Know this: all the treasures of Heaven are offered in the grace of God's Love and the beauty of His Touch. My beloveds, we are together. We are together in the oneness of His Love and grace.

I am with you, my beloveds. I am Jesus and my love for you is great. God bless you, in all the goodness, all the light and all the Love that is of God. God bless you. I am Jesus and I love you.
