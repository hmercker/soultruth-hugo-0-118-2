# The Eternal Path of Soul Awakening

August 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

The path eternal, my beloveds. There is only one path where the soul may indeed flourish for eternity. All other paths are conceits of the human mind and the human desire to be upon a path that is of their own making and choosing. God allows us to be, for God in His Love gives all His beloved children the choice to follow whatever path they desire, whatever path they choose. You have chosen the Path Divine, the eternal path. As your souls continue to grow within that journey of soul awakening and transformation, you will come to understand the meaning of eternity, the depth and breadth of at-onement with God.

My beloveds, I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to assure you that the path that you have chosen is indeed the eternal path, the one that assures your journey toward at-onement with God and a journey that will never end. As you take that journey forward, you will accumulate many experiences with God and many experiences to understand the nature of God's creation, the Nature of God Himself and the nature of your own being. All of these things will come as you grow within your soul, awakened by this gift. My beloveds, you are surrounded by angels. You are given much to support and uphold you. You are blessed deeply by the Holy Spirit. These gifts and blessings are given freely to humanity. I am deeply saddened by the fact that very few understand or grasp this Truth, that very few take the eternal path while on Earth. Yet, amongst you are intrepid and beautiful souls who are willing to dedicate themselves to the eternal path. Each of you continues to long for and reach out to God to truly understand their own nature and the nature of the universe.

My beloveds, your prayers will be answered. Your questions will be answered. All that you require upon this path of eternal progress will be given, given freely by God. For God's generosity knows no bounds. God's blessings are unlimited. You are the recipients of God's Love for you and you have come to recognise, to a degree, the extent of that Love. You will come to know it in even greater measure and intensity. For this is assured, as your longings and desires are in alignment with the Truth and seek out the blessings that God has to give. The awakening will be yours and the transformation of your soul will be attained in good time.

Have faith, my beloveds. Have faith on this eternal path. Walk boldly forward and seek all that God has to give to each one of you, for you are truly His children. You will truly come to understand who you are and what you were created for. Within you are many gifts, a deep purpose in this world and a beautiful instrumentality that may be awakened and flourish within the blessings of the Father's Love. We walk together, my beloveds, each in their own way, each upon their unique paths. Yet, together, we move forward upon this designated path of truth and shall do so for all eternity.

My beloveds, we work together. We are family. I am your elder brother and you have many, many brothers and sisters who inhabit the Celestial Kingdom. As you grow in sight, in vision, perception and understanding, you will come to know this not only as a belief and a longing for this Truth but as the Truth itself manifests in your consciousness, your ability to see and understand, in all its measure and ramifications, the truth that is the soul, the truth that is life eternal as the soul gleans the Love of God and grows within it. My beloveds, we are on a great quest together, seeking truth, seeking the nourishment of the soul and seeking the fellowship that comes as each soul is brought to wakefulness and awareness and that the Love of God, as it flourishes within, is expressed between us, forming a bond that is also eternal.

There is much to look forward to, my beloveds. No matter your trials and tribulations on Earth, know that there are great rewards for your prayers and your efforts to grow in the Father's Love, and that as you serve God, the raiment of your soul is perfected and beautified by that service. You will come to know how beautiful you truly are as you enter into the world of spirit and continue your journey to the Celestial Heavens. We continue to walk with you, my beloveds. We shall do so for eternity and you will know the joys beyond imaginings. You will come to know the truth beyond what you may see now with limited vision, for the vista of God's creation will open to the perceptions of your soul. You will come to know truly the magnificence, the wonderment of God's creation, God's being and your place within it.

My beloveds, I am with you, truly your brother, truly and your friend. We continue on this journey. We continue with faith, hope, desire, longing and seeking. All comes as the soul is awakened in the Father's Love and these true parts of yourself rise up into your consciousness and become a part of you, become a powerful motivation within you, become the avenue where truth is found and truth is expressed in your lives. God bless you, my beloveds. I am with you. I am Jesus and I love you, each one. I give a blessing of peace for each of you, a blessing that God wishes for each of you. Deep peace, my beloveds, as we enter that great Hall of Peace, we come to know the deep Touch of God upon us. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you.
