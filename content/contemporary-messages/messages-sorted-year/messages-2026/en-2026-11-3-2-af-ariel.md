# The Joy of a Soul in Love with God

November 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Ariel received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved children. I am Ariel, an inhabitant of the Celestial Kingdom and I come in joy. For those who are attuned to God know joy, joy everlasting, joy deep, joy expansive. For every soul that is in at-onement with God knows that this feeling of joy is an integral part of that relationship. Those of you on Earth often do not feel this joy, but I come to share it, to tell you about it, to encourage you to come and embrace the joy that is your soul in love with God. For as you continue to grow in this Love that is God's blessing and gift to you, you will find that bubbling up from your soul is the true sense of joy, the essence of joy.

It comes as you are awakened by this Touch from the Divine and you come to see yourselves as part of the Divine, a beautiful soul created, individualised and blessed to follow the road of true transformation of soul through His Love. How joyous this is! How wondrous! Each of you who are souls, individuals, have this opportunity. Everyone on Earth and in the spirit world has this opportunity to come to know God in this special way, to receive the gift of His Love, so that your soul may perceive, feel, know, and experience at-onement with God.

My beloveds, be joyful on this journey. The awakening is shedding all that which is not of joy and embracing all that which is of joy. Each of you comes to know God in this way. There will be a twinkle in your eye, a resounding, joyful expression of soul, a light that reflects the joy, a capacity to love that brings joy. All these things are a true expression of who you are and what God created within you. Everyone has this capacity to express joy, to be joy and to use joy as a healing, beautiful touch, a blessing for another, uplifting, uplifting all you meet. This is what you are meant to do, to uplift, to bring light, to say and find the reasons and expressions of yourself in joy, beloveds.

I am Ariel. I come in joy and I am with you in joy and I want you to feel that joy welling up within you. That acknowledgement, that gratitude, the expansiveness and the awakening that comes as God touches your soul in such goodness, Light and Love that all you can feel is joy, my beloved, beloved friends. Life is joy and joy is an expression of life. God bless you all. I love each one of you and I am here to spread the good news of God's Love to every soul who is willing to listen and know its blessing. God bless you, beloveds. I am close and I love you. God bless you.
