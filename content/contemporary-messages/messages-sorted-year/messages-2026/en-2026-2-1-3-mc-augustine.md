# Honouring Karen Swanson: How Her Gifts Bless the Local Community Weaving the Fabric of Love into the T&T Circle

February 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Augustine received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you, beloved souls. It is your teacher, Augustine. I would like to make a few comments about the dear sister (Karen) who sits to my left and about how she brings the Light of God’s Love into the world, honouring the gifts of her own soul as a teacher, as an example and as one who listens deeply to the Will of God. Because of this, each one who is a part of your circle, your extended circle, is blessed. Your circle is held together, weaving the fabrics of love and light through each one.

Every time that you come together, another thread of God’s Love weaves you together as a group, powerfully in strength. Each time you are able to meet, whether that be over the wires or in person, make every effort possible to align your life, to come, for when you come together in this way, there is a power. There is a strength and you build community. You feel that within your hearts and your souls and you are nourished deeply, for these teachings and this opportunity to converse with one another, to pray with one another, to be educated together in this way, are very rare. By your coming together, you are a light in your nation, for in time, each one will grow in their own light, in their own way of loving and being that instrument, a channel for God to use you in a way that helps with the transformation of your Earth, your Mother Earth, who strives to carry each one of you, who strives to recover herself through the powers of God’s Love and God’s plan.

More than a prayer circle, see yourselves as a Circle of Light that is changing the world. You too are also being changed with every drop of God’s Love that enters your soul. Yet that Love, God’s Love in your soul, desires activation and demonstration. It desires to move through you so that the world can see the Substance of God’s Light that you carry. Your light will be a powerful attractor to others who are seeking. Allow this Love, this Light, the Truth that is within your soul, to do its work. Free yourself from the inhibitions that you carry. Allow your human condition to be resolved, dissolved and dissipated so that your divine condition is what emanates through you.

We will always come to be with you and pray with you and support you. We are so very happy that such a light, a Circle of Light, exists in your beautiful nation. God bless each one of you on your journey, taking and drinking these waters, these Living Waters of Divine Love, to nourish and replenish and cleanse you and transform your soul into a divine soul. God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine, so happy to be with you in this way. God bless you.
