# Whether or Not They Accept It, This Truth Takes Root in the Souls of Those Who Hear It

February 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Manifesto of Light, Christchurch, Barbados

James Tudor Sr received by Al Fike

It is James, your father and grandfather to some. I made my efforts and inroads to bring this truth to this island. Some took it seriously and some did not. It is the way of the world, as you well know. I am very pleased that you take up the chore of educating and showing the way of this truth to your fellow Bajans. It is a formidable task, I know, one that does not reap rewards very often. Yet, seeds are planted and you would be surprised as to how many have come to me in the spirit world and thanked me for the truth that I spoke, even though they did not listen at the time. That truth was seeded into their souls and took root in time.

Your words are not lost, my beloved friends. Your efforts are not lost. Yet, if you are seeking gratification of numbers and the reward of those acknowledging your efforts, I say it is a very hard row to hoe. You will find that there will be those who truly understand, feel and acknowledge, and they will become your true and good friends. You will find at times, others will reject you. Still, you have sown the seeds of truth.

You must, my friends and my beautiful family. You must sow those seeds. Do so with inspiration, do so with love, do so with kindness and generosity. I know I had a reputation for being harsh and adamant, strong in personality. Thus the response from others was not favourable. Yet, my voice carried, my words spoken were embedded within the souls of others and that reverberated in time. If you are meek, you will not be heard. If you are strong yet gentle, like Jesus, then you will have the best of those attributes working in your favour. You will find the people will come close.

Every soul on this planet is yearning for God, but very few understand this or acknowledge it. Your job and your purpose is to ignite that desire within others, a feeling that something is missing inside of them. Truly it is for many. You must elicit a kind and comforting word, words of truth that they are loved by God, that they may know that love as a real and tangible gift. The message is simple, but in this world full of fear, mistrust, misguidance, misunderstanding, it is difficult to penetrate the barriers and to be heard amongst your brothers and sisters.

I urge you to persist, as our beloved Master has spoken. Persist, my brothers and sisters. Persist. The time will come when you will speak to many and many, through desperation, fear and anxiety will listen to you because you will stand as a rock upon the shores and speak the truth and speak the truth with authority and love together. Many will listen, because you are not caught up in the fear and the tumult that is coming, but you are calm and sure of your place with God and your place on this world. They will look to you because of your confidence and your surety and the truth that you speak and the love that emanates through you. They will be comforted and they will listen.

I speak this to assure you, my beloved friends, and those who are coming amongst your Circle of Light, that there is purpose and there is a destiny. Do not doubt this. Do not doubt this. I am James, James Tudor, and I come to you with all earnestness. I am with you to help you. I come to do whatever is required of me to assist you in this work. What greater work thing can there be than to speak the truth, to live it and to express it? You do God’s work, the highest work of all, and you are blessed to do so. Be true to your soul. Be true to the truth. Be true to that which God has put within you, a destiny formed long before you were born. If you are able and willing to express this destiny, you will know the greatest of all joy, something that very few on your world will know. When that time comes to pass into the spirit world, you will look back and say, *“Yes, I have fulfilled God’s Will within me and through me and I am truly God’s child, acknowledging in love the gift that God has given me, the gift of life.”*

May God bless you on that journey, my beloved friends. May God bless you deeply, abundantly and may the fruits of your labours be realised. My love is with you. The harshness that I once carried has melted. I am now soft as a flower, and though it took some doing for God to remake me into such a being, I am a testimony to the power of God’s Love. God bless you all.
