# The Power of Daily Prayer / A Blessing for God's Children

March 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus & Mary received by Al Fike

**Jesus:** I come to pray with you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come in love. I come to assure you that the Heavenly Father knows your prayers and your longings. I come to affirm the Truth that the Father's Love is the way to the Celestial Kingdom. As you pray to receive it, so you shall come closer to that time when you are eligible to enter into that Kingdom. That transformation that happens within the soul happens with each day that passes. Though you may worry about the times that are now and ahead, I would urge you to make your efforts with God one day at a time, that gift that God has given you of a day.

How would you utilise that day? How would you be with God on that day? For are all the machinations and events of your planet more important than your efforts in prayer with your Heavenly Father? Though the world may be lost and in chaos and the darkness proliferates, within your being and your home, build the light. That light can only come upon the heels of your prayers. The light in your soul can only be built as you receive a greater inflowing of the Father's Love. This basic understanding, which you all know, builds the foundation of spiritual light and truth within you.

All that is of the world, all the worries and cares of the world, cannot touch you when you are in that grace of God. I ask you, my beloveds, go to the Source of all life and all Love. Go to your Heavenly Father, the Creator. Seek out His blessings. When you do so as the primary objective of your day, then all else will be in harmony. All will fall into place and you will be guided as your day progresses. The chatter and clatter of the outside world will no longer have precedence and will no longer distract you. Instead, you become God's instruments in the world with that day that is yours, a gift given by God. That day is precious. That day has crucial precedence and expressions that God will guide you to bring forth, to demonstrate, to be a channel of love and light and healing for the world.

When I walked the Earth, this is how I performed my ministry. I asked God to guide me in that day and God would bring souls onto my path. God would bring blessings through me and around me. God would do what you call the heavy lifting of the day. For you, my beloveds, what is required of you is to be present with God, to open your heart to God, to have the faith required to move within the Will of God and the gifts within you that hear that quiet voice, that prompting, that guidance that comes as you walk in faith in the world. There is much for each of you to do. If you focus your thoughts and energies upon the problems of this world, then you redirect yourself beyond the reach of God and into the realms of men. Thus, that day, that day's potential, is lost or diminished by your focus and your efforts.

I urge you, my beloveds, to start your day in prayer, to be with God and to ask God to guide you, that you may be used as His instrument for that day. That will do more good than any focus and thought and worry for the world. It will guide you along a path that, in its practical ways, will use you to assist another, to inspire another, to be an instrument of blessing for another, what you may do in a day. Yes, you may pray for the world. This is important, but you may also touch another soul in a way that is meaningful and deep and may have, in time, profound consequences. This you may do in simple measures and ways as you are guided. The way will be clear, the efforts will be easy to manifest and express in the world and you will be uplifted and feel the joy of your journey in life, a journey that is of this day, this precious day that is given in love, that is given as a gift and has the potential to receive many gifts and many blessings.

I am with you, my beloved brothers and sisters. My love is with you. You have many angels with each of you. Utilise the resources that God has made available to you in order to do this work, this simple and humble work that requires your faith and your prayers and your efforts to be a light in the world. May God bless you as you move, as you pray, as you exist, moment by moment in this world of yours. These blessings are available to you at any moment and every moment. Avail yourselves of what is being given and be true to the longings of your soul to be with God and to receive His great gift of Love. All else will fall into place and there will be harmony in your life and joy in your heart and love all around and within.

God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you all. I am your brother and your friend, Jesus. I love you. Oh, I love you so, my brothers on Earth, my sisters on Earth, all angels in the making, all with great potential, great light, great beauty. You are truly a part of us. We stand together in the world as a cadre of light to be expressed each and every moment. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you.

**Mary:** I come to bring a blessing to you all. I am Mary, mother of Jesus. I come in love and light to all of you who live upon this beleaguered Earth, to uplift you, to uphold you, to bring the blessings of God to each one of you, for I love you so, my beloved, beloved children. I am here for you in all the love that is of God's Love and all the blessings that are of God's blessings. I come to bring this to you as a gift, to bring further light to each of you, that you may be a light in the world, that you may walk in light, that your faith may be increased and your capacity to love may be realised. I come to bring a blessing that will remove the barriers from this and remove the scales from your eyes and open the awareness of your souls.

This comes with the Father's Love, His blessings. It also comes as your angel friends work with you to remove the obstructions that keep you from this awareness and this truth. God gives many blessings to His children and so this blessing is given today, this moment, to each of you. God bless you. I am Mary. I love you so. I am here with each of you in the Light and purity of God's Love. I am here and I love you. God bless you.
