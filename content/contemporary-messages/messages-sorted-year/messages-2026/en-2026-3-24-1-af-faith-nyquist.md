# Open Your Soul to Receive God’s Abundant Blessings

March 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Faith. I come to you in love, acknowledging your beautiful souls, acknowledging God's beautiful Soul and all that can come of this relationship between your soul and the Soul of God, the miracles that can happen, the shifts and blessings that can be a part of your life as you continue to establish this relationship with your Creator. For God wants you to be in alignment with Him, not in a way that He insists or He pressures you, but He invites and He does so with love. All that is of God, all that God gives to us, is found in the gentle stream of His Touch and blessings upon you. As you beseech God in your prayers, as you open yourself with earnestness and desire, so the response is in accordance to the intensity of your prayer.

Each time you come to God, remember the cornucopia of blessings that may be yours is determined by your receptivity and desire. Whatever holds you from that blessing, or the many blessings, is what is in you that is resistant, that does not know how to ask and be receptive to God. Allow that resistance to fall away, my beloved friends. It is important that you move beyond the biases and fears, the humanness within you, and feel and know the freedom of a soul that is in rapport with the great Oversoul. A soul that knows God is a soul on the road to liberation, to healing, to awakening and transformation.

You are all on that road, my friends. With you is great company, your angel friends, the bright spirits, even your mortal friends who pray with you. So many are here in the light, the Light of God's Presence and peace is here with us all, wanting to lift us up into that great Hall of Peace, wanting us to be open and receptive and attuned to God's blessings for each one of us. For our needs are great. Each one is unique. Each journey, each life, has its impediments and its strengths, its light and its darkness, but together we form a unified field of desire, stretching out to God, open and receptive to God's Touch.

Here we are, my beautiful friends, seeking to be healed and awakened, to be in the Light and harmony of God's Love. May we all be open to receive these moments together, rising up into that great Hall of Peace, being in the joy of it, in the peace and stillness of it, in the light of it, in the love of it. It is ours for the asking. I encourage each one of you to seek this out in all its glory and wonderment and be in the rapture of God's holy Touch upon your soul.

God bless you all. I am Faith. I love you and I pray with you, as do many. God bless you all in His Love.
