# The Clouds Are Parting and the Warmth of God’s Soul Shall Shine Upon the World

October 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

God bless you, mon amis. I am Mary and I come on the heels of your love, your prayers and your light. Come to be with you in these prayers. I have a great affinity with the French people. For they have open hearts and many have come to know me and seen me in their prayers. It is a great blessing for me to have this relationship with my friends, with those who follow the way of love. Though many do not understand as you do, in their hearts they know the truth and many have received this great gift of Love, even though they do not have the words to define it. They feel it within them. The blessings of God upon them.

My beloved friends, children of God, the clouds are parting. Soon you will feel the warm rays of the sun of God's Soul, the beautiful warmth of His Holy Spirit upon you, the touch of that great blessing within your soul, that you may receive His Essence. Indeed, the world will experience that parting of the clouds of fear, pain and loneliness. Many will be uplifted and inspired. Many will wonder. Many will be humbled. My beloveds, you must go forth and comfort them and teach them the way of the Father's Love, that they may know what it is that is being given and they may be empowered with knowledge and understanding of what they may receive and why God has given this gift. For He loves us all.

He loves me and He loves you. There is no difference in that Love. It is so immense, so great, that you could not possibly truly comprehend it, but indeed, as the warmth of His Love shines upon you, you will feel its comfort. You will know its blessings and all is given freely and abundantly. This is God's Love. The extent and breadth of it shall continue to envelop you and you will continue to experience and comprehend it for what it is. So much is given, so much can be received and yet God is not diminished by it. Yet, you, my beloveds, are awakened and transformed by it.

My beloveds, I pray for the children of Earth. We in the Celestial Kingdom pray for you all for a transformation to take place, a healing of the world, a changing of attitudes, perceptions and priorities, that you may follow the path designated by love, that you may understand the Laws of Creation and the power of your choice to adhere to these laws and to bring greater harmony to yourselves, that you may learn to love one another, embrace each and every child in love, every soul, every brother and sister. These are the teachings of my son and these are the teachings you must bring forward in the blessings and Light of God's Touch.

For God will be with you when you speak the Truth. You will demonstrate this Truth to many and many will feel the impact, the power of it and the joy of it, will find themselves succumbing to God's invitation to be with Him, to know Him, to glorify in His Love. It is available to all. Indeed, so few understand this, but the time is coming, my beloveds, when the knowledge of this will be given far and wide, beyond the limitations of your Circles of Light. It will expand and reach many.

The time is coming, my beloveds. The clouds are parting and the light shines in all its brightness, warmth and comfort. God's Love is for everyone. Remember this as you walk your daily life. Remember this, that God's Love is for everyone and that you, my beloveds, may be a channel of that Love in the world.

You are beautiful, each one. Beautiful souls. Beautiful creations of God. In the light, you walk. In the light, you have your being and expression. Continue in your work. Continue in what is God's blessings and guidance for each one of you. Continue to be His instruments of light, truth and love and you will know the joy that your soul will rejoice and your life will reflect the beauty of it and the abundance that is God's Love.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Mary. I come in love. I come on the heels of your prayers and I am yours, a servant of God. Ask for me and I will come and assist you when you are in need and pray with you when you long for God. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you all. I love you. I love each and every one of you. God bless you.
