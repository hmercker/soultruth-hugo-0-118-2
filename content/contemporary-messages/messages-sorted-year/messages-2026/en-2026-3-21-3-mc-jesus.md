# Comes to Help Us to Acknowledge Our Calling

March 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Divine Prayer & Elevation Retreat, North Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Maureen Cardoso

I come. I am Jesus. I am here to acknowledge each one of you, the blessing that you are, the light that you are, each soul here truly longing to know who the Creator is, longing to have that communion, that deep understanding from within. I have been with you, each one, blessing you, helping with an opening in your soul, so that your longings that are held deep within your soul can come into your conscious awareness. These longings desire to receive our Heavenly Father's Substance and Essence. As your soul is imbued with this Essence, you begin the journey of transformation, the path of the New Birth, making your travels to become a divine soul, something completely different from its origin.

I am sent to you to give you your calling, to help to awaken the calling that is within your soul from God, who desires His children, His creation, to know Him, to help that calling within you, to make its presence known so that your instrumentality can serve the light of your world through its transformation. You all are experiencing these great changes within you and around you. What will bring you stability is this Essence. I am here to offer you this blessing, this calling, this truth, God's Soul yearning for your soul and His Soul to be at one, bringing your world into harmony.

God bless you all on this exquisite Path of Divine Love with brothers and sisters, true eternal family. I am Jesus. God bless you all. God bless you.
