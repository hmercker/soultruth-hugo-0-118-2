# What Holds You Back from Fulfilling Your Soul's Purpose?

April 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Andrew received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Andrew. Oh, there are so many conditions in the world that are preventing God's blessings to come to every soul that inhabits it, so many reasons why, so many decisions made, so many thoughts expressed and so much love that is not given. Conditions build upon conditions creating great barriers to God. Together, you create a focus of light, a node of light in the world that is emanating and pulsating out into the world, providing some relief and some comfort to those who are receptive to the conditions that are being built here with each prayer. When you are present together, the lights of your souls are like a big necklace, joined by your desires and longings for God.

My beloved friends, there is much work to do. There is much to be done. Indeed, we beseech each one of you to release the barriers to your own reticence and fears around what you may do for God, for God stands always poised to utilise you as His instruments. There is no barrier from God to you. There is only the barrier between you and God. We urge you, and we have been discussing these things together for the last while, for you to look at these barriers, look at the reticence, look at the fear, look at the judgement, all conditions within you that are contrary to what your souls know to be true: that each of you has a purpose and a destiny in the world and that God has placed that within each of you. In fact, God has placed these blessings and this mandate within every soul upon the planet. They are unique to the individual. They are a reflection of your gifts, your personalities and your leanings of expression and being in the world. Nonetheless, that purpose exists, waiting for its opportunity to be expressed. The expression of that purpose brings great joy and fulfilment to the soul.

Are you willing to take that risk, even to discover what that purpose might be, even to go deep enough within to realise what holds you back from that beautiful gift and purpose that God has given you? It is time now to release the obstructions, to be vulnerable, to be faithful, to be with God and to love God. For when you come to that place where you and God truly understand each other, where you are truly aware of who you are and what you are meant to do in this world, then the true work can begin, the true purpose revealed, the true gifts manifest and the true love, pure and clear, flowing through you. As this alignment takes place, what you will truly know is joy within your heart, a sense of fulfilment and indeed a sense of purpose.

My beloved brethren, my brothers and sisters, walk with God. Listen to God's Will and know God's Will. You will be on a journey like none other. You will find yourself upon that road and find what you are truly made of, what God has put within you, how this may be expressed and given in loving ways and means and how God will uplift you and the angels will surround you in that expression, in all that is meant to be, all that is within you, blessed and vital and true to who you are. Each of you is on that journey of discovery, of knowing yourself, of realising what is within and the potentials that lie within that can be ignited by this great gift of God's Love given to you over and over again, drop by drop, blessing by blessing.

So much awaits you, my beloved brothers and sisters, so much. Many of you are very close indeed to these discoveries, to this awakening and the transformation that comes. I urge you to have faith, faith in the journey, faith in yourself and faith in God. I urge you to trust in all of these things and to know that God will never put you in harm's way. God will only put you in situations that will bring greater harmony and greater light, even though at the time it may not seem so. Yet, in the bigger picture, upon the journey of life, surely you must look back and see how certain situations and conditions that challenge you actually strengthened you, gave you more wisdom, more compassion and more love, not only for yourself but for those around you.

This is the way of God. For God does not focus on just one thing or one blessing but many blessings, many things to be healed, to be recalibrated and realigned, to be transformed, to be blessed, so that you are truly that angel in the making. In this, you will know the journey that you must be on, that God has designated for you and why that is so. For as you understand yourself, as you come to understand how God made you and for what purpose and reason, then the fulfilment comes in the knowledge and understanding of these things embedded within your soul, seeking to be awakened and seeking to be transformed, a vitality that comes that will be insurmountable in terms of negative forces trying to put out the light. You will be a light that grows brighter with each day. In that light will come wisdom that grows deeper with each day. In that wisdom comes knowledge and understanding of yourself. In that understanding comes more of a desire for the inflowing of God's Love. In that inflowing of God's Love, so you are changed, transformed and awakened and understanding, true and deep, will come to you.

My beloved friends, there is still so much to discover, to come to know and see, feel and express. You are a work in progress, as has often been said. Yet, this progress is magnificent. It is only in one direction and that is to a higher level of expression. It comes step by step. You come to accept, embrace and acknowledge your own progression and the truth that comes with this. You come to love yourself in a greater way. You come to love others, to have compassion, to bring God's mercy, God's healing Touch, God's comfort of Love to another. You will grow and come to understand many things. You are continuing the journey and we are with you, as you well know. Each of you will have your own opportunity, your own way of knowing our presence and communicating with us. It comes often subtly at first, but it will grow, I promise you. It will grow and you will come to be that beautiful, beautiful light that you are meant to be. We will come to know each other as good friends, good friends indeed.

My beloved, beloved brothers and sisters, we are all in the Celestial Kingdom very happy that you have come together in the way that you are, that you discuss not trivial matters but important matters, that you come and speak to each other in tones of love, acceptance, reverence and acknowledgement of each other and the beauty that God has created, the beautiful souls that you are. So it is given, these gifts, this opportunity and these blessings. You are in the light, my brethren. We are in the light together and shall be blessed accordingly.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Andrew and I love you. I acknowledge you. I see you, I truly see you, and I love you. God bless you, beloved souls. God bless you.
