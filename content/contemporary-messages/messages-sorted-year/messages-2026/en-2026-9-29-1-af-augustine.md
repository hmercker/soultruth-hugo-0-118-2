# Search Deeply Within to Determine Whether We Are Ready for What is Coming

September 29<sup>th</sup>, 2026

State College Retreat, State College, PA, USA

Augustine received by Al Fike

The power of love to unite, to bring harmony and joy and all the goodness and sweetness of life, the power of God's Love is all these things. I am your teacher, Augustine and I am very pleased to see the harmony here in this Circle of Light, how you conduct yourselves with one another, with grace, affection, reverence and love. Would it not be wonderful that the entire world would be in this frame of behaviour, thinking and soulful expression? We continue, we persist in joining you together and showing you the way that is of love.

It is not that we have created these conditions for you, rather you have created these conditions from your soul for one another. You have made the effort to be loving, kind and generous and this is a beautiful expression of light in your world. Keep in mind that there are many spirits surrounding you and watching you and they are learning from your example. They are coming to know that there is great possibility when the soul is developed in God's Love and you, beloved souls, just begin your journey. What may your journey look like 100 years from now, 1000 years from now? How will you be together?

Will it be the joyousness of the Celestial Kingdom for you, for all of you and your loved ones, for all you may bring into the fold of this great gift of Love? This is our goal. To awaken you and to encourage you in the process of awakening others, we spend a great deal of time with you, my beloveds. We put a great deal of effort into your progression, into your awakening and transformation. If you knew the effort that was made, you would be humbled and grateful. What is most important, however, is the effort that you make for yourself.

How often are you with your Creator? How often do you seek out the blessing of God's Love? How often are you thinking of these things? How often is your desire to be within the grace of God in your consciousness, in your daily life? You all make great effort. This is not to be challenged.

You are all sincerely dedicated to this path, but the times of change and challenge are upon you and it is very important indeed that you make great effort at this time. For the lingering conditions of your world, the darkness, the fog of ignorance and the lack of consideration and love for this beautiful planet that you inhabit, all of these things conspire to bring about the changes and rectification of conditions that are needed in order that humanity may flourish with a flourishing world. God relies upon each one of you to be His instruments to bring truth, to be an example of love for your brothers and sisters. Much effort is made, and has been made, on your part and on ours to reconcile you with God and with the truth. It is time, my brethren, my beautiful students, to step out into the world and be that shining light to bring the truth, even though the truth may bring to you rejection. It is time to stand for the truth.

For without truth in this world, that vacuum is filled with pain, error and lack of love. Not because humanity desires this. It is because humanity, through its lack of attention toward soul, toward God, toward truth, toward light, has allowed their baser instincts and desires to creep in and fill that void. You must be that shining example. You must show those who are willing to listen and to see the way to clearing the smoke and darkness within them and bring the joy that you feel, the love and light that you have, the awareness of God, the true awareness that is thriving within your souls and your consciousness.

The time is now, my brethren, to act, to be and to show the way. What better time is there than now? What greater need is there than what is needed at this time? There is much work to do, my brethren. There is much work to do indeed. Search your souls.

Ask yourselves, am I ready? Do I truly wish to be God's instruments? Am I truly in alignment with God's Will? Do I even have a faint inkling of God's plan for the salvation of humanity? These are important questions to ask yourselves, beloved and beautiful students. For now is the time for action.

It is the time to truly commit, to be true to the very purpose that God has put within your souls and that purpose realised will bring to you great joy and great fulfilment. What is your purpose, beloved souls, do you know it? Do you feel it within you, within every part of you? These are questions to ask and to ruminate upon. For without clarity and a commitment, how is action put into the world? How are you to be God's instruments in the world?

You have the ultimate say. You are free to choose and so we encourage you to choose wisely and to act in harmony with God's Will. These are the things that are most needed to be addressed when you come together in this way. I encourage you to speak of these things, to take your turn around this circle and express truly and sincerely your thoughts on these matters that are of great importance.

I leave you, beloved students, beautiful lights, to be in that great crucible of light, of awareness, of growing, growing truth. Together, you are in this place, given this opportunity to truly sound the depths of your souls and understand truly who you are and what that relationship to God and God's creation is. How does that manifest in your life, how are you with God? How is God with you? How are you as God's instruments in the world?

May God bless you, beloved souls, with wisdom, grace, love and peace. God bless you. I am your teacher, Augustine and I come once again to help you to see, to feel and to know from that deep place within that God has given you, that God provides for you to truly learn, grow and function within the Laws of Love.

God bless you all. God bless you.
