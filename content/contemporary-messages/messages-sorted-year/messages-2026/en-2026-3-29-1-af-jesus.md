# The Time Is Now: Step Forward as a Light in the World

March 29<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Thank you, my beloved brother. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you this day, to be your companion in prayer, as are many, many Celestial spirits who are with you in this prayer. For as you pray in numbers, so you add the light to the world that is so needed. You are part of God's intention to bring many blessings to your world. As you pray together and as you receive this great gift of Love promised, as you are blessed in many ways, so you become a channel of light for many. God is able to utilise this light to bring comfort and peace, healing and love to many others. When you pray for the world, you are acting as God's agents in the world. For intention is important. Desire to serve is necessary. Most of all, as that great gift of Love is lodged within your soul and continues to expand therein, then the capacity and power of your gifts, your prayers, your light is magnified and can be used in many ways.

My beloveds, the time is now for you to share this truth with many. The time is now, as God's plan is unfolding for the salvation of humanity. I ask you, my beloveds, do you elect to be an instrument for God's plan and intention? Have you prepared yourself accordingly? Do you consider yourself a light in the world? Are you cognisant of the Truth of Divine Love and can you demonstrate this to others? These are the criteria necessary to be a teacher of these truths. You do not have to come from high places. You do not need serious education. What is most important is humility and to carry the grace of God with you at all times, this light, this blessing that brings peace and comfort to others. This is what God wishes of you as His channels of love.

Are you prepared in this way? If you are not, I want you to ask yourselves, what holds me? What is it that makes me reluctant to be that channel of love and light in the world? Is it fear, my brethren? Is it judgement? Is it a sense that you are inadequate? My beloveds, the power of this Love will conquer all fear, will wipe away all barriers and will show you how you might love yourself and live in the way that is in harmony with love. These things come with this blessing. They activate many blessings on your behalf and they become the ways in which God may work and express these blessings through you.

Have faith, my beloveds. Have faith in the power of Love that is burning within your souls. Have faith in God's plan for the salvation of humanity. Have faith in your capacity as God's instruments in the world. Be strong in love. Choose love with every breath. You know that feeling of love. You have experienced that blessing of Love. You know that it is something unique, powerful and beautiful. Seek this Love with every day. Seek to be within the flow, the blessing, the Light of this Love. You will have all that is required to be that powerful instrument, that agent of God, so that others may benefit from your light and the truth that you know and all that is within you and about you.

Many souls will be drawn, my beloveds. They will be drawn to the light. You will find yourself in unanticipated places and conditions where God will utilise your gifts and together, you will bring blessings and peace. Many surprises are coming, my beloveds, opportunities of service, situations of darkness that may be turned to light with your presence within it. There is much work to do, my beloveds. I beseech you, I urge you, I ask you to seek in your prayers that you may be God's agent of love, light and truth, to ask for this to be so. In this high prayer and desire, you will draw many angels to you. As you grow in the Light of God's Love, so the capacities and possibilities are realised and expressed in wondrous ways.

Each of you may be a powerful channel for God. God does not select, nor does He favour His channels of love. More so, it is you, my beloveds, that choose to be that channel. When that choice is made, firm, strong and unmovable, then the work begins through you, my beloveds, and in accordance to your gifts and your situations. That work will be tailored to your abilities and situations. There is nothing to fear here, my beloveds. There is nothing to concern yourself with, merely to feel the joy and anticipation of what may come through you for the benefit of others and that will be demonstrated. I assure you that that will be demonstrated. The proof of your gifts and capacities as God's agents of change and light will be given to you and there will be no doubt nor confusion. There will be a steadfastness, a solidity of faith and trust that will grow and will be the cornerstone to the work you carry forth for God as my disciples, as my harbingers of the Truth of God's Love. You will go forth and you will be protected, guided and inspired.

My beloveds, walk with me in the world and help God and the angels to bring light where there is darkness, desperation and deprivation. There is so much work to do, my beloveds. Do not hold back. Walk at a steady pace with faith and strength, fortitude and desire to serve. God will answer your prayers. God will open the way. The doorways to service will be evident and you will know, feel and see it for what it is and the blessings that will come. My love is with each one of you. I love you so, for you are my brethren. I wish for many more to gather, many more to know this truth and to experience it. I wish for you to convey this message of truth to others. As you come together in Circles of Light, many will experience the blessings, the power of it, the wonderment of God's Touch through the Holy Spirit upon their souls, that His Love will be conveyed abundantly and clearly upon the penitent soul.

God bless you. My beloveds. Be God's instruments and my disciples. You will benefit from a life fulfilled and filled with promise, joy and experiences beyond what you may comprehend at this time. What will come will be a blessing to you, my beloveds, a powerful blessing indeed. For the instrument is blessed as those whom he ministers to shall be blessed. All is given in love and the power of that love to transform and heal will be conveyed in many ways. God bless you, my beloveds, I am your brother and your friend. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Heavens. I bring my message to encourage you forward in the work ahead. God bless you, my beloveds. I am with you every step of the way. I am with you. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you in His Love.
