# Striding Forward Together: Fulfilling God's Plan

October 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

**Note:** This message was received during a special one-off convening of the community on a Zoom call after Jesus’s recent awakened state messages received by Maureen Cardoso.

**Jesus:** I come, my beloveds, your brother and friend, Jesus. I am here. You all know the story of the first parents who were given the choice to receive this great gift of the Father's Love and they chose not to. That great door, that great opportunity, was closed until I came upon the Earth and opened that door once again. I talked about this Love, this gift. Those around me received it well and began to teach this Truth to many. Yet, in time, this Truth was obscured. Though the door was not closed, it was not open as much as when I came. Now I see that the door will open some more, a greater opening for humanity, that they may know of this opportunity to receive the great gift from God, His Love.

I ask you, I beseech each one of you, my beloved brothers and sisters, to toil. Toil toward the ends that God has put before us to fling open that door, to open the Truth of the Father's Love, that all may know it and make that choice. For there is no greater choice in one's existence than this choice to receive the great gift. Each of you is called upon. Each of you is given the opportunity because you have accepted this gift. It is for you to demonstrate, to show your brothers and sisters what is possible, what can be given, what the soul is capable of, what each individual is in the creation of God and its potential, your potential, to become fulfilled in this great gift of Love.

Our beloved daughter has demonstrated a gift and you all acknowledge the power and the beauty of that gift. My desire is to come to each one of you, those who have witnessed it and those who have not in the flesh, but that I might come, that you may experience a moment with me and know my love for you. For this too is a demonstration of the Truth and the power of God's Love. My desire and my hope for the future is that all may be touched, not only you who are here, but all of humanity may be touched and awakened to the possibility of the Father's Love and its power to heal, to transform, to awaken the souls of humanity.

This is our goal. This is God's mandate that He has given to all of us. Indeed, listen. Listen to that, your heart and your beating soul. Listen to that crying out within that says, *"I must be a part of this. I must be a part of You, dear God, in that way that only can be through Your Love and that in this might I serve You and serve humanity and may the angels come close and the light shine on all of us in the coming days."*

Yes, the world has changed and will continue to change. Great change is upon you, my beloveds. May that change wash over you in the Pentecostal experience of the Father's Love. May it change you so radically, so powerfully, that you will abandon all reticence in sharing the joy and wonderment of this gift, that you may walk in the world as a true light and a light of truth for all to see. This is my hope. This is all of our hope.

The world must change. The world must be healed. The world must find harmony and balance. God relies upon us all, my beloveds, to extend our loving arms toward our brothers and sisters, to uphold, to grab their hands and say, *"I love you and I am here for you."* This is what hope is. This is what hope means.

My beloveds, walk with me. Walk with God. Walk with your angel friends and all the others of light, so that we may change the world as God's instruments, that we may do so with humility, grace, light and joy. It is up to us now, my beloveds. God has demonstrated to you the power of His Love in many ways and so you know its power. You know its glory and its blessings.

Grasp all the truth that you can, take all the Love that you are capable of, stride forward with great faith and you will truly be God's instruments to help bring the world back to peace and harmony, where the power of love may truly transform everything. My beloveds, I am with you. I want you to know this, that I love you and I will make myself known to each one of you through many ways and instruments and situations. It shall be. This is my dedication to you, my promise.

We shall all work diligently to fulfil God's plan for the salvation of humanity. God bless you, my beloveds. I love you. I am Jesus and I am with you always and evermore. God bless you.
