# Acknowledge the Power of God’s Blessings Upon You

June 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Indeed, may God's blessings continue to befall upon you, my friends. I am Orion. God's blessings are great. They are everywhere. They make this universe operate in harmony. They bring humanity into light, if they so choose. God continues to bless all those who are receptive and open to that blessing and the many blessings that come. Indeed, God continues to ensure that this world of yours strives toward harmony, even though, from the onset, humanity chose otherwise. As you go about your daily life, do you not see how the blessings of the Creator are with you? Indeed, even though the world seems contrary to harmony, you are guided toward harmony. You are given the opportunity to see what will bring harmony. Your souls long for harmony and they long for the great gift of His Love, for the key to harmony is love. Indeed, you are striving to be an example, an expression of love. Thus, you continue on your sojourn of seeking truth, of seeking to know your true self, seeking to know the universe as it truly is created and the universe of God as God is in that universe.

My beloved friends, there is much that contradicts your path. There are many obstructions upon it. Yet, God lifts off those obstructions that are great, leaving you to toil and lift those that are small. Together, you clear the path, you walk upon the road toward greater harmony, light and love. It is your efforts that are recognised by God, for God indeed supports and uplifts you in these efforts. He sends angels into your midst, sends beautiful stellar friends, bright spirits, so much, so many who are supportive, who are with you and protect you, inspire you, guide you and bless you.

So much is given by God and so many blessings continue to flow from God. Acknowledge this, my beautiful friends, for it is in that relationship with the Creator of the universe that you will find what you are seeking, that your souls will be nourished in the way that they are in need of nourishment, that your consciousness will grow in a way that will encourage and create love, wisdom and joy. All the goodness that light brings, my friends, is given to you, not so much because of your efforts, though they are appreciated, but it is God's efforts to open the doors, to awaken you, to transform you with His Love, all of the Divine Touch upon you, that is the great impetus. It creates greater harmony within you and greater love growing, fomenting within you.

This is all from the Divine, my friends. We acknowledge this. All beings in the universe who are advanced acknowledge this, for it is the Source of this advancement and progression. It is not the mind as you upon the Earth think of it. It is the mind of the soul, expanded, transformed and cleansed, that brings the insights, the perceptions, the abilities and the gifts that allow true progression and true advancement in terms of your civilisation and technologies. All the aspects of human endeavour, when touched by the Divine, create harmony and create advancement that is truly a benefit not only to humanity, but to your world.

This is the missing piece that so many do not realise. They separate what they call *"religion"* from science, from the human condition, etc. All these things are boxed up into different concepts and different paradigms. Yet, when the soul is opened and the perceptions within come to be realised, there is an understanding that all is connected and that all can be interconnected into a great lattice of understanding of truth, of action, of invention, of creativity and of all the many possibilities and potentials of humanity coming together, not by analysis, not by the mental faculties, but by the soul awareness, the mind of the soul.

We teach you this, my beloved friends, above all things, that the soul must be fed by God and the soul awakened by God's Love is the key to true advancement and true progression of humanity. This is the great plan, my friends, to show humanity the way out of the dilemmas that it has created, out of the conditions that are so severe, out of the pain, lack of joy and love that is so rampant in your world. This is the way forward, seeking this great gift of Love, the key to everything, the key that opens the gates to true understanding, true knowledge, true advancement and progression. It is all at your feet, my friends. You understand this. You are striving toward it. Indeed, God blesses you upon that path so beautifully, so wonderfully, that you do not even see a fraction of what is given and what has been blessed upon you and in your life. Yet, as you grow, you will see more, appreciate more, find more joy and find more understanding of how God loves you so truly, so thoroughly, so beautifully and so expansively. That Love is great.

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion and I love you. My love is merely a tiny, infinitesimal fraction of the Love of God, but I possess what He has given me and so I express this to you and all the universe. It is glorious in its expression, for I am with God in alignment, in harmony and resplendent in His Love. God bless you, my beloved friends. You too will find your way and be as I am and so many who have found the key. You will be this light. You will know this Love and the power and wonderment of it. It is given freely. It is given in accordance to your desires. God bless you all. Orion loves you.
