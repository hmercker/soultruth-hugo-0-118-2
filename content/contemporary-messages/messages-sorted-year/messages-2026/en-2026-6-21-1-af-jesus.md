# Prayer, Divine Love, and Being God’s Instruments in the World

June 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you. I come to be with my precious and beautiful brothers and sisters. I come because I love you. I come because I am my Heavenly Father's instrument, channel of love for all the peoples of the world and you have invoked my presence in prayer and desire. The laws are obeyed and I am a part of those laws, as are all the angels of heaven, to work diligently, to be with you, to uphold you, to love you and to bring blessings to you, for this is how the instrumentality of heaven works. We are all part of a great flow of the intention of God to heal and uplift all souls who are seeking these gifts and blessings.

I am here, as are many. Each of you has an angel with you. Each of you is being blessed at this very moment. I beseech you to open your souls wide to the great blessing from the Heavenly Father, His blessing of Love that comes with the Holy Spirit. We are together, unified in prayer. We are truly as one in the gift and Truth of the Father's Love. Seek this with all your heart and soul. There is no greater gift and no greater blessing. Indeed, as you walk forward in the world, exercise the leadership that is within you, that soul wisdom, love and ability to walk in the world as God's instrument.

For the world is in need of you, my beloveds. The world requires guidance, leadership and light, the firm but gentle touch of love, expressed in all the multitude of ways that are a part of this gathering, each bringing their unique gifts, each able to utilise those gifts to bring greater light, harmony and peace to the world, to bring the Truth of the Father's Love to all who are willing to listen. As such, you are my disciples. As such, you are God's instruments. The great journey continues, my beloveds, the journey toward peace and light, harmony and love, not only within the individual, but within this world of yours and within all the worlds of spirit, so that in time there is a unity of light that is part of your world and continues in all worlds. This is God's plan to unify humanity in light, in truth, in love, in harmony and in peace.

My prayer is such that all will know this. Indeed, when you walk out into the world and bring this blessing forward, whether that be by your presence, by your words or all the gifts that you possess, you do my work. You do the work of God and you further God's plan for the salvation of humanity. It is for you to choose each and every day the opportunities that God lays before you and the possibilities that are set in your life. For there are many possibilities and many doors opened. How you respond, how you elect to walk through those doors, is entirely of your decision and desire. Indeed, if you feel inadequate or incapable, pray. Ask God to guide you and uphold you, to protect you and strengthen you, to envelop you with His Love. In this way, you will have all that you require to walk forward in light and to be God's instruments for that particular time and circumstance.

There are many opportunities waiting for each one of you. You are beautiful lights and you often do not understand or recognise the potential, the possibilities, the strength and gifts that you carry. As you continue to receive the gift of the Father's Love, so these things are strengthened. They expand with this gift. You are changed, healed and made brighter in spirit by this gift of Love. My beloved disciples, brethren, brothers and sisters, we walk together on a path of service, on a journey of awakening and transformation, a journey that shall never end, a journey that is magnificent and of the highest. My beloveds, we are truly God's children. Shall we express ourselves in that light and that truth with every breath?

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus and I am with you in love. God bless you.
