# The Law of Rapport and Communication and Preparing for Times Ahead

June 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Seretta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Seretta Kem and I am happy to step forward to bring a message that supports you on your Path of Love Divine, continuing in your awakening as an instrument for God to bring blessings of Love, light and encouragement through to your brothers and sisters, being a channel of love not only for your brothers and sisters, but for Mother Earth herself. She too requires those who are acting in harmony because it helps to support her and her recovery, coming into balance in the way that God intended for your earthly home to be.

The message that I want to share with you today is one that I hope will help you to understand the importance of working together with your angels, all guided by God. For in the times ahead, the changes that are unfolding now, but will further unfold in the future, will require each one to be able to perceive their guidance, each one to be able to be a light in the world. For there may be a time where you will not be able to gather as you do in the present day. Because of this, we want you to be able to stand strong and steady, stable in your communication with God and in your prayers, continuing to have your prayers be your focal point where God’s Love can flow into your soul and continue your transformation and awakening. You will need us, beloved souls, in this time. The Law of Rapport and Communication requires that you have a level of God’s Love within your soul that will enable us to become close to you. We will be able to communicate with you, to inspire you, to bring visions to you for even the smallest thing that may concern you.

My encouragement is that going forward, you consider yourself in a great preparation for the times ahead. Do not wait until these times are upon you to prepare spiritually. What you do to prepare for your physical means is up to you, but in order for us to be able to be close to you and communicate directly to you, your souls require Divine Love. Many already are able to perceive our presence. They can sense the atmospheric change as we come close. They are aware of the condition that we bring, which allows you to be in a condition within yourself that makes it easier to open your soul and to allow God’s Love in. Yet, not many are able to perceive our words, perceive the guidance as we would like, so that in the coming times, you will be well supported.

At present within your community, your family of Divine Love, it can be that you lean into the communications that we can bring through those mediums that we can speak with and through. While this serves its purpose, it does not fulfil the law with each individual for our rapport and communication to be close in future times. In your prayers, contemplate whether you actually wish for us to be close and to be able to communicate with you. What is your desire? As your desires are pure and you are willing to communicate with the angels and have them close, then God can fulfil this desire. Yet, if you are not necessarily drawn to make this effort, this is a choice that you can make, but you must understand that it will limit our ability to be able to guide you, should there be a circumstance that you are in. We long to be able to come close to you, and it is through the blessing of God’s Love, the desire within your soul and your willingness to have us close that communication becomes possible.

Now, communicating does not always mean communicating in this form of trance mediumship. No, there are many other ways that we can communicate with you. It is for you to ask and to be willing to be open to the ways in which we can come close and communicate through your soul and your mind. Most importantly, prepare. Prepare yourself in the best way that you can for the times ahead. I do not say this to generate fear within you. No, I say this to encourage you to be prepared and one cannot be overly prepared for such circumstances that are ahead.

My friends, pray for God’s Love. Seek within you your desire. Do you want us to be able to communicate with you when the times ahead shall not afford you to be able to sit in circles like you do now? Do you want us close? Do you want your rapport with God to be strong? If this is the case, then let us in. Let God in. Allow your souls to be developed in such a way that the rapport and communication will be possible.

I am Seretta Kem, here to guide you, encourage you and inspire you on your progression of spiritual awakening in Divine Love. God bless you all.
