# Receiving Divine Love Makes You At-One with God’s Will

May 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Ann received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Ann of the Celestial Heavens. I have been here for some time. You do not know me nor my history, but I am one of those redeemed souls, redeemed in God's grace and Love. I come today to bring you joyous news of your upliftment, of your progress and the Father's Will. As you become redeemed by the inflowing of this Love into your souls, you may reflect upon the changes within your souls. You may also give thanks to God for the blessings bestowed upon your soul. This is a good beginning when communing with God, our Heavenly Father, our protector, our giver of Love and life.

His Will comes to you. You need only look deeply within your souls to feel the Love that He has given to you, to know the Truth of His Love for you. This, my dear children, is the acknowledgement that you are progressing, that you are transforming, that His Will is being fulfilled by this transformation of your souls. The gift you are being given is sacred and makes you at one with His Will. Is it not clear, given the proper understanding, that God’s Love has no boundaries, no limitations, that it can remove from you those things that create great weight upon your souls? With the enlightening of your soul, you beam forth light, joy, love and the Truth of God's Love for you.

This is to be shared the best way you can with your brothers and sisters, if only through a smile or understanding, a look, in touching one's hand in a prayer. As you give your love, you brighten another soul into recognition that there is something more available to them, something more to reach for, something to search for. Your kindness, your love, your blessing, it is all one in this Love. As with any celebration, you share, you share. With this Love, there is much to be celebrated, so go forth in celebration of this Love. These are not duties. These are joy, joyful exchanges. This is the way it is with God, this Love is love expressed.

I am so happy to be able to come here today and share this with you and share my love with you. It is an honour and a privilege to be with you, my brothers and sisters in this prayer, as God blesses you and all of us who are here in this Circle of Light, bringing more light into this world that is in such need of light. I honour you, brothers and sisters, for the work that you do, for the joy that you express, for the happiness and love from your souls touching your brothers’ and sisters' souls. I honour you and acknowledge you as one of Jesus's followers, disciples and lovers of God. I am your sister, Ann. I thank you for listening and I touch each one of you with my love. May God continue to bless you throughout your day and night.
