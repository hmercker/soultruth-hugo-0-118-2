# What Is A Humble Soul

May 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Starpoint Circle of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Faith Nyquist received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Faith, happy to join you as you come together to discuss, to pray, to receive and to rejoice all things related to the soul, Divine Love, our Heavenly Father and the path of progression. It is remarkable to hear what comes from you in your awareness, in your discussions. What is a humble soul? Such a powerful topic. Ultimately, a humble soul is one that knows they cannot do the ultimate acts of love and kindness, the ultimate displays of this, without God. For when the Substance of God comes into the soul and the transformation of what was once inharmonious transforms into harmony, the complete life of this soul is changed. They walk a new path.

A soul that is strong in humility always seeks the Father, always seeks the Love of the Father, always seeks to be guided by the Father, listening, longing and yearning for wisdom. For as the soul grows in Divine Love and humility begins to weave through its demeanour, it recognises and sees through its consciousness that the Source of all is through the Creator. The Source of all goodness, of all harmony, comes through the power of God. A humble soul is one that allows others to be as they are, yet can still love them, can still appreciate and accept them. It does not need them to be as they are, for they understand that they too are on their own path to at-onement with God. Even if that soul is one that does not pray, they are still walking their path, where eventually, at some point upon their journey, they too will turn to God. They too will turn to love, because love is the eternal frequency of creation and eventually love can permeate all things.

A humble soul is one that, when they see a need and they are able to fill that need, they do so without being asked. They see what is before them and they feel that nudge from within them that says, *"I can help here,"* no matter how small the task. They allow themselves to meet the need that is before them. How is it that this soul continues to grow? How is it that this soul continues to progress? I ask this question now. What is your inward answer? It is love, beloveds, to receive the grace of God's Love that will transform all things that are not in harmony to harmony, the blending and the merging, the camaraderie, all of God's creation working together in perfect harmony. This is what God has created, all things, all things working in harmony. When you see disharmonies in the world, say a prayer, say a prayer for God to bless the situation, for His Love and His condition to come into the scenario to shower its Light, because this is what you can do and a humble soul does what it can do.

God is not asking you to save the world. God is asking you to come into harmony, to come into harmony within you, that universe that is within you, to be harmonious within your own being. You will see the reflection in your daily lives, in your material world, of who and what you are inside. Know that God's Love within your soul is transforming everything. Remain humble, remain peaceful, remain compassionate with yourselves and others as you continue along your journey. Pray deeply, beloved souls. Pray for that Pentecostal inflowing on this great day of Pentecost. I shall sit with you and pray, empowering you and your soul to reach more deeply toward God, to open and be receptive to a powerful inflowing, each one.

I am Faith. Thank you for hearing me, receiving me and allowing me to convey this little message from humility, humility and love. God bless you.
