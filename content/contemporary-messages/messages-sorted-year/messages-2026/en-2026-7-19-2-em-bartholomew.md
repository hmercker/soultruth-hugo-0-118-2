# Pray For the Seekers

July 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Bartholomew received by Elizabeth Mack

Blessings to you, brothers and sisters. Blessings from God Almighty, who even now looks down upon you with such Love and such care. As we raise you higher to brighter, more loving, more gentle places in God's universe, as we lift you higher, your souls open wider in this safe, loving environment. Your souls open wide and you drink up the Love that continuously pours from God's Soul. I am your brother Bartholomew and as promised, I come to you today with open arms for all of my brothers and sisters upon the Earth as they struggle to transform and transition from such negativity to the Love that the Father has for them, that they may become whole, beautiful expressions of love to walk upon the Earth and share their enthusiasm, their life and their light with those who are still seeking.

More and more of those souls are finding their way, in part because of your prayers, in part because of their own soul's longings. For many pray for them. Many angels and many high spirits pray for the souls upon the Earth to seek out God and the Truth of His Love and that is you too, dear children, in this circle, as well as many other Circles of Light and other Divine Love souls upon the Earth, with whom you are unacquainted at this time in your material aspect. Many prayers are offered for seeking souls and we would encourage you in your daily prayers to continue to pray for those seeking souls that they find the Truth of God's Love, whether it be through your expression of it directly to them or through their own souls’ desiring, seeking and longing for God's Love. Pray for these souls that they may come to this path, this Divine Love path that brings them closer to God and transforms them into true, loving children of the Father.

Much of this work was done earlier today, when the great Master came and extended his arms in your world and shared his glory and his love and blessings from the Father, extended to the entire world and even beyond. Such magnitude, such beauty, such truth, such glory, such love and still he progresses, as we all do. For the Father, being the source of all love, has so much more to give.

Open up wide, dear children, wider than ever before. Desire, long for the Love. Let your souls express what you truly feel for your Heavenly Father. Let your souls express all that they are as they come closer to the source of all love, that you may be blessed as much as possible, as fully as possible. For the Father has no limits to what He will give to you if you let go of your own limitations and allow all that He has for you to come into your souls, to be enlivened, to be brought forward. Open widely and allow the great gift of the Father's Love to enter therein.

God bless you. I am Bartholomew and I drink of the Father's Love with you.
