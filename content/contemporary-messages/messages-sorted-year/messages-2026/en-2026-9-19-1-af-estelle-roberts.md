# Explore Higher Truths Beyond Psychic Knowledge

September 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Acton Spiritualist Chruch Retreat, Acton, UK

Estelle Roberts received by Al Fike

Greetings to you from spirit. I am Estelle Roberts, one who spurred on the spiritualist movement in the past, who now resides in a beautiful place in the spirit world. There is a missing piece in the spiritual movement of late and this piece has been missing for some time now. It is your relationship with the Creator, with God. There is much talk, many conversations and experiences that revolve around your relationship with the spirit world and this is a good thing.

Many of you have been instrumental in helping others to understand that the spirit world is a real and existing reality. Yet, is it not the progression of the soul that is most important? The progression of the spirit, the continuation of the spirit body, is certainly a truth to be shared and I commend all of you for doing so. Yet, it is the soul that fuels and is an integral part of the spirit body. The health and well-being of the soul is not particularly focused upon in your teachings.

I put a proposal forward to you, my beautiful friends. Think about your soul. Think about the well-being, the health of your soul. We all know our souls exist. We exist because of our souls. I would encourage you to add the truth that these dear souls have explained and shared with you today to your teachings. Is it such a problem to add this truth to what you have and will share with others? I say not. I say it completes the picture. It adds that piece of the puzzle that is most important. For soul progression is the determining factor of an individual's progression in the spirit world as well as in the material world.

One can explore and understand all the elements of the psychic and this is giving knowledge pertaining to these fields, but one cannot truly grow with psychic knowledge alone. One must continue to endeavour to explore their whole selves. Who are they in the universe? Indeed, who is God in the universe? When you explore these questions and find your answers, you will come to the realisation that love is the most potent force in the universe and love will heal all that is out of harmony in the universe.

The most potent love of all is God's Love. That graciousness of God's Touch brings peace, healing, comfort, joy and truth. I encourage you to go to the source. Reach for the highest, my friends. Reach for that which will truly nourish all of you, not just a part of you, but all that you are.

Indeed, when you start with the very core of your being, which is your soul, then you start with a good beginning. You will indeed find yourself awakening to truths and experiences that are truly revolutionary and revelatory. Those of you who continue to lead and teach in the spiritualist movement in this country, know that we are with you and that we encourage you to continue to explore and understand higher truths and to be in greater light, to express love in all that you do, to put aside the mental conditions, the judgements, fears and all that is part of human existence. For when you are leaders, when you wish to be examples, when you continue to provide others with truth in whatever way that is manifest in your gifts and your work, you must strive to be above the human condition, create and express something that inspires and leads others forward.

Your example is most important. Your light is most important, beloved friends. Understand the Will of God. For when you can do so, your life will take a journey that reflects that Will. It is not that you are giving up something. Rather, you are gaining the universe in your life. The Divine Touch will bring so much into your consciousness, into your experience, into your spiritual life that you will wonder why you had not aspired toward these things earlier in your life. Yet, there is no better time than now, beloved friends, to step forward and to be in alignment with God. For then you truly become a teacher. You truly are a light.

Those whom you encounter will benefit greatly from these things. Of course, you, my friends, will benefit the most. May you be blessed on your journey. May you continue to open to truth. May you find the power of love working within you and around you and may God truly bless you.

I am Estelle. I am here to encourage you. We, our great band of spirits of light, will be here at your disposal with each one of you as you continue to seek and grow in truth. This is our mission, our commitment. This is our love for you and what you are doing in the world.

We shall journey together, my friends, blessed souls, all of you. May you continue to find your way upon the path of truth. Thank you for listening to me and may God bless you.
