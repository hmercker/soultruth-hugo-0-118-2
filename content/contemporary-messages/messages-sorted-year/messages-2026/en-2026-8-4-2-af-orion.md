# Be Free of Your Own Human Condition to Become a True Agent of God

August 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

The consequences of human endeavour are quite plain to see, is it not, my friends? As fires rage and heat builds, conditions worsen and people suffer. Yet, through all of these conditions, humanity cannot look at how it is compliant and complicit with creating these conditions. Thus, the conditions will worsen. The turmoil and the changes will come, not because God wills it, but because humanity creates it. You will all be swept up within these conditions, my beloved friends, these conditions that continue to worsen.

Yet, there is light at the end of this tunnel. In order for change to happen, there must be some crisis in this world, not because this is the law, but because humanity has created the circumstances that require these things. Each has his free will. Each is able to make choices. Indeed, collectively, those choices could certainly neutralise these difficult conditions. Yet, this is not the way of humanity. Each goes about their own business, each not considering the collective that is humanity, but often feeling victimised by conditions that are unfavourable, by earth changes that continue to devastate, rock the world, bring flames and heat and change the oceans and the waters. The animals suffer greatly. Much continues to build upon itself, a momentum that seems unstoppable.

Yet you, my friends, are continuing with faith and are continuing to make effort to try and stop the momentum of this darkness that overtakes the world. This is why we work with you and so many others, because you are dedicated to being God's servants. Yes, you are imperfect. Yes, there are ways in which you contribute to these conditions that are dark because you are participating in the material world and whatever your cultures, societies, governments etc. dictate. This is a very difficult position to be in, for you are asked to participate in error and you recognise this. Yet, it is difficult to extract and extricate yourself from these conditions. These dilemmas continue to play out in your lives. God does not punish you for these errors, for the errors are, for the most part, the result of humanity punishing itself. Indeed, as you open, your perceptions widen. As you continue to see through the eyes of your souls, you will see how entrapped humanity is in the momentum of its own making and the conditions of its own doing.

This is where you are important, channels of love, examples of light and truth. As you quietly go about your business like all others, are you truly doing that work? Are you a part of the change or complicit with the darkness? It is not that it is black and white. It is often grey. Partly you are involved and partly you are not. Yet, you continue to seek to be in harmony with God. That harmony builds as you receive more of the Divine Love in your souls as you continue to awaken and grow in that Love. The inevitable must happen to break free of the compliance of the mind, to truly be in harmony and in relationship with the wisdom and understanding of the soul and all that God brings to you in that understanding and the blessings of this Love.

My beloved friends, it is not an easy task to break away from these powerful currents of thought and deed that are swirling about the world. You see the Earth suffering. You see many people suffering as a result. I am sure that you must feel somewhat hopeless. For the power of these changes, the power of this suffering is great, far beyond what any one of you or any collective of you can truly do. It must play out in its own way. It must find its balance in its own way. My hope is that you will be in alliance with, connected to, aware of and, in your own way, make that decision to walk the path of truth. In such a quagmire of their arrogance and error, it is for you to understand and it is not easy. We realise this is not easy, not easy indeed. Yet, you persist. Your soul persists. God continues to bless you. In time, you will have the strength, perception and wisdom to follow the guidance that is required to make you an active agent of change, of healing, of bringing light into the world.

My beautiful friends, do not acquiesce. Do not feel hopeless or powerless. For in your soul and in that relationship with God, between your soul and God, there is a wondrous transformation happening, an empowerment that is beyond anything that humans and their feeble minds may accomplish. This is empowerment of the soul, bringing forth the wisdom, the love, the beauty, the wonderment and the light that is needed to instruct, to show the way to humanity as it continues to struggle and continues to be lost in its own wilful condition. It will show the way of putting aside that wilfulness, of putting aside that mindfulness, of putting aside the conditions, the anger, the frustrations and the paranoia. All of these conditions that are of the mind may be healed and may disappear from your consciousness. In its place is the great potency of love that will change everything within you. In time, you will change everything around you. This will be the great teaching example, the beautiful light that will shine, be blessed and will be the agent of change in the world.

My beloved friends, I know it is difficult to see how the Earth suffers, how humanity suffers, how those around you suffer, but you first must be free from your own suffering and ignorance and walk in the Truth of God's Love and truly be that active agent of change for God, the strength, the beauty, the wisdom and the love, manifesting in all its glorious ways in the world. We are here to guide you toward these ends, to be your support, your protection and to help you along the way. We continue, my friends. We continue.

I am Orion and I love you so. I feel for your world, your lives and all that is so much out of harmony and we continue to dedicate ourselves in service of bringing harmony and balance back to the world. We are God's active agents, as are many and they are all here at your disposal. They are all here to help. Take advantage of the blessings and what is available to you. Open your eyes, let the scales peel away, my friends, so that you may truly see. Yes, you will see the horrors, but you will also see the light and the joy. God bless you, my friends. Orion loves you truly. Orion loves you. God bless you.
