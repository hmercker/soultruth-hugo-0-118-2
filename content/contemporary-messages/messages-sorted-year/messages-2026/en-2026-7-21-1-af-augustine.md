# Do Not Hide from Your Feelings; Rather, God to Replace Disharmony with His Love

July 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. It is your teacher, Augustine. May your souls open like the wings of a butterfly, opening wide and revealing all the beauty within you, for there is great beauty within each soul. In this world of yours, it is difficult to be so open, revealing so much of your true self. Yet, this is what is required in such a world as this, for so many are closed from their true selves. So many do not understand who they are and what is within them. They are busy with their mindful applications and distractions, thinking this is what life is.

Unfortunately, this is how life has been created by humanity, not by God. God intended something very much different. God intended for all His beautiful children to be like those butterflies, open to love, expressing love, expressing the beauty within and indeed flying up into at-onement with God. This is God's intention and my hope is that it is yours. For when you are open in this way, when the feelings, the desires and the heart is revealed, and there is an intention to receive the great gift of God's Love, then all becomes more sensitised, more beautiful, transformed into something so utterly beautiful that it defies description.

I urge each of you, my beloved friends, beautiful students of truth, to continue to awaken to your true selves. Allow that vulnerability to shine forth. For when God's Love touches you deeply, there is a strength that comes, a capacity to withstand the onslaught of an unloving world and still be loving, still be that light and still be that beautiful butterfly. What holds you back are the fears of the mind. This is quite evident, not only in you but all of humanity. It is a current of fear that runs strong in the reality of this world. It is for you, my beloved friends, to counteract that current, to go against it and to be within the consciousness of soul. As that soul consciousness grows, then you will understand God's creation. You will be able to understand the cosmic expression that is God's creation. In this, you will be connected to all of humanity, not just the humanity of this world, but of all the worlds. You will know love for all, all of creation and all of humanity. That love will grow, strengthen and blossom.

Indeed, it is your choice each and every day to be like that butterfly, or to close yourself from these sensitivities, from this beauty that is within and to be like your brothers and sisters who go about their day, numbed and unable to truly feel. Yet, it is in the feeling, my beloved students, that you will find the rewards, the joy, the insights, the perceptions and the Love of God. It is in these feelings of vulnerability. At times, it is sadness. Other times, it is joy. Yet, in the feeling, as has been said, there is life. You acknowledge that you are alive, for God gave you the gift to feel. God gave you the gift to feel from your bodies, from your spirits and from your soul.

The expressions are always filtered through your minds. When your minds are pure and open, then the feelings become more intense and more beautiful. You will even appreciate the sadness. You will appreciate those feelings of all hues and colours that indeed reflect your awakening. As you are transformed by the Father's Love, the sadness disappears. There is no sadness, nor anger, nor all the other emotions that are not in harmony with the soul and with love. Yet, first, you must acknowledge these things. Feel them. Embrace them. When they are uncomfortable, when they are out of harmony with love, release them.

It is all part of the journey, my beloved friends. As you continue to grow, seek and awaken, so the feelings that you possess will change, will deepen and will be a beautiful expression of who you are. Those feelings are precious to God. Those feelings are part of God's creation. It is important to allow all that God created all that you are, to be expressed in the fullness of that creation. As you receive the Father's Love, the fullness will come. You will be truly a part of the children of God, acknowledge His creation in all the ways that you can and express yourself in all the ways that are possible, as that creation is honed, purified and then transformed by God's Love. My beloved students of truth, look within yourself. Have compassion for your feelings. Allow your feelings to be expressed. If they are dark or negative, put them at God's Feet. Say, *"This is how I am feeling, God. Allow me to be free of this. Put Your Love in its place. Help me to grow and be transformed in Your Love. May all that which is not of Your Love fall away as You continue to bless me."*

My beloved students, each of you is capable of releasing all conditions that are not in harmony with God's Love, but first you must acknowledge what is within, release that which is not in harmony and nurture that which is all part of the journey, my friends, my beautiful students, all part of what God's Love does for you, its healing, its transformation and the wonderment of this gift manifested in each one of you. Butterflies of all different hues and colours, each unique, beautiful winged creatures that you are. Fly into the Arms of God and know His embrace, His appreciation and Love. For He wishes to acknowledge His own creation and who you are in that firmament. He does and He will. In time, you will feel and know that acknowledgement. You will feel the joy and know the wonderment that is God.

God bless you, my beloved and beautiful students. Your teacher, Augustine, loves you and is here to help you grow, to break beyond the pupae of that which is the worldly conditions and become truly that butterfly that you are. I am here to assist, as are many, and we shall continue to do so. God bless you. God bless you all.
