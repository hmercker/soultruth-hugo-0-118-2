# Be Instruments of Peace Through Divine Love

March 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

The remedy of peace is with you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come on the heels of your prayers, dear daughter, the desires of each soul here and the desires of many souls upon the world. For peace is desired within each one. Yet, there is an allowance of darkness and destruction to prevail.

Indeed, I pray along with you that the remedy of peace shall be given to this Earth. The way in which that may be given is the blessing of the Father's Love upon the soul, for when that gift is given to sufficiency and abundance, then no individual can wrought harm and damage to another or to this world. The strength and wisdom of the soul, born and shaped in Love, transformed by Divine Love, cannot possibly step forward in the world with intentions other than love and light. As you pray together, my beloveds, you exert the remedy of peace. Indeed, as you receive this great gift of Love, you are bringing peace into yourself, the peace that passes all understanding. This is the way which I taught and the way which I continue to teach until that great day when my work is finished. Until that day, I urge you all to join with me to bring this truth to humanity, to be instruments of peace for the world and to be a light.

My beloveds, God utilises each and every soul who desires to be that channel of love, light and peace in the world. That utilisation is every moment, for when you draw that Light of Love to you, when you become that Light of Love, you are, by default, His instruments, His active agents of light and love in the world. Remember this, my beloveds. You carry a great responsibility, a great gift and a great blessing. As you continue to monitor your thoughts and your actions to ensure that they are in harmony with the Laws of Love, as you maintain this light around you and as God maintains this light within you, so your channel of love, this beautiful gift you have for the Earth, is sustained and grows more powerful with each day.

You walk the path which I have prescribed and taught. You walk the path of love, of truth and of light. My wish for each of you is that deep insights and deep understanding, the gifts of the soul, may emerge into your consciousness and guide you and show you the way toward greater light and greater harmony. As you grow in this consciousness, the consciousness of the soul awakened and transformed by the Father's Love, so you become my disciples, instruments of truth and beautiful channels of love. Each of you is drawn to the well, the Living Waters. Each of you drinks thirstily from those waters. I say to you, continue, for that well will never run dry and your thirst will continue for eternity, a thirst that is not difficult to quench, a thirst that comes from a soul that desires so intensely and so needfully that which will heal, cleanse, awaken and transform.

This desire has been put into you by God. God created a soul to desire to be at-one with Him. Each of you has been gifted with a soul that has deep longings and desires, a thirst to be quenched. My beloveds, we walk together. We are that beautiful light in the world. As you continue to do your work and walk in the world in this way, know that God will lead others by your side. Some you will teach. Some will teach you. Yet, the truth shall be spread in many ways, ways that you have not imagined as of yet, but ways that will be open to you. Doorways of opportunity will be given to you.

My beloveds, continue to be optimistic and enthusiastic in your spiritual pursuits. Awaken to all that which the soul has to share with you, that your consciousness of mind and the consciousness of soul may have a clear passageway of communication and understanding. In time, these two will merge into one and then you will truly know, my beloveds. You will truly be a part of us, your Celestial friends who continue to be with you and guard over you, inspire and assist you in many ways. God is providing every way and means for you to grow in His Love, for you to grow in light and to be a powerful progenitor of light and truth in the world.

My beloveds, you are truly my brothers and sisters. I love you so and I thank God every day, every moment, that He has provided ways and means for humanity to know this truth and for souls to open to it. You are part of that provision, my beloveds. You are part of the way and means for many to come to know this truth. See yourself in this light. Understand your instrumentality, the potential that lies within you, that you are truly a disciple of love and truth and all that is good and all that is of light. My beloveds, we walk together. We are God's children, instruments to bring blessings of light and truth to the world. Allow yourselves to be in that flow of God's Will, that mighty river that is manifesting God's plan for the salvation of humanity. Wade deep into that river and allow God to carry you forward.

My beloveds, you are truly, truly loved and shall be loved for all eternity. I love you, my beloveds. Know that I am truly your friend and your brother and shall be so for eternity. We walk together, my beloveds. May we walk in peace, harmony, joy and love. God bless you, beloved souls. May God be with you always in a conscious way that comforts your mind and your soul, brings you peace and allows you to see beyond the human condition to all that which is of light and that which is important and necessary for your continued spiritual awakening and growth and expression. God bless you, beloveds. I am with you and I love you. I am Jesus.
