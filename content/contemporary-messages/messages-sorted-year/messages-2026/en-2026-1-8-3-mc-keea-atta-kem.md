# Every Drop of God’s Love is Transformative

January 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Retreat, Christ Church, Barbados

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

We thank the dear daughter for her prayer, for it helps your souls and your minds be directed upon God, to open to truth, to invite through your free will choice to allow God in, God's very Essence and Substance, into your soul and the awakening and the transformation that it causes.

Our dear friends, we are happy that you come together. Though you are few in numbers, every soul is important that is here. Every drop of God's Love that comes into your soul is significant, for it is transformative. You try to understand what transformation means through your mind, but what I want to give you is assurance: your soul is under the greatest care in all the world, God's care, for your soul is that child of His, that true child, that union that He so desires to have with you and in truth, your soul desires to have with Him. As you journey along on your path of awakening, it is that the soul is coming into greater strength and power through God's Substance, to stretch, to exercise, to shower its gifts upon others.

This, dear ones, is all by the grace of God. Your soul in union with God's Soul makes you a powerful, significant instrument for change and light. I encourage you, if you do not already feel your soul, to ask God to truly help you to feel your very being, that part that communes and longs and yearns to be with God. Ask God to awaken this part of you, for as the messages say, the time is now. This is not a mental exercise. It is a soulful exercise, an awakening, something that you are learning and growing and discovering to be.

Beloved souls, know that God loves you deeply. Know that your souls love God deeply. It is not with words that you commune, but it is in an exchange of love, in the bonds of Love that unite the Father and the child. I am here, Keea Atta Kem. We are here to pray with you, to help you and develop you, as you sit and pray, allowing the inflowing of God's Love into your souls. God bless you all.
