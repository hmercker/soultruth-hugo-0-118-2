# A Lesson on Implementing the Will of God

April 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Moses received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved souls. I am God's servant. I am Moses, a Celestial Angel, one who comes to speak to you about the Will of God. God conferred to me, while I was on Earth, a task, a purpose, one that I felt unworthy to perform, inadequate in every way to be His servant. Yet, God provided me with the ways and means of being His servant, of leading my people, of creating the trusts and circumstances amongst my people that they would listen to the words of God through me.

What was my strength? How was I able to accomplish this? Well, it is and was the very essence of what you are doing and how you are striving to accomplish God's Will through you, through prayer, through listening, through developing your soul, the gifts and strength that lies therein. If God's Will is steel, then you are the elements that are added to that steel to make it strong and worthy of its purpose. Each of you adds to the Will of God in your own way. Each of you interprets the Will of God in your own way. For the most part, that interpretation and addition strengthens the Will of God, the steel that is God's intention and Will, so that you may wield this thing effectively and purposefully in the world.

God cannot do these things without you and you cannot do these things without God, but together, in the way that there is a blending of will and expression, the outcomes become beautiful and harmonious. They are not perfect. They are not exactly as God intended. Indeed, as our dear brother has indicated, we need to strive for that fulfilment of God's Will to the very essence of what it is. Yet, as you move along in the world, as you are practising to be God's servants, do not denigrate your efforts, but say, *"I have done what I can do. Dear God, empower me with Your Love and your blessings to help me to do better, to help me to be in greater alignment with Your Will, Your desire for the Earth, for me, for my loved ones, for all those around me and for this world."*

It is a simple request, but one that is most powerful. It requires your desire, your intention and, to a degree, your obedience, so that the Will of God may indeed manifest through you. I was successful in expressing God's Will in the time I lived on Earth. Yet, how long did that take for that expression to be fulfilled? Forty years in the wilderness, many trials and tribulations, efforts to find our way that often led to dead ends and unfulfilment. Yet, what carried us, my beloved friends? It was faith. It was faith in God. Above that faith, you carry something I did not at the time. It was the great gift of God's Love burning within your soul. We did not have that blessing and benefit. We did not carry the wisdom of the soul that that benefit brings to you. We did not have that direct and powerful relationship with God.

Yet, we persisted as you do. We continued as you do now. You will find, as you persist, as you intend to express the Will of God in your life, that indeed that expression will become refined and in greater alignment with not only God's Will, but with the laws that God has created in the universe, the Laws of Love most particularly. If you have doubts, put them aside, my brothers and sisters. If you are willful, look at this and reshape that will into obedience, into the capacity to listen to the guidance that is God's Will and then act accordingly. The essence of being willful is to be persistent, to step forward, to exercise your expression and endeavours in a certain direction. When you listen to the Word of God, His Will being expressed in guidance and inspiration, then use those gifts within you, that ability to move forward and to persist in order to express this will to the best of your ability to understand what that is. You will know if you are in the right direction of that expression. You will feel the rightness of it. You will know the joy of it. That expression will bring you joy. That direction will feel good, needed and strong in its pull and you will feel the Hand of God upon you.

My beloved brothers and sisters, each of you prays to do God's Will. I say each of you has your own particular obstructions to having that expression of God's Will come through you in a way that is effective, but those obstructions are not great. They are certainly surmountable and they can be pushed aside with prayer, faith and love. Do not be frustrated when things do not turn out the way you expect. Do not grieve that you have somehow thwarted God's Will. Do not be angry because the outcomes that you have prayed for have not been realised. Instead, ask God to show you why and how His Will was expressed through you and how that expression is the one that is in harmony with God's intention and plan for you and for the world. For God's Will and God's plan do not always jive with your own.

This is where humility comes in, my friends, to accept that will for what it is, to accept the outcomes for what has been so. If they seem imperfect to you, then so be it, but that does not mean that you should judge yourself for being an imperfect instrument. Rather, understand that the flow of God's Will is persistent in its own way and the outcomes will come, whether they are accomplished through you or through another, but they will come nonetheless, for God's Will is indeed persistent and powerful. In this world of yours, there are many obstructions, many who try to obstruct God's Will. You see, the outcomes may be imperfect, but the attempts to do God's Will are blessed and those who attempt to do God's Will are blessed, for these are the rewards of your efforts, my beloved friends.

Move within the flow of that great River of God's Will. You will see many outcomes that are unexpected, some that are unrealised, but much that carries you forward within your lives, your instrumentality, your beautiful souls growing because you are attempting to be in alignment, to be in that great River of God's intention for the world. As you grow in the Father's Love, so the wisdom of your soul will indicate to you what that will is and how it will be expressed. As you follow this, as you express these things over and over again, so you become more attuned, your expression more beautiful and more harmonious with the Will of God.

This is your goal and a great part of your purpose, to do the Will of God in your daily life, to walk the Path Divine in all its ramifications and expressions, to be that beautiful soul attuned to our Heavenly Father, a true child of God. You will find deep fulfilment there, my brothers and sisters, deep fulfilment indeed. With faith and persistence, with desire and love, so you will be successful as one of God's active agents, one of God's lights in the world. When your time comes to pass into the spirit world, you will reap the rewards greatly and deeply, and you will look back on your life and see how you have earned your place within the light.

Is this not what you desire, my beautiful friends, to live a life of fulfilment, of love, to live a life well and a life of service to humanity? You come together, my beloved friends. You seek this out. You seek ways and means in which to do it and to express it. You are learning and you are practising with one another how you may express service, how you may embrace and love one another. Those who have come into your midst who are strangers, those you do not know well or not at all, have you turned them away? Have you said, *"No, you do not belong here?"* No, you have embraced them in love and you have shared what you might share.

So it is as you walk the Earth. God will put before you opportunities to serve, possibilities manifest right before your eyes, but you must pray, *"God, give me the eyes to see. Help me to understand where You have guided me and for what purpose."* In this, you will be shown and you will understand, though you may resist the opportunity because you may find it difficult, repulsive, or whatever reason your mind may come up with. If you go deeper within your soul, you will feel that there is purpose and meaning to your connection with another, this opportunity given. That is where you must go and that is where you must listen. For when you do so, you will learn a great deal and you will benefit a great deal from that exchange and that situation. God gives you these opportunities for many reasons and there are many outcomes and blessings that come with it.

Follow the Will of God, my beloved and beautiful friends. You will learn and grow, become greater lights, more powerful servants of God, channels of love. Greater harmony will come into your lives. More beautiful opportunities will manifest, one step at a time, one lesson at a time, one test at a time, one opportunity at a time. So it goes, my beloved friends, along the Path Divine. We learn, we grow, we serve, we integrate, we understand, we love, we accept and we express. In this way, you become God's instruments, powerful and beautiful, one step, one drop and one increment. Yet, as you accept the opportunities given, the pace will quicken. You will come to understand why you have taken the route that you have taken, the route that God has guided you upon. It is the wisest route, the highest route and the most blessed route, even though at the time it may seem obscure and confusing. You will come to know, with the wisdom of your soul, that the journey you have taken and are about to take is a journey that is meant for you. God will bless each step, will light the way, will help you to understand your true purpose, your true journey, your true awakening and transformation as God's child and God's service.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Moses. I hope I have been instructive. As you know my story, the challenges of serving God in my life upon the Earth were great, but I overcame every challenge. Indeed, you know me today because I did so. It will be so with you, my beloved friends, although I hope your challenges are not like mine. My desire for you is to do the greatness that changes the course of history in ways that bring greater harmony to humanity, in ways that will show the way to many souls toward light, truth, joy, fulfilment and love.

God bless you, beloved souls. May the Truth of His Love continue to shine within you. May you come to know this Truth, embrace it so that it is the very nature of your being, your motivation and your efforts. God bless you, beloved souls. My love is with you as well as my appreciation of your efforts. God bless you. I am Moses and I love you.
