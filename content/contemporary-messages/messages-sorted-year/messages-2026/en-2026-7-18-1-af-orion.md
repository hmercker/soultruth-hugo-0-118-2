# Each Person Must Make Their Own Individual Choice to be in the Light and Earth Changes Will Facilitate This

July 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. You walk in a world where the ways of living often contradict the Laws of Creation and the Laws of Love. Each day can be a struggle. Each day is a challenge to go beyond those conditions and the patterns that have been set from the very beginning of your life to transition to something that is more in harmony with what was meant to be.

My beloved friends, we understand your dilemmas and your challenges. Even though I myself have never experienced these challenges, I see them. I see them with my soul's perceptions. I see them as I witness your daily walk in life. I see them in a world that hangs by a thread, but the power of creation, the power of God's creation in this world, is being severely challenged by the power of human creation. Indeed, that power is growing each and every day, where what is meant to be, what is in harmony, is often faltering. Indeed, you see this in your daily life. You see this in the world where there are many disasters and problems and many people suffering. Yet, the world continues to base its ways of living and its priorities upon materialism and a desire for power and control, often motivated by fear, fear of not having enough. Yet, so many of you have more than enough and some of you have very little indeed. This inequity is not in harmony.

Indeed, there is much work to be done and the momentum of this terrible disharmony is great. God has continued to bring interventions and blessings to the world and to you. Yet, much of this is ignored, unseen, unrecognised, unappreciated, not by you, my friends, but by the world. As the momentum of God's Will builds within this world of yours, as the changes continue to be established, as your dear Mother Earth continues to rebel and try and heal herself, humanity will come to a point where they cannot ignore their misdoings any longer. I say this generally and yet that realisation must come individually and in various ways and means. There will be the opportunity to see and know what is out of harmony with the individual's life.

Yet, you cannot do this for the individual. You can only do it for yourself. This spiritual awakening must come with each individual. For many who are in darkness and who are lost, the momentum needed to shift consciousness in this way is tremendous. This is why changes are coming and are in process of being. In order for that momentum to build, in order for those doors to be opened, often they require a great deal of pain and suffering. For how is the mind shifted in its realisations? It is often in crisis, as is often when the individual reaches a place of desperation. The world becomes more desperate, covered by a veneer of materialism and physical gratification. Yet, this can only be so effective when underneath there is a tumult of emotions and a sense that all is not right. The veneer will fall away. The truth will be revealed and the suffering will be intense. Great regret, great realisation, great shifts of consciousness will come to every individual in the world.

Yet, each one may resist. Each one may say, *"no, I prefer to be ignorant and in the conditions that I have felt comfortable in."* These individuals will fight to the end and there will be an end. There will be a turning point where either the individual accepts change that is for the better or not. It is for you, my beloved friends, to explain to those who are confused and lost what it is that is causing this confusion, how it is that there is great loss, because the world cannot tolerate these conditions forever.

There must be a shift toward reclaiming harmony and balance, a shift toward acknowledging the Laws of Creation, of honouring creation. These things will come. It will be gradual because change does not happen all at once, but it will be that each individual will come to realise the errors of their ways. You as well, my beloved friends, are still somewhat immersed in error and you will come to realise this. You have the strength of a soul that is emerging, awakening in Love and thus the transition will not be so great. You will have through that process a strength and a perception and love that will assist others on the journey.

There is much work to do, my friends. The world is hanging by a thread and indeed action is required. I urge you to pray about this and to understand that change is not the enemy, nor meant to cause pain. Change is meant to cause greater harmony, greater light and the survival of your planet and your peoples, all that this entails, which is an integral part of the universe. Thus, you have many who come to help, for they do not want to see the loss, such a beautiful creation of God. They do not want all those of you upon this planet to suffer greatly, suffer great pain and loss that might come through human endeavour and intervention.

The world must right itself. Unfortunately, because of the way your world is created by your realities, your consciousness, these crises must come to a head before change happens. How unfortunate that you must come to that place rather than having wisdom, understanding that would prevent such crises. Yet, it is your way and it is your choice, the choice of humanity, to go along in the bliss of ignorance, to continue in ways and means that are comfortable and pleasurable, not realising the long-term consequences of such behaviours.

It comes to a place where humanity will reap the whirlwind of their actions and this will not be pleasant for any of you. Yet, it is the way forward. It is not the end of the world, but the beginning of a new one. It is what will be and we will do whatever we can to assist you, to uphold you and to guide you, as will many, provided you are open and receptive and have that faith that will carry you through whatever turmoil you may experience in your life. God will find a way to ease the suffering, to bring harmony back to your planet, to show each soul how to love, what love truly is and how love is the answer to all dilemmas.

This will come. It must come. It is inevitable. It is a law in the universe that all must seek harmony. Whatever avenue and way that this is accomplished is indeed part of the process of the journey. We walk together, my friends. We are the children of God, are we not? We are beautiful instruments of light. You will be called upon, each one of you, to be that light in the world, a world that will be darker and yet is stretching and yearning toward greater light. You must show the way, indicate to others a way out of their dilemmas and their suffering, for you know the way. You live the way. This is what is required. This is where your gifts will shine. This is where all the time and development and prayer will come to fruition and be utilised for the benefit of humanity.

God bless you, my friends. I am honoured to be a part of this great movement for change, this beautiful plan that God has for the salvation of humanity. What would you do without these interventions? The world would be lost and there would be great regret, not only amongst humanity, but amongst the universe and this cannot be. We strive forward. We work diligently, each of us, you on your side, we on ours, working, working to bring harmony and peace. It shall come, my beloved friends. Seek the gift from God, the key to all, true harmony. God bless you. I am Orion. I love you. God bless you.
