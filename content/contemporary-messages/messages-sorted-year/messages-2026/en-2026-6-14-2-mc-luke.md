# Seek From Your Soul to Know God's Truths

June 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Starpoint Circle of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Luke received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. I am Luke. I join my fellow seekers of Love in this time of prayer, where you open your souls and you ask God to help you to open further for this great inflowing of Love, Love Divine. As this Love comes in, changing your nature, you experience a transformation of your very being. When we talk about being a seeker of Love, I ask you, from what part of you do you seek? Is it the intellectual aspect of your material mind, or is it through your soul seeking to know God in a way like no other, seeking further truths, seeking wisdom and seeking understanding of the knowledge that you carry? For when one seeks for everything through the soul, the material mind in time will become informed with this conscious truth that comes into the soul.

In everything that you do as you walk your path of progression, allow it to be sought from your being, your soul, for as you do, it is from here that your earnestness flows to God. As you well know, God responds most efficiently and clearly to sincere and earnest seeking. As you do this, you will be amazed at what comes into your being. You will be able to experience being in the world, but not of the world, because your inner world is informing you and your outer world has less influence over you and how you live your life. A soul that seeks for God's Love to come into it is progressing in the transformation of God's Substance, changing its nature, becoming more divine in its nature.

How can you recognise changes that are happening within you? The attributes of your soul are empowered by Divine Love and there is a sincere and even innocent longing to be with God and to ask God to teach you, to show you, where you become like a child safe in the arms of your Heavenly Father, where you can be taught, loved, cared for, guided and supported in all ways. It is for you to find that innocent, childlike aspect of yourself, where you can weep in the arms of God, baring yourself in the way that you are able to only with God. For as you do this, you are able to release the disharmony that is within you, because a courage comes to you where you can speak to God about those very things inside of you that you may not be able to acknowledge with others, but the courage that comes within the soul allows you to be able to speak to God about these things and with your sincerity ask God to help you to transform that part of you, to release you of the burdens that you carry from these parts of you that you hide from others and even yourself.

It is being able to be strong in the face of who you are at this moment in time, to not judge but more so to acknowledge and to say, *"this is where I am currently on my path, walking closer and closer with God, and God has helped me up to this point on my path. God has shown me I can trust and I can be vulnerable with Him and His Love as the antidote to all the things that I harbour and I carry unnecessarily."* For God wishes to free you from your burdens. Because in that He sees you becoming within your own self, your own perceptions of yourself free and whole. As you become free and whole within yourself, you experience the love of yourself, able to love yourself as God loves you and this is a pinnacle part of your journey. Because when you love yourself, when you truly love yourself, you do not have the capacity to repeat harmful situations, actions and deeds.

Coming to learn to love yourself is a part of having the Substance of God within you and its transformation. Seekers of love, be sure that you allow your seeking to be of the soul, to be curious, to make room for those things that you carry within you, to be healed, to be transformed and to be shown a new way of acting and choosing to live. For any small action that is done within harmony, builds the process of being able to choose further and further actions that are in harmony. You are changing in your nature as you walk this path of Love Divine. We are here, your angels, your stellar friends and high spirits, to guide you, to support you, to bring our blessings, our love and our gifts to you. For remember, we too have walked this path and we understand. We understand your struggles. This is why we come to help you as you are transforming from a soul created in God's image, into a soul that is of the Substance of God.

God bless you all on this magnificent journey of awakening as you seek to know from your soul the many Truths of God. I am Luke. God bless you.
