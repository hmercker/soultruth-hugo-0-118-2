# You Contribute to the Birthing of a New World

April 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Timothy received by Elizabeth Mack

Dear children, you contribute to the birth of this new world, today, yesterday and tomorrow, every day that you pray for God's Divine Love, every day that you manifest that Love in whatever it is that you are doing, you are birthing a new world, a world of harmony, a world of grace, a world of Divine Love. I am your brother, Timothy. I have come before. I am not a Celestial Angel. I am one who travels across the universe to join you in this prayer. I am a brother and in the flesh. I come with my love for all of you upon this Earth.We join you to strengthen your prayers, because we too desire a more beautiful life and planet on which to live for all of your people, upon this Earth, now and in the future.

You are God's true children. You deserve the very best, the most loving environment possible. We are here to help make that happen. It is crucial that you participate in bringing forward all of the love in your souls, all of it manifested upon this Earth, that this Earth may heal, that the souls upon this Earth come to feel that love and know that there is something, something that they have been seeking, that is present upon this Earth and that they are searching for a way to find it.  We are all here for this purpose of birthing a new planet, a new way to be, a way in which God blesses each soul, each tree, each life, each child, where there is no more disharmony and disease. Pray with all your might and we will join you, that we may help transform this Earth and all who live upon it.

God blesses you now with an abundance of Love flowing into your souls, into our souls, into the souls that are open and willing to receive the blessings He has to offer, even those in the spirit world. Some of whom still reside upon this planet, they too are being blessed. Continue to pray, dear ones. Continue to love. I am your brother, Timothy. I love you, all of you, each of you, with a full heart and soul. I love you and God's Love is beyond measure for each one of you. God bless you.
