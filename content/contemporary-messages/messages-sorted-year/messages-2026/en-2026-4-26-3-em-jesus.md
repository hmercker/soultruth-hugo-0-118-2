# Ask the Father to Show You All That You Have Been Given

April 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Tell City Indiana Retreat, Tell City, IN, USA

Jesus received by Elizabeth Mack

Dear children of our Father, my Father has sent me here to bless you, to raise you, your souls in this Love that He is pouring down upon you. I am one with my Father and you too are one with our Father. Your will is in harmony with God's Will at this time and He blesses you in multiple, multiple, multiple ways. He blesses you. Accept, embrace and let this Love grow even more within you.

As we come to the close of this retreat, I give you my love and my blessings, the Father's blessings and He, His Love for you. You have come forward during this time together. You have done wonderful work, each one. As you continue to walk this journey, this path on this journey, you are going to come to see more and more truth. You have been given the vision to see more clearly, to love more deeply, to know more truly what it is that God desires you to know at this stage in your progression.

Reflect on all that you have been given. Ask the Father to show you all that you have been given. Have faith that what you see will unfold, for it is God's Will and part of God's plan. Know it will happen. This is faith, faith based upon the knowledge of your experience with God. You have been blessed with more faith, more truth and more Love. You have been greatly blessed. Accept and embrace.

As you travel to your homes, you will be accompanied by Celestial Angels who will keep you safe along the way and as you reflect upon all that has happened here, you will be inspired by Celestial Angels and you will always have angels by you. Take this in deeply and learn to work together with your angels.

I now close this circle and leave behind this portal for future retreats, future prayers and future communions with our Father. May God bless you. With His mighty Hand, He blesses you. My love is with you. I am your brother, Jesus. I will be watching over you also. Bless you, dear, dear true children, my disciples, my apostles, my followers. God bless you.
