# Pray to Express Your Gifts

April 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

James (Brother of Jesus) received by Elizabeth Mack

I am James of the Bible. I come to speak with you about my Master and my brother, Jesus, whose life upon the Earth was short. Yet, he sought to perform such changes in human behaviour. One might say he was driven, when really he was the instrument of God. He prayed. He prayed almost non-stop. He was teaching or he was praying. For every movement that he made was a prayer to God to bless the children upon the Earth. This was his ministry, to teach them and to express that special Love that God had given to him and, eventually, to several others of us.

This too is what you are being asked to do, to pray and to express that special gift and all of your gifts to the children upon this Earth. You will come to be known as true children of God, as was Jesus. This, of course, is not the reason to do it, to follow his example. The reason, if you will, the intention, is because that is why you came to the Earth, to fulfil God's plan, to help in the transformation of this Earth and all who live upon it. You are not alone in this endeavour. You will never be alone. Yet, there are many, some of whom live upon the Earth in the flesh, that are unknown to you. There are many upon the Earth and, of course, we in the Celestial Heavens join you all, guide you and bring to you those souls whom you can help, whom you can teach, whom you can heal through your prayers and your expressions of love for God. Many more will be coming soon.

So, pray from your souls, your hearts and your souls. Pray to be these instruments of God. Pray to receive these souls who come to you with the greatest love that you have within you. Of course, we are working with you. Much will be coming and changing. You yourselves will begin to feel the changes within you. The more you are these instruments, the more you will feel the difference between who you used to be and who you are becoming. Shine brightly, children, shine brightly, and let God's Love come through you to touch your brothers and sisters in ways that you cannot imagine. Let His Love come through you and bless these children.

I thank you for listening. I thank you for praying. We desire to pray with you at this time. God bless you. God bless you, mightily. I am James, the brother of Jesus, who is my Master.
