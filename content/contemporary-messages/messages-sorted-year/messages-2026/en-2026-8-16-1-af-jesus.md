# Beloved Instruments of God: Walking in Love and Service

August 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds. I come once again, your brother and friend. I am Jesus and I am well pleased with your efforts, my beloveds, to bring the Truth of God's Love into the world. The ways and means that that may be accomplished are many and the opportunities to do so are reflected by the gifts that you carry. Some do so with music, some with words, some put these truths on print, others speak them in groups and yet you all pray together as one body unified with that great desire to receive the Father's Love within your souls.

Each of the efforts that are being made, each thought that is being given toward these truths, each prayer uttered, each act of kindness and love as a channel of God's Will and intention, each time you step out your door and walk amongst your brothers and sisters, every breath, my beloveds, every breath, you are bringing the Light of God's Love into the world and none of you are remiss in this regard. Each of you acts as God's agent in some way to bring the Truth of His Love into this world, for even your thoughts enter the ethers and combine with the thought conditions of this world, adding an element of truth to those conditions. Every time you pray, you bring light to yourselves that is infused into this dark world of yours.

When you recite a poem or compose a piece of music or bring a book forward or go onto the wires and speak the truth or go to a gathering, a church, and share the Truth of God's Love, you are doing God's work and for this you will be blessed. The indication of that blessing is the presence of an angel with you. As you work, as you compose, as you write, as you speak, we are with you. For it is our work as well and we do so in collaboration. We make rapport with you in order to help you in your work and to make your work easier. Indeed, we help in many ways, influence many souls and open doors that would be difficult to open, so that you may indeed bring the Truth of God's Love forward.

Have faith in this, my beloveds. Have faith in the work that is in progress. Have faith in God's plan to bring the truth to humanity and to heal this world of the dark conditions that humanity has formed and accumulated for so very long. There will come a time of peace, a time when the darkness will disperse and the Light of God's Love and God's Presence will shine upon His children. This I tell you, my beloveds, for it is the truth. We in the Celestial Kingdom understand this and have seen it.

The ways and means, the timing, when and where? It is not of such great importance to you on Earth. For what is important is that you walk each day with the intention of being a channel of love and light in the world for God and for humanity and with that intention, you will be guided, protected, inspired and blessed. With that, many others will be blessed with your intention and desire to serve God. Do not diminish who you are and what you might do in the world. Rather, have faith in the possibilities that are inherent within your souls.

Have faith in God's plan for you and what you may accomplish through your guidance, inspiration and desire to walk in the world. Each ministry, each effort begins small and humble, but as you continue to listen to that still voice within you, which is God speaking to you, you will grow in your efforts in the ministry that is yours and yours alone. You will find that God will guide you forward and that opportunities will open to you that you would not have imagined. This is the power of God working. It is not like the clashing of cymbals and the loudness of your world, as it speaks the material truth and tries to convince humanity of various mindful things. This is not how God works.

God works in subtle ways and yet in these ways the soul is reached, inspired and touched. This is where change will happen, not from the mind implementing but from the soul encouraging, yearning and being guided by God. As that inspiration bubbles forth into your consciousness, you will know the steps to take. As you pray to be used as God's instruments, you will be informed, you will be given step-by-step guidance. This I promise you.

An angel will be with you, my beloveds, as you walk upon this road of service, this road of love. An angel will be with you and you will find yourselves often in the most peculiar places. Yet, you have been guided there. The door has opened, the opportunity presented to you. Listen to that still voice within you, my beloveds. Listen to what God has to say to you, what God has to give to you. God has every faith in you, my beloveds, in what He has created within you, in what is possible, the great potentials of your soul. We come together to pray for that ignition of these potentials, that key that opens the door to them and that is His Love. As you continue to open to this gift, as you continue to pray to be used as His instruments, then what I speak of will come to pass.

What I speak of is the truth of the soul. My beloveds, we are all eager to know that truth and to listen to the wisdom within. Each of you have been blessed with gifts. Each of you may be used by God to express those gifts in love and harmony, humility and grace. Step forward, my beloveds, in faith, faith in yourself, faith in the Will of God, faith in all that is a part of God's plan for the salvation of humanity and you will find great joy, comfort, strength, clarity and love as you open that door and allow God's Will to flow through you into the world.

It awaits you each and every day, my beloveds. Each and every day is an opportunity to serve and to be in the grace of God, to know His grace, guidance and Will in your life. This gift grows more powerful as the inklings of your soul grow and expand with the Father's Love. Clarity will come, my beloveds. Those of you who have great questions and wonderments, answers will come if you are receptive. Remember that the ideas of your mind, the ambitions of your mind are not what God utilises nor feeds in this plan. It is what God has implanted within your soul and what will come of your soul, expression of that soul in the world. This is part of God's plan.

Remember this, my beloveds. It is that deep knowing that what you do is in harmony with God's Will is a part of God's plan. This blessing will come to you with greater faith and greater love building within you. You will awaken to something magnificent and wondrous within you, a true understanding of who you are, a true understanding of your purpose in this world. It will come with bits and pieces of experience. Yet, as you add each of these pieces to the great puzzle, the picture that comes forth, then the understanding will become clear. As you truly understand, I urge you to be loyal and committed to what God has given you and the truth and purpose that lies within you. It is given each and every day, with each and every prayer, each and every thought, each and every action that is motivated by love and by truth. All is given, my beloveds. All that you require is given freely and abundantly.

We gather around you. We, the angels of the Celestial Kingdom, we who serve God. We gather around you and we ascend into the Hall of Peace, that you may find respite and light, receive greater portions of the Father's Love and find peace there, the peace that passes all understanding. Shall we proceed, my brothers and sisters? Shall we go into greater light? I am with you, your brother and friend. I am Jesus and I lead the way to this place of blessings.

God bless you, my beloveds. I love each and every one of you. I am with you, with you all the days of your life. I am with you, beloved instruments of God. God bless you, each one. God bless you.
