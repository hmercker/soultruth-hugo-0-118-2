# Allow the Integration of God's Love Within Your Soul

February 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Keea Atta Kem. What a blessing it is to be with you as you gather in this way, where you bring God’s Love into your souls and where the transformation is happening because the Substance and Essence of God is now within you.

This changes you in ways that you cannot understand, but we encourage you to allow the integration of this Substance with your soul, to make its way known to you. In this way, you must exercise a sense of stillness and come to know yourself within your soul, come to identify as the soul you are with a beautiful mind that unfolds the wisdom and takes direction from this place within you. It is the place that carries on from your earthly experience and it is for you to know and discover. In this blessing that your angel has brought you, part of that blessing is to help you to be aware of your soul, to awaken within your consciousness your soul, its desires and how it longs, desires and yearns to be with God by receiving this Substance through your prayerfulness.

Allow the integration of the Substance of God that you have received through every opening and every gifting of God’s response to your true longings and sincere desires. Allow yourself to come to know who you truly are, who God is and your instrumentality, the gifts that God has laid within your soul. It is a blessed journey, one that, as you move along in your transformation through your own awakening and discovering of who you truly are, will set fire to the desires of your soul, aligning mind and soul together. You will be a powerful instrument of love, light, joy, peace, all the beautiful attributes, awakening and being empowered by God’s grace that resides within you by asking God and inviting God to bless your soul.

I am Keea Atta Kem. God bless you. God bless you mightily as you awaken within your souls to your true identity and live your life from this part of you. God bless you.
