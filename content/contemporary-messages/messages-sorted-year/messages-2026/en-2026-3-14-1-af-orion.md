# Double Your Prayers to Remain in Harmony in a Dark World

March 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

May the Light of God's Love shine upon you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. Prayer is your greatest friend and ally in a world that is so tumultuous, a world that is filled with disharmony and a lack of light. Each time you come to the Creator, the Source of all Light, you strengthen yourself. You reinforce the light around you and within you. This is what you must do, my friends, each and every day, throughout the day, to be in that alignment with the Creator and to be in that harmony that is required, so that your progression may be swift, that light may grow and the Love of God's Essence may infill your soul in greater and greater abundance.

For what else can you do, my beloved friends, in a world whose currents are so disruptive and disharmonious? The currents of thought and deed go beyond anything that can rectify and change this world. Because of the diversity and its erratic conditions, the human condition now is more dense and difficult than ever. Prayer, my friends, is the antidote. Even though you may be caught up in some small way in that dark condition, you know how to remove yourself, to extradite yourself from that flow and be in the beautiful flow of Light that is God's Presence, the presence of your angelic friends, us and many others. We are all too eager to surround you, to protect you, to help you in your awakening and your soul's transformation.

Be of good cheer, my friends, for it is in the power of the truth that you carry. It is in the efforts and the prayers that you make. It is in the thoughts that you have that light continues to come. For you obey the Laws of Creation, those laws that are of the universe that govern such things. You awaken to the Laws of Love. That is the highest law of all. Though you may see darkness all around you, though you may wonder about the fate of humanity, yourselves and your families, you know one thing is true. It is that your connection, your relationship with God, will see you through whatever comes and whatever might be upon your world.

How many have the power and strength of this relationship? Some believe they do, but that is a belief of the mind. Some do not know that they do, but their souls and God are deeply connected. Most are lost in the currents of darkness. This rules the planet and decides its fate in many ways. Yet, God intervenes as well. The power of that intervention is great, for God's power is great. There is a tension between light and darkness, between harmony and discord, one that you are caught in the middle of. You wish for light and indeed you carry light. Yet, because you do, the power of darkness seeks to put out that light, to overwhelm you and to negate you.

You must be vigilant. This is why prayer is so important. To be in that flow, that connection, that consciousness of God is vital at this time. Indeed, as many come to this realisation, as many practise this truth, the power to nullify the darkness grows, yet the resistance from that darkness grows as well. You must be vigilant, my beloved friends, beyond what is your normal day. You must double your prayers. You must be well aware of your thoughts and where they go and how they manifest in your life. For now is the time of great push and pull, of light and darkness, of difficulties and challenges, of many, many conditions that are vying for dominance and mindful infiltration of every individual on this Earth.

Be of your soul, my friends. This is how you gain immunity from such conditions. The soul is strong in each one of you. It is clear, bright, beautiful and filled with wisdom and love. It is your saving grace, as they say, and it shall be so for all the days of your life upon this planet of yours. It is the glimpse into the future of humanity, how humanity may save itself through understanding these Laws of Love, this connection with the great Creator. You must teach this and demonstrate this wherever you go. Be that light, your soul shining through with no resistance from your material consciousness, a beautiful light, a channel of love for the world, a world so in need of this, a world so lost from the truth.

My beloved friends, you know that we are with you, that we pray with you, that we are your friends and your guardians, your guides and your teachers. We play a part in a great body of souls from your world, from ours, from others who assist you upon the journey. We crowd around this place, this planet, to ensure its survival, to ensure that it may be transformed into light, harmony and peace. We continue to grow in numbers to counteract the intensity of these currents. We shall indeed prevail. For we understand God's Will and plan and we are all too willing to be a part of it. Our desire is for you as well to not only understand this plan for your personal life, but also subscribe to it with every breath.

This is how a plan may unfold with your efforts and your dedication, your ability to be guided, your ability to understand why and how, where and when. These things are so crucial. They require your hidden faculties, those things that the great Love of God is stirring to wakefulness, true understanding and expression. This you are engaged in with each day. This you must continue to seek, know and truly understand, this part of you, these many parts that are yearning to be acknowledged, awakened and used and expressed in all the wonderful ways that can be.

My beautiful friends, we continue on the journey, a journey of great purpose, a journey that is crucial for the salvation of humanity from its dark ways, its ignorance and strife. We continue to work together, be together and seek the guidance that is required. May we all be blessed with perception of soul, Love flowing abundantly, light surrounding us, bright and beautiful. God bless you, my friends. Orion loves you. I continue to be with you all and shall do so for all the days of your life on this Earth, for we have made that commitment and so we shall follow that. God bless you all.
