# Honouring Mother Earth as a Path to Deeper Spiritual Awakening

April 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Wyndell Elderclaw received by Al Fike

Greetings and the blessings of God's Love be with you. My name is Wyndell Elderclaw. You have not heard from me before, but I come today to acknowledge your prayers for the Earth. I love and have loved the Earth. I lived many hundreds of years ago as a druid upon the Earth in the country known as England today. I made a study of the Earth over many hundreds of years since I passed into spirit. I am also a resident of the Celestial Kingdom. I know of the Father's Love and I seek it with all my heart and soul. My studies have been a way of blending my knowledge of the Earth and my knowledge of Heaven to make for a harmonious teaching and understanding of the two and how they may come together in harmony and peace.

As you have been told, the Earth itself has its own consciousness, its own way of existence. Few honour this in your world today. Yet in my world, when I lived upon it, we honoured mother Earth as the source of our life, our existence and our nurturing. Although we did not know the Earth as God, we knew the Earth as a magnificent creation of God and so we combined the two reveries into one religion, the druid faith, which was ancient and in many ways more attuned to God's creation than it is today. We knew of the healing blessings of the Earth and of heaven. We knew of God, but of course, our definition of God back then was quite different from my understanding of it now. Yet, within our honouring of the deities, we found peace and harmony. We honoured one another. We honoured the world.

You may take some lessons from what we understood, for in that soulful awareness that we all have, there is the capacity to know and understand the nature of creation and the nature of Mother Earth. Indeed, as you venture further into soul awareness and that awareness is nurtured by the gift of God's Love, you will be able to recognise, experience and delve into the intricacies of Mother Earth's being, Mother Earth's existence and Mother Earth's consciousness. Indeed, you do not speak often of these things, but they are important. You are a beneficiary of the blessings that Mother Earth gives, giving you all that you require to sustain your physical being. Honouring her presence is a wonderful thing, a thing that will bring you deeper awareness and consciousness of life and this can only benefit your spiritual progression.

The beautiful song that we heard comes from a soul that knows her mother well. As you send your reveries and thoughts to your mother, there is a response, a light that grows, a love that flows from your thoughts, efforts and prayers. I do not wish to detract you from the true purpose of your gatherings, which is to know God as the Creator, as the One who Loves you so. Indeed, this is the highest Truth. Yet, as you venture further into truth, you will discover other aspects, layers and elements of truth that exist in God's universe. I share my wisdom and I share my thankfulness and gratitude toward your efforts to bring a greater light to this Earth plane, so beleaguered by neglect, ignorance and darkness. Your intention is to bring life and truth, awareness and love. I join you in this and I am with you in it.

I thank you for listening to me. I am your elder and I continue to walk with you in the Love of God, an angel redeemed by His Love. I love you all. God bless you. God bless you all.
