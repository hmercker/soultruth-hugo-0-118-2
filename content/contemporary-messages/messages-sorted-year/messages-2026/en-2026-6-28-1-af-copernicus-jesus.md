# The Science of the Soul’s Awakening Through Divine Love & Blessing from Jesus

June 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Copernicus & Jesus received by Al Fike

**Copernicus:** Blessings to you, my friends. I am Copernicus. I have the honour today to bring a teaching to you, a teaching of the nature of soul development. When we are speaking of soul, we must use symbols and words in an adequate way in which to teach of soul, even though the soul is not material. Therefore, using material ways to explain the soul is rather inadequate, but since you live upon the Earth plane, this is the best we can do for today.

Think of the soul as a cluster of cells. Within that cell, like within the body, there is a DNA, a *"mandate"*, so to speak, of what that soul will do within the body. Each cell will contribute to that destiny of soul. With this spiritual DNA, as it is encrusted in the conditions of the earthly plane, it is in a form of stasis, not moving and growing in that condition. It is darkened and held within the detritus of earthly life, memory and experience. When there is no attempt to grow spiritually and to cleanse the soul, each cell then is inert. Each cell cannot express and fulfil its function, its destiny, because of this inertia, this encrustation that keeps it in that state.

Yet, as you pray for the Divine Love, as this blessing cleanses and ignites the cell, then there is movement and development. The cell is awakened. The potential of the cell is given empowerment and can indeed come alive. As you continue to grow and receive this gift of Divine Love, that which holds the cells in place with conditions that are of the Earth is cleansed. It is prepared for its next stage of development. For this is the first stage, that of cleansing, that of bringing the cell into harmony with its destiny. As you continue to pray for Divine Love and the cleansing at some point is complete, you will begin to experience the awakening of each cell. The DNA is active. The purpose of that cell is revealed and these are the gifts that you carry, beloved souls.

There are gifts that are basic to each individual and all individuals. There are gifts that are unique to each individual. Indeed, these rather rudimentary gifts awaken in the beginning, but this awakening leads to the stimulation and empowerment of other cells, other parts of you, of your soul, that will come alive. You each have your own unique identity, your own unique cluster of cells, so to speak, and your unique destiny. With this Divine Love, this gift, so powerful, so beautiful, you will discover who you truly are. You will come to experience the uniqueness of your being, the beauty of what is within you. It will be revealed in many ways, beautiful, harmonious ways, within your life. As you continue to grow in this blessing, this awakening, you will come fully alive and many, many gifts will be revealed.

For it is not a finite situation. It is eternal and so the journey is eternal. The awakening is eternal. The cleansing brings one closer to God and in that closeness, that rapport with the Creator, there is a longing for this awakening, this blessing of Love. As that longing is fulfilled, so the awakening of each cell, each part of you, is accomplished in time. It is not ever complete, my beloved friends. It continues on the eternal quest and development of your soul. It is God's greatest miracle, the awakening of a soul, a soul fulfilled in Love, a soul that releases all conditions and restrictions to that awakening through the efforts of your prayers receiving this gift. In this is a journey like none other. So many discoveries, you could not count them all. Yet, there is more and it will continue forever.

This is why God makes His Love available without restriction and without a limit of time to that availability, for His plan is eternal progress. His plan is to awaken all of you. The potential that is within you you could not possibly fathom at this time. It fascinates me. I am a scientist and have always been. To understand how the workings of the soul continue to function, continue to change and grow and be enlivened by this great Love, this great gift, fascinates me. It inspires me. There are others like me who continue to seek out the truth in this way. I am just one, one whom you may recognise, but in truth, there are many more who continue in this pursuit and continue to understand the complexities and the subtleties of a soul on its journey toward alignment with God. All you need to know is this truth in its simple form. What I say is not particularly relevant to your journey, but I know many of you are curious and would like to know, would like to have a structure of understanding, so that you might realise the nature of the journey.

I give you these words, which are inadequate in themselves. Yet, I hope that I have made it simple enough that you might understand that the journey of the soul is the most important and powerful journey you might ever make. It dwarfs all other things, all other journeys. Humanity in your world values the mind, the intellect and the material. This is so minuscule in its importance on your journey that you must realise that the focus upon receiving this great gift of Divine Love is the primary impetus in your life. It must be, for it will set you upon the eternal path. It will help you understand your true self and true nature. It will nurture and bring forth the multitude of gifts that each of you possesses.

Continue, my friends, to seek truth, to seek love and to seek light. These three things together constitute the greatest journey of all. As you seek the Father's Love, these things manifest in your life and consciousness. There is little else that can truly fulfil this journey and the longing, the aching of your soul to truly come alive, to release the impediments, to release these incrustations, so that you are free, free to truly live, free to truly understand and to relate to that which God created and that which is God. For much comes with your efforts, my friends, and every effort toward truth, every desire expressed to God, every prayer for His Love, is important and will bring you upon this path, so exalted, so beautiful, so wondrous in its nature.

Indeed, you are entering into the miracle of this gift that God gives, this awakening and transformation that is His Love. You will find joy unimaginable, fulfilment, revelations upon revelations, as those aspects of your soul begin to awaken. They do so often in clusters, relationships with one another that will add to your perceptions and experience in life. God has created a wondrous thing. That wondrous thing is you and in the wonderment, in the beauty and in the miracle that is your soul, there is an eternity of discovery, knowledge and fulfilment in Love.

May you each take that journey, my friends, seriously and intently, for it is your intention, your desire, that will determine the pace of your progress. It is your longing to receive this gift of Love that will release you from the burdens and the encrustations. It is your acceptance, embracing God in the way that is innocent and full of love for God, that you will find all that I speak of. All the truth of your soul will be revealed. All the Truth that God has to give will be given. We of the Celestial Kingdom will be by your side, for we take great pleasure and joy in witnessing the birthing of your soul, the awakening of its potentials, that spiritual DNA, empowered by Love and bringing forth all that you are, changed, transformed and put together in a way that is different from now. It is different and it is magnificent.

God bless you, my friends. I am Copernicus. I come with my message, my little lesson. I hope you have benefited from it. I will come again another day to speak of things of the soul, as I continue on my journey of discovery of my research and my experience that brings to me the revelations of truth. It will be so with you, my friends, each of you. Revelations of truth, given by God. As you change within your soul, you will be receptive and able to understand all that God gives to you, all the blessings, the upliftments and the awakenings that will come with His Love. God bless you, my friends. My love is with you. God bless you.

**Jesus:** I come, my beloveds. I come to bless you and uplift you. I come because I love you. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you.

Our dear brother (Copernicus) desires to teach you of things. I desire to bring you into the fold of God's Love, that you may know the experience of this gift. We all work together to uphold you and to show you the way to truth. We shall continue to do so, my beloved brothers and sisters, in ways and means that you might understand, in ways and means that will uplift you and inspire you. What is most important is that you know that you are loved truly, that you may feel that Love as it truly is, the experience of your soul awakening and those aspects of the soul continuing to be touched, transformed by the Father's Love.

I bless you, my beloveds, in the journey of truth, in the awakenings of your soul, its transformation never complete, but in time, will enable you to enter into the Celestial Kingdom, where we are truly brothers and sisters upon the path of eternal progress. God bless you, my beloveds. I love you. I am with you. I am with you. God bless you in His Love and Truth. I am Jesus and I am with you.
