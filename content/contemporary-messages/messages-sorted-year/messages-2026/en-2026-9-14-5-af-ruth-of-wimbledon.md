# This Church Will Be Maintained in Light and Love

September 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Wimbledon Spiritualist Church Retreat, Wimbledon, London, UK

Ruth of Wimbledon received by Al Fike

My name is Ruth. I was once a member of this church a few decades back and I am happy to be here again with these lovely souls who have gathered. I want to assure you all that this church will be maintained and it will flourish, provided you give what people want, not what you think they need, but what they are looking for, what they desire in their hearts. If you can give of your heart to them, then they will be receptive and they will listen to you. Yet, first, it must be love. Indeed, this church does not lack in love, it is full of love.

Continue to maintain that sense, that perspective, so that many will come through the doors feeling a warm welcome, knowing that they are accepted. As they feel comfortable, as you reassure them, as the spirits come and speak to them, they will feel open and receptive to the words of wisdom you may have to say. It is a very different world now, a very changing world now and it is time to bring stability, a strength of character, a strength of wisdom, a strength of love, into the lives of the lost souls and there are many thousands all around you, my friends, very lost indeed. Can you be empathetic? Can you come with sympathy? Are you able to relate to their dilemmas?

Indeed, each of you wishes to serve God, wishes to serve this church, which is a house of God. I encourage you to seek your own guidance, to seek the guidance of your hearts and souls and to seek the guidance of God and you will find your way. New insights and new inspirations will open to all of you and you will be reinvigorated, somewhat challenged, of course. Change is difficult, but doors will open when your hearts are open and willing to listen to your brothers and sisters who are eager to have a place of refuge, a sanctuary of light. Their hearts will be drawn and when there are enough who are drawn, they will draw in others and the congregation will grow. In fact, in time you will not have enough chairs to bring the people in and you will have to do services in shifts of congregations. Would this not bring great happiness to you, my friends, to have the fullness of the spiritual message being given and heard in all its glory and light?

My friends, seek out God. Seek God in your hearts. Seek out His Love and through this, everything else will be made possible. For it is a soul penitent in love that will hear the Will of God and know it and live it. God's Love is available to each and every one of you in an abundance you could not imagine, in a power that will transform and change you. You will know the way to go. You will know the words to speak. You will give the messages of love and reassurance to those who are seeking and who are lost.

My beloved friends, we have brought these dear souls to you to help you see that way toward the fulfilment of the purpose of this church. God's Hand is upon it. You feel the light that is here. You know the power of the spirit that has manifested here. All the signs and wonders are with you. Now it is time to step boldly forward and the Will of God, God's plan, God's desire, to utilise each and every one of you as a channel of love, truth and light, to step forward, to do so bravely and with faith and with trust and we will do the work, my friends.

We will do the work required to draw in the souls and there will be great joy, resounding joy, at what will come to this beautiful church. For in its beauty, in its openness, its friendliness and its desire to serve in light, there will be a resounding bell that shall sound in this place and draw the souls to you. You will know, feel and express all that will be given, all that will flow in the name of truth, light and love. Hear God's words. It is simple. It is direct. It beckons your soul to respond. You must, my beloved friends. You must.

God bless you. I am Ruth and I am very happy to speak to you today. I am very happy that you listen. I am very happy to be here amongst you all, beautiful souls that you are, a beautiful light that is this church, its foundation, its desire to be a light for the world and so it shall be. God bless you all in the Love of God, in the light of truth, in the journey of service. God bless you all.
