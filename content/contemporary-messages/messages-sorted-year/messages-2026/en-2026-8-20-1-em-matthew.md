# The Sweetness of Progression

August 20<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Matthew received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Matthew, your brother in Christ, which means I am a redeemed soul. My soul is filled with Divine Love. I bring you my love to help uplift you, to join you in this prayer, to talk to you about the sweetness of progression. Once those blocks are removed you have the capacity, the ability, and could have the experience of feeling the joy, the sweetness of a renewed life in this love that permeates your soul and continues in its process of transformation. For you, dear souls, are transforming each time you pray, as you are praying today. Your deep desire to progress your souls brings you all that you need, including healings, so you may see as those negativities are leaving you so that you may commit not to engage the way you have previously engaged. It is a thorough cleansing of your soul, your spirit, your mind and even your body. Why wouldn’t it be? It is the essence of God, your Creator, your Caregiver, your Overseer who loves you beyond your imaginings.

You may feel a bumpy road in this process but as you do, remember what comes afterwards is that part of your soul is renewed and enlivened. You have more love, joy, peace, truth and life. Isn’t that worth the bumpy road —enduring that short period of time that these dark parts of you are leaving to become rejuvenated, transformed and blessed? Have faith in these healings; that includes these healings of the Earth and those who live upon it. If they believe incorrectly that human beings are powerful, they certainly will appreciate with renewed awareness the power of the Earth and hopefully will recognize that God’s power is even greater.

This is all I have to say today as you reflect upon my words and upon the Earth’s ability to help people move forward in their spiritual lives. God bless you today and all days of your human lives and beyond. God bless you. I am Matthew, your brother, and I live in the Celestial Heavens with my brothers and sisters whose love for you is great. God bless you.
