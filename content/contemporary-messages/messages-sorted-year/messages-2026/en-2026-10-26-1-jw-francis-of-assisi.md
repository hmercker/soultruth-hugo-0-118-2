# The Seventh Sense and Prayer

October 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Francis of Assisi received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, your brother, Francis.

I come in the love of God, in joy, in praise. Let us be still. Let us allow our busy minds to step back, that our souls may be ascendant, and our longings may take precedence over all else in the simplicity of prayer to the Heavenly Father. May the Father’s Holy Spirit be upon you, bringing His love into your souls, receiving it as children.

I was speaking to my brother here earlier, reminding him of the message I had given him a few years ago, which you may know of. I talked about the five senses that most people perceive the world through, and I told him the sixth sense could be called intuition: when you sense something, you have a feeling about something, you have a hunch, and that, when utilized, can be very helpful. One can avoid danger or be simply guided to the right place. But the seventh sense is one you would call soul perception, and this is a gift to a soul who has prayed and received the Father’s love and sits in the great Holy presence, even if only for a moment. This place where all things become clear, where your perception becomes clarified, spiritually, materially, and brings one a sense of wonder, a sense of the miraculous, the workings of God, and alignment with the will of the Creator, and as you say, being in the flow.

It’s a bright sunny day; the weather is perfect. There are just enough clouds to remind you of us in the heavens. The rains come and nurture the earth. The animals are in harmony within their own paradigm, and all is well. Wouldn’t it be a great thing that all beings could enjoy such bliss, such wonder? And this, of course, is the great possibility for every soul.

When you band together, you bring us into your sphere, and together we are protected in the bubble of Divine Love. One can do this on their own by simply removing distractions and by taking a busy mind to the ear of God, by committing one’s soul to the great soul. When we speak of the depths of the soul and the openness of one’s heart and even an open mind, we are pointing the way. We are showing you, whenever we can, to take the time, to let the world go. And as I have said many times, one does not need to live in a monastery or an ashram or a temple, but one can create a temple, ashram, and monastery of one’s own soul. This beautiful connection aligning with the soul of God, allowing the blessing of love to come in, that your heart may be at ease, your soul awakened, released from the bondage, whether it is of the world or self-created, into the joy of Divine Love.

I will tell you also that I had many beliefs that were in error. For instance, I thought that if I could suffer like Jesus and have his wounds, which I did psychosomatically produce or induce, that somehow this would make me unto like him. But you know, as I simply prayed with my brothers, sometimes just alone, I felt the transcendence of my soul and could let these beliefs be what they may.  And so I say to those whom you know who may believe in many errors, many dogmatic things that are out of harmony or not the truth, that all can experience and receive the love of God in prayer. So, we encourage this, and we invite all, and I encourage you to invite all into prayer. For the benefits are joy and freedom and transformation.

I shall step back now, and I thank my brother as always. May God bless this beautiful, beautiful circle of humble and sincere souls. It is my privilege to come and be with you and remind you that your light is a blessing to this world. Go in peace.

With all my love, I am Francis. God bless you.
