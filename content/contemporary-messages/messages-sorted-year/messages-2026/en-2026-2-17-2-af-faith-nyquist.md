# Be Brave and Carry the Flame of Truth

February 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

Thank you for that beautiful prayer, my brother. I am Faith and I come to pray with you and to speak with you. My beloved friends, you must carry the flame of truth wherever you go. It warms our heart, those of us in the Celestial Kingdom, to see you continue to pray for this gift and to hold steadfast to your commitment for soul awakening and transformation with the Father's Love. Each of you is a bright light. Each of you carries that testimony, that beautiful affirmation that God's Love is real and God's Love upholds His children. Those who are willing, those who desire this blessing, are blessed mightily by God's Hand and the Holy Spirit. You continue upon your pursuits, your desire to be within the grace of God, to understand fully the power of His Love to heal and to bring that transformation of soul that will awaken you to the many blessings and gifts that God has placed within.

When I was on Earth, and it was not that long ago, I spoke of the Divine Love and the Truth. I did not fully comprehend nor appreciate the words that I spoke. Yet, I used those very same words, that God's Love is there for you. When my time came to pass into the spirit world, I progressed into the Fifth Sphere and I was delighted, overjoyed with the gift of that place and that upliftment that comes with God's blessing upon it. My beloved friends, as you continue to strive for this truth and receive this gift, would it not be wonderful that you too might land in the Fifth Sphere, or even beyond? It is possible, but it requires dedication, requires you to set forth into the world and be God's channel, God's instruments in the world. That may be accomplished in a variety of ways, for each of you is unique in your gifts, your personalities and your abilities.

When you walk in faith with God and seek to serve God in all the ways that God desires you to serve, then you will find deep fulfilment and surprising outcomes. For God does not want you to hide this truth away, to be alone, to not express love and be a channel of love for others. God will provide you with opportunities, ways in which you may express yourself and give to many. Often these ways are challenging, for they are not things that you are comfortable with or common in your life. Yet, with faith, hope and desire, trusting in God, you can step forward and God will give you the strength, the tenacity and the confidence to do so. Whether you speak in a crowd or to an individual, you will have the words required. You will be the light glowing and expressing that special gift that God has given you. Those you speak to will feel this and will, at times, seek you out, want to be close, because they feel the comfort and the warmth emanating from you, your love expressed in its beautiful and unique way.

Allow yourself to be guided by God. Seek to serve. Seek to be truly His instrument. There will be outcomes and answers to your prayers that will be surprising indeed. None of God's instruments of the past knew their destiny really, but they knew they had a purpose. They realised there was something within them that needed to be expressed and they wanted to walk a path of service for God. These are the simple ingredients that will make you a success as God's instruments. It does not require you to be beautiful, materially attractive and have all those accoutrements of someone who is famous. No, this is not how God is or works through the individual. It is often humble and simple. It is often in a unique situation and circumstance that brings you to connect with another and, at times, you may speak to a crowd, you may have the opportunity to speak the Truth of God's Love.

What is important here is knowing that when you walk in faith, humility and grace of God, God will utilise your light, what you have to offer, in ways that may not be so obvious, but at times very obvious. You will know a joy within your heart as you continue to serve God and to be that light and to offer comfort, peace, truth and love to another, or maybe many others. Think of God's plan for you, my friends. There is much that you might do. There are many opportunities that may come to you, but unless you are willing and you allow yourself to be open to God's plan, that unique plan He has for you, then you will not know the rewards, the joy and the effects of your instrumentality expressed in the world.

God will love you no matter what. We will love you no matter what. Yet, as we have been telling you so often, in so many ways, the world needs you. The world is in desperation. The world lacks love, comfort, light, peace and you, as God's instruments, can offer these things to many. Pray that you might be used. Pray that you might be open to God's guidance and see the path before you lit and clear, and you will embark upon a journey like none other, one that is so beautifully glorious, wondrous in its expression.

We want you to know this joy, to know the fulfillment that is being God's instrument in the world and you will be happy, fulfilled, joyful upon that journey. We all pray for each of you to be upon that path, to be expressive and beautiful in your expression, to be wise yet humble, to be loving and gentle yet firm with the truth, to be that beautiful light expressed through each one of you. My beloved friends, you have a great destiny in front of you. Think of what that is. Seek out your purpose and God will show you the way.

God bless you, my friends. I am Faith. Oh, I love you so. Beautiful souls, be strong and brave. Be forthright and filled with faith. All will come about as it should. I can assure you that, my friends. God has things well in hand. All you must do is follow and seek out His guidance and you will be astonished with the outcomes. God bless you, my friends. God bless you all. My love is with you.
