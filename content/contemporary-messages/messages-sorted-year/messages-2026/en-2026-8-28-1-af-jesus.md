# It is Time to Step Forward in Divine Service

August 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your friend and brother. I am Jesus. My beloveds, I want you to continue to serve God, to serve the cause of this truth that is so needed in your world and to put aside all reluctance, all resistance and to ask God in your prayers that He may guide you along the path of service, that you may see your way forward. That even upon that road that may seem difficult, even impossible, if this is God's Will, God's Will will be done and you will find your way through and overcome all obstacles that may be in your way.

My beloveds, the time is now in service in bringing truth to others, even though it may seem that many are resistant and will not listen and will not see. Yet, within their hearts and their souls is a deep longing and a deep dissatisfaction that comes because of the hollowness within them and you will show them the way to fill up that space, that part of them, their souls, that are longing for the Father's Love and acknowledgement from God. There is so much work to do and you who have found your way, you who have found the fulfilment of God's promise with His Love, you must now step forward in the world and seek to serve. Seek to be God's instruments as channels of Love. For how else will the world be healed?

How will the world change? How will all those conditions of darkness, pain, suffering and error be rectified? It is not that God will send some lightning bolts of truth into the hearts of men. This is not how His laws work. It is through His instruments, whether it be we in the Celestial Kingdom or you upon Earth, we must all work together to serve God, to be His instruments and that will come and you will see from your own eyes and perspective what that is through you, your unique gifts, your unique personalities, your unique souls.

All of these aspects of yourself will shine through when you walk in faith, my beloveds. When you serve in faith, when you desire to be God's instruments with deep desire and longing, then the road opens and God's Light shines upon it and it becomes clear to you. You must overcome your reluctance. You must put aside that which is of the mind, the intellect and the perceptions of the material world, from that material perspective and truly be God's instruments, channels of Love, channels of the soul in relationship with God. In this way, God will show you, God will demonstrate to you how these laws work, how the Laws of Love may be utilised and obeyed, so as to bring you harmony, joy and peace and to bring others the same.

It is a matter of exercising your will and your desire. It is the matter of practice, repeating these efforts, though every situation is unique. Yet, the solution is often the same. It is giving love where love is needed. It is bringing peace and comfort where these things are needed. It is bringing truth to those who hunger to know truth. It is being a light in the world in many ways, many aspects of your being lit by God's Love and expressed in this way. We continue, my beloveds, upon this journey of service, of serving God, of serving your own soul's desire to love your brothers and sisters, to be a light in the world. When that commitment is made, when the faith that you carry is strong, when you are able to walk as the light, a clear challenge for God, there is no limit to what you might do. There are many blessings that will flow and you will be carried upon a great wave of light, love and truth and you will know deep peace and joy that abounds and is expressed in such great measure that you will not know yourself.

You will be surprised as to what you might do and how you might be in joy in the light and wisdom of God's Touch. These things come abundantly when you are in alignment with God's Will and you subscribe to what God wishes of you. My beloveds, it is time to truly test these waters, to truly make that decision to serve God. Not in small ways necessarily, but in big ways, in ways that are affecting your brothers and sisters, in profound and deep blessings, as God's channels, changing their lives, changing their perspective. This is what I mean by big ways, profound and deep ways, to serve God. For when you change a soul like this, as God's instruments, then that will reverberate through their lives and will bring change to all who are around them. This, as the pebble falls into the pond and makes its ripples, it widens, these circles of influence widen and widen. When there are many pebbles dropped into many ponds, you can see how the world could change, how many will be affected by your efforts, by God's efforts through you.

My beloveds, think of these things. Have faith, have trust, have a desire, enthusiasm for what God might use you for. The potentials that you have no idea exists within you may indeed come to the fore and be expressed in powerful and beautiful ways, in loving ways, bringing truth and blessings to many. These things I ask of you. For I know how God may use you. I see each one of you. I see the potentials of your soul. I see what might be and I have great hope and faith of what could be, because you might make that choice, might step forward in faith and might ask God to use you as His instruments in the world.

Many blessings will come to you and many blessings will come to others as a result of your decision to serve God in this way. My beloveds, I am your brother and friend. I am Jesus and I come to encourage you. I come to say that each one of you are beautiful, powerful, beloved channels of the Father and it is for you to discover how deep the length and breadth of what you might do in the world. May you be blessed, my beloveds, truly blessed, deeply blessed, as you continue to seek out God and to seek the avenues of service in love, truth and light.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am with you. I bless you with strength, with fortitude, with deep soul perceptions, deep wisdom and deep desire to serve. My beloveds, I come to bless you, to uphold you and to encourage you upon the path, path divine. God bless you all. My love is with you always and evermore, I am with you always. God bless you in the grace of His Love. God bless you.
