# Open Your Soul to the Gift of Divine Love

July 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Blessed be the children who seek the Truth of God, that their souls may open and receive this great benediction of Love from our Creator. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come in this Circle of Light. I come to this world in need. I come to all who are seeking truth. I come in love. For my desire is to lead all God's children into the Truth of His Love, that they may follow the road Divine to at-onement with their Creator. I am a light in the darkness, as are you, my beloveds. We must shine our lights and show the way beyond the error, beyond that place where so many are lost and unaware, to a place of peace, to a place where God is there, giving the blessing, the comfort, the great comforter of His Love.

May you join me there, my brethren, that great Hall of Peace, where we may go and replenish our souls, the refreshment of His Love, that blessing, those Living Waters that shall remain within our souls for eternity. We are His children truly and He loves us truly. Open your hearts and souls, my brethren, to receive His gift of Love, to know it as it is, its power and glory, its Light and peace and all that is part of this gift. May you come to know it, my brethren. Know it truly and surely within you, that its strength and transformation may lead you on the path of redemption, may lead you to that place where joy abounds and you know truly that you are loved. A gift given, my beloveds, a gift that shall last for all eternity, a gift that is our Father's Touch to each of His children who so wish to receive it, who are open and willing to know this Touch Divine, awakening, healing and transforming each soul that follows this path, each soul willing and open, yearning for this gift.

We are truly brothers and sisters upon the path and we shall be for all eternity, my beloveds. All eternity, we shall walk the Path Divine and know its glories, joys, blessings and benefits that shall multiply and expand exponentially within us, through us and around us, bringing light, bringing light to the dark world, bringing light to our dark souls that are craving, desiring to be free from the impediments, from the shackles of error and pain. It is given to each one of us freely, with no conditions other than you may make that choice to know it, to acknowledge it, to receive it and to live its blessings, its effects upon you.

God bless you, my beloveds. May the Holy Spirit bring this Divine Love into your souls, lifting you up beyond this earthly plane, lifting you into light, to divinely bless the Presence of God with you, the holiness of His Touch felt deeply and profoundly upon you, within you and around you. God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you in all the glories of God's blessings and Love. I am with you. God bless you.
