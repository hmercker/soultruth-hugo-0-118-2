# You Become Stronger and Stronger as You Stretch Toward Light

March 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beautiful friends. Orion is with you once again. The conditions in your world are in flux and they are stretched, stretched toward light and also stretched toward darkness. This counteractive condition continues to work in all of your lives. You are making efforts to be in light, to be in harmony and to be with God, but also the conditions of the world are bearing upon you in adverse ways. The tension builds between these two conditions and you find yourself somewhere in between, sometimes in greater light, sometimes in greater darkness.

This is the unfortunate reality of your world. Yet, if you did not have the faith that you carry and the blessings that come, your angel friends, stellar friends, all the beautiful brightness that is around you, I wonder if you would succumb to the darkness like so many. It is heartbreaking to see such suffering in your world, conditions that have built over millennia, conditions that come with free will choices, conditions that are victimising many. The darkness continues to proliferate. The choices continue to be made, but you have the anchoring of God in your life, the strength of His Love and His blessings upon you and within you. You stretch toward the light. You strain and you put your desires into prayer and seek out relief from the pain and darkness of this world and that which is within your own personal world.

We are there to help you as you stretch and strain toward the light. We are there to help you see and recognise that there is indeed light and that your efforts are not wasted or unheeded. Prayers are heard, responses given. Even though you desire to be free from all of these conditions, might I suggest that because you must struggle, because of the strain, the stretching in either direction, that you become stronger as you choose light more often, as you stretch more diligently toward light. This increases the strength and solidity of your soulful desires and light. This is the way it is in your world. In ours, it is very different indeed, but it is a great learning for us to observe you, to see the tenacity, the strength, the consistency, the eagerness and desire that you carry to be in the light.  You are strong, my beloved friends. You seek out that which is good and harmonious. At times you fail, but at many times you are successful.

My beloved friends, we continue to be with you, to uphold you and support you. We know your struggles. They are quite obvious. We know those habits within you that still weaken you and drag you into a darker condition. We know the yearnings of your soul that pull you from that condition. In other words, we are well aware of your humanness, your vulnerabilities and the inconsistencies in your life, but we see continued progress toward greater light, releasing those conditions that hold you back from the light. It is a subtle change, given a day by day reckoning of your progress, but it is toward light with some back steps toward darkness. Yet, generally, the stretching is toward greater light and truth, love, harmony and peace.

This gives us hope, as we see your world capitulate into darkness, ever greater darkness. We see those of you, and not just you that I speak to, but many others, who are seeking light, who want harmony and peace in the world, who want to learn how to love themselves, love their children, their brothers and sisters, all who are in their lives. They seek this. In seeking, they ignite the Law of Attraction that brings us and others. They benefit from their efforts in many ways, deep and abiding ways that continue to help the soul awaken as you seek out the great gift from the Creator, His Love. You are awakening slowly but surely and the light grows and the harmony establishes itself within your life.

Yes, there is still much work. There is still a way to go, but each day marks a milestone in your steps forward and so you are blessed, my friends, because you wish to be blessed, you make the efforts to be blessed and you are in that neediness, that vulnerability, that motivates you to seek out God and the blessings, the strength, the healing and the peace that comes with God's Touch. We have great compassion for you and great love. We continue to walk with you. Many surround you, protecting you, bringing their light and love. You are uplifted, my friends, in many ways. You shall continue to be uplifted. Continue on this path toward greater light.

May the blessings of God's Hand be upon you, truly, truly guiding you along this path of light, helping you to see and know and feel, experience all the wonderment that is the soul being awakened and transformed. Blessings to you, my friends. Orion loves you. You are blessed and you continue to seek greater light. This is sure and the direction is set. May you be blessed, my friends, and know the joy of it, the peace of it and the truth of it. God bless you in Love.
