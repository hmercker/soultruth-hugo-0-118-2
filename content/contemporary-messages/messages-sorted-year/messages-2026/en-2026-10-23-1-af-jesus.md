# Be Instruments of Truth, Light and Love

October 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of God's Love be yours. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to be with you in prayer. I come to affirm your commitment and your desire of soul to be with God and to open to a fresh and powerful inflowing of His Love. I come because this world is in need of light and unfortunately, this beautiful planet that God created has been sullied by human intervention and decisions to use this world for their own good and benefit. Yet, it does not seem to be so. Rather, it is a dark place. I and all the angels and all those in the higher spheres of the spirit world are in light and we do not agree with this inequity. All should be in light. All should be blessed in light and love and have peace. Those who live upon the Earth should live in a rich and satisfying way, that all of them, all that they are, may be fulfilled in harmony and love.

We come to you. We come to you in prayer. We come to speak the truth. We come to open the door so that indeed the light may flood into your world. Indeed, the awakenings, the inspirations, the blessings, the healings, the upliftments may come, not only to you, my brethren, but to all in your world. A great flowing, an opening, a cascading of light, love and blessings for the world. We all work diligently for this as God's instruments. This is God's Will, that the flow of His blessings, the Touch of His Love, the light that must come and be a part of this world, is given freely and abundantly.

The tragedy is that many do not see, feel or understand what is being given. Though much flows, little is accepted and embraced and this saddens all of heaven. For we know what could be given, what can be upon your world. Yet, so many turn a blind eye. So many do not understand, not because they wish to. It is because they are ignorant and unable to see with the eyes of their soul what is being given by God, their Creator, the Great Oversoul that has given souls to humanity for a purpose, for a reason. It is for us and for you, my beloveds, to work diligently to inform and awaken your brothers and sisters to who they truly are, to awaken themselves to that part of them that has long been asleep, long dormant. Because the soul has not been nurtured, the soul has not been awakened or acknowledged, and so it is our job, our duty, our desire to work till the work is done, to make efforts until no more effort may be made, to walk amongst humanity until God sees that we have accomplished our task.

All of these things continue to compel us to walk amongst humanity, to speak to you now, to pray with you, to help you and assist you till humanity, all of humanity, can see and understand the choice that is given by God, that they may walk free from the burdens, the darkness and the disharmony that has been created by the gift of free will and the efforts of man combined, to make this so that they may see what they have done, that all of humanity may understand there is another way. There is a way to salvation, love and freedom from darkness, fear and all that is of the human condition that is not meant by God and by God's creation.

My beloveds, it is a tremendous task. It is much that we must do, but here we are, beloveds. You have made effort day in and day out, consistent with the truth, great efforts to be with God, to be in the blessings, peace and Love of God. We toil, my beloveds. We toil until the day is done, shall do so whether you are on the Earth or in the spirit. We shall continue. For though we have been mightily blessed with the Father's Love, it is our duty and it is our desire to share this truth to whomever desires to hear it.

Yet indeed, when we speak the truth, no matter how simply, they have heard and in this, choice is given. So must you, my beloveds, speak this truth. Though many will turn away, they have heard what it is that God offers to them. It is then their choice whether they will follow or reject this. It is not for you and it is not your responsibility that they may do this. It is theirs alone, but it is your responsibility to bring the bell toll of truth to the world so that they may choose. May each of you be strong enough to ring that bell loudly, to bring that truth clearly, to not worry about rejection or ridicule, but to be strong in faith and to be God's beloved and beautiful instruments of truth, light and love. This is our task. This is God's plan for the salvation of humanity.

Are you willing, my beloveds, to step forth and speak and demonstrate the truth? Each of you has been mightily blessed. Each of you continues on that journey toward at-onement with God. There are many opportunities that God gives to you to open that door, to bring that truth, to be a channel of love for others in the world. Do not hide away. Do not put a covering over your light. Be bright, joyful and truthful for God, to thank God for the blessings of His Love upon you, the opportunity for salvation given through many ways and means. Yet, your prayers were answered through some channel, through some effort, blessed and accomplished through God's handiwork, through His angels and through mortals. So much to be thankful for. So much you may give in that gratitude.

God bless you all, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus, and I love you. I am with you, never to leave. For we are embarked upon an eternal journey, one that we will do together, one where love bonds us forever. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you all.
