# The Gift of God’s Will Imparted Into Your Souls Will Guide You Forward

June 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Johnson City Retreat, Johnson City, NY, USA

Moses received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am your brother Moses. I welcome this gathering of souls who are blessed in so many ways. Yes, you have many gifts and you will have many more bestowed upon you. In God's giving, there is the gift of His Will that may be imparted upon your souls, such a gift that comes into your souls will allow you to know, to see, to feel and to carry out God's Will before you. This is a powerful gift that comes, that allows you to be who you truly are in service to your Creator in assisting in the transformation and transition of the souls upon this Earth.

This is a great gift for each one of you. Open yourselves to allow this gift. Embrace this gift and pray to recognize this gift as it unfolds within you. This gift will serve you well as you walk not only your journey, but through this time of transition of the Earth. I say to you, bring it to your awareness and bring it with the Love that is within your souls. It can be responded to in no other way than with the Love of God.

You are greatly blessed, true children of God, more than you can conceive of. Pray to know the truth of this gift and allow it to be utilized as God intends. I am your brother Moses. I walk with you and will continue to walk with you throughout this life, assisting you and manifesting God's Will upon this Earth.

God bless you, God bless you, God bless you, true children of God. God bless you and thank you for being willing to receive this gift. I am with you and my love is passing to you at this time.
