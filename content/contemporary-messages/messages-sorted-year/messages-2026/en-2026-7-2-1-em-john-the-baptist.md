# True Spirituality

July 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

John the Baptist received by Elizabeth Mack

Here I am, John the Baptist. I come to you once again from the Celestial Heavens, where my Father's Love is raining down upon the souls in this circle and in many other souls that are open and longing for His Love.

We come to you to help you, to teach you and to bring you closer to your understanding of your true relationship with your Heavenly Father, for there is nothing greater or more important than this relationship that you foster every time you pray. Your thoughts of God, even your beliefs, sometimes erroneous about God, are still reaching to come closer because it is what your soul desires and longs for. This Love that changes everything within you is what your soul seeks, your desires reaching to be at one with God.

These are the mainstays of your soul progression; your longings for God's Love, to be at one with God, to seek the Truth of your Father, your desire to be at one with God. This is spirituality. This is the true spirituality, that connection to God and that desire to be with God. This is spirituality and this comes from your soul. Your spirit body is only a covering for your soul to express itself and your material body is only a way to express your soul as an individual in the material. True spirituality is you, your soul, longing, desiring the closeness, the Love, the communion with God. Take this in deeply. Pray on this, so that you truly understand what spirituality is.

I am thankful to come here to help you understand what spirituality is, as you have been considering it for a while. We hope all of you will consider what your soul may desire to know and pray to God to know it. You will be given all that you desire and more because that is the nature of our Heavenly Father to give to His children unceasingly, willingly and truly.

May God bless you. May you entreat the Father to bring you closer in every prayer. I am John the Baptist and I am grateful for this time with you. May the Father's blessings be received by your souls and integrated into your very being and your expressions of love. I give you my blessings.
