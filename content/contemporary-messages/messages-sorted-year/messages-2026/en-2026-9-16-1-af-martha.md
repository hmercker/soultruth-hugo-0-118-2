# Martha Counsels Harrow Spiritualist Church Volunteers and Members

September 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Harrow Spiritualist Church Retreat, Harrow, London, UK

Martha received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved souls, children of God. I am Martha and I come to be with you in your prayers. I come with many who are in this church to support and uplift. For there are many of us who continue to work through this vessel of light, who continue to minister to those who come seeking comfort and guidance, light and love. I continue to encourage you, all of you, to contribute to the work that is being done here, for the work never ends. The need is great and when God's Love flows, there is great healing and peace, upliftment and truth.

Do not underestimate the power of this beautiful place, blessed and ordained by God, a centre of light in the world. May you joyfully go about your business, the work that you have taken on, the Love that flows. For when this is in alignment with God's Will, in alignment with God's Truth, then all benefit. All who enter therein will benefit. May the doors open wide and may your embraces be full of love and gratitude. For when God brings the soul through those doors, God gives an opportunity for those who wish to minister upon them to be God's instruments. Yet, also, there is something to learn, something to gain from that interaction, from those blessings, for the giver and the receiver.

Be true to your guidance and seek to open the way for further work, for much is coming, much work for all those willing. Many blessings to flow and for love to grow in this place, multiplied exponentially in all the ways, manifestations of love and light. Beloved friends, those who have travelled far, I thank you for coming. Those who are the mainstays of this church, I thank you for your consistency and dedications. For all those who are in need, I say, come to God with your needs. Pray and ask for the blessings to flow, for you will not empty the great vessel of God's Love and blessings. For they are infinite and all who are in need are worthy of blessings. All who ask will receive and for those who give, they too will receive. Blessings to all. Great peace to all. His Love flowing, healing and changing all of you.

God bless you. God bless you, my beloveds, beautiful friends. I am Martha. My love is with you. My love is with you. God bless you.
