# Have Faith and Make Efforts to Establish this Truth in the World

February 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Peter, Maria & Jesus received by Al Fike

**Peter:** May the gift of the Father's Love be yours, beautiful seeking souls, souls for truth, for God and for love. I am Peter. I come to you today to encourage you to continue in your pursuit of God, your pursuit of the awakening of your soul and the pursuit of truth.

For how will the truth be established on Earth, unless those who are dedicated put in the time, the effort, the expressions and prayers that require that a foundation be built upon the Earth? Jesus dedicated me to be the Rock, the one who laid the foundations for his church. Indeed, I fulfilled my purpose, but the truth that Jesus taught has long been lost. Now it is time for that truth to re-emerge and be clear in the minds and hearts of all people, that this gift is offered no matter what religion, no matter what perspective, no matter man or woman, no matter dark or bright in conditions. This love is available to all. It is for you, my beloved friends, stalwarts in the truth, to make those efforts to establish the knowledge of this truth in the world.

Indeed, as you look at the vastness of humanity and the complexity of your world, a world far more complex than when I lived upon it, I am sure many of you feel that it is a formidable task, one that is even impossible. Yet, I say to you, my beloved friends, that with enough faith, with enough dedication and prayer and receiving of this gift within your soul so that it is awakened and transformed by it, then you will have the means and wherewithal to do the task at hand. God and His angels are with you. That spiritual force of love, the wisdom, the guidance, the peace, the healing and the light is all with you.

My beloved friends, none of you is bereft of these gifts and these blessings, but it is for you to choose to express these things in the world in ways that you are guided, in ways that allow the gifts within your soul to open and be expressed, in the way you walk in the world. Your demeanour, your light and your capacity to love will bring the communication, the expression of truth, in everything that you do. Do not be shy and have the trepidation that causes you not to express this beautiful gift that resides within you. God wants you to be fully who you are, not to express certain aspects that you might find socially acceptable in the world, but to be fully who you are. As you accept all of you, all these parts of you, and His Love infiltrates these parts, then you will be that wonderful and wondrous individual that God intends for you to be.

As is always the case in your world, it is fear and reluctance that does not allow the full expression of the soul to walk in the world in its own unique and beautiful way. Yet, as I have said, you have an army of angels. You have God's Hand upon you. You have the truth residing within your soul and the knowledge of that truth within your mind. What is holding you from speaking and demonstrating this truth? I say to you, my brothers and sisters, walk in the world as a light. Allow your light to shine. Allow God to guide you. Allow the flow of His Love through you. In all of this, you are doing God's work and you are a disciple of the Master. I speak to you today, encouraging you to step forward in a world that has gone awry, is dark, chaotic and has little regard for their own souls and for the Creator of all the universe. It is time to walk as a light, to speak the truth and to gather those individuals who are willing to listen around you and be God's instrument, so that He may work through you and that you may speak the truth that will ignite the desire within the souls of many.

My beloved and beautiful friends, I once led the charge for this truth after the tragic passing of my beautiful brother, Jesus. I urge you to do so now. The world is in far greater need than it was then. The world is on the brink of great darkness and destruction. It needs your prayers. It needs your presence, your instrumentality, your ministry and your gifts to help bring it back from the brink of more chaos and destruction. The time is now, my friends. Do not linger. Do not hesitate, but step forward. If you cannot do so physically, then pray. Double your prayers, triple your prayers, for the world, for those around you and for all that must be done, all the efforts that are needed to transform the minds and hearts of men into something that is of light and harmony and love. We will be with you, beloved souls. We do not stand at a distance watching. We try to come close. The more you are in prayer, the more Love that you receive within your soul, the closer we will be and the more reinforcement and light will be with you so that you have the strength, the bravery, the desire and the guidance to do this work.

May God bless you on your journeys, beloved souls. I am Peter. I come to you in all earnestness to encourage you upon the path and to tell you that we will embrace you as you make greater effort, so you bring greater influence and greater blessings. God bless you, my beloved friends. My love is with you. Oh, the love of the angels and the infinite Love of God is with you. May you carry the cloak of truth, the regalia of love, the Light that is God's Touch upon you as you set out into the world. These things will be a part of you and God will guide you and protect you and show you the way. God bless you all. Peter loves you.

**Mary:** I am Mary. I want you to know how much I love you, how much the angels love you and how much God loves you. For without this Love and the power that it brings, what our beloved Peter has taught will not come to pass. It requires the foundation of Love within your soul and how that will ignite and open the faculties and abilities of your soul. Then you will be guided and you will be equipped with what is necessary to reach the hearts of humanity, to change the tide of darkness and, with humility, listen to the Will of God. You will be a formidable force in the world and a beautiful light.

May you see that within yourself and allow it to emerge in all its beauty, to walk in the world as a light, to be a speaker of truth, to be a channel of love. This is what God asks of you. This is what the angels speak of. It is up to you, my beloveds, my beautiful children of light, to be that light and to do that work. God bless you, my beloveds. Mary loves you. Oh, how I love you. God bless you.

**Jesus:** I come to bless you, my beloveds. Your brother and friend, Jesus, is here. I come to bless each one of you, to uplift you and reinforce that beautiful gift within you that is His Love, the gift that shall empower you to do this work. I come to bless you, beloved souls, children of God, my brothers and sisters in this work. God bless you. God bless you.
