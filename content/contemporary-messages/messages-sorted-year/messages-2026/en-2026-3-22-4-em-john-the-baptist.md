# If Only Humanity Knew

March 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, TX, USA

John the Baptist received by Elizabeth Mack

I am one with the Father in my love for His children upon the Earth, where I once lived as His messenger. I am John the Baptist and I come to join you in this prayer. Your desires for God's Love and blessings have brought us here and I come forward to speak to you. It is a challenge to speak to your brothers and sisters who are in the flesh, for they are so immersed in their own will, their own personal desires and their longings for those material things, whether it be land or other possessions that are so available upon the Earth.

But these things do not last. They do not last like the Father's Love. They do not fulfill as the Father's Love. Even those people to whom Earth people are close to, even those relationships, in many, many cases, come to an end long before people pass into the spirit realms. The only thing that lasts forever, is the Love of God in your souls. If only those in the flesh could understand that they have souls and those souls need to be nourished and how to nourish those souls. If only they could open their minds, their hearts, their souls, to receive the greatest blessing that is theirs for the asking.

This is a challenge for all of you true children who live in the flesh upon the Earth. It is a mighty challenge, perhaps mightier than my challenge when I lived on the Earth. I was by myself and lived a scant life except for my prayers.

Children, use that love within your souls to touch the souls of your brothers and sisters and pray for them. Help them. They are in such need. They are in such misery. I do not speak about those who are immediately around you, although many of them are without this Love, but I speak about all the souls upon the Earth. They do not understand what is happening as the changes are flowing from God into the Earth with His Love. They do not understand and they are full of fear and anxiety.

You have the answers for them. You can help them. You must, for the salvation of humanity. It was agreed upon before you entered this life, so do reach out. Speak to them, but lovingly. Touch their souls with your love for that is the power of God in your souls. We will be with you. We will help you. We are your friends. We are God's protectors of His children upon the Earth. We are your guardians.

God bless you, true children of God. God bless you. Take to heart all that I have said and let it sink deep, deeply into your souls, so you will know the truth of what I say to you. May God bless you. May His Love fall upon you and may your soul drink up, drink up the nourishment that is His Love. I am John the Baptist. My love is with you. God bless you.
