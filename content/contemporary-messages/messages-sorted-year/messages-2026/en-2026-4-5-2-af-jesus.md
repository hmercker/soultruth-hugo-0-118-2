# Easter Sunday: Live and Share the Truth of Divine Love

April 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours, my beloveds. I come. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come. I make a special appointment to be with you, my beloveds, for generally all of this worship and error that comes in these days, I often decide to remove myself from the earthly plan. Yet, for you, I come. For you, I am here in prayer and in blessings. Indeed, the message that I brought so long ago continues to be alive today in the hearts and souls of all of you and those who follow the way of God's Love. This brings me great joy, for I came to Earth and had the trials and tribulations and blessings of the earthly life in order to establish this truth in the hearts and minds of humanity. When I left the Earth, much of that truth left with me. Indeed, those who followed me made great effort to establish the teachings which I shared and, in ways, this was successful. Most certainly, my moral laws were shared and understood, but the great Truth, the redemption of the soul through the inflowing of God's Love, was lost for many years.

Now comes the restoration of that knowledge. Now comes your vital voice, your powerful example, your beautiful light to re-establish this truth amongst humanity, to do so at a time when it is more needed than ever, to do so in order to fulfil God's plan and Will for humanity. Follow the way of this truth. Make it your daily expression and commitment. Do not falter, my beloveds, but carry this torch held high so that all may see. Live this Love in such powerful and beautiful expressions that all who may encounter you may come to know this truth by your example. Be that light for God. Be that disciple of my ministry. Be the truth in all things, for the world desperately needs your example and your presence. The world cries out because so many are lost in pain, without comfort and without love. They engage themselves in ways that detract and distract from their true selves, finding ways that bring the illusion of happiness, the material creations of men designed to ignore that which is real and important and so you must continue to live the life that is of the soul, of the spirit, of the mind, joined together in truth and love and harmony. That way, you become a formidable presence in the world. That way, you may teach and demonstrate the truth. That way, God will utilise you in many ways.

The truth, as you well know, is simple. It can be understood by anyone who is willing to embrace this simple prayer, this simple truth that reaches God in ways that are powerful and unique, bringing this great gift of Love, bringing its transformative power into the souls of humanity. This knowledge must be understood and must be embraced. For what else will save this world from its darkness and pain? It is not the imagination and invention of humanity's gifts of mind and ability to manipulate the material. This only continues to sustain ignorance and a lack of connection with one's true self and with God, the Creator. A balance must be struck and when that balance is achieved, then the inventions of men will come into harmony with the intentions of God.

Until that day, disharmony prevails and darkness continues to blanket the world and keep the world from light. There are only the few brave souls, the ones willing to penetrate that darkness and come to God. These are a pitiful few compared to what must be in the world in order to change it irrevocably, permanently. Each of you who have penetrated this darkness are indeed blessed and needed to be within the flow of God's Will and plan for the salvation of humanity. You have been called many times, my beloveds, to step forward with this truth, to share it with humanity. I call you once again. You are the flock, I am the shepherd and we move together in the world. I offer my protection, my guidance, my love. God offers His blessings to us all. We must move forward, bringing the truth in whatever way will reach the souls of humanity.

It is so needed and required at this time, a world where chaos continues to reign and strengthens with each day, a world where wars, suffering and deprivation are so prevalent. Can we live with this, my brethren? Is this what we are willing to accept in the world? I say not. I do not accept the errors of humanity and therefore, every moment in my existence is dedicated to bring the truth forward, to be God's instrument in the world. I beseech you to do the same. Of course, you come from many places in the world, many life choices and many cultural differences, but it is the great power of God's Love that binds you together with one another and with God and I join you in this. I am a part and so, in the great power of love, what love brings to you personally, to your lives, may also bring much guidance and opportunity to serve humanity in love, to bring this truth forward in love.

I beseech you to go deeper each and every day, to truly honour your soul, to truly honour what God has given you, this gift of life, to truly honour what God intends for you to be and the instrumentality that exists in each one of you, unique, beautiful and powerful. Honour your life, my beloveds, by honouring what life is truly meant to be. Honour God by honouring the truth that God offers to you. Honour yourselves, for God has made something magnificent and so full of potential and gifts and abilities. Honour the world, for the world nurtures you. The world brings you life and you must honour this and do what you can to help sustain light in harmony and peace.

My beloveds, my brethren, we walk together. Indeed, each of you is loved and cared for. Each of you is guided and protected. Each of you knows the Touch of the Father's Love and each of you knows me as your true brother and friend. We have the comfort, the peace, the strength, the insight, the joy and the power of love in our lives. May we express this in all the ways and means that are possible in our lives. God will truly guide us upon that road and bring forward the possibilities of our expression, our instrumentality, our gifts to help uplift the world and bring light. We are one, my beloveds, one in the great prospect of fulfilling God's Will and plan for the world. We continue, my beloveds, to walk the Path Divine, to truly be His instruments, His children, His lights in the world. So we are, my beloveds, so we are. This shall only grow. Others shall join us. The light shall brighten. The truth will be made evident to all. This is our destiny together and we will fulfil it with every moment.

God bless you, my beloveds. I walk with you. I shepherd you along the road and I love you as my brethren. God bless you. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Kingdom, and your friend. God bless you, forevermore, in His Love.
