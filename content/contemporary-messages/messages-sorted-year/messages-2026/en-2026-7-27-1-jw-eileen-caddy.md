# Earth Changes and Aloneness in Community

July 27<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Eileen Caddy received by Jimbeau Walsh

My dear ones, it is I, Eileen Caddy. Your Aunty Eileen.

May the Love of God be upon us and in us and through us.  As you have been told concerning the spirit world as well as on earth, the law of attraction applies whereby souls who are in a similar condition are attracted to each other. On earth, even those souls who are out of harmony have a certain attraction to each other, though their coming together rarely affords a chance to progress. So, as in spirit where one in a better condition must lift up another ( in order to progress), this is also paralleled on the earth plane. We have noticed, and you may have noticed, that the intensity of events of change upon the earth has necessitated more souls coming together and helping one another. This might be, of course, as simple as someone having a flat tire, or as compassionate as feeding a hungry soul, perhaps as kind as providing shelter to the homeless or as blessed as coming together as in this circle of souls wishing to progress and be changed in At-One-Ment with God.

As events on the earth intensify as you have been told ( that they would) and now can see, you will have many opportunities to come together in community to sustain your lives, preserve the truth, and to live it with others. When I helped found my community, Findhorn, we did not think of ourselves as survivalists but rather as community, as like-minded souls wishing to create a peaceful, sustainable, spiritual environment. Everyone was invited to contribute with whatever gifts they had, just as those in your community are invited to contribute in so many ways. Often people assume that those living in community have no privacy. That they must be engaged with others every minute of their waking hours. I have never witnessed that kind of living, and from talking with the beautiful Clare who founded her cloistered convent, we found we had much in common. Everyone working together, everyone having their own space as you say, to be alone, to be with God in private and in community.

For those here who wish to be part of a more sustainable future with others who have the same soul longings, I encourage you to do so and allow each one the freedom of aloneness as well as the pleasure of each other’s company. As you find yourself in the flow of grace, you see how things fall together so beautifully and in such harmony when you lift each other up and bring your gifts to all; God blesses you, and we assist you.

This is a great joy for us. And in this coming together, allow this joy of togetherness and simultaneously your own unique soul to just be. Let us all open to the great inflowing and infilling of our Loving Creator and the blessings of His Love. Let us be still and let us be together as one.

I thank you for this time, and I shall remind you that when you call, we come. And when you need assistance, we shall help you for the asking.

With all my love and the blessings of God, I shall step back in your beautiful circle. May your journey be ever blessed. Go in peace. I am Eileen, and I love you.
