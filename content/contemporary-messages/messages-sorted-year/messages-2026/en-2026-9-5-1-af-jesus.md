# Allow Your Soul Desires for God to Shine Forth

September 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the gift of the Father's Love be yours, beloved souls. I come. I am your brother and your friend. I am Jesus. I come to open this gathering and to affirm that what you do and what you desire is of the highest, the Truth that is God's Love.

The floodgates are at the ready. You are holding back a great torrent of Love, but it requires your openings and desire to elicit that opening of God's great Soul to you. It cannot be the other way. For God always desires to bless you, to give you this gift that He offers, but you must be at the ready to receive, to take in what is given, to use the power of your own free will and desire to ask, to be in that state of readiness, to be in the grace of God. This is the goal and the blessing to be given.

Do not hold back these feelings, this desire for healing, of awakening, of transformation, all of these things directed toward God in prayer. Your silent prayer to Him, your need to be close. All of these human needs that obstruct this must fall away and be in the purity of your own nakedness, your own soul longing and being with God. For in this comes the torrent of His Love. In this comes all that you seek, all that you are looking for. In this, you are fulfilled and given what is within God's ability, capacity to give to you, to change you forever and heal you for eternity.

May God bless you with His Love. It is here waiting for you. Seek it out, my beloveds, and you will not regret a moment in prayer, but have deep gratitude for what God has to give. God bless you, my beloveds. I am with you, praying, opening. As you do, so do I and so do all the angels that are present, the bright spirits, even your stellar friends that come to join in in this Circle of Light, bringing light to this part of the world. You are all instruments to help bring these blessings forward to many. Seek to be used as God's instruments. Seek to be in God's grace. Seek to be filled with His Essence and in this way, you will fulfil the intention of this group, this gathering, this place and time in God's plan for the salvation of humanity. It is simple and straightforward. Nothing to complicate your thinking. Merely to let go and let God do His work amongst us.

May God bless you deeply, my beloveds. I am with you, your brother and friend. I am Jesus and I love you, each one, a special and unique soul in God's universe, gifted, bright and beautiful. Allow that brightness to shine, that beauty to be expressed and you will be a light, a light within the world. God bless you, my beloveds. Open yourselves fully and be blessed. I love you. God bless you.
