# Be Brave, Prayerful and Aware at This Important Turning Point for the World

March 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

May the blessings of this universal Love be yours. The Essence of God's Soul is available to all souls in the universe. It is important that you pray for all souls, all your brothers and sisters, not only here on this planet, but on all planets. For the destiny of all souls is to find at-onement with God. That journey may be long or short. It may come because the environment in which that soul was born into nurtures that Love and it becomes second nature to each one who pursues it, or it may be a struggle, like your own planet, where many do not know it or understand it. Yet, it is offered equally and powerfully to every soul.

My beloved and beautiful friends, we are gathered together in prayer and desire to be in that alignment of truth, that beautiful blessing of Love, the awakening of your souls and its transformation. All of these things are happening now as you have opened the door, the passageway to God. Indeed, that passageway becomes wider and more direct as you continue in your pursuits of prayer. In your contemplations with God, the blessings come. They flow more readily. The upliftment is a gift given abundantly. The messages of truth flow and your souls absorb all that it can in order to continue on this journey toward greater light, purity, purification and transformation that you may be a beautiful channel of love in the world.

As all these things are happening for each one of you, then you come closer to this goal, this ideal of being a clear and potent channel of truth for God, that love may flow abundantly. Light may be all around you and indeed manifestations come through you, so that others may be reassured and inspired to seek out this gift that you know so well and yet so few know well. My beloved friends, continue to seek out the wisdom and machinations of your soul. In this, truth will come abundantly, clearly, directly and powerfully. As this Essence of God grows within you, so your capacity to have love for yourself and compassion for others will grow with it and you will be guided. You will be guided, my friends, upon this path of service, of strengthening and creating light in the world.

Is this not God's plan, God's intention, as He makes abundantly obvious for each one of you the manifestations of His blessings, His gifts for you and for the world? A great focus has been placed upon your world. It is the time of change, the turning point. Great effort is being made in order to ensure that this world survives the darkness and negativity that humanity is voicing upon it and foisting upon each other. Wars and rumours of wars, dreadful conditions, deprivation, hunger, starvation, illness, many, many aspects of disharmony are manifesting in your world and growing. This indeed is alarming. Yet, to counteract these conditions, those such as yourselves are being reinforced and blessed and upheld like never before.

This can be the way to counteract the darkness. Your efforts, my friends, are crucial. Your dedications are needed. As you continue to release all negativity within yourself, you ensure that that channel of love, that beautiful instrumentality that is a part of you, will manifest in many different ways and in powerful and direct ways. This is needed now. It is not a time for casual effort. It is a time for prayerful dedication and great effort to release all inhibitions that are dark within you, to be strong in the Light and the Love of God, for many things are fomenting in your world and much misunderstanding is continuing to be a part of the growing darkness and efforts to put out light.

You must be clear, brave and powerful. Now, my friends. It is important. It is a crisis and a turning point. It is for you to acknowledge rather than hide away and bury your head in the sand, so to speak, rather than be aware and cognisant of what is happening in your world. I do not suggest that you be fearful and take into account every detail, but awareness and knowledge is a powerful thing. It is important for you to direct your consciousness toward God, toward your soul and toward all that may come of this beautiful connection. For now is the time to demonstrate the truth, to be that truth, to be that light, to do so with earnestness and even concern for your brothers and sisters, for we are applying ourselves in all the directions in which I describe. We wish for you to be that point of light for us, for God, for your angel friends, where blessings may flow and light may neutralise the darkness. It is important that you apply yourself more than ever before. I implore you, my beloved friends, to take the truth that has been given, to take the Will of God and the blessings of God forth through you into the world and be that agent of light, change and truth.

May you continue to be blessed, my friends. We are here with you. Many are here. We continue to work to apply the light in whatever way that we can. It is for you to be open, receptive, diligent and focused upon what is needed and you will find much will flow through you and many blessings of light will go to the leaders of the world, will go to those who are suffering, those who are victimised, those who are in darkness, those who have great anger but no understanding. Much is needed of you, friends. I ask of you to be at the ready and to be prayerful and open to your guidance. God bless you, beloved souls. Orion loves you dearly. God bless you all.
