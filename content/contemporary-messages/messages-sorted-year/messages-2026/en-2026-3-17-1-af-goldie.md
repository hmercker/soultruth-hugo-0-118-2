# The Rising of Joy Within the Soul

March 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Al Fike

God bless you, everybody. I am your friend, Goldie. I come to bring joy, to bring joy in your hearts, to bring joy to this world, a world that is often bereft of joy, especially in these days where there is so much fear, stress and tension in the world. Joy is the antidote. Where does joy come from? From the heart of the soul, the heart of the soul that knows that even these days upon the Earth are numbered and limited and that the journey of the soul goes on and there is much more to life than what you experience here on Earth.

I want you to realise that even though there may be strife and problems, there can always be joy. For that joy does not come from outside of you, but from inside, from your hearts and your hearts connected to the great Soul of God, who is all joy, all Light and Love. This Source is what you need every moment of the day, for when you are attuned and in alignment with God in this way, you can only feel joy. Would it not be wonderful if the world felt joy all the time? Could there be strife and anger and judgement? Could you harm another person when your heart is filled with joy? No, it cannot be that way. For when you strive for joy, when you reach for the highest blessings of God's Love, then there can only be light, harmony, joy and peace.

This is what we want for you, my friends. This is what God wants for you, to be in that state of joy, to truly be in the Light and harmony of God's Love. All will come to you in perspective, in perception of what life truly is and what is important in life. When that comes, when the wisdom of your soul shows you the way, then there can be joy. You will be able to release all those mindful concerns, all those material concerns that bring you from joy. In their place, joy will infuse you and be truly a part of you.

Ask God to put joy in your heart as He puts His Love in your soul, so you may feel these things that are so nurturing, so powerful and so beautiful, that you may truly be a soul that walks the Path Divine and you are free from the encumbrances of the Earth. In this way, you might be a channel of love and light for many. For many, for there are so many who are downtrodden and deprived, in darkness, suffering. With a bit of joy in their life, there could be relief and healing. They could look up and realise that God loves them and that that Love is real and can be a part of their soul.

My wonderful friends, be joy. All that you can be, be joy. You will be a light like none other, a beautiful instrument for God to bring His Love, light, peace and joy to the world. This is what the world needs. You, each of you who know God's Love for you and have that Love within you can be a channel of joy and blessings for others. Be true to this and let the other things, the human things, fall away, so that you are free, that you are expanded in love and light. In this, only joy can come to you.

My beloved friends, I am Goldie. I love you and I am joyful to be with you. God bless you all. Feel the rising of the joy in your heart and you will find that all the worries will fall away and you will only have care for God and for brighter life, experience and expression of soul with God. God bless you, my beloveds. I love you so. I love you so.
