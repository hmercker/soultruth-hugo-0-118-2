# Release That Which is Misaligned

January 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Retreat, Christ Church, Barbados

Faith Nyquist received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Faith. It is always a pleasure, always a delight, to descend into your midst, as you ascend in spirit, and we meet. We meet in a place that is elevated, in love, in peace, in acceptance, with a desire to grow, a desire to awaken, to awaken to more love, more truth, more wisdom, through this great blessing of the inflowing of God's Love into your soul, where the at-onement soul to soul happens.

As you progress upon your journey, at times you feel so close to God. Your heart is open, your mind is aligned with your heart and your being and you experience through your physical body the bliss, the awareness, the love and peace. At other times, you are distracted by the many demands of your material life. We are always making the suggestion that you learn to release the duality and to allow your being that is filled with Love and Light to move you, to inform your speech, to be your sight in how you see the world, to be the love to touch in a way that only a soul that is imbued with Divine Love can touch, touch as though God touches.

We continue to make this a suggestion, because, as we do, it helps you to put your consciousness upon your soul, upon God, upon truth. You become curious and desire to be informed by the quality, the attributes of God and God's Love that is within you. You are seeking for new words to come to you through our mediums, but the foundational teaching is not yet established within you to a degree that allows you to be steady in the ocean of God's Love. As any good teacher would do, we repeat, we review, for we observe what is happening within you in truth and where you are misaligned. It is our service to help you, to love you, but also to speak truth and to help you to grow in your desire to hear truth and to be able to perceive truth, the truth of what is happening within you, the truth of the world, the truth of God's Laws, the Truth of Love.

When you try to bypass what you know is out of harmony, it only perpetuates the human condition. It takes courage, a desire to have your eyes opened to what is in alignment with God's Love and what is not. This is the great blessing of a child who is willing to be shown in love, with compassion, but in truth. For this is how we guide you. This is how we help you. This is how we serve God as His messengers, coming to you to make rapport with you as your guides and your teachers.

You desire to progress on your path and you are progressing, but know that every piece of energy that is not in alignment through the Law of Love is required to be transformed. Be courageous, dear ones. Heed the guidance that we bring you. Do your best to not turn your face or your eye to not see the truth that we are bringing you. Do not fear truth, for truth is freedom. Truth brings joy. Truth releases you into the great many blessings of God's Love. In time, as your courage and your faith grows, you will long to see more truth. You will long and yearn to know what is not in alignment with your Creator, because in time, there will be nothing more important to you than to be at one with God. Your soul is so greatly expansive and filled with this Love that you are compelled, when you come before your Father with a heart that is open, to pray sincerely to be shown, to be changed and to be awakened.

With this and the courage to truly stand open and desirous to be shown, you transform. Your free will is aligned with the will that is of your soul and your will and God's Will become one. You know through every breath how God desires for you to move throughout your world in alignment with His Will, His Will of bringing humanity into peace, into joy, into love. We come with a similar message many, many times, because it is the most powerful message that we can deliver, to help you, to awaken your soul. The bounty of Love that is within your soul is expressing itself, God expressing through you, His Substance of Love activated in you, moving you, breathing you, speaking, seeing and touching through you. This is transformation, where your instrumentality and God are aligned, in union, in love, in desire, in demonstration. Be of great courage. Desire to be shown the truth of the matter at hand, at any moment.

I am Faith. God bless you all on your journey to at-onement, your road of becoming a divine soul, a Celestial Angel, all in God's grace. I love you and I am with you. I am Faith. God bless you.
