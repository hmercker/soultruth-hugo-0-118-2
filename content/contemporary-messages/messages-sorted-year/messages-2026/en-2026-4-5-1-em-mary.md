# A Mother's Faith

April 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Mary received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Mary. I am the mother of Jesus who even at this moment is blessing each and everyone one of you with his love. I come to speak about this day (Easter) as celebrated by those upon the Earth. It was a very different experience for me as a mother. Yet, even in that moment, I held onto God’s promise for a new life for my son. It was not easy as a mother. But my faith was strong. I held on to that promise that my son would be better close to God than on the face of this Earth at that time.

He had tried to teach our brothers and sisters the truth of God’s Love for each of us. Yet, his message was misinterpreted and he was gone. As I grieved for him and I chose for him to go to God, I did not understand what was truly going to happen once he left this Earth, but I knew it was better than what was happening with him on the Earth and God promised me that all would be well.

So here you are, true children of God, teaching other souls upon this Earth the truth that he did not die for their sins but that he lived to teach God is a loving God, (a Father who) offers each soul (to partake of His) Divine nature. I am grateful to you for continuing the teachings of my son and I now know God’s promise to me as his mother was kept. I look to you now to continue your own development in this Love, your own expressions of this Love and your own experiences in sharing this Love with your brothers and sisters and that you are all God’s children to turn to God and come to know the Truth of God’s Love for them.

Many of us, all of us in the Celestial Heavens, are grateful for your work upon the Earth. We are here to support you and love you as you continue your journeys. I am your sister Mary. I give you my blessings and my love. May God bless you every day of your lives as well as when you pass over into the spirit world. God bless you, dear children. My love is with you.
