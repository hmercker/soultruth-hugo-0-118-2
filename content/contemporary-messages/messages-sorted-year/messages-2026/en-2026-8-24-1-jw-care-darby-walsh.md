# A Prayer from Care

August 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Care Darby Walsh received by Jimbeau Walsh

My dear ones, let us take this time to rest in the arms of our loving Creator, removed from the hustle and bustle of the world and even our own concerns. If we are in need of healing, let us reach out with our souls, that those angels who do this work will come. Let us step back from our brains, from our doubts, and from our wondering, so that God may touch each one.

May our souls be opened that the Holy Spirit may bring this love Divine into each soul here and there are many. May this be a time of timeless at-onement, of gentle awakening, of loving kindness, of Holy communion, allowing our hearts, which may hold many wounds, to be blessed, to be sacred. Let us feel the presence of the Most Holy God in this moment beyond the words.

Dear God, we ask you to open up our souls. We are your children who humbly come seeking transformation and awakening and joy in at-onement with You. We thank You for this great blessing, and how You love us; only heaven knows.

I shall step back, and with all my love and blessings, I shall continue to be your celestial, eternal friend.

I am Care. God bless you.
