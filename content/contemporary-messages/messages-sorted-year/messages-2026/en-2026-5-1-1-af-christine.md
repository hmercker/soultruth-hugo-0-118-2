# Healing the World Through the Power and Simplicity of Love

May 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Christine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. My name is Christine. I do not come from your world. I come from another world, but I come in support of your world. I come to help uphold your world and to give what I can to help heal your world. For there are many around your planet who are involved, concerned and dedicated to help bring this world into harmony, where you are an integral part of the harmony of the universe, one of God's beautiful creations. When your world is out of harmony, is not in balance and is not of light, then there is a subtle effect throughout the universe from this condition. Those of us who are developed and advanced spiritually and materially seek to remedy these situations and these conditions.

We come to you, my friends, with humility and love to help you to understand what is necessary to be within the simple Laws of Creation, that which will bring harmony to your world and to all within it. It is not that difficult. Yet indeed, when most of you upon this planet are engaged elsewhere in your minds, your thoughts, your deeds, your reality, then the complications that come from this require a remedy. They require each of you to be in harmony with the simple Laws of Creation and the very foundation of this is love. When you emanate love, when you express love, when you apply love to everything that you do, then harmony is a result of this application. It may not be obvious and it may not be instantaneous, but as you practise love, as you continue to honour one another and honour this world of yours, then that light and the light of that love grows and builds until there is an effect that not only changes you, but the world around you and, in time, influences and inspires others who inspire others until the world is reclaimed in love.

These are simple things that you can do, my beloved friends, to be in harmony with the Creator of all, to seek out that great gift that is available to all, to utilise that gift and to build within that gift so that you are a light. Within that light comes wisdom and a great capacity to love and have compassion, to have sensitivity and appreciation, to have the many attributes that guide and direct you in your daily living. Yes, it is not complicated, but because of the human condition, you have complicated the matter greatly, for your minds control your actions and the mind, distorted as it is, becomes a very complicated instrument within the world that creates conditions that are disharmonious. How you respond and interact with one another is mostly governed by your mindful conceits and conditions.

Yet, what we speak of, what we know and what many in the universe know, is the development of the soul, the awareness and the faculties of the soul. The soul mind is the key to healing, harmony and peace. You must consider your priorities, that which you may put to the forefront of your actions, your thoughts and your condition. When love is involved, life becomes simpler. Priorities become harmonious and almost reflexive. There is no great mindful thought involved. Rather, it is the flow of love. It is the flow of light that brings harmony to you and to all around you, that great Love of God, that Essence of his Soul, is a powerful antidote to all that is out of harmony in your world.

Bring it into yourself, my friends. Allow it to reside there, to work to change you and transform you and it will then become an easy task to bring reform, to bring harmony and peace into your life and into the world. Be faithful and true to the creation of God, the laws that God has put into place, which are not so demanding that you cannot understand them, nor can you apply them. It is easily applied and easily understood, but it is not easy for you to detach yourself from this human condition and be within the true alignment and harmony of love. This is a challenge and you well know it. Yet, I say to you, you have many friends around you, many who are eager to help you, many that will indeed protect you from these distractions and darkness that are so prevalent in your world and indeed a light will surround you so that you are in that beautiful condition, that joyful condition of light and love.

This is what the goal must be for each and every one of you and each one upon the planet. When the majority achieves this, then the world will change dramatically and you will no longer need our assistance, but rather we will become your equals and your friends. We will help you to see the bigger picture, the great scope of God's creation, the beautiful planets that exist, the wonderful souls that exist there and how there can be a great family of brothers and sisters, those who possess a soul, who are truly like you, a child of God. In this way, the dilemmas and the overwhelming conditions that you face will find solutions, will find healing and balancing into harmony.

Continue on in your efforts, my beloved friends, for it is in your efforts, in your dedications, in your understanding of soul and that wisdom that comes as Love grows within it that will indeed bring the healing that is necessary. In time, your planet will heal and become bountiful once more, filled with life, beautiful life in all its diversity and wonderment. You will learn to live within that system, that ecology, as you call it, that God has created. You will find yourself in awe of what God has created. Indeed, as you glow in harmony, light and love, the world will reflect this. For you are a powerful emanation that affects the world and you do not understand how powerful you are, how those emanations are important and part of what God created and what God wishes for the world and for you.

There is so much to understand, my friends, but when the perceptions of the soul open, when you come to understand what true love is, what Divine Love is, then you will gain the wisdom, the perceptions and the understanding that you need. That will flow and be realised through each one of you, a channel of love and light in the world, a beautiful agent of change and healing and peace. Come to know your true potentials, my beloved friends, come to realise who you truly are and what you are capable of. When these things are put into place and you act in wisdom and harmony, then that agency that is within you and around you will help bring the world back from darkness into light, from chaos into harmony. You each have that potential, my beloved friends. We are here to help you realise this and many others are here as well. May you discover much of the truth of your existence, the truth of the universe. God created such a wondrous universe and the truth that is love and the power of love to heal and transform bring grace, peace, light and harmony to what God intends, what God created.

May you all be blessed, my beloved friends, with deep wisdom, with deep capacity to love, with deep perceptions, knowings and closeness with the Creator of all and that way we will join brothers and sisters together and help create and evolve into what is intended, that destiny that God has for all of us. You step upon the threshold, my friends. You will find yourself understanding, truly knowing, feeling and being within the light of love, the power of love within your soul shining forth. God bless you, my friends. I am Christine. My love is with you. Prayer is an important part of the work for now. Continue with sincere prayer, deep prayer, intentional prayer, for your own healing, for the healing of the world. God bless you, my friends. God bless you all.
