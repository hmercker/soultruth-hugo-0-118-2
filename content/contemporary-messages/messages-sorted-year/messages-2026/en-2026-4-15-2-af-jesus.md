# Blessed Are Those Who Are Willing to Deliver the Word of God

April 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

The Breath of God has been upon you, my beloveds. You have felt the power of His Touch. Your souls continue to awaken and are drawn to His blessings. We come to the fruition of our time together, our prayers, our conversations, all done in love, all done to bond and bring us together in greater unity and harmony. We are given the gift of time, of presence, of prayer and of blessings. May you take with you the thoughts and recollections of this time. May you think of one another fondly in loving appreciation. May you continue to walk the walk of love and truth in the world. We all are a part of God's great plan, the unfolding of God's Will within our lives. We are His children, truly. Though the world may be rent asunder with all the dark conditions therein, with us, each of us, we carry an island of peace with us wherever we go. We are the mediators for harmony and for truth. It is our destiny, my beloved and beautiful brothers and sisters, to walk in this light, to do God's bidding with each and every day, for we have committed within our souls to do as God wishes, to be His instruments and His channels of love.

What will hold you from this, my beloveds? What could hold you from this? Nothing. There is no force in this world that can bring you away from God and away from your destiny with God. You are His children, steadfast and truly committed, seeking out His Love with every breath, seeking the Light of His blessings upon each of you. Oh, my beloveds, carry the light truly, be the light truly and spread the light in ways and means that you are guided to do so, for the light must spread and the darkness must diminish. The truth must come and the choice given to all humanity. If you are not willing, then others will. If you cannot, then others can. Yet, you are given the opportunity, my beloveds, to do, spread and bring the Word of God into the world.

Are you willing, my beloveds? Can you do so? For those who are willing and those who express the Will of God through them are truly blessed and shall reap the rewards of that blessing. We continue upon our life's journeys. We continue to discover and awaken to the truth. We continue to be transformed by it and we will find ourselves in places and situations with other souls, other places in the world where we may speak the truth, live the truth, express the truth in ways you cannot even imagine at this time, my brethren. For there is much coming into your lives, doors that will open suddenly and surprisingly, God guiding you forward. Where you were fearful or reticent, you will be strong and clear in your instrumentality, your gifts flowing in accordance to God's Will and in alignment with God's Love.

Many will benefit from this. Many will be blessed by it. There is the potential within this room to change the world, my beloveds, but you will not be alone in this. Others will come. Many will stand by you as you step forward in leadership and strength, in the capacity and ability to do God's Will and bidding in the world. It will not be a burden. It will be a joy. You will find yourself speaking and being used by your angel friends and by God Himself in ways that will bring harmony and peace in the world and will show your brothers and sisters what true love is and what the Love of God can bring and manifest in one's life.

There is much yet to discover, my beloveds, much to discover, know and experience. You will find yourself wanting to change in ways that are unexpected, your circumstances coming into greater alignment with God's plan and God's Will for you. You will find that you will meet individuals, beautiful souls, who will come and support you, stand by you and work with you. God's plan is not unfolding at its peak, but is barely beginning. You will find yourself, the momentum, the expression of yourself and the world will intensify, evolve, change, be transformed and touched by the Will of God and the blessings of God's Love. As has been told to you many times, much is coming. Though some of what is coming will be of great concern, as you see suffering and chaos in the world, you will be a rock, a pillar of light and a channel of love. It will flow and manifest in ways and means that are truly astounding and miraculous, truly wondrous and beautiful.

Allow God to work through you. Do not resist. Do not hold back. Do not fear. Do not judge. Rather, stand within the grace of God and find peace and fulfilment there. It all comes to you now in great abundance, blessings flowing powerfully. The benefits of this will be tremendous. You will all be changed in ways that you do not anticipate. You will not recognise yourself as these changes have their effects upon you. Beloved souls, we give ourselves to God. We release all that which is not in harmony with His Laws of Love. We stand forth, ready and willing to do God's Will and bidding in our lives, for this we know brings great blessings and satisfaction.

May you walk in life in the flow of God's Will. May that be of ease, peace and joy. It is possible, beloveds, to be in that state always. It is for you to release the conditions that are contrary to it and so I ask you to do so. There is no time for anything else. There is no time to consider and ruminate. There is only time for action and dedication, for following the Will of God truly and deeply and to be that channel of love, powerful and full of grace. May you all be blessed, my beloveds, as you return home. Consider the words that have been spoken to you. Consider the experiences that have come to you. Consider what God has given you. Consider what you can give God and give your brothers and sisters. You will find yourself upon a course of action, of expression, of life that will be different, but with greater harmony, peace and joy.

All will be well, my beloveds. You know that we are close. You know that God has His Hand upon you and so you will be well, the light will shine and you will know this. Love will be the mainstay of your life. May you be blessed, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to uphold you, to encourage you, to guide you and to assure you that all is well. You will find yourselves in the midst of the tumult, but what will come and be revealed to you will truly assist you in your awakening and transformation. It will come abundantly. It will envelop you, it will carry you and it will change you. This is God's plan for you and that plan will be fulfilled, beloveds, my disciples. That plan will be fulfilled.

God bless you upon this journey of love and keep you in the Light and protection of His care and show you the way forward each and every step. I am with you. God is with you. The angels are with you. The bright spirits and stellar friends are with you. You have great support, tremendous support. May you take advantage of all the resources, all that is given and all that shall be given. You will indeed take that road less travelled and find the wonderment of that road and the expressions and opportunities that will come upon that journey. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus and I love you. I am with you for eternity. God bless you. God bless you.
