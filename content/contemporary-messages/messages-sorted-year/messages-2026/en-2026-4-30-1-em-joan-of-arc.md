# Changes You Can Expect to See

April 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Joan of Arc received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Joan of Arc. I am your sister in this Divine Love, this Christ Spirit, which God has bestowed upon many of us in the Celestial Heavens and even upon the Earth. Dear children, God is transforming this Earth and many of the souls upon it. From this, you will see many beautiful changes coming to the Earth, a cleansing, a reawakening, a rebuilding, not so much of structures as of communion with God, loving communication with brothers and sisters, care for brothers and sisters and a growing development of love that stems from one's soul.

These are the changes that you can expect to see upon the Earth. What else would a loving Father do but provide for His children the best that He can provide, which in our Father's position is the best that there is? So much Love pours into this Earth, so much Love is impacting many souls, changing thoughts, behaviours and even desires. More and more souls desire to walk with God, to be with God even while in the human flesh.

You have prayed for more eyes to look to the Heavens, more souls to be opened and receive the gift of His Love. Know that your prayers are being answered. Every time you pray, there are children who receive this Love. What a beautiful work, what a beautiful group of souls who pray and ask God to care for the children upon this Earth and to even receive the greatest blessing that can be received.

You are so blessed. You are so cherished. God has you in His Hands, protecting you, guiding you and bringing you closer and closer with each prayer. Continue on this way. Continue to be open. Continue to commune with your Father, whether by yourselves or in circles such as these. You are being blessed and blessed frequently and mightily. Allow those blessings to enter deep into your soul and pray to express all that you have been given while still in your human bodies. My love is with you. I am Joan, known to you as Joan of Arc. Our Father continues to bless you, even at this moment. May God's Will be done. (The peace of God is descending upon us.)
