# Is it any Wonder?

November 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Care Darby Walsh received by Jimbeau Walsh

My dear, ones, it is I Care.

When we come, we always intend to encourage you with all the love in our souls and in our hearts. When we give you a message that is perceived as a warning sign, or I might say a wakeup call, we do not wish to instill fear, but sometimes to convey a sense of urgency, because we are like the prophets of old who could perceive how events would play out and therefore inform their people of what to do next, to in fact say ‘get your act together cause a storm’s brewing’. In the Old Testament there are lots of demands and even wishes to incite harm on one’s enemies, whereas in the new testament the admonitions are filled with love and though they may be serious at times and convey a sense of urgency, they are simply encouragement to take action, whether that be to greet all with the love in your souls, to pray more often, to give your worries and concerns to God, to strengthen your community and your communion, not only with God, but with each other and the world.

As has been stated, you know not the hour, or the day, or the time you will come into spirit and this is good because you need not concern yourself, for you will experience a wonderful passing, believe it or not. For when you consider all your loved ones in spirit and all the angels who speak to you and you are in prayer, is there any room for doubt? When you feel the love of God and the light descending upon you, in you and through you, is it any wonder that this is your destiny? So, no need to worry about these things and we are not encouraging you to run a marathon or be the fastest in a race to some spiritual goal. You may even feel that you are the tortoise in the race with the hare and you know how that ended, that story. What did the tortoise do but persist steadfastly at his own pace with his eye on the prize and the determination of his being. This I encourage you to do as you know every true prayer brings a gift, brings an answer, brings a healing and lifts you up.

So as the Master encouraged his friends in the garden, *"stay awake with me"*, stay awake with each other, with all the kindness, with all the humor, with all the grace that God has given you. For you are so loved and we welcome you as you welcome us. Let us rest in this grace. I thank you and with all my love, I shall step back. I am your celestial sister.

I am Care. God bless you.
