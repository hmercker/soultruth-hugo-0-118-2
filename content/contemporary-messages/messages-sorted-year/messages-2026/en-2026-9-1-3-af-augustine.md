# You Must Take Heed of Our Warnings and Prepare for the Storm of Change

September 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

May the grace of the Father's Love be with you, my beloved students. I am your teacher, Augustine. The time fast approaches where you will be needed in the world. We have been warning of these times for many years now through various groups, channels and mediums. That time is now upon us, although many of you do not feel the immediacy of the conditions. Yet, indeed, change is coming. Conditions are intensifying. The need grows great in a world that is filled with chaos, ignorance and darkness, all manner of conditions that are challenging to the soul. You continue to persist, my beloved students. You continue to seek out God and to seek out your own soul. His blessings continue to flow upon you and within you. This will be the case and this will intensify as God continues to put His focus upon this world and bring about conditions, blessings and light that will support this world through the tumult and through the conditions that are so contrary to His creation.

You must be vigilant, beloved souls, vigilant with God, vigilant with your own self and the conditions, the currents of thought and energies that continue to affect you. This may be subtle at times, but at other times it is clearly evident that you are being influenced by these earthly conditions. You must be vigilant. For you cannot ignore the fact that things are in flux and change is indeed part of your daily life. The shifting of consciousness must come to you. That shifting must be a permanent situation, so that you are fully prepared for what is to come and how God wishes you to respond to what is to come. Indeed, it is not all darkness, chaos and pain. Rather, God brings His blessings and His influence to bear to help ameliorate these conditions.

You are part of that plan. You are part of God's retinue of angels, angels on Earth, who are to serve Him and serve humanity in light and blessings, thereby neutralising these formidable dark forces that continue to run roughshod over the Earth and continue to cause chaos, havoc and pain amongst humanity. Many, as you well know, do not understand this. They do not realise what is happening, although they feel unease. They feel reactive. They feel angry at times. There is definitely much feeling in the air, much in the way of conditions expressed, thought about and acted upon that are not in harmony with love.

My beloved students, you have a choice. Will you ramp up your dedications to God and seek to open avenues of expression of these dedications as God's instruments and channels of Love, or will you go along half asleep and half awake, hoping that things will be for the better? You know full well within your soul that a storm is coming. Conditions are worsening. You are just on the very edge of that storm and it shall intensify. Are you prepared? Have you donned the clothing of light, the clothing that will resist the lashings of the storm, the torrents of rain that are burning and acidic, the torrents of anger and confusion that will come, the torrents of reaction to God's and your attempt to rectify the situation?

My beloved and beautiful students of light, you have been given fair warning. You are shown the way. Doors have opened. Guidance has been given. Blessings have poured upon each one of you. I urge you to take stock of what has been given and what steps are ahead of you. For it is not time to linger any longer. It is time to quicken your pace, quicken your efforts, strengthen your faith and be with God. For as the storm lashes, you will have that steadiness. You will be able to attach yourself to that pole that is God's strength, so that you will not be blown away. You will not be harmed, but will be steadfast as the tumult continues to create conditions so contrary to harmony and love, so contrary to light and peace, so contrary to truth and wisdom.

Yet, you will be those channels of such things, such blessings. God wishes to utilise you, but the intensity, the strength, the possibility of what can be given through you is completely determined by your conditions, your thoughts, the level of your faith and your trust in God. As you receive more of His Love, so that will increase. As you continue to awaken to this Love and all that comes with it, you will be a more effective instrument in the world. You will find yourself very much engaged in the world, not engaged in the human condition, but engaged in that which is of God and that which is of the needs of your brothers and sisters. Are you strong enough? Have you prepared? Are you strong in physical, in spiritual and in soul? For you must have these in alignment. There must be a strength and a harmony within that will serve you well as God sends you out into the storm to help others, to be that rock, that pinnacle of strength.

It is your choice each day, beloved souls. I know it is easier to stay with old routines, to think of the world from old perspectives, to be in that place that is comfortable and satisfies your needs and your wants in the physical and the mental. Yet, that day is fast coming to a close and these things will not be available to you. What will take their place? Where will you find the nurturance that every soul requires? Of course, it is with God. Yet, you must strengthen that connection. You must push that doorway further open and walk through it many, many times, so that it becomes second nature to you, second nature to what it is that you desire, who it is that you are and the light that you bring.

I know I have warned you many times. I know I have said these words a number of times and others have said so. Even our beloved master Jesus has talked to you about these things. I am not sure how much warning you require. It seems clearly evident that you need to understand and to embrace what it is that we say. It is important that you take heed of the signs and warnings that are given and to walk in the light. Nothing else will manifest without you being in the Light and the Truth of God's Love. This is what will carry you. This is what will open the ways and means of being God's instruments. Humanity waits for you. Though humanity is asleep, they are about to have a rude awakening. They will be disoriented, startled and confused.

You must bring clarity to all you meet to ring that great bell of truth, that all will hear it and heed it. For they must, if they are to survive what is coming. So must you, my beloveds. What a shame, what a tragedy it would be, if you were to pass into spirit prematurely because you were not able and willing to heed these warnings and prepare yourself accordingly. All the blessings, all the preparation, all that we do for you could be lost upon this earth plane. Although you will benefit greatly in the spirit world, we do not wish for you to go there yet. For you are sorely needed now and in the times to come.

Consider my sober warning. Be penitent in your daily lives, to be in harmony with the laws you know so well, to listen to the messages that have been given many times in many different ways through many spirits. I urge you to listen, to take stock of yourself and your condition, to consider what will be beneficial to you, to help you in these last moments of preparation, like the sprinter at the blocks, poised to run. Here we are. We are all poised. That door, that storm, that darkness will engulf us. Yet, we will hold steadfast and be strong and not afraid, to be of faith and not have doubts, to have Love, which is the most powerful antidote to darkness in the universe and to be that healing channel, that comforting soul, that will help the lost ones find their footing and follow you into greater light.

May God bless you, my beloved souls. I am your teacher, Augustine. I cannot say things more plainly and I say things in earnest. I hope you are able to listen without fear, but with greater resolve, strength and desire to be in alignment with God and to know His Truth not only from a mindful perspective but from that place of soul knowing and the urgency of soul expression. God bless you, beloved souls. Your teacher, Augustine, loves you. God bless you all.
