# Growing Awareness of Soul Perceptions and Attributes Guiding You

March 29<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

May God bless you. I am Keea Atta Kem. How wonderful it is to join you as we open our souls to the great flowing of God's Love, His Essence, the Divine Love, to come in. You bring great light to the world as you do this, each one sitting at a point around the world, drawing the light in as this Love flows to you, into you through the Holy Spirit. It is beautiful to see the illumination in your beautiful souls, how they expand as the light flows in and the Love flows in, emanating, sparkling with light, dancing so beautifully in joy, as the soul has a great sense of fulfilment when it is in union with God and communing with God, awakening and transforming in God's Essence.

Each one of you is growing in your ability of soul perception. In time, you will have your consciousness set within the soul, walking as a soul that is fulfilling its purpose, informing all that you do and all that you are. This grows in each one of you and you notice this, when you do experience times when you are aware of soul perceptions. You are aware of how the attributes of your soul are guiding you and informing you. You desire to be more honest, to live in truth, to see through the eyes of compassion. These are all the ways that the soul is informing your life and unfolding its awakening before you. As you continue to move forward, these two centres of consciousness, your material mind and soul mind, continue to grow in their ability to work in harmony.

Your soul grows in its strength and your mind grows in its ability to be receptive and open and this is a beautiful breeding ground for the garden of your soul, the flowers and its blessings, the love, the truth and wisdom that it holds to bubble up and inform your awake state. As you continue to pray for this Love and its workings continue to inform, expand, develop and release those things that are not in harmony, you notice your changes, each one changing in their own subtle ways. Allow this Love to come in. Allow your soul to flow its longings and yearnings of desire to be in communion with God. In this way, your transformation will be one that is very joyful, that continues to shower love upon the environments that you find yourself in. It allows you to be steady in the presence of your being, your soul, alive, well, thriving in the essence of yourself.

Dear ones, as you come into the fold of prayer, whether that be sitting or as you move about your day, be certain that with your sincerity, offering your longings and your special hello to God, God's response will come to you. The inflowing of this Love will touch you in a way that will create great satisfaction and fulfilment within you, awakening the desire to continue to be in this state of soulful longing and receptivity. Your mind grows in its capacity to focus on allowing the longings of your soul to come to the forefront. Your mind is growing in its awareness of the union with God and is growing in its capacity to feel stable and secure on its journey of awakening. Your mind is learning and growing in its ability to serve the soul, to allow its awakening to be and to fall into line with its awakening.

Dear ones, you are all on a path, a journey of soul transformation. Be strong, sincere and desirous of holding soul consciousness in your awake state. For as you do this and you practise, it is a partnership of attuning to your soul's desires and coming in alignment with the centre of your being, allowing the centre of the soul to lead you, to guide you. Your mind is learning to follow, to fall in line, to be one with your soul.

I am Keea Atta Kem. God bless you on your continued journey of awakening. May you revel in the glory of God's Love, Love Divine with every breath. God bless you.
