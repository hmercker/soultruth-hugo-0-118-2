# Follow the Promptings of Your Soul to Seize Opportunities for Service

June 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Doors are opening for each one of you, my beloveds. I am Orion. We are working diligently, as are your many friends, working to open avenues and possibilities of service in the world. I would urge each one of you to look at your lives in this context, that indeed, the opportunities are there, but it must be that you are open and accepting of those opportunities. Otherwise, if you follow the time-worn patterns of your life, those opportunities will not become evident and you will miss out the possibility of what you may do to serve God.

There is much work to do. You have been told this over and over again. Much is needed. Many are lost and there is so much chaos in your world. I know it feels overwhelming to you. It does to me on occasions, when I see how complex, difficult and out of harmony this world of yours is. I wonder how it may ever find a way toward harmony. Yet, this is where your desire to be a servant of God comes in, for it is God that has the plan and God that knows the way through. We, even our perceptions and development of soul, have not the capacity to solve the myriad of problems that are part of your life. It is the Creator of all who has the solutions and will find the ways. It is for each one of us to find our part, our thread in the tapestry, so that we may fulfil that function well and be a clear channel of God's intention, desire and plan.

As you well know, to do this, you must develop your soul, for there is the source of the information, the guidance and the understanding that is needed. It is not the mind trying to ruminate and figure the solutions to the problems. This has been humanity's approach for a very long time and yet it has failed. Things are getting worse, not better. Conditions are worsening and the solutions that are coming forward merely add to the complexity of the problems, rather than finding ways through and out of this miasma of dark conditions.

My beautiful friends, stalwarts upon the journey of soul awakening and transformation, I ask you to truly consider what your soul tells you, to be in alignment and attunement with your soul's guidance, the revelations and understanding that come as you navigate life. For in the portal that is your soul, that place that can and will be attuned to God, the solutions will come. They will not be solutions for the entire world, but they will be solutions for your world and your life. This will add to the harmony and the light that will be created by you acting upon those solutions and you being guided forward.

Faith is required, my beloved friends. You walk in the darkness and yet you walk forward in faith, trusting in God, trusting in the solutions provided. Indeed, at times when you perceive those solutions, your mind has judgement and may recoil from these solutions, thinking they lack logic and they lack practicality, whatever the judgement might be. Still, if you have faith, if you act accordingly and you feel your way along that road of action, the solutions will bring harmony, will bring greater joy into your hearts, will affect those around you and bring peace to them as well. This is the way of God. It is not the way of man. Therefore, you must see that the solutions and the approach are different and that trust is required too.

Do not reject easily what is given, but indeed it is important that you test what is guided. If you require confirmation of that guidance, then of course seek that out. Yet, in time, you will see yourself upon a road that opens up in a wider context, bringing greater possibilities and solutions into your life. It requires that first step, requires your capacity to walk forward in faith. In that, the insights, the wisdom, the light will shine through and you will walk in joy, my friends. The joy of a reality, a truth that is different from many of those you observe, but it is truth and reality nonetheless, one more viable than many you know and seen.

My friends, walk forward. Seek for the solutions that are given. Be humble and graceful in that walk. Your love for God must grow as God's Love for you grows inside of you. In that love, that place of love, all that you require to find solutions, to be in harmony, is there. The wisdom of your soul will provide what you need. All of these things that are so different from your world are indeed a gift to you, a gift to the world, for as you become that example, as you find the solutions, as you are in greater harmony, so those around you will notice. They will see this. They will wonder about it and you then may share the road that you are on. Give to them the prospect, the possibilities of walking in the light, in harmony, in peace and knowing the power of love and how it changes everything.

My beloved friends, be the peacemaker. Be the one who is strong in light. Be the one clear in action. Be the one that is a channel of love. Your desire to manifest those of us that are around you that are not seen by others will come as a result of these actions of which I speak. It is your choice, as always. Yet, indeed, the time grows short and the solutions that you bring to your life will have powerful effects and outcomes in times to come. Be true to your soul, my friends. Your soul will be true to you and God will be true to us all.

God bless you. I am Orion. I love you so, my beautiful friends, who are seeking solutions to their lives, to this world, seeking harmony and peace. May your love shine through and the solutions pour forth in all their beauty and intricacy and power. God bless you, my friends. God bless you in His Love.
