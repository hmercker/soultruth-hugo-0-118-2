# Your Gathering Here is Divinely Blessed; Jesus Opens State College Retreat

September 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

State College Retreat, State College, PA, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come, my beloveds, your brother and friend. I am Jesus. I come to open this circle, to tell you that the angels are arrayed around you, that God is pouring His Love upon you and that at this moment, this represents the greatest light in your world. That your gathering here is significant, powerful and divinely blessed.

Open yourselves fully to the blessing of the Father's Love. Allow His workings to have their effects upon your soul, awakening you to further truth, light and joy, all the goodness of God's Touch upon you. My beloveds, how truly blessed you are in the world! How truly blessed is this place as you continue to seek out your Father's Will and blessings upon you. All good and beautiful gifts will be bestowed upon you, my beloveds, depending upon your receptivity and desire.

Together, we shall journey a ways that will bring openings, shifts, blessings and peace. The Father's Love flowing abundantly. The blessings of this Love opening you to further understanding, expression of gifts, insights, revelations and awakenings. It is all given freely and abundantly, my beloveds. We are here.

I shall be here during the duration of your time together, as will many angels. God's Hand will continue to bless you and awaken you. All is arrayed in your favour, my beloveds. Take full advantage of what is given.

God bless you. I am Jesus. I am with you in love. God bless you.
