# Orion Describes the Dimension Connecting All Souls in the Universe

April 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Orion received by Al Fike

Greetings to you, my friends. It is Orion. I am assuming you would want me to make an appearance and speak to you today, considering the topic of your discussion. [^1] If you can imagine this, for it is indeed a reality, that God has created a multidimensional universe. You, in your limited experience on the earthly plane, see very little of this and understand it even less.

I say to you, my friends, that on one dimension, we are all connected. We are all children of God. We all possess a soul. Indeed, with these attributes, the connection that we have soul to soul exists in the beauty and the power that this dimension affords, that God has given us the gift of knowing one another in this way, of being together, brothers and sisters all of us, in a world that has no delineation or even form. This place that I speak of is a place that you may all go to and experience this form of awareness, this interconnectedness that is of the soul, whether you are of the ilk of the natural love, as you call it, or the Divine Love as we all are here, that connection exists because it is of consciousness. Indeed, as you grow in your consciousness, and that consciousness merges with your soul consciousness, this experience will come to you. It may not happen on the earthly plane, but it is inevitable that you will understand the interconnectedness, the oneness, of all of us, all of God's children.

This is how we make our connections, our rapport and our understanding of one another. It is through the flow of this dimensional state, this reality, that we become known to one another. In this knowing, in this relationship, which is fuelled by love, we will assist, collaborate and become truly a part of that brethren of souls in the universe. You may come to know me and my cohorts. You may come to know many and their cohorts in the universe, without travelling great distances and without being stuck within mindful and material states of consciousness. As you grow in this great gift of Divine Love, access to this level of consciousness of which I describe will be made available to you, for each and every soul has within them the possibility of knowing this and experiencing it for themselves.

Yes, there is much for each of you to learn. There is much that God wants to give to you, there is much that we wish to give, and many others wish to give, all for your greater good and for the greater good of this world, this precious planet that you call Earth. It is filled with the wonderment of God's creations. Yet, indeed, the horror is that these creations are disappearing on a daily basis because of the lack of sophistication, sensitivity, perception and love that so many possess upon your planet. We are always amazed how dark and light can coexist here, for this does not happen in many places in the universe. You are somewhat unique in this regard. There is great goodness happening and manifesting here on Earth and there is great darkness as well. This uneasy relationship continues, the pressure back and forth, the darkness increases, then the light increases and then the darkness increases. It goes on in a rather disturbing loop that reinforces itself.

You, my beloved friends, have the potential to break the pattern, to sever the loop, to unravel what has been happening for so very long upon your planet. This is the time, for the intensity of these patterns is growing and the power of the darkness is growing. It is time for great change. Indeed, you know of this, that God has a plan. This is why we are here, as well as many others, because God's plan includes all resources available in the universe to assist humanity and assist Mother Earth. This is so that all may come to a place of harmony and peace, where love is recognised for what it is, its power, its glory and its grace, the human love and the Divine Love. The world needs it all. Each of you has the possibility of being powerful channels of both. I would encourage you to look at this, to see how the Divine Love is infiltrating your natural love abilities, changing and transforming it, making it into something truly wondrous, powerful and able to change the world.

We may assist. We may observe. We may come to be with you. We may give of what we can for each one of you, but we do not have the capacity at this time, nor do we intend to be here in the flesh. It requires your efforts, your gifts, your dedications, your prayers and your individuality, all that you are, to be applied to the task at hand. You have been asked over and over again to apply yourself in this way. Jesus came to you not more than a few days past and asked you to step up and be that beautiful instrument, his disciples. Well, Jesus is the greatest example of truth in your world and indeed stands out in all the universe in his light, beauty, perfection and love. When such a being asks of you your help, it is a serious matter. It is not a trifling matter. It is something you must truly consider in all seriousness and consider how you might fulfil his request to be a light in the world, to be truly in harmony with the universe that is, that which has been created by God. When you find your way to that place of awareness and truth, revelations will come and gifts within you emerge as this great gift of Love continues to grow within you.

It is a simple process, but of course, the speed of which is determined by your dedications, your efforts and prayers. Are you truly dedicated, my brothers and sisters? Are you truly of that light? Do you truly have faith in yourself and what you are capable of? Do you have faith in God and what God is capable of? These are the questions you must ask yourself. When you find your answers, it is important that you apply them to your life. For individuals such as yourselves, who have been afforded the truth in such a generous way, uplifted and upheld, protected and guided, blessed again and again, if you do not apply what has been given, there will be consequences, my friends. This is not punishment that I speak of, but deep regret. When you come into that second phase of life, life in the spirit world, and you realise that you have not applied yourself as fully as you could, there will be deep regret.

Indeed, we continue to remind you and speak to you of what is required of you and the responsibility that you carry. We will continue to do so. For in this world of yours, there are many distractions, much to bring you away from this clarity, this knowledge that lies within your soul, but we will remind you, as will many, Celestial Angels, bright spirits and ourselves. God will remind you that there is work to do, a great task at hand. Those of you who are strong enough, who have enough faith, courage and desire, may step forward. With each footfall forward, guidance will be given, the knowledge of the next step, the next outcomes that must be accomplished in order for this great plan to find traction in your world and expression in deep and profound ways so that true change may come about in your world. A change that is not only desirable, but crucial for this world to survive and be in harmony once again, for humanity to find its way to God and for all of us to be able to express ourselves in conjunction with God's Will and to be a part of this great plan.

Oh, what joy this will bring, my beloved friends. What joy and what peace will come to you and what experiences you will have beyond your imaginations, my friends. Many of you have had profound experiences, experiences that have changed your life, but I tell you that the depth and breadth of what is coming will come to you on a daily basis. The change that will happen within you will leave you breathless in awe and wonder. I say to you, my beloved friends, there is much waiting. There is much to come. It is waiting for you right at your fingertips, waiting for you at the very edge of your consciousness. It is waiting for you in your prayers. It is waiting for you in your waking life. It is waiting for you with every breath. It is for you to grasp, to embrace, to come to it with faith and trust. You will find your way beyond the prattling of your minds, your speculations, your wonders and wonderment of your mindful conceits. No, this is something entirely different. This is the experience and expression of your souls, another level of awareness and being.

Your soul waits for you. The entire universe waits for you. I urge you to step forward, to be conscious of what you are doing and how you are doing it, to seek out with all earnestness and diligence the guidance from God that will lead you upon the path that I speak of today, that glorious path of service, that glorious path of knowledge, of the awakening and transformation of the soul, not done in increments so subtle and so small that it is a snail's pace, but change that comes with the leaps and bounds of the jackrabbit, of the cheetah! This great surge forward waits for you. It is available to you, but you must set your stance and be ready to run, for this is what is needed now. No casual sauntering along the river bank, but a sprint, a run, where you may become breathless in the exertion, but you will also become exhilarated with the results.

This awaits you, my friends. We are eager to watch, to cheer you on, to run with you and to help you to integrate and understand what is happening, for the cacophony of these experiences will be great and somewhat chaotic. Yes, after each sprint, there will be a time to rest, recuperate and integrate, but it must come. It is waiting and you must be aware of this, my beautiful friends. For if you turn your head, there will be consequences. Yet, each of you is eager. Each of you is poised, so ready, set, go!

My beloved friends, take that leap of faith beyond the chasm, for there is solid ground beyond that, but it is different and you will be different. We await you there, for the time will come when whether you are ready or willing to do it or not, you must leap, for the changes in the world will dictate this very need. I encourage you to decide to take that leap now, to not wait, to hear the *"go!"* and to respond accordingly. Our love is with you, my friends. I hope I have been clear with our intentions to help, to uplift, to uphold you all. For we love you. We know God's Love. We have great quantities of it within us and we see that you are aspiring toward the same goal. Thus, we are brothers and sisters working together in God's universe to bring that great light and harmony forward so that the universe may progress further, that there is greater harmony within it.

God needs His instruments. God needs those who are willing to take the leap. Here you are, each one poised, embracing one another, upholding and supporting with love, giving and receiving. Continue to do so, whether you are together in the flesh or not. Continue to embrace your brothers and sisters and know that amongst you is a great force for good, a beautiful light that builds an interconnectedness that is helping you to reach that dimension of which I speak and to do so together. Much help is being given for you to accomplish this, to accomplish the tasks at hand and the purpose that you all carry.

We continue on our journey together, my friends. I am Orion. No, I am not of your planet, but I am your brother, as are many who work with me, are connected to you, brothers and sisters all. You will come to know us in days ahead. We will come to help you to navigate the changes coming. It is all part of the plan. We are happy to be a part of that plan. May you be blessed, beloved souls, with truth, with the beautiful gift of Divine Love, the Essence of God, with humility and grace, strength and forbearance, tenacity and wisdom.

These things you will need and they are a part of you. Bring them up into consciousness. Utilise what you have within. Utilise what is around you. Utilise everything to help accomplish what is needed. You will be on a journey like none other, my friends, a journey so profound and so wondrous that indeed you will think, *"why did it take me so long to put my foot upon that journey, to take that leap, to hear that edict to ‘go’"*? My brothers and sisters, I will run with you. Many will run with you. We will go together. God bless you on that journey. My love is with you. Your friend Orion speaks to you with truth and speaks to you with love. God bless you, all of you. God bless you.
