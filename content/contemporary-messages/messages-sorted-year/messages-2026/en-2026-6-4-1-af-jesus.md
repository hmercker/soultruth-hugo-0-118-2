# Each Soul Here Has the Light of God’s Love: Jesus Opens Johnson City Retreat

June 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Johnson City Retreat, Johnson City, NY, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come, my beloveds. I come to welcome you into this Circle of Light and for the time that you spend together, for it will be a holy and blessed time where God's Hand will be upon you. I urge you to focus upon your prayers, your times together, to confer, to be supportive and loving toward one another and to walk in harmony and grace. It is God's Touch and God's Love. For each of you have been touched by God. Each soul here has the Light of God's Love burning within them, glowing all about you.

My beloveds, as you are drawing the angels close, you are drawing the blessings pouring upon you and you will feel God's Touch in all its splendour, glory and wonderment. My beloveds, we come in this sacred time to be with God, to know God and His blessings upon you, upon us all. May the light shine. May we be uplifted beyond the concerns of the earthly plane to that of the harmony and grace of God's Touch upon us.

I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I greet you. I welcome you. I embrace you, beloveds. I am here with you in prayer. God bless you all in His Love. God bless you. I love you.
