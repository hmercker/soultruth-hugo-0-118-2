# Messages of Love, Guidance, and Companionship from Spirit

May 26<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Andrew, Jesus & Mary received by Al Fike

**Andrew:** Indeed, may the Love pour into your souls, my friends. I am Andrew. The world lacks clear direction, clear understanding of purpose, clarity and truth, clarity about themselves. There is so little that is clear in your world, muddied by the human condition, that indeed your prayers are important and needed. Yet, within each soul is the longing for truth and direction and purpose. Within each soul is a feeling of emptiness because these things are lacking. I urge you to pray for the world as you have. Pray for what is needed. Pray for each individual soul to find what they need in order to be in light, to be in harmony, that the Truth of the Father's Love may enter into their consciousness, that they may find their way to God and, in so doing, find their way to purpose, to truth, to what true love is and how it may be expressed.

My beloveds, my beautiful friends, yes, there is so much work to do, so many who are lost and aimless, who lack purpose and understanding. Indeed, as you walk in the world, you become a beacon of light. You are an example where truth, purpose and love may be found and awakened within each soul. My beautiful brethren, continue to strengthen yourselves in the Father's Love. Continue to find the truth that is waiting to be given, that is waiting to be understood and waiting to be lived. There is so much for you to comprehend and to experience. There is so much that God wants to give to you. Be clear in your desires and your prayers. Seek out that what your souls long for. Understand what it is that your souls desire and put that forward in your prayers and in your daily lives. For this is where purpose resides. This is where truth can be had. This is where love can grow, transform and heal you and make you a beautiful channel of love and light in the world.

My beautiful friends, we are brothers and sisters. We are together in this work, on this journey. Each of you has your purpose and your works to do. God will guide you forward. In this, you will come to know that purpose embedded by the Hand of God within your soul. Be true to yourselves. Be a loving channel to others. Seek out harmony. Seek out all that is of light and you will be that beautiful instrument you are meant to be.

God bless you, beautiful souls. I am Andrew. I am a friend and teacher. I am with many of you. I guide this instrument as I have done all his life. What a gift that is, a blessing for me to have this purpose and this responsibility. I continue in joy to embrace it fully. I will indeed be with you to help uphold you, to love you, to acknowledge you, for you are all my brothers and sisters, all of you, and will be forevermore. God bless you. Andrew loves you. God bless you.

**Jesus:** The blessings of the Father are upon you, my beloveds. His benediction of the soul is yours to the extent that you desire it and pray for it. I am with you, your brother and friend Jesus. I am with you in prayer. I am with you often, as you know, my beautiful brothers and sisters of Earth. You have one true and lasting friend in me. I am with you. I shall continue to walk with you for eternity. God bless you all, my beloveds.

**Mary:** The doors are open, my beloveds. I am Mary. I stand at the threshold with you. I will take your hand. I will lead you in. We may both appreciate and bask in the glories of the Father's Love, His blessings upon us. We are all His children and He loves us equally, profoundly and infinitely. Seek out this gift. It is yours for the asking and we will bask in this glorious Love together. God bless you, I am Mary and I love you. I am with you. Take my hand and we will walk forward in light. God bless you, beautiful children of God.
