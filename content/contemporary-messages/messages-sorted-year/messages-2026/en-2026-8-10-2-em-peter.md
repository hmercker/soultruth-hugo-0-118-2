# What Is Meant By, 'We Are With You’

August 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Peter received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Peter, the apostle. I bring my love into this circle and strengthen you each with even more with the love and the gifts that are within my own soul. For your work upon this Earth is great. Your intention to do this work is also great and as you know, we will always be with you as you are being instruments for God, doing the work that you came to the Earth to do and fulfilling God's Will for you. Never hesitate to think that we are not with you because we are.

The strength of our love is great and is with you, each and every one of you. The power of your prayers to receive this Love, to be channels of love, to be instruments for God. That power exceeds your expectations of what it truly is. When we say we walk with you, that is a figure of speech. What is truly meant is that our souls, the love in our souls, the gifts that we possess in our souls visit upon you when you are fulfilling God's Will. It is not just you yourself alone doing this work. There are many, many who contribute to your actions, your feelings, your intention, your words and support you with our love and your love combined, to touch the lives and souls of your brothers and sisters upon this Earth.

There are many laws put into action when you are doing God's Will. This is the highest behaviour that you can manifest and so many laws which bring about God's Will and your desire to fulfil that Will are included into your actions. Be open. Be open to God's Will. Be open to being a channel of love for God and an instrument for God.

Being open is very important and remember, we are with you every step that you take to manifest God's Will. You will never be alone, never. Now let us pray and ask God to allow you all to be channels for Him, to be instruments for Him throughout this prayer and this becomes a most powerful prayer to impact souls upon the Earth. God bless you. God bless you all. I am Peter, the apostle, also known as the Rock. May your light shine brightly as we ascend.
