# Bringing the Living Waters of Divine Love to Humanity

October 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come to bring all of these blessings to you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. At this moment, I am the spear point of God's blessings to you all and to the world. I gladly take this role so that we may penetrate the world in love and light and that God may utilise us all as His channels of love and light. For the world is thirsting. This world is dry as a desert, souls thirsting for that which nurtures them. Yet, there is nothing to be found. Though everything is there, available to all, many cannot see, feel or hear the words of truth, love and light. They do not have the eyes to see, though their souls long for this. They have been long buried under mindful conditions, traumas, pain, distractions and all things that are not of love.

The majority of humanity accepts their lot to live in this barren waste, this loveless land, where all the riches of the soul may be had by a simple and earnest prayer. They do not understand. They do not seek. They merely go along in their day and night without true purpose or focus, merely seeking the gratifications of the flesh and the desires of the mind. How unfortunate for humanity. Yet, I come, as do many, to help remedy this dreadful situation. As God's instruments, we are all here. You are all here to be that channel of love in the world. Indeed, many of you thirst. Many of you have lived a life within that barren place. Yet, you have found what you are seeking. Your souls continue to drink with abandon. This brings God joy, as it does us.

There is much work to do, my beloveds. For to be the water carriers in the world is a tremendous task. It requires dedication, time and focus. It requires faith. It requires your longing for God's Love. It requires all that is necessary to bring the truth to humanity. I once again beseech you, my beloveds, to bring this truth forward, to offer and to show the way to the Living Waters that will quench the thirst of the souls so hungering, so desiring of relief from the burdens, from the emptiness, from that which burdens all in darkness and error. Yet, God offers all that is required to remedy this situation. God gives abundantly to all who seek it. You must inform your families and your brothers and sisters, all in the world, that they need not live as they do. Rather, they may find what you have all found, comfort, upliftment, joy, healing and truth, all these things given freely, an army of angels waiting to minister to all in the world who are desiring of these Truths of this Love.

It is ironic that so much is offered and yet humanity turns its face toward that which is material. It is a tragedy. For the solutions to all the dilemmas of humanity are available through one simple act, that of prayer and seeking out God, at-onement. Yet, so many do not wish to humble themselves and to seek God's blessings in this way. They do not understand. The mind resists. The world laughs at those who are penitent seekers. Yet, this is our way, is it not? It is the way that has brought so many blessings to each one of us. You must be those forthright examples of truth, those loving and humble expressions of love, those instruments of God.

It is for you, my beloveds, to follow the way prescribed, simple and direct toward alignment with God. As you do so, so the possibilities of being God's channels of love will present themselves, so the light shall gather around you and so your example will shine forth. This is God's plan. You are all a part of this great and magnificent plan to bring humanity back to harmony, to truth and to God. As you walk through your daily life, I tell you, you are serving God. You are becoming God's instruments. Your light shines forth. My beloveds, be true to God's Will. Be open to those changes, transformations and awakenings that will come with His Love. Seek to be used as God's instruments. For your will is important. Your desire is necessary. Be that channel, my beloveds. We will walk with you and God will bless you mightily.

I am your brother and friend. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Kingdom, and your servant in this great venture to bring truth to humanity. We continue in our efforts. We are guided, protected, loved and nurtured. All is given by God. God bless you, my beloveds. My love is with you and the great blessing of His Love is upon you, my beloveds. It shall enter your heart and soul, piercing, opening, awakening and transforming. I come to bring you this blessing of Love and all that I can do and be for your benefit and your blessing. I am with you, each of you, and I love you. God bless you, beloveds, my faithful Apostles. God bless you.
