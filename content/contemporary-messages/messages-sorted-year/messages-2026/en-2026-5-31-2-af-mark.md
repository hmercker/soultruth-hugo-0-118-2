# Seek the Love of God

May 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

First United Spiritualist Church, Burnaby, BC, Canada

Mark received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am Mark and I am the protector and guardian of this church. The work of this church is still in its infancy. There is so much more to be done, so many more souls to partake of the blessings of this beautiful, beautiful place. Indeed, we are working hard and diligently to bring in many who are seeking, that they may find refuge, acceptance and love here together in this community.

I applaud all of you for your efforts to come together. I applaud those who are leaders of this church who continue to sustain its light, its conditions and its very being. I say to you that there are many lost souls in the world and many who are seeking but know not what they seek for. There are many who are heartbroken, who are desperate and who are lost. I ask you to pray for those souls. Pray that they may find their way to a place such as this to find refuge in healing.

I urge you, all my beloved friends, to seek your own healing, to seek peace within and, most of all, to seek the Love of God, the Essence of the Creator, to enter into your souls. For in this blessing comes many, many blessings. In this will be the salvation of your life. It will lead you along a path of light, a path lit and guided by angels, a path to at-onement with God. Seek out truth, beloved souls. Seek out all that can bring to you, enlightenment and the power of transformation, of progression. It is a responsibility that each soul carries in this world, to apply themselves toward greater light, harmony and love in their lives. In doing so, you will reap the rewards of this blessing, of all the blessings that may come through your efforts.

May God bless you, beloved souls. I am happy to come. I am happy that you are here. Know that I am with you all, your guardian and your friend. I am here as a channel of love, an instrument of God and a blessing for you all. I am Mark and I love you, each of you. God bless you. God bless you.
