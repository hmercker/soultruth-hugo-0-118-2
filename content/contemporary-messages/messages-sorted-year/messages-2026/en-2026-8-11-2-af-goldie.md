# The Joy of Being Loved by God

August 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Al Fike

May God's Love enter your souls and may you know the joy of it. I am Goldie and I want to encourage you to feel joy, to feel joy in your daily life, to express joy in all the things that you do. I know there are many things in your world that do not bring joy, but when you choose joy, when you awaken to joy and you go back to bed in joy when your day is done, then this uplifts you in light. It carries you and ensures that your day and your night are blessed by light and by joy.

My beloved, beloved friends, be joyful when you meet others. Be joyful when you recognise the beauty of your own creation, the beauty of your own life. Be joyful because you are loved, not only by your loved ones but by God. The deepest, most profound Love of all is yours, my beloveds. Is this not something to be joyful for, to sing the praises to God and say, *"Thank you, thank you for creating me, thank you for loving me? I am your child. I am your humble servant and I do so enjoy to serve my brothers and sisters. I do so enjoy."*

My beloveds, my beautiful friends, it is the upliftment of joy, it is the expression of joy, it is the wonderment of joy that expresses the Love and light in your soul and it does so by uplifting others. It does so by increasing the light around you. It does so as God touches your soul and you grow even more in joy. This is the way of love. This is the way of light, to be that joyful channel of love in the world. Yes, there are times when tears may come, but there are also times when tears of joy may come. My beloveds, the world needs joy. The world needs this upliftment. The world needs positive thoughts and feelings that you may embrace others in this condition of joy and it is infectious. When you do so, you bring joy to another and maybe they will bring joy to another.

My beloveds, my beautiful friends, be joyful in life, for God has given you a great gift in being who you are, the beautiful, unique soul that you are. God then magnifies that gift by giving you His Love. With this is the expansion of the heart and soul in joy. My beloveds, my beautiful friends, is not life magnificent? Are you not magnificent? As you gaze upon one another, do you not see how beautiful your souls are? Do you not see that this is a miracle, a joyful miracle of creation and you are a part of it. Each one of you, each beautiful soul, is a part of the joy of life and the joy of the new life that comes with God's Love.

May you be blessed, my beloved friends, in the joyfulness of being alive and knowing how much you are loved by God. We all celebrate. We all acknowledge the joyful truth that God loves us and that God created us. God bless you, my friends. God bless you in all that is of light, joy and love. We are uplifted and so we go happily and merrily into the Hall of Peace and receive ever yet more blessings, ever yet a deeper Touch of Love and joy.

God bless you, my friends. I am Goldie and I love you. I love you so. You bring me joy, my beautiful friends. God bless you. God bless you all in His Love.

2026-08-11-AF-Goldie (G)
