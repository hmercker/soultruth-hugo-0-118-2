# Faith, Momentum and Expanding Spiritual Impact

March 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

DLSF Board Meeting, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Michael Collier received by Al Fike

Greetings to you, my friends. It is Michael Collier. I am pleased how you have harmoniously supported the efforts that are being made from both sides of the veil. You in the material world, dealing with the material obligations and conditions, seeking to do God's Will and seeking out the guidance in order to do God's Will, and we in the spirit world and the Celestial Kingdom, making great efforts to ensure that God's Will will flow abundantly and clearly through your lives and your efforts. As we have said often, do not worry or fret about the material resources needed for this work, for they will be provided. There will be an abundance and no one will suffer as a result of the efforts made here. Rather, all will be blessed, uplifted and gifted with Love.

As you venture out into the world seeking to uplift, inspire and bring truth to other souls, I want you to know that there are many who have been affected and will be in the future. When you gather in these Circles of Light, take into consideration that all those in the flesh who attend are bringing with them many more spirits who are also attending your circles. Therefore, the influence is exponential in its effects in both the material and spiritual world. Your time together in Circles of Light is very important indeed. When you move about the world and touch other souls, strangers, others whom you know and love, others who are seeking and who are somewhat worried about what you may say or the conditions that you bring, God's Hand is upon it all, my friends.

You are acting out God's Will in many different ways, through many different forms. When your time comes to enter into the spirit world, what you have accomplished will be revealed to you. How you have moved and who you have moved in your efforts, in your service, will be a profound experience for you as these things are revealed. Do not worry and fret over numbers, whether that be numbers of attendees or costs for your travels or what have you. What is important is that you are stepping forward. You are doing so in faith. You are allowing God to work through you and, in this, you are benefiting and learning, as are those around you. There is much work to do, my beloved friends. As the work intensifies, so the support will be given, so the ability to move will be available to you.

My beloved brothers and sisters, I applaud your efforts. I am especially excited that you are returning to my homeland, where I walked the Earth, for that place is in great need of you. There are other places, other individuals, souls who are seeking, souls who are supporting, that you will meet and come to know. The momentum builds, my friends. I know it seems slow from your perspective, but for us who have waited, some for thousands of years, for this message to be given to the Earth, for it to be clearly stated and for others to experience it in its purity and beauty, to see this happening in the world now gives us great joy and great hope for the world. You are providing that for God, for us, for humanity. In this, you are to be applauded, thanked, blessed and loved. These things are yours, my friends, even those of you who stay behind but are supporting from the background. I tell you, the efforts that you are making, the work that you are doing, is vital and important. In time, you will be the front-runners. You will be out in the world, supporting and helping many. It begins with simple tasks but will end with magnificent efforts and outcomes.

May you be blessed, my beautiful friends, and feel within your heart the rightness, the welling up of joy acknowledging what you are doing and how you are listening to God's Will and plan for the future of humanity. God bless you all. I am Michael Collier. I will be with you on your travels and I will continue to support you in whatever way that I can, one of many hundreds who are involved in these great efforts. You will see how things unfold and accumulate and momentum builds. All blessings come. God bless you, my friends, in the Father's Love. God bless you.
