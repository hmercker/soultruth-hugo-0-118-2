# Free Will and God’s Will

August 17<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Judas received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, your brother, Judas.

I am your friend in the love of God and in this healing prayer. I should like to talk about free will, this great gift from God, unique in every soul and not interfered with. Yet, when one considers aligning their free will with the will of God, there is often a reaction, a trepidation even, that they may lose their individuality and become an automaton of sorts. Nothing could be further from the truth. For you will never lose your individual soul nature, and when you align your will with the Creator’s, everything opens in harmony. What was considered impossible not only becomes possible but a genuine reality. Fear transforms into faith. The soul opens in love, able to release negative habits. Addictions can be overcome when one aligns with the will of God. For it seems one could be incapable of overcoming negative behaviors without intervention. Many healers call this aligning with a higher power, and of course they’re referring to the human love in every soul, and for those who believe in God, this alignment produces transformation beyond recovery.

Looking out at the world, one can see so much negativity, so much to be fearful or reactive in anger about. For does not the material world seem addicted to habitual behavior that is less than love and inharmonious. It can be something as simple as substance abuse or as complex as sexuality out of harmony. So many are addicted to violence, feeling powerless and seeking revenge, or simply just addicted to violence in movies. And yet, in your world, there is a good degree of light. There are many who serve others. There are billions who pray, each in their own way, to be aligned with their Creator and to overcome their shortcomings. And so, miracles big and small happen in every moment.

And yes, as brother Charlie recommended [^1], it would be wise to see beyond the political spectrum and love your enemies, for they need it more than anyone. Imagine what it would take to forgive and love those who might persecute you, to see them as souls suffering out of harmony, reacting in violence and in so many ways that hurt our hearts to even think about it. So, I say this, my dear friends: when you feel that hurt or even the anger, the reaction, feel it a hundred percent and then allow it to open in love as if you were asking a blessing for the negative, dark, and lower spirits surrounding you, as they do at times. And when you ask God to bless them, they dissipate, or they accept it. This is a great work you have been called to do in both the material world and the spiritual world.

Let us send the love of God in our souls out to this world so in need. Let us work together as one in the love of God. May this miracle of the great gift be given to all, and may your work and our work together bring many in need into the circle of light and love, and into the destiny which our Creator wishes for all. Let it be so. I thank you. I am your brother and friend, in Christ.

I am Judas. God bless you.
