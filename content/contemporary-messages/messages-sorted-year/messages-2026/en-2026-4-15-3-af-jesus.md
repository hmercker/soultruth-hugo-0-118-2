# Guided Meditation to the Hall of Peace Followed by Prayer

April 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

**Al Fike:** Breathe in through your nose and out through your mouth. Feel your body relaxing, sinking deeper into your chairs, letting go of any thoughts, anything that may take us from this journey.

God has given us a beautiful gift, a place, a refuge, where we can go to be in the light, a place infused with peace, a place where the material world does not exist, a place where our souls can rise up and enter into this Hall of Peace. We are all God's children and God is gathering us. God is asking us to enter into this beautiful chamber, bright and spacious, full of love. It is safe and warm and, as we rise up and come to the threshold of this beautiful place, the doors open wide and the light shines through.

Who is there to greet us but our own special angel, who looks you in the eyes with love, embraces you in love and says, *"Welcome. Welcome, dear brother and sister. Welcome."* They escort you to your place, your special seat. It is reserved for you. It has been there all along. Every time you wish to enter into this Hall of Peace, your chair is there and it is a beautiful chair. It is a chair of your own making. It is designed for your own personal comfort. It is there beside others who are beautifully compatible with you, a resonance of love between you all.

You sit down, you look around, you greet your loved ones, the ones that are with you there. They nod and smile back to you. You can feel their love for you. They can feel your love for them. There is excitement in the air, anticipation of something special, a gift, a blessing, is given in this sacred place, this Hall of Peace. The gathering comes together, many individuals, not only those in the room here with us today, but many others, beautiful souls that have risen from that earthly plane to come and join us in prayer, to be with God in this sacred and beautiful space that is His gift to us. Look around. What do you see? Whom do you see? Do you have a sense of where you are and where this place is? Do you feel the sacredness of this place, the energy, the light, the peace that is truly a part of this beautiful setting?

As the time grows close for our prayers together, through the doors come the angels, those special ones who are going to sit in the middle of the circle. They are high and beautiful angels from the Celestial Heavens. In the middle is our beloved brother, Jesus. He comes in last. He closes the doors and strides in his usual purposeful but graceful way to that chair that is risen slightly above the rest, sits and, as he sits down, he looks at all of us. He gazes at each one with a loving gaze, an acknowledgement of that special relationship that he has with each one. Our hearts feel the thrill of this connection and skips a beat. We feel special in his eyes and, indeed, we are truly loved. This light grows bright and, as the light increases in the room, we begin to pray. Jesus prays:

**Jesus:** Beloved Heavenly Father, we come to You with open hearts, our souls seeking connection with You, beloved God, our souls yearning for your Love, the Essence of Your Soul. May we all feel the unity, the grace, the peace that is Your loving Hand upon us. May we all be in that grace, uplifted and unified in light and that the peace that passes all understanding may be ours, gracious, loving peace.

As our souls open to this gift of Love, we feel its mighty inflowing. We feel the power of God's Touch upon our very being. We acknowledge that power of Love that is healing us, transforming us, making us whole, complete and worthy to enter into the Celestial Kingdom. It is Your gift to us, dear God. You have made a place for each one of us. You are showing us the way with each and every day. Together, we send our love to You, dear God, our Heavenly Father. With this interchange of love, we see the brightness and the rightness of our presence, our prayers, our sitting together in this gracious place, welcoming the Holy Spirit into our hearts, our bosoms, welcoming the blessings of healing and peace coming upon us, welcoming all that God has to give, all the graciousness and wonderment that is God's Touch for each one of us.

O beloved Father, open our souls to a mighty inflowing of Your Love, greater than has ever been in our lives, the Pentecost of Your Touch awakening us and transforming us. For this is what we seek, beloved God. We seek the highest of blessings, the purity of your Love entering into us, residing there for all eternity. We beseech You, dear God, we beseech You and we are open and ready for this gift. We are Your humble servants, my beloved Father. We are Your children, redeemed angels in the making. We are here to consummate our relationship, the love that we have for You and the Love that You have for us. It is a gift like none other and we acknowledge this, beloved Father. We acknowledge and we are here to receive.

Open. Open wide, beloveds, for this is your opportunity, the moment of grace upon you. Be willing to receive. Put all resistance aside. Allow Love to enter therein and change us forever.

Beloved Father, we are here. We are here in peace and fullness of desire and readiness of our souls. All that You require of us, we give and we express in our prayers together. We await Your blessings, beloved Father. We await Your Touch and we come with humility, grace, desire in all the fullness that can be our expression to You, dear Father. We are here. We are here. Open us, bless us, transform us in Your Love, for the grace is what we seek and so, we are here. We thank You, dear Father. Amen.
