# Be Flexible and Faithful as Necessary Change Comes to the World

June 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Ann Arbor, MI, USA

Elijah received by Al Fike

Prepare for change, beloved souls. Prepare for change, for this is inevitable. I am Elijah. I come to tell you that as you are ready and even if you are not, change will come. As change comes, so the darkness in the world is shed by these changes. The disharmony will be eradicated and the lack of love will be changed to love. Even though there are conditions and situations which you find comfortable and comforting, what will come in their place will be greater and more in harmony with God's Laws of Love and Creation. Seek out your Heavenly Father. Seek out what your Heavenly Father has to give to each of you. This will be your anchor, your nourishment, the light in your life and all else that may foment around you will not affect you. For indeed, the power of God's Touch will anchor you in light. You will be able to release conditions and desires that are attached to the old ways and be free in what is coming and that which is new.

Remember this, beloved souls, that change is inevitable, that you must be willing to be flexible and of good faith as you continue to stride forward in the Father's Love and all that that brings. We are with you, my beloveds, very close, very close. We walk with you, we guide you, we sit with you in prayer and we are your guardians that will keep you from harm. Do not fear change, but welcome it. It is part of the changing world, the world that is in need of healing and will be healed, the world that is in need of peace and will find peace, the world which is in need of love and will find love. Light all around you, beloved souls, light coming to you, light upholding you, Light from God upon you.

I am Elijah. My love is with you and I bring you strength, light and love to help you to be that light in the world, to help you to understand why change is coming and how necessary it is. So much comes on the heels of God's wish and planned desire for your world. He intercedes, nullifying the human condition and restoring harmony. Be in alignment with God's Will and plan. You will find joy in all the change, in what is coming and what will manifest as a result. God bless you, beloved souls. God bless you all in His Love and Light.
