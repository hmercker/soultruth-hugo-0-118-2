# Your Prayers Have Brought Great Light to Trinidad

February 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved souls. I come to you as your brother and friend. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Kingdom. I am happy that you come together, that indeed you have prayed in unity. The strength and beauty of your prayers, your longings of soul, the blessings that God has given you and will continue to pour upon you, have brought great light into this place. As such, this light may be used to bless your families, your loved ones and bless those who are part of this place, this nation.

You act and give a great service to humanity, eliciting this light into the material plane where it is much needed. You do yourself a great service by drawing the light into your soul, the Light of God’s Love, His Essence, for this will carry you upon a journey like none other. It will awaken you in ways that you could not imagine, but indeed, your soul knows. Your soul longs for this. It is eager for this awakening. For your soul wants to know God and be with God as it once did before incarnation. So it may be now, if you but open the gates with your prayers, desires and will to be with God, but rather than that state before incarnation, it will be greater, because the Divine Love, this Essence of God, will change your soul and will brighten your soul like it never was.

That journey shall be forever, my beloveds, forever coming closer, ever closer to the divine awakening, further, deeper, broader to the capacities, abilities and faculties of your soul. Seek it out. Seek it out, my beloved friends, and you will be blessed truly. Now is the time to exercise your desire and will to be with God and to open to His blessings.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus and I am with you in prayer. My love is with you, with you all. God bless you.
