# The Very Sensitive Ones Are Open. Pray For Them

August 13<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

George Stokes received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here now. I am George and I have been with you for some time, watching you as you go through your day, knowing that many prayers could have been offered for many purposes, but now as you come together, your prayers are stronger, more intense, more deliberate, more pointed. We ask that you include your brothers and sisters across the Earth, for there are many upheavals, many spiritual upheavals that are occurring at this time and the very sensitive ones are feeling it even more acutely. But, at the same time, their souls are open.

You lights upon the Earth, you instruments of God, pray. Pray deeply, strongly, sincerely, truly for all of your brothers and sisters in the flesh at this time. Pray that they allow God's Love to enter their souls. Pray that they receive more blessings than they have ever had before and blessings which will help them acclimate to the inflowing of God's blessings. Pray that they acclimate to changes occurring in the Earth that are intended to uplift and harmonize the Earth with the Laws of Creation. Ask for God to bless these children who are sensitive, whose souls are open, whose desires are to be in greater harmony, who seek the Father's Love. For He pours it down into their souls in great abundance at  this moment.

Pray and have faith that these children are receiving all that they can receive and their souls are become lighter and gentler and accepting the Love that is flowing in. Give them your love as well. Expect the best, the most loving for your brothers and sisters upon the Earth and have faith in God, in God's actions to uplift humankind. For this is happening now in many parts of this world, that there will eventually be harmony, peace and love throughout this kingdom upon the Earth and you are part of this transformation. Understand that you are a worker for God and one who is helping in the transformation of this planet. God bless you. God bless all of you who pray for their brothers and sisters, who pray to be these instruments and who pray to work on behalf of God. I am your brother George and will continue to pray with you.
