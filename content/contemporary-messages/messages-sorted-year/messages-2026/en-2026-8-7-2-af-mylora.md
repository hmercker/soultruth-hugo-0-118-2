# Honouring and Accepting Our Place in a Changing World

August 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mylora received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Mylora. We too have concerns for your Earth, the changes that are happening now and the changes that will come. For when change comes rapidly, it is difficult for you, as a species, and all who dwell upon the Earth, to adjust to the rapidity of these changes and events that cause disruption. Indeed, the Earth is responding. The elements of the Earth are responding, the waters are warming, the Earth is drying, all the plants and trees are suffering. Humanity insulates itself from these conditions, making artificial environments comfortable. Yet, what happens when that which brings these comforts no longer exists, when the changes are so rapid and so intense that you do not have these comforts?

You must strengthen yourself, my beloved and beautiful friends, for the rapidity of change will indeed bring its consequences. Now there are people suffering, but the world continues to be as it has been in the human life condition. That too will change as God brings the momentum of change into the world. He does so in Love to save and to revitalise His creation. You are all part of God's creation. You will all benefit from the blessings that God brings. Yet, in the interim, you will suffer from the consequences of your actions. Though many of you are often innocent and unknowing in your actions, there are still laws in place that cause a response, an action that will, in time, bring correction and balance.

Humanity thinks of itself as powerful and contains the Earth, but the Earth is an object to them. The Earth is a place to be exploited and to be taken from. Yet, the Earth is much more than this. The many layers of Gaia, as you call her, have feeling and consciousness, have laws pertaining to its existence. It has a light, as all things created by God have a light. When humanity impinges upon that light, Gaia is forced to defend herself and to act in a way that will bring harmony. She does not consider your well-being as much as she considers her own, for she is the one great sustaining mother of everything on this world. She must consider all that is here, all that is a part of this world of which you are a part, but you are not the world. Humanity must change its attitude and think of itself as merely a part of a great whole that is existence, that is life and to be more humble and more considerate, to honour what is, what God has created. If you are able to do this, then you will avoid much suffering and much of the consequences of the actions of humanity as a whole. Yet, if you bury your head in the sand and not look with clarity and soul wisdom, you will find yourself swept away in the consequences of human action.

Be wise, my beloveds. That wisdom comes from the soul and that wisdom is born with the gift of God's Love. Seek out this blessing and seek to honour it, as you honour your place in this beautiful world and you honour the world and its place in God's universe. There is much to consider, much to realise in these things and it is a serious situation, one that will bring serious consequences. Honouring, loving and being aware of what is happening in the world and what your part is in these things is very important now. There is no ignoring or turning back from that which is now in motion.

You must come to realise that in order to be in harmony with God's intention and plan for the salvation not only of humanity but the world, must consider how you may be a part, how you are in alignment and harmony with this plan. My beloved friends, we are here to help. We are here to guide and to protect. Provided you are aware and listening and penitent to a degree and seeking to be in the highest condition, receiving the highest of Love and being that instrument of light in the world, then God will guide you, protect you, show you how this may be accomplished, how you may truly be that instrument in the world. All is in place, the support, the upliftment, the guidance and the truth, is given freely to you all, provided you listen, are aware and embrace the truth. This way you will be strengthened, have clarity of thought, clarity of soul perception and beautiful love that will be expressed in many ways, honouring God's creation, honouring your brothers and sisters.

This is what is required. You, in the life that you have, helping to create through your prayers and desires to be in alignment with God, will be those instruments of light, will be that beautiful channel of love. This is what will heal the world and it is coming. The changes will open the doors, the opportunities and the portals for you to serve God in many ways. It is coming, my beautiful friends. It is coming. God bless you. I am Mylora. I love you. I am with you to help you and enfold you, to love you, to guide you, as there are many angels and many more who step forward in this time of need. God bless you, my beloveds. Mylora loves you. God bless you.
