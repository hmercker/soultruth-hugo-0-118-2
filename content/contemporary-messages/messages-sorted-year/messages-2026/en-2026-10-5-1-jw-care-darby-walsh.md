# Allowing your Soul, your Life, to be changed

October 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Care Darby Walsh received by Jimbeau Walsh

My dear ones, it is I, Care.

We are aware of the many changes occurring, not only upon the earth, but through your connection with spirit and the celestial realm. As things become, or at least appear to become, more tenuous, more challenging on earth, they’re also becoming easier and more in grace with the celestial heavens. These manifestations that can be felt and, by the clairvoyant, seen, often have the effect of an emotional change as one is struck in awe by the presence of a celestial, the Master himself, or a sign from the heavens that gives guidance. And sometimes it is just a simple act which one realizes is blessed.

To be in awe is an opportunity to change, for the experience itself is intended to propel one into a prayerful, soulful, loving condition. So, to state this perhaps a little more clearly for you, these miraculous events as I may call them that you are experiencing and hopefully perceiving with your souls are meant to change your lives.

When my beloved and I were visited by the angels [^1], for me, this was an experience that I had some familiarity with, having experienced many miracles, increasing my faith. But for my beloved, this changed everything. It changed his lifestyle. It changed his prayer. It changed his music, and he allowed it to change his soul. This is what is intended for all. When you have a blessing, make use of that moment, for it is not in analyzing things, dissecting all the parts, trying to intellectually understand the minutiae of such things and what it all means, but rather allowing your soul to be changed and carry your life with it. This makes all the difference in the world. For, as we like to say, how are you feeling? Are you feeling the love of God? If so, all things come into harmony; all things fall into the grace and flow.

The more you pray, the more you feel the magnetism, the light, and the power, the soul power of the love of God; the more you will allow your life to be changed, and in doing so, you will change the lives of others in beautiful ways.

So, my dear ones, be free, be joyous, and be love. What could possibly be better? I embrace you, and also send a big hug to my grandson and all those here, for I love you.

I am your sister in Christ. I am Care.
<small>

[^1]: John the beloved and Mary, mother of Jesus.
