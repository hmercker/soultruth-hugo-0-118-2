# Each Individual Must Find Their Way to a Relationship With God

April 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Isaiah received by Al Fike

May the grace of God be upon you, beloved souls. I am Isaiah. The struggle to find God has been a search that has lasted millennia in this world of ours, for God was present in the creation of humanity, and humanity knew God in that creation. Yet, because of the gift of free will, humanity turned away, decided that they had no need for further assistance from God, for they were happy and fulfilled in the condition of their natural state. Now, so many, many years later, humanity suffers the consequences of that decision.

You, beloved souls, wish to reverse that trend away from God. Yet, you live within conditions and attitudes that are constantly reinforcing this detachment from our Creator, bringing great strife and great unhappiness to your world. Each of you must be strong, steadfast, consistent, prayerful and filled with faith. For it is with faith that you find your Creator. It is with that desire that comes from faith that the Creator becomes evident in your consciousness and your souls find what it is truly seeking.

There is no other way to do this, my beloved friends. God has set the laws in motion. God has created the opportunities, has opened the doors and has given what He can to assist you on this journey, but it is a journey that you must take on your own. It is a relationship you must forge with God, between yourself and God. You may come together with the comfort and knowledge that you are brethren upon this road. This is a great blessing indeed. Each individual must find their way. Each individual must confront those conditions and barriers between you and God. Each individual must honour and acknowledge the true desires of their souls. For despite all these obstructions and despite the conditions of the world, God is always there. God will continue to have the door open and will find a way that you and your dear Creator may connect and establish a bond of love that will last for eternity.

You just begin your journey, my beloved souls. Yet, because of the cultural attitudes of the day, there is impatience and a desire for everything at once, that there is no struggle, there is always good and there is always that which satisfies. All those conditions that each of you carries that are contrary to the Laws of Love, you must take that time. You must step beyond the attitudes and the chimeras of thought that keep you held into an idea of fantasy that all is well and that there are no conditions that exist within you that are contrary to God.

Yet, you are human, my beloved souls, beautiful friends of Earth. These conditions, contrary to God, exist around you and within you. It is not that you live in darkness. No, this is not the case, but you live in the mediocrity of your time, reflecting the mediocrity of your true spiritual condition that is reinforced day in and day out by habits, attitudes, desires and the expression of those desires that keep you from the purity of your relationship soul to Soul, your soul to God's Soul.

It requires great effort to overcome these things, probably greater effort than you have made in your life so far. Yet oh, the rewards, the blessings, what comes with breaking down the barriers, the conditions and the attitudes that are contrary to God's Laws of Love and God's intentions for each one of you in love. You come together in these groups to assist one another, to uphold one another, to pray with one another and to be in that beautiful bliss of God's Touch. You are rewarded and shall be rewarded with these blessings and gifts, but this does not mean that you are free from the struggle. It merely means that you are upheld in these times together, a gift given by God, a sanctuary from everyday life.

I urge you to take advantage fully of what is being given to you in these times of prayer, in these communications and conditions shared, where you will learn and you will grow. You will find the grace of God here. You will be uplifted, given healing and given release from your pain. All manner of blessings and gifts are available to you. It makes that table filled with different flavours and items [^1] look paltry compared to the table that God has set for you, that you may feast from the soul on the blessings today, delicacies of God's Touch upon you, for your soul hungers for these things. I urge you to know and recognise that urgency within you, that fervency is truly a part of you. You may talk of many things, my beloved brothers and sisters, but it is in prayer, it is in desire, it is in that quiet moment with God that the true relationship is forged. The healing and refinement of your soul happens in those moments and those gifts given.

May you be blessed deeply, beloved brothers and sisters. As the dear sister has said, *"dive down into the recesses of your soul."* Look, acknowledge, feel, receive, pray, open and be in that graceful place. The answers will come and the truth will be laid bare so that you may not deny it or set it to a different song, merely to accept it as a gift from God, the gift of truth given to you, a gift that shall remain with you for eternity, alongside of that gift of Love that opens the doorway and brings you to that place so beautiful, peaceful and filled with light.

God bless you, beloved souls. I am Isaiah. I come to pray with you. Here we are, surrounded by the grace of God. Open yourselves, desire with fervency and you will indeed know the power of God's Love to change you, to reclaim you, to transform you. God bless you, beloved souls. May His Love make a great inflowing into your souls. May the flame of truth be all around you. May the light of grace be with you. God bless you, each one of you.
