# It Is Through You That God's Love Is Expressed in the Material World

May 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Jesus received by Elizabeth Mack

May God bless you in this circle and beyond. I am your brother, Jesus. I welcome you to this retreat, this special place of blessing where love may grow and be expressed in the material world for the benefit of humanity. I thank you for coming, for setting aside all of those material things that keep you so busy to focus upon our Father and the Love that He desires to be expressed in this world. It is through you, my brothers and sisters, that His Love will be expressed in the material world, touching the lives of many who not only currently live upon this Earth, but through generations who are yet to come.

This world is in transformation, as are your souls and the souls of many. Yes, it is the Father's Will, but it is also your will, and that has brought you here now to pray together, to raise the vibrations, the energies, as you call them, of this land, of this world, which is so necessary to maintain a home for souls who are yet to be born here. This work that you do is greater than even your imaginations can construct. The Father's Will is being done, whether you are aware or not. You are carrying out your purpose, His plan now and into the future. You know, you are aware that there are many others who are assisting you.

Now, even at this time, in this place, as well as around this world, there are many who come to fulfil God's Will and His plan for this world and all who live upon it. So you see, there is much that is occurring, much beyond what you know, very much beyond what you believe. We come together to pray for the Love from our Father to help us in this work, that each of you has chosen to do willingly and lovingly. For this, I thank you, my brothers and sisters. I give you my love and I give you my Father's blessings to fill your souls, to show you the way, to bring you to that place of unconditional love and your souls are blessed. Open even wider. Accept all that He has to give to you, that you may work in unison, in harmony and in love for the Father who gives you all that you need and much, much more.

God bless you, my beloved brothers and sisters, my disciples, my workers upon this Earth and those of you in the spirit world my beloved followers, I love you. I present you with a host of angels to pray with you.
