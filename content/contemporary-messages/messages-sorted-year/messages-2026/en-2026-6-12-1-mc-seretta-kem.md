# Beyond Earthly Life: God's Care for the Transitioned Soul

June 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Global Healing Experience, Christ Church, Barbados

Seretta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am here. It is Seretta Kem. You have prayed a significant prayer where you asked God to help you with the longings of the soul. Because that initiates a great offering and opening for healing to come into the soul, for the soul to be empowered, transformed by the Divine Substance, something each one of you carry within your soul. The light continues to expand, your souls continue to be transformed. Each of your gifts are being developed. Faith grows within the soul. Humility is demonstrated. Love is embodied. Compassion is offered. For the eyes of the soul are awakening, your capacity to perceive God within you and the workings that God is making in your life, for the love and support that surrounds you.

You are all growing in your capacity to see these blessings. I want the dear daughter to know that the prayers that have been offered for her loved ones who have been in the unfortunate circumstance [^1] are heard and ministered to. There are angels surrounding these individuals. The circumstances and the soul that has lost his earthly life and transitioned into spirit is also being cared for. For this too is a child of God, one that was troubled and will be well attended to, cared for and guided as they seek.

You see, dear sisters, that the healing journey of the soul is one that is unique to each soul, but the showering of God’s Love, His response to prayers, heartfelt prayers, is not unique. For God loves continuously. He adores His creation. He ministers to all heartfelt desires and every soul. Within every soul, there is a crack that is opened where God’s Love may reach and find lodgement. It is God’s hope and God’s own prayers that His children may come to Him all in time, God exercising His own faith, knowing that He has created all of creation and, in time, harmony will ensue. For this is the way that He has created and His own Will is one that allows each soul to make their journey, however diverse, however divergent the path that is unique to that individual soul. God is always with open arms, lovingly caring and watching over. The power of free will choice, as you all know, is one that is a very, very powerful gift that God has given. As each soul grows in their ability to exercise their will that is in harmony with God’s Will, then there is true healing and release of all things that keep the soul away from alignment with God.

Our dear sisters, we thank you for your dedication and your offering of service. For your souls grow in your capacity as an instrument for light and love. We shall come to each one of you now, ministering healing, the blessings and the offerings that you put forward to God. Say a prayer, each one of you, for your own healing now and God will minister to you through the angels as we pray and prepare for our upcoming global healing together.

May God’s great inflowing of Love reach you all. The showering and the blessings of peace, faith and humility be yours. I am Seretta Kem. I am happy to serve, happy to be with you as we all prepare for what is coming up. God bless you.

[^1]: **Note from medium:** Each month, before the Global Healing Experience, we gather for a preparation prayer. There is something about the message we received from Seretta Kem, and the way he addressed a recent incident in which a loved one was harmed and a soul transitioned as a result of the altercation, that prompted us to share it with the community.
