# Call to Become God’s Agents of Change Through Divine Love

July 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Thank you, my beloved sister. Your prayers are sincere and go to God. I am your brother and friend. I am Jesus. I am also the Prophet of Truth, the Conveyer of Love and the highest demonstration of the Truth of God's Love. I come to you to speak of your commitment and desire for this gift, for it is not a trifling matter. It is a matter most crucial to you, your life and this world. Those who are dedicated to this Truth, those who truly desire this gift of the Father's Love to enter into their souls, shall be the initiators, the ignition of change in the world. For when this Love is truly received and its effects truly felt and demonstrated through the individual, a light is generated, a reaction comes from all around that individual.

That is change. It is the effects of the power of that Love to change the world. I beseech each one of you to continue to grow in this Love, to make it your priority, your destiny, to make it the reason for your life. For when the soul is ignited, when the soul truly finds its place within you and in the world, then there will be no doubt. There will be no pain. There will be nothing to hold you from truly being a pure and beautiful instrument for God's Love, blessings and Truth in the world. I say this because I demonstrated this while I walked the world and I know it to be true. It is not because I was the only one or could be the only one. It is because I came to demonstrate what can be, the potentials that lie within each one of you. I urge you to acknowledge who you are, what you are capable of, what you can be in the eyes of God and the Love of God. In this, you may be that agent of change so desperately needed in the world.

You all, to some degree, see yourselves from that mindful place, that place that has been influenced by many things and many individuals. I say to you that that attitude and that perception is false. Your true self waits for you to acknowledge it and to acknowledge the potential and the gift of your relationship with your Creator, receiving that gift, acknowledging it, embracing it with all parts in alignment with it, that those falsities of the mind will fall away with the power of Love. The grace of God's Touch will bring great joy, great perception, insight, strength, ability to see beyond the veil of the human condition. You will find yourself free from the burdens of this world and walk in the joy and light and lightness of being that is your soul resplendent in Love, dictating to your very self how you might be and will be in the Light of God's Love.

I came to teach this when I walked the Earth. I continue to do so to all those who will listen to my words, all those who aspire to what I have aspired toward. My beloved brothers and sisters, I say, step forward. Be in the light. Do not shirk from this, but be in the light and know the glories of God, for they are here, ready to touch you, ready to bring that Pentecost of awakening to you. It is here, available to you at this very moment. I urge you to open your souls wide, to put forth your faith and ask with all your earnestness and desire to receive this gift as it should be given in great abundance. The changing and effects of this gift, you must feel and know. My beloveds, it is here for you. God does not withhold it, but gives this to each of you and His angels accompany you and walk with you to help you on that journey, that experience of true awakening.

May the transformation of His Love change you forever and make you truly His agent of change, truth, light and love. I walk with you, my beloveds. We are brethren and together we will change the world. God bless you, my beloveds. I love you. I give you my love. I touch each one of you. Each one, truly my friend. Each one, truly, a child of God. I am with you. I love you and I bless you. God bless you.
