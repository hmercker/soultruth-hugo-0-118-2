# Encouragement to Trust Our Guidance

April 25<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Tell City Indiana Retreat, Tell City, IN, USA

James Padgett received by Elizabeth Mack

I am James Padgett. Oh, she is surprised*. It is my privilege to be here in this circle with you souls who speak about the Truths of God, those truths that I wrote over 100 years ago. I bumbled my way through it. I was not sure. My dear, dear beloved wife kept encouraging me. Now, I see that the Master was right. It was important that I do what I did and I regret those times when either I destroyed the writings, doubted or channelled spirits who were not of the highest, but here we are, God’s forgiveness, God’s mercy and God’s Love.

Here we are over 100 years later. These truths, these volumes, contain God’s Truth and are being spread as part of God’s plan for the salvation of not only humankind but of this entire world. Here are but a few of the workers. Each of you may question yourselves. You may even have doubts, but sitting where I am sitting, you do not need to doubt. You do not need to question. You know your guidance. You know what to do and you are guided heavily, strongly. You are to carry these truths out into the world in every way you can. With God’s blessing and God’s Love, you are protected, and your future and your right now are very secure. The Love in your souls guarantees, with every breath that you take, you are protected. With every word that you speak in love, you are doing God’s Will.

Is that not enough for you to feel confident about your mission? I trust that it is. I trust that you can feel as well as know the truth of what I say to you. I say to you, I am grateful. As the Master was grateful to me, I am grateful to you for the work that you do. Do not worry if you fail. If you make a mistake, know that God will get it fixed and forgive you. For the Love, the Love is beyond comprehension. The mercy is great, immeasurable. The Father loves each one of you more than you can imagine, each one different, each one having special gifts, special qualities reaching different people and different times. I am so proud of you for doing what you do and what you are going to be doing. I thank you with my entire soul. I thank you for doing this work for God, for all of us and for humankind. I thank you.

May God continue to bless you. May you enjoy His blessings. May you accept, embrace and love the blessings you are given. I am James Padgett. I thank you for listening to me and thank you for allowing me to speak. God bless you. My love is with each and every one of you.
