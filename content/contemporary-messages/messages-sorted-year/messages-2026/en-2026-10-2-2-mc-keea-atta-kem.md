# Know the Instrument of Your Soul

October 2<sup>nd</sup>, 2026

Global Healing Experience, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

It is Keea Atta Kem. Welcome to each one of you as we all draw together, standing in the grace of God's Love, having our souls nourished by these Living Waters of God's Substance. I wonder if you recall long ago when we began this journey of offering these healing opportunities, where beautiful souls that live on Earth and also in the spirit world come together to receive healing, to come to know God's Love and to understand the journey of the soul. There was a time when we addressed the growth of this gathering, where some were seeing many people attending. While this is greatly attended by spirit, when we were speaking about larger numbers of attendants, we were also referring to those in your material world. That time is approaching. For as your world continues to transition and the Earth is changing in many ways, there will be a requirement for souls to be able to receive the replenishment of God's Love and the healing blessings by the angels.

Yes, indeed, we have turned a corner where the rapport has been established in greater ways [^1]. You are currently exchanging your experiences and there is a significant importance on coming to understand what it means to truly be an instrument. It is our hope, our desire, that individuals who are praying for God's Love, desiring for their souls to be awakened and developed, can reflect upon this, upon their instrumentality, the instrument of their soul, the soul consciousness awakening to the Presence of God, where God is able to establish a strong instrument where many blessings are able to flourish.

It is far more than your physical experience. It is of your spirit and it is of your soul. I ask you, in deeper ways, to become conscious of that precious place within you, through which you can know God, allowing your soul to awaken further and for you to surrender yourself to the ways in which God can orchestrate His instrument. In this way, you will experience a number of changes within you and you will be able to have your day flow in a way that God can truly touch others through. It is in the everyday small ways that this will occur and unfold.

Please be open to the experience of these small ways, everyday ways, coming to experience your own soul and its love, filling you. In that way, you are preparing yourself to come to know the love that is within your soul and all the expressions that move through you. This allows you to be of greater innocence in how God will bless many, give you words to say, help you to know when to speak, when to listen, help to guide you where to go and how to be. We ask that everyone help themselves to grow and progress in new ways. For there is a turning point with every beautiful soul that is happening now.

So awaken yourselves to the changes. Align with your desires and come to know that sweet, gentle, graceful, humble, compassionate, loving soul that you are. In this way, my dear friends, I can promise to you that God will orchestrate much through you and you will enjoy the beauty of that relationship between you and God, soul to Soul. This will draw God's instruments, your Celestial Angel friends and your stellar friends near to you to collaborate in ways that are yet to be experienced. Let us all allow the progression, the evolution through God's Love, to unfold in each one of our lives. For we are all moving forward.

I am Keea Atta Kem, elated at these turning points, both on Earth and in spirit. God bless you all.
