# Be a Channel for God in Every Way that is Possible

July 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come to you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend. I am Jesus. You speak of serving God. Indeed, you do every time you pray, every time you gather in circles such as this, every time you walk through your door into the world and every time you bring others into your homes, you are serving God. For how else is light brought and shared in the world? You have discovered the power of prayer. You have discovered the power of God's Love. You have discovered the potentials that are within you, that are revealed day by day. You are a light. You shine forth, my beloveds. I urge you to see your life from this perspective, to be truly a channel of love for God in every way that is possible, every way that is within you and around you. For as you build the light, as the light builds within you, so the possibilities of manifestations and blessings coming through you and around you become greater as you grow in light.

It is very simple, my beloveds. God does not make a complicated world for you. God's Path of Love is not complex, but simple. As you continue to go into your souls and discover that inner world, that beautiful world, what is within you reveals itself and all its power and glory, wonderment and what is with God. As you discover your ability, this innate ability to be with God, you discover who and what God is. You discover the power of love. My beloveds, you are being guided, protected and developed with soul qualities and abilities, prepared for what is to come. Indeed, it is difficult for you to be patient and wait, to be faithful and consistent, but here you are, my beloveds. Here you are, continuing to pray and ask to be used as God's servants, to ask that your gifts may come to fruition, to ask that your soul may be transformed by the Father's Love.

This is all that is required of you. This is all that God needs to utilise your instrumentality. As you follow your guidance, as you allow we from the Celestial Kingdom to be close, to inspire and to assist you upon the journey, so many things may manifest through you and around you that are a part of God's plan and God's intention to assist others to disperse the darkness and bring light. Much of this you do not know and you are not aware of. You innocently go through life, thinking and feeling from your material senses and abilities. Yet, another world exists, the world of your soul, the world of spirit, the world that is unseen by the physical and yet very much alive, very much a part of your life. With this innocence and your faith combined, you become a powerful channel, instrument of God's Love. You are a light. You shall always be a light.

All that God wishes of you is your dedication to be with Him and your desire to be that channel of love. With this, all manner of blessings may come, not only to you, but to your families, your loved ones and to the world. You are creating light in this world with every breath. It is being channelled through you. It is your purpose. As you are a light, so you draw light to you. The laws are obeyed, my beloveds. As you seek to serve, so God will open the doors of service for you. As you seek to progress and develop in the soul and His Love continues to flow in, so your gifts and abilities will grow and open and manifest in many different ways. This gift of Direct Voice, as you call it, is but one of many.

Yet, your dedications together, your focus and desire, your prayers and efforts, all bring the culmination and answer to your prayers. You have great faith, my beloveds. You shall walk the world as God's instrument and our instrument to bring healing and truth, to bring love and light, to bring harmony and peace and most importantly, to help others discover joy, the joy that emanates from the soul when the soul is truly in alignment with God and receiving the gift that comes with this. Each of you has the key to these blessings. Each of you knows the journey to be taken and each of you does so willingly.

May you be blessed, my beloveds, blessed mightily, with an inflowing of His Love and feel the expansion, the transformation, the awakening that comes with this gift upon the heels of your prayers and the desires of your souls. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus and I love you. I am with you often. You have a special place within my heart. For how many walk the world as you do? How many understand the truth as you do? You are my disciples and we are brethren, working with God to help change the world from darkness into light. God bless you, my beloveds. My love is with you always. God bless you.
