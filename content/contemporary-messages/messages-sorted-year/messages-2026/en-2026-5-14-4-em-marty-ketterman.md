# The Joy of Progression

May 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Happy Days Farm Retreat, Dallas, OR, USA

Marty Ketterman received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Marty. My beloved sister knows me well, so this will be an easy channelling. We do come from the Celestial Heavens. We travel down, down, down to the Earth plane and we are happy to do so because our love for you is great. Our love is just a shadow of the Love that God has for each one of you. Here I speak to all who are here, even those who are not physically present, not in this circle. God's Love is beyond measure and God sends us to you to assist in your development and to assist you along your path to at-onement with Him. I say Him, but many of us, including my dear sister, know that He has a Her component as well, so in respect to Her, I acknowledge that God is a dual soul, has components of male and female. Were it not the case, there would be only males upon the Earth. That would not be so pleasant, would it, gentlemen?

Anyway, I jest with you in part because when I was on the Earth I often spoke about having joy and progression, how important it is that you enjoy your progress and not be so very serious about what you are doing on your path to God. Joy is a part of God and joy and happiness are parts of you when you receive this Divine Love. I came and enjoyed your banter at the dinner table. I came, I influenced and I want you to understand that it is not work. It is joy.

Yes, there are times when there is serious work to be done, most of which is releasing, releasing those things from within you that have attached themselves to you that are not of love and not of God, but are mistakes, errors. This is when the work comes in, but serving your brothers and sisters, following God's Will, opening to God's Love, that is not work. That is play. I hope you consider how playful it is to bring your friends, your family and your loved ones along this path. How joyful you feel when they get it, when they understand, when they seek more. That is joy. That is happiness. That is fulfilment of God's promise to bring His children home to Him.

So, laugh, brothers and sisters. Enjoy each other's company as you walk this path together and sometimes alone. Enjoy, lighten up, as I used to tell my young sister. Lighten up.

I am Marty and I am going to be with you for a while to help you have fun while you progress. May our Father bless you all and all of your family, all of your family, your natural family, your biological family and your Divine family. My love is with you, each one. God bless you.
