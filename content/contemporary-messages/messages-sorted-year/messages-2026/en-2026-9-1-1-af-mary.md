# All Things Start and End With God

September 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Father be yours, beloved souls. I am Mary and I come to be with you in your prayers, in your desires and efforts to be with God, for all things start with God and all things end with God. It is God that has provided all that you require in your spiritual pursuit. He has given you His Love and He has given you the capacity to know His Love, to receive it and to have it grow and expand within you. He has given you the blessings of the angels to help you along the path. He has given you a life, a life where the ministrations of His Hand and those of the angels uplift you and help you along that path.

Within you are great gifts, my beloveds. Within you are the capacities to be His channels of love, to be that light in the world. Oh, how God is generous in the abundance of His Love, in all that He gives to you and He gives to the universe. His beautiful creation continues to thrive with God's Hand upon it and for those of you on Earth, you within desire and know that God will make the world to thrive in harmony, peace and love. You may be His instruments to do so.

You may be those channels to bring peace, comfort, harmony, love, wisdom, truth, light. All the goodness of God's creation may manifest through you, my beloveds, provided you are in alignment with God, that you come to be with God each and every day and that as you live your life, may it be a life filled with God, filled with Love, filled with light. As you walk this world, my friends, beautiful children of God, seek out that which God guides you to do. Seek out that which God gives to you in the abundance of His Love, in the desire, His desire, that you may thrive and be fulfilled in the spiritual blessings of His Hand, His Holy Spirit upon you.

My beloveds, I walk with you. I am with you. I love each one of you and all who are a light in the world. I come to uplift, to bring comfort, strength and peace. All the goodness that is of the soul, may it shine forth within you. As this is your expression, your being, the angels will surround you and much light will come from God, bathing you in His grace, peace and Love. May you be blessed, my beloveds and beautiful children. May you be blessed mightily with a great inflowing of His Love and come to know Him, come to know your Creator. For your Creator longs to know you in such a way that you are aware and you commune in such depth and wonderment, that joy springs forth from your hearts and understanding is imbued within your soul.

All is given freely, abundantly, my beloveds, all is given. Open yourself to what is and what shall be and we will commune together in the Light of God's Love. God bless you all. I am Mary and I love you all. God bless you.
