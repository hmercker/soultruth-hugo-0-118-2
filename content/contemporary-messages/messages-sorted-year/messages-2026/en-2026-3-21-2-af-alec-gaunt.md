# Alec Gaunt Describes His Role in Creating Circles of Light and Explains the Process of Awakening in Love

March 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Divine Prayer & Elevation Retreat, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Alec Gaunt received by Al Fike

May the blessings of this Love be with you, my friends. May you open to its gifts. For as you open, you open the door to a wondrous journey, a journey toward truth, toward universal love, light and all the goodness that can come to you. My friends, I am Alec. I am gratified to see so many here today. As some of you may know, I began Circles of Light before any of you were involved. Indeed, the work that I took on reached many souls and the testimony of this work continues as you all have an interest in this wondrous truth.

Here we are today, my friends, an opportunity to share with one another, to pray with one another and to break bread together. All the goodness of life can be had in this little circle. All the richness of the soul can be blessed in this little circle. Indeed, God's intention is that each of you may awaken further in soul consciousness and awareness. In this awakening comes the transformation of the soul. What you are today can be changed and transformed into something, someone who is quite different from now. The moment, the possibilities of that change, that transformation, that awakening, is at this very moment of time.

It is for you to take advantage of the blessings that are here, my friends. For each of you has an angel standing beside you and with that angel friend of yours, the two of you can set forth in earnest prayer, igniting the laws that pertain to this Gift of Love. Each of you may find something quite profound that may change your awareness, your consciousness toward yourself, toward God, toward the universe and toward what is truly life. This is the wonderment of this gift of Love, for it opens up the faculties of the soul, awakens these things so that you may see and hear and know with clarity and power and wonderment what truth is.

A drop of Love that is the Essence of God can do all of this. My beloved friends, there is nothing else in the universe that can do this. Many are on a spiritual path and pursuit, but they often use the faculties of their mind, the material consciousness and, at times, the faculties of the spirit body, the spirit consciousness. Few go deep enough to truly understand the power and faculties of the soul. Our wish, as Celestial spirits, ones who have gone through this process, completely transformed by it and know the journey well, wish for each one of you and all who are interested to subscribe to this journey, this journey of soul awakening and transformation.

It does not require many books, reading and discussion or an effort of intellectual delineations. All of these things are irrelevant. What it requires is a simple task, seeking out God from that place deep within you, so deep you cannot go any further. It is the very core of your being. When that core and desire is ignited in prayer and asks for the simple blessing that says, *"please open my soul to a great and flowing of Your Love,"* this gift from God. When one is persistent and dedicated to receiving this Gift, then the transformation begins. All that you think you are and all that you think life is will change irrevocably upon a path that awakens the perceptions of the soul, the understandings of the soul. In that awakening and knowing, truth will come. Truth will come to you individually.

The awakening is an individual process. It does not require others to help you birth this new awakening, but others may help and be with you. It is not an exclusive thing, but the true awakening, the true experience of it, comes with the individual's pursuit of this gift and to be open and receptive to the results of your prayers. God is patient and kind and generous. He does not judge. There are no restrictions. This gift is given freely and it is the highest gift that can be given in all the universe. Indeed, there are many souls throughout the universe that have received this gift, some of them are here today, ones from other planets and other realities. All are intent on instigating and assisting you upon this path of soul awakening. It comes with the peace that surrounds you. It comes with a blessing of God's Love pouring within you. It comes with your understanding, your awareness, of who and what God is and who and what you are.

Simple but profound questions, my friends, questions that can be answered not with the mind, but with the mind of the soul. We urge you to make efforts this day and every day, if you can, to seek the answers to those questions and God will ensure that the answers will come and that this great Love of His Soul will be given abundantly and freely. Thank you, beloved friends. I am Alec and I come to pray with you in these moments. Blessings to you, to you all.
