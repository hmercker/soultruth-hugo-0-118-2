# Importance of Your Ministry

April 25<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Tell City Indiana Retreat, Tell City, IN, USA

Moses received by Elizabeth Mack

I am with you today to talk about movement, movement of my people, continuously moving, sometimes in the wrong direction, sometimes into the Light. We each have a choice given to us by God. Those choices determine where we go, how we go and, for some of us, where we end up. It is God's Will for His children, all of those souls that He has created, to be with Him in the Love, the Light of His Existence. But it is the choice of His children that determines where they go, how they go and where they end up. His Love is immeasurable and, therefore, there is no end for those who receive His sacred gift of Love into their souls. It is merely a question of how quickly they move along their paths, how quickly they join with Him in this Love and how far they choose to go in this Love.

My brethren, you each have work to do. Certainly, you know this within your souls. We urge you, we ask you, to give yourselves to this work of becoming at one with our Heavenly Father. It is essential. Of course, you may choose how quickly you do this, but for the Earth in transition, it is essential that you devote yourselves to this work of loving, of developing your Love, of moving forward and closer to God at this time, when there is so much impetus to assist you in your soul's growth and development. Now is the time. You are the chosen, for you have chosen to be at one with God.

You each are ministers of this Love, as you walk through this life and minister to those souls who come before you and help them advance in their Love and soul development. Thus, you move forward in your own true soul development as well. This is the gift that you have been given by God, to assist your brothers and sisters in your and their development of this Love. This is a gift and Father implores you, be open, dear children, all the time. Be open and allow that Love to flow through you, that your brothers and sisters may feel the Presence of God in their souls. Teach them. Teach them how to pray. Teach them to long, to desire to commune with their Creator and teach them to receive and allow this Love to flow into their souls. There can be no higher ministry, no higher goal, no greater work than to bring them to the Father in this Love.

My love is with you. May God continue to bless you, guide you and develop your souls in this Love. I am Moses, your brother and also a leader of humankind. I am one with my Father, as you are becoming.
