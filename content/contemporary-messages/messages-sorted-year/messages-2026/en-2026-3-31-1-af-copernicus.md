# Beyond the Mind: The Soul’s Endless Journey to Know God’s Creation

March 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Copernicus received by Al Fike

May the blessings of the Heavenly Father be yours, this great gift of Love, may it pour in in abundance. I am Copernicus. I spent my life on Earth studying the world and the firmament, measuring, deciphering, putting together an understanding of how God's creation works. Indeed, I discovered many things and revealed many things that were provable and viable to the mind. Yet, what I discovered and what I understood was merely a fraction of what God's creation is.

There are so many things yet to be discovered by humanity. Yes, humanity has extrapolated and come to understand the nature of the physical world far beyond what I understood at my time, although I discovered certain basic principles to help lead the way toward those understandings. Yet, now I understand that it is not the mind and the intellect that will truly know the creations of God. It goes far deeper than that. I did not understand the intuition and the soul perceptions that come with true spiritual awakening. I, along with all who are part of my country and place of residence, were caught up in deep dogma. Although I challenged some of that dogma, I was not altogether wise in my perceptions of things. I felt in my heart that there was more to life than what was described in the scriptures and in the dogma of the time. I worked diligently to discover what it is that I did not know. When I came into the spirit world, I came to understand that my reckonings and discoveries were paltry in comparison to what was known in the higher spheres of spirit. Now that knowing and those discoveries that have been added to discoveries of the past are far beyond that of the earthly plane.

You see that discovering truth is ongoing. When you come to understand something that might be contradictory to your understandings in the past, you must welcome these things. You must embrace them. For when you have an open mind and an open heart, when you are in true rapport with your Creator and your souls are singing in that beautiful light and harmony, much truth can come to you. Many insights may come to you. The worlds beyond this one, oh how magnificent they are, and I am referring to the many planes of spirit and the Celestial Heavens that are far more advanced than that of Earth. There is much for you to experience and discover, my beautiful friends, as you make that journey from earthly existence into the spirit world. The beauty and magnificence of these places are beyond description. The joy that you may feel in the conditions of these places will take your breath away, so to speak. There is such depth of beauty and such harmony is there, such joy.

Indeed, you will come to know these things in time. As I discovered them, it helped to expand my consciousness and my understanding of God's universe. I have written many more theses on these things in the spirit world than I ever did in the material world. I continue in my studies. The greatest advantage that came to me was understanding the power of God's Love, its gift residing within my soul. For the soul, mind and consciousness has far greater capacity to know truth and to understand God's universe than the material mind could possibly fathom or articulate in any way. I am gloriously happy in my pursuits. I reside in the Third Sphere of the Celestial Kingdom. In this condition in which I reside, I have come to realise that even in that exalted place, the level of my knowledge, the understandings that I have are paltry in comparison to universal truth and universal creation.

You see, the journey of the soul in its eternal quest for growth, awakening and transformation will never be satisfied, nor will this journey ever end. It is eternal. I have had a glimpse of this in my soul's perceptions. Indeed, it brings me to a place of awesome reverence of our dear Creator who never finishes creating. We are behind that great wave of creation and we will never catch up to it, so the possibilities of fully understanding God's creation is not available to us. Getting a glimpse with soul perceptions will give us some idea of the depth and breadth of it, but as far as the details, as far as the great depth and intricacy of God's creation, we cannot, with our limited souls, even though exalted in Love, understand these things in their fullness.

You just begin your journey, my beloved friends. Many of you have put aside the intellectualism of the day, seeking to develop your souls. This is a very wise choice, for with that foundation laid and your souls beginning to emerge and be transformed, you will be well equipped to truly understand the nature of creation. As I have said, to fully understand it is not possible, but that motivates, inspires and guides us forward in our pursuits. It brings us joy, as God reveals to us many things. Indeed, if you are of that ilk of the scientists, the explorer, the one who desires to know, then you will, on one hand, be satisfied and, on the other hand, never be satiated by your pursuits. It is the nature of the universe. It is the nature of God's creation. I wish to reassure you that that feeling, that double edged sword, gives me great joy and endless hope for what I might experience in the future.

You have a great journey ahead of you, my beloved friends. I am happy to describe some small part of my own. You will see as you progress what I truly mean by my descriptions. Indeed, someday, maybe you will join me and many others on that pursuit of truth, travelling through the spheres of the Celestial Kingdom, travelling far and wide in the great firmament of God's creation, affirming to one another, joining one another, assisting one another in that great pursuit. Indeed, how blessed we are. How wondrous is God's creation? How wondrous are your creations and what God has created within you. As it is ignited by the pursuit of the Father's Love, then you will truly understand and come to know the magnificence of God and the magnificence of His creation.

May you be blessed upon that journey. Many angels accompany you. Many angels will assist you. Indeed, God will ensure that all you are and all the potentials within you will come to light and be expressed in all their beauty and magnificence, another beautiful and unique flower in God's garden. So you are, my friends, unique and beautiful. Continue to seek out the Father's Love. You will know all that I say is true and all that God is, is magnificent. God bless you. I am Copernicus and I love you so, my beautiful friends, more companions upon the road. It gives me joy. God bless you and keep you in Love.
