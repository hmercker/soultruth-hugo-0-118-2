# Faith and the Soul 1: Step Forward in Faith to Fulfill Your Destiny

February 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Manifesto of Light, Christchurch, Barbados

Jesus & Augustine received by Al Fike

**Jesus:** Beloved brothers and sisters, I come. I am Jesus. I am with you in your prayers and your Circles of Light. I come to be with you. For these circles are precious and not common in your world, but the power of the Love that you seek to enter your souls, the Truth that you live, brings me close. For it reflects the Truth that I carry, I teach and continue to teach in this world and all worlds.

The time comes for many of you that gifts will open and opportunities will arise that will make you a more effective and beautiful channel for God, an instrument for His Will to manifest, gifts that will allow you to see me, know me and to hear me without the addition of an instrument such as this. Indeed, this is what you require in this Circle of Light as you continue to establish the Truth within your soul, your relationship with your Creator, expressing the Love that comes as you seek it. The power of this group will expand and the numbers will multiply. The time comes when you will be very much needed in the world to bring comfort and peace to others. For in our ministrations, in God’s Touch, we will help you to understand what is coming and to understand what is rising within you.

As that gift of Love grows, as it has its effects upon you, my beloveds, wisdom will flourish within your consciousness. The capacity to love will grow and multiply. The opportunities to use that gift of wisdom and other gifts that will arise within you will become evident. You will not be in the dark and you will not be confused, but you will know God’s guidance. You will know the immediacy of that guidance, the power of that guidance and the purpose of that guidance. If you do not understand, I suggest you move forward and test guidance given.

For those who are reluctant, I suggest that you put aside your reluctance. Be brave. Be faithful. Step forward and speak and demonstrate the truth. For how will others know without your expression of love, light, truth and wisdom in the world? How will you grow, my beloved souls, if you hide away and are not an evidence of truth in life, a witness of God’s Touch. For it is your destiny and it is God’s wish that you become one who demonstrates the truth in all ways and means possible. God has picked you and you have picked God. This is for a reason. God has placed within you a purpose, a destiny that needs its expression. Without it, without your acknowledgement of it, you will not be fulfilled, nor will you be happy. For the great impetus of the soul that knows its destiny, knows its purpose, must come through your consciousness and be activated by your will.

If you are passive and wait and think that in waiting God will make everything happen to fulfil your destiny, then you are sorely mistaken, my beloved brothers and sisters. I did not walk the shores of Galilee and where I was born so long ago without expressing the truth that I knew, without taking the risk, without stepping forward into the sea of humanity that was around me. I have the blessing of the world knowing of me. Unfortunately, the truth that I taught was long lost, but there are snippets and parts of my teachings that continue today and are expressed.

You, my beloved friends, have all the truth you need and the truth resides within you. The power of that truth may guide you and bring you far in the world, meeting and connecting with many souls. You see the example of this instrument and his mate and their willingness to answer the call and the guidance. We do not mean for this to be unique to them, but we mean for all those who are truly a part of this great Truth of Love, dedicated and knowledgeable, to be guided and to set forth in that guidance, to reach out to the world. You may think within your mind, *“How may this be so? I do not see the ways and means for it to happen,”* but I tell you, God has a plan. As long as you are willing, my beloved friends, to listen to God and be guided, you will know His plan for you and you will understand the ways in which that plan may be enacted and realised.

First, you must have great faith and continue to build the Love within your soul, not necessarily study and understand the intricacies of the truth that is written, though this is valuable and can be used in ways that will help others. Yet, without the firm foundation of the Father’s Love and the Truth of that Love residing within you and being expressed openly and outwardly, then what you recite, what you give to others, are merely words. When you recite these words with love, with the power of love in every word spoken, every breath taken, every action made in the world, then you become God’s true instruments and you become my true disciples.

My beloveds, the world waits for you. The world desperately needs you. Though the world is blind and ignorant and does not truly understand the power of love, the power of God’s Love, as you manifest this light, truth and love, as you walk the Earth as I once walked the Earth, they will feel drawn. Those who are truly seeking will want you to be close and they will listen to your words. They will feel the glow of your soul warming theirs and they will be comforted. They will listen and take within themselves the words of truth that you might speak.

Be that light, my beloveds. Be that light. Spend your time in prayer. Seek out God’s blessings. Allow God to take care of you. Material concerns will be resolved by us, your angel friends and by God’s Touch. You need not be overly concerned and engaged in the material life. The more faith that you grow within you, the more Love that is part of you, then these material concerns will dissolve and drift away. You will find yourself on a path that you would not have expected, that would surprise you, but would be and is fully gratifying and fulfilling.

This is the walk of faith. This is what we ask of you, my beloveds. We are there to help you, to guide you, to protect you and to take care of you. Provided you are with God and you seek this Truth above all others, then these things will be yours. This, I promise you, my beloveds, that all your needs will be met and you will know the joy, comfort and deep knowings and understandings of a soul redeemed by God’s Love. Thus you will be qualified to be my emissaries, my apostles, my disciples and God’s instruments.

Do you not wish this within your soul? I see within you there is a desire to serve in this way. Listen, my beloveds. Listen to that wee voice within you, that Voice of God that whispers, that voice of your soul that hears, knows and wishes to be known in your conscious self. In this way, you will understand your guidance, you will understand your destiny. God will put you upon a path that will astound you and that will bring joy, deep and abiding joy. Your soul will realise its destiny and purpose.

My beloveds, I come to affirm your destinies, to speak to you about how you may accomplish what God wishes of you. Is this complicated? No, my friends. It is simple. It requires prayer and faith, obedience to God’s Will and desire to serve. Who here does not understand this? Who amongst you does not agree with what I say. What is lacking is commitment, true commitment and trust in the Will of God and the power of His Love to heal and transform. You seek this out and you desire it, but that desire is somewhat mild in its temperament and desire to receive. It must be a fervency within you, something that will be first and foremost in your thinking and in your life. When it comes to that place, when you are truly in alignment with God in this way, then the things of which I speak will manifest and be a part of your life, not only for the moment, but for all the days of your life. It will be extraordinary and purposeful. There will be many, many souls reached, many blessings given through you and much knowledge imparted to others, all in the name of love and truth, all in God’s Will and God’s desire for humanity to be uplifted and awakened.

Will you be God’s instruments, my beloved friends? You draw the angels close. You know the ways and means to draw the Love within your soul. You are beginning to know God and to understand God’s Will and Divine purpose for each of you, but the awakening can no longer be a slow process. It must be sped up with the impetus that you supply, with the desires of your soul and the efforts you make to be close to God and to express the guidance that God may give, to test the truth and the expressions of your life, your being in the world.

Are you able to do this, my beloveds, my beautiful friends? I know you love me. I know that you love God. I know that you love humanity. Be true to that love. Be willing to express it in all the ways and means that are possible for you to do so, for when that expression is given sincerely, through faith and guidance, then it will grow. It will strengthen. New capacities, new gifts, will open to you. The awakenings will bring many blessings to you and through you to others. Is this not the Divine Will, God’s plan? It is, my brothers and sisters. It is. We will be close every step of the way, guarding and protecting you, helping you, uplifting you, inspiring you and loving you.

We are with you. I am with you and shall be with you for eternity. We are truly brothers and sisters upon the Path Divine. Live it, be it, breathe it and you will know the blessings and rewards of your efforts. God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus, and I love you so. I love you so. You are so precious to God, so precious to me and the angels, and so precious to the world. My beloveds, my beloveds, God bless you. God bless you, in His Love.

**Augustine:** As Jesus spoke and blessed you, my beloved friends and students, these blessings went out into the world and are going now to many. For all the Circles of Light that are attended to on this day, these blessings will be given and Jesus will attend each of these circles and bring his light and blessings to all who attend. You see, the work is powerful. It is all encompassing, but it needs to grow. For there are so many souls in the world who are in need and few workers who can be enabled and used to be a channel for the world. Think of yourself not only as this small group, but think of yourself as a channel for the world, your individual gifts and channel for many.

The work continues, my beloved students, and I continue to be with you. God bless you with your work, your journeys, your efforts and for this place so in need of upliftment and joy. May you be blessed as instruments to bring this to your brothers and sisters. God bless you all, beloveds, beautiful souls. God bless you. God bless you.
