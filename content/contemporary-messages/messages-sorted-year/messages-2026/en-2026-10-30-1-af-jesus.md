# Perceptions and Insights Naturally Come With Soul Development

October 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come as your brother and friend. I am Jesus. My dear brother mentions Earth changes, things to come, but our concern today is the changes that are happening within you. These changes are just as dramatic and powerful. For they come with the transformation of your soul. Within that transformation are new insights, perspectives and perceptions that are born within the soul. As God's Love continues to grow within you and heal you, your sight, your capacity to know, to perceive, will change. As you allow these changes and these shifts of consciousness happen within you and are accepted by your conscious self, then the world will look differently, my brothers and sisters.

The way you love and how you feel love, express love, will change as well. For when you are in alignment with God and the flow of His Love is strong and pure within you and through you, so all that is around you, all the individuals, the souls that you associate with, will feel the power of this grace as you do and as you express it in the wisdom of your soul shining forth. You will see all with the eyes of love, my brethren. There will be no judgement or fear, misconceptions or error. Your souls will reflect the truth. Your souls will reflect its own capacities, through gifts and abilities within, to be a servant to humanity, to be God's instruments in the world.

Thus, you are angels in training, my beloveds. What better training ground than on Earth, where there is no end to the opportunities to serve? There is no end to the suffering, the dysfunction, the error and the darkness. Yet, you are a fount of light, a beautiful channel of love. Thus, wherever you go, whatever you are doing, you are a servant of God and very much in need in this troubled world.

See your lives in this way and accept the changes, the shift of perceptions and attitudes that will come within you and through you. You may teach truth. You may bring comfort. You may be a channel of healing, of love. All of these things, my beloveds, you may do because you have subscribed to the highest truth of all, the greatest journey of all, the most wondrous blessings of all. Thus these things will make their mark within you, around you and you will truly be a light, God's instruments in the world.

I am with you in this. I encourage you. For I walk with you. I am truly your brother and friend and I love you. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Heavens, and your servant. Through the grace of God working through us all, we shall bring light and change to the world. As the world changes, we will be prepared. For we have changed ahead of these changes. We will be in that place of true loving, perception, strength and wisdom. It will be yours to give, my beloveds, and yours to uphold. God bless you on this journey. I will be with you always. My love is with you. God bless you all.
