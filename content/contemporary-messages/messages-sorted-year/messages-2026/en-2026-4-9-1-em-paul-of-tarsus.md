# You Are Part of the Blessings Helping to Transform This Earth

April 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Paul of Tarsus received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Paul of Tarsus. I welcome this opportunity to pray with you and to bring to you my love and the blessings that I have to bestow upon you. There is much going on in the world, much unhappiness and much disharmony. At this moment, your prayers are uplifting the souls in this world. When praying together and attracting those of us who are redeemed souls, as you call us, there is a mighty inflowing of Love into your souls which impacts all that is going on on Earth.

This is why we suggest to you that you come together often and pray sincerely and deeply. You are having an impact upon this Earth, as well as God's blessings upon this Earth. You are part of those blessings that are helping to transform this Earth and all who live upon it. While only God's Divine Love may enter the souls of human beings upon their request, you nonetheless are influencing the spiritual environment of this world, and we come to help, to pray with you, to uplift you and the environment.

This world is in transition. It is transforming into a more loving environment, which is also impacting those souls who are dark to express that darkness in a stronger way, which is why there are such conflicts going on at this time, not only wars, but conflicts in the souls of humankind which are expressed in many ways. Pray for these souls who are in darkness and who live upon the Earth, that they may heal, that they may be willing to change for the better, that they may seek our Heavenly Father.

This is an important time in the life of this planet and the people on this planet. This is the time for the great change that many have talked about, foretold and have come to expect, regardless of their spiritual orientation. This is the time, so you who know the Truth of God's Love and all that that entails, pray deeply and sincerely for the salvation of humankind and the salvation and transformation of this world. You are co-creators in this effort to move from what has been a very dark place to a much lighter and much more love-filled place in which to live. Every moment of every day, send your love to God that He may respond.

I thank you for listening. My prayers are with you and for you, as are the many, many Celestial Angels and others who are praying with you at this time. Go deep, dear children. Go very, very deep. God bless you. God bless you.
