# The Big Boat and the Small Boat

March 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Eileen Caddy received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, Eileen, your sister in the love of God, delighted to join you in your prayer circle.

As we view events occurring around the world presently, we work very diligently to provide safety for those in harm’s way and to influence those in positions of earthly power to come out for peace. For the leaders of this world are meant to be servants. And as each one of you opens to the inflowing and infilling of the great love of God, you find many opportunities to serve.

I would like you to consider the Buddhist idea of the big boat and the small boat in terms of your community, which you can look at in several ways. The many souls around the world praying for the love of God to be in their souls, longing for truth to overcome error, for light to fill the areas of darkness, and wherever they are, these souls, which you have designated as being a part of the lattice of light surrounding the earth, as if concentric circles of protection, of radiance, and most importantly, of love, this is the big boat. And as you are aware, I helped create a small boat, sailing on a big ocean, following my guidance from God and the angels every day, and establishing a community that made a difference in the world and continues to have an effect.

When you come together, whether it is in this form through media or together physically, you dispel any distance, and the magnetism of the love of God, when allowed, permeates your circle, and you begin to see now around the world these small boats coming together. In a sense, you could call them love batteries, although I’m not sure that description encompasses the divine reality. The difference is that the emanations of your souls make not only with each other in harmony, but over and above the earth plane. It is imperative for the sake of the earth to pray for peace and pray for your enemies, especially. Pray that those who are possessed by the material world and lust for power that they can be changed. You all may know the story, the legend of Paul falling off his high horse and having an epiphany and becoming a great force for the teachings of the Master Jesus and the early church. You all have seen a movie where all seems to be lost, and you’re sitting at the edge of your seat wondering how can love and truth and justice prevail when all seems lost? And then, as in a movie you’re all familiar with, *It’s a Wonderful Life*, our protagonist in desperation sends up a prayer, *“dear God help me, I am lost”*, and an angel comes, and ultimately goodness prevails. That movie was completely guided by celestial spirits and progressive angels. I tell you all this because I want you to remember that help is yours for the asking. There is no dilemma that cannot be turned into a blessing, and though it may seem impossible, in your souls, you know all things with God are possible and with the love of God,(it is) impossible to fail. So, take heart and have hope. Trust in the power and glory of God and His mercy and shine your light upon all. I thank you for allowing me this time, and I shall step back in light and love with you all.

I am Eileen Caddy. May God bless you.
