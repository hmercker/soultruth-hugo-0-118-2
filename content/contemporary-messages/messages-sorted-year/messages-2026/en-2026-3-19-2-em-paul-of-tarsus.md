# Come To Know God

March 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Fort Worth, TX, USA

Paul of Tarsus received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Paul of Tarsus. I come to join you in this prayer, this adoration of God, the One True God, the One God who created everything and created everything with the Touch of His Love, so that all may receive blessings, so that all is a blessing, for God is truly almighty, purposeful, loving beyond human comprehension. God gives and gives and gives, desiring only that His children love as they are loved by Him, with the Essence of His soul.

To say that God is almighty is an understatement. To say that God knows of our purity, our sincerity, the Truth of the Love within us, is to say that God knows each and every one of us intimately and completely and loves us as we are. To say that God is merciful is to say that God knows us as perfect creations. Those of us who have chosen Divine Love to come into our souls have chosen to be at-one with God. He knows that the best will become of us.

True children of God, try to open your minds as well as your souls and let go of those comprehensions that you have of who and what God is. Ask to be shown who and what God is. For God desires that you know Him, that you come to know Him more and more clearly. This is a primary relationship between God and each individual soul that He desires to have with each of his children. Ask. Ask to know the Truth of who and what He is and experience for yourselves who God truly is, the Light that He is, the Essence that He is and how much He loves you, each and every one.

I have said enough for now. I will pray with you now that you may come to know God, truly. God bless each and every one. May your souls be filled with His Essence, His Love, His mercy and all that He chooses to give to each one of you. I am Paul of Tarsus and I thank you as I step back and join you in this prayer.

2026-03-19-EM-Paul of Tarsus (T)
