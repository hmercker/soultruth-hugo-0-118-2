# Brothers and Sisters in the Universe 

March 15<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Christine received by Al Fike

Greetings to you, my friends. I come to you from a long way. I am Christine, at least that is the name I have given myself so that you might identify me. When I say I am a long way, I mean a very long way, another planet, far beyond the reaches of your telescopes and observations. Yet, I am drawn to you and this world. Why is that the case? It is because your soul and my soul resonate with the same substance of Love that comes from the Creator. With that resonance and that connection, we are able to communicate, even though I am a different being and have a different appearance. Even my skin colour is different. Yet, soul to soul, we have a rapport. I have a rapport with this individual with whom I speak.

My beautiful friends, the universe is made in such a way that there are many, many avenues and expressions of God's creation that you may relate to and you may communicate with. There is much in the universe that you still do not understand or realise exists. Yet, if you look up at the night sky and see all the stars, all that is in the firmament, do you not wonder who is here, who is there, who is everywhere? Indeed, life is everywhere in the universe. Indeed, God has created many souls, many beings who have souls within them that are indeed related to one another. You are one of them, my beloved friends. You are my brothers and sisters in the universe. You have a soul which has great potential, great possibilities of expression, once awakened, once clear of what you call the human condition.

What is it that binds us together? What is it that makes us brothers and sisters? A soul with its potential is needed, but also Love is what ignites and transforms the soul and opens the faculties and the abilities for communication or connection. You have found this key, this universal Substance that is offered by the great Soul of God to all children of His creation who possess the soul. This Love has its power to not only transform, but to awaken the faculties that lie within you. I come to express my desire, my goodwill toward humanity and to encourage you upon a path that will lead to much development and evolution of your race. In that evolution, there will come greater harmony, greater joy and greater love. These things are the result of your efforts to awaken to your potential. It is not a mindful, intellectual thing, my friends, for the mind and the intellect, though a part of you and a part of me, are not the key components to true evolution. It is the soul, its mind and its faculties, when awakened, that will lead the way toward true progression and evolution of humanity.

I come to encourage you upon that path, that higher Truth that you have discovered and you wish to know further. As you grow in this Love, as your light shines brighter and deeper and wider, so you catch the attention of others that are not of this world but also shine with this Light, with the power of the Divine Love, as you call it. This beautiful gift from God ensures that all of humanity throughout the universe has the ways and means of connecting and being as one body in that great firmament. So we are, my friends. You are learning and growing and brightening and your faculties are awakening. It is just the beginning, the beginning of a true journey that is magnificent and full of wonderment and joy. Yet, indeed, as the momentum grows within you and within others in your world, you will open the door to our being fully recognised and understood and others as well from other planets.

This is beginning. The doors are opening a crack, but they will open wide in time and the world will be changed. You have prayed for change in the world, for peace in the world, for greater light in the world. Indeed, as you continue to attune yourself to what light truly is, what love truly is, then your prayers are fulfilled and the openings come as a result. Joy exists within you because with love comes joy. With love comes many things. It is that which binds the universe together. It is that which is of the Creator. When you feel love, when you experience the Love of God, then you are acknowledging, understanding and absorbing the truth of the universe in its way that is consistent and complete throughout the universe.

God does not just give His Love to you, my friends. God gives His Love to all His children. There are many, many throughout this wonderful creation of God. My beautiful friends, it is encouraging to see light grow in your world and that light, as I say, has drawn many who are not only curious but who want to assist you and uplift you in this process. For you are engaged in an evolutionary condition and awakening, a thing that is most crucial for your planet. As the darkness continues to pull you from this destiny toward light, there are many forces and elements of light that are working to help you strengthen and put more effort and more of your expression of love and light into the world as a channel of this beautiful gift and truth.

My beloved friends, this is a crucial time. It is an exciting time. It is the time of many revelations and understandings that are coming to humanity. My wish is that you will be clear with your understanding of the evolution and the transformation that is coming to each of you individually, that your souls are beginning to awaken and they are awakening to universal truth. As you do so, you will be the shining light, the example for many. This is important because those of you who are mortals upon the world are the ones who will gain the focus and the sight and receptivity of your brothers and sisters upon this world.

We will not come until the world is ready. The world must be readied. You, my beloved friends, are part of that mechanism that will open the minds and hearts of humanity. This is needed before we can be accepted, before we can be a part of the evolving and expanding consciousness of humanity, toward the truth, toward the universal Love that is God's Essence. You must come forward and show and demonstrate the power of this beautiful gift and be that light, showing others its beautiful transformative effects, what it will do not only to you, to the individual, but to the world. You are needed, my friends. Each of you is desperately needed to bring this universal Truth to the world, so that the world may step through its illusions, darkness and disharmonies into harmony, light and truth. Be that instrument, my beloved friends. Be that light. Seek this Love that will transform and awaken you.

I am very happy to come to speak to you today, for there is much thought in the world these days about other beings on other planets. I have come to tell you that, yes, in our consciousness, we are with you and we are making efforts to help you and to teach and guide you, as are many. Yet, ignorance still prevails. Yet, we will work together, will we not, to ensure the truth will prevail and that there will be harmony in your world, that you may learn how to wield the power of love, to bring harmony and peace, to nurture your brothers and sisters, to help you take that leap forward into greater and deeper consciousness of the soul. In this way, you will be prepared for the next step in the evolution of humanity.

May God bless you deeply, my friends, deeply in His Love. For it is in that deep blessing that all will be revealed, the truth will come, awakenings will prevail and souls will be of light and love. Blessings to you, my friends. I am Christine. I come to you in love. I come to you because I love you and you are my brothers and sisters. Blessings to you. God bless you.
