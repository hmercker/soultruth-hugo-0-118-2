# God Awaits Your Invitation & Receptivity to His Blessing

July 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Keea Atta Kem. I greet my divine family, family of souls who long for God's Love, receiving this great blessing and inflowing into their souls, a journey of awakening that allows much to be changed, bringing all things into alignment and harmony with God's creation. Each of you notices in your own way the changes that are happening to your nature. Where once you may have struggled, there is now peace. Where once you may have pushed, there is acceptance, all things in alignment with Love, Love Divine.

Coming to know who you are within the soul is a precious journey, one that you take with God in your own unique way, your own pace and timing, your own twists and turns. God allows all of it, loves you through every corner, every twist, every turn, every song and dance that you hold within your heart, the ways in which you creatively express the life of your soul. It is a great blessing to your Celestial friends and stellar friends to serve God in your awakening, to help you along your road and to celebrate the ways that you expand in perception, how you are growing in your capacity to perceive the Will of God.

It all starts incrementally. With every drop of God's Love that comes into your soul, you move ahead little by little in the workings and the transformation that your soul is undergoing. This is God's Love's purpose within your soul, to change you, for this Substance to become fully who you are. I want you to trust me when I say this is ongoing in each one of you, whether you can feel it, perceive it or acknowledge it, matters not. For it is a law, it is the Truth that, as this Love comes in, the transformation takes place and is ongoing. I would encourage you to not try to rush your road and your path. Rather, to be present and conscious of where you are, seeing how you have changed and acknowledging where you can change. In those places that you see within you that are yet to be changed, have compassion for them and offer these to God, knowing that God will respond to your sincere desire. God is waiting for your invitation and your receptivity to His blessings, this gift of Divine Love at all times. You are growing in your awareness of the desire that is within the soul to receive this Love and commune with God at all times.

Celebrate this, beloved souls. Celebrate your path of progression and see the changes that you have undergone. Be a great partner to God in the way that you come to Him like a small child and truly bear down your heart, your vulnerabilities, your sensitivities to the one who can love you like no other, to the one who sees you like no other and accepts you like no other. You do not need to justify yourself to God to receive His Love. God wants you to see your worthiness purely because you are His child. You are His creation. God desires for you to know that all things that are in harmony will freely be given. In this way, you become a soul that is so bonded to God, in love with God, awakened to the blessing of your capacity to know God in its pure form.

As you continue on your journey, know that with every inflowing of His Love that your soul receives, you are closer and closer in a very expanded sense of knowing who God is in your unique and special way. This Love then will find a form of creativity through you, because it is who you are to a degree and measure. As this grows, this is who you are becoming more fully, a soul, a divine soul that is in concert with God, aligned, an instrument that is in tune with its Maker. This Love can make love through you and the world can go around in love.

Friends, attune to your soul, its longings and desire to grow in its capacity to be an instrument of love. As you have those things that are not in harmony purified, you will perceive yourself as an instrument that walks in the grace of God, because Love Divine is who you are. May each of you be deeply blessed in this time as you commune with God, receiving the Love, Love Divine. I am Keea Atta Kem. God bless you all. God bless you.
