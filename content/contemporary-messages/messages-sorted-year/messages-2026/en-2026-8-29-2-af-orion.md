# The Process of Soul Cleansing and the Potential for Purification While on Earth

August 29<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

Blessings to you, my friends. I am Orion. Shifts in consciousness do not come readily or easily in your world. It requires some sacrifice. It requires a sense of capitulation, of allowing and embracing something new. When you ask to be used as a light in the world, to release those things within you that are not of light, to allow God's Hand to work through you, these things are not readily accomplished.

Merely the prayer in the fiat within your mind that says, *"this is what I wish. This is what I want to do."* Of course, it is the beginning, but the actual process, the actual letting go, the actual cleansing that comes. For this desire to be fulfilled can often be painful or uncomfortable. Often you are letting go of long-held beliefs and feelings that are not in alignment with the condition of your soul at this time. The timing and pacing of that release is totally dependent upon your free will.

What do you allow to happen in your consciousness? It might often feel like an unravelling of parts of you that you felt were truly a part of you, but in truth they were not. They were applied from outside forces, or conditions, or experiences that you had along the train of your life, and they have attached themselves to your consciousness. Yet, they are not in the purity of God's Love and that Light that is God's Love. In time, these things must be released, must go, so that you are freed from these conditions, these aspects of your being that are not in harmony with the Laws of Love. These releases, this cleansing, happen in increments and stages as you grow in the Divine Love and when there are major shifts and major releases, you may feel that you are done, that you are pure.

What has happened has finished the process because of its dynamic and dramatic experience that you have when the release takes place, but there is always more, my beloved friends, unfortunately. Because of the nature of your world and the conditions that you have experienced in your world, the process of soul cleansing continues for a very long time. There are very few who are released from their physical bodies and progress into the spirit world that have totally purified. This is an extreme rarity in the world.

For those of you who have been receiving this great gift of Divine Love for many years, the potential and possibilities are there. They might indeed happen and this is our desire for you, for you to know that freedom, that purity and that clarity. Soul condition and conscious condition would be a great asset as a spiritual instrument for God, for light, for truth. For the inhibiting factors will no longer be in place and you will feel great joy, a great sense of expansion and a great love flowing, uninhibited, a love for yourself, a love for the world, for every person within it, a love for God, a love for your friends like us who come to help you.

Yes, there are so many benefits to the cleansing of the soul, and yet that is the beginning point of the transformation of the soul. The cleansing must take place and indeed aspects of your being, that cleansing has taken place. In this way, transformation is beginning, rather subtly and not completely, because it requires complete release of these conditions, but the process begins and you are changed. Often, you feel a change of attitude. Feelings are different. Your capacity to love grows.

Your love for God grows. Then the healing is daily. It is constant. It is the power of this great gift of God's Essence that works with every breath, every moment of your life and continues to work for all eternity as you continue on this path, so that one day you may enter into that place of not only purity but of transformation, what you call the Celestial Heavens. This too is your reward for the efforts made.

Yet, would it not be wonderful if you were totally purified while on Earth and well on your way toward the Celestial Heavens? Would this not be wonderful? Would you not have much to share with your brothers and sisters as a result of this condition of light that you would carry within your soul, the truth and the light that shines forth through you? The mindfulness that so often inhibits would also not be present. I urge you toward these goals, my friends.

I know it is difficult. I know that it takes much effort, many prayers, much consistency and desire for change and openness that often brings you a feeling of vulnerability to be unguarded in the world. Yet, if you persist, a new strength will come, a beautiful awakening will be yours, a wonderful grace will inhabit your being and you will find yourself truly within the realms of the Divine, Divine Consciousness, soul awareness. All of these things come. Is this not what you desire? Is this not what you wish to share with others?

If you are in this exalted condition, then the ways and means that you may share with others will be great and varied, including this desire for manifestation. My beloved friends, we continue on this journey, realising that it is a very long journey and the effort never ends. The commitment must be great, the consistency must be there, faith must be strong and you must grow in all of these things day by day. My beloved friends, we are together in this. We are there to assist you on that journey. We are hopeful that indeed you will make that place, come to that place of true purity, the cleansing complete and the transformation ongoing.

God bless you on that journey. I am with you. Many, many are with you on that journey and many do so with great love for you, great desire for you to accomplish this task, to enter into that place of beautiful light, of grace, love and joy, strength, deep perceptions of the soul and the gifts of the soul awakened and manifest. Oh, what a treasure trove! What a wonderment is the journey and we are all proceeding, my friends, as we continue to receive this great gift of God's Love, even those of us who have purified and been transformed continue to be transformed, to continue to change, to open and to awaken.

It is an eternal journey, a journey of wonderment, of awesomeness, of cosmic truth and Divine Truth, coming into your awareness, coming into the fullness of your capacity to know, to feel, to love, to understand the truth of God's universe. God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion and I love you so, beloved souls. God bless you.
