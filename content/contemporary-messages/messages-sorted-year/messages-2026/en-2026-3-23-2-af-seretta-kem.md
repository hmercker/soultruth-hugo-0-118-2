# As Your Souls Awaken So You Will Come to Know Your True Selves

March 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Other Prayer Groups, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Seretta Kem. I am very pleased with the conversations that you are having together, how you are attempting to discern the truth and to understand the process of your own development. For it is important that you recognise consciously the journey that you are on and that you have enthusiasm and desire for that journey. As you awaken to deeper spiritual truths and soul consciousness, you see the markers upon that journey. You awaken more completely and deeply in consciousness, the way in which you may recognise and acknowledge the journey that you are on.

As your soul grows in Love, so the strengthening of perception, of discernment, of wisdom comes with this love and you see the journey ahead of you as one where greater depth and meaning come to your life. There is no turning back. There are no distractions that will cause you to take a side step on the journey, because within you is a great strength, a great wheel of momentum that pushes you forward and allows you to open your eyes. As God peels those scales from your eyes, you begin to see more clearly. There is no confusion, no obfuscation of thought that causes you to be confused or distracted. Rather, the focus is forward. The understanding becomes clear and somewhat simple in its nature. The awarenesses become global in perspective, deeper toward truth and soulful recognition.

We are very pleased that you continue with earnestness, integrity, desire and love on this journey of awakening. As the capacity of your soul, its perceptions and abilities begin to have its effects and impressions upon your consciousness and your dealings with the world, this will become more powerful, clearly stated and understood. There will be no confusion, rather a sense of direction that is a deep commitment from within, a powerful journey that opens up the petals of this great flower of your soul, awakening one at a time, adding to the whole and making for this beautiful and unique flower that is your soul that has been created by God.

My beloved friends, there is no turning back. There is no turning to the left or the right. It is straight ahead upon the path narrow and direct. You will find yourself walking with intent, joy, confidence and faith. In this awakening to your destinies, you will come to know yourself, that true part that is you, with such intimacy, depth and understanding that no one will draw you away from this centeredness of being, this true awakening and expression of soul that is meant to be. The petals of this flower continue to open one by one, but you are beginning to see the intention that God has in His creation, the beautiful aspects that are truly of you. As you slowly but surely come to see, recognise, accept, embrace these parts of you, the soul will rejoice. The soul will find its proper place, its rightful place, within your being and your consciousness.

It comes one day at a time, one challenge, one triumph, one sliver of awakening, one true perception, one falling away of that which is not of love. Incrementally, step by step, you grow closer to that place of true being, unsullied and uninhibited by the material world and the material conditions. You are that light, bright and beautiful, that must burst forth beyond the constrictions and distractions of the material world, free and uninhibited, true to God, true to God's creation, true to God's intention, true to God's Will for you, the journey that He intends for you to take.

It happens day by day. Sometimes you hesitate. Sometimes that day is not remarkable and does not have any clear and acknowledged progress. Yet, indeed, the momentum is there and the direction will persist. You will find yourself, in a month's time, a year's time, several years down the road, a very changed and different being, one that is truly in alignment with God, one that has released the impediments to that alignment, one that is truly of light, truth and love. You are surrounded, beloveds, by the great Hand of God and those who are with that Hand, those who are an expression of God's Will, those who are truly of light and reside within the Celestial Kingdom. They surround you. You are protected. You are guided and the way is shown. So it shall be, my beautiful friends. So it shall be.

I am Seretta Kem. My love for you is great. I honour you, the progress you have made thus far. I see through my soul's perceptions the journey that is ahead. It is magnificent, beautiful and full of many surprises, awakenings, joyful experiences, revelations and understandings. God bless you, my friends. We journey together, always and evermore. God bless you in His Love.
