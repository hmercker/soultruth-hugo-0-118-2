# Seek the Gift of Divine Love and Become a Light in the World

May 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

Blessings to you all, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you. I come to pray with this dear daughter (D.C.) and her daughter (J.C.) as they seek out Your Love, dear Father in heaven. The longings of your soul, the quest for God’s great gift to enter into your soul, will bring its rewards, my beloveds. There are many rewards indeed, the least of which is eternal existence. As you, my beloveds, continue to seek out the truth of your soul, so you will come to realise who you truly are. You come to be in the world as a light. You come to express in the world the power of love. Within you will rise up deep compassion and capacities to bring comfort and healing to many, to speak truth always and to be true to God. For God seeks you out as you seek Him. In this desire to be together, to be at one, your souls open like a flower seeking the nurturance of God’s great Love, that sun, that warmth shining upon you, opening you and revealing the beauty within.

I am with you, my brethren. I continue to walk with you, each of you. I continue to hold the banner of truth for all to see. You, my brethren, help me in this work. You are the living examples of it in the world. You are the seekers of truth, the highest of truths, the highest of blessings. In this, you have a special place in my heart and, in that, you are my disciples and my brethren. Never forget this, my beloved friends, that we are linked, bonded in the great Truth of God’s Love and the expression of that Love that flows from each of you and flows from me and all the Celestial Angels and the bright spirits who are seeking to enter into that Kingdom. Together, we are a formidable force for light, truth and love. We shall work ceaselessly to bring this truth forward into the world, into the dark spheres of spirit, until every soul, every individual that has ever lived and will live, will know the Truth that God loves them and that that Love is a real and palpable blessing that can be given to them and they may retain it and receive it abundantly and continuously until they too may enter into the Celestial Kingdom.

This is God’s promise to humanity, to all souls throughout the universe, that His Essence, the Essence of His Soul, is available to each one, a gift given, a gift for all eternity. Seek to be in alignment with God, my beloved brethren. Seek out this truth in every way, express it in all aspects of your daily life and you will find joy, harmony and peace. As you apply these truths, simple as they are, they will give you much, far more than if you were not to seek it out. You are added to in ways that you cannot imagine. You are given blessings that would be beyond your reach if you did not pray for this gift of Love. Your soul will awaken and be transformed by it. So much is given by God. The generosity of His great Soul is fulfilled in this gift.

My beloveds, you are truly God’s children. God seeks to nurture you. As a mother nurtures her child, so God nurtures you even beyond this beautiful gift of Love, but the blessing of God’s Love transforms, heals and makes you into that which will become an angel, finding your way into the Celestial Kingdom as you are transformed and grow within this Love, joining us, all of us, in the great wonderment of this place and condition. It is for you to discover, my beloveds, so seek this out always in earnestness and prayer, desire and openness of heart, mind and soul, so that all that is available to you in this blessing may be given in abundance. Indeed, your faith will grow as you experience this Love and awaken to its blessings.

May God bless you upon this journey, one that you earnestly take. The awakening of your soul is the greatest venture of all and you are on the Path Divine, truly knowing and feeling its grace. I am with you upon that path, as are many angels, bright spirits, even your stellar friends join you upon that path to awaken, to be truly who you are meant to be, unique and glorious in your own right. My beloveds, know that I love you. I am with you in my heart. My love for you is great and I seek to be with you, my beloveds, to uphold you on your journey, to help you along the way. I reach you in love. I am your friend and your brother. I am Jesus and I am here with you. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you in His Love.
