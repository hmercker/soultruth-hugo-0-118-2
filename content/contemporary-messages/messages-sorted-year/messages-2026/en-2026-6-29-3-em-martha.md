# Pray for the Resistant Ones

June 29<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Martha received by Elizabeth Mack

My beloved children, I am Martha. I come to you today with all of my love extended to each one of you and, of course, to our Heavenly Father, it is always extended. For His Love nurtures my love and builds stronger within me the desire to serve Him and to be at one with His Will, His Love and His guidance. I am your sister in this Divine Love and I receive all that my Father gives to me, every moment. For I seek it, I long for it, I desire it. I receive the Truth of His Love at all times. I share this with you, my brothers and sisters upon the Earth and those of you in the spirit world who join us, because this is a testament to how one becomes, achieves the condition to be able to be in the Celestial Heavens.

While we recognize that you are relative newcomers, given that the Master came 2000 years ago, that you are newcomers to this Divine Love Path, we recognize that your love is growing. It is more than a bud. There is so much more to the flowing into your souls of this Love, so much more. You will persist because once you have Love, this Love, in your soul, you will never return to what you were. This is a blessing that is part of this Divine Love. It never leaves you and it only grows. It grows based upon your willingness to receive and allow it and possess it.

Possessing it entails not only reception but activation, using it, expressing it, sharing it with your brothers and sisters, your brothers and sisters who are searching, your brothers and sisters who are longing and even those brothers and sisters who are unsure. As you share this Love with your brothers and sisters, this expression of your Divine Love, their souls will be touched and motivated. In many cases, there will be those who choose not to be touched and those are the ones who need more and more of your prayers to help them, establish them on the path of this true Love. So, do not limit your prayers. Do not limit your love. Just express it and pray. Pray to God for all of His children, that they become receptive to the greatest Truth that exists.

I am so happy to be able to share this with you, knowing that you will take it in deeply and it will be with you, along with my love, which is sincerely given to each one.May God bless you abundantly this day and always. May you always be open and ready to receive all that God has for you. God bless you, God bless you, God bless you, dear children. I am your sister, Martha. I share with you my joy as we ascend closer to our Father. Open widely, dear ones, and receive.
