# Moses Describes His Journey in Spirit and The Transforming Power of Divine Love

April 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Moses received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloved souls. I am Moses. I come from a long way. I come to you because you are earnest souls seeking to better yourself, to awaken your souls in that great gift of God's Love. I lived for many years in the spirit world not knowing of this Love, because it was not bestowed upon humanity until our beloved brother Jesus came to open those doors. I lived a good life in the spirit world in the Sixth Sphere. I was very happy indeed, knowing the heaven that this afforded us. We practised our religion there. We were gathered there as kindred spirits together in the joy of our existence and our perfection.

Yet, indeed, when we came to know of God's Love, and this happened when Jesus began his ministry, it garnered our attention even from that far distant shore in which we live. We joined him, we watched him, we observed our fellow Jew from afar and we saw the light, the workings and the blessings that he brought to our fellow man. Indeed, we were very impressed and inspired by this and listened to his words. This is how we came to know the blessing of God's Love and embrace it for ourselves. We found greater happiness and greater life in our embracing of the Truth that Jesus brought. I tell you, my friends, even when you have attained perfection in the Sixth Sphere, there is more. There is more to be had. It is beyond perfection. It is transformation. As you are transformed by this great gift, then indeed your perceptions, your desires and your experience with God is changed. You become someone who is different, different from before, added to. The perceptions and gifts of the soul become alive and true to what love is and can do for the soul.

I give you my testimony. I was a man of God on Earth. This does not mean that when I transitioned into the spirit world that I was at a very bright place, for I merely awakened in the Third Sphere, which is a beautiful place in itself, but there is so much more, my friends. As you seek this Love, this gift, it will propel you into higher spheres, so that you may not go through the transition periods, the testing and the learning that come from these lower spheres. You will have accomplished all of this on Earth, if you allow yourself to do so. You will find yourself in greater light and enlightenment. As you seek the Father's Love, it works to change you, even when you are not focused upon change. It comes, regardless of your desires. It comes because you have received this great gift and this gift allows you to expiate all conditions that are not in harmony with this great Love of God.

I urge you forward, beautiful souls of Earth. Be that light and bring the teachings of Jesus forward into the world. His teachings are simple. They do not require much of the individual. There are no segments of dogma and ritual and that which you must dedicate yourself to that is of mindful origins. It is pure soul, a soul with God, communing, praying, receiving and loving God. All of these things come naturally to the soul. The soul desires these things. The soul yearns for it. Be aware of your yearnings and desires. Allow them to enter into your prayers, earnestly and clearly. You will find yourself receiving great blessings from God. This Holy Spirit that comes will bring such an outpouring of Love that you will be washed, uplifted and taken to greater heights because of it. Seek out God, my beloved friends. You will, in time, find your place in the heavens in which we reside, know the joys which we have and feel constantly and abundantly, and know the Love that continues to flow into our souls, forever changing us and adding to us. All of these things come with a simple prayer and desire and God will answer those prayers. That is the law and, indeed, as you ask, so you shall receive.

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Moses. I come to reinforce the truth, to bring to you a blessing of light, to say to you all that is needed for you to continue on the journey of your own soul's transformation and at-onement with God. It is given. It shall be given and be given for eternity, for those who seek it out and desire it. God bless you. My love is with you. Your friend Moses is here, praying with you, as there are many others from the Celestial Kingdom. We are with you in prayer for you and for the world. God bless you all in the Light of His Love. God bless you.
