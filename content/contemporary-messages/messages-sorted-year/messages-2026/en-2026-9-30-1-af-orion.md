**Question:** *It is my understanding that when the Earth changes ramp up, our general way of living on the grid will likely not be possible. During this time will your people come to teach us how to live in harmony with the Earth when we re-establish civilisation?*

**Orion:** Indeed, this sounds like a fanciful science fiction movie. Yes, help will be given, but given in ways that you cannot see or anticipate at this time. Given in ways that are more spiritually attuned and require spiritual awakening and perception. For in this way, much help will be given, not only from us but from many others. It is not that we will land in our flying saucer and say greetings to the world, giving the gifts of technology here, there and everywhere, making your life easy, sidestepping any struggles and processes that you may have to go through in order to discover these secrets of the universe.

Rather, as you strive, as you continue to open yourselves to truth, then the truth and the help will be given and you will devise by yourself, by the collective communities that you all are a part of, to bring technologies and answer solutions to problems that are in harmony with creation, rather than contrary toward creation. There are many gifted people in the world. There are many, many people in the world and amongst you and within these groupings are people of light, but also people of great gifts and abilities. When their lights are aligned with their minds, ambitions and ideas, then the true awakening to technological advancement and abilities will come. We work as we do.

We work with many, many who you do not know. We help to prepare them to the awakenings and processes of spiritual development that will assist them and assist you in the future. There is indeed a plan and we are a part of it, as I say, just a portion of the many who are part of it. Your anticipation of fanciful intervention, of us coming to make it all better, to wave our hands like some genie who dissipates the problems and brings the harmony for you, is not the case. For God provides opportunities for every soul in the universe to grow, to discover, to utilise their own potentials and abilities, to create their realities and situations, starting upon the physical plane but indeed moving forward into the spirit planes.

In fact, many of the technologies that you require are well known within your higher levels of spirit. It does not require we, who live so very far away, to provide these solutions, where they are indeed available to you now if you but listen, if you are open and if you have the gifts and abilities to understand how these technologies may be implemented for your benefit. As Earth changes continue to happen and establish themselves upon your planet, then the urgency, the need for a different perspective, a different application of technologies, a way to initiate and recalibrate these technologies will come to you. God will not abandon you. We will not abandon you, but we urge you not to abandon yourself, who you truly are, what you are truly capable of and have faith in this.

Come to know this and indeed, as you are open to your guidance and as you gather together, as you do with your spiritual truths, as they are given and as you apply them, then those who will help in the material sense will also be drawn in, provided they too are in alignment with what is of light and what is of harmony. Powerful technologies and understanding are not given without the understanding that they may be used in harmony with the world, rather than used in destruction and disharmony. These things are given very serious consideration and given to those who are responsible and able to have the wisdom to utilise these things. You have been on the verge of destroying yourselves for a number of years now, technologies that are serious indeed and incredibly destructive.

This has perked up the ears of many who are your neighbours in the universe. For this sort of destruction, this sort of disaster, will not only affect you but will affect a large portion of the universe. First and foremost, the understanding, the true understanding of these technologies, important and required and wisdom to bring about harmony and peace in your world is of the utmost urgency at this time. We work with many to help them to understand this. We work with the leaders of your world to help them to understand this. Although many are blind to our efforts, some are receptive.

Some leaders are beginning to see more clearly what it is that must be done in order to assure the survival, not only of humanity but of this planet. It is indeed a precious jewel of God’s creation and many of you understand this. You understand that bringing light, furthering the development and progress of this Earth of yours, is important and necessary. We enter into an era of change, of recalibration, of opening up these understandings among many others besides yourself. You are well on your way, but you will have the opportunity as well to help others see more clearly who they really are.

For is that not the key? Is that not the beginning of self-realisation, self-understanding, to know that we are souls first and foremost? We continue to progress in this way and with that progression comes wisdom, compassion, love and all things that are of light and harmony. When these realisations come, when this consciousness is well established on your Earth plane, then how could there be any impetus that would cause harm? It is not possible.

Are you able to cause harm in the world? Yes, you may falter and unwittingly cause harm of some description, but your intention is to bring harmony, peace and love and this is what is needed in the world. Technologies are not particularly needed in your world, but some things will be given and come to the realisation, fruition, that will help the new world. Yet, these are not so important as the revitalisation of the individual, the soul, the awakenings that must come. These are the keys to a new world, not the technologies. Thank you.
