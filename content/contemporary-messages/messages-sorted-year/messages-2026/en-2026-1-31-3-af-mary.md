# You Cannot Drain God of His Love or Ask Him for too Much

January 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Mary received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Mary, mother of Jesus. I come because the desires of your souls draw me close. I come in love. I come because God has provided a beautiful Light here in this circle, nurturing you in His Love, providing for your needs, providing for the longings of your souls to be answered in Love.

This is the way of God. You cannot drain God of His Love. You cannot ask for too much. God is infinite, universal and cannot be taken advantage of by anyone, because God has created His universe in love and in harmony. When you pray for something, consider that your prayers are that which seeks out harmony, love, peace and light, all the goodness that is of God. In this way, you will learn the ways of God. They will be manifest and demonstrated to each one of you. You may test the ways of God, for as you pray, ask and see the results of your prayers, then the proof is offered in those results.

Do not hold back from God. When you speak, when you pray, when you are in harmony and rapport with God, know that He is there and that He knows you. God knows each of you. In that knowing, there is Love, not love that is conditional, but Love that is universal and open to be given to each of you without restriction, without God saying, *“If you do this, then I will love you. If you are this, then I will love you.”* No, God says, *“You are my perfect and most beautiful creation and, as such, I love you. I am your heavenly Father, your Creator. I am God. In my creation, there is always love. There is always the great potential for love.”*

I ask each one of you, my beloved and beautiful children, to seek out His Love, to know what that is, to know how profound and beautiful is His Love. For this is the Love that brings healing, change, restoration and transformation. It brings you close to God always and evermore, ever closer, ever in His Touch, His grace. In the joy of it, in the wonderment of His beautiful gift, is the awakening of the soul to know of these things of God and to feel His Love from the very depths of your being.

Know it and seek it, my beloved and beautiful children. I and the angels of God, my beloved Son, will be close in your pursuit, in the exercise of your faith to be with God, to know God, to be with yourself and know your true self. This is all that is required. It does not require elaborate ritual or specific instruction; it is merely you and God together in His Love and grace. It comes with your outpouring of desire and love for God. It makes that beautiful connection, that beautiful response that is God who loves you and gives to you in such abundance, in such a way that it will never end, my beloveds. It will never end but come and come and come in abundance, without restriction and will bring you ever closer at one with God. Seek this out, my beloveds, and you will know that which I speak of as a truth, as a powerful truth that will bring you all into harmony with that which is love, that which is His grace, that which will transform and make you divine in many ways.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Mary. I love you. I love each one of you. I embrace each one of you. God bless you, beloved souls. God bless you.
