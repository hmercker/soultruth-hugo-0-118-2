# This Is a Time of Harvest

March 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Barbara Davies received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Barbara. I live in the Celestial Heavens and you know me well. You have sought out a Celestial Angel to come and speak. I am more than willing, for my love for my brothers and sisters in the flesh is great. I recognize the difficulties that you face each moment of your lives upon the Earth, for I too had difficulties. Yet, in my own way, I was able to serve God every day and my soul was filled with God's Love, which helped me to breathe and feel loved and purposeful, as each of you are striving to do. Your prayers for your brothers and sisters may seem enough for you. For indeed, prayer is powerful when combined with the love that you have for God and your deepest, sincerest desires to serve God and to receive God's Divine Love.

Most of you already know this. Some of you know how to reach out to others, touch them with your love and bring them to a place of greater understanding in their minds, or at least questioning their former beliefs. All of this is helpful toward the transformation of this Earth. Understand that part of that transformation is God, God's energy flowing into the Earth, flowing into this plane and impacting people around the world. This is the time of harvest, harvesting souls, bringing them into the fold, that God may bless them and help them begin and stay on their journeys along this path.

There is much to do for many people are ready and are seeking the truth. Be that truth. Be that love. Reach out to your fellow human beings and allow them to feel the peace, the love, the faith, the knowledge that you possess. Then they will follow you, ask you questions, increase their desire to know more. Say a short prayer with them, with your love outgoing to them. This is an experience that you can provide for your brothers and sisters to touch their souls. Of course, it is up to them to accept what you have to offer. There are many who will be very grateful for what you offer to them. Take them by the proverbial hand and help them move a little bit closer to this path and you may even help some beyond who are already on the path but are unaware of it. As you talk to them with your love active, they will feel the truth in their souls and their souls will be nourished by it. This is how we in the Celestial Heavens assist our brothers and sisters in the lower spheres and upon the Earth. It is a fulfilling job and one which we embrace with our love.

May God continue to bless you with Love flowing into your souls, bringing with it all that you need, all that you desire in your souls and all that He desires to give to you. May God bless you. I am Barbara. I thank you for listening and my love is with you, each and every one. God bless you.
