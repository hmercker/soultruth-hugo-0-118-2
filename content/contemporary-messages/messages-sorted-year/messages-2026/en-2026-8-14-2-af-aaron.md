# The Key to Healing Humanity Is Divine Love and Not Human Endeavour

August 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Aaron received by Al Fike


May the Love of God enter your souls and flow in great abundance, healing and changing you forever. I am Aaron and I come to speak to you about the folly of man and the potentials that humanity has to solve its dilemmas. Man has put a great deal of resources, effort, time and thought into solving the many problems of this Earth. Yet, what do we have as a result? We have further decay and problems, turmoil.  For the intellect of man is incapable of solving these problems. Even though the ego of man, the pride of man, insists that it can. Yet, we all know better, do we not, my beloved brothers and sisters, that the only solutions will come when humanity puts aside its pride, adopts faith in God and understands the mechanisms by which God may communicate with each one, understands the yearning of the soul to receive the great gift of Love that is God's Essence.

When this understanding comes to humanity, then true solutions will be found. For understanding will dawn upon the consciousness of humanity, but until the mind, the intellect, is put in its proper place behind the consciousness of soul, then there will be no true solutions. The dominance of the mind, the material mind, has only brought pain, my beloved friends. It has brought many comforts to you. It has been used to invent many things, many objects. In turn, it has made humanity a very physical being, lacking in the expression of his true self, soul. In times to come, the balance between the two will be made one way or another.

Solutions will come one way or another, but it will not come with the wisdom of humanity. It will come with the Will of God and you will find yourselves humbled, my beloved friends, even those of you who feel well-endowed with the Divine Love. You still continue to allow your mind to dominate and to engage in the material world in a very real and palpable way. Humbleness will come as the power of materialism is diminished and the truth of your true nature rises forth to meet that challenge. When you are able to truly see yourselves as God made you, then you are truly able to allow yourselves to be in alignment and in harmony with the creation that you are and to be with God in this way. Then the dilemmas, the lusts, desire for comfort, gratification and materialism will be replaced with the simplicity of the soul's needs, followed by a more humble and simple way of meeting the material needs.

This is coming, my beloved friends. You continue to enjoy your comforts and to be in that lap of luxury that, with every day diminishes the Earth, but the time comes where God will say enough. You have had your way with the world. You have taken all that you can from the world, but you will not destroy the world. In this, you must come to realise how, with the gifts, blessings and abilities that are given to you, each of you and all of humanity, you may step forward and start healing the worlds. In order to do that, you must step forward to God to ask for healing for yourself, to ask for the adjustments of consciousness and understanding that will help you see the world in a way that is more in harmony with God's creation. I urge you to do so, my beloved friends, for there is little time for you to take advantage of this opportunity to be healed, to be in harmony, to know the power of love and express that power in all the beautiful ways that can be done.

These are serious times and you see for yourselves that the world is reacting. Mother Earth, as you call her, is beginning to respond to the insults, the deprecations and all that humanity does in the name of its own sake to walk in the world and take what it might, believing that God has given humanity the mandate to do so. Indeed, you do have the power to do so. The blessings of free will allow you to do so. Yet, this does not mean that because you can choose that you are forgiven for choosing wrongly. For when those choices are indeed wrong and out of harmony with God's creation, there is always a consequence.

There is always the price to pay. This is not because God wishes to punish. It is because God made the universe in such a way that it strives toward harmony, that it will default that which is not in harmony and change all that which is in darkness into light. This is the way of the universe. This is the way of your world. You will have a palpable demonstration of the Laws of Creation in your lifetime and how God in Love will ensure the longevity of your planet and the longevity of coming generations, in harmony and light. It is obvious that you cannot do so, my beloved brothers and sisters.

You seem incapable indeed to truly step forward, but God will do so on your behalf. If you choose to follow the Will of God to truly understand and accept what is to come, then you will find yourself in harmony with the new world that is being birthed at this very moment. Unfortunately, you do not have the eyes and perceptions to see this, but it is happening at this very moment. It is happening with every breath you take. It is happening because it must happen. Otherwise, humanity, in its ignorance, selfishness and desires, will bring the world to its knees until there is no life left within it. I know you do not want this to be.

I know that you pray for the world to be in harmony, to be vital and beautiful. It will be once again, my beloveds. Humanity will have a second chance to start over, to be true to its creation and to all creation, to be true to God. I know within your souls each one of you desires this. You are praying for this and you are seeking solutions as best you can.

The tipping point has already happened, my beloveds. The solutions will be harsh, but they will be effective and the world will be healed. Those of humanity who will not see and cannot see will perish from this world and continue their life in the spirit world, where they will have ample opportunity to grow, learn and to be in light. God has His solutions in place, God has His Will unfolding, His plan being implemented. You will see this step by step as you continue to grow and awaken in His Love. You will learn to appreciate and love God for His wisdom, His way of healing this world. You will find yourselves, while you still live upon this world, to be in great light, in peace, in harmony and this is what is meant for your world and for all worlds. True peace, true harmony.

The power of love manifest in many ways. This is the destiny of humanity and you will find your way upon that path, my beloved friends. I speak harshly, I know. You wish to have words of comfort and assurance. Instead, I give words that are concerning and difficult to hear. Yet, the truth is the truth, my friends. We cannot hold the truth back any longer, as we see the suffering intensify, as we see the Earth suffering, as we see the natural cycles of your world altered and perverted into conditions that are not meant to be in this world.

It is time to truly look, my friends. It is time to truly be penitent to God and to seek His Love, His wisdom, His peace and His Truth to enter into your conscience so that you may truly act in concert with God's Will and God's plan for the salvation of humanity. You possess the key. The key is His Love. Now use that key to turn the lock and open the door of true soul consciousness and awareness and find your freedom there, your joy and your empowerment in love.

God bless you, my beloved friends. Know that we are close and as conditions intensify, we will be even closer as God's Love and blessings continue to pour upon this world. God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Aaron. I walk with you. You may call upon my strength, my clarity and my love. I am with you, as are many. We walk with you side by side, step by step, breath by breath. We are with you. God bless you, beloved selves. God bless you all. In His Love.
