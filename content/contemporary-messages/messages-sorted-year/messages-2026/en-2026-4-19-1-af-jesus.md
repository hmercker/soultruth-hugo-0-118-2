# You Are the Light: Called to Receive and Share God's Love

April 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I am with you, my brethren, your brother and friend. I am Jesus. I am with you. Indeed, the world needs your prayers. So many are suffering. So many are displaced with no anchor in their life to bring peace to them or harmony. This world of yours is full of disharmony and darkness. Even those who have the security of a home, food to eat and all that is essential have disruptions in their heart and their minds that do not bring them peace, for the material comforts are not the source of peace. It is a heart reconciled with God that brings true peace to the individual and that heart, when it opens to the inflowing of God's Love, the Essence of His Soul, knows a deep and profound peace and finds joy there.

Yet, how many in your world know of this? How many understand truly why they are walking the Earth, that gift of life, the purpose of that gift? Some see the world as a finite place where one lives a finite life and there is no progression beyond it. These are the ones who are in despair. They cannot reconcile the conditions of this earthly life with that of harmony and peace. They are withheld from true understanding, not because of God, but because of their own minds and attitudes toward life.

My brethren, you are blessed. You are blessed to know that life goes beyond this earthly plane. You are blessed to know that more life may come into you through your soul awakening and being transformed by the Father's Love. You are blessed because you see from that different perspective and the vision of your soul increases as the gift of God's Love continues to flow in. So many, many blessings are given to you, my beloved brethren, and God wishes to give those blessings to all. It is God's Will and plan that all may know of this gift of Love and be given the choice to receive it or not. Those of you who are willing to step forward, speak of this gift and demonstrate this gift in the world are truly blessed, for you have heard the call from God. God's wish that each of you may become the servants, the harbingers of truth. That each of you may be a light in the world and that as you live your life and move in the world, you may be those harbingers of truth, you may touch other souls, where the bell will toll, awakening their souls, so that they may seek and search out the truth. That they may understand that there is so much to God's creation and to their own lives. That what they live now is merely a shadow of what can be.

Every soul has the potential to become an angel, an inhabitant of the Celestial Kingdom. Every soul's birthright is this, but they must comply with the laws that pertain to this gift. They are simple, but they cannot be ignored. The conscious desire, the effort, the prayers to ask God to open our souls to the inflowing of His Love, must be a conscious effort, a desire to be close to God, an expression of love toward God and a receptivity to know God's Love toward each individual. My beloveds, we continue on this road of progression, our souls awakening, step by step, day by day. Indeed, we will continue upon this journey so wondrous and magnificent, infilling our souls with Light, truth, Love and joy, helping us to come to the conscious understanding of what the truth is, pertaining to our souls and pertaining to our lives.

These things come on the heels of prayer, your desires to receive, to know and to understand. God makes every effort, through various sources and channels, to answer your prayers and to awaken you to truth. Your greatest allies are your Celestial Angel friends. I am with you as well. I am here to be with you, to pray with you and to uphold you and support you. All the resources of Heaven and Earth are directed toward your well-being, your healing, your upliftment, so that you may truly be in alignment with the Laws of Love and the blessings of God upon you. All is given freely. It is for you to receive, to open your hearts and souls to the blessings that God has to give. Indeed, they come in abundance, an onrush of Light and Love for each one of you. May you take in these blessings, my beloved friends, my beautiful brethren. May you come to know the Truth of God's Love and the truth of God's creation with such depth and breadth that you will be changed forever, so that what you may give to your brothers and sisters may be powerful and have an effect upon them that will bring them to God and bring them to their own souls. You have this power, my beloveds. As you grow in the Father's Love, so you become his instrument, his channel of love for another. This is the way and means to open the understanding to that individual.

My beloveds, you are the hope of the world. You are God's instruments and you are a part of God's plan for the salvation of humanity. May you come to know this consciously, fervently and with great commitment and dedication. May you be guided, shown the way of service for many, for each of you have many gifts, have great capacity to serve your brothers and sisters. Allow it to be so. Do not hold back. Do not have doubt, but have faith in your own well-being and your own instrumentality and with faith will come the guidance, the blessings and the opportunities. Each of you is blessed. Each of you glows with the blessing of the Father's Love within you. Each of you has great potential. Together, you have the possibility of saving this world from greater darkness, destruction and suffering.

Do you not want this, my brethren, to be a part of the great change that is coming, to be engaged with your own instrumentality, your own service to God, for the benefit of humanity? Each of you is capable of this. You have many gifts and abilities that you know not at this moment, but will come to your consciousness as you continue to grow in the Father's Love, as you begin to see your way along the path that God has designated for you, as awakenings and revelations come to your conscious self and you begin to know truly the resources that are within you, the power of your own soul, those gifts expressed as a gift to humanity, to your loved ones, to all around you. Each day is a gift. Each day holds great potential to be a channel of love in the world. I beseech you to ask to be used in this way and to follow the Divine Path toward greater enlightenment and love and joy, truth, all that is good, all that is of light.

My brethren, we continue the journey together. We are all God's children. We shall walk this path for eternity and we shall be bonded in love for eternity. Take comfort in this, my brethren, for though you may feel alone and though you may struggle, know that God has a plan for you and that there is a journey ahead that will bring great fulfilment and great peace. It is for you to decide to make that next step, to journey ever closer to your Heavenly Father and it shall be your truth and reality. It is a gift that God has given you with such abundance, with such freedom and joy, that you will be changed, my beloveds, ever changed by these gifts, by the great Love of the Creator. It is given, it shall be given for all eternity.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother, I am your friend and I am with you. I am with you and I shall be forevermore by your side, helping you along the way, in love, in light, in truth, in harmony, in peace and in joy. God bless you. God bless you all upon the Divine Path. My love is with you. I am Jesus and I am with you, beloveds.
