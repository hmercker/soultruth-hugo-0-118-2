# Development For Participants at the Barbados Retreat

January 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Retreat, Christ Church, Barbados

Seretta Kem received by Maureen Cardoso

I am Seretta Kem. As you sit in this circle, we come close for your development, each of you progressing with particular gifts. We are strengthening them, fine tuning them, changing them so that the purpose of the gathering of the particular individuals that are together now can be fulfilled.

I come to let you know that in the way your gifts have currently been serving, you have felt a great deal of comfortableness with them and familiarity. Yet here, going forward, these gifts have been changed in a way and new gifts opened so that your instrumentality will have a broader spectrum, a broader scope, where you will find your ability to attune to your soul, the wisdom and its guidance, the impulses of your soul to carry you forward in the world in ways that you may wonder, how is it that you know these things?

You will all feel the presence of the angels much closer, our rapport deeper, where your attention and focus throughout your day will sit more easily in the consciousness of God, your celestial guides and friends and your purpose. This time together has brought forward these gifts, these awakenings and your instrumentality anew, with new doors in new ways. You will all feel a deeper sense of harmony within your physical body, a sense of rightness, of being at home, a sense of truly knowing decisions that are in harmony, directions to move in, choices to make. You have all been strengthened as a powerful pillar, as God's instrument to stand tall, erect, with a knowing that comes from deep within you.

Allow the integration of your development. Give yourself the time to recognise and to embody these changes. Some will notice new guides with them. Some will be aware of how influenced their mindful thoughts are, but influenced in a way that is in harmony, so the gift of greater alignment between soul and mind has been established within each one of you, your committed path a little more clear with certainty, awareness and growth. The legs that you walk with, grounded on the Path of Love Divine, leave, with every footfall, the imprint of God's Love upon Earth, the current, the stream, the Living Waters moving to you and through you, nourishing all that you touch, bringing healing to all places, circumstances and environments that you engage in. There is not a time where God's Essence will not be moving through you and to you. You are pillars of light. You are pillars of love, whose instrumentality has been changed because of these prayers together, your dedication in the presence of the angels with you, all of us working in harmony with God's Will.

I am Seretta Kem, here to serve the blessing of your awakening. God bless you. God bless you all.
