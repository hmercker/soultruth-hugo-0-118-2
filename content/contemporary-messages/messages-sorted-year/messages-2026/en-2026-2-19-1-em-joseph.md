# Gratitude

February 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Tucson, AZ, USA

Joseph received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am Joseph. My beloved Master who was my son when we both lived on the Earth. I am here to talk about gratitude and what it means, for gratitude is what I felt after I recognised the error that I made when I lived upon the Earth, the error of not acknowledging, recognising, of not knowing that my beautiful son was the true son of our Heavenly Father, our God.

Gratitude I felt after feeling the compassion from our Heavenly Father, the mercy and the Love that my son taught: Gratitude, the very deep feeling in the soul that recognizes the greatness, the goodness, the compassion, the love, the sympathy, all of those things, all of those characteristics, that allow us to be forgiven by God; Gratitude that our Heavenly Father takes us by the hand and shows us the mistakes we make and then removes them from us; Gratitude that is a motivation for continuing the journey to become at one with God.

Gratitude was my best friend for a while as I learned more and more about the Essence that was becoming my soul, transforming it from a shadow to an alive, awake, living soul. I speak about gratitude in this way because often you pray that you are thankful to God. It seems appropriate to explain to you and give you the experience of the depth of gratitude that is possible to feel for the One who created you, who guides you, protects you and blesses you and gifts to you all that you have and all that you truly desire, if only for the asking. Even an earthly father cannot possibly provide all that our Heavenly Father provides for each one of us. Gratitude is a very deep emotion. I say to you, as you offer your gratitude to our Heavenly Father, let the depth of your gratitude be expressed and expressed to God. For there is much, much more than even I have mentioned to gratitude for what God does for each and every one of His children who allows God to do for them.

May you be blessed this day and every day, every night, throughout eternity with this gift of gratitude, enlivened by the Divine Love. I thank you for listening to me today. I thank you for hearing me. May God bless you. My love is with you, each and every one of you. I touch your souls with my love. I am Joseph, the father of my Master of the Celestial Heavens.
