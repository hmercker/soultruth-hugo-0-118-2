# Take the Leap and We Will Be Waiting for You on the Other Side

June 16<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. A strength grows within you, a solidity that is truly of the soul. As this dear instrument has prayed, yes, indeed, the soul yearns for God. The soul is seeking growth, development, awakening and to be freed from that human condition, to be truly within the harmony of the soul awakened by this great Love of God, this Divine Love. You are all upon this journey. Is it not a great blessing, a beautiful gift, that you have one another, that you are friends upon the journey, even though you, each of you, is different and each of you has your unique journeys to take? You are still connected in love, a bond of friendship and fellowship that you can rely upon, that is there as you continue to awaken. Whether you are struggling or whether you are joyful, whatever the situation is in your life, at any moment, you know that you have your friends to collaborate with and to connect with. That bond of love is powerful.

How beautiful is your life, my friends? Despite its challenges, you continue to find the joy of your awakening soul. You continue to know the comfort of God's Love upon you. You continue to be in that journey of true transformation of the soul. Many blessings given and it started with you, and with others, with one choice to pursue this Truth once you knew of it, once you understood or at least were curious about this gift that God has to give to each of you. That single choice opened up a doorway in your life that continues to open up many other doorways and much is given with God's blessing upon it, upon you and upon your life. My beloved friends, the solidity of this blessing, this gift, is born within each one of you. It opens up your perceptions. It strengthens you, gives you wisdom and helps you to truly understand what it is that God is offering to each of you. It helps you to understand this gift of life and what it means, the potential that lies within it. It helps you to see your way into greater light. It helps you to understand how it is you may serve humanity and how you may serve in love. It gives you compassion for those who are lost and suffering.

There is so much more that comes with this gift, the soul truly awakening to who and what it is and you allowing, trusting, opening and embracing this true self within. In time, the consciousness that you have now and the consciousness of the soul will be so changed and transformed that they will become one. You will have no contradictions, difficulties or hesitancies. The knowledge of your soul will be the motivating factor of your life. You will find yourself in a state of joy and freedom. That strength of which I speak will be so intact within you that each decision you make, each action in your life, will reflect the power of love and the power of love that resides within you. Change will continue to happen, my friends. The rapidity of that change will increase and the transformation will have its effects within you, around you and in your consciousness. So much is about to change. You are ready and you are willing. How beautiful is your soul, ready to step forward and to take that great leap beyond that place that is so human to a place Divine, beckoning you forward.

God bless you on your journey, my friends. Know that we are with you and as you take that leap, you will be supported. You will not plummet into the chasm, but will float and spring forward to the other side. In this will be great joy and a sense of true accomplishment, an acknowledgement of your true self. It is coming. Can you not see yourself there upon the precipice and a desire to leap, to go beyond it? What is on the other side awaits you, my friends. It is there waiting for you, waiting for your decision, waiting for the momentum to build within you, that you may take that running leap and do so joyfully, without fear, without hesitation, but complete commitment to be that soul, that true self shining within, but indeed glowing and expressing itself without hesitation, without distortion, but truly as you are and are meant to be.

God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. Oh, I love you so and I am excited for you to see what is coming, to witness this great leap of faith and to truly join you as you leap and as you land. I will be there, as will be many. We will all celebrate together, my beautiful friends. God bless you and keep you in His Love. I love you. I am with you. God bless you.
