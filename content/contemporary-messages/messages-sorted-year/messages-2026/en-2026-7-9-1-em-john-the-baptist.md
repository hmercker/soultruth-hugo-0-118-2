# Become Conscious of Who and What You Are

July 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

John the Baptist received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am John the Baptist. Each of you knows that God loves each and every one of you. Some of you know to a greater extent the value of this Love, but truly to know its truest value, your individual expressions of it, will provide you with a greater understanding, a greater comprehension, a greater experience of this Love that comes directly from God, directly to your individual souls, alighting upon those things within you that God created and those new gifts that are given. Once you experience this activity, this development of your soul and all of its attributes, you will begin to comprehend all that the Father has given to you and truly who you are and what it is that God created you to do.

Yes, each of you is created with a purpose and that purpose lies within your soul as attributes and soul gifts. When activated, when manifested, you become truly who you are. You become conscious of who and what you are. All of this is given in love (God’s expression) enveloped in love and is the purpose that Father gives to you, the expression individually as well as together of love.

Dear children, receive this Love. Pray, pray, pray to activate this Love within your souls and express all that the Father has put within you with this Love and see how much brighter your lives are, how much more loving and peaceful your lives become, how much more in harmony and in joy your lives are, for this is what the Father desires for each and every one of you. Such beauty, such love for each one. Strive for this, dear children. Strive for this complete happiness while you are still upon the Earth and see the difference.

God bless you. God bless you. May the Father continue to pour His blessings upon you. I am your brother, John the Baptist, and I will welcome you when you come into the Celestial Kingdom.
