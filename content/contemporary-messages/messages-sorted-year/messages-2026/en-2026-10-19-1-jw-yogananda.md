# Real Peace beyond Fear

October 19<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Yogananda received by Jimbeau Walsh

I come in the love of God. I am your brother and friend, Yogananda.

I shall elaborate a bit on [the message that the dear sister read](/contemporary-messages/messages-sorted-year/messages-2019/the-true-meaning-of-divine-love-jw-14-oct-2019/), received a few years ago by my brother. We know that the love of God carries with it the peace that passes intellectual understanding, that is beyond the world and yet embraces it, and we know the whole world speaks about peace and many pray for peace. The leaders of the world negotiate for peace, and it is fair to say that within the human condition, through prayer and meditation, and even in the natural love, peace is a possibility. But that peace can fluctuate along with the human condition. So, if you wish to find this great peace that the Master spoke of, you will find that it is available in communion with God to receive the perfect gift of His love, His joy, and the wisdom of the soul. This soul is transforming in immortality above the earth and yet embracing it.

The fear of the world is embedded in the evolutionary process, which alerts one to danger, physical danger, whether it be a predator or an earth change, an enemy, or simply violence, this visceral reaction is enhanced by the amygdala of the brain which causes the secretion of adrenaline and cortisone which you may know as the *"fight or flight"* response. This, of course, is quite helpful in times of danger, when it is imminent, but the fear that many suffer is not in the now; it is in anxiety about what might come, and though it is real, it has no inherent spiritual value.

So, we encourage deep prayer, deep meditation, going unto God for that peace, real peace that settles in the soul of one who receives the love of God. This, my dear friends, connects you to eternity, immortality, and the bliss that is inherent in a transformed soul. You who are on this path will all receive this transformation in God’s time beyond fear.

So, I say, do not worry. Do not even wonder where you are exactly on this journey, this journey that does not end but truly begins in prayer. Allow your worries and concerns, your doubts, to dissolve in the Father’s love, Who wishes for all His children this blessing, this benediction, this holy communion.

We all embrace you, and we are beyond many. You may call us the celestial body who Christed in the love of God. Your destiny is among us, and so I shall leave you, my dear friends, with my great wish for you to be relieved of any doubt or fear, and for the grace of God to permeate your being and change each soul. May this gift be yours. With all my love and blessings from all of us here, go in peace. I am your brother and friend, in Christ consciousness, the love of God.

I am Yogananda. God bless you.
