# The Pentecost is in The Works You May Accomplish: The Call to Be Disciples of Divine Love

May 24<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come, my beloveds. I come. I continue to tend my flock. I continue to minister to all those who seek the truth and receive the Truth of the Father's Love within their souls. I come to be with you all. On that fateful day when I left this Earth, I did not cease tending my flock. I continued with my ministry as I do today and many souls have benefited from the truth that I brought with my soul's awakening to the Father's Love. Indeed, until you understand and experience this blessing of the great gift of God's Love, you will not truly come to know God in this way, as it was with my disciples, who until that moment of the Pentecost did not truly understand what it was that I was teaching. Yet, at that moment, when the Pentecost touched their hearts with the flames of the Father's Love, the realisation came with the experience, so powerful that it changed everything for them. They were then touched with their ministry and their purpose. They spread out amongst the four corners of the known world at that time and taught this truth with all their hearts and souls. They dedicated their lives to bringing the glad tidings of the rebirth of the soul to others, those who would listen, those who are open to this message.

So it must be with all of you, my beloveds. You have been touched by this gift. You come again and again to drink of the well of the Father's Love. These Living Waters have touched you and changed you. Will you dedicate your lives to bringing this truth to others, a truth that was long lost, a truth that has found a revitalisation amongst you and some others? It is not enough to change the world to truly acknowledge this in your heart. It must be within that effort of bringing the gospel of truth to humanity, to bring that which changes the soul, that which redeems the soul. It is for each of you, my beloveds, to be my disciples, to spread the gospel far and wide. When that dedication is clear and firm within you, you will be guided and protected. All means and ways for you to reach other souls will be given and God's Hand will be upon all that you do, all the efforts that you make. The Pentecost is in the works that you may accomplish, the touching of other souls that is deeply needed in this world. The revelation sits within your hearts. It is there now. There is no need for God to shake the world with the inflowing of His Love into your hearts. The need is for you to bring your power, the glory of your light, what you know to be true, to the world. The power of that will shake the world. The power of the Father's Love and the knowledge thereof will change the world.

Are you willing, my beloveds, to truly step out into the world and say, *"I have been redeemed by the Father's Love and I know what that is. I have felt the power of it. I have been changed by it"*. That message, that witnessing of your own journey and experience, will be enough to change souls and to bring the truth forward. When it is done with angels by your side and God's Hand upon you, you will be a mighty instrument for God. You will bring a wondrous blessing to the world. It is not just I who was God's instrument. It was my disciples and those who follow and so it is. You are my disciples. It is required of you to bring the truth to others. It is needed now as it was then. In fact, it is needed much more, for your world is in jeopardy. Humanity has run amok and created such dreadful conditions, has polluted the world to such a degree that it barely survives the impact of human touch and will upon it.

There must be change, my beloveds, great change. It will come. It is inevitable. As a response to human endeavours, God will correct the error, the darkness and the chaos. Will you be a part of this correction, or will you join in the chaos and continue to feed the darkness? The choice is yours, yet what is different in you compared to so many others is that you know the truth. This carries responsibility. It carries a need for you to act, not only to accept the changes within and express them in your life, but to venture forth guided by God in whatever road that God has designated for you, whatever way God has deemed as your purpose and your gifts and your abilities to help bring this truth to humanity. Each of you is gifted. Each of you is magnificent, the magnificent creation of God. Each of you can carry the flame high, that flame of truth, that wondrous gift that God has given and you have received in measure according to your prayers. Continue to pray as the disciples prayed, with the earnestness of the heart, the desire to be changed, healed and brought up from the darkness into light. You will find yourself in that beautiful grace of God. In this, you will find the ways and means, the strength and the power to be that disciple of truth, to be that beautiful channel of love for our Creator.

My beloveds, we pray now. Open your hearts and souls wide to the Father's Touch, His Holy Spirit flowing, the abundance of which can equal or surpass that of the Pentecost, if you but ask with the earnestness and deep desire of your soul to receive. It is available and it is yours for the taking, if you but ask with all your heart, with all the earnestness that will open the channel wide and bring the blessings. I am praying with you:

> Beloved Father, open us to Your gift. We are Your children truly. We acknowledge this and we wish to be those souls redeemed by Your Love. We wish to be within the flames of this Love that shall purify and transform us as You intend it to be. We thank you, Father, for this gift of Love and we are with You in Your plan and desire to transform the world and bring peace and harmony to it. We are Your instruments. We wish to serve and we are here to receive the benediction of our souls, the truth to live within us, the capacity to love beyond our imaginings, the light so bright it shall cleanse as we walk on the Earth. Bless us, dear Father, for we are Your children and we love You. Amen.
