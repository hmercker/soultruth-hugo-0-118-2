# Gifts Being Given

January 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

Anna received by Elizabeth Mack

I am here. I am your sister, Anna. I live in the Celestial Heavens, not quite as near as where my daughter, Mary, the mother of Jesus, is. I come to you today with bouquets of gifts to be presented to each one. This bouquet of blessings comes directly from God, to me, to you, as you are His true children and as He is the most gracious Heavenly Father of all of us and chooses to gift each one of us every opportunity that we are open to receive from Him. He sent me to present these gifts to you.

Each one is unique, for each is a unique soul. Over your Earth time, you will see these gifts develop and probably be surprised when you recognise what they are and, hopefully, remember from whom they came. He is a most gracious, giving, loving Father and loves each one of you so dearly. He still presents gifts to each of us in the Celestial Heavens. The more we grow in this Love, the more He gifts us opportunities to help those of you upon the Earth. It is a gift given to many of us, for it is important to bring forward those souls who still do not know, do not understand and cannot feel the presence of God in their lives.

The Father sends us to His true children in the flesh upon the Earth, to give you those gifts that will help in attracting those souls who are unaware of His Love for them. As you attract these souls, know that you are being blessed with opportunities to share your love with them and to teach them about the Love of God and how to receive it. How joyous your souls are each time you are given this opportunity and you perform that service for God. You see, everyone benefits, everyone. Such is the nature of God's Divine Love. When expressed, everyone benefits. Is He not the most wonderful, gracious, giving Father? You will be receiving many opportunities in most unusual ways to help your brothers and sisters along the path. Remember, it is service to God that benefits everyone, yourselves included.

I am your sister, Anna. I thank you for being receptive to these gifts given so liberally to God's dear children. God bless you. God bless you.
