# The Door to God’s Love is Open to You

May 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Jesus. I come. Yes, I have been with this instrument. I continue to work with him and through him, as I do with many, we do not waste our moments in idleness. We are always active within the flow of God's Will, or we are in prayer with God. No moment is wasted, for the needs of the Earth and the needs of the spirit world are great. We must toil away and spend our time in service for humanity, whether they have a material body or a spirit body, the longings of the soul are the same. The needs of the soul are the same. We tend to those needs. We are God's angels who do God's bidding. We do so in joy. We do so in love. We do so because the world suffers and God does not wish for suffering to be a part of this world. God wishes for harmony, peace and joy, for all souls to thrive in love.

Thus, we do our ministrations. We have worked with many. We are engaged in a great pursuit to eradicate the darkness of your world and the spirit world, to truly bring that which is intended, that God's plan may move forward and find its expression. How is this done, my beloveds? It is done through each one of you. It is done through each one of us and for every willing mortal and spirit who are willing to do God's Will through them. I urge you to act in faith, to ask in prayer to be God's instruments in the world. The labour is great, but the labourers are few. I ask you, I beseech you, to take up the mantle of service, to understand God's will, which can be understood through the faculties of your soul and as you grow in the Father's Love, that understanding grows as well. You come to understand what it is that God wishes of you. God created you. He created you with a unique set of skills, blessings, gifts and perceptions, all that can be utilised to bring love and harmony to your lives and to the lives of others, your capacity to be creative, to hear the Will of God, to put your will and God's Will together into an amalgam of service, of manifestation and of light in the world. There is still much within you, my brothers and sisters, that you do not understand and you do not perceive. Yet, as you grow in the Father's Love, so you will come to know who you are and the magnificence that is your creation. This will empower you further to do this work.

I ask you, my brothers and sisters, my brethren, my disciples, carry the work forward and bring greater light to the world. Be God's instruments, channels of love and light. You will have a fulfilling life. When your time to come into the world of spirit comes, you will look back with great joy and satisfaction that you did not shirk the opportunity, the invitation to serve, to be a channel of love, that you walked in the light and served God in all the ways that were possible. In this, your reward will come, a place within the spheres of light will be yours and, in time, entry into the Celestial Kingdom. All effort made for light, love and truth is rewarded. Blessings come to you as blessings come through you. Remember this. If your life feels hollow and unfulfilled, then take my words to heart and practise the instrumentality and flow of love through you, for God will utilise you. If you so desire this, God will place before you the opportunities that will open the doorways to these blessings for you and for others.

Much awaits each one of you. Great potential is there, my beloveds. Listen to the heartbeat of your soul. Understand the power of love to heal, to create, to disperse the darkness, to comfort and to bring truth. So much is given to you, my beloveds. So much. In turn, I ask you to give to others, for there is great need in the world, as you well know, and it is your instrumentality that may be used to feed the hungering souls, to bring greater light to the world, to be used as we are used to do the Will of God and to answer prayers and bring light, bring truth and beauty into the world. Use your gifts, your creativity, your vision, all that you possess to help bring the world into greater light and truth.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come once again in hopes of inspiring, in hopes that you will hear this message, in hope that you will know God in ways that are deeper and wider than they are at this moment, but this comes with the blessings of God. This comes with your prayers and your desires. May you be blessed, my beloveds. We are here together in this Circle of Light, uplifted into the Hall of Peace. Breathe in these blessings. Feel that peace permeating you, expanding out from you. Know that God's Love is ever ready to enter into your soul in accordance to your prayers. God bless you, my beloveds. God bless you. My love is with you always.
