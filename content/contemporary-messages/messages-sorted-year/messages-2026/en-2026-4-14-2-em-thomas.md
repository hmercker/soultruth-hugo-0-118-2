# Breathe in This Love from God

April 14<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Thomas received by Elizabeth Mack

I am with you now. I am Thomas, formerly known as Doubting Thomas. I come to speak with you about love, the truest Love that there is, the Love that is growing in each one of your souls. Some of you feel it. For some of you, it is just beginning. Over the course of your lives, it will continue to grow, actively filling every part of you and helping you to feel more and more that love that comes from God, more and more, bringing you closer, bringing you more truth that can help you live a life dedicated to loving God, to being God's channels on this Earth, to serving your brothers and sisters on behalf of God, who desires to bring His Love to each of His children, each of His children upon this Earth, as well as those who are yet to come.

You are instruments for God. That is why you will be receiving so much more of God's Essence in your souls. Unique though each of you are, you will be bound together by this Love, bound together with all of the Divine Love souls who exist in this universe, in the Celestial Heavens, in every part of God's creation and connected to God. What a blessing. What a blessing you have. An experience of such Love, of such magnitude, is given to you because you ask. You ask and this is given to you, these opportunities and these challenges to overcome those things which are impeding your progress, impeding your at-onement with your Creator. You are given everything because God's Love for you is beyond comprehension.

Accept as much Love as possible and you will progress in relation to your willingness to be open and accepting of all that God desires to give to you. You are not giving up anything except those things that are harmful to you. You are gaining everything, everything beyond your wildest imaginings. Allow. Allow the Love to come in greater and greater abundance. Allow the blessings to flow into your lives, into your souls, into your beings and into your experiences. No expectations, dear children, no doubts, just open, wide open. Here, now, the Breath of God is upon you. He breathes deeply into your soul. Allow it, welcome it and embrace it as it transforms.

God bless you. I am your brother, Thomas. The Love is all around you, in you, of you. Breathe it in. God bless you, beautiful souls, beautiful children of God.
