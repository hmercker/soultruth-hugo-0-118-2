# Alec Gaunt Describes His Ministry on Earth and Encourages Us to be Bold In Service

October 27<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Alec Gaunt received by Al Fike

Thank you, dear daughter (JC). A beautiful prayer. I am Alec Gaunt. It was not that long ago that I walked the Earth, but like you, I was earnest with my desires to bring the Truth of God's Love to as many who would listen. I was not afraid to speak. I spoke to many and I was inspired in my speaking and sharing with others because I had faith and I had desire to bring this Truth to as many individuals that I could. In this, I connected and drew in many souls.

In the years that I worked on this Earth, I can say that I brought much in the way of truth to a number of people. This was not because I was meek and gentle, for I had a strong personality. I still do. Rather, I was insistent and persistent. I walked in the world seeking opportunities to bring this truth to others. God provided many opportunities to do so. If I did not have desire, if I was not willing, if I was not insistent, then how far do you think I would have gotten in my ministry? Not very far, my friends. For it takes that intention, that strength, that clarity and that love to work as God's instruments in a world such as this.

In the end, I had many Circles of Light. I had many individuals who were curious. Indeed, what often brought them in was a curiosity around spirit, mediumship and psychic phenomena. Yet, that was only the beginning. That was only the way of opening the door. As that door opened, as people came in with their curiosity and often with their skepticism, my angel friends used me in ways that helped them to see, to demonstrate the truth, and not only me, but those who were gathered around me and around my ministry.

You see, God will utilise whatever gifts you have. If you are sincere, if you wish to serve and if you are a channel of love in the world, no matter what your personality is like, who you are as an individual, God will utilise these elements within you. God will be able to use you because you are an open channel, an instrument who is willing to step forth into the world, into the fray which is the human condition and speak the truth. The truth will bring its reaction. Some will accept and some will reject. Yet, what do you have to lose, my friends? I lost nothing and gained a great deal. Those who rejected me, those who had negative judgements about me, what did I care? They were obviously not my friends. They often were instruments of negative forces to discredit and to bring greater darkness into the world. Not that they willingly and wittingly did so, for often this is done with ignorance. Yet, I had compassion for them. I have compassion for everyone on this Earth, this place so full of confusion, error and lack of love.

God needs those who are willing to step forth, willing to say, *"Yes, beloved Father, beloved Creator, I will serve. I will bring whatever I can, whatever I am able, whatever You place through me as Your channel to bring truth and light into the world."* With that dedication, my beloved friends, with that intention and desire, many things might happen in your life, totally unexpected, totally unplanned, but completely within the flow of God's Will and plan. This is how it works, my friends. It is not you anticipating, you planning, you worrying about the reactions. No, it is not that at all. It is you stepping forward without guile, without protection and being a humble servant of God. This will ruffle feathers. This will stir the pot. Yet, better the feathers are ruffled and the pot is stirred than the acquiescence and the somnambulance of a world asleep and ignorant.

Sometimes stirring and causing reaction is the only way. If you are faithful enough, strong enough and you work as God's instruments and are inspired by the angels as to what to say and what to do, then are you responsible for the kerfuffle? No. It is their reaction and their condition. Indeed, though a number may react in ways that you would not prefer, there are a few that would and will open themselves to the message you have to give. Thus, you gather a few more of God's children into His Bosom, pointing the way, pointing how God will love, heal, transform, guide, inspire and bring joy and truth to the individual. Are these things not worth fighting for, my friends, not worth standing up for? Yes, they are. They always have been and there are few willing. Yet, I say to you, my friends, each of you has the capacity and the ability. No matter how meek and mild you may see yourself, within is a soul that is strong, is a heart that is big, are many blessings and gifts that are just waiting to be explored and expedited by God's Touch of Love.

Think about these things, my friends. Think about how you might make a difference in another individual's life, how you may give with love and be truly that channel of love and truth in the world. You only need to pray and ask to be used as an instrument and do so with sincerity. The whole world will open up to you, my friends. You will do things you never imagined you would do. You will meet people you never imagined you would meet. You will make a difference in the world, not through your own doings, your own mindfulness, but through the humility and grace that comes with God's Love entering into your soul and the results of that Love being expressed through your being. It is a great adventure, a beautiful journey and indeed never a dull moment when you walk with God.

May you be blessed, my friends, in many ways, through many ways and means. May you serve our Heavenly Father and be that true channel that He desires all of us to be. God bless you. I am Alec and my love is with you, each one of you. I come to help, to uplift and I will help if you ask. Ask for my presence. I will give you my strength, my clarity. You will see how God provides all the ways and means that you need in order to be His instrument in the world. God bless you all in His Love. God bless you.
