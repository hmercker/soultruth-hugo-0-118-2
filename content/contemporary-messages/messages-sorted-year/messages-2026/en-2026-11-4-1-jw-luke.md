# Peace

November 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Luke received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, your brother and friend and guide, Luke. Perhaps you have heard the expression peace comes on the wings of a dove.  And you may know that the dove symbolizes bringing that peace from the heavenly father into the heart and souls of those who pray as His holy spirit brings His love.

So as you pray to receive this wonderful gift, the gift of gifts, you are changed.  Your anger becomes acceptance and empathy.  Where you have been disheartened, you feel hope.  For eternity’s hand is upon you in the love of God.

In each moment lies the possibility of resting in the will of God, in the bubble of his love and the safety and protection of the celestial realm. So do not be afraid to step forth.  And when you have doubt, go deep that His love may bring faith, real faith into your soul, knowing that all things in God are good and possible.

You have been told of your gifts and encouraged to use them.  Live them and walk in grace. Allow the places where you are wounded to be healed in the balm of God’s love.  Allow your worries to dissolve as we rest in the peace that comes.

There is a blessing upon this place.  And it is easy to come into this welcoming space.  Let this be a day of joy, of moving forward, of simply resting in your souls, in the love of God.

My love is with you. And we are all embracing you in the love of God. I am Luke.  God bless you.
