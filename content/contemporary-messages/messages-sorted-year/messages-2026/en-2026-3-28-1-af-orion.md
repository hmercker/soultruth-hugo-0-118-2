# The Nature of Reality

March 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. Again I come to speak to you and I thank you for the opportunity to do so. I wish to speak about the nature of reality. There is much talk about the shifting of reality on your planet. Indeed, many believe that that shift will come with more intellectual knowledge and mindful pursuits, that the material mind is the source of originating your perceptions and experiences in the world.

Yet, as we have taught, the true reality exists within the reality of the soul, the very core of the individual. The mind can shape reality and certainly it influences very directly the perceptions that you have with your five senses and what you make of those perceptions is the result of mental reckonings. Yet, the reality shift that I speak of is the one where consciousness shifts from this somewhat rudimentary ability to perceive and to experience life into something deeper and more complex. That complexity is buried deep within the spirit and soul mind. When the material, spirit and soul mind come together in harmony and alignment, then the true ability to see what is real can be accomplished.

We encourage you to strengthen your soul, to expand your soul in the great gift of God's Love. For without this expansion, the strength of the soul, then the ability to align these realities together into one single reality cannot truly take place. A form of soul consciousness can be attained even for those who travel what you call the natural love path, but true, deep and clear soul consciousness comes from a soul that has been awakened and transformed. When that awakening is accomplished, then the ability to see clearly, to understand, to perceive, to know, comes with this transformation. We begin at the beginning, your true self. We work upwards into spirit reality and then to material consciousness, although in your world it happens the other way around, with material consciousness being predominant. Thus the perceptions of reality are highly distorted and inaccurate, and I would say illusional, in its quality of knowing and perceiving the truth.

You must start to nurture and acknowledge the reality that comes with the soul that has been awakened and transformed. In that reality, the many layers of consciousness, of perception, of what has been created in the universe may be perceived and understood. The material mind cannot possibly do this. It does not possess the faculties to do this. It is only the soul mind that has these faculties. They are enhanced and supported by the spirit mind. All the material mind has to do is to accept, to be open and receptive to the reality, the perceptions, experiences of the soul.

This can be done, even though your world is so contrary to supporting this form of reality, of expression of life. It can be done with the help of your friends, with your souls awakening and strengthening in Love and the peeling away of the scales of your eyes, the eyes of your soul, so that you may see clearly. You must be strong enough, faithful enough, trusting enough to allow this process to take place. So far, you are midway along in the level of trust and faith that is required.

You are inching forward toward the school, but you are not there yet, none of you. For each of you has a very strong material mind, will and desire to be in control and powerful. It takes a great depth of humility, that grace of the soul, that faith that comes with true connection with the Creator. All of these things combined will allow the portal of experience, perception and understanding to come to your conscious self.

What brings this portal about is love. The power of love transforms. The power of love brings strength, trust and faith. The power of this Divine Love is the greatest power of all. All those planets in the universe that are of light, great light, have utilised this power to make it so. They understand the workings of the universe. They understand how they may obtain truth in all its purity, love in all its greatness, strength in all its flexibility and ability to accept. You, my beloved souls, are on the journey toward this. You continue to dedicate yourself to this each and every day. Yet, remember that the consciousness of soul is far different from that of the material mind and that, as long as you support and reinforce material mind reality, then you will have great difficulty accepting that of the soul.

I urge you to dive deep within the soul, to seek out that reality, even if it is momentary. Each time you go to that place, each time you accept that reality, you strengthen it and you make it easier for the next time and the next time. In that way, you forge that reality into your consciousness, one step at a time, one prayer, one contemplation, meditation, one thought, one desire and one feeling. Engage all of these things in your pursuit of the soul and your pursuit of God. Inevitably, your reality will change. It must, because the reality that humanity subscribes to is false. It is not a true reality. It is a construct of the collective minds of humanity. Indeed, because of your energetic conditions, that reality becomes solidified and your scientists believe this is the only way and the only expression of reality, but this is not true. Reality can shift according to consciousness.

The reality that you are experiencing now is somewhere in between the two. Therefore, you often feel like you do not fit in either world or with the common thread of humanity. This is understandable because your shift in consciousness, in reality, is continuing. It is ongoing. It is in flux. You must trust in the process and allow yourself to drift deeper into soul consciousness and its particular reality. As you do so, you will be able to teach others how to make that travel, that journey toward soul consciousness. You will truly be an example of it. This is coming, my friends. Your reality is shifting and so shall you accept, absorb, integrate and be joyful with this new reality that emerges.

My beautiful friends, there is much yet to learn, to know, to experience, but you are willing and receptive to this and so you draw many close to you who are helping you upon that journey. God's Hand is certainly upon you. The grace of His Love burns within you. You are equipped to make that journey, to make that shift, to transgress from this physical reality that humanity calls truth to the reality that is of the soul and Divine consciousness. This is the true reality, the most potent reality, the one that shall lead you further along the road and create for you true understanding, wisdom and ability to love and accept that is needed. It is needed. It is sorrowfully needed within each one of you and within everyone on your planet. It is coming. It will come. It is inevitable. This is part of God's plan and desire for this planet and so we have been called by Divine intention to be with you, to help you upon that road.

Thus, we shall, my friends, so we shall. May you find truth in all things. May your reality be that that is the truth. May this reality be your cornerstone, that which guides you in light. May love overshadow all things and turn all things into harmony, grace and peace. God bless you, my friends. Orion loves you and I am close. God bless you all.
