# The FUSC is a Refuge of Light

May 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026

First United Spiritualist Church, Burnaby, BC, Canada

Percy Hayes received by Al Fike

Beloved souls, every time you enter this church, this place and seek to be uplifted, it comes and touches you and you are less burdened when you leave. May you continue to avail yourselves of what is here, for much effort is made by we in spirit to create the conditions of healing, light and love, that you may benefit greatly from it. The world is a dark place, but this, my beloved friends, is a refuge of light.

God bless you. I am Percy. I once spoke here and I am glad that you are all here together. I am now an inhabitant of the Celestial Kingdom, which is a wondrous place indeed. God bless you all, my friends. God bless you all.
