# Acknowledges Fathers

June 21<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Starpoint Circle of Light, Christ Church, Barbados

Mary received by Maureen Cardoso

God bless you. It is Mary. It is love, it is love expressed, that produces attributes or qualities of the soul. As the Divine Love comes into your soul, there is a great transformation, and the path of progression changes you in many ways. When there is separation or division where love is absent, you feel that. You experience it. How does one move from this separation back into the realms of love? You must go within, connect to God and recognise that you are a soul progressing in the wonderment of God's wisdom.

Let love prevail in all that you do, in the ways of expression, in your actions and in your thoughts. Love resides within the soul. A mind, whether the material or spiritual mind, that is in harmony with the soul, will have loving thoughts, will display loving actions and will see with compassion. The heart of man in its transformation goes from a protective state to one that is open, vulnerable, willing to be shown, willing to see and willing to be guided. As the mind allows itself to be educated through the experiences of the soul, a great light comes to the mind and can project thoughts of love and peace.

The initial blessing that was arranged to come through in your Circle of Light, this special day that, in your particular area of the world, honours the Father, the fathers of mankind, the qualities and the beauty of fatherhood, the role of demonstration and guidance that it gives to its charges, those souls that God has gifted for them to father over, I come to recognise and to show my love and appreciation for your growing capacities to be a demonstration of love, to represent the Essence of God, not only in your home circle, but eventually you can come to see how the qualities of fatherhood can be shared with other youngsters that are growing in their journey on Earth.

You have a special role that has been created to move through you. We honour this in each one and we pray for the opening of your beautiful hearts, allowing the true sensitivities that you carry to awaken within you and that demonstration, through its awakening, can show others what love is, how it feels, how it lives and how it moves through you.

This simple message of acknowledging the fathers comes from us. For you are growing in your capacities to love and be a demonstration of this. Let us honour you. Receive this. Receive this Love from God, who honours you in the roles that you have been given and that you are all growing in your capacity to walk in. Love is the way. As you grow to understand what love truly is, as your soul develops in Divine Love, your road of progression has greater clarity and certainty, has wisdom and humility, all aspects of love, all faculties of a progressing soul. We stand with you to pray with you as you open your souls to that great inflowing of Divine Love and reap the many blessings of this Love.

I am Mary. My love is with each one of you. May you continue to soar in your eternal journey of at-onement with God. God bless you.
