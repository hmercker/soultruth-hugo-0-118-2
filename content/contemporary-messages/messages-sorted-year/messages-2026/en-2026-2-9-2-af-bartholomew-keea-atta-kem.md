# Faith and the Soul 2: You Must Trust in Yourself, Your Soul and God

February 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Barbados Manifesto of Light, Christchurch, Barbados

Bartholomew & Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

**Bartholomew:** Greetings to you, my friends, I am Bartholomew. There is much work ahead for each one of you. Much to do, my friends, for a world that is hungering for truth and yet does not know that it is hungry. You must help to awaken and remember that any movement in the world, and this is truly a movement for the reclamation of humanity, starts off small with a few dedicated souls and grows from there. The power and strength of those souls who begin the movement, the strength of their commitment and the power of their faith, is what will ignite the spread of this truth, the simple Truth of God’s Love and what it can do for humanity.

I encourage you to continue to be dedicated in your prayers and your efforts, to be that bright light in the world, to be true to what God has intended for each one of you, to not give up hope, but to fuel the flames of hope within your consciousness. That fuel is faith, having faith in God’s guidance, having faith in the power of His Love to transform you, having faith in your brothers and sisters and having faith in God’s plan. If you can carry that level of faith, that expression of faith, to a high level within your consciousness, making it the focus and the premier thought within you, then you are well on your way to fulfil your part in this unfolding of truth and the efforts being made in many different ways, many different levels, many different spirits, angels, stellar friends, so very many who are dedicated, such as you are, but even greater toward this goal that stands before us, this goal that God has imbued within all of us, including ourselves.

It is a goal to reach out to your brothers and sisters and say, *“There is a way forward, despite all the turmoil, despite all the confusion and the darkness and the pain, there is a way forward.”* You must be strong enough in this truth, expressions of love and wisdom, generosity of spirit, the joy and the truth that you have the capacity to reach another soul, not merely in words, but in what you emanate, who you are, the channels that you are. Do not underestimate the power of God working through you. That is the greatest power of all. Even though the individual you may be facing at the time is sceptical and reticent, you will have planted a seed that, in time, will grow. As the Earth goes through its convulsions and turmoil, those who are seeking comfort will remember your words. for God will send His angels to inspire them in that memory and then they will begin to see, to realise. This is our only hope. The hope of the world lies within the soul of humanity, not their minds, not the material, but in the soul. When the soul is ignited with desire, curiosity and even desperation, then God is there always to reach out to that soul and bring comfort and love, to bring peace and to bring truth. Your work continues. It may seem modest and you wonder what impact you are having in the world. This is understandable, since most of your reconciling of experience comes from the five senses in the mind. These ways of understanding are very limited indeed. You do not see with the eyes of your soul as of yet, but you will and you will understand the dynamics, that undercurrent of truth that you are bringing with you wherever you go, as God’s instruments and channels of love.

On that level, that place and way of connecting soul to soul, you are having profound impacts on others. This will show up in time. In times of need, there will be an awakening of this truth within many, many you have made contact with, many that God has sent to you in all the ways and means that God guides and awakens. Yes, there is much work to do, my friends, many, many more souls to connect with. The greater challenge of this time is your own faith in yourself, your own trust in your soul’s knowings and the guidance that comes. You need further awakening, my brothers and sisters, further exercise of faith, further trust in yourself and in the power of God’s work through you.

Truly coming to know the faculties and perceptions of your soul is of the utmost importance of this at this time. I urge you to pray to God to help you to awaken to these things. I urge you to come closer to your soul that you may know these things and also strengthen your faith. For when you know your soul, faith comes easy. It is not the struggles of the mind in deduction and analysis. It is the feelings of the soul in its knowings, something that at this time you may feel are contrary avenues of understanding, but they are not, my friends. Both work in harmony if they are allowed to work in harmony. Each aspect of your being, each part of your consciousness, can work together to put the picture toward clarity, the lens of perception adjusted and clarified to bring you that perfect and crisp picture of what is true. You must exercise your faith and your abilities to help focus that lens, to help bring that clarity forward and to trust in it.

When you jump back and forth between the soul perceptions and the mental perceptions, then you are only part way there in the process of awakening and integrating both aspects of perception, but it is a start. I urge you again to pray and ask to clarify, to help you to feel comfortable, to feel confident of what it is you are experiencing and to ask the angels to explain and help you to understand this process, this experience. From there, new ways of perceiving, new ways of understanding will become evident.

First, this marriage between the perceptions of the mind and the perception of the soul must come about. This is the first step, but other steps will take place and deeper awarenesses will come. I would ask this instrument to elaborate on this in his own experience of life and his own journey of Divine Love, for he has had a number of experiences and realised and has trusted those perceptions that have come. As you are well aware it is a process. It requires your dedication and focus. When you put your focus primarily on the material things of life, you make it very difficult to go deeper and to integrate in any significant way the feelings, the perceptions, the visions and the awarenesses that come in the ways that I describe.

It is time, however, my beautiful friends, to focus on these things, to put your time and effort into this if you are to be an effective instrument in the world in the coming times that will require this way of being and perceiving, so that you may uplift and help many souls who will be in terror, anguish and confusion. If you are to be the rock that has been described, then you must have the solidity of a rock, the surety of a rock, the presence of a rock, not in the material sense, of course, but in the spiritual sense, that power, that clarity, that faith, that understanding of truth and, most of all, the power of the Love to help convey to others a sense of comfort, a sense that they may trust you and that they may listen to you, that they too may understand the power of God’s Love to protect, to cleanse, to bring peace, to bring God and to bring love into their lives.

This is the mission. It is clear and it is simple, but it cannot be truly understood without these faculties awakening and your acceptance of this. The mind has been your companion all your lives, but I say to you that it is time to leave this friend and make some distance between you and this place that you have put so much trust and ability into. Not to make it a distant friendship, but to have enough distance that you may be comfortable with that other newfound friend, which is the soul. Explore that friendship, test it, wonder about it, pray about it, have confidence in it and trust it. In time, the friendship you will make with your soul will be far more fulfilling and rewarding than that which you have had with your mind. When that is done, that friendship solidified, then you may bring the mind closer again, once again, and the two of them together may know each other in a way that they trust and feel comfortable. Then, the true understanding and work will begin through you and there will be that beautiful intermingling of consciousness that is so needed and required for the work ahead.

I urge you forward, my beautiful friends. I urge you upon the journey of your awakening, the journey of the friendship between the soul and the mind, the journey of being God’s instrument, a clear and beautiful channel for God, for love and for humanity. May you be blessed on that journey and find yourself in places and experiences you could not fathom within your mind, but are indeed a reality of the soul. Allow yourself to drift into your soul and truly understand what resides within you. It is a truly beautiful place, a place that you will grow to appreciate more and more as you journey closer and closer to that reality. It is a reflection of God within you, a reflection that, as you receive more of His Love, becomes a solid and viable entity, life renewed within you. It is the most wondrous journey you could ever take and it is laid before you at your feet, waiting for you to say, *“Yes, I am willing. Yes, I want this. I trust in this. I desire this.”*

God bless you upon that journey, my friends, my love is with you. Know that we are close and we will assist you on that journey, for we know that journey well and we will show you the guide posts and the markers on the way. God bless you. God bless you.

**Keea Atta Kem:** Yes, the gentle Touch of God is with you. This will assist you upon that journey in which Bartholomew speaks. Remember this, that the key to all of these things is God’s Love and that as God gently guides you forward, the awakening of the soul comes in all its beauty and its gentleness and its light and upliftment. The soul weeps with joy when it comes to know God once again, but in a different way, the way of His Love, the way that heals and awakens, uplifts and transforms. This is the true mercy and beauty of God’s Touch upon every soul. If they are willing to accept it, it will come in all its ways to transform and to awaken you.

Be true to His Love. All else will come in this way, in the way of light, in the way of Love, in the way of God. God bless you, my friends. Keea loves you. God bless you.
