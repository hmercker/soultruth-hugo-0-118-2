# After this Period of Respite, It is Time for the Work to Begin

September 10<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Bassingham Retreat, Bassingham, UK

Michael Collier & Jesus received by Al Fike

**Michael Collier:** The work continues, my friends. I am Michael Collier. You have had the luxury of this respite, the generosity of your dear host and all the goodness this land has to offer. You have felt refreshed. Now, it is time to work, to apply yourselves, apply your gifts, apply your knowledge and your love for those needy souls you are about to encounter. I urge you to be prayerful, to consider what lies before you and to see these opportunities as great blessings. I know that you do.

Application of building light is important. Do not shirk your responsibilities but each moment consider what God is asking you to do, the doors and portals that are being opened for you, the possibilities being presented. These are gifts, beautiful blessings for each one of you. Even those who are not in the flesh, working in these environs, I suggest that you pray in support. Even those across the waters who may hear this message, pray for these dear intrepid souls that are willing to step forward, speak, heal and bring blessings, channels of love. Pray for them. Pray for the upliftment. For where they go needs much upliftment and indeed we will be there in great numbers.

Yet, in all of these things, these elements put into place by God require your prayers, require the infusion of your light, your faith and your love to help bridge the gap between the spirit life and the material life. It is important that your focus and energies are put upon these things or without them, without your focus, without the exercise of your faith and your efforts, things will fall flat. There will not be the vitality of spirit, the vitality of love and light that is required to touch deeply the souls that will attend these circles to come. All of these opportunities are tests. All bring their specific challenges. All bring their specific rewards.

I say these things to encourage you, to not cause concern but to encourage you toward the focus of what is to come. For these times are important, they are precious and they are rare. We wish for you to be very diligent and honour what is being given as a gift to all of you to help serve God, to help bring truth into the world and as I have said, even though the numbers that you speak to are small in comparison to the numbers in the spirit world, remember, you are making a very profound impact as you go and there will be a rippling effect out into the spirit world and into the material world as you bring these blessings forward, as you are God's instruments in the world. Take up the mantle and do so with earnestness and there will be many blessings, not only given to you but to those around you and to that area in which you will inhabit for a brief time.

May God bless you all in your efforts, in your times together, in your words, in your actions, in your prayers and in your instrumentality, everything blessed by God, His Hand upon all that you do. God bless you. I am Michael Collier and my love is with you. I will be close. I will certainly speak again. God bless you all.

**Jesus:** I come to bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend. I am Jesus. I come to congratulate you on your efforts thus far. You are reaching to God, absorbing what you might within your souls, seeking truth, seeking all that is a blessing. I have been close, helping to uplift, helping you and your insights, understandings and loving you, beloved souls.

You have been blessed mightily and you carry on with the work as my disciples, as God's emissaries of truth and light and love. We will touch many souls. We will indeed work together in tandem with God's Will. We are blessed, are we not, with all the blessings, all the upliftment and light, protection, love and even material comforts, that you may be at ease in your work and it may flow in harmony. God bless you, my beloveds. My love is with you.

My love is with you. My love is with you. God bless you. Upon the journey of service and soul awakening and transformation, it comes in great measure. It comes as you walk forward in the light. It comes as God's Will is enacted through you. It comes in service and many blessings as God utilizes you as His instruments in these days and the coming days, the power of which will help affect the transformation of this world from darkness to light.

God bless you, beloveds. God bless you. In His Love, we all have our life, our blessings, meanings and purpose. God bless you.
