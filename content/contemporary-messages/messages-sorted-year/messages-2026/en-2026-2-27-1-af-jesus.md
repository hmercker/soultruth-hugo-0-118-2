# Agents of Light: Walking in the Grace of God

February 27<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

May the joy of His Love be yours, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to pray with you and to pray for you. I come because with your desires and prayers, as you carry the banner of Divine Love in your life, by the very Law of Attraction, you have drawn me into your midst. We are together in this great effort and prayer to bring light into your souls and bring light into your world. As you pray for this gift, I come close and I am truly with you.

My beloveds, I have said, and you know this saying, “Seek ye the kingdom and all things shall come unto you.” It is a truth. The kingdom is the Celestial Kingdom. In order to seek it, you know that you must imbibe with God and receive His Love into your souls. We come together to affirm this Truth, to be in the grace of God's Touch, to know His Love flowing into us. Open wide, my beloveds, open wide to this Touch. Feel the grace of His Touch upon you. Feel the Holy Spirit awakening your soul with this holy gift.

We are in the thrall of His Touch, the peace that passes all understanding, the awakening that comes as we open ourselves in this Circle of Light. I bring many blessings to you as do your beautiful Celestial guides and gatekeepers. Together, we bring great light and great blessings, not only to you, but through you, that you may know the Celestial Kingdom in time as your souls are transformed by this gift, as your awakening comes and your consciousness is that of God and the great Soul of God and that consciousness comes within your soul, that reflection of God. As the scales are peeled from your eyes, as the joy of His Love wells up from your soul, as you are uplifted by the angels, as you enter the Great Hall of Peace, may we come to realise that this blessing, this effort, this prayer and all prayers and efforts that are focused upon the gift that God has to give, will reap its rewards and benefits, not only in heaven, but on Earth.

Many will benefit from your prayers. Many will benefit because you have prayed. Many who are connected to you, your families, your loved ones, your friends, your neighbours, all are benefiting from this prayer. It is going out through the great Lattice of Light, touching many who are connected to it. As you pray, that lattice is being invigorated and blessed by your prayers. The lattice is far reaching. There are many who are connected. There are many in the world who long for this. I urge you to share this truth and at the very least to pray for these individuals who are seeking redemption, healing, relief from their pain, suffering and lack of love. You are agents of peace, my beloveds. You are agents of light and truth, channels of love. We are together in this great effort to awaken and to awaken humanity. Walk with me, my beloveds. Walk in the grace of God. You will know the joy, the abundance of love, the awakening of your soul and its transformation in Love. All is given freely by your mere desire, your earnestness in prayer. The gifts are given, the blessings abundant.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Kingdom. I am your brother and your friend. I love you, each of you. I know each one of you well, very well indeed. You truly are my brethren, that family of love that grows and sustains itself with God's blessings. God bless you and keep you in this Light. My love is with you always. God bless you.
