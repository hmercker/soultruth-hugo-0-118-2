Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Blessings to you, my friends. It is Orion once again. There are many being connected to you, beloved souls. We utilise the Lattice of Light to connect you with others around the world.

Indeed, none are aware of that connection in a conscious way, but soul to soul, there is indeed an awareness. Indeed, with this interconnectivity, there is support and there is an addition to your efforts. Indeed, you help to add to theirs as we continue in our work to secure the light in the world, to bring about such a powerful way of communicating and working together, soul to soul, that when changes come that are severe, you will be able to rely upon this interconnectivity, the Lattice of Light. Much can be accomplished in this way and is being accomplished as we continue to weave this beautiful web of light through your world, around your world.

For there are many in the world who are seeking, who wish to be purposeful and who have light. It does not necessarily mean that they have your philosophy and your approach, but there is a purpose and plan that God has to help to heal the world and in time, to help reconstruct the world. Your little enclave of light, this beautiful gathering of souls, this Circle of Light, is important. It is a node of light that can be utilised in powerful ways. It is a beautiful opportunity for us and your Celestial friends to make contacts, to bring connections to do our work.

We encourage you to continue with your efforts, your consistency, your desires, your prayers, so that we may continue in our efforts to bring light into the world, to help the world move toward greater light and greater truth. To establish harmony in the world is of the utmost importance at this time. To bring the fullness of spiritual awareness and soul development is crucial. So very few, in a conscious way, do this as you do, but this will grow, my friends.

You will continue to draw in those who are interested, who have a soul desire to awaken and to be a part of a great movement. For this is the beginning of a great movement toward true awareness of soul, toward alignment with God, toward awakening to your soul's potentials and gifts. You, as an example, will, in time, draw in many others. As your light grows, as you continue to be in greater alignment with God, so your guidance and the opportunities will deepen and widen as you continue to travel and be effective in your own wider community. Although time is crucial, for there is little time left, but we work more diligently, do we not?

Your persistence and consistency are important, crucial in these efforts. Continue to seek out God, the great Creator of all the universe, the source of this great Love that is the Essence of God. We will continue to make our rapport and do our work. This is always and evermore our goal. Much of which you are not aware of, the work that we do, the efforts that are being made, but it is not necessary either.

For you, in your innocence, in your ways, in your prayers, have established a foundation that allows us our work and our efforts and thus we continue together. You, with faith and desire, we with knowledge and ability, will continue in our collaborations, our efforts to knit this Lattice of Light around the world, and in time, to encompass this world with a powerful gift, a beautiful capacity to communicate, to work together in harmony, to be able to turn the tide of darkness and bring light. There is much work still to do, beloved friends, and we will continue, will we not?

Beautiful souls of God, beautiful brothers and sisters of all of us in the universe, who desire peace, harmony, who know the way to love and the power of love, we will continue, my beloved friends, until the work is done. We will continue. I am Orion and I love you so, beloved souls. Thank you for your dedication, your service, your intentions, your love. Blessings to all. God bless you.
