# The Influence Our Path Has Upon Our Loved Ones in Spirit

August 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Abbotsford, BC, Canada

Melvina received by Maureen Cardoso

May the blessing of God's Love infill your souls. My name is Melvina. I am a Celestial Angel and I am the maternal grandmother of this instrument. I am so very happy to be able to commune in this way with all of you, where I can share with you the great blessing of God's Love. For we have this in common, all seeking and yearning for God's Love to infill our souls, and that was through observing, from the spirit world, this instrument's journey in Divine Love. We have always had a loving rapport. I could come around her at various times as she walked her path of life, bringing my support. For it was easy for her to recognise me, even before I became a Celestial Angel.

One thing she is always being diligent on is ensuring that the spirit who she communicates with and draws near her is Celestial. Now, does that always mean that she was not drawing other spirits near her? No. Yet, what it allowed me to respect was her journey. This put a great light in my spirit to know that there must have been something very special about being a Celestial Angel. I began to see the light of a loved one or teacher that would be around me. I began to follow them and be taught by them as I traversed through the spheres of the spirit. I atoned for the error that I had in me. It was a journey that, in many ways, I observed from a distance, because I saw the diligence not only in her, but I also see it in many of you that you wish to have around you those spirits who can help to educate and influence you on your path of walking in love with God to the at-onement, your soul and God's Soul.

I am so happy to be able to say that I too have arrived into the Celestial Heavens. I experience the glory, the peace, the wonderment and the joy of knowing our Father in a way like none other. It is a relationship of love that until you achieve such a level of soul development, you really cannot explain it in words. For it is a state of being within your soul. I have been given the opportunity to help my family members on Earth and those who are in the spirit world to come to understand God's Love, the beauty and the simplicity of seeking and receiving, and our transformation from the substance in which we were all created into the Divine Substance.

Each of you, beautiful souls, is growing in this Love and the desire is awakening more and more within you to know that special union between you and God. This is a great blessing to humanity, because you bring great light to the Earth and you allow the light of your soul to bless many. You acknowledge the instrumentality that you have and how you have dedicated it to God. You have given yourself back to God, being a willing servant for God to enact and bless through you what He knows another needs. That is a powerful place to be and acknowledge within yourself. For it is nothing that you need to do, but everything that you need to be in the allowance of this Love and the blessings that God can minister through you.

As you continue to progress in your soul and the unique gifts that God has imbued your soul with awaken, you are recognising your self's expression in a more purposeful way. What is awakening within you is a very deep, grounded and steady relationship with God through your soul. You are blessed in this way because you choose to receive God's Love. You choose to be His instrument. You choose to see through the eyes of love. You choose to speak the words that are uplifting, truthful and supportive. You are becoming angels, Divine Angels.

I want to encourage you on this most extraordinary path, because, as I have recently arrived in the Celestial Heavens, I am very familiar with the efforts, with the commitment, with the sincerity and the genuine desire of the soul. It is the part of you that exists through all eternity, because you have the Divine Love in your soul. Your soul's consciousness is taking new form, layer by layer and awakening by awakening. You will look back on your path and see the extraordinary plan that God had for you. You will look forward, knowing that there is more ahead and you will welcome it with open arms, because there is nothing higher in all the universe. There is nothing more fulfilling, more blessing and more awakening. There is absolutely nothing higher than this Substance. In receiving this Substance, all else comes to you, the awakening of your gifts and the instrumentality that they shall serve, the blessings of the many homes that you shall have in the spirit world. For as you move through the spheres, each time there is a home that you reside in that reflects the glory and the beauty of you.

It is remarkable. It is joyful. It is filled with reverence. For God Almighty has placed His Love in you. You live by the grace of this Love and your soul's expression is a reflection of this Love. Where you are here and now, on your individual paths, that Love is reflecting through you. Yet, there is so much more to come, so much more ahead of you, the light of your soul magnified in ways that you would not even be able to see if you were to look through material eyes. Yet, the eyes of your soul are equipped to see the Light of God's Love and the beauty of God's creation. Take in as much as you can. For in this way, the river of Love moves you along its path with a little more ease, a little more joy and a little more anticipation of greater things to come.

I am Melvina. Thank you for being able to receive my message, allowing me to speak and to communicate through this instrument for my first time. As she prepares for her travels, I have the blessing of guiding her along her way. God bless you all in God's grace. My love is with you. God bless you.
