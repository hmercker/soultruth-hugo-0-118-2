# Mary Describes the Blessing of God’s Touch Upon Her, Jesus Blesses the Group

June 23<sup>rd</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Mary & Jesus received by Al Fike

**Mary:** May the blessings of the Father's Love be yours. I am Mary. I come to bring you peace and comfort. In a world such as this, God brings His blessings to all who are in need and all who suffer. Indeed, you all, in some way or another, suffer from the conditions of this world. I come to uplift you, to love you, to acknowledge you and to tell you that any circumstance, all pain and suffering, shall pass in time, and that as you pray to God to be with Him, to benefit from His blessings, then He will lift from you the burdens. He will bring to you peace. His Hand will rest upon you. Beloved souls, we are all His children. Whether you reside on the earthly plane or in the Celestial Heavens, the prayers are the same, to seek out the Love of God, to be with God in alignment, in peace, in the Light of His Presence. We are His children and we continue to make that journey and drink from the Living Waters, to be close to God and to know the benefits of this closeness, this love that we share, our love for Him and His Love for us.

It is a powerful Truth, my beloveds, one that you must truly understand, that even in the midst of darkness, the Light of God can penetrate, uplift and uphold you if you but ask in prayer, sincere and earnest in your prayers. Those prayers will be answered. I have had my share of suffering on Earth. I had to witness the death of my son and all the things that came with that. Yet, I had faith. I prayed to God. I was given great comfort indeed. God showed me how my son did not suffer as he ascended into the Celestial Heavens. It gave me great comfort to know this and to know that someday I too would make that journey. Indeed, I did in time. I lived a life of prayer and faith while I was on Earth and God touched my life deeply. There were manifestations of His Touch. I saw my son grow into a great spiritual leader, the result of God's Touch. I saw my family benefit from my prayers and God's response. I saw the disciples of Jesus walk on the Earth and add to the lives of many the Truth that Jesus taught, the Truth of God's Love.

Indeed, God works in many ways. God uses many instruments. Each of you has the potential to be a powerful instrument, a disciple of the Master, a beautiful light in the world. It is all accomplished through faith and prayer, through asking and receiving, through acting and being guided on the path of love. All these things come with one simple choice to be with God, to be His child, to acknowledge this relationship, this beautiful bond that you share, where you are God's creations. God has not abandoned you. God does not leave you to your own devices. God will assist you, help you, protect you and guide you. With His Love burning in your souls, there is all possibility of blessings, all possibility of light manifest and truth expressed in your life.

Be that child of God, my beloveds. Be true to what you have been given as truth. Be clear on your journey of what is in harmony with God's Laws of Love and what is truly a blessing and a benefit to you through your relationship with God. You will find deep peace and joy within, great insights and experiences that will uplift, inform and give you deep purpose and understanding of that purpose. The meaning of life will grow within you, that you may truly understand the benefits of God's creation and you within that creation.

God bless you, my beloveds. I am Mary. I love you and yes, my son is with you as well, praying with you as always. Many of us pray with you, for you are the centre point of that light that will grow in the world. Many of you will be a part of the great plan that God has for the salvation of humanity. See yourself in that light, my beloveds. Know that you have great potential, gifts and the possibility to be a powerful instrument in the world, a beautiful light in the world. It begins with love. Love is the keystone, the foundation of all that we do and all that you do. With love, God can use you in many ways. Seek out that instrumentality, a channel of love in the world and all will be well, despite its challenges. All will be well, my beloveds. I am with you. I love you. We pray together. God bless you. I am Mary.

**Jesus:** Know that I am with you, my beloveds, I am with you. Bring God's Love into your hearts. In this, you will know me, know the angels of heaven and the Celestial light and love that comes with your prayers. I am with you and I bring my light into your midst. As we ascend into that great Hall of Peace, we are together in the grace of God. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you.
