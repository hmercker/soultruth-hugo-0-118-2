# True Instrumentality Requires Fearlessness and Faith

August 7<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Orion received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Orion. It is surprising to us how well humanity shutters their souls from their consciousness. We see this within you and within all of humanity, the separation between the consciousness of soul and the consciousness of the material mind. This, as I have explained, does not happen where I abide. Our souls and minds, all of us, are integrated into one and this indeed is the goal. You shutter your soul less than many. You continue to honour your souls, honour the capacity and abilities of your souls to be in alignment and in attunement with God and the universe, God's universe.

Yet, often because of the conditions in which you live, that shuttering of the soul is a defence against so many conditions that are contrary and are, in a way, challenging the sensitivities of the soul. Thus, you open yourselves in situations such as this in prayer and, at times, when you move about in the world. Yet, often after these times, the shutter remains intact and you walk in the world not as a soul freed and awakened by this great gift from God, the Divine Love, but as a human being, with all the mental accoutrements and conditions that are human.

Yet, you have been asked to be an example in the world, so we are engaged in a process of strengthening you, helping you to walk in the world as an open soul. God, of course, is the great healer of such things. Indeed, as you continue to receive more of the great gift, you are strengthened by it. What we are engaged in is helping you in your mindful conceits and ideas, to be more open, more flexible, more able to walk in the world without feeling that you are vulnerable, without that sense and desire that others may accept you as you are. So much within you continues to not be expressed in the world. As you continue to step back from such conditions that are of the world, then the example that is necessary to be a teacher, is necessary to bring this truth forward in the world, is lost to your reticence and your fear.

You must be fearless, stepping forth without concern of the reaction that you may get from others, stepping forth in situations that require a light to banish the darkness and the error, stepping forth when you are guided by God and stepping forth as God's instruments. You do so on occasions and, at other times, you either do not hear the guidance given or you reject it, but you must be that nimble and able instrument in the world, willing to say yes to God's Will and the merging of the two into the effectiveness of that instrumentality that does exist within you.

It is a challenge, of course. It only may take those that are strong, those who can bear forth what would invariably be rejections, innuendos and suspicions from others. Yet, when you are in alignment with God and when you have such friends such as ourselves, your angel friends and many more, are you lonely? Are you isolated in the world because of these rejections? No. You have a rich life, a full life, because you have acknowledged your soul and you desire for it to further awaken and be expressed in your life.

I ask you to put aside your reticence, your fear, to be true to who you are and be true to the goals that you have set before you, to be that beautiful soul of light, that channel of love, all the wonderful gifts that exist within you, shining forth for the benefit of your brothers and sisters. Until you are truly willing to do this, can manifestation take place? I think not. I think that when manifestation takes place, you will already be equipped and the shift of consciousness will be attained, so that you might go out into the world fearless and without reticence. Are you able to be that individual? Are you able to put aside those mindful, material conditions that hold you back from this? For it will require an individual who is dedicated to that degree. It will require fearlessness, faith and all that you know God desires of you. God has put within you the capacity to express yourself in these ways.

We continue on our journey, step by step. We inch toward that place of purity and strength, clarity and faith and most of all, love. I merely remind you of the goals that we have set together and the requirements of fulfilling those goals. It is not an easy journey and it is most certainly the road less travelled. Yet, the rewards, my friends, what may come as a result of these dedications and your faith, will help change the world, will most certainly change your world, but it will assist God in this great plan to change the world and to disperse the darkness and the suffering that continues to proliferate in this world of yours.

We continue to work together, my friends. I do not say these things in judgement, but merely as an observation of the challenges that you face and that which lingers within you, not necessarily within your soul, but within your material mind, that obstructs the fulfilment of our efforts. Continue to observe yourself, to come to understand what these obstructions are. For they are unique for each individual and to strive to overcome them. It takes your efforts, dedications, your prayers and your discipline to go beyond these things, these well-worn habits within that are not conducive to fulfilling the goals that we have together.

May you be blessed with a mighty inflowing of this great gift of God's Love. In this, insights may pour into your consciousness, light, joy and release from the shackles of the mind, so that all may be pure, wondrous and beautiful in their expression. God bless you, my friends. I am Orion. I love you dearly and my dedication to you is complete and lifelong. God bless you all, beautiful friends of earth. God bless you all in love.
