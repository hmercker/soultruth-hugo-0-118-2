# The World Needs Your Prayers

March 1<sup>st</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Al Fike

The world is in great need of your prayers, my beloveds. I come. I am Jesus, your brother, your friend. O gentle lambs of God, beautiful souls of light, continue to shine forth and bring your light to the world. This Light is God's Light. This Light comes from your Creator, given to you as a gift, a gift of Love. It is for you to carry this lamp into the world, to walk as His instruments and channels of love, to be truly in the flow of His Will, so that you may help God change the world. As you accept the blessings and changes that come to you as a result of His gift of Love, you are an agent of change in the world. You are that beautiful instrument that God may use to touch many. You allow His Love to flow through you to others, the healing gift given abundantly and purely through each of you.

Oh, my beloveds, seek out the grace of God always. For it is there. Every time you ask with sincerity, it is there and given upon request. Seek to be in the harmony of this Love, that the laws pertaining to this Love may be followed and expressed in your life and in this way, you become that agent of change. For it is an expression of love that you affect the conditions of your world. It is in your prayers and longings that you affect all that is around you as God answers your prayers and changes all that which is out of harmony with you and with God. My beloveds, how loved you are, how truly beautiful each one of you are, how much I love you and know you. We all seek change for this world of yours, to bring greater harmony and light to it. Indeed, the foundation of that change must be your own life lived in prayer, supplication and truth.

How does anything start in your world, but with one individual who seeks to be that ignition point of change? We have many here gathered, many possibilities presented as you seek to be in alignment with your soul to God, as you seek the Truth of His Love within you. The changes that come to you, my beloveds, may be the starting point for many others. I urge you to continue in your endeavours to accept the truth and to live it with every breath. For how does one teach truth but to demonstrate it? How is one an active agent of change, but to move in the world in a way that is guided and in harmony with God's Will? How does one love in the world, but through the influence and the changes and awakenings that come with the Divine Love? This quality of Love is like none other and it shall work through you like nothing else could. You become the active agent of God, the agent of change, the bringer of light, for you have been ignited by this truth, your souls awakened and continuing to be awake on this wonderful journey of soul transformation.

Be true to yourselves, to that light that grows within and all will come to you and through you for your benefit and for the benefit of those around you. You will be those nodes of light that this dear instrument has indicated and you will feed that great Lattice of Light that is so important to help save this world of yours and bring it from darkness and the brink of destruction to that of light and harmony and peace. Be true to God, my beloveds, for God is ever waiting for your prayers and your communications with Him. Each prayer, each utterance that comes from you to God, is heard and responded to. Continue to walk this path, my beloveds, and to share this truth with others in whatever way that you can and whatever way you are guided to do so.

My beloveds, we are all close, ascending to the Hall of Peace, your angel friends with each one. The light grows brighter, the peace more tangible and God's Love abounds within this glorious place that has been gifted by God. Open your souls wide, my beloveds, and seek the Elixir that brings all that the soul requires and transforms it into something divine. God bless you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus, and I love you. Forevermore we walk together on this great journey toward at-onement with God. God bless you. God bless you all. I love you.
