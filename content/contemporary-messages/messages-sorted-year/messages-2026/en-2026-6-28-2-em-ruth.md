# Discern Your False Beliefs

June 28<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Beams of Light, Chicago, IL, USA

Ruth received by Elizabeth Mack

I am Ruth of the Bible. I come to you from the Celestial Heavens, for my soul has progressed, progressed beyond the natural love, progressed beyond all of those beliefs into the Truth of God's Divine Love, of God's mercy, a mercy which He bestows upon all, any of His children who desire to know the truth, to live the truth and to become the truth. These are the conditions under which a soul may enter into the Celestial Heavens.

I will say to you, it was a process. For I was truly, deeply involved, subscribing to what the Jewish leaders, the men of the so-called men of God, taught us when I lived upon the Earth. When I came into the spirit world thinking that I would ascend quickly to be close to God, that did not occur. For I was filled with many false beliefs, which I accepted into my heart, into my soul, into my mind and even with my body, of what I was taught about our Heavenly Father and many other aspects of what you call spirituality. It took me a while, quite a while, to let go of those falsehoods once the Divine Love was available and Jesus came and showed us the way, showed us that there are higher laws, true laws and a closeness to God that we previously did not feel. Rather, we feared God.

Yet, we also believed that we were chosen by God. Somehow, those two beliefs were in contradiction with the Truth that God is the Father of all souls He created. That was a mountain that was very hard for me to climb. Yet, once I felt that Love in my soul, I began to know the truth and could feel the difference between what was truth and what was belief. Still, it took time for me to break down all of those false beliefs and God, our most beloved and true God, continued to nourish my soul with Love and Truth and many blessings that helped me to progress.

This is the path of progression that you are on, dear children. Look at your false beliefs. Look at your beliefs. Look at the truth that you know. Feel the difference. Pray to God for more and more of His Love, more and more of His mercy to break down those mountains of false belief. I am now a true follower of my Master Jesus and I am refreshed by the truth. Refreshed, enlivened by the Love and unencumbered by false beliefs, I am alive and I am one with my Father in this Love. There is nothing quite so refreshing as Truth given to us with the Father's Love.

May God bless you. May you seek the truth. I will stop now and pray with you. God bless you. Be open, be open, be open and allow the Love and truth to enter therein. I am Ruth, your sister on this eternal, Divine Love Path. God bless you. My love is with you now and always.
