# Keep Yourself in a Condition of Light and Love to Fuel Your Instrumentality

August 4<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

God bless you, my friends. I am Faith. I come to pray with you. It is important that you maintain the light around you, my friends, my beloved brothers and sisters, that each day that you say that prayer, you make that connection with God and you reinforce your soul with God's Love, asking for this gift to enter into it. As you do these things, you reinforce the light around you. You ensure that your angel friends can come close. You ensure that God's Hand is upon you. When you desire to be an instrument of God, it requires these things to be maintained and to be upheld. For the truth is the truth. When you reach out to God, God will reach out to you and there will be a joining of blessings and love between the two of you. When you allow the truth to unfold within you and around you, you become that beautiful child of God. As you continue to grow in that light, to grow in the Father's Love, then further awakening comes and further desire for this gift. As you continue to answer that yearning within your soul, all that is and will be given by God is allowed to enter. You open that door. You invite God in and you allow God to fill the room with His Presence.

It is simple, but it takes that time, practice and effort and to come to know the yearning of your soul, so that as you understand this, as you acknowledge it, as you feel it, then it becomes more powerful within you and the expression of it through prayer is clear, light-filled and beautiful. You walk the Earth in light. It is a dark place, but you become the lanterns. You become the ones who bring the light, because when you are in prayer consistently and daily, even multiple times per day, you strengthen your light. It is like a lantern that is being charged and brightened. The closer you are to God, the brighter it will be, more beautiful. Those with eyes to see, those who are yearning, will feel drawn to that light, will want to know you, for they feel the warmth of the lantern. They feel the warmth of your channel of love in the world. If you give them some words of comfort and wisdom, if you allow God to utilise you to give a blessing of healing, peace, love and upliftment, then you are doing God's work.

Each of you is capable of this. Each of you has many opportunities to do it. Even when you are alone at home, you may still be God's instruments, for He will use you to help spirits. He will use you to help your loved ones, to be a battery, a reinforcement of light as God responds to prayer from others. He will utilise you in that response. There are many ways God will use you, my beloveds, but you must be a light. You must build that light around you. You must be in alignment with God so that His Will might be done through you. It will be miraculous and beautiful in its expression. God needs you, each of you and all who are willing to bring the light into the world. Be that light and maybe in your day you will be used to ignite yet another light and that light will ignite yet another and so it goes, my friends. The lighting of the world starts with one candle, but could embrace millions. Have faith in this, for it is a truth. When you engage in light, you become a progenitor of light for others, because God will use you to do so.

May you be blessed, beloved friends, as God's instruments, His lights, His channels of love. You will know and feel the joy, the reinforcement of love that will come with this. It is yours. It is given freely and you will be upheld by it and your light will grow. God bless you, my beloved friends. I am Faith. I love you, continue to walk with you, work with you and pray with you, all in love, my beloveds, all in love. There are so many others who are working with you to bring that light to the world. God bless you all. Faith loves you, continues to pray with you. God bless you.

2026-08-04-AF-Faith Nyquist (G)
