# The Blessing of Serving God

February 8<sup>th</sup>, 2026

One Nation Circle of Light, Liverpool, NY, USA

George Stokes received by Elizabeth Mack

There are many angels who are here, but I will come forward. I am George Stokes and yes, Bartholomew was the first to come close and then Joan, but you hear me best of all, so I will speak. It is quite lovely to see all of you here today. We have been waiting for this circle in particular to come together, for as you know, it is a powerful one.

Deep Love lives in your souls, powerful Love, the Love of God. You are becoming more and more comfortable with each of your individual Celestial guides. There is a purpose for your coming closer in rapport with them, so that you may be guided each and every day to come closer and closer to the path that is intended for you, to recognise the guardrails that are in place for you and to focus so very accurately on your goal of becoming at one with God, the Creator of everything, the source of all love, our Father, the one who loves us more than anyone else, anywhere, could love us.

I am your brother in the Celestial Heavens and I watch over this medium closely. She brings with her a retinue of souls willing to assist in the development of souls who come to her. Each of you will also be given a work to do as you progress along your path. We use the term “work”, but we do not mean that it is great effort. It is rather great love expressed and the joy felt when expressing this love is beyond comprehension. As you do this work, as many humans call it, be open to receive God’s Love, God’s guidance, God’s Truth and the joy that will come from serving God by assisting your brothers and sisters in ways that are particular to your gifts. All of this will come to you. Of course, with praying for Divine Love, more and more of your gifts will open and it will come quite, let us say, naturally, yet really divinely. It will not be a stretch for you. It will come and you will barely recognise it as anything different from what you have had, only it will be divine. It will be this Love that activates and is manifested in these gifts that is the difference. Of course, as I said, the joy that follows will be extraordinary.

Pray, dear children. Pray deeply, sincerely and truthfully for this Love to open those gifts that you may express in joy and assist the human beings and the spirits who come your way. I am your brother, George Stokes. It is a pleasure to be with you. Your Celestial Angel guides are surrounding you with their love at this time. Please be open and allow your longings to go directly to God for His blessings upon your souls. I love you, dear children of God. Let us receive God’s Love at this time.
