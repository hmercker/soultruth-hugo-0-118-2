# Angels' Promise for the Trinidad Retreat

January 30<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Trinidad Retreat, Grand Couva, Trinidad

Jesus, Mylora & Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

**Jesus:** May the Light of the Father’s Love be within you, with His Hand upon you, my beloveds. I am your brother and friend, Jesus. I come to open these sessions, this beautiful time together, earnest souls seeking the truth and coming to experience the Touch of the Father’s Love within their souls.

My beloveds, we will be with you in prayer, in conversations, as you eat and as you gather. For God has blessed this time. You have asked for this blessing and it will be given. Open your souls wide to the inflowing of the Father’s Gift of Love. Allow yourself to be carried away into that beautiful light, that gift of the Hall of Peace, the blessings of healing and upliftment, as the angels surround you, as you are uplifted and blessed mightily.

I will be with you. I am your brother and friend and I will be with you. I love you. I love each of you and you will come to know my presence as you pray. God bless you, beloved souls, beautiful children of God. I am with you in Love. God bless you.

**Mylora:** God bless you. I am Mylora. I am happy that you are in this environment so rich with God’s blessings and creation. The Earth itself emanates a beautiful light and you all benefit from the blessings of this place. We will work diligently to take advantage of what this area, this piece of land, has to offer and to give to each of you for your enhancement, your health and well-being.

There are many levels and layers of blessings that will be given. My beautiful friends, we from the Celestial Kingdom will bring all that we can in our gifts and abilities, in our ways and means of utilising what God has created to bring blessings to you all. Of course, as you open yourself to the great Gift of God’s Love, then there is greater harmony and greater absorption of these blessings and these “energies”, as you call them. We have lots of work to do, my friends, much to do together, for this place and for this world, to bring light, peace and love, to bring many blessings forth to help humanity and to help you as you continue to forge ahead in your efforts, in your prayers and in your service. Many blessings given, many opportunities afforded to each one of you, to grow, to awaken and to be in alignment with God.

May the blessings of your soul rise up into your consciousness, helping you to see and hear, feel and know the wonderment that is God and the wonderment that is God’s creation. God bless you, beloved souls. I am Mylora. I will be with you as you pray and as you speak and share with one another. Many of us will be here. You have great anticipation and desire for us to be here and so we will be here in abundance, many of us. God bless you, beloved friends. My love is with you. God bless you.

**Keea Atta Kem:** It is your friend, Keea Atta Kem. I too, will be working hard on your behalf, busy to help you, assist you in many ways as you seek and open yourselves spiritually and as you come together to uphold and to love one another. Many blessings indeed and much work to do.

God bless you, my friends. Keea is here and will be here for the duration. God bless you all. My love is with you.
