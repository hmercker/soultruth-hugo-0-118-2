# Guided Meditation Followed by Blessing from Jesus

April 12<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Scottsdale Retreat, Scottsdale, AZ, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

**Guided meditation by Al Fike:** Oh, all my beautiful friends, beautiful souls that you are, we are going to walk together for a while. Each of us is gathered with our brothers and sisters and going on a little walk by the river, this beautiful, beautiful, wide and powerful river with clear water, trees lining each side of this river, a lovely path. It feels like God has created this place just for us, just so that we can enjoy this beautiful creation that is God's handiwork. The coolness of the river on this warm and sunny day makes us feel relaxed and happy. We are walking quietly, listening to the sounds of the river, listening to the rustle of the trees as the breeze flows through. There is such contentment in our hearts. Can you feel it, like all is right in the world? All is peaceful, calm. We are where we are meant to be at this very moment, together, in this light, this beautiful landscape.

We decide to sit by the river, sitting on the lovely grassy bank. Some of us have our feet in the water. Some of us are sitting cross-legged, some facing others, but all of us feeling like we are one body, one body in this beautiful light and warmth and blessed day. We are all thinking of the same thing. How good God is. How wondrous is life to feel and know these things, our bodies relaxed, our minds clear, our souls feeling that beautiful joy from within. It is a perfect day, a perfect day. Indeed, as we sit together, communing with God, communing with all that God has created, we feel the reverence that rises up from our souls, a joy that says: *"Dear and gracious God, I love You. I am Your child. I exist because You created me. I breathe because You put life into me. I experience this world because this is the world You created. I know myself because I am the soul that You created that is unique. I wonder at myself, how beautiful is life."* I think to myself, how beautiful I am, how truly beautiful that God has created such a magnificent being, a wondrous being able to perceive and know and be conscious of all that is around me, able to love my friends who surround me, able to love God who is loving me. All of these gifts, this reality, this knowing. There is more and more as I look back on my life, realising that I have experienced life, all the facets, all the blessings have come to me.

There is a thankfulness that wells up from my heart and is expressed to God, a deep resonance, gratitude. This filled with joy, filled with a steadiness of knowing that I am loved, that I love and that the true, true purpose of life is love in all its ways and expressions. The deepest love of all is that love for God and that God loves me. I can feel it. I know it. From the very depths of my being, I know that I am loved. There is a warm glow within me, that glow of life eternal, that drop of God that has made me and ensures that I am alive. In that drop that is me, I have a longing for more of God, His beautiful Touch upon me, that beautiful comfort, expansiveness, that Love Divine. It is mine to grasp and to bring into me forevermore.

I feel at-oneness with God, with all that God is. In this, my consciousness expands. The awareness of God's creation, God's universe, comes to me. In the awareness I know that my brothers and sisters are experiencing the same thing, the awesomeness of life, the wonderment of our individuality, who we are, where we are going, the journey that we come to realise is eternal. We begin to pray:

*"Gracious and beloved God, we are here with You. We want to be with You. We want to truly know You. Dear and gracious God, we are Your children, Your creations. You have given us the gift of awareness, of knowing You. We are here. We are here. Gracious God, open our hearts and our souls that we may receive this great gift of Your Love, this Love that never ends, this Love that is given and shall be given for eternity. We honour You with desire and acknowledgement and joy, for we are truly Your children, seeking to be redeemed in Your Love, seeking to truly know ourselves in this Love. Gift this to us, beloved Father. I know that You want this for us. I know that we were created to receive this gift. I know that within me, I acknowledge and desire to receive it.*

*O beloved Father, I want to be Your child in all the ways that I can be. I want to be true to You in all the ways that I can be. I want to know your grace with the potency and power that it is, that I might walk in the bliss, the expansiveness and the peace that passes all understanding. I am Yours. I give myself to You. In my heart, I am Yours. May we walk together in the realms of light and know the joys of this relationship of love that is beyond anything I have experienced and known in my life, the depth of which is greater and wider, deeper and more powerful than anything.*

*O beloved God, I thank You for this, this gift beyond gifts. I am here to receive, to be close, and my angel friends to be close with me, that we may all commune together in the oneness of this great Truth, in the knowing that it is Your Holy Spirit that blesses us. O beloved Father, we are here together at one with love overflowing, with love inflowing, with love everywhere. We thank You, God, and we are with You. Amen.*

**Jesus:** My brethren, I come to be with you. I too sit by the river*. I too join you as your brother and your friend. I too love God. Indeed, you are my brethren, and I will be with you for eternity.

We shall know the wonders of God. Together, we shall know our place in the firmament. The expression of each of our souls will be like stars in the heavens, bright and beautiful, bringing hope and promise to the world, bringing light where there is darkness, quelling the fires of fear and bringing the light of truth. The awakenings must come to the Earth, and all who dwell upon it. It must come, my brethren, for this is God's Will and God's plan to awaken humanity to the truth, to bring love to every aching soul parched and seeking the Living Waters.

My beloveds, will you join me on this quest to bring water to the thirsty, to bring truth to the lost ones, to bring love to all? For how else will God do His work without His instruments, His angels on Earth? You are they, my beloveds, you are they. We shall walk together and do God's bidding and know the wonderment and the glory of His blessings upon us, my beloveds. It shall be so. It is our destiny together. I call you. I call you to be in alignment with God's great plan and to be that beautiful light that you are, powerful and gentle, bright and glowing. The warmth emanates from each of you, the truth is upon your lips and the comfort through your gaze to another.

Be that light, my beloveds, and walk this road. It shall take us on a magnificent journey beyond anything you might imagine. My beloveds, I call you. I call you to be close and to listen to your guidance and to listen to the Will of God. We will work tirelessly to bring peace to the Earth, a peace that is destined, but must come soon. My beloveds, we walk together. We are as one.

God bless you. May His Love continue to pour into your souls, brightening, brightening the beautiful beings that you are and empowering you to be God's messengers and instruments of truth and love. God bless you, my beloveds. I am Jesus. I come to be with my brethren. God bless you. God bless you.
