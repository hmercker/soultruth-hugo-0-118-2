# Attachments

May 11<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Online Circle of Light, Nashville, TN, USA

Clare of Assisi received by Jimbeau Walsh

May the breath of God be upon you in His holy presence. I am Clare.

Dear ones, we walk with you in your struggle, navigating the vicissitudes of earthly life, encouraging you to make choices that will benefit your spiritual growth, as well as healing you in body, mind, and soul. So many souls are coming over into spirit, having no knowledge of this great divine truth, clinging to their earthly attachments and eventually realizing that they have stagnated, giving them the impetus to walk towards the light, to accept guidance from higher spirits, and to allow us to come and serve them. If you find that the attachments you have do not bring happiness, do not promote good health, I encourage you to replace them with what uplifts your soul, what brings you true joy.

Living on Earth in the material world can be quite difficult. So many things vying for your attention. So many things to distract you or simply make you numb. This is not the way. The way is engagement in love, and the great love of our heavenly Father will magnetize you towards every good and perfect gift, towards serving the world in His love. Even those who are what we would call progressive spirits are sometimes surprised at how attached they still are to things that they did not realize held them back. These can be very mundane, but heavy things. Attachments to a house, for example, or something as odd as a sports team. Now, those attachments you have to your family, to your loved ones, to your community; these are generally healthy and will serve you well on your journey, provided you remember to respect each soul’s free will and always encourage in love, be willing to forgive others as well as yourself, and recognize in your prayers the worthiness of every soul and its preciousness to God.

Let us cling to the heavenly garment of our Creator, lifting us up and filling us with His essence. This is joy, this is happiness, and freedom in the progression of your soul in the love of God.

I shall step back in this beautiful prayer with all my blessings in this sacred circle. I thank you. May God bless each one. I am Clare, your sister in Christ, once from Assisi and now a grateful and redeemed celestial angel. I love you. Go in peace.
