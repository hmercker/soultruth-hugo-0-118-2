# Allow All That You Are to Manifest in the World

May 5<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Direct Voice, Sechelt, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

May the blessings of God’s Love continue to flow into you, changing you and transforming you. I am Seretta Kem. I come to reinforce your knowledge of yourself, this beautiful soul you possess, this wonderful capacity for love, for perception, for compassion, for joy, for reverence of God and for life.

My beautiful friends, you only scratch the surface of what you are capable of. So much within you, you are unaware of. So much within you is waiting for your acknowledgement and waiting for the acknowledgement of God, which comes with this gift of Love. Yet, within you is a whole universe of wonderment, of experience, of joy, of love, of knowings beyond knowings, of wisdom beyond wisdom. It is within you, my brethren, my beloved brothers and sisters. It is within you and each of you are on this journey of exploration, understanding and truly coming to know yourself. As you do so, my beloved friends, as you do so, you are acknowledging the creation that God has made, the seed that God has put within the universe, this magnificent seed, this magnificent creation that is you.

Allow all that you are to come to consciousness, to manifest in the world that which you have recognised thus far and with the desire and the enthusiasm for what may come, for you will surely surprise yourself with what is present within you, the potentials that you possess, the potentials that are ignited with this great gift of God's Love. When you acknowledge this, accept this and embrace it, your journey quickens. Your capacity to understand, to open to what is truly within, is more readily accessed and to come to consciousness. We will help you, as we always have and always will. We will help you to realise certain gifts, certain abilities that will be very useful in the times to come, to help uplift your brothers and sisters, to help teach them, to help show the way toward greater harmony and truth and to be a lantern of love in the world, my friends, a lantern of love, so that many may benefit from the great efforts that you have made upon your spiritual journey. For there is so much that you may give and there is so much that you may use to inspire and to uplift your brothers and sisters. Ask God to show you the way to be that example, that channel, that light in the world. God may use you in ways and means that will surely surprise you and possibly astound you.

These are what you might call *"miracles"*, but are truly utilising the laws of the universe to manifest, to bring forward gifts and blessings that will open the eyes of your brothers and sisters, awaken them to the possibilities that exist. Rather than their heads cast down to the Earth, sullenly and forlornly, they may lift their eyes to heaven and you may give them hope, hope that truly, there is a way through the pain and the darkness, the suffering and the loneliness and that you, as an example, a shining bright example, may point the way and say, *"I have done this. I have walked this road. I have come to understand the power of love and the power of God's Love to transform. I am truly in a state of brightness and joyfulness that indeed is not something I have put on as a cloak or I am pretending to express, but is authentic and real and powerful, beautiful."* As those around you see this, they will come to question how and why you are as you are and you may share with them this potent Truth, that they are truly loved by God and that with that Love they may be free of these burdens and darkness, that it will truly change, awaken and transform those who pursue this gift in the way prescribed.

My beloved friends, as we have told you many, many times, there is much work to do and you grow brighter with each day, with each prayer and you will continue to do so, provided you apply yourselves to the laws that exist. They are simple, yet they are necessary. If you are dedicated and you truly pray for the gift of Divine Love, then this manifestation of your true self will take place. You will not hide away but be bold and expressive within your authentic self, that true soulful being that you are. Others will take heed, my friends. Truly, they will see you for what you are and acknowledge you and be curious and desire what you have. It is the living example of truth within you, expressed in every moment, every breath, that is the key to manifesting truth, manifesting light. When you are that individual, that being stepping forward into the world, the material manifestation will merely be an addition to what is part of God's plan and what is God's plan for you.

Walk with faith, my friends. Trust in God. Trust in your own soul. Trust in God's plan and be dedicated to your relationship with your Creator, ever seeking His Love. Much will come to you, my friends, and much will come through you for the benefit of many, many others. I am your friend, your helper, your teacher. I am Seretta Kem. My love for you is great, truly. God bless you, my friends. God bless you all upon the journey.
