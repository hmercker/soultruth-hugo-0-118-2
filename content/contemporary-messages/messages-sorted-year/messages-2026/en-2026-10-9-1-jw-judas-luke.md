# In-Person DL Prayer Circles

October 9<sup>th</sup>, 2026

Nashville Prayer Circle, Nashville, TN, USA

Judas & Luke received by Jimbeau Walsh

It is I, your brother **Judas**. My dear friends, I wish to commend you for creating this inaugural prayer circle and to remind you that many things have humble beginnings. I would encourage you to invite those you may feel have a soul interest to your circle. For are you not offering this prayer for God’s love so that it may be received by all? Those who have some basic attraction to spirituality, those who believe in God but perhaps have been dismayed or wounded during their lifetime with religion, with orthodox beliefs that were and remain unfulfilled.

You have the knowledge of soul transformation in God’s love through prayer, and as we say, it is simple, it is direct, and it is real. So, offer to anyone you feel may wish to be in this presence to be healed, to be changed, to be set free from the chains of orthodoxy and error into this simple yet beautiful truth of God’s love and grace and mercy. You are well on your way on this path, which will be one of endless surprise, endless revelation, endless transformation, in joy, in peace, and in holiness.

I shall not continue at length today, but I shall come again, and those who come will be guided by the many. I shall step back and join you. May the blessings of God’s love fill your souls, touch your hearts, and change your lives in grace. I am your brother and friend and a redeemed soul in Christ.

I am Judas, once of Kerioth, now an inhabitant of God’s celestial kingdom. With all my love, go in peace.

I come again. I am **Luke**. As I told my dear brother and sister yesterday in silence, it is time to throw caution to the wind and allow the Holy Spirit of God to inform your walk. This will inevitably bring many seeking, hungry souls into your orbit. You know the adage: *”Seek and you shall find; knock, and the door will be opened.”* There are many seeking, many knocking, and so I ask you to be aware of those in your circle and beyond whose souls would benefit greatly from this time in grace, in prayer.

Speaking as a physician, I can tell you spiritually every soul is wounded, every soul needs healing, and as in the physical realm, there are techniques and medicines and practices which will bring a body into harmony. In the spiritual realm, it is prayer, and as you receive the great gift of the Father’s love, the angels come to heal, to guide, to educate, and to support you far beyond what you may perceive in your mind, but certainly apparent to your soul perceptions when you are in grace. May you open the doors of your soul, the doors of your heart, to all, that they may join us in the oneness, in the freedom, in the bliss of God’s love. I love you.

I am Luke. God bless you.
