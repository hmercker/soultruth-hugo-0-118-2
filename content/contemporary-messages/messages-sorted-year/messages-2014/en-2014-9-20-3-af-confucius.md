# Be a Channel of Love wherever you go

September 20<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, Confucius is with you.

My children you brought the living waters into the desert which is this city of monuments. You brought light and you quenched the thirsts as God’s instruments. You came together to pray for this blessing. You were indeed the channels of love. And this is your purpose, my children, to be a channel of love wherever you go. And I would encourage you to accept those invitations from others who seek your presence, your instrumentality. And indeed, the means will be provided for these travels, and the Angels will be with you, and you will see God’s Will manifest in your world, my children, through your willingness to meet the needs of your fellow man.

Indeed, you are ready and your souls eager for the next steps and God will open these doors and usher you through and bring you to those places of great need and deprivation, for everywhere in this world is this desert, this parched place where God’s Love does not reach the souls of mankind. Indeed, these living waters must flood the world and bring sustenance to the thirsty souls. And you, my children, are the water bearers. Continue in your work, in your labours of love and God will uphold you and we will protect you and sustain you.

You are blessed. You continue to be blessed, and you will know great blessings, vision opened, awareness emerging, great love in every part of your being, and wherever you travel, wherever you live. Trust in God’s wisdom, my children, and trust in yourselves, for within your souls you are aware of God’s wisdom and guidance. Manifest this in your lives.

A blessing of peace upon you, beloveds. Confucius has great love for you and honours your efforts to bring light into those darkened places, to nurture your brothers and sisters. Continue to pray together and walk in love. Blessings to you, beloveds.
