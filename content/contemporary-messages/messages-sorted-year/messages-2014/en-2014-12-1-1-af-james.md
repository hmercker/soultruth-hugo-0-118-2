# You are a Light in the World

December 1<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

James received by Al Fike

Precious child, beloved daughter who toils so selflessly for others, you have been guided to this place and this circle of love for your soul longs for this, my daughter. You have found a place that you have sought for many years and you will know a fellowship that will nurture you as you nurture so many. And God will embrace you, my beloved, and carry you upon this wondrous journey of love. And you have many gifts to share, many gifts you are not aware of at this time but shall flourish within you as your soul opens to God’s Love and brings to you the truth that you long for. And the relationship that truly grows within you, between you and God, it will ? you and we are so happy that you have accepted the invitation that was given. Beloved daughter, you are loved and cherished and are a light in this world. And you are embraced in love. God bless you. I am James and I am with you, my daughter, often. God bless you.
