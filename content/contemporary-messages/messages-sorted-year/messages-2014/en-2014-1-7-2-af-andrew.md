# We cannot interfere with the Free Will of Mankind

January 7<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Andrew received by Al Fike

Please do not blame God for this outcome that is disappointing, for this is the result of the human condition. That even we of the celestial heavens cannot control events upon this earth as you may surmise. Yet your prayers were powerful and the light in that room was intense, I must assure you. The angels were there. But we cannot interfere with the free will of mankind.

And although the room was filled with love there was an outcome that was not in harmony with the Will of God. And this is the human condition, that which you must deal with every day in your lives. And yes, you wish to believe that all is positive and light, goodness and just. Unfortunately, this is not the case and this is why you are so needed in this world: To push back this darkness, to challenge the error, to bring love where there is not love and in many ways and in many incidents you are successful but at times the darkness is too great, the ignorance too deep and the rage and the anger …. the judgment… and the lack of love bring great burdens upon the innocent.

Do not allow this to burden you, my children; do not let the darkness affect your light. Have faith, be with God. Know that God lives within your souls with His Love and lives within the soul of your son. This time that he is not free is merely the blink of the eye in all eternity and he will benefit in some regards to this time--to this continuation. Continue to pray for him

and love him and to confront the darkness, for there are many challenges ahead.

Such a great wall of darkness to push upon, to push upon; to push back, to bring the light. You must be strong. You will be strong. And your son will be comforted, and he will let go of his bitterness, his anger. He will continue to strive for goodness for he has truly turned this corner. He will not fail. And with your love and prayers he will feel the support. And we will be with him always; this is our promise – we will be with him always. Protecting him and encouraging him towards the light. In his prayers, God will touch his soul, in his thoughts we will bring inspiration and peace. In his efforts there will be success. And when his time comes to be free, he will be well prepared for his new life.

Be reassured my daughter (Jeanne) that he is safe and God cares for him and he will succeed. Though you are eager to have him within your arms and lead him to greater pastures, you must be patient and in the end, light will prevail over darkness. Love will bring healing; wisdom will reside within his soul. For he grows strong and will continue to grow strong in this struggle. For within his reckless acts of the past lies a great opportunity for negative forces to stifle him and burden him and pester him and try to draw him from the light-- and I say the light will win and God’s Will will prevail. Just a little longer my child, just a little longer. Continue with your prayers, your constant vigil. We are always with you and with him. And though your life is not completely as you wish, there is still great light and great work to do in this time. And we are joyed at these opportunities and possibilities. Do not let this weigh upon you but have faith that he is in God’s Care as are you and all of you here. Peace is upon you my dear, sweet souls. Peace and love, peace and love, the healing grace, the loving touch. Blessings to you. Andrew loves you. Good night.
