# Love will conquer all

September 20<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Abraham Lincoln received by Al Fike

I have come again, Lincoln, Abraham Lincoln. And I am pleased to come and speak to you, my children, my friends, and to reinforce my message that there is a great work ahead to change the world, the conditions of humanity, for the great deprivation that now is prevalent in your world cannot continue. For deprivation breeds more deprivation, inequities bring more inequities, and greed breeds more greed. And these situations upon your world require all of you to challenge the conditions that are prevalent today. And your duties, my friends, are to be purveyors of love, light, wisdom and to stand up for truth. For who will stand up for truth, who will shout truth so that those who turn a blind eye to the darkness and perpetuate these conditions will hear that voice from the wilderness.

And in your soul condition and what you carry within you, you will make an impact upon those, upon those who are powerless, upon all the children of the world. For the power of God’s Love is the greatest power of all. Its ability to bring change, to bring light and harmony is beyond your imagining, my children. And God will use you to do this, to accomplish these deeds, will place you in those positions where the children must listen and will feel the power of your love and God’s Love.

Yes, there is a great work ahead and much to accomplish. Do not allow those small things, those small barriers to frustrate you or inhibit your efforts but know that you are in God’s care and you will walk down that straight and narrow path which will bring you to that place of light and change and healing for the world. Many are being prepared, my children, with this great effort. And soon the truth will be shouted from the rooftops. Soon those in power cannot turn a blind eye. Soon the changes in the world on many different levels will force those in power to take a good look at what they have created, take a good look at themselves.

And those of you who stand up for light will play a role in advising the leaders, advising those who are frightened, and those who are confused. You will bring your truth and in their desperation, there will be an opening where you may speak and bring this truth in a way that they will listen. Yes, there is a great plan and much to do. And I and many others are here to support you in your prayers and efforts, (with) our wisdom and our love. If you could see but a portion of those Angels who are here and are willing to support you, you would be astounded at the numbers, at the light, the blessings that are available to carry you forth on these wings of truth.

God bless you, my friends. God bless you, for the world needs you and many more like you. Be courageous, yet humble. Be strong, yet wise. Be joyful and be loving. Love will conquer all. Blessings to you, my friends and thank you for allowing me to speak. I am Abraham Lincoln and I am pleased to be with you, in your prayers, beloved, beautiful souls. Bless you.
