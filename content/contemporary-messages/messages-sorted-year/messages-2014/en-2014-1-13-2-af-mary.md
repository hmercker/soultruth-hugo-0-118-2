# Pray and be blessed

January 13<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

Bless you in your prayers and in your longings and in your tears, as you beseech God to rain down his healing upon your loved ones. Bless you for your efforts to bring light into this world. Bless you to open your souls and ask God to fill your souls with this Love. Bless you for trying to struggle for the light with every day that you live and to live these truths as best you can. It is the power of prayer, my children, the power of prayer. And in this beseeching God, comes the light and blessings that you seek, the Love that your soul longs for, and the truth you are eagerly seeking. In your prayers these will come.

Bless you children, for we pray with you, we pray for you, and we all pray together to be close to God, to be close to the fountainhead of His Love, to walk a path of Light, to know peace, abiding peace within. You are loved, each one, every soul worthy of God’s Love, every soul. And for some this Love is known, is familiar, for some it is new, but all are worthy. Pray for this, my children, for the key to all that you desire, all that you need. The floodgates will open and you will come to truly know God in all the glory, the light, the Love. Thank you, my dear, precious souls. I am Mary. I love you. I am with you often. God bless you.
