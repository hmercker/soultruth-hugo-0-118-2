# The Role as a Channel of Love has many Aspects

October 20<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

God bless you, it is Seretta Kem. I have been here bringing energies and chemicals into this group to help balance your bodies and to develop faculties that will be useful in the future for each one of you. And I wish to remind you of one of the purposes of this group of prayer for God’s Love is to continue to establish and reinforce the bonds between yourselves and other members who are not with you tonight, and those who you met in far off places whom you have established a bond with, and they are scattered around the world. I would ask you to pray for these individuals, to continue to reinforce the bond of love, this network of light. And as you pray for these souls, you fuel this network with light, and you grow this network and make it stronger.

This is part of your purpose, beloved souls. For have you not travelled far and wide to make these bonds possible? And have you not communicated over the wires to reinforce these bonds and remind those dear souls that you are there in your loving ways and words and prayers. Continue, my beloveds, to work in this way. And this is but one way of many actions and activities that are possible through this group; to help bring light into the world there is much possibility here, many possibilities, which are yet to be discerned and acted upon. And you will discover these as you go along in your efforts, in your loving efforts to come together in prayer for Love.

Yes, there is much work to do, and you begin to spread your influence far and wide as God’s channels of love, and this shall intensify as the years go by, and we will draw in others who are willing and desirous to work in this way, and whose souls desire God’s Love. Important work, my children. Important, necessary - dare I say, crucial - to help bring your beautiful planet back to harmony, to help bring your brothers and sisters into light and a greater understanding of what their lives can be and are meant to be. Yes, this work is complex, and I do not wish for you to ponder upon the complexities, but to be aware that we work in many different ways with you as a group and individually as channels of love.

The role as a channel of love has many aspects. The role as God’s instruments of change bring many challenges. And the greatest challenge for you, my children, is to release all that which is in the way of you being a clear channel of love. For in this development of your souls, in this healing of your being, many more possibilities will become probabilities. Great potentials will be exercised and become reality. Wondrous gifts, that which lay dormant within your beings, gifts from God, will become apparent and active, all with the blessing of God’s Love within and your willingness to continually reach for the highest and allow that which is not of the highest to fall away from you. Inner harmony and joy will ensue with this healing.

Blessed souls, continue on your journey of discovery, of service, of love. And we will do our part to help you along the road and work with you to the highest of your abilities and gifts and love. It is our honour. It is our joy to do so. And we pray constantly for your ever-evolving soul to be in greater light and harmony. God bless you, beloveds. Seretta Kem is with you. I have been with you for many years, and I have been working with other groups which have preceded this group. And I shall continue to work with other groups that will follow this group. A continuous chain of light and work and service and love. God bless you, beloved souls.
