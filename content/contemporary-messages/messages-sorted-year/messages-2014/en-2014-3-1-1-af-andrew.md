# Seek and you shall find

March 1<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children, beautiful souls. I am Andrew, and I’ve come to be with you in your prayers, to pray with you, and to beseech you to send forth your longings to the Heavenly Father, to open yourselves to that deep and holy touch from God, that you may receive His Love deep within you, in your souls; for in this blessing will come many of the answers that you seek, and much of the comfort that you long for. And as you go to that place of communion with God, this will be an abiding comfort and place of respite and healing and joy. In this simple effort, in this pure desire, in this beseeching of the Father, you will come to know yourselves in such a way that all of the gifts, all of the wonderment, which is you, your creation, your being, your soul, will come to your consciousness, your awareness, and will flourish and express themselves through you, about you, within you.

For within each soul lies many gifts and aspects that you do not know at this time but will come to blossom within you. This is the wonderment of God’s Love, this wonderful light, that ignites so much within. And all that is required for you, is to pray, to be open, to express these soulful longings within, and to listen, to be still, to allow God to touch you in this way that brings the wonderment, the change within. And through this much light, much love will flow through you to others. Much light will flow around you into every part of your lives, and you will know a freedom, you will know a wonderment, you will know a relationship with your Creator that will be your rock that you can come to rely on, and you can come for sustenance, for healing and peace, and knowledge of the truth; for this knowledge will be put within your souls as this Love flows in. So will come the understanding, the deep understanding of what is the reality of Creation, the wonderment of Creation.

God puts forth an invitation to you, my children, to seek His Love, to accept this gift, to long for this relationship. And indeed, great changes will come within, and wonderful changes without and around, and you will be a channel of light in this world as this transformation begins within your souls with the inflowing of God’s Love. Can you not feel God’s presence, my children? God is with you. You are with God. And we are so happy that you come together in this way, seeking this light, this respite, this Love.

It will always lead you in the right direction, my children. It will lead you into light and harmony, healing and peace. You are all beloved by God. You are all beautiful. You are all precious and loved. Come to know this, come to truly know this, and all that burdens you will fall away, and the conditions of this world will not bring you into darkness, for the light of God will burn within you and help you to make those decisions, those choices, for light, for harmony, for love. God bless you. May God keep you on your journey, and may the angels be with you always as you seek truth, as you seek succour for your souls. Ask and you shall receive. Seek and you shall find. Knock and the door shall be opened. It is your desire, my children, your choice, your longing, your openness, that will bring what you require, the answers that you seek, the love that you long for. God will show you the way. God is showing you the way and shall continue to do so. God bless you on your journey, beloved souls.

Andrew loves you, as do many. God bless you.
