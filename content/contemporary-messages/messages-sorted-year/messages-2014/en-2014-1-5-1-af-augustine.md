# With the physical Manifestations of Change will come Calamities and Disruptions

January 5<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Augustine received by Al Fike

My students, it is your teacher, Augustine. Do you feel the upliftment that has been given to each one of you of late, that the angels have come closer, that God’s Love is more readily available, that you have been supported in many ways, and given much - many resources, for travel, to connect with one another? This is part of the plan, my dear children, a plan that is vast in scope, and is barely revealed to your consciousness, for it is complex and diverse, dynamic and intricate, and this is why we have made these efforts to support you, and to bring you into this light more fully, so that you may be receptive to the guidance that is required that you may fulfil your part within this intricate plan, and to help facilitate much that is waiting. For the world, my children, is on the verge of great change and transition, both physically and spiritually. And with the physical manifestations of change will come calamities and disruptions. Every soul upon this planet will be affected by this, and the life that you so comfortably take for granted, will in time disappear, to bring something that is more in harmony with the laws of Creation.

And of course, transitions are difficult and uncomfortable, and there will be great rushing about to hold back this dam that is about to burst; but this is God’s will, my children, that life upon this planet will change; and so you see why we have been making great effort and preparation for each one of you. For now, the invitation is given, for you to be lights in this world, assisting in this transformation and transition, bringing the wisdom that is necessary, touching the souls so that they may have the strength and the understanding to turn to God, to make their own changes and transformation.

And yes, it is sad that many will turn away and will be lost in the tidal wave of this change, but many will turn to God and assist this change. And you, my children, have already turned to God. And this makes you leaders, teachers. And in your strength and faith many will turn to you, and you will calm their fears, and you will bring the knowledge required for them to come into alignment and harmony with this change. And it will be a new world, my children, a world of peace, a world where all of Creation will flourish, and so will mankind, in the end, flourish, and come to a higher level of their progression and understanding. And this will usher in many new cultural and material manifestations.

There is much awaiting mankind to help bring a harmonious life to each individual upon this planet, where there is not deprivation and such violence and ignorance. Yes, you think I speak of Utopia. But I tell you that the world, in time, will come to that place of light and greater understanding, and the life filled with meaning and purpose that fits harmoniously within God’s plan and will for Creation. And so, this transition begins, and you are all a part, an integral part, of ushering in this new era. And yes, this work is immense. It will require a great deal of each one of you. And for those willing to step into the fray to do this work, you will be sustained, both physically and materially.

You are not to worry about these concerns, but to increase your faith, to pray for greater Love within your souls, to ask God to bring you the awarenesses that you require, and to release all of that within you that is not required, that you may be pure and attuned and strong and loving. This is not a step in the evolution of the material body of mankind, but a step in the evolution of the soul of mankind. That soul needs to be awakened. And you will ring the bell, the clarion call to waken these souls, to open their eyes that their scales may fall from their eyes, that they may see with clarity that God loves them, and that through this Love they may manifest love and harmony.

Continue, my children. I have given you something to discuss amongst yourselves so that you may come to greater understanding of why these efforts have been made to bring you together, why you have been guided and supported. And I say that for many they were given this purpose as they incarnated into this world, and so the soul desires to fulfil this purpose, to live the life intended, to be the channel of love, pure and bounteous, to follow the truth with integrity and strength. It is a great opportunity, my children, for you to live a life so greatly fulfilled and joyous. Of course, the choice is yours. You may go through this door, or you may wait. Either way God still loves you. Either way, you will progress in the Love. But in one way you will help many, many others, and in the other there will be a much more limited life.

So we ask you, will you step through this door and hear the Master’s beckoning call, and know the will of the Father, and that purpose that he has put within you? So much for you to consider, so many plans to be made, so much guidance to be given. And if you wish to listen, it will be given, and we will be with you in your planning and your efforts, always with you. We will never abandon you, my children. No matter what choice you make, you will not be abandoned but always loved. Change is good if it is change towards light. It is not always easy, but as you look back upon your life that is ending, you will see that God has guided you and sustained you and shown you many, many things, for God is bounteous and generous in His Love. Bless you precious, precious souls, pearls beyond price, beautiful creations of God, who step forth in the unknown, yet through their love of God are willing, are willing in love. God bless you. Augustine loves you.
