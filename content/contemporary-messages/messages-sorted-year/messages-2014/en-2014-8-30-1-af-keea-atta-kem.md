# Blessings

August 30<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Keea Atta Kem received by Al Fike

It is Keea. Bless you, my children. I have not been with you to speak for a long time, but I have been with you very often, and I’m with you my dear, dear child, and your baby. And I help to guide you and inspire you, my dear child. And the dear soul has Love Divine within her, a beautiful start for a life, a wondrous road to walk upon for her. And she has many gifts to give to the world, a beautiful precious soul. And you, my dear daughter, will nurture her and mentor her and bring her into the fulness of her destiny.

And so, you will walk hand in hand upon the divine road, working together, bringing much light to the world, and teaching many, the way to harmony and health and love. Bless you, dear daughter. Bless you both daughters, whom I love and care for. God bless you, beloveds, all beloveds here. I love you dearly. Kindred souls, upon the journey together.
