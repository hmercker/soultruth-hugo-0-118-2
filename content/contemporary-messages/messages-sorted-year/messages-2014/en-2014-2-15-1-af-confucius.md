# A mighty Mission

February 15<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you. I am Confucius, and I come to greet the new faces within this circle, to welcome you to this sanctuary of light and love. Indeed, the door is open for you, my children, the door is open for you. And your souls have brought you to this place. Your desire to connect with this love and truth, with God, has brought you to this place. And I say to you, open yourselves to the Love; God’s Essence may flow within you, and it will have a residence within your being for all eternity. If you but pray for this blessing it will be given, it will be given. And this highest of all blessings, greatest of all truths, will set you upon a path of light and wonderment and grace. And we, the angels of love, will accompany you upon your journey, and support you in your efforts to seek at-onement with God. For God desires for you to seek this, and in so doing, to open yourselves in prayer and longing to receive the Love, His Essence, this beautiful light, eternal light, powerful gift, transformative and holy.

Yes, our two precious souls have returned from their journeys and they see again the possibilities of bringing light to a dark place in this world. And there is a powerful, powerful mission in this effort that shall indeed bring much light and truth to many, many souls in this world. And yes, you question the wisdom of God in His beckoning you to a place that is far off and does not have the charms of your present residence. But I tell you, that God in His wisdom will guide you where you must go, will beckon you along the journey that must be taken, and you will be accompanied by many angels. And those you leave behind, my children, will not suffer, for God will care for them as well.

And there will be comfort given, and love poured upon each soul. And I ask you, my children, who are indeed left behind, to pray for these two souls and their mission, for it is a mighty, mighty effort. It requires your support and your love and your prayers. For there is still much to clear away from the path, there are still many changes to come, and I tell you, my children, for each one here and for all upon this world there are many changes to come. Indeed, you must be strong in the Love, in your faith that God will guide you and set you upon the right path. And in your faith and your trust in your loving God, you will find the correct path for you to fulfill your purpose, to be a channel of love in the world, to be an agent of change, to bring comfort and truth to many souls. And I commend these two precious souls, who are willing to journey forth in love, who walk in faith, who are trusting in the will of God. And I say all will unfold in harmony and peace.

There is no need for worry, no need for fear, no need to question or doubt, but to carry on in faith and trust and love and joy, for God’s hand is upon you, my children, God’s hand is upon you. Wherever you journey, God will be; wherever you are, God’s Love resides within you, making possible the loving connection, the guiding light, the angelic support. You will be in the light always. Beautiful, beautiful souls. Confucius loves you.

And yes, I guide my precious children and shall continue. It is a gift from God to have this wonderful association, and I am joyed at this gift. God bless you, my children. Confucius loves you and is ever with you on your journeys, blessed souls.
