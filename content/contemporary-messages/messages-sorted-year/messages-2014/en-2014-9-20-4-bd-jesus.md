# Blessings

September 20<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

You have been blessed by Divine Love. Each of you, my beloveds, you have received this blessing. You must continue in your world to let it flow to all the lives that you touch, (to?) all the blessed, dear souls. I am Jesus. Dearly beloveds you are deeply needed in your troubled world.
