# God will answer your Prayers

January 7<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Alec Gaunt received by Al Fike

Friends! Alec here!

Yes, God has heard your prayers and will answer your prayers. As for your son, he will turn this corner and choose the light. And in this time that he has been away from you, he has learned much & we in spirit have taught him much and there has been a great shift in his thinking and his desires. He will choose light. And with the miracle of God’s Love, his pain and his experiences can be transmuted into great compassion and love for others and understanding of their struggles. And so it is, in the miracle of God’s Love, that through your struggles, your pain, and through the difficulties that ensue every soul in this world - comes a transmutation of your awareness that brings compassion and love for others. For if God carries you in a life that does not have struggles and difficulties - like a flower grown under glass-- it is weak when it comes out into the fresh air. And so, my children, through your struggles and pain you have grown strong and you are able to perceive the struggles of others and understand…and have compassion. God allows you to bend but not break and though much of your pain is the result of your own free will and choices, as well as the conditions which you inherit from your forefathers and the environment of this world, this brings great difficulty and dysfunctions. You have struggled into the light, like a seed buried in the ground that is germinating, feeling the warmth of God’s Love and emerging from that dark place to that light place.

And you shall continue to grow in this light of Love and emerge as a perfect flower, unique, that is you – which God created. Part of the process of life, my children, part of the process of life. And you’re strong. And my daughter, (J) you will love your son and bring him great comfort and my son, (A) you will love your son and bring him wisdom and guidance. And all of your loved ones will gather about him and bring him what he needs to start his new life. And yes, he has laid in the darkness and sorrow for many years and yet now he emerges into the light and in this there will be joy and welcoming and compassion and forgiveness.

Love him! Love him for he is reborn into light! And yes, all of you do start a new day and within that day comes many possibilities and many opportunities. Struggle for that light ever brighter. Struggle to understand God’s Will. Focus your attention on this and on receiving His Love and many doors will open. Great light will shine. Much guidance will be given, and you will walk a wondrous path, my friends, a path leading to joyful fulfilment of your purpose. Be open to the possibilities. Accept the generous hand of God’s Guidance and Will. And all will be provided to facilitate the unfolding of this. All that is required is your acceptance and your desire to follow God’s Will. And the way will be made clear. It will be made clear.

My precious friends, I understand your struggles and no mortal is perfect. But all mortals are worthy of God’s Love, and you have chosen love and you are an expression of God’s Love, and you will continue to do so in ever more beautiful ways. And you will teach others how to love and how to be in the world of love-- how to have compassion for yourself and others—and to discern truth—and to make those choices for light. You have much to give and the world cries out in need and sorrow. So many are lost in the darkness - in that darkness-- in that soil. Yet with the light of God’s Love, they shall emerge as you have. It is for you to call those souls to struggle for the light, for themselves and for others. Bless you, my friends.

I am so pleased to see how those from that part of the world where I lived on earth have spread, have travelled and connected to so many others from around the world. This was my dream and I see that this instrument and his dear mate have fulfilled this dream and will continue in greater capacities and wondrous ways to bring the bonds of love to all the children who desire this love to make a family. To make a movement for truth that will change the course of history as all of you band together, contributing your skills, your gifts and your love to make this a reality as you follow God’s Will.

Rest assured that from our side of life that you have armies of angels at your side, and we work diligently to bring this truth to the world. Listen and you will know your place. Have faith and you will not fear the next steps. Be a channel of Love, what greater purpose can one have in this world than to be a channel of Love? Walk with your head high and your feet firmly planted on the divine path and all will be well. I assure you…all will be well.

Bless you my friends and thank you for the efforts you have made, and I anticipate so much more as you walk in your faithful path. Bless you. Alec loves you. And I will be with your son. I will be there at the hearing as will be many angels. Rest assured light will be there. God will be there. Be at peace. Bless you.
