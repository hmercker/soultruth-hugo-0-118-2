# Unshackle yourselves from the Conditions of this World

December 31<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloveds, it is your teacher, Augustine. Yes, another year upon your Earth has gone by, a time to reflect upon the experiences, the growth within your souls, the Love ever accumulating within that place and the deepening of your relationship with your Creator. For you have progressed, my beloveds, you have walked this path Divine and listened to your guidance, and walked in faith, and beseeched your Heavenly Father to draw you ever closer to His bosom, to be nurtured and healed, to be shown the wonderment of Creation, to open your eyes to the reality of God’s presence, (to create?), and to unshackle yourselves from the conditions of this world, the thoughts and fears, the distortions and error, all those things inherent in your world that are not meant to be in your world, nor are they meant to be within you, for God wishes for you to walk in freedom and light, joy in life.

Every day your Creator beseeches you to put away your cloak of pain and to walk in light, joyous, with renewed vigour and strength, love and humility. And there are times when you cling to this cloak and you kneel in pain, beseeching your Heavenly Father to wipe away your tears and your fears. Yet it is your responsibility and (choice?) for within you is great light and understanding, yet so often you allow that which is within your mind, those thoughts and energies so resonant with this Earth plane to take hold of your consciousness and burden you with these terrible conditions.

So, in prayer, in choice, in thought and deed you will choose light or you will choose the heavy conditions of this world. It is your choice, for thoughts are powerful, my children. They cling and oppress and bring pain or they can bring liberation and openness and love and joy in conjunction with the soul and God will lead you into light always. God will carry you when the burdens are great. God will show you the way beyond the dilemmas, beyond the worries and cares. But you must desire this, you must choose this. It is your will, this gift of free will, a powerful gift, an important challenge for each soul to choose light over darkness, joy over tears, acknowledging the gifts of the Heavenly Father bestowed upon you each and every day.

Indeed, we understand that this is not easy, for the conditions in this world are powerful and ever present. It is a struggle to choose light often. Yet, the love within your souls, the light within your beings, the help which we provide, and the touch from God which is ever flowing and beckoning, with all of these wonderful gifts and attributes within yourselves, this is not an impossible task. And it is required at this time for you to rise above the conditions of this world, to be an example of light and love wherever you go, for the coming year will be filled with challenges, surprises, blessings and choices. Opportunities abound for each one of you if you are willing to step upon this road in greater light, in strength, commitment and love.

The doors will open and many souls await your choice. And with the choice of love and light you will open the floodgates for many to receive the truth, the love, the blessings of His healing, the touch of comfort and peace. God awaits you, my beloveds, to walk this road which so few take yet is so needed in this world. Are you strong enough, my beloveds? Can you make this choice each and every day? Not as a burden or an obligation but as an invitation and a promise. For if you choose to be in the light in your thoughts and actions, to allow the love to flow through you, compassionate towards yourself and towards others, then your life will truly change and many opportunities will come your way to be that channel of love which God beseeches you to be and your souls desire to be. And the love within you ensures that you will be.

Choose well, my children, choose light, walk in love. Be aware of the choices that you make every day and pray to be guided and assisted upon this path of love and it will be given. Your openness and your willingness and your strength and your commitment will bring about all of these things. And you will be surrounded by the Angels, you will be touched by God, deeply, beautifully, joyously and wondrously.

God bless you, my beloved students. Your teacher Augustine is ever with you, and I am pleased at your progress, the efforts you have made, the love that you share, the light that you reach for, and eager to understand the truth. God bless you, beloved and precious souls. Your teacher Augustine loves you dearly. God bless you.
