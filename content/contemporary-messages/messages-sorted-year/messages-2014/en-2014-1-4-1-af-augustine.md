# It is time to offer Solutions to these great and timeless Dilemmas

January 4<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Menifee, CA, USA

Augustine received by Al Fike

Here is Augustine, Bishop of Hippo. Much guidance will be imparted to you in the coming days, for the time grows short for this work to begin in earnest, and if each of you desire to be guided, to listen and to follow this guidance, it will be given, clearly and succinctly, and it is for you to accept and to walk in faith upon this path, for each has an invitation from Jesus to do this work, to be his disciple, to follow the light that he shines forth for you. There is great suffering in our world - in your world - and it is time to offer solutions to these great and timeless dilemmas, as mankind has made so much of himself and so little of God. It is time to reverse this, and to turn to God, to turn to His loving blessing upon you, to all humanity. Do not be distracted by the material, my children. Do not worry and fret over the complications of your material lives. For as you pray for the Love and receive the guidance, this knot of worry and complexity will unravel and become a thing of peace and beauty. Your lives will change.

That which you desire now you will release, and your soulʼs desires will take precedence, and you will walk a path guided in the light, filled with wondrous journeys, meeting beautiful souls, teaching truth. Do you not desire this? Yes, each soul desires this. And for the most part, you have held yourselves back, for fear of othersʼ judgments, for fear of calamities that will beset you because you dare follow the will of God rather than the will of man. It is time to put aside these fears and to work for God, to turn to God, to have His Love become a constant inflowing into your souls; and in this way, the dilemmas will melt away, the fears will be as smoke rising and dissipating, and your doubts will fall upon the floor, and you will have joyous, faithful, knowing journeys upon this world as your Father guides you and takes you to those far off places, and takes you to those inner places that require the Love and the truth and the healing. Are you brave enough to release your fears? Are you strong enough to allow the Love to flow to every stranger you meet? Are you willing to be an instrument of change in this world and go beyond the limitations of your mental understanding of the truth, and know your true soulʼs understanding and knowing? Do you desire enough within you at-onement with God, and what that means...? will be great change within, and you will not be

able to go back, but only forward in light. This will not happen overnight, my children, but in gradual harmony as you accept this invitation; so the changes will begin, and the understandings will be given, and the guidance will flow. Each one has great potential.

Each one has many gifts. Each one has a powerful purpose in this world. And these gifts, and this purpose, is given to every soul, but cannot be fulfilled without the Love. And you have the Love, my children, and the possibility of your purpose and your gifts to flourish in this world, and make the changes required. The choice is yours, the path is laid out before you. Are you willing? Can you step beyond where you stand today to a place filled with wondrous, beautiful, glorious light? Thank you for listening to us this night, for coming together as you have, and I wish for you to contemplate our words, to pray about these matters, and to seek ever more intensely for the Fatherʼs Love to enter your souls. And we will meet again, my dear students. Continue communicating with one another, sharing your stories and insights, your love and support; and as you do this you will feel our presence, and you will be inspired, and others will be inspired and give you what is required as you give to others, as you are knitted together in Love, as you walk in light, as you aspire to be a channel of Godʼs Love. Much work to do, both within and without, and you have the angels with you, you are deeply blessed and privileged to have this truth; yet this truth is for all. Do not hide this truth, but give, and we will inspire you to speak the words, to give the knowledge, to put forward the invitation to your brothers and sisters. Pray together, my children, pray together. For much is accomplished in this. And love one another. Love. Be the Love. Know the Love. And express the Love. I love you. I am Augustine. I love you, and I am with you often. God bless you and goodnight.
