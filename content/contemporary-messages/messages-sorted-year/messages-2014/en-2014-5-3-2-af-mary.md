# Blessing to the Child within the Womb

May 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

A blessing to the child within the womb. This beautiful soul who comes to this world shall bring light with her, beautiful light, a loving presence. Pray that God’s Love shall enter her soul, for she will receive this blessing with your prayers. God has gifted, gifted you, my dear daughter, with a beautiful soul, and this soul will give you comfort, will bring joy; her vitality and her wisdom will be a gift in your life, to this world. Blessings to you. And God’s hand is upon you, my daughter, and His hand is upon all of you, my children, my dear family. And God’s Will will manifest through each one of you, my children, in accordance to your willingness to allow this manifestation in your lives, to walk in that deep flow of Love, to be truly a child of your Creator, and to have the desire to do His Will in your lives. With this, with this prayer, an expression of your faith, you will change the world, my children, in wonderful ways, in wonderful ways. God bless you. You are loved. Mary loves you dearly, and I am with you often, dear sweet souls. Bless you.
