# God’s Will and Plan are manifesting through you

January 7<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Dr Leslie Stone received by Al Fike

Yes, this is Dr. Stone here. I am touched by your love, your compassion for this dear boy. And I am touched by your efforts to keep the flame alive of this truth in the world. And I do work with you to do so, especially with these gentlemen to my right and left. And there is a project that is emerging that you will collaborate on and there will be many more, for the complementary aspects of your skills and gifts will bring a very acceptable and effective way of bringing truth to many in this world through your writings and your efforts. Yes, there is a great work ahead and each will play their part that will give voracity and credibility to this truth. For this must be approached from many different angles and many different ways, for the thinking in the world is very varied and requires different approaches. And that is why we are working with every individual within this Divine Love movement to develop their gifts, their unique perspectives and in time you will all be working together in harmony in a strong, united front of love and light. And in this way, you will draw to you much attention and curiosity.

Yes, God’s will and plan are manifesting through you, each one, and many others. Continue in your efforts. I will be there, for we all continue to work to bring the fulfilment of God’s plan in this world and in the world of spirit. It’s a great work, a great work and I congratulate you on what you’ve accomplished up till now. But I tell you there is much, much more. Do not be distracted, do not doubt, but walk in faith and in love. You will be successful. Thank you for this opportunity to speak to you, and I have been with you in your conversations and speculations, and in time all will be revealed, your questions will be answered. And yes, there is something beautiful awaiting you in your gathering in the capitol. Bless you. You are all so good, and I am very happy to be part of this. Bless you. May God’s hand always be upon you. And I love you. I am Dr. Stone. Leslie Stone.
