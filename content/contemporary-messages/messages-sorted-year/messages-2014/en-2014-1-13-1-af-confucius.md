# The Key to this Light and Joy is God’s Love

January 13<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

My dear, sweet children, God blesses you with His Love. You are in the light of His presence. It is Confucius and I love you. Yes, I have been in the presence of the dear soul (G) who struggles as his body withers; his soul grows in light and this shall continue. Be at rest.

Be reassured, my children, that this dear soul will progress in light as he makes the transition to the world of Spirit. And the burdens and the pains of the physical body will fall away and he will know peace. For he has grown much in these past months and I have been with him in support and love as have been other Angels, for your prayers, my children, are answered in this way.

Every soul struggles in this world for light, for peace, to know true happiness and joy and the key to this light and joy is God’s Love, my children. To pray for this blessing and to receive this touch from God brings great joy and understanding and knowledge of the true meaning of life and its purpose. For each soul incarnates into this world in order to obtain an identity and this identity stays with the soul for all eternity, my children. So each soul travels the path of life, discovering who they are, the beautiful creation that God has made and as that soul journeys towards God then the discovery unfolds. And as the Love flows into their souls, so this identity and this gift that is their unique being unfolds and flourishes and grows. And upon the divine path, with the blessing of God’s Love, this journey goes for all eternity, my children. And it is towards God and at-onement with the Creator. The blessing of God’s Love brings you to God and the gift of God’s Love in this life brings an opening that eases all transitions and growth and journeys. God’s hand is upon you, my children and guides you upon the way. Pray for this gift. Pray earnestly and pray that God places the desire for this Love in every soul in this world. For in this, the world will change from darkness to light, from disharmony to harmony, from anguish to love and peace.

Blessings to you, my children, beloved souls, peace to those who feel anguish for their loved ones, joy in the knowledge that they step into light, in joyous liberation, a loving embrace from God. I love you, my children, Confucius loves you dearly, each precious soul here. And there are many, many precious souls with you this night, joining you in your prayers, basking in this light. God bless you all.
