# Let His Love flourish within you

December 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, beloveds, it is Confucius. A healing has been given to each soul here, a blessing of love within their souls, a peace surrounding you, my beloveds. Go into the world and know that you are loved that God’s hand is upon you and God’s Love burns within your souls. And walk this world in joy knowing that your souls rejoice in love, that you are cared for, that you are protected and loved and carried by your Heavenly Father, my beloveds. You have indeed the riches of the world within you, all that you require to bring your happiness and your peace, the flourishing of your being, the expression of your gifts, the living of your lives in harmony and peace and love.

Acknowledge this, my beloveds, and those obstructions within your minds, let them go and be with your Heavenly Father in all ways in all of your precious and beautiful beings may you be with your Heavenly Father and live a life in harmony with His laws of love. God will carry you and guide you and show you the way beyond those conditions and attitudes and biases and fears that you carry, that all of mankind carries and burdens them and leads them to darkness instead of light.

Indeed, God beckons you to release these conditions, to let His Love flourish within you, to walk the Divine as He has shown you so clearly, so very clearly. Each breath, each moment is a choice for this. It challenges you to release your burdens and be with your Creator in joyful communion, in the flow of His Love.

This is not a sacrifice, my children, it is a gift, it is a gift of love; it is for all of you, my beloveds, to walk this Earth, expressing this gift in all that you do. I beseech you to walk in light, to live the love, to allow God to use you as His channels of love, as instruments of peace, of change and walk willingly, my children. The desires of your soul carry you forward and into greater light. You have the riches of the world within you, beloveds and may these riches reflect in every part of your being in your lives, as God shows you the path to atonement and love. God bless you. Confucius is with you in his love and his prayers. God bless you. God bless you.
