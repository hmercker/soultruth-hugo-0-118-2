# I would urge my charges to continue in your prayers to seek the Father’s Love

August 6<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

Blessed children of the Father’s Love, it is Augustine your teacher. Yes, the Father blesses you with His Love, it flows into your souls at this very moment, and it carries you into light beyond the darkened conditions of this world into a place of respite and healing and love.

Yes, this instrument and his mate feel somewhat at loose ends, waiting and impatient, and I have told them and have told them many times that they need not feel this impatience, this consternation, but to approach each day as a blessing, to approach each day as an opportunity to serve God, to grow in love, that their souls may expand and grow, and this is, indeed, a time of preparation, and learning and growing. And yes, they will arrive at their destination, within good time. They are not to worry or fret but continue on in faith.

And indeed, many blessings have been given in this time awaiting, this time of preparation. Some insights have formed and greater commitment has ensued from their prayers and their contemplations about the future. Yes, there is no time lost here, but indeed a continuation of the awakening of the soul and the understanding of the mind of what is necessary, what is required, and what is to come in the future. I would urge my charges, all of you, to continue in your prayers to seek the Father’s Love, to ask your questions to God, for they will be answered. God will show you, God will show you in many different ways. And His Love will carry you through all the obstacles, all that which you feel is a burden and a complication and a barrier; God will carry you through, and you will come to know how truly cared for and guided and protected you are, that His Love is present in abundance in all aspects of your lives.

And as you grow to accept this and harmonize with this, then the true work begins, the great purpose unfolds, the job will be taken up. And the work will be mighty, and powerful. And when my charges look back upon these days of simple rest and time within the garden and for prayer, they will be thankful that this time was given, that there was opportunity to continue, to grow, and contemplate. Erase all doubt from your consciousness, my children. Walk in faith and allow God to work His miracles in your lives, in your beings, and all about you, and with this faith comes the mighty river of Love that will heal all, replenish all, and change darkness to light, chaos to harmony, anger and judgment to love and peace.

Bless you dear, beloved souls. God bless you and keep you in His care always. You are precious and loved, beautiful, and beloved. God bless you. Augustine loves you.
