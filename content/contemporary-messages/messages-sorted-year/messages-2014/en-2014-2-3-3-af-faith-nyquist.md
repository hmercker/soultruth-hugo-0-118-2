# The old Man of the House approves of you

February 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2014

UK Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Faith Nyquist received by Al Fike

Well, you see I am Faith, and you see that the old man of the house approves of you, as do many. Yes, you begin your work, and it will fall into place. You are not to worry, but continue in your inquiries and your simple but steadfast efforts to bring this house back to life, to bring this mission back to life in a new way, in a glorious way, in a beautiful way. You are truly my brothers and sisters, and we walk together, and I love you, and I thank you for this day, this day of the beginning of change and light and truth, that shall abide under this roof in this place. Thank you. God bless you. Faith loves you dearly.
