# Pray with all of your Earnestness

January 8<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

I come to be with you, my children. This is Jesus. I come to tell you to go in Peace. As you have gathered together, you have increased the light within your souls; you have made bonds of love that will last for all eternity. You have forged a path for which you will all walk, in times to come. And as you dedicate yourselves with each day to being with God, to knowing the Will of God, to being a channel of God’s Love you will find many opportunities to love.

Nurturing your children, doing acts of kindness for your neighbours, in prayer for others; learning and growing within your souls and many, many more things for you to place as a dedication of your love to God. And in these simple acts and in those efforts more complex, you increase the love within your souls as you make these choices for light. That which halts you from God dissipates, and that which you do brings you closer to God. And in this way, as you pray for Him to put His Love within your souls, it comes in greater abundance as your inner condition, that which surrounds you, is in greater light and harmony.

Pray with all of your earnestness and with all of your desire and your faith for God to put His love in your souls, for in this way, you will see clearly the opportunities laid before you…you will know what is required, you will see the opening so that you may touch another soul and God may touch another soul through you. Yes, you live in a complex world, yet these truths, and this service, and this love is simple …and in these simple ways and in these loving dedications and service will come the salvation of your souls. As you keep your feet firmly on the divine path and pray to the Father for His Divine Love, for His essence. I love you, my children. I love you so much. You are my brothers and sisters, and you shall carry this word of truth, this love into this world that so desperately needs this blessing, this truth.

And as you learn to love, you teach others. As you grow in the love, you touch others. As you are the love, the world responds in love. These are the laws of the love. This is the way of love. This is the power of love. The world is changed by loving acts. The world is changed by the flow of love through you, from God. The world is changed by your prayers, by your beings…as you walk amongst your fellow man. As you confront the error; as you soothe and comfort the lost, as you find those means and ways to do God’s Will in the world. As God provides what you require as you dedicate your lives in loving, faithful, trusting and humble ways to the service of God. And as the doubts fall away and the truth becomes more evident, and the Love glows brighter within your souls then greater works will you do my children—greater works. Clearing mighty paths of light through the darkness and beseeching the souls to follow this light, to be in this light. To KNOW the source of this light….to discover how to truly love. We will always be with you, my dear sweet children. I will lead the way for you…..the way to atonement with God. And we will all rejoice in this atonement and sing our praises and joyfully acknowledge the bounteous gifts from our Heavenly Father. God’s hand is upon you and His light surrounds you and His light burns within your souls. As you leave this place, you will carry this wherever you go. And we will follow you and be with you and support your loving efforts. God Bless you. Your brother Jesus loves you dearly. Loves you dearly…..God bless you.
