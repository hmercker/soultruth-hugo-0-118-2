# Do this Work of Light

February 6<sup>th</sup>, 2014

UK Retreat, Blackpool, UK

Jesus received by Al Fike

...Jesus. I come to bless each child here who is laying down before the Lord our God in loving commitment, in joyous service, to do this work of light, and I come to bless each one in this effort, in the beginning of this great, beautiful work for the Creator. For the doors shall open wide, my children. And the love and light shall flood through you and through this place and bring the children who hunger and thirst for this loving touch, for the healing touch, for the comfort. At this moment it is a bridge over a chasm, and you step upon this bridge to walk to the other side, and you will walk hand in hand, surrounded by the angels, blessed by God. And with each step the barriers will fall, and the understandings and the awarenesses will come clearer and firm, and there will be no doubt or fear or concern, for you will know that you do my work and fulfill God’s plan for mankind. Every venture has humble beginnings and simple faith and joyous commitment, and in these small steps bigger steps will follow; in these small decisions, greater opportunities will unfold. And you will be led, my children, through your prayers. As you ask for guidance it will be given, and there will be no turning back. And there is a great unanimity in your thinking, and all that cannot or will not join you in this will fall away. And for those who have trepidation and concern, this will fall away as you see the unfolding of the Father’s plan. Each step will bring greater awareness, a joyous acknowledgment, an increase in your faith and your love; and God will use you in wondrous ways.

And for this instrument, my brother: do not fear, do not have any concerns, for you have stepped in love, stepped forward, and you know within yourself this is what God wishes for you, and in this all that is required will be provided, and in this a great channel of love will open. There is no need to feel inadequate. This is just the old thinking interfering with the new beginning. Release your fears, my son, be at peace, for look before you - these beautiful souls gather about in loving support, in joyous acknowledgement of this beginning, and each blessed with special gifts to provide the opening door for the work, this wondrous work to begin.

Be joyed, my children, be joyed, and know that God loves you so very, very deeply. Come to know this Love, and in this knowing you will know yourself, and in knowing yourselves, you will know your purpose, and in knowing your purpose, the work shall flow in powerful ways in abundance and love and joy. You cannot fail, my children, for the light within you is strong and your faith is strong, and you will grow in greater awareness, in loving acknowledgement of your Heavenly Father’s Will and plan for you. My beloveds, my beloveds, I love you so, and the angels rejoice at your loving commitment and your efforts to resolve all the barriers in love, all misunderstandings into clarity, and filling every part of your being in this love. Bless you. For every gift you give you are blessed, for every effort you make you are blessed, every time you release that which holds you back, you are blessed mightily, and you grow in the Love in purity and strength and beauty. God bless you, beloved souls. Yes, you will gather many around you. Yes, this is just the beginning. Follow your  guidance, my children. Be sure. And be at peace. I love you. God bless you.
