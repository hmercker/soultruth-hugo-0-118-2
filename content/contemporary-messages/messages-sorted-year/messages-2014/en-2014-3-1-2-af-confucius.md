# Truth comes through Prayer

March 1<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Yes, the souls seek truth, I see. I am Confucius. And truth comes through prayer, and prayer is answered by God, and in God’s answer comes truth. For some of you, you are about to make great changes in your life, to step forth with faith, and I see a little trepidation, but I want to assure you that God will guide you on the path that is meant for you to walk. And there is no turning back now. And you are being prepared and given much to open your gifts, to open the awarenesses, to learn those lessons required to equip you to deal with the conditions of this world, and the many souls who clamour and are filled with despair and confusion and anger and turmoil. For the vast majority of humanity lives in this dreadful state. And as we ask you to move forward into the sea of darkness, we wish for you to be strong and aware and perceptive and loving. For this is not an easy task that you have claimed, my children, yet it is required, for mankind teeters on the brink of greater darkness and turmoil. So many are blind and cannot see. So many turn away from God and blame God for these terrible conditions and are angry and put around themselves many barriers and judgments. And it is for you, my children, as channels of God’s Love to disperse these conditions, these barriers, to enlighten, to be a channel of healing and love, an example for all to see. And yes, there will be responses which you do not anticipate or do not wish to have.

But I tell you, my children, many souls will respond with love as you give love. And your care and your comfort to these souls will change the conditions of this world and change their lives, so that they may be encouraged to seek the light, to let go of their angry vestments and be free of the ignorance and the darkness. It is a great, great undertaking, and armies of angels surround you, my children, in this effort. A great work that is indeed God’s Will, desire, to help heal humanity, to bring greater truth and love, to steer the children away from that dreadful cliff into light.

And yes, you will be guided, you will be guided. Do not fear the future but embrace the Will of God. He will bring you to places of wonderment and light, healing and love, greater than you can imagine. As you place your trust and faith in the Heavenly Father, so that river of love shall carry you, carry you far, carry you where you are needed, carry you in light, bring you to deep and abiding peace and expansive awareness of your Heavenly Father who desires to give you all that you require, all the knowledge, the love, the tender care, the protection, the guidance. It will all be given, as you trust and are open to this mighty flow of God’s Love and Will. And we angels will surround you always, and this instrument’s guidance about the council is correct. It will be as a council for him, giving him the guidance required. It shall be so. All that is required is for him to open his eyes and allow the awareness to flood into his consciousness, for we are there now, and we make this commitment for the remainder of his life upon this planet.

This is God’s gift to you, my son, a blessing indeed to have such wise counsel. You will hear us and see us. Have faith. God bless you, beloved souls. Yes, those who are new to this path yet are sure that they long for this, and indeed they do, shall continue to do so. Blessings to you, my children, and blessings to the elders amongst you, to those that have been steadfast to the truth and love.

Blessings to all. God bless you. Confucius loves you dearly.
