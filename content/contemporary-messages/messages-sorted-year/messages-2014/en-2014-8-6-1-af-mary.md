# God will use you to bring Change and Light and Love in this World

August 6<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

...this is Mary. I’ve come because of the longings of your souls to receive God’s Love, and I’ve come because my family has gathered, and I wish to be in your midst with my love. And I wish to encourage you to long for the Father’s Love, to bring that longing that is deep within you to the forefront of your consciousness, and beseech the Heavenly Father, that He may bless you with a mighty inflowing of His Divine Essence, His Love, that ever waits, that ever waits for each soul who longs for its inflowing; this precious gift, this eternal light that will be yours always and forever more, just for the asking, for that prayer, earnest and full of longing and deep desire to be with your Creator, your Heavenly Father who loves you so.

Yes, this truth is so simple yet so powerful in its manifestations within your being and in your lives and in this world. Continue upon this path, continue to desire to know God, to truly know God in all His magnificence, beauty, and Love. And this pursuit will bring you joy within and great understanding of the meaning of life, the meaning of your creation and who you truly are, this precious soul, created by God. Walk along this path with me, with all the Celestials, with my son, with all who desire to lead you and show you the way beyond the darkened and murky path of this life, into renewed life within, greater understanding, wondrous insight.

And God will use you, my children, God will use you to bring change and light and love in this world. And many times, you are used in this way but are not aware. And this is not necessary for you to be aware of how God uses you. Your efforts to be close to God, this consistent effort, is all that is required for you to be in this flow of love. Many souls await your presence amongst them. Souls far away, souls close by. Many await this nurturing, this love, your channel of love. And in many cases, the love is given wordlessly, simply, through a look in your eyes, a smile on your face, a touch. As you pass by a stranger, as you gaze upon an infant, as you live your daily lives, you are a channel of love if you wish to be so, if you ask to be so, and you walk this path in simplicity, with integrity, and with great honesty.

For to be honest within is required. You cannot fool yourselves, and you certainly cannot fool God, for God knows all. And we in the Celestial Heavens see you clearly and love you dearly, my dear beloveds. I ask my gracious God, my beloved Father to bless you with a great inflowing of His Divine Essence. I ask that we all be within this light, receive His Love, this never-ending Love to flow within each one present: every soul, every mortal, every spirit present here. May the Father’s Love touch that soul, touch that soul deeply, and bring the healing balm that is so required for every soul who is not redeemed by the Father.

And you my dear, dear sweet children, are on that road, that road of complete and utter healing with the Father’s Love and grace. You are close to that place, much closer than many mortals, yet in many ways you do not acknowledge this. You are blind to this, for the ways of the world distract you so. But I tell you there is light amongst you and within you, and this light will grow, and you will indeed travel in the world, bringing this light. God’s hand is upon you, and these blessings will flow forth from you and through you. You are God’s children, His angels on earth, and you will walk in light and be unassailable by the darkness.

And we walk with you always, always, in our prayers, in our loving touch, in our presence. You are always with us, and we are always with you. God bless you, beloveds, God bless you. Mary loves you dearly.
