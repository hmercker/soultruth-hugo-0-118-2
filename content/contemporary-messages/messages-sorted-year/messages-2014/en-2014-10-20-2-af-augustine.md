# With Faith and Trust, God will lead you to surprising Places

October 20<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my beloved students, it is your teacher, Augustine.

Never underestimate the power of God’s Will, my children, for God will enact His Will in this world in many different ways, through many different avenues. And He has beseeched you - many of you - to be a channel of His love in the world, to go forth in loving action, to bring the truth of His Love in tangible ways, to offer your love to those in need, to reach out to the children, and those who are suffering, those who need comfort, those who need material support, those who need healing, those who desire the truth; many avenues in which to be a channel of love and God’s Will in the world.

It is important, my children, not to colour your life with negative thinking, or to project mental expectations, or to walk that road in which you desire recognition and reward, but to be humble and simple in your ways, as God’s instruments. And with this innocence and this openness, with this trust and this faith, God will lead you to surprising places and encounters and opportunities for you to be this channel, required and invited.

Indeed, your mind has many ideas and perceptions of what this means to be a channel of love. But I tell you it is not your mind that has the answers and the understandings of what this is, it is your souls, my children, your souls, which are connected to God with this Love. And in this communion, in this resonance, in this connection, you will know the answers that you seek, you will enact God’s Will in His wondrous plan for the salvation of mankind. Yes indeed, you are eager and desirous to be that channel of love; and of course, you are human and are prone to enact from the mind rather than the soul. But I tell you your challenge, and your ability is to be this channel through soul.

Much awaits you, each one who is willing, who acknowledges the blessing of God’s Love within, who walks the Divine Path in faith and trust and humility and love. God will carry you far, will guide you to far off shores, and bring you to the awarenesses you require to be a powerful, wise and loving channel of God’s Will and Love. Continue in your efforts, continue to walk this path to your Creator, to be open to His gifted Love, and to allow this blessing, this transformative blessing, to enact itself within every aspect of your being and your lives, to be true to this truth, to be in harmony with God’s laws of love, to walk gently but firmly in the light.

In this way, you will truly know what your next steps are, how to proceed and be in that joyous flow of Love that will indeed change this world. And you will be a part of this, an integral part of this, as you walk in truth, as you let go of that which obstructs you in your thinking and your doing; in your prayers, in your desire to live this life fully and joyfully. God’s Love will melt all that is not in harmony with His Love away. And you, my children, know this and live this, and when you falter, does not God pick you up and dust you off, send you upon your way? It is for you to forgive yourselves, to love yourselves, and to acknowledge that your lives are indeed blessed; but the human condition in this world is a difficult place, full of obstructions and negativity and misunderstandings.

And God always loves you, is with you, as are we who come to support you and love you. Blessed children who are eager to serve and be a part of God’s plan, you will indeed find your way, fulfill your commitments, and be a channel of love and healing and truth for many, many souls in this world. Each day brings a new opportunity, each day a new promise, each day a renewed commitment, each day a joyous expression of love. Appreciate what is given, acknowledge the gift of your lives, and love God with all your hearts and all your souls and all your minds. Love God. And in this way, nothing can hold you back, nothing will hold you back. You will be propelled forward into the light. God bless you, beloved students. Your teacher Augustine is with you always, and pleased when there is an opportunity to teach, to bring each one of you to a greater understanding of the truth, a deeper understanding of your true selves, and a closer communion with your Heavenly Father. God bless you and keep you in His light and love. I love you.
