# The Vision of the Centre for Transformational Studies takes Shape

January 31<sup>st</sup>, 2014

UK Retreat, Manchester, UK

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children, it is your teacher, Augustine.

Yes, the vision takes shape for your centre for transformational studies. And within this vision and this place will come an opportunity for many individuals from many cultures and walks of life and perspectives to come and find replenishment, opening of their awarenesses, bringing truth and light and healing to them, so that they may go out into the world and ignite the changes that are required to bring harmony for mankind, this world. Yes, it is difficult to discern and to understand how this can be, how it will come to pass; and I say, through God’s guidance and will, through the ministrations of His angels, through the great work of all those spirits surrounding you, it will come to pass. And this place of light will be one of many, and there will be a great resource for mankind to take the next steps, to reach to a higher place of consciousness and love for one another. And yes, God gathers you together to bring this vision to reality, to bring what you have to give to this light and this place. And that Divine Spark, that Divine Inspiration, the understanding will come, drop by drop, touch by touch, from God. Do not doubt this, but be open to this guidance, and pray for the inspiration and the strength, and most of all, pray for God’s Love to enter your souls, to bring you to a place of knowing and loving and joyous expression of God, God’s Will to this world. You cannot falter with this. You will make steady progress upon the wave of God’s Love.

Bless you my dear, sweet, dear, sweet children. I shall continue to guide you as you make your steps and add your love and light to this vision. Bless you, beloved children. Augustine loves you.
