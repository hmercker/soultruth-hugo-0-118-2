# Love will reign supreme

December 31<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

Precious souls, it is Mary. I have come to be with you in your prayers, in your gathering, for I desire to be close to my beloved, my family on Earth who seek the Father’s Love. And I wish to convey my love to each and every one of you. And know that you do walk in light, that you are in your Heavenly Father’s care, and that the struggles that each of you have, the challenges and the difficulties, will dissipate and love will reign supreme within your being, within your lives; it can only be so because this is your soul’s desire and wish and prayer to be in the light and free of ? And you have travelled on this path for many years and each coming year marks a progress, a milestone.

If you look back, beloveds, do you see how you have grown and progressed, how you’ve opened to God more fully. And your Heavenly Father is no longer a stranger to you, but He is indeed your beloved helper and guide and nurturer and parent and so much more. Continue to pray for His Love, for in this will come all things, all answers, all the healing, peace and all joy. In this comes everything for it is the essence of God that will light the way and bring the healing and show you the truth and open the door to wondrous insights and awarenesses and understanding. Beloveds, I walk with you. I am Mary and I love you, precious, precious… The babe (Ember) is in the light. Her soul already infused with God’s Love. She will continue to grow and be a wonderful channel in this world. A blessing to you all, God bless you, God bless you. Mary loves you.
