# I too struggled upon the World when I walked and brought my Message of Love

January 4<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Menifee, CA, USA

Jesus received by Al Fike

(First words of message were not recorded)...who push back the darkness in their lives, who are a channel of your Love in this world. Father in Heaven, imbue them with your Love, heal them of their worries and cares, free them from the burdens of humanity, that they may walk in joy and peace, wisdom and strength, that they may be your instruments of change in this world to bring light, to bring grace, to bring truth and love.

My dear brothers and sisters, I see your struggles. I see your struggles. And I know your longings. And I too struggled upon the world when I walked and brought my message of Love. Though I was blessed with a pure heart, I knew of the struggles of my brothers and sisters, of my family, of all those around me, and the dilemmas that I faced to be God’s instrument of truth in the world. And I want you to know that God uplifted me always and we will uplift you in your efforts to be a channel of love in the world, to bring truth of love in the world. Do not falter, my children, but be strong in the Love. Walk in this light evermore and bring this simple truth of Godʼs Love wherever you go. And as you live this truth, you will bring this message.

And as those human parts of you that are not in harmony with God’s Love fall away, your lights will shine in brighter glory, a beautiful, beautiful light that will draw many souls to you, my children, and you shall speak the truth, in clarity, simplicity, and beauty. The time is now, my children, to walk boldly in this world, guided by your Heavenly Father. The time is now to release your fears, to walk in truth always, and we in the Celestial Heavens will walk with you always in your efforts to clear away the darkness, the ignorance, the deprivation, the lack of love. I have called you, my children, and I call you again, to do this work that was begun many, many years ago and shall continue in this world, in the world of spirit.

And God will grant you the means and the ways, the path to be followed, the choices to be made, the visions and the inspiration, the love and the glory, that will be a gift, a door opening, a path to follow, for each one is unique, each one suited to your purpose in this world. Let go of your judgments, let go of your fears, and walk knowing that God’s hand is upon you, my children, and His Love flows into your souls, and the angels uphold you, and the life, the living waters of His Love flow through you and about you and within you, opening your souls and the inherent faculties of your souls, and you will see the world differently, my children. You will see your fellow man differently, and in this seeing, and in this knowing you will teach and heal, you will love with such depth that none will turn away from you. The world hungers for this, the world is hungry to know the truth that God loves them, that they are loved, and that this Love is worth pursuing and receiving. So many do not know, so many live in darkness and ignorance. And they fill their lives with material pursuits that cannot fill this hunger, that cannot give the nourishment that they so desperately need. And you, my children, know this truth. God has fed your souls, and with this blessing comes a responsibility to be in this world in loving ways, in ways of truth, in ways that do not compromise this light, but enhance and bring forth this light of truth and love. The time is now to step forward boldly, to be God’s instruments in this world, to find your place within this plan of salvation for mankind, for each does have a role to play. Pray that God will reveal your purpose, my children, and open the way for this purpose to be expressed in the world.

And in your earnest prayers, and your receiving of more of this Love, will come the answers that you seek, the openings that you require, the understanding that must come to your consciousness, and the mighty flourishing of your souls in Love manifesting, manifesting light and miracles and grace. Will you follow me, my children?

For I wish to lead you through the darkness of this world into lighted pastures, joyous beauty, places filled with light and glorious Love. Will you follow me? I will hold your hand. I will lead you. I will nurture you. And the angels will bring you to the light in many ways. And in this you will bring many, many souls to this light. Yes, for many of you it is difficult to be different, to walk this path that is so different from many others.

Yet you have come to this truth through your soul’s desire, and this desire is strong, and it will bring you to greater truth, greater love, and as you open, and come to truly understand yourselves and God’s nature, and the beautiful creation that you are, then you will be joyful that you have walked this path, that you are redeemed by the Father.

And in this joy, in your acknowledgment, you will touch many souls and give the opportunity for them to experience what you have experienced, to know what you know, to gather God’s Love within them. Do not doubt this, my children, but walk in faith, and know you are loved, truly loved. I am Jesus, Master of the Celestial Heavens, your brother, and I love you, and I will always be with you. I love you so. God bless you.

Continue with your prayers, continue with your efforts to bring the truth and the light, the love and the peace into your world. So few of you on this world. Gather together in strength and harmony, in loving devotion, to support one another, to coalesce into an army for truth and love. You may march upon this world in a river of God’s Love. The time is now. You are so desperately needed. Bless you, my children, my brothers, my sisters. Bless you. And may you be enfolded in Godʼs loving arms and gifted with that which you require to be the mighty channel of love you are meant to be. Thank you for listening to me. Thank you for loving God. Thank you for loving one another. You are dear and precious to me and you will not falter. You have the entire Celestial Heavens supporting you. God’s mighty hand upon you. No greater work is there than this, my children. None more powerful, more sacred, more beautiful. Do not turn away but turn towards the light and be my disciples of truth. Bless you.
