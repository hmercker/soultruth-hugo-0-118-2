# The great Goal is the Salvation of Mankind

January 27<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Andrew received by Al Fike

I see that you are all stepping forward into the world, and many wish to align themselves with you, and some wish to tear you down. There are some petty jealousies and some falseness and some difficulties to maneuver as you make your next steps in the world, and I would encourage you to not pay these distractions any attention. Do not give credence to those who would criticize, that lay fault with you. For as you step forward into the light, my friends, negative forces will indeed try to extinguish this light and many who harbour disharmonious feelings and judgments within may be used by negative forces to try to undermine or sow doubt within you.

My dear son, this instrument, is struggling with this at this time and I have encouraged him to listen to his guidance and to be sure of his next steps, for they are clearly laid out for him to step into this role of leadership with humility and grace, knowing that God will guide you and will guide all of you to the next steps that are required on this wondrous journey, this great adventure. Because the great goal is the salvation of mankind, to bring these truths to the world, to show this Love, to ignite a passion within every soul to go to God to receive this great blessing and thereby heal this world of so many, so much darkness and so many dilemmas and difficulties.

Of course, the choice is always yours. *"Shall I make a comfortable life, praying for God’s Love and insulating myself from the conditions of this world so that I may not look at what is so desperately needed to be done in this world, or will I step forward bravely in my trust and dedication in God’s Will and be guided into the world as a channel of His Love and Truth?"* Yes, I think within each one of you lies this choice and I am sure part of you wishes the comfortable life, the easy road. Yet God compels you further, from that comfortable place to a place more challenging yet filled with promise, and giving and loving in this world.

You are all strong, you can take the challenge. You are all gifted, beautiful souls, and you have been nurtured for many years in the truths and taught and healed and loved and shown the way. And now the time has come to walk through that door, to joyfully accept the invitation and to express fully your capacity to love, to bring love and comfort, healing and truth to that darkened world out there that is in such desperate need, in such turmoil and deprivation. And I believe within each one, there is a desire to take this challenge. And this does create discomfort within as you wrestle with the meaning of this, with the possibilities of this and your worry and your own comforts and security.

And yet God has promised you that He will provide for you and protect you and love you and within your souls you are well aware of this, but your minds still doubt and fret. Let this go; you are wholly in the flow of God’s Love and Will and allow this to carry you to many surprising places within yourselves and within the world. A great journey awaits each of you. All it takes is your willingness to wade within that river and be carried. Allow it to carry you and it will indeed be a great adventure and of course, there will be challenges, as this life in this world is full of challenges. But there will be a deep and wondrous fulfillment and knowing that you have followed your souls’ purpose and desire to serve God. What greater work can there be, what more wonderful journey and service can there be? And when the time comes at the end of this life in this world, you will look back with joy and satisfaction that you have been true to yourselves and true to God’s Will.

I love you my friends and am joyed at the prospects of the coming years, of the dynamic events that will take place through your commitment and service to God and the many, many souls that will be touched with light and truth through this commitment and service. Be joyed at these wondrous prospects and be at peace, be at peace and know that God will guide you in harmonious ways. There is nothing to fear and such great love to bring, to know, to be, in this world. God bless you. I love you.
