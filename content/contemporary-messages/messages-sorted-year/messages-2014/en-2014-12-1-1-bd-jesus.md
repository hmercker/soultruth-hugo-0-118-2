# Blessings

December 1<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

My precious ones, you have been blessed with God’s Divine Love…it pours upon you in great abundance and it flows to all the lives you touch. You are precious and beloved and as I have said many times, you are deeply, deeply needed in your world. Precious and beloved souls, I am Jesus.
