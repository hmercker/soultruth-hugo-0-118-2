# There are many Ears who will not hear, yet their Souls will feel the clarion call to come to God in this Way

December 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, most precious and beloved souls, it is your teacher, Augustine. I want to pray with you and speak with you. Yes, you’ve travelled from near and far, my beloveds, over waters and crossings, roads and byways. The yearnings of your souls bring you together, yearnings of fellowship, of receiving the blessings of the Father’s Love together. And how many in this world have this opportunity to come together with the full knowledge of the Father’s Love and His blessing, very few, my beloveds, very few.

And each of you carries a precious gift and a beautiful light and when you come together in this way you add your light and your gifts and your beings into a beautiful circle of light. And in your prayers, in your togetherness and in your longings, you bring a light to this world and with all of our efforts and the blessings of God on all, you bring this flow of light and love to many, to the many souls whom you love, to the many souls who are in need at this time, those in need of healing and comfort.

You act as God’s instruments of light as you come together as you do and each soul present here has a purpose, is needed and all that is required is your desire to be a channel of God’s Love in this world to receive His gracious blessing of the Divine Love to all your souls, this wondrous bliss which will transform you, my beloveds. This is the message the Master Jesus gave to the world and you sustain this truth with your prayers and your efforts and think back upon this year, beloveds, where you have been, how you have been in this world, the travels, the souls you have met.

This is God’s doing, my beloveds.  You will continue and this work will expand and you will support one another in these efforts for light, for healing to the world, for God’s Love to the hearts of many. This message is so simple yet powerful. And there are many ears who will not hear, yet their souls will feel the clarion call to come to God in this way and there will be an opening and light will flow in the souls eager, imploring God to bring them peace.

How blessed you are, beloveds, to do this work, to know these truths, and to listen to God’s guidance. And this will become clearer as you progress upon your road. And beautiful blessings will manifest in your lives in a multitude of ways. Be open and receptive, my beloveds, to God’s guidance, to God’s touch in your lives. Be humble, be true to yourselves, (hear?) your soul’s desires and let all that which is not in harmony with love fall away from you in your thoughts, in your feelings.

And if this is difficult for you, my beloveds, ask your Heavenly Father to touch your soul so this may be a time of peace and healing and light. Precious and beloveds as you encounter so many at this time of year ask for us to be with you. Ask for God’s channel of love to flow through you, touch the souls of those who are close to you, bring the truth wherever you can and this may not necessarily be in words but in deeds, in words of comfort, in love. Blessings to you, precious souls, and may His Love work deeply within you, healing all those places that need this touch, this blessing. God bless you. Your teacher Augustine loves you dearly and I am with you often. God bless you.
