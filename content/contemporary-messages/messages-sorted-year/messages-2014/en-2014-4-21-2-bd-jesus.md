# Each of you are needed and loved

April 21<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

God bless you, my precious souls. You are deeply needed, beloveds, deeply needed in your world to bring the peace and joy and understanding to all the lives that you may touch. Know this, my beloveds. Seek the power of the Divine Love to guide you and God will lead you. Each of you has a deep purpose and you are needed in your world to give the Love and let it flow from you to touch all the lives you touch. You will bring this peace and Love that is so longed for in your troubled world. Each of you, my precious ones, are needed and loved. God will guide you. Yes, my precious ones, God will guide you. Continue on this path of Divine Love and you will open the doors to many lost souls who struggle to live. Precious and beloveds, precious and beloveds, know this and bring this peace into your world and save many lives, my precious ones. God bless you and you must know this. God bless you. You are treasures with a deep purpose beloveds, know this.

A blessing of love, and a deepening understanding of the Peace and Love. Acceptance flows into your lives, my beloveds. You are blessed to know this.

Each of you has a blessing. You have received a blessing from God, your Creator. Know this, my beloveds. Let this grow within you. For you are needed in your world to bring the Peace, Love, acceptance and happiness to all the lives in your world. I am Jesus and I uphold you. I will always uphold you, my beloveds.
