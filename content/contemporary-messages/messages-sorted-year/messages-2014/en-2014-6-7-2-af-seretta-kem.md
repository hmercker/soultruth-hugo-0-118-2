# In your Prayers lay down your Fears, your Doubts

June 7<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Seretta Kem received by Al Fike

God bless you, I am Seretta Kem and I come to tell you that each one here has received a gift to help clear the obstructions to receiving the Love in your souls. For some of you, you acknowledge that deep desire within to receive this Love and it is a burning desire, a compelling desire. And for others, it is more difficult to acknowledge this because we acknowledge that each of you have busy lives and great distractions within those lives, and therefore, those times of prayer may become sparse or are not well disciplined or regular, and this is because of the difficulties within of the human condition which all souls live upon this world; many great distractions.

Yet this gift, if you choose to utilize what has been given, will help you to acknowledge the deep desire within for it does reside within each one of you and it will help to reinforce your choice and your efforts to be close to God. God gives His children many gifts to help them come close to Him. These many blessings are there for the asking. His Love is there for the asking.

So, my children, I would encourage you to in your prayers lay down your fears, your doubts, your pain and the ambivalence which is so much a part of the human condition. And step forward in faith and ask God to gift you with all the blessings that await. Open yourselves. Breathe in the Love. Know this deep peace, the peace that passes all understanding. And free yourselves from the shackles of your minds and know the great expansion that comes within your soul with this Love. You can only benefit from this. It is not difficult. But it does require for you to focus and to allow this loving gift to enter.

There are many Angels here this night helping each one of you. One stands behind and one stands in front for each one and there are prayers said for your continued progression. And a great light is above you and many, many spirits surround you, many. And as you get together in this way, you not only benefit yourselves, but you benefit many others, those in the flesh and those who are not. Each are (…) through your prayers, your efforts and through God’s blessing a great light that can be used in the world to change the conditions from darkness to light which will then affect the souls who will then yearn for greater light and more truth and relief from the dreadful conditions that seem to permeate everything in your planet and bring difficulties, unnecessary and undesirable.

For God wants all His children to be in joyous light and peace and to live a life filled with light, filled with joy, filled with love. God bless you, my dear friends. Walk a little further upon the path, the infinite path. And I am joyed at your progress, that you desire to continue and we will continue with you always as you seek light and truth. God bless you. I love you.
