# Every Desire bring to God

May 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

God bless you, my dear, dear children. May our Heavenly Father’s Love flow into your souls in great abundance and bring you the peace, the awarenesses, the strength within your souls that you require to do our Father’s Will in this world. For you are needed, my children, you are greatly needed in this world - a place filled with turmoil and pain and error, and in need of great healing and peace. And you come to this oasis of light, this place of healing and nurturance, to pray for the Father’s Love, to find refuge from the cares of your world, to walk in light and light upon this world, to bring this light with you, to be God’s channels of Love.

My children, continue to pray for God’s Love with every breath; every desire bring to God: that you may be blessed, that you may be upheld, that you may walk the Path Divine unimpeded and in joyous light. The door opens for you, my children, the door opens wide, and you are beckoned to come forth in this light, to serve God as His channels of Love, to seek the truth, to live the truth, to be God’s instruments of change in this world.

And yet, parts of you are reluctant and fearful. You cannot see clearly, for your minds are not completely convinced of what your souls already know. Yet you struggle with this, this great struggle of awareness and faith, of love and fear; for you are human, and every soul struggles in this way and tries to be in the light, to be free of these chains of doubt and fear, anxiety and error. And you, my children, have struggled greatly, and have made great progress to free yourselves from these impediments. And in your struggles in this great, great journey, you have learned much, and you have been a great example for many. For my children, you are always watched, if not by those in the material, many in spirit watch as you progress and struggle upon your path and try to live the truth of God’s Love with each moment in your day.

And this does indeed have an impact in many ways, on many levels of existence, and many souls, curious, watch, observe and understand through your struggles and your attempts to find truth. And many who are connected to you in relationships and through family who have passed upon this side of life are with you, my children, many are with you; as you make this journey, you make this journey for them, for you open the door of light that they may also walk through and find the great comfort in God’s Love.

So, my children, your journey has great impact, a great effect on all around you. So many whom you do not even know exist in your lives as they in spirit crowd around, attracted by the light, curious. For I say many souls in this world do not possess this light - a great many. And you, my children, do walk in this light, and in this way become a beacon, a channel, an example of truth. And many of you doubt this, but it is so.

God has blessed you, my children, rejoice in this, now that you walk in His Love and light, that you are an example for many. And in this struggle, in this effort, in this journey, you lead many souls to light, to love, to healing. And as you journey around the globe, you will bring this light with you, in your humble demeanour, your loving ways, in your prayerful and earnest connections with the Creator. You bring this light, and this light touches the souls of many as God uses you as His channel in the world.

Bless you, my children. Bless you. I am John the Beloved. The Master stands within your midst, blessing each one. You step in a great tower of light, and this light reverberates throughout this world. Your prayers have brought this blessing. You are a part of God’s effort, His plan to bring this world to light and peace and harmony. And as you make your efforts closer to the Father, you will come to know this more clearly and the light will intensify, and the channel will be great, and many, many manifestations of God’s beautiful gifts to mankind will come through this group and others who will join you in this great effort to heal your world, to bring light, to bring light. Bless you, my children, bless you. I love you so, as do so many. Continue in these efforts, in your prayers, your desire to be close to God. God bless you.
