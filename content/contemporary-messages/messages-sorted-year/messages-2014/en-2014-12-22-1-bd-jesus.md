# My Beloveds, you are needed in this World

December 22<sup>nd</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

God bless you, precious souls, precious souls with a deep purpose in your troubled world. I am Jesus and you my precious, beloveds have a deep purpose in your world. Yes, a deep purpose. Bring the peace and love that flows through you, my beloveds. Let it flow deeply. Yes, many need your touch and the power of your love that you carry within yourselves, within your own souls. Yes, this is deeply needed in your world. So many beloveds are lost and suffer to be so lost. The Divine Love is flowing and each of you is receiving this gift, and you must continue to let it flow from you. My beloveds, you are needed in this world and each of you carries a deep purpose. Continue upon the Divine path. You are precious and beloveds and you are deeply needed to bring this peace and love into your world. I am Jesus and you are beloved who are deeply needed, deeply needed. Each of you has been touched by an Angel. Yes, my precious, feel this and let this flow from you as you continue with your lives to bring this love, this power of Divine Love to all.

You are each being blessed (with) Divine Truth and happiness and love and each of you, let this flow from you, my beloveds, let this flow from you to save the lost souls. Beloveds, you are beloved souls and you have a deep purpose to bring the Divine Love to all the lives you touch. In this world (it will?) deepen, my precious, beloveds, yes, it will deepen.

Each of you has received a gift of deepening Divine Love and you will carry this through your lives and you all have a deep purpose to bring love, God’s Divine Love, into your world. Keep (channelling?) to the lost souls. You are blessed, blessed, beloved souls and this will deepen into your souls, my precious beloveds. I am Jesus and I shall always, always uphold you, precious, beloveds. (There?) is a deepening power of God’s Love.
