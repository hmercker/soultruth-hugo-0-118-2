# God uses many Avenues to bring Truth and Light into the World

December 15<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

James Padgett received by Al Fike

God bless you, it is James Padgett here. I have been with this instrument as he has been writing his book and I wish to reassure him that the information he puts down on paper is correct and he has been guided and influenced by we in spirit who are encouraging him to write this. He has a gift to bring these truths and the stories he is attempting to tell in ways which many will understand and appreciate. And he is meant to write, it is his gift.

There will be many such writings and books which will be a wonderful expression of truth and love for the world. I want to encourage him and congratulate him on his choice to pursue this venture and I will assist him in any way that I can. There are many spirits who wish to encourage and influence him as he captures the words and puts them within his machine.

God uses many avenues to bring truth and light into the world. He uses your gifts; he uses the wonderful light within your souls. You all have many things to give to the world. You are all very capable and your intentions are for love, and you all have a deep compassion for those who suffer, who are lost. Express your gifts, beloveds. Be a channel of God’s Love in whatever you do, seek for the highest and know that God will lift you up to that place of greater vision and clearer truth and higher love.

And we are always joyed when you reach and struggle for this and in our joyous, closeness with you, we love you and support you and guide you. God bless you, beautiful souls. You will each find your purpose. You will fulfill this and know great joy in the doing and the expression of your magical souls. May you journey to that place quickly and find fulfillment that awaits you. God is eager for you to reach this destination as are we. God bless you, beloved souls. You are truly loved. God bless you.
