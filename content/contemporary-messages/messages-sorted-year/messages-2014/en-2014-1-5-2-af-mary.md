# God increases the Fellowship, the Love, the Bonds

January 5<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Mary received by Al Fike

...Mary. God bless you. The beautiful blessing of God to bring you together. Is this not miraculous? For each leads their lives; different lives, different places upon your world, yet God brings you together, provides the means for you to be together, and increases the fellowship, the love, the bonds; that you may feel that you are connected to one another in this fellowship, in this great effort to bring light into the world, the light of God’s Love, the truth of God’s Love. God is weaving you all together, my children, weaving you all together in a beautiful tapestry that shall shine forth in all its grace and power and beauty and truth, that you may work together in this world and bring the truth that is so desperately needed. Such precious souls, such earnest longing and desire to do God’s work, and each has forged their path to God, and struggled for the light, and searched for the truth, and longed to God within their souls to receive this Love. And indeed, in these efforts, in your experiences, you will have the means and the knowledge and the wisdom to help many souls in this world. For each perspective is powerful in what may be conveyed to those who feel close in rapport and harmony with the personalities around this circle and the many who are connected to this circle. For each gives something; each gift adds more to this effort - God’s will and plan for the salvation of mankind. We love you so, because we see the light within you, my children, and the wonderful gifts that you possess, and the purity within your souls to receive this Love.

And God will strengthen you, and teach you, and love you, so that you may do this work in glorious, unfolding light, in graceful, harmonious ways. And yes, we will surround you, and guide you, and be with you, and answer your prayers, and help you to be that channel of love in the world that will bring change, that will touch many souls, that will gather those dear and precious souls who desire to be with God in this Love, and a channel of God’s Love in this world. The work begins, my dear, dear children. It begins in earnest. There is so much to do, and as you use your gifts of writing, as you go about your daily lives and touch the souls around you, as you sing your praises to God, as you send your prayers to God, as you attempt to dig for more truth and more wisdom and more love, so the work will manifest in your lives in beautiful ways, surprising ways.

Listen to God’s guidance, my children. This still whisper will bring you the truth that you require, the guidance that awaits. And God will set you on the path that is meant for you and equip you with what you require to fulfill your purpose in this world. Your faith is strong, my children, your desire and intentions are pure and the Love glows within your souls. May God uphold you with every step and every breath, and you will journey in this light and love, and fulfill this purpose imbued within each one to bring the light and love and truth into this world in your own unique ways, using your own beautiful gifts.

All are equally loved, all are equally beautiful, and each has great potential. And through your willingness and desire to be with God and to serve God, you will change the world, both within and around; in far-off places, and within your homes. This is the beauty and the miracle of God’s Love, its transformative nature, its powerful healing, its ability to bring harmony and to imbue the light into this world. Yes, we are pleased that you are together. We are so very happy that there are children in this world who are willing to walk the Diving Path, who are strong enough to resist the distractions in this world, and who desire truth above all else. You will forge the path for many, my children, you will forge the path for many. And as you walk in your faith, and in your humble service, many will follow and learn how to truly love. Continue with your prayers. Be prayerful, my children. With this connection and rapport and communion with God all will come, in due time, and will unfold in beautiful, gracious harmony. God bless you. Mary loves you, and I am with you often.
