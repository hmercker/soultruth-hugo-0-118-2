# Ask God to make you an Instrument of His Will

September 20<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Augustine received by Al Fike

God bless you, my students, your teacher Augustine. Upon returning from your journeys you see much of the blessings you have available to you in your own lives. The strength that you carry within your souls, the understanding clear within your mind. Yet you shared your love with your brothers and sisters and many of these dear souls have struggled to stay upon the Divine Path, to understand what is required for this, for this journey to God. Many lack the support. Many lack the soul awareness which you, my children, have within your midst and carry with you wherever you go.

And indeed, you must see clearly that God has blessed you mightily in this regard. He has brought you together to support one another in a loving fellowship complete with many gifts, many insights, many opportunities for growth and support that is given to you as an individual and within the groups that you have participated in and shall continue to do so as you continue on this journey. And God will draw in many into your midst and to your orbit.

And it is time, my children, to be those teachers, those channels of love, to use your soul wisdom and your soul understanding to help others, to bring these gifts forward in the flow of God’s Love and Will, to support your brothers and sisters and all who come thirsting for truth and love, light and peace. All you need to do, my children, is step out your door and God will bring upon your path those hungering souls and in many cases, you have no need to proselytize, it is the flow of love, the healing, the touch that comes through you to these blessed souls that is required. Opportunities are before you everywhere you go. And in many cases, you are not aware of how God uses you to bring light to another soul, to bring healing, comfort, love.

And as you make your journey to God, this work will expand, this work will intensify, and your instrumentality, your gifts will flow, in unexpected ways, down unexpected roads to people, souls you are yet to meet and places you do not know exist. Many souls await you, my children, many souls await the work which God has planned for you. Do not ever doubt this but be joyed at the opportunities, the possibilities which lay before you. This is all possible because you have prayed and received the greatest gift of God’s Love and in this dedication of your faith, in this acceptance of truth, in your willingness to listen to God’s Will a great door opens for light and for many years we have been with you, my children, and we have prepared you for this and we have done great work to bring the possibilities of your instrumentality to light, to fruition.

Now it is merely the case of you accepting the invitation for the groundwork has been laid, the progress has been made within, the strength of your souls bring great light. So, I ask you, my children, ask God to make you an instrument of His Will. Ask God that He may make you a clear channel of His Love. Ask God to guide you. And in these prayers, in the acknowledgement of your true longings, in the unfolding of the wondrous purpose that God has put within each of your souls you will find your way, you will fulfill God’s wishes to bring love wherever you go.

Blessed are the peacemakers. Blessed are those who bring change into this world. Blessed are the humble. Blessed are the wise. Blessed are those who acknowledge and love God and in so doing are a channel of His Love. You are blessed, my children. You are blessed. Continue to seek the Love and express the Love. You will always have God by your side and the Angels upholding you. And this flow of love will carry you far. Beloveds, I love you. And together, you carry a deep purpose, my children, a deep purpose. God bless you.
