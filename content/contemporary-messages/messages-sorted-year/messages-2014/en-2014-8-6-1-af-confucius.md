# To come to Truth, to come to Clarity is not easy

August 6<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Confucius is here. Bless children, beautiful souls. Yes, my charge is anxious and worried and feels he will lose face if his plans do not materialize soon (laughs), but I tell you, if God’s plan unfolded according to his will and his concepts, it would be a circular route, my friends. There would be no change at all. Yes, mortals have limited vision and understanding, and your souls grow, my children, your souls grow, but your vision still is weak, through a glass darkly, not seeing clearly, and this is why you must pray and pray and pray to come closer to God, for the closer you are, the clearer is the vision and the understanding of truth and His beloved plan for the salvation of mankind.

We are all a part of this, and I do not mean to denigrate my dear charge, for he is earnest, and he tries mightily to be a good and clear channel of the Father’s will. And for this he is well loved by the angels, and there is much light in his life. But with humility and faith will come his true purpose and the unfolding of God’s plan in his life, as it will with you, my children. Indeed, he does forge a path for all of you, and you will all in time be a part of this great work with great commitment and strength and understanding. That is if you choose this, for God does not interfere with your free will, nor do we. It must be your desire. And I see my charge has come to know his deep desire to serve God in this short time and this is a valuable insight and a powerful step in his instrumentality.

To come to truth, to come to clarity is not easy, my children, as you live in this world, it is always a struggle. And often the lessons learned are difficult, often the barriers overcome feel insurmountable, and your feelings of anxiety and fear and alone at times and cut off from so many who do not understand. My children, we understand your struggles, understand your loneliness; we understand so much of this struggling, as we too struggled while on earth. And I need not tell you that God is always there for you, as are we. And we encourage you and inspire you and pray for you as you surmount these barriers and put aside those feelings which do not bring you close to God.

Indeed, faith is a powerful tool to wipe away your own inner doubts and insecurities. And God’s Love is the catalyst that will expunge these conditions within you forever. As this Love flows within you, so the doubts and the hurts, the wounds and pains, the feeling of loneliness that is within every soul that does not know God, will fall away and in its place will be a glorious, joyful acknowledgment of the truth of God’s existence and the truth that you are loved beyond measure, precious beyond price, beautiful beyond description, each one, each soul, beautiful.

So, my two little birds will fly away soon. Is it not a blessing that they are with you tonight? And it is a blessing for them to have this time with God’s beautiful sun shining upon them, to reside in this place of beauty, fresh and clean, full of life. God bless you, beloveds. God bless you. May His care always be with you and about you, and His Love find a constant inflowing into your souls. Blessed are you, my beloveds. God bless you.
