# To walk the Path of Truth

January 8<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, I am Confucius. Do you not come away from your gathering refreshed? And renewed dedication I hear from you all, to walk the path of truth, to be in God’s Love for all eternity, my children. And as you fly away from this little nest know that we are so very pleased with your efforts and your re-dedications and your conversations as you forged these bonds of love; so that although you are at some distance from one another you are always close. And in these bonds will come wondrous works, opening doors to do God’s will in the world. No matter where you are, you are always together.

Remember this. And as one steps forward, you all step forward. As one does a work of light, you all are a part of this, and support one another in loving ways and encouragement and prayers. And are you not well blessed by having these bonds of love as God has brought you together, and will bring others within your fold, strengthening this family of love, adding their gifts to your gifts, you adding your gifts to their gifts. Blessings to you. Confucius loves you and bids you good journeys; God’s loving protection around you, my children, as His breath fills your wings. Blessings to you.
