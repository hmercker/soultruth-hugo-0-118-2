# Be at Peace and release your Fears and your Judgments

January 27<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Mary received by Al Fike

My children, each one, such beautiful souls, so deeply committed to God, to being a channel of God’s Love... and you desire to be with God, to know God’s Will, to be a channel of God’s Love in this world. And many of you step upon the brink of great adventure, great opportunity to do God’s Will in this world. For some this brings fear and discomfort to step out of your comfort zone and place of routine and familiarity to something new and different.

And I tell you, do not worry, my children, do not worry or fret or feel that God will abandon you, for God will not, nor will His angels turn away from you; but in this loving commitment, in this stepping forward, you will feel a greater closeness to God and you will know the presence of Divine Angels to support and guide you, my children. For as you live more closely in the alignment with God’s Will, so this Love and light and flow will envelop you more intensely. You will walk in light, my children, and as one steps forward, you all step forward and each has a role to play. Each has a purpose in this world, and as this manifests, as your souls grow, so each gift, each child will work in harmony with the other, to bring about God’s Will in this world.

As you listen to your guidance, as you walk upon God’s Divine Path, as you pray for His Love to enter your soul, you will see clearly what is meant for you, the role you are to play and the channel of Love within you will grow and grow and the steps will become easier and the discomfort will lessen as you acknowledge your place in this flow. Be at peace my children, be at peace and release your fears and your judgments, your speculations and your discomfort as you walk as a child of God, an instrument of God’s Will in this world, harmony will be yours, all will fall into place with each soul in harmony and grace.

Bless you in your prayers, bless your loved ones; bless you in your dedication and your sincerity, in your love for God and your love for one another. Bless you. It is truly beautiful to behold and the angels acknowledge your efforts to be at one to accomplish your mission.
