# All that you have experienced in your Lives will be used by God to help others

April 21<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

Beautiful souls who long for Light and Love to walk the Path Divine, I am John, John the Beloved, and I wish to tell you that each soul here has been touched by God’s Love and is beginning to awaken to this precious gift and all that unfolds with this touch of Love within. And some have travelled the path for many years and some just begin, a glimmer of awakening, a rising of hope from within, that they too are loved, they too may be with God in peace and harmony and acknowledgment of their gracious being and creation. You will walk in light, my children, as you long for this gift of Love; you will change deeply from within as you come to know your Heavenly Father who longs to touch your soul as you long to be touched by this gift of God’s Essence. And in time, you will walk the earth as a channel of His Love, you will know His Will and your life will unfold as an adventure, each day unique and filled with opportunities to be a channel of Love and healing, to touch another soul, to give comfort to the vulnerable and the lonely and the desperate.

There is so much work, my children, to do in this world, so many long to be healed and to be loved. They long for what you have and for what many of you know with such surety, that you are loved, that God does guide you, that you walk a path filled with light, filled with life, filled each day with the opportunity to reach further into that awareness of Love and God’s Presence and all that this entails.

Be joyed, my dear, dear beloved children, for what you know, for what you have been given, and for the many blessings that have manifested in your lives. And continue to long for God’s Love, continue to find ways to express this Love in every aspect of your lives, in every aspect of your beings, for in this, you will know great joy; in this, there will be great healing from within and you will be a channel for great healing for others. And all that you have experienced in your lives will be used by God to help others, for through your pain you have great empathy for others, through your struggles, you understand the struggles of mankind, and through your efforts to reach for Light you understand the way out of the darkness into the Light.

Each one here has many gifts, many potentials that God will use if you desire this to help others in the world. For the world desperately needs channels of Love, souls like yourselves to dedicate themselves to the service of healing and touching and expressing Love to mankind. For God shall reach those that are in need through you. And as you walk that path of Love you will learn how to be that channel of Love, will grow and expand and flow deeply and beautifully and powerfully.

Be the Love my children, be the Love. Walk in Light and know that you are loved beyond measure, beyond understanding. You are loved as deeply as the oceans are deep, broadly as the universe is broad, and you will come to know this Love as you journey for all eternity, to God. God bless you, my beloved, beloved brothers and sisters. I pray with you and I love you and I am with you. God bless you.
