# Blessings

December 31<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

You are each beloved souls, blessed. I am Jesus and you, beloved, are bringing Divine Love to all the souls you touch. It flows from you, my precious ones. (It is healing?) to all from the Divine Love, yes this flows from you as well. Each of you have a deep purpose to let this Love deepen and bring peace and joy, happiness and love. It is so deeply needed in your world….. the power that you carry within your souls, love and light, it will bring you deeply.. peace and healing.

You will continue. Each of you will save many lives, you have already but you do not become aware. You have a deep purpose, you have a deep, deep purpose, a deep love…. yes my precious souls… you are deeply loved…. Know this… Let your light shine? It is a blessing from God to you
