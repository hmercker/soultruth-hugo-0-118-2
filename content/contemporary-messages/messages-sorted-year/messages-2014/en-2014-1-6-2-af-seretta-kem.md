And you will feel the benefit as you rise in the morning. Blessed souls, you long for the Love, you long to be different, you long to be at one with God. And through these longings, and the answering to your prayers, come many blessings in many different ways, and many opportunities to grow in the Love is in your souls. And in this loving bond you will work together, and much will be accomplished through this effort and prayer and love bond and fellowship. And the misconceptions will fall away, and clarity will ensue as the Love lights the way and brings you greater understanding. Blessed children, continue in your prayers and we will continue to minister and give what is required to solidify and strengthen these bonds, and the power of this group, and we shall draw others, many others, as time goes on, and God’s plan unfolds. There will be many to meet, beautiful souls, and many who hunger, and you will be given the gifts of healing, of knowledge, of wisdom, to minister to the many who will clamour at your feet and want to know and want to be loved; for so many lack the simple knowledge that they are loved. To acknowledge and accept that Love within your souls. You will break free of the encumbrances, of your material mental conditions, and walk in faith and clarity and love.

And thus the healings, the comfort, the loving compassion will flow through you and from you and touch many, many souls. And God has laid the foundations within you, my children. All that you require is there within you. It is for you to accept, to acknowledge, and to be fully within this reality of soul, soul perception, soul mind, soul wisdom. And so, we pray for you to make these steps within, to acknowledge what God has blessed you with and placed within you, and to accept His will above all else, to walk upon this divine path unencumbered by those conditions that would hold you back. And we surround you with love and light, protection. And our prayers are constant, our efforts vigilant. And God has His hand upon you, my children.

And He beckons you forward, always forward. And your souls respond and are eager to be ever closer to our Heavenly Father. Continue, my children, to step forward, to be open to the guidance, the promptings, the inspiration, the visions, the words, the lighted way, and you will find what your souls seek. You will express what God intends for you to express, and you will walk the path divine with ease and grace, straight and narrow, loving and bounteous. God bless you, my children. I love you. I am Seretta Kem and I work to serve you, to make you stronger, to bring you what you require, to be God’s beautiful channels of Love. And I love you. God bless you and good night.
