# The World is in desperate need for Light and Truth

December 31<sup>st</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

Beloved and precious souls, Confucius greets you on the eve of the new year. As you enter this new beginning, I wish to remind you that God has much work waiting for you. There is much to do, my beloveds, for the world is in desperate need for light and truth and for all the complex creations of man to fall away so that humanity may accept creation and the laws of His Love and the harmony of His Will.

To be infused into this world as it is for you, my beloveds, to usher this in as His instruments and you will join many others in this effort. The time is upon us, my beloveds, it approaches very, very near, for these efforts to take shape, for the changes in the world will intensify and multiply and cause great upheaval in the world. But with change comes opportunity to bring light and truth and you will be guided, beloveds, shown the way.

There is a road for you to walk. It is straight, it is lit, it is sure and true. Walk upon this road in faith and trust and all will be well and do not allow the conditions of this world to overwhelm you. Be prayerful, be consistent in your prayers, be earnest and allow God to push aside the barriers. God bless you, beloveds. Confucius loves you dearly. (He’s?) with you ever more, beloveds.
