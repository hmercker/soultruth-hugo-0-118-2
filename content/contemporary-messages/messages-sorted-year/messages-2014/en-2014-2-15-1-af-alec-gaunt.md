# Every Step you take brings Opportunity into your Lives

February 15<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, Gibsons, BC, Canada

Alec Gaunt received by Al Fike

God bless you, this is Alec. Every step you take, my friends, brings opportunity into your lives, and every prayer you make, my friends, brings greater light into yourselves. And indeed, with every day there are greater opportunities for your growth to be a channel of God’s Love, to serve, to bring light to others, and comfort and healing. Yes, every day brings greater opportunities. If you desire to be a channel for God’s Love and Will in this world, you will be guided. You will be given great opportunity, wondrous opportunities, and doors will open that you cannot at this time imagine, and surprises will abound in your lives, as you meet many souls, many beautiful souls upon your path and travel around this world. And if you are willing, and if you allow yourselves to be in the flow of God’s Love, with humility and grace, integrity and strength, then much light will flow through and around you, into your lives, into this world, into many lives around this world. It is all in desire, my friends. Your desire - your desire for God’s Love to enter your souls, your desire to be a loving individual in this world, your desire to grow in the Love, understand the laws of love, to be in harmony with God’s laws of creation.

Yes, many choices. Yet a life filled with passivity, with lethargy, with ignorance, with fear, is not what you desire, for each one here has a strong soul’s desire to live a life filled to the brim with acts of love and beauty. And each soul here desires to be close to God. I say, act upon this desire, my friends, and step upon the Divine path, the path that leads to at-onement with God, and this is accomplished through receiving the Essence of God within your souls, and this is accomplished by praying to God, beseeching God to put His Love within your souls. There is only one way, there is only you and God. Your soul and God’s soul. Your soul’s desire and God’s response. It is so very simple. So very simple, yet alludes many who allow themselves to be greatly distracted by this world. And there are many in such deprivation that they cannot see beyond their own suffering. And yet all of the dear souls in this circle have a great opportunity to know this truth, to exercise this truth, and be in the flow of God’s Love.

You are blessed, my children, blessed mightily. Choose well. Listen to that inner voice. Allow yourselves to trust in God and acknowledge the great blessings that can come in this relationship, this wondrous touch from your Creator. And you will find great joy and happiness in this, a wonderful fulfillment in your life, and great opportunity to be a channel of God’s Love in this world, for God desires many to be His channels of love, to help your brothers and sisters, to walk in light and truth. The door opens for you, my children. It is but for you to step through and welcome the invitation, and know the joy of God’s embrace. No greater joy is this. Thank you for listening to me, my friends. I am Alec, and I have been with this group for many, many years; and for some - for one, I have sat and prayed with her, this precious, precious soul. And I see through her, much has been accomplished, for as this chain of light has passed, it has passed to many through her and her diligent efforts, and humble, loving ways. She has nurtured many of you, and I give my thanks that she has carried this flame and shared, in loving ways, these truths to many. Humble beginnings will lead to great, great results, and wondrous, wondrous light and blessings in this world. Thank you for listening. I am your brother and friend Alec, and I love you. God bless you.
