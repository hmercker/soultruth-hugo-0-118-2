# Love is the most important Thing

January 4<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Menifee, CA, USA

Mary received by Al Fike

We are so happy that you’ve come together. Love is the most important thing, my children. Love your children. Love the stranger. Love all of humanity. For love will change the world and bring what is required to bring harmony and balance back to your dear, suffering world. Love yourselves. Love all of Creation. For in love comes all the solutions to your problems, in love comes acceptance, humility, knowledge, true power.

And in God’s Love comes at-onement, redemption, healing, peace. Each of you has received a gift to help you move forward in your spiritual progression. Each of you has been blessed by dear Jesus. Each of you has much to give to the world. You must believe in yourselves, you must believe in this glorious blessing, which is you, unique and beautiful, imbued with many, many gifts and abilities. Some of you barely know yourselves. In your prayers, as you beseech God, you will be given greater understanding of who you truly are. This will come as you allow yourselves to be fully embraced by the Creator, as you release your own negativity and thoughts, as you let go of those old ways and old habits, as the Love heals all and brings you to that place of full awakening and understanding and peace and joy. Do you not feel the stirring within you? As your soul is eager to manifest itself fully within your being and your consciousness, that you may be fully alive and with God. Precious souls, we love you.

We love you. Bless you. Mary loves you.
