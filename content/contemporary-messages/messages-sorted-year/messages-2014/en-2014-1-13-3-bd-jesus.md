# You have a deep Purpose

January 13<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Jesus received by Barbara Davies

God bless you my dear ones, I am Jesus. Remember and accept that each of you have a deep, deep and needed purpose in your world. And peace and light and understanding are (?) so deeply needed. As you continue upon your path of love, the doors will open for you, my precious ones, and you will let the love flow, and this will cause the peace and love and deep healing for the lost ones. This is a deep purpose for you as you continue upon your path of light. And Angels will uphold you, my beloveds, and you will bring peace, love and understanding to all the lives you touch.

Know this, beloveds, and accept this with joy and love. You are precious souls, beloved souls, and yes, you are deeply needed in your world. Beloved souls, you have each received a blessing. You must let this flow to all the lives you touch. As you continue upon the divine path, in your world, each of you has a deep power of Divine Love flowing through your souls. My beloveds, know this and accept this, powerful blessing of the Love divine. You have a deep purpose and will let the power of Divine Love flow from you to the lost ones. Bless you, bless you and bless your beloveds. I am Jesus and you are deeply needed in your world. Accept this and the love will grow.
