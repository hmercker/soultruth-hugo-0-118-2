# You sit in the Light and Desire the Love of God to enter your Souls

February 10<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Confucius received by Al Fike

God bless you, my children, it is Confucius. Beloved souls, you sit in the light and desire the Love of God to enter your souls, my children, and in this, you are unified. In this is a powerful channel of Love into the world. In this, you fulfill your souls’ desire to be close to God and to be in loving fellowship. This simple prayer for Love will bring all you require to be a channel of Love into this world, to be an agent of change into this world. And yes, the pace does quicken, my children. Changes, great changes within and without, as you rise to meet the challenges put before you by the Heavenly Father. Doors of opportunities open and Love floods your lives in many ways as you allow God’s Will to permeate the flow of your life, as you seek ever higher for this blessing of Love and Light, as you come to know your purpose my children, your purpose, so powerful and unique to each. Each individual soul has a purpose to fulfill in the flow of God’s Will.

Continue in your prayers, my children, and we will continue to guide you and walk with you, as you seek to know your purpose, as you seek to know your Heavenly Father, as you seek to know yourselves. And this awareness unifies in the Love and becomes clearer as you walk the Path Divine. It is indeed difficult to know your future, where you are going, and yet God has lain this path before you and you must walk one step at a time, and your awareness will grow as you walk thus upon this path, this journey, which God has gifted you with, this precious life that is yours to live.

And your precious soul which is God’s greatest creation longs to flourish, to grow and to overtake your consciousness in a way that your perceptions, your understanding of creation, will transform and become in harmony with God’s Love and the laws of creation. This transformation continues within you and at times you feel uncomfortable and confused with this, as any change brings some measure of discomfort. But I tell you, each of you continues to grow and that which is uncomfortable today will be joyful tomorrow.

Sweet children, God leads you forward; your souls eagerly desire to take this journey and for you to make this conscious choice to stay on it, to step in faith, in service, commitment and most of all, love. It will bring you great fulfillment, my children, joyous, wondrous acknowledgment of the beauty of God’s Creation.

Continue in your prayers, continue in your efforts to bring light into this world, to bring light into yourselves, to choose with every breath to reach higher, to walk in harmony with the laws of Love, and to receive this greatest of blessings, the Father’s Divine Essence. Continue to desire this with all your heart and soul and this journey shall continue, shall continue. And we will come, my children, as we have, so shall we continue, in joyous movement towards God. I love you, my children. Confucius loves you dearly. I will never leave you; always there to comfort you and to encourage and to teach. Beautiful souls, God bless you.

God bless you.
