# No one can assess what God has put within your Souls

January 6<sup>th</sup>, 2014

USA Tour, Flagstaff, AZ, USA

John the Beloved received by Al Fike

This is Divine Love, my children. Let no one tell you differently, for no one can assess what God has put within your souls. And for those who in their effort to be an authority take such liberties, I say pray for them, pray that they will have the wisdom to love each soul in this world and to see the beauty within, for each soul is worthy of Godʼs Love.

And there is no effort to understand this truth of Love that can be constructive if there is judgement, and there is none upon this world who have the perceptions of the soul to assess the soul progression within another. They cannot see. But you, my children, know that God lives within you, in His Love. Do you not feel it? Can you not acknowledge this? And this is between you and your Heavenly Father, and none other.

It is error to make this assessment of another, to judge the condition of another soul. For it takes great soul perception to see in this way. Love your fellow man. Know that God loves them infinitely and they are all worthy, they are all worthy. Do not judge, but pour the Love upon them, and in this way you will heal this world as a channel of Godʼs Love. Bless you, my children. I am John the Beloved and I love you. Bless you.
