# Exchange of Souls

February 10<sup>th</sup>, 2014

Prayer Circle, West Vancouver, BC, Canada

Goldie received by Al Fike

It is Goldie. I see we have an exchange of souls across the waters (chuckles); two precious souls for two precious souls. I think this is lovely. Yes, God’s Will is mysterious is it not? And it is intriguing and beautiful. There will be many more surprises. Yes, you should all laugh at how intriguing and beautiful is God’s Will, bringing joyous, interesting events into our lives. These two, two precious souls are drawn to the light, and they are indeed beautiful.

I love you, dear souls, and you will be nurtured and shown the way to God’s Love. God bless you and yes, we will send two precious souls to replace you. Yes, Goldie loves you. Beloved souls, bless you.
